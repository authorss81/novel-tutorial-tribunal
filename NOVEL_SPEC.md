# Novel Specification

Title: The Tutorial Tribunal
Repository slug: novel-tutorial-tribunal
Genre: system mystery / legal fantasy
Male lead: A former game tester who becomes the first defendant before the System
Length target: 850 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A System judges whether a fantasy world is fit to continue, and every challenge becomes evidence in a trial about who created it.

## Ending
The verdict decides whether the world is a game, a prison, or a place where people can refuse the rules.

The volume's last image was delivered on the first of the seventh month at the sixth bell, at the turn, at a standpipe, on a mud bank, to about two hundred and forty people: a rate that has not gone up, a man's name, a woman's nine sentences, a quarter of a penny, and a quarter of a sentence with the gaps in it — and then **a woman of sixty-one who has walked four miles every morning for eighty-six days used a rule for the first time in the two months since it was made, was asked a question by a person who has no right to ask it, and answered it, and the answer was hers: she did not tell the district because the district does not know, because if the bench knew that a washer of sixty-one was paying a penny a day then the next time there was a hole in the fund somebody would work out where a penny a day was coming from and it would not be a person, it would be a ratepayer.** Nobody can do one single thing about it and that is correct. The chapter closes on a boy of seventeen reading out a question about a person — *whose name is the last one in a witness line on a form that nobody has to answer to* — and on nobody answering it, and on the road not being touched. **The right of refusal is not restored. The Bridgeward is one step further along and no further. The continuity line has been refused seven times, in the same words, nobody named, and the holder has still not read it.**

The leg does not clear, the token was not touched, the rendering is unchanged, and the vantage has not moved in ninety-nine days. **He said out loud for the third time that he is not going to chase the sealed preservation item, and why: the price of going on after that desk is that records stop coming, to everybody.**
