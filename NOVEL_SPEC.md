# Novel Specification

Title: The Tutorial Tribunal
Repository slug: novel-tutorial-tribunal
Genre: system mystery / legal fantasy
Male lead: A former game tester who becomes the first defendant before the System
Length target: 850 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A System judges whether a fantasy world is fit to continue, and every challenge becomes evidence in a trial about who created it.

## Ending
The verdict decides whether the world is a game, a prison, or a place where people can refuse the rules.
## Status

Volume 02 is in progress. **Batch 0003, Chapters 71–80, is complete, and it uses the volume's machinery instead of building it. The carrier's clause is exercised for the first time: a district eleven miles from a bridge, which never asked for the charter and cannot pay for the printing, asks for the terms because a man with a sheet of paper came to a pump and read one out, and the asking is a piece of paper that nobody in the case can verify, and the only reader in that district is a cowman's wife of thirty-eight who finds the defect he cannot — a short copy of the charter that had already been carried down a lane on a cart and was missing the clause that names him, because a clerk does not copy a man's name into a district's paper when a district has not asked for a man. The district tells him what it wanted and it was not a charter: *a person to come and stand in our room.* Ilyan walks eleven miles when he is only obliged for four, because past four miles the clause makes it a favour and a favour cannot be entered, and the leg is his own for all of them and stops forty yards past the end of the lane.

**Four findings, each a worse answer than anybody wanted.** A word printed at the top of a form is not a label on a rule, it is a claim that the rule is temporary, and it cost a working man a season of four months and a roof while the weighhouse foreman who did it said out loud that the form gave him a sentence instead of the sentence he had. A holder is a door with no room behind it, because the one rule that keeps her out of appeals is the rule that puts her in front of everybody, and she asks her bench for a rate for a haul and pays for it by becoming the author of a rate she must read aloud every quarter and cannot appeal. A public answer is a price: a bench that has told the truth about itself in its own hand has priced a room in its own district at one answer in eleven days, it cost a woman a fortnight's notice, one bench of the seven withdrew its answer in writing because of a woman, and the one *yes* in the city turned out to be a yes about paper and a no about people, because an office with no person in it is a shelf. And a place can only be given a page by making a person a body, and the only person available to a place of sixty-one people with no name in it is the carrier — and a holder is not a carrier, so the cost of a page for a place is the door closing for four hundred people four miles away.

**The debt of thirty years got a figure and the figure is wrong.** A hundred and thirteen thousand four hundred hours at threepence the hour is one thousand four hundred and seventeen pounds and ten shillings, six years of a district's road fund and thirty-two years of the cost of its charter, and the gate hand corrected it herself and unasked, because the working tide of the turn is one day in seven. It was entered over her refusal, in three hands, on the same page, and it is the sixth thing Ilyan has put against his own name and the first one he was not asked to enter. The token was used a fourth time and it took the words: for four days he could see a line of writing and could not read it, the second person in the world to know is a clerk, and it is not on a page — and the rendering resolved one step further, because the vantage is a room, with four people at a table and one of them a gate hand he has never seen at a table, and the vantage has not moved. **The public maintenance review was held on the twenty-seventh and it decided a sum.** Four new props are bought for a bridge that has wanted four for twenty-nine years, the old four come out, that deck is open in the weather, and the under-deck watch at the ninth bridge drops to one hand for the year: **T. Blore, twenty-two, eleven months under a deck, is the only person in this case's record named as having lost something in it.** He is produced in person with his report and the two copies of his own sequence and names who paid. Wen Lask's question is read out and answered and the answer is entered as not being the answer, and the road is not touched. Harrow invents a fourth procedure — how a carrier is sworn in — and it cannot be used, because the only district with a carrier is the only district that does not need one. The continuity line has spoken a seventh time and been refused a fifth, and the holder still has not read it. **The appeal is in use and is not secured; there is no procedure in this city for what happens to a page when the holder cannot keep it; the Quiet Chamber's quarter sentence and the page over a pump are side by side in a record and are not the same sentence and nothing in this batch is better than that; the survival of the right and any recovery of the original Quiet Chamber terms are Chapters 81–100; no new enemy has been introduced and the planned ending is untouched.**
