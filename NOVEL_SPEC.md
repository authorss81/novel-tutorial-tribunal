# Novel Specification

Title: The Tutorial Tribunal
Repository slug: novel-tutorial-tribunal
Genre: system mystery / legal fantasy
Male lead: A former game tester who becomes the first defendant before the System
Length target: 850 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A System judges whether a fantasy world is fit to continue, and every challenge becomes evidence in a trial about who created it.

## Ending
The verdict decides whether the world is a game, a prison, or a place where people can refuse the rules.

Volume 02 is in progress. **Batch 0002, Chapters 61–70, is complete, and it stages the volume's climax.** A magistrate's court finds that the word *ward* is a guild's word for a duty and not a place, that a duty with nobody in it is not a duty nobody does, and that a rate can be paid to a ward only because a duty falls on whoever is standing nearest. Two texts are put in front of a room — a clean instrument written and signed by a cart owner, and a nine-sheet charter that gives a poor district an appeal — and both cost the same water, and a magistrate who is right votes for the clean one and is out-voted four to one. The charter is made with a person in its fifth term, held by the refuse hauler who reads every notice in that district and who cannot appeal from it, and with a carrier's clause that names Ilyan Vester and stays the district's appeal if he refuses. **His whole contribution was nine words; the clause is the fifth thing in this city's record he cannot un-enter, and it is the only one anybody can call out and ask to see him.** Ilyan's Stage 2 becomes a practice and not a power — the afterimage clears inside a burden of his own and does not clear when he rests, and the woman whose hours he is counting says out loud that he has made her thirty years a job for a man who has a leg — and the token is used once, at a cost, and the third layer resolves into a rendering with a vantage he cannot find.
