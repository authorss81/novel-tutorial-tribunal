# Novel Specification

Title: The Tutorial Tribunal
Repository slug: novel-tutorial-tribunal
Genre: system mystery / legal fantasy
Male lead: A former game tester who becomes the first defendant before the System
Length target: 850 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A System judges whether a fantasy world is fit to continue, and every challenge becomes evidence in a trial about who created it.

## Ending
The verdict decides whether the world is a game, a prison, or a place where people can refuse the rules.
