# Batch 0005 Review — Volume 01, Chapters 41–50

An independent reviewer subagent audited the finished batch against `AGENTS.md`, `outline/volume-01.md`, the batch prompt, `bible/power-system.md`, and the canon state files, and against Chapters 38–40 for voice and continuity. Its findings were applied as a fix pass. **The prose was preserved, no chapter was restarted, and no planned beat, ruling term, or fact was removed.** Fourteen concrete defects were fixed; the rest of its report is recorded below as observed or deliberately not changed. Volume 01 is complete at Chapter 50 and the plan for Volume 02 is intact.

## Verdict

The batch is sound. All ten chapters exist as finished scenes that each end on a completed beat. There are no duplicate paragraphs, no meta markers, no chapter numbers inside prose, no `Ilya`, and no real software jargon. The batch's two required turns both land and both are structural rather than rhetorical. The volume's concrete resolution is delivered in full.

## Fixed in this pass

- **Ilyan's restored standing and his personal continuing burden were not in the prose.** Three state files asserted them; no chapter did. Harrow now reads the whole limited grant out **twice** before Ilyan answers — on the ground that it was read out to him once at the first hearing and he accepted it before he had read it, and that this court does not do that twice — and Ilyan names the burden himself: identity, report, post-hearing testimony, and everything he has entered into this city's record stay attached through the review, and he may not conceal what the record cost or hand the cost to the household, the guild, the district, the ward, or the woman whose column four districts will read. Sera enters the clause *and not the price of the precedent* on her own initiative and says on the record that it is hers. A fourth line for it was added to the back of her ledger in Chapter 50, which is the volume's closing document and had three.
- **The Quiet Chamber yield was over-read.** The facing-leaf line had been given a leading *…may answer no, and*, merging the already-known public fragment onto a page that canon says yields one line, and Sera said she was writing "the four words" over twenty-two. Both corrected. The line now begins with a lower-case *a*, because the sentence it belongs to begins on the page that is gone, and the shadow is what she declines to read. `state/continuity.md` and the Volume 02 prompt were corrected to match, and both now state explicitly that *the governed may answer no* is **not** part of this yield.
- **Chapter 44 broke the three-measurement rule three times**, which matters because that finding is the batch's spine. The first non-return was made a hold at one and a half, which destroyed the escalation; the held-at-one reading was measured from the iron ring, which is the reference for the *permanent set* and is a different number; and Ilsa claimed a finger reading on three earlier days, when canon says the joint returns to mark on every count observed. All three corrected. Oren now states in terms that the band is the band **for a load going across** and that every figure in that book was taken with a moving cart, so the standing-load reading is a new category and not a contradiction.
- **Chapter 44 also had Ilsa say the joint came back "when I took the salt cart off it" while the cart was still on the span.** Rewritten so that it came back on its own *with the cart still standing on it*, which is both correct and worse: the water returned part of the loan even under a standing load, and could not return all of it.
- **Chapter 47 contradicted Chapter 36 and itself about the bell's pin.** Tamsin said Oren had written the pin down; Oren said nobody had ever told him it was worn; Chapter 36, canon, has Tamsin asserting Oren's board. Oren now says he has rung the bell four hundred times and has never once *looked* at the pin, and that a thing which lasts a year is a thing you do not look at — which reconciles all three and keeps the "nobody asked her what needed looking at" turn.
- **Weekday names.** Chapter 48 named a Sunday; Chapters 45 and 49 named a Thursday. The batch prompt, `state/current.md`, and `state/continuity.md` all forbid one. All three replaced with relative dates. **The state files' claim that "no chapter in the whole volume names a weekday" was false** — Chapter 40, closed and canon, names Tuesday, Friday, and Thursday in passing. Both state files and the Volume 02 prompt now say that Chapters 41–50 name none, that Chapter 40's three stand, and that they are not to be extended.
- **Chapter 50's dates could not all be true.** The unloaded period was dated from "the seventeenth to the sixth," the rider arrived on "the fourth day" of it and also on "the twelfth of the month," the strip went up on "the seventeenth day," and the seat was said to be cured nine months out. Rebuilt on one anchor: the unloaded period is twenty days and began **at dusk on the day of the ruling**, when the span closed on four props; the seat was opened that morning and bedded the next; the lower road's roof went over on the **morning of the ruling**, the last morning the span was open; Chapter 50 is the eleventh day; the print arrived on the fifth; the strip went up on the eleventh with twenty-three names on it; and the pale line will sit for another nine days until the props come out and a fortnight after that. Chapter 46's roof date was changed to match, and Joren's line about putting a date and its opposite on one line of the ward's book was written into him.
- **The ruling silently changed the district's own offer.** The district offered the guild's hands at the guild's *levy* rate; term three of the ruling pays them at the *ward* rate. Harrow now stops the clerk before the last term and enters the difference, raises the rate, and records that the district did not object and that the wage will not stop in the meantime if it later wishes to.
- **The cart fee was left with no ending.** The eighth of a load-fee from nine named cart owners is public and load-bearing and A. of the eastern yard keeps the book. Harrow now enters that the ruling cannot end other people's money, that it adds to the toll instead — the toll now also pays the six hands of a called pause — that the toll is short on a thin day, and that **a road which cannot pay a pause does not get to refuse one.**
- **Chapter 45's fourth-bridge keeper was sixty-one**, which is Hanne Kolm's age; he is forty-nine. And "a twenty-two-year-old span" collided with Oren's twenty-two years in the post. Both replaced.
- **Chapter 43's fen-crossing conference was "a fortnight ago."** It was a week ago. Also corrected: the district's book "stopped on the fourteenth" (it lapsed at dusk on the sixteenth and has no line in it for a roof at all), and "two of them are on a district" (two districts, so "districts").
- **Chapter 48 cited a day that had not happened** — a warning from Sera "on the twenty-second," during a hearing on the twentieth — and it placed itself three days before the charter was amended ("three days of a charter left in it"). Both corrected. A line she had said in a corridor about the precedent was moved from after the ruling to before it, which is where it belongs.
- **Day counts.** The case's convention, fixed in canon, is that Day *n* is "elapsed *n*−1 days." Chapters 41–44 and 46–49 were carrying "twenty days" on the nineteenth and twentieth days, and Chapter 43 had Joren claiming twenty days of roof deliveries when the frame had been up three. All recounted: **eighteen days** in Chapter 41, **nineteen days** from Chapter 42 through Chapter 49, and Joren's count rebuilt as "three days of it with a date on the frame, and a carrier reading it before he loads."
- **Two narration slips.** Harrow's "no clerk had seen him do in nineteen years" read as his tenure when the court's is nine; and Chapter 50's "four hundred people who had no office at all" was left inside a sentence about who was "from a court." Both fixed. Three places where the narration restated what the action had already shown, and one overclaim that called Ilsa not arguing with herself "the most important thing that happened during the recess" in the recess where Dalla's answer and Vale's impossibility both landed, were cut.
- **The crowd count went from two hundred to a hundred and forty in consecutive paragraphs of Chapter 42.** Normalised.

## Observed, not changed

- **Chapter 41 is 5,401 words, longer than any chapter in the manuscript.** It is not padded: it carries the toll book, Puck's discovery, the entire costing on the board, the district's answer, and the roll at the rope. The prompt's guidance was not to cut a scene to hit a number, and nothing in it was cut.
- **Chapter 48 carries four payoffs**, including the volume's only mystery yield, in one night. It is the single shape complaint in the batch. It was left as written rather than split, because splitting a scene that a court sits through in one night into a second night would have changed what the chapter is.
- **The phrase *lawful traveler* stands in the instrument and the ruling does not use the word *exception* anywhere.** Term ten carries the time limit and term five carries the bound, so the substance is a narrow renewable time-limited settlement, but the outline's word is not on the page. Recorded rather than inserted, because the phrase and its absence are both load-bearing for Volume 02.
- **The bell description recurs three times with three different cadences** (Chapter 42, Chapter 44, Chapter 47), which reads as deliberate recall across court, keeper, and child. The Chapter 44 instance was compressed so that no two of the three are near enough to read as a copy.
- **The District's answer is accepted and found impossible in the same scene, and the fix is Dalla's.** The reviewer noted that Chapter 41 states the district's version of the fix ("paid to the person who stood there, not to a ward") before Chapter 46 re-derives it in Dalla's sharper form. Dalla's contribution is the one that survives — the ward rate is a number and not a recipient — and Chapter 41's version is a district's phrase rather than the answer, so Chapter 46 reads as a correction rather than a confirmation.
