## 1I. THE THIRTEEN THREADS OF 681–690, AND EVERY ONE IS A PERSON DOING A THING

**A THREAD IS A THING A PERSON IS STILL DOING. A HAZARD IS NOT A THREAD. NONE OF THESE THIRTEEN WAS CLOSED BY THE BAND THAT OPENED IT, AND NONE OF THE THIRTEEN OF 671–680 WAS CLOSED BY IT EITHER.**

1. **A CUT ACROSS THE RIGHT PALM OF A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE, FROM THE BASE OF THE THUMB TO ABOUT THE FIRST JOINT, AND IT IS NOT HEALING AT ANY RATE ANYBODY HAS A FIGURE FOR.** It is dry on the Thursday, and the skin round it has not gone white and has not gone soft and the palm of it has not come up. **A man of thirty-one said out loud at `685` that nobody is to say it will keep, or that it will not keep, or that it is not much, because there is no figure for any of those three, and he said he is not going to say it was worth it and is not going to hear anybody else say it either, and nobody did.** `685`, `687`, `688`, `690`
2. **THE WAY BACK HE HAD SINCE THE EVENING OF THE WEDNESDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FIRST WEEK, WHICH IS GONE, AND WHICH HE SAID HIMSELF IN A PASSAGE ON `686` BEFORE ANYBODY ELSE DESCRIBED IT.** **There is no figure for what it was and nobody is going to be given one, because there is no book with a figure in it and there is not one person in four counties who was keeping it.** What is left of it is about three inches of loose gravel where there was about a foot of dry walked gravel, and a walked place about as wide as one boot where there were about nine. `686`, `690`
3. **NINE TERMS, WHICH IS NINE JOBS WITH A NAME ON EACH, AND NOT ONE OF THE NINE HAS A NAME ON IT, AND NOT ONE OF THE NINE HAS BEEN GOT BACK.** Between the two hands on every mark there is a space cut square about as wide as a thumb, cut to the depth of the hands and old. **A man of thirty-one said out loud on the last day of the band that there are nine of them and not one of them is here, not a name and not a bit of a name, and that it is not the work of this week and he is not going to apologise for it.** `682`, `689`, `690`
4. **EIGHT DOORS THAT NOBODY ON THE FACE OF EARTH CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT, AND A MAN OF FORTY-ONE IS WALKING ROUND THEM ON FOOT, ONE AT A TIME, AND HE HAS ASKED SEVEN PEOPLE IN SEVEN DAYS AND GOT ONE NAME OUT OF THEM AND THE NAME IS A MAN WHO HAS BEEN DEAD ABOUT THIRTY YEARS.** About six of the eight said they had never heard of a door under a bank; one said nothing at all and shut a door. **The eighth is in a village he has never been inside a gate of and he goes in the gate on the Monday after this band, alone.** `688`, `689`, `690`
5. **A WOMAN OF FORTY-FOUR WHO HAS HAD A NUMBER SAID OUT LOUD ABOUT HER THREE TIMES IN NINE DAYS AND HAS BEEN ASKED ONCE, AND WHO SAID ONCE THAT A PERSON WHO CANNOT BE ASKED IS NOT THE SAME AS A PERSON WHO HAS SAID NO.** About nine houses four miles up a cart road are a day late and about four of the nine cannot do a day without it, and one of those four is a man of eighty-one who has a Thursday four days after a Sunday morning. `683`
6. **ABOUT THREE INCHES OF LOOSE GRAVEL IN A PLACE THAT WAS WALKED, AND A GOING IN A WALL BEHIND A SHELF IN THE NINTH ROOM THAT NOBODY KNEW WAS THERE UNTIL THE FIRST DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND TWENTIETH WEEK, AND A MAN OF SIXTY-SIX WHO WILL NOT SAY WHEN IT WAS FILLED OR BY WHOM.** He is the only person anybody can be sent for about the word *loom* and he did not say the word again. `685`, `686`
7. **A GOING WHERE NO GOING HAS EVER GONE, WHICH A WOMAN OF FORTY-NINE CALLED THE BLANK OUT OF A BAKEHOUSE RACK, AND WHICH NOBODY IN FOUR COUNTIES CAN GIVE A FIGURE FOR.** A groove is a route and a route is a going and a coming, and the only figure anybody has is the length of a cut in a hand measured against a groove in a wall. `685`, `686`
8. **WHAT ABOUT NINE PEOPLE CAN HOLD, WHICH HAS BEEN ASKED THREE TIMES AND HAS NOW GOT TWO HALVES AND IS NOT ONE FIGURE.** About nine people can hold about nine doors, one each, and they cannot hold the nine names, and there are not nine names. **And about nine people can hold about one scar, and the one who can hold it is the man it is on, and there is no form that says a man may be sent for about his own right hand.** `688`
9. **ONE SENTENCE IN A DAY BOOK THAT HAD HAD NOTHING BUT NUMBERS IN IT FOR ABOUT NINE DAYS, WRITTEN BY A WOMAN OF TWENTY-EIGHT IN HER OWN HAND WITH NO OTHER HAND BETWEEN, WHICH NOBODY ON THAT BANK CAN READ AND NOBODY AFTER HER WILL EITHER.** She broke her own rule and is not going to say what the rule was for. `687`, `690`
10. **A NUMBER WRITTEN IN A BOOK ON THE LEAF AFTER THE LAST HEAD, WHERE THERE HAD BEEN FOUR LINES AND THERE ARE NOW FIVE, AND IT IS THE FIRST NUMBER A MAN OF SIXTY-ONE HAS WRITTEN IN THAT BOOK IN ELEVEN YEARS.** The number is nine and he announced it three days before he wrote it. **He said out loud what he will not write next to it, which is nine people, because there are eight of them and a book with a nine in it and eight names against it is a list of what is missing.** `688`, `689`
11. **A STONE ABOUT A HUNDRED AND THIRTY FEET PAST THE LAST OF ABOUT NINE BEAMS WITH NINE LINES IN IT IN NINE HANDS THAT CANNOT WRITE, THE NINTH CUT ON THE THURSDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND EIGHTEENTH WEEK, AND NOT ONE WORD OF IT HAS BEEN READ BY ANYBODY IN FOUR COUNTIES.** And a lamp burning on a hook in the ninth of nine rooms about a quarter full of oil that nobody lit and nobody is going to put out. `681`, `685`, `689`
12. **A THING ABOUT NINE FEET LONG THAT GOES ALONG ABOUT TWO INCHES AND COMES BACK WHILE THERE IS A PERSON ON THE ABOUT NINETY STEPS AND DOES NOT GO WHILE THERE IS A PERSON IN THE ROOM, WHICH WAS DONE TWICE AND CONTROLLED ONCE AT `678`, AND WHICH WENT ALONG TWO INCHES FOUR MORE TIMES THIS BAND AND WAS NOT NAMED AND WAS NOT PERSONIFIED AND WAS GIVEN NO MOTIVE.** `682`, `685`, `686`, `689`
13. **A LOCAL HEARING THAT NOBODY HAS GRANTED AND NOBODY HAS REFUSED AND NOBODY HAS WRITTEN DOWN, AND A SHEET OF DEAL WITH A SEAL ON IT LYING FACE DOWN ON A BARREL THAT HAS HAD IT FOR ABOUT FORTY DAYS, AND A MAN OF SIXTY-ONE WHO HAS STOPPED COUNTING THE HEARING AND WHO ANNOUNCED THREE DAYS BEFORE HE WROTE A NUMBER IN HIS BOOK THAT HE WAS GOING TO WRITE ONE.** **And nobody on that bank has said out loud that a lamp on a hook in a room and the thing that keeps the record in four counties are the same thing, and a woman of twenty-eight checked it a third time at `681` and a fourth at `687` and a fifth at `690` and did not say it, and said each time that it is not because it is not true.** `681`, `687`, `688`, `690`
