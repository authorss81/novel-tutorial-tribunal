## 1m. What 671–680 changed

**NOBODY IN VOLUME 14 BAND 0003 WAS RELIEVED, FORGIVEN, REDEEMED OR THANKED. NOBODY IN IT IS A VILLAIN, AND THE THING THAT KEEPS THE RECORD IN FOUR COUNTIES IS NOT ONE AND IS NOT RELIEVED, AND A LAMP ON A HOOK IS NOT ONE, AND A STONE WITH NINE LINES ON IT IS NOT ONE, AND A BEAM ABOUT NINE FEET LONG IS NOT ONE, IS NOT NAMED, HAS NO FACE PUT ON IT AND HAS NO MOTIVE ASSIGNED TO IT, AND A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE SAYS OUT LOUD THAT HE IS NOT GOING TO SUPPLY A FIFTH THING ABOUT IT AND SAYS ONE OF HIS FIVE ON THE LAST DAY OF THE BAND AND IT IS NOT ABOUT THE BEAM.**

- **ILYAN VESTER, THIRTY-ONE.** He is nobody’s. **He has been in the county of Kell `chapter − 554` days, one hundred and seventeen at `671` through one hundred and twenty-six at `680`, and every one of the ten is said out loud in its own chapter with the subtraction spoken, and the speakers are six different people and not one mouth.** He put the plainest question anybody has put in that county in this volume — what has to be done on a bank on a Monday by people who are alive — and got an answer about nine people doing nothing in two sentences that he had not expected and did not want. **He said out loud that he had come into the county wanting a figure for something and had left it with nine of them and had not put a single one of them on a form, and that he is not going to be told that is progress and is not going to be told it is not.** He offered to be the thing that is sent for a third time on the Sunday, in about nine words, at the head of the flight, and was refused a third time in about nine sentences out loud without raising his voice, and he said out loud in a passage with nine people in it that nobody in that county is to be forgiven for anything and that he is including himself first. **He said the one sentence he has had in him since the Monday of the hundred and fifteenth week and it was not about the beam: he has spent one hundred and twenty-four days counting what he knows and has never once counted what he does not know, and that is the only figure anybody has ever wanted out of him and he is not going to give it to anybody.** He did not answer the band’s own question and said out loud that he was not going to supply the other half of it and that he has not got one person in four counties entitled to hear it from him. He is still nobody’s and there is still no column, no form, no rate and no hundred in that county, and `citizen` is at 0.
- **NEVIN TRASK, SIXTY-ONE,** who has kept the book of heads and carts eleven years. **He wrote down one figure this band and it is the only thing he has written down in a fortnight: a man of forty-three has been wrong by a day about a figure he has given four times, and the reason he wrote that one down and not another is that there is a difference between a figure a man gets wrong and a figure about a person.** He gave a count of the days in the county, of the boy of thirteen, and of the two people in a place and not in a room on `673`, `677` and `680`, and did not add the two. **He counted eleven years of his own book out loud and said it has about nine hundred heads in it and carts against about as many and that there is not one name in it of anybody who did anything in those eight doors, and said that is eight doors and no names and it is a figure of a book and not of the doors.** He said there is no form in that county that says a person may be asked whether they will still come and that he is not going to cut one. He refused to start the hearing count again on the Thursday and said why: it is the last morning of a week and a man of thirty-one would like it. He is not relieved and he is not the author of anything.
- **SIMON ROOK, ABOUT FIFTY-TWO, WHO CANNOT SEE WELL,** who is the person the volume’s midpoint belongs to. **He is not one of the nine, he has not said he will be near it, and he has been on that bank and has said almost nothing in a fortnight apart from one sentence given three times.** He refused the man of thirty-one for the third time, out loud, at the top of about ninety steps, in about nine sentences, without raising his voice, and said he was not going to give a new reason because the only reason he gives has never once worn out in eleven years. He said out loud that he has never been down that wet flight, that it is about as tall as a man and he cannot see well, that he has not tried and is not going to, and that he is standing at the top of it on a Sunday morning because he has stood at the top of things for eleven years, and asked for it to be said that he said it first and that nobody is to make a figure of him out of it. **AND THEN ON THE MONDAY HE PUT THE FINDING TOGETHER, IN FOURTEEN SENTENCES, OUT OF THE FINDING THAT THE NINTH LINE IS THE NEWEST AND THE FIRST IS NEARLY GONE: IT IS WHAT NINE PEOPLE AGREED TO DO, WRITTEN DOWN IN A STONE IN THE NINTH ROOM, ON NINE DIFFERENT MORNINGS, FOR THE MORNINGS THEY ARE NOT THERE. IT IS NOT A PERSON. IT IS NOT A MACHINE. IT HAS GOT NO OPINION. AND HE ASKED FOR IT TO BE VERY MUCH RECORDED THAT A MAN WHO CANNOT SEE WELL WORKED IT OUT STANDING ON A BANK AND NOT IN THE ROOM.** He addressed the First Witness once, flat, to the ground, and said he was saying it to the stone and not to anybody in the bank and that he was not going to shout it and was not going to say it again, and nothing whatever happened. He said out loud that there is no form in that county that says a person may be asked what they said to a stone and that he is nobody and that nobody in that county can be sent for about what he just did. **HE SAID OUT LOUD ON THE WEDNESDAY THAT HE IS NOT GOING TO BE A KEEPER OF ANY OF IT, IN TEN SENTENCES, AND THE REASON IS THAT THE DAY HE IS A KEEPER OF THIS HE IS A THING THAT IS SENT FOR AND HE WILL HAVE SAID SO HIMSELF. HE IS NOT RELIEVED, HE IS NOT A SAGE, AND BEING RIGHT IS NOT A THING ANYBODY IS THANKED FOR.**
- **CORVIN TACE, FORTY-THREE,** who keeps the landing nine miles up that river nineteen years. **He gave a reading on ten mornings out of the last ten and then stopped, and gave the reason: a figure said out loud twelve times and not written down is a figure that about nine people in that bank have and about four hundred miles off nobody has.** He owned his own error out loud — the next time about ninety steps can be walked is about four days after that bank is full, it was full on the Saturday, four days is the Wednesday, and it came off on the Tuesday night, a day early — and asked a man of sixty-one to say so on the bank rather than him, on the ground that a figure a man of forty-three has got wrong five times ought to be got wrong by somebody who keeps a book. He said he was glad about a day off when the two who lift the board were not needed and said there is no form that says a man may not be glad about a day off. He refused to give a figure for a night in which about twelve inches of a bank came off, twice. **He is not relieved and he is not offered anything for it and the figure for the year is still not a number.**
- **ABEL FENNER, SIXTY-ONE,** no: **ABEL FENNER, SIXTY-SIX,** a sinker, and the only person in four counties anybody can be sent for about the word *loom*. **He said the word is older than he is, that he is not going to say it again, that he is not going to say where he got it, and that he is not going to be the man who says it a second time on a bank — and he did not say it again.** He said the guild mark is a different mark for the third time, under the ninth opening, and gave the reason: a guild mark has a bar under it and a numeral on the bar, and a mark with no numeral on it is not a mark a builder puts on a thing he made, it is a mark a person puts on a thing they have to come back to. He said what one hand is for in part of the four things he gives everybody and refused to finish it, saying a man of sixty-six is a sinker and knows what a hole is and will not be the man who tells you. He asked for it to be noticed that a man of thirty-eight could have said the word in the dark to about nine people and did not, because it was not his to say and he knew it. HE IS STILL THE ONLY PERSON WHO CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT THE WORD AND HE IS STILL THE ONLY PERSON WHO CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT THE NINE WORDS.
- **THE MAN OF FORTY-ONE WHO KEEPS TWO CARTS,** who is named for the doing and not for the wanting. **He said out loud, standing in a doorway about as wide as himself, in ten sentences, that the twice-a-day he has done for nineteen years was never his work and was never anybody’s job, that nobody asked him, that there is no form in that county that says a man may be asked to do a thing twice a day for nineteen years and no form that says he may not, and that the reason he was saying it was not that he wanted a day counted for it.** He found out on a Friday morning that his two carts and a woman’s bread were the same road and said he did not know it until that morning. He said he will leave the carts at the head of that track on a morning he goes down, about a morning of cart work on about five mornings in ten and about two hours of a cart each and about ten hours in ten days, and that this is a figure of his carts and not of her bread, and that he is not going to be thanked for it on a bank and not going to be forgiven for it either, because there is nothing to forgive him for and he did not do it to her, and that if a person wants him to be sorry they can come and find him on a Wednesday. **He said two sentences about a roof he has never once seen anything come out of in nineteen years and that he had assumed like about nine people standing in there that somebody was holding it up, and that he has got to thinking about that on the Wednesday night and has not put it down and is not going to put it down in a passage with a lamp in it.** He said what about nine people can hold in thirteen sentences and that he is the only one of these nine who can say what it costs and that he is not going to be asked. He added up his own eight mornings out loud in six figures, gave the total at about twelve hours and a half, and said the two mornings with nothing in them are the Friday and the Saturday, when nobody went down because the bank was at eleven inches over the top step and then full, and that he is not going to leave those two out of it, and said the convention out loud in his own mouth — he counts from one morning to the next and not from a morning to itself — and said what the figure is a figure of. HE WAS NOT THANKED AND HE DID NOT WAIT TO BE.
- **AVELINE MARSH, THIRTY-EIGHT,** who keeps a mill four miles off that landing, about eleven people in four houses eat out of it and one of the eleven is her mother. **She said out loud that one is not a smaller version of two, and that if it is then about eight of those nine doors is a door that gets opened once instead of twice, and that she does not know what is on the other side of any of them.** She stopped a man of thirty-one from putting nine openings in the same line as the nine jobs, in seven sentences, and said she had already had to stop him making a number into a shape twice that week. **She said, in ten sentences, that none of it is a way for anybody to say no, and gave both halves: a thing a person wrote down on a morning they were not there does not give anybody a way to be told they are not wanted, it means that if a person stops the line is still there in the stone and nobody has to be told; and she is a woman who turns a wheel once a day, and if she stops then the peg goes into a plain hole on the seventh morning and about four years from now nobody in four counties will ever know that chamber was a week, and she has already had that and is not having it again for a stone with nine lines in it.** SHE IS NOT RELIEVED AND WAS NOT THANKED.
- **HESTER OLLERTON, FORTY-FOUR,** who keeps a bakehouse four miles up that cart road, about nine houses in that village take her bread, about sixty loaves in a night and about forty of them go up that track. **She is new in this volume and is not one of the nine and is not a keeper of anything anybody can be sent for about and has never been asked anything by anybody in that county in nineteen years, and she came up that road herself and nobody sent for her.** She said out loud that she would have said yes to being told what it costs and that saying yes to being told is not the same as being asked, and that nobody in that county has the second one and she has wanted it for about nineteen years. **She said the word out loud on a Saturday morning, once, in the middle of an argument about a road, and gave the reason in six boards and a gap: a rack in a bakehouse, six boards across and four shelves, sixty wet trays on at about the eleventh hour at night and sixty off at about the fourth hour in the morning, the trays must not touch or they go to slime, there is no nail and no glue and no heat in that rack, it works while somebody is putting them on and taking them off, and it has never once worked on its own in nineteen years and would not last a morning.** **She is now the second person in this band somebody can be sent for about a word, and she does not know what a copy is, and a man of fifty-four who cannot read said out loud in front of about nine people that she and he are the only two people anybody can be sent for about in that county who can be sent for about what they said, and that neither of them will ever know what is written down.** SHE IS NOT RELIEVED AND SHE WAS NOT THANKED AND SHE WAS NOT ASKED.
- **BARNABY CROVE, FIFTY-FOUR,** who holds the gravel and cannot read. **He said it seven times in the band and never once was asked a question about anything.** He stopped a man of thirty-one from saying a fourth name for a place, and said the count of him stopping him is four in four days and that he would like about four people to have that figure instead of the name, and that a row is a thing you put things into and he has not seen anybody put anything into one of them and has not been in one. He stopped in the doorway of the ninth room and said one word nobody had asked him for, and the reason he gave is that he cannot see very far and knows a lit room when he is stood in one, and he has been in two of those eight times in nine days and none of them were lit. **He said the part that was his after the word was used on a bank, before anybody asked him, in four sentences: she cannot read either, there is no form that says a person may be asked what they said, and he knows exactly what happens next and it is that somebody with a hand and a book writes it down, and there is one hand and one book in that county and the man holding it has told them twice what he will not write.** He said why nine lines are in nine hands and that a hand that cannot write cuts hard on the first stroke of a line and light on the last stroke of the same line. HE IS NOT A DEVICE AND HE IS NOT RELIEVED AND HE IS NOT THANKED.
- **GARRIN TOLLEY, THIRTY-EIGHT,** who may carry the board of thirty-two notches, and has a child of nine. **He put the finding on the bank in about nine sentences because that is the only thing anybody in that county can do with a thing, and he said what it was under, in about eleven sentences, and that he was not saying it as a complaint.** He said that a notch is a count and that he has thirty-two notches cut in the edge of a board he carries in a coat and nobody has ever asked him what the thirty-two is for, and that he counted them again in a room with nine people in it and got the same number he got the last six times. He said a hundred and thirty feet is about a hundred and thirty. **He held his own lamp up on his arm at the height of a beam for about a quarter of an hour, put it down about four times to get his shoulder back in, asked for the four to be counted and not the quarter of an hour, and said his shoulder is going to be no good for carrying a pole up that water on a Thursday, and that is a figure of his shoulder and not of anybody else.** HE WAS NOT THANKED.
- **WENNA CALLOW, FORTY,** who has asked for a day against a name for about nine weeks. **She is not in this band except in a sentence a man of thirty-one says about her: that the first naming in eight days went to her by accident, and that the cost of the second did not go by accident. She said nothing at all in 671–680 and nobody said a word to her.** SHE IS NOT RELIEVED.
- **THE WOMAN OF FORTY-NINE WHO CAME DOWN THAT ROAD TWICE** and came down it a third time, and who does not know what a copy is. **She gave the days in the county out loud at `672` and said she counts days because she counts loaves and that is the only sort of number she has ever had, and that she had eighteen the previous morning and did not know what to do with it. She said out loud on the Thursday that she does not know what eight means and that she has said she does not know what a copy is more times than that and that neither is the same as not knowing what eight means, and that she knows what nine loaves is and what about nine houses is and that is all she has got, and that she has been in that bank for about nine days and has liked it and does not want to be the reason it goes wrong.** She is one of the nine and is not a device.
- **SERA QUILL, TWENTY-EIGHT,** who cannot be sent for about in two counties, with two unread letters in a bag. **She has been in that county `chapter − 642` days, twenty-nine at `671`, thirty-five at `677` and thirty-six at `678`, each figure said out loud with the subtraction.** She said out loud that nobody on that bank had said that a lamp on a hook in a room and the thing that keeps the record in four counties are the same thing, that she has been standing there since the Thursday listening for somebody to say it, that about four of them have thought it, that she has checked twice that morning, that she is not going to be the one who says it and that it is not because it is not true. **She writes two numbers a day in a day book in her own hand and gives him his copy with no other hand between, and said she is going to go on doing it and that nobody is going to thank her for it.** SHE IS NOT RELIEVED AND IS NOT THANKED FOR FOUR HUNDRED MILES.
- **IVO KELLOW, THIRTY-FOUR,** in the ash on the low side of that landing, on no board and in no book. **He has been in it forty-one days at `671`, forty-seven at `677` and fifty at `680`, each spoken with the subtraction out of `630`. He said nothing at all in this band and about nine people on that bank walked past that doorway about nine times a day and nobody stopped at it.** He is not one of the nine and nobody asked him.
- **NELL PRINCE, TWENTY-NINE,** in the reed. **Forty-two days at `671`, forty-eight at `677` and fifty-one at `680`, each spoken with the subtraction out of `629`. She said nothing at all in this band and the two numbers are not added.**
- **THE BOY OF THIRTEEN,** who is not a head in the book of heads and carts because the penny a head is on everybody over sixteen. **He went down on the Friday of the hundred and fifteenth week and has not gone down since, which is eighteen days at `671` and twenty-seven at `680`, every one spoken with the subtraction.** He did a sum of his own out loud in front of about nine people: four miles twice a day is four hours, there is a third four miles on two of the mornings, that is two hours more, six hours out of eight is three-quarters of a working day, and that is a figure of his legs and not of a man of thirteen. He has been saying a figure every morning for a week and said one every chapter of the band, and he is not a device and nobody said he was.
- **PELL VARN, SIXTY-EIGHT,** who keeps the last quarter mile. He is not in this band. His rule’s age is not printed in this band.
- **ORLA FENNIMORE, FORTY-FOUR,** who keeps the chairs book nineteen years for thirteen shillings and fourpence a year. **She stopped a man of thirty-one from saying his own question a third time in about nine people’s hearing and said out loud that he had not learned the thing, he had learned that a woman of forty-four will stop him, and asked about four people to be able to tell the difference.** SHE IS NOT RELIEVED.
- **THE NEW CAST OF VOLUME 14, ALL OF THEM ORDINARY AND NONE OF THEM A DEVICE: Hester Ollerton, forty-four, who keeps a bakehouse four miles up that cart road, and is new in this band; a man of about fifty-two who is not Simon Rook, unnamed and unnamed still. Nobody else new was added in this band and no other new name was fixed in it.**
