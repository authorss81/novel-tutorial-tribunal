## 1l. What 661–670 changed

**NOBODY IN VOLUME 14 BAND 0002 WAS RELIEVED, FORGIVEN, REDEEMED OR THANKED. NOBODY IN IT IS A VILLAIN, AND THE BENCH THAT DECLARED A HEARING IS NOT ONE AND IS NOT RELIEVED AND NAMES NO KEEPER, AND A BEAM THAT MOVES WITH NOBODY IN THE ROOM IS NOT ONE AND WAS NOT NAMED AND NO FACE WAS PUT ON IT AND NO MOTIVE WAS ASSIGNED TO IT, AND IN THIS BAND A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE SAYS OUT LOUD THAT HE IS NOT GOING TO SUPPLY A FIFTH SENTENCE ABOUT IT.**

- **ILYAN VESTER, THIRTY-ONE.** He is nobody's. **He has been in the county of Kell `chapter − 554` days, one hundred and seven at `661` through one hundred and sixteen at `670`, and every one of the ten is said out loud in its own chapter with the subtraction spoken.** He read a list of nine jobs out loud on the Saturday and said before he began that he could do none of them, and **the eighth of the nine is a job that cannot be filled because there is no form in that county that says a person may be sent for, and it is a job he wants.** He offered to be the thing that is sent for on the Sunday, out loud, in front of about eleven people, and was refused in about eight sentences by a man who cannot see well, and the refusal was the volume's own thesis said back to him. **He opened his mouth to argue a woman of forty out of her no and did not, and said out loud why, and the reason was that a thing that is sent for is a thing people are sent for about and she is not the instrument of anything.** He asked a man of sixty-one to refuse a division so that no true number about eleven people would exist, and was told that if it were about the coalition then nine people would owe a day and nobody would owe a name, and that he is the one person in that bank who cannot be given a day at all. He said the same want six times in a week with no number in it after the Wednesday, and he said out loud that he had not once put a number in it since. **He tried three names for a place about nine feet past the last beam and was stopped three times, and the third stop was by a man of fifty-four who cannot read, and he said out loud that the three are in about nine mouths by morning and that he cannot get any of them back and that it is the first thing he has said out loud in about nine days that he cannot take.** He said the band's own question a second time at `669` and said he is not going to answer it and that the answer he was given about a door is not an answer to it. **The count of times he has said the true of one half of a thing stopped at the twenty-third and HE SAID OUT LOUD THAT HE IS NOT COUNTING IT ANY MORE AND WHY, AND THE COUNT CARRIES NO DAY-COUNT AND NO ORDINAL IN THIS BAND.** He is still nobody's and there is still no column, no form, no rate and no hundred in that county, and `citizen` is at 0.
- **NEVIN TRASK, SIXTY-ONE,** who has kept the book of heads and carts eleven years. **He read the nine out loud at `670` with the reason each person gave and said he was not giving a number, because a mouth is not a figure of anything.** He refused to say how many of a coalition there are, in about six sentences, and gave the reason that a number becomes the thing and the people become the figure. He said what a coalition is and applied a man of sixty-eight's sentence about a Crown that binds nobody to it out loud. He gave the cost of it as a day and not as money. **He did the division he refused at `659` and did not refuse it this time, said why before he gave the number, and the reason is worse than the last one and not better, and the number is about four hours each and forty-five is an hour over forty-four, and he said an hour over is the same kind of thing as a quarter of an hour under.** He said the figure is a figure of a mill and not of the woman of thirty-eight and not of her mother, and that there is no line in that book that will take it, and that it is the first number he has had in eleven years he could not put anywhere. **He did not cut a fifth line in the back of the last leaf and said where the fifth line is going to sit instead and that in about four years one of them will have to say out loud what that cost.** He gave the working on the door at `669`, one million three hundred and fourteen thousand hours divided by nine, and **mislast the number as one million three hundred and forty out loud, corrected it out loud in the next breath, and said he had done it twice and that about four of the people standing about had noticed.** He said he was not going to be a man who is glad about a door, and that the gladness is what will be sent for about in about four years. **HE IS NOT RELIEVED AND HE IS NOT THE AUTHOR OF ANYTHING.**
- **SERA QUILL, TWENTY-EIGHT,** who cannot be sent for about in two counties, with two unread letters in a bag. **She has been in that county `chapter − 642` days, nineteen at `661` through twenty-eight at `670`, and every figure this band gives for it is said out loud with the subtraction.** She asked to be told the cost of a coalition and not to be talked about, said so first, and then said it to the room, and gave the cost in the only unit she has: if she is one of nine in a county where nobody can be sent for about then a room four hundred miles off can say out loud that she was one of nine, and there is no form there either. **She is not one of the nine and said so, and she has not been in about nine feet of anything for a week, and she has been standing about nine feet off since the Friday of the hundred and sixteenth week, which is ten days at `670` and is said out loud with the subtraction, and everything she has said in about ten days has been about herself and not about the nine.** At `668` she said out loud that she had been listening since the Thursday for somebody to say that the two names were the same thing, that nobody had, and that she is not going to be the one who says it either, **and that it is not because it is not true.** **SHE IS NOT RELIEVED AND IS NOT THANKED FOR FOUR HUNDRED MILES.**
- **ABEL FENNER, SIXTY-SIX,** a sinker who digs for water. **He was asked to go down a third time at `661`, said no, took the no back in the same breath, and said neither yes nor no, and gave the reason, which is that he is the only person in four counties anybody can be sent for about a word he said in a gallery.** He came down on the Tuesday and said the reason he came was a narrowing and not a question, and he went past the narrowing for the first time in forty-one years and did not say a word about how that was, **and he has been down three times in three days, on the Tuesday, the Wednesday and the Thursday, and in five hundred and sixty-seven he said on a Monday that he had never been in seven of the nine steps under the water and that what is past the narrowing is not one of the seven, and that if he is wrong about it then he is the one who is wrong and not the water.** He said what a wet flight is for the third time anybody asked him and the third time had a new thing in it. **He gave the same four things in a gallery on the Monday of the hundred and fifteenth week, and again in a passage on the Wednesday of the hundred and seventeenth week, where he was asked twice in the same morning and answered the same way both times, and again on the bank at `668`, and at `668` he said out loud that that is three times, and nobody in that bank accepted them and nobody in that bank rejected them.** **HE IS NOT RELIEVED, AND HE IS STILL THE ONLY PERSON IN FOUR COUNTIES ANYBODY CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT THE WORD *LOOM*, AND HE IS STILL THE ONLY PERSON WHO CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT THE NINE WORDS.**
- **AVELINE MARSH, THIRTY-EIGHT,** who keeps a mill four miles off, about eleven people in four houses eat out of it and one of the eleven is her mother. **She was asked what she thinks she is keeping and answered, having said for about nine years that she did not want to be asked, and the answer is worse for everybody than the not-knowing was: she is not keeping a week, she is keeping the fact that the peg has a hole for it, and the not-knowing had a wheel in it and the knowing has her in it.** **She has come down four times since the Thursday of the hundred and fifteenth week, on which she said she would not come down again this year, and not five, and the fourth of them is the Thursday of this band.** Her mill stopped four times in the band and forty-five hours of it is a figure of nobody and she is not part of that figure. She said yes in about four seconds when she was asked to go down a fourth time and a man of thirty-one noticed the four seconds and said he was not going to make anything of them. She is one of the nine and is one of the four who said they would not first. **SHE IS NOT RELIEVED AND WAS NOT THANKED FOR THE MILL AND WAS NOT ASKED.**
- **WENNA CALLOW, FORTY,** who has asked for a day against a name for about nine weeks. **She refused the cost of a coalition in about four things for a reason about nine people and not about the man of thirty-one. She then said no to going down a second time, out loud, in front of about nine people, to a request that was made fairly, in nine sentences that she counted herself and that the instrument's own splitter also returns nine, and the reason is that a coalition of nine with no seal and no list becomes in about four years a thing with one person in it that everything goes through.** Nobody argued her out of it, nobody thanked her, and nobody said the word. **She then said a name out loud, flat, once, on a Saturday, and gave the reason that she was the one a room looks at after a no, and the cost fell on her and not on the man of thirty-one: she is now the second person in this band somebody can be sent for about a name, and she is the one who has spent nine weeks asking for a name to be used against her.** She is not one of the nine. **SHE IS NOT RELIEVED AND IS NOT A VILLAIN AND NOBODY CALLED HER ANYTHING.**
- **SIMON ROOK, ABOUT FIFTY-TWO,** who cannot see well. **He refused the man of thirty-one for the second time in about four days, in about eight sentences, and said he was not going to give a new reason, and gave the same one: a thing that is sent for is a thing people are sent for about, and in about four years there will be a form with a name at the top of it and it will be a man of thirty-one who asked for it out loud in front of witnesses, which is the worst way of all of them to be one. He is not one of the nine and has not said he will be near it. HE IS NOT RELIEVED.**
- **BARNABY CROVE, FIFTY-FOUR,** who holds the gravel and cannot read. **He said it six times in about nine days, and in this band he said it in a gallery in the dark with a hand on somebody else's coat, and he said in that same breath that a coat is a thing a person can be sent for about.** He stopped the man of thirty-one from saying a fourth name for the place past the last beam, and gave the reason himself: three names in three sentences in a passage with a lamp in it are in about nine mouths on the bank by morning, and he is the hand in that room that cannot write, **and a man who cannot read has just kept a place out of a list, and that is the whole of what he is.** He is one of the nine and is one of the four who said they would not first. **HE IS NOT A DEVICE AND HE IS NOT RELIEVED AND HE IS NOT THANKED.**
- **THE MAN OF FORTY-ONE WHO KEEPS TWO CARTS,** who is named for the doing and not for the wanting. **He said what a road is after abolition, and it is a figure of carts and not of a rule. He offered to do the bailing before anybody asked him, bailed nine steps under about four feet of water for about four hours and a quarter on the first descent, about two hours on the second and about two hours and a quarter on the third, which is about eight hours and a half in three days, and said the reason in about five sentences and the reason was that it is the same work as a wet load.** He said out loud that a load of stone is not going up that track on the Monday and that a Monday that somebody else does not get is the whole of it, and that he does not know whose Monday. He said at `669` that eight hands is not better than two hands and that nine people are not better than two people and that it is about how many hands are on it and not about how many people agree to it, and that somebody has to keep coming. **HE WAS NOT THANKED AND HE DID NOT WAIT TO BE.**
- **THE BOY OF THIRTEEN,** who is not a head in the book of heads and carts because the penny a head is on everybody over sixteen. **He found the two people who lift the board at about the seventh hour on the first descent and he is not one of the people who went down, and he said nobody sent him and nobody asked him.** He has walked four miles with a barrow since he was nine, four miles is about two hours, and he went down once, on the Friday of the hundred and fifteenth week, and has not gone down since, which is thirteen days at `666`, sixteen at `669` and seventeen at `670`. He did a sum of his own out loud, unasked, and said that a working day of a boy of thirteen is a figure of nobody and that he is not going to pretend he is better than the man of thirty-one. **He is one of the nine and is one of the four who said they would not first, on the strength of four miles twice a day and not on the strength of a descent, and a man of sixty-one said that out loud so that nobody could smooth it later.**
- **CORVIN TACE, FORTY-THREE,** who keeps the landing nine miles up that river nineteen years. **He corrected the band's own standing saying out loud twice, with the working: the next time about ninety steps can be walked is about four days after that bank is full, and not the next time that block of limestone comes out, because the water in those steps comes out of a drain and not off the top of a river, and the bank has a person in it and the stone has nobody in it at all.** He gave the reading in inches four times in the band with the subtraction spoken, and said he has been asked about the crossing four times in about nine days by four different people and the answer is the same three words. **HE IS NOT RELIEVED AND HE IS NOT OFFERED ANYTHING FOR IT.**
- **PELL VARN, SIXTY-EIGHT,** who keeps the last quarter mile. **He is not in this band except in a sentence another man says about him, and the sentence is applied to a coalition and he is not asked about it.** His rule's age is not printed in this band and if it is used the subtraction must be spoken.
- **ORLA FENNIMORE, FORTY-FOUR,** who keeps the chairs book nineteen years for thirteen shillings and fourpence a year. **She said a list is a column and that a column in that county is a thing people get put in inside about four years, and she made no fifth refusal and said she had said no three times to putting a hearing in that book.** She said out loud that a person who cannot be asked has not refused anything and that it is not a discovery. She said out loud that two is the correct number of times to say one thing. **SHE IS NOT RELIEVED AND IS NOT THANKED FOR ANYTHING.**
- **GARRIN TOLLEY, THIRTY-EIGHT,** who may carry the board of thirty-two notches. **He took his lamp down on all three descents and he did not bail once in this band, and the bailing is a man of forty-one's, and he said that out loud on a bank twice, and nobody thanked him for the lamp, and the man of thirty-one noticed that the man of forty-one did not wait to be thanked and said so out loud.**
- **IVO KELLOW, THIRTY-FOUR,** in the ash on the low side of that landing, on no board and in no book. **He has been in it thirty-one days at `661`, thirty-three at `663` and thirty-nine at `669`, each spoken with the subtraction out of `630`. He said one word on the Friday of the hundred and sixteenth week and has said nothing since, and a man of sixty-one said out loud that he is not going to make him into a reason for anything and that there is no form in that county that says a person may be sent for about whether he has finished. Nobody in this band stopped at that doorway.** He is not one of the nine and nobody asked him.
- **NELL PRINCE, TWENTY-NINE,** in the reed. **Thirty-two days at `661`, thirty-four at `663` and forty at `669`, each spoken with the subtraction out of `629`. She said one word in the hundred and sixteenth week and has said nothing since. A man of sixty-one said out loud that he is not going to add the two numbers together and did not.**
- **THE WOMAN OF FORTY-NINE WHO CAME DOWN THAT ROAD TWICE,** who does not know what a copy is. **She said so three times in the band and said she is not going to pretend she is difficult about it, and compared a written thing to a fence post in front of about nine people, and she is one of the nine and is named by the doing.**
- **THE NEW CAST OF VOLUME 14, ALL OF THEM ORDINARY AND NONE OF THEM A DEVICE: a man of about fifty-two who is not Simon Rook, unnamed and unnamed still; nobody else new was added in this band and no new name was fixed in it.**
