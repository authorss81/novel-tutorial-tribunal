## 1o. What 691–700 changed

**NOBODY IN VOLUME 14 BAND 0005 WAS RELIEVED, FORGIVEN, REDEEMED OR THANKED. NOBODY IN IT IS A VILLAIN, AND THE LAMP, THE STONE WITH NINE LINES ON IT, THE BEAM ABOUT NINE FEET LONG, THE GOING IN THE WALL AND THE CHAMBER BEHIND IT ARE NONE OF THEM ONE. AND NOBODY IN THE BAND SAYS THAT A PAIN WAS WORTH IT AND NOBODY COMFORTS THE MAN WHO PAID IT. - **ILYAN VESTER, THIRTY-ONE.** **He is nobody's, and there is no column, no form, no rate and no hundred in that county, and `citizen` is at 0.** He has been in the county of Kell `chapter − 554` days, one hundred and thirty-seven at `691` through one hundred and forty-six at `700`, and three of the ten figures are his own with the subtraction spoken. **He has a cut across his right palm that is fifteen days old at `700` and there is no figure for what it cost and nobody in the band has given him one, including a man of fifty-four who cannot read, who said on the Friday of the hundred and twentieth week that he was not going to be asked a fourth time and gave the figure once more and then stopped counting.** He has lost the way he came back on and said so himself in a passage at `686` and did not say it again in this band, and the loss is not refunded.
  **HIS MISTAKES IN THIS BAND ARE FOUR AND HE OWNS EVERY ONE OF THEM IN HIS OWN MOUTH.** At `691` he took seven doors in nine days and sixty-one days of a man in an ash and divided one by the other to make a rate, and a man of sixty-one took it apart in public and he took it back in the next breath. At `692` he said *somebody is still doing it* and a man of thirty-eight took it off him in four sentences and he took it back in one. At `696` he said a cut about as deep as a nail is a function and a man of sixty-six took it off him in two sentences and he took it back. At `698` he tried to hold the ninth of the nine and a woman of twenty-eight stopped him in three sentences and a man of fifty-four who cannot read stopped him in six more, and he gave his board to a man of thirty-eight. **AND AT `699` HE PUT A ROOM INTO A SENTENCE THAT DID NOT NAME ONE, AND HE SAID SO HIMSELF AND HE SAID THAT A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR HAD SAID THE SAME ABOUT A SPACE IN A WALL IN A DIFFERENT SHAPE FOUR DAYS EARLIER.**
  **AND AT `700` SOMETHING THAT IS NOT A PERSON ASKED HIM IN PLAIN WORDS WHETHER HE WOULD WRITE IT, AND HE DID NOT ANSWER IT, AND HE SAID OUT LOUD WHY HE WAS NOT GOING TO ANSWER IT: HE IS NOBODY'S, THERE IS NO FORM IN THAT COUNTY THAT SAYS A MAN MAY ANSWER A QUESTION LIKE THAT AND NO FORM THAT SAYS HE MAY NOT, AND ONE OF THOSE IS WHY.** He said he was not going to answer and was not going to say he had not understood it and was not going to say he needed a week. He said nobody on that bank said no and nobody said yes and a man of thirty-one did not open his mouth and there is no word in that county for that and he is not going to make one. **HE SAID HE WOULD LET ANYBODY SAY IT WAS WORTH IT AND WOULD NOT HAVE SAID IT, AND NOBODY SAID IT.**
  **HE ASKED WHAT ABOUT NINE PEOPLE CAN HOLD NOBODY IN THIS BAND. HE SAID THE COUNT OF TIMES A MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT HAS SAID A THING IN ADVANCE AND BEEN RIGHT IS A COUNT OF TIMES AND HE REFUSED IT HIMSELF. - **NEVIN TRASK, SIXTY-ONE,** who has kept the book of heads and carts eleven years and has five lines on the leaf after the last head. **HE GAVE THE ASH AND THE REED COUNTS EVERY MORNING AND HE HAS NOW SAID HE HAS NOTHING TO SAY ABOUT A NIGHT FOUR TIMES AND ON THE WEDNESDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SECOND WEEK HE SAID IT A FIFTH TIME AND THEN SAID HE WAS GOING TO STOP SAYING IT, AND THAT THE TWO FIGURES HE HAS GIVEN OUT LOUD IN ABOUT SIX WEEKS ARE THE ONLY TWO THAT ARE HIS.** He gave his own days in the county at `696` and `700` with the subtraction, and gave the woman of twenty-eight's fifty-eight days, and gave the boy of thirteen's figure, and did not add any of them to anything. **HE COUNTED A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE'S SPEECH ON HIS OWN KNEE IN THE WIND AND GAVE THE COUNT OUT LOUD BEFORE ANYBODY ASKED HIM, AND HE SAID HE WAS NOT ASHAMED OF IT IN FRONT OF THOSE PEOPLE, AND THE COUNT IS TWELVE. - **CORVIN TACE, FORTY-THREE,** who keeps the landing nineteen years. **HE FOUND OUT ON THE FIFTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FIRST WEEK THAT HE HAD BEEN GIVING A FIGURE OF A MAN IN AN ASH EVERY MORNING FOR SIXTY-SIX MORNINGS AND HAD NEVER ONCE ASKED HIM WHAT HE WAS DOING IN IT, AND HE SAID THAT OUT LOUD BEFORE ANYBODY HAD ASKED HIM, AND HE SAID THAT A FIGURE OF A MAN IN A TREE IS NOT A FIGURE OF ANYTHING EXCEPT THE COUNT.** He gave the reading every morning and stopped after it, and he said the working out loud, and he said a bank fills in about four days and empties in about three and that both of those are figures he gave the bank and neither of them is his. - **GARRIN TOLLEY, THIRTY-EIGHT.** **THE SECOND THURSDAY ARRIVED AND HIS SHOULDER WAS NO GOOD AND HE DID NOT GO DOWN, AND NOBODY TOLD HIM HE HAD BEEN RIGHT AND NOBODY TOLD HIM HE HAD NOT.** He asked for the count of the times he has said a thing in advance and had it come true and a man of sixty-one refused it in seven sentences, and he accepted the refusal and asked for nothing else. **TWO COPIES NINE MILES APART ARE NOT GOING TO BE READ FOR SEVEN DAYS, AND HE SAID THE TRUE OF IT: THE COPIES DO NOT KNOW, AND THERE IS NOBODY IN THAT COUNTY WHO COULD TELL THEM.** He held his lamp up for about a quarter of an hour on the Friday of the hundred and twenty-first week and got his shoulder back in four times. **HE HAD SEEN ABOUT A THUMB'S WIDTH OF LIGHT UNDER THE EIGHTH DOOR-STONE ON THE FIRST DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FIFTEENTH WEEK AND CARRIED IT ABOUT FOR SIX WEEKS AND SAID NOTHING, AND ON THE FRIDAY HE SAID IT OUT LOUD AND GAVE THE REASON AND ASKED FOR IT TO BE SAID THAT IT WAS NOT MODESTY.** He said a thing is a function and not a stone and that nobody is going to say what else could do it.
