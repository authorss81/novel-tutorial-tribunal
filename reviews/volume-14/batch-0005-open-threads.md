## 1J. THE THIRTEEN THREADS OF 691–700, AND EVERY ONE IS A PERSON DOING A THING

**A THREAD IS A THING A PERSON IS STILL DOING. A HAZARD IS NOT A THREAD. NONE OF THESE THIRTEEN WAS CLOSED BY THE BAND THAT OPENED IT, AND NONE OF THE THIRTEEN OF 681–690 WAS CLOSED BY IT EITHER, EXCEPT THE ONE THAT WAS A PREDICTION: the second Thursday, which a man of thirty-eight said on the Thursday of the hundred and twentieth week was seven days off and would be the same, and which arrived at `694` and was the same, and was not a vindication and was not a proof and was not told to anybody.**

1. **A CUT ACROSS THE RIGHT PALM OF A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE, FROM THE BASE OF THE THUMB TO ABOUT THE FIRST JOINT, AND IT IS FIFTEEN DAYS OLD AT `700` AND HAS NO FIGURE FOR WHAT IT COST.** A man of fifty-four who cannot read counted it three times in nine days, gave the figure three times with the subtraction, and then said he was not going to be asked a fourth time this week and stopped. **Nobody in 691–700 says it will keep or that it will not keep or that it is not much, nobody says it was worth it, and nobody is comforted in any chapter of the band.** `685`, `687`, `690`, `694`, `700`
2. **THE WAY BACK HE HAD SINCE THE EVENING OF THE WEDNESDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FIRST WEEK, WHICH IS GONE, AND WHICH HE SAID HIMSELF IN A PASSAGE AT `686`.** There is no figure for what it was and nobody is going to be given one. What is left is about three inches of loose gravel and about one boot of walked place in about a hundred and thirty feet of dry passage, and a man of thirty-eight measured it with his own arm and it has not moved since. `686`, `700`
3. **NINE TERMS, WHICH ARE NINE JOBS WITH A NAME ON EACH, FOUR OF THE NINE JOBS ARE BACK AND FIVE OF THEM ARE NOT DONE AND NOT ONE OF THE NINE HAS A NAME ON IT.** The first was put back together on the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-first week out of five things in that bank, and the other three of the four were done after it in about four days, and on the last day of that week a man of forty-one gave the count: nine openings, nine jobs, four of the nine put back together, five of them not done, and not one name. **Nine and nothing is not a sum and it was not added to anything, and not one name is not a smaller number than one name but the other kind of number. THE FIVE OF THE NINE THAT ARE NOT DONE ARE STILL OPEN WORK AND NOBODY ON THAT BANK HAS SEEN A MOUTH PUT ONE OF THEM TOGETHER. `693`, `698`, `700`**
4. **EIGHT DOORS, SEVEN ASKED IN NINE DAYS, ONE NAME, AND THAT NAME IS A MAN WHO HAS BEEN DEAD ABOUT THIRTY YEARS — PLUS A GATE IN A VILLAGE HE HAD NEVER BEEN INSIDE, AND A WALL FOUR DAYS ON WITH A HOLE IN IT ABOUT THE HEIGHT OF A MAN.** The wall came from a man of sixty-three who mends dry stone, who was about nine when he was taken to look at it and has not been back and is not going to. **A man of thirty-eight refused to walk four days to look at a hole in a wall, and nobody on that bank can be sent for about any of it.** `691`
5. **A WOMAN OF FORTY-FOUR WHO KEEPS A BAKHOUSE FOUR MILES UP THAT CART ROAD HAS NOW BEEN ASKED TWICE IN ELEVEN DAYS, AND BOTH ASKING WERE ABOUT A DOOR NINE MILES UNDER A RIVER AND NEITHER WAS ABOUT HER.** She gave the two out loud as a figure and said she is not going to say which of the two cost more, and she is not relieved, and she said the next time one of them comes up that road about a door, come to this door. `691`, `693`
6. **TWO WORN PLACES IN THE FLOOR OF THE NINTH ROOM, ONE OF THEM HIS AND ONE OF THEM ABOUT AS DEEP AS THE THICKNESS OF HIS OWN FINGER, AND A MAN OF SIXTY-SIX PUT HIS THUMB IN IT AND SAID THERE ARE TWO OF THOSE AND ONLY TWO AND CHOSE NEITHER.** Either about four hundred years or somebody this morning. **About four people were asked to be able to tell in nine days that nobody chose, and a man of thirty-eight went on with the ninth of nine and did not stand in it.** `692`
7. **ABOUT A THUMB'S WIDTH OF LIGHT UNDER THE EIGHTH DOOR-STONE, SEEN ON THE FIRST DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FIFTEENTH WEEK AND CARRIED ABOUT FOR SIX WEEKS BY A MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT BEFORE HE SAID IT OUT LOUD ON A FULL BANK.** He still does not know what it is. **He is not a keeper of it and he did not say he was right first.** `695`
8. **THE COUNT OF THE TIMES A MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT HAS SAID A THING IN ADVANCE AND HAD IT COME TRUE, WHICH HE ASKED FOR ON THE THURSDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FIRST WEEK AND WHICH WAS REFUSED IN SEVEN SENTENCES, BECAUSE IT IS A COUNT OF TIMES AND IT WOULD BE A FIGURE OF A MAN.** The first one was the shoulder, and the second one arrived and was the same, and nobody told him he was right. `687`, `694`
9. **NINE BOARDS WITH NO ORIGINAL AND NO KEEPER, BURNED INTO BY A STICK AND CARRIED OUT OF THE COUNTY BY ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, AND A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR WHO CANNOT READ WHO HAS THE EIGHTH OF THEM INSIDE HIS COAT AND HAS NOT ASKED ANYBODY TO READ IT TO HIM.** A man of sixty-one refused the ninth and gave the reason: he is not going to carry a copy of it in the inside of a coat, where he would be a man in a coat with a thing in him. **A woman of twenty-eight made all nine, was not asked, and has not refused, and said a person who has not refused has not said no either.** `698`
10. **A BOARD UNDER THE COAT OF A MAN OF FORTY-THREE, WITH ABOUT FOUR INCHES OF ONE END BURNT OFF IT AND A CUT IN IT ABOUT AS DEEP AS A MAN'S OWN NAIL, WHICH IS A REMAINDER AND NOT A COPY AND IS NOT WHOLE.** A man of thirty-four carried it before the first day and for every one of the seventy days he has been in an ash, and nobody put it there, and it went to the man who counted him and not to a man with a book. `696`
11. **A STONE ABOUT THE SIZE OF TWO HANDS LAID FLAT WITH NINE LINES IN IT, IN NINE HANDS THAT CANNOT WRITE, THE NINTH CUT ON THE THURSDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND EIGHTEENTH WEEK, AND NOT ONE WORD OF IT HAS BEEN READ BY ANYBODY IN FOUR COUNTIES. AND A LAMP BURNING ON A HOOK IN THE NINTH OF NINE ROOMS, ABOUT A QUARTER FULL OF OIL, WHICH WAS NOT LIT AND WAS NOT PUT OUT.** `681`, `689`, `699`, `700`
12. **A THING ABOUT NINE FEET LONG THAT GOES ALONG ABOUT TWO INCHES AND COMES BACK WHILE THERE IS A PERSON ON THE ABOUT NINETY STEPS, WHICH WENT ALONG TWICE IN THIS BAND AND WAS NOT NAMED, NOT PERSONIFIED AND GIVEN NO MOTIVE, AND WHICH ON THE DAY A SENTENCE CAME BACK IN THAT GALLERY WAS WHERE IT IS AND NOTHING WHATEVER HAPPENED.** `686`, `689`, `699`
13. **A CHAMBER BEHIND THE WALL IN THE NINTH ROOM THAT WAS SEALED, AND A DOOR-STONE ABOUT FIVE FEET HIGH THAT TWO PEOPLE PUT THEIR HANDS ON AND THAT DID NOT MOVE, WITH ABOUT AN INCH OF WATER UNDER IT, THE SAME AS IT WAS NINE DAYS EARLIER — AND A QUESTION A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE WAS ASKED ON A WEDNESDAY MORNING BY SOMETHING THAT IS NOT A PERSON, IN PLAIN WORDS, WHETHER HE WOULD WRITE IT, WHICH HE DID NOT ANSWER, AND WHICH NOBODY ON THAT BANK ANSWERED FOR HIM.** Nothing fell down. The roof is where it was, the shelf is empty, and the lamp is burning behind a door that will not open. **A sealed door is not the right of refusal and a chamber that is not kept is not a grave, and both of those were said out loud, and the right of refusal is unrestored and is given to nobody.** `699`, `700`

**AND THE FIGURES THAT TRAVEL WITH THE THIRTEEN, AND NONE OF THEM IS SETTLED: the water came off the top step in the night four times in this band and a man of forty-three has said he has nothing to say about a night five times and has then stopped saying it, and **the ladder of water readings is at `state/continuity.md` §0O.9 and it reconciled against the rate the band inherited WITHOUT A REPAIR, at about four inches on a morning from one to four and sixteen inches at four days and holding at five and six**; the pair of figures and what the water does have come apart and he has asked about four people to decide between them themselves; about nine people in four counties cannot be sent for about; a man of thirty-one is nobody's and has offered to be the thing that is sent for four times and been refused four times and has paid a hand for a going; about three inches of loose gravel and one boot of walked place in a hundred and thirty feet; seven doors asked in nine days and one name out of them and that name thirty years dead; four of the nine put back together and five of them not done and not one name; sixty-two feet and sixty-three feet for the same place and a foot between them; six inches of a night, of a water, and forty years of a man in an ash and seventy of another; and `citizen` is at 0.**
