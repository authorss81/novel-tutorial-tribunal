# Volume 14, Band 0004 — §1n of `state/character-state.md`, the whole cast of Chapters 681–690,
MOVED WHOLE, NOT ONE WORD CUT, at the writing of Volume 14 Band 0005, because that file stood at 63,352
bytes against a hard cap of 60,000 when §1o had to go in above it. It is the whole of what the fourth band of
Volume 14 did to the people, including the paid scar and the lost way back. Nothing was summarised and nothing
was cut. It is still canon and §1o does not contradict it.

**NOBODY IN VOLUME 14 BAND 0004 WAS RELIEVED, FORGIVEN, REDEEMED OR THANKED. NOBODY IN IT IS A VILLAIN, AND THE LAMP, THE STONE, THE GOING IN THE WALL AND THE THING ABOUT NINE FEET LONG ARE NONE OF THEM ONE, AND THE FIRST WITNESS IS NOT NAMED AT ALL IN THE BAND.**

- **ILYAN VESTER, THIRTY-ONE.** **He has a cut across his right hand and he has lost the way he came back on, and he says both of them himself before anybody else describes either.** He is nobody's, and there is no column, no form, no rate and no hundred in that county, and `citizen` is at 0. He has been in the county of Kell `chapter − 554` days, one hundred and twenty-seven at `681` through one hundred and thirty-six at `690`, and each of the two figures he gives himself is said with the subtraction. **He put his hand on a going in a wall where no going has ever gone, on the first day of the hundred and twentieth week, and said out loud before he did it that he was not going to say a word to it and why: a man who cannot see well said a name to a stone at `677` and nothing whatever happened, and he is not going to be the second man to say a word to a wall.** He went through and came out in the same room with the cut there, held the hand up in front of a lamp and said nothing at all about it for about as long as it takes a man of forty-one to push a stone nine feet, and then said what it was not: not a burn, not a cut he has seen anybody make, and no word for it in this county. **He said out loud that nobody is to say that it will keep, or that it will not keep, or that it is not much, because there is no figure for any of those three.** He said he did not offer the hand and nobody offered it to him. **He said he is not going to say it was worth it and he is not going to say it was not worth it, and that if anybody on that bank says either he is going to let them and it will have been said and he will not have said it.** Nobody did. **On the Wednesday he said out loud, standing on the gravel, that he has had a way back since the Wednesday of the hundred and first week and does not have it now, and that there is no figure for what it was and no person in four counties who was keeping it and nobody is going to be given one.** He refused to be the somebody who tells about nine people, refused to be sent for, and asked what about nine people can hold for the third time and got an answer with two halves. He counted six nines and one eight out loud, said the total was sixty-two and would not give it, and said at the end that there are nine of the terms and not one of them has been got back and it is not the work of this week and he is not going to say sorry for it. **HE IS STILL NOBODY'S AND HE HAS PAID SOMETHING HE DID NOT OFFER.**
- **NEVIN TRASK, SIXTY-ONE,** who has kept the book of heads and carts eleven years and was the fifth person anybody sent for about the nine spaces. **He refused it three times in ten sentences and gave three reasons out loud, and the third reason was that the day a keeper writes down things he has been told about in a place he has not been, the book stops being a book of what happened and becomes a book of what somebody said happened, and he has not got eleven years left to find out which of those two he has been keeping.** He gave the days in the county in four of the ten chapters with the subtraction, gave the difference between 133 and 132 out loud and said that nobody has ever argued with him about it, and refused to put either number in the book. **HE WROTE ONE NUMBER IN THE BOOK THIS BAND AND IT IS THE FIRST NUMBER HE HAS WRITTEN IN IT IN ELEVEN YEARS: nine, on the leaf after the last head, where there had been four lines and there are now five. HE ANNOUNCED IT THREE DAYS BEFORE HE DID IT AND SAID HE WOULD HAVE PREFERRED A TUESDAY.** He said what he will not write next to it — nine people, because there are eight, and a book with a nine in it and eight names against it is a list of what is missing — and said the count of six nines is a figure of how many times the word was said in a room and refused to add them to anything. **HE DID NOT START COUNTING THE LOCAL HEARING AGAIN AND HE SAID IT IS NOT GOING TO MOVE ON A SUNDAY.**
- **SIMON ROOK, ABOUT FIFTY-TWO, WHO CANNOT SEE WELL.** **He is not one of the nine, he is not going to be a keeper, and this band he said the thing about himself that nobody asked him for: a woman of forty-four is going to have her week made worse by a man going up her road and asking her a question instead of coming to her door and telling her, and there is no form in this county that says a person may be sent for about that.** He said he knows what being sent for about is because he has been the fifth line of two sheets of deal for eleven years and nobody has ever asked him what his line was for, and that he is the only person on that bank who can say what it costs and is not going to be asked. He said he is not going to be the one who goes to her door. **He has said that sentence three times in this volume and the count of the three was given by other people and not by him. HE WAS NOT THANKED AND HE WAS NOT FORGIVEN AND HE DID NOT ASK.**
- **THE MAN OF FORTY-ONE WHO KEEPS TWO CARTS.** **He said the plainest definition anybody has given in that county in about nine sentences — a term is a job with a name on it — and said he had got no better one and was not going to look for one.** He said the name is not missing off the stone, that the space is cut and cut square and cut old, and that he is the ninth of nine and has been it for nineteen years and nobody ever told him there was a place for his name. **He gave the band's mechanic in eight sentences — a groove is a route and a route is a going and a coming — and then a woman of forty-nine gave the word blank out of a bakehouse rack and he gave her the credit for it in front of about nine people.** He went round eight doors in a week and got one name out of seven doors opened, and the name is a man who has been dead about thirty years. **He said he knew he was going to use her and he said he was sorry on no day but a Wednesday and that it was a Sunday, and that there is no form that says he may and no form that says he may not, and asked for both sentences to be left where they are.** He said on the Wednesday that he could not get his cart up about two hundred yards of the cart road for the ninth morning and has no reason and is not going to be given one. **He is going into a gate on the Monday after this band that he has never been inside.**
- **GARRIN TOLLEY, THIRTY-EIGHT.** **He measured the scar with his own hand against a groove in a wall and said it is about as long and would not round it and would not say it was exactly that, because the groove has a notch in it and the hand has not.** He measured the walked gravel with his own arm and said it is about three inches where there was about a foot, and that the walked place in it is about as wide as one boot and not two. **He said out loud on the Thursday of the hundred and twentieth week that his shoulder was no good for carrying a pole up that water on a Thursday, having said it in advance on the Tuesday of the hundred and nineteenth week at `678`, and he was right about that first Thursday, and he did not go down that morning, and at `687` he said the second one is seven days off and that it will be the same and that he has not asked anybody to take his word for that part — the second Thursday is `694` and `687` does not assert it and no chapter may.** He said a Thursday is reading two copies nine miles apart from about the fourth hour to about the ninth and that he cannot lift his right hand above his shoulder this morning, and that there is no form that says a man may be sent for about a shoulder and no form that says he may not. **HE WAS NOT THANKED.**
- **AVELINE MARSH, THIRTY-EIGHT,** who keeps a mill four miles off that landing and about eleven people in four houses eat out of it. **She said a mill is a keeping and that it grinds on the seventh morning because somebody turned the handle on the sixth, and nobody has to be told because nobody is in the room. She said that if a thing down there is a route because a person goes along it and comes back on it, then three inches of loose gravel and one boot of walked place is a mill with nobody in it. She said she is not a keeper of it and that she is not going to say it again this week.** SHE IS NOT RELIEVED.
- **HESTER OLLERTON, FORTY-FOUR,** who keeps a bakehouse four miles up a cart road. **She was asked on the Saturday at about the eighth hour and the cost of it arrived on the Sunday morning, and she gave the working out loud: about a quarter of an hour, a cart that could not pass a cart because there is a wall on the north side of that road for about forty yards, forty loaves at about the tenth hour instead of about the seventh, and two hours and three quarters of a cart for one morning of bread, which is the same figure she gave on the Friday of the hundred and eighteenth week and which she has not improved.** About nine houses got it a day late, about four of the nine cannot do a day without it, and one of those four has a Thursday four days off that Sunday morning. **She said the cost she is going to talk about is not the bread. She said being told: twice in nine days. Being asked: once, and it is a question about a door nine miles under a river and not about her. And there have been about nineteen years of neither. She said she is not going to tell anybody which of those three is the one she would have given back.** SHE WAS NOT ASKED WHETHER SHE WANTED TO HEAR IT, SHE IS NOT RELIEVED, AND SHE WAS NOT THANKED.
- **BARNABY CROVE, FIFTY-FOUR,** who holds the gravel and cannot read. **He found the first of the nine missing terms.** Between the two hands on every one of the nine marks there is a space about as wide as his thumb, cut square, cut to the depth of the hands, old, and not a mark anybody makes. He said he cannot tell anybody what is in it and cannot tell anybody whether anything was ever in it. He said the count of him stopping a man of thirty-one is five in five days and that he is stopping because the man of thirty-one has stopped, and he asked for the five instead of the thanks. **He said that a man who cannot read is the only person who can be sent for about a wet place about as wide as one boot, because every one of the others can be shown the other widths and can count them; and that if the book is written he will not know what it says and neither will anybody who comes after him.** He said he asked a woman of twenty-eight on the Saturday what she was going to do about it and reported her answer, which is that there is no figure for it and there is not going to be one. HE IS NOT A DEVICE AND HE IS NOT RELIEVED AND HE WAS NOT THANKED.
- **SERA QUILL, TWENTY-EIGHT,** who cannot be sent for about in two counties. **She found out, without being asked to look, that the scar on a man's hand is dry, that the skin round it has not gone white and has not gone soft and the palm has not come up, and she said she is not going to be a keeper of it and is not going to be asked about it.** She wrote one sentence in a day book that had had nothing but numbers in it for about nine days, in her own hand, with no other hand between, and said she broke her own rule and is not going to say what the rule was for. She gave her own count, forty-five days, and his, one hundred and thirty-three, both with the subtraction. **She checked the lamp-and-the-record thing a third time at `681`, a fourth at `687` and a fifth at `690` and did not say it, and said each time that she is not going to be the one who says it and that it is not because it is not true.** SHE IS NOT RELIEVED AND IS NOT THANKED FOR FOUR HUNDRED MILES, AND HER TWO LETTERS ARE NOT READ.
- **CORVIN TACE, FORTY-THREE,** who keeps the landing nineteen years. **He stopped saying the pair of figures out loud in the middle of this band and gave the reading every morning and stopped after it, and said the pair of figures and what that water does have come apart and asked about four people to decide between them themselves.** He refused, twice, to give a figure for a night, and said he was on that landing at the second hour and there is nothing else to do at the second hour. He counted the man in the ash at fifty-one and the woman in the reed at fifty-two on the Friday and at sixty and sixty-one on the Sunday and did not add them. **HE IS NOT GLAD ABOUT ANYTHING AND THERE IS STILL NO FIGURE IN FOUR COUNTIES FOR WHEN THE BLOCK OF LIMESTONE COMES OUT.**
- **ABEL FENNER, SIXTY-SIX,** a sinker, and the only person in four counties anybody can be sent for about the word *loom*. **He put his hand flat on the back wall of the ninth room and said it was not a wall, it was a hole that has been filled, and gave the three reasons: the colour is wrong, the joint is wrong, and the stone behind the facing is not the stone of that wall and he has never in forty-one years seen the two stones together anywhere. He refused a fourth thing nobody asked him, which is when it was filled and by whom.** HE DID NOT SAY THE WORD AGAIN.
- **ORLA FENNIMORE, FORTY-FOUR,** who keeps a book of twenty-nine chairs nineteen years for thirteen shillings and fourpence a year. **She said a thing anybody can be sent for about is a thing worth having and that she is not going to send a keeper of a book to look at a wall in the dark with a lamp he does not own; and she let a man of thirty-one ask what about nine people can hold for the third time on one condition, which was that he say out loud why he was asking.** She said she would rather be wrong about herself than be right about herself. SHE IS NOT RELIEVED.
- **THE WOMAN OF FORTY-NINE,** who counts loaves, does not know what a copy is, and came down that road for the fourth time this band. **She said the word *blank* out loud, gave where she had had it for nineteen years and said it had never once been about a road, and said a rack is six boards and four shelves and about half the time one of the shelves has nothing on it at all.** She gave the days in the county at one hundred and twenty-eight with the subtraction. **She was asked for her nine in the counting at `689` and said no, and asked for it said that a person who cannot be asked is not the same as a person who has said no, and said she knows that and would like about four people to know that she knows it.** SHE IS ONE OF THE NINE AND IS NOT A DEVICE.
- **IVO KELLOW, THIRTY-FOUR,** in the ash, and **NELL PRINCE, TWENTY-NINE,** in the reed: sixty days and sixty-one days at `690`, neither added, and neither said anything in the band, and about nine people walked past both of those mouths about nine times a day and nobody stopped at either. THEY ARE NOT ONE OF THE NINE AND NOBODY ASKED THEM.
- **THE BOY OF THIRTEEN IS THIRTEEN.** He said thirty days at `683` and thirty-four at `687`, both with the subtraction, and he gave a count of times as well as a count of days and said which was which, and he counted nine times this week that he decided a thing and said nobody had told him to stop and nobody had told him to go on. HE IS NOT A DEVICE AND NOBODY SAID HE WAS.
- **THE NEW CAST OF VOLUME 14 IN THIS BAND IS ONE PERSON AND SHE IS OFF THE PAGE: A WOMAN OF ABOUT SIXTY WHO TOLD A MAN OF FORTY-ONE, WHEN SHE WAS A GIRL, THAT A MAN USED TO TELL HER HE WENT IN AND OUT OF A DOOR UNDER A BANK. She gave his name and he has been dead about thirty years, and he is the only thing the week of asking came to. `688`** No other new name was fixed in this band and no other name was invented in it.
