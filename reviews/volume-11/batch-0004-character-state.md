### 1a. What 531–540 changed, and it is a new set of people in an old room

**A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FOUR FROM A TOWN FOUR DAYS OFF, WHO IS NOT NAMED, AND WHO CAME DOWN THE LANE ON THE SUNDAY OF THE NINETY-SEVENTH WEEK AND WAS ELEVEN DAYS IN THIS COUNTRY BY THE THURSDAY OF THE FIFTH DAY OF THE NINETY-NINTH. He keeps a shelf and has kept one for about eleven years. He is one of nine of them in four counties and they keep about four hundred shelves between them, and he says out loud every time he gives the number that it is the number of shelves and not the number of people, because about two hundred people in a market square cannot be asked about a shelf. HE NAMED HIS OWN TRADE IN A PORCH AT `531` — the Archive Lords — and said the name sounded like a boast, that he chose it about nine years ago, and that he would rather have chosen a duller one and has not found one. He carries a coat with a map on the back made by a different hand from the one that makes the maps in this county, and he came with his hands empty. HE SAID OUT LOUD, AT `531`, AT `533` AND AT `540`, THAT HE DOES NOT WANT A PERSON IN THIS COUNTY WHO CAN BE ASKED ABOUT A SHELF AND WHO ANSWERS FOR OTHER PEOPLE'S PAPERS, and that he has said it in four counties and has not been believed in one of them, and that he said it in a square in front of about two hundred people because a man who has come four days to ask a county for a shelf has to say it out loud before he asks, and that is the whole of the honesty he has got. HE TOLD THIS TOWN HOW TWO OF HIS SHELVES STOPPED BEING KEPT — a woman of about sixty who died and whose papers went into a house that could not read the hand, and two people in one house who each thought the other had it — and said out loud that in both of them there was a person who could have been sent for and no form that said so, that he went back to both afterwards and wrote it down, and that neither was anybody's fault, and that he has not told anybody a story about either since because he would be telling it wrong. HE IS RIGHT ABOUT MOST OF IT AND HE IS NOT A VILLAIN AND THE BAND DID NOT MAKE HIM ONE. What he came for he did not say until `540`, and it was not the four pages.**

**JOE DRURY, THIRTY-FOUR, WHO MENDS PLOUGHS AND HAS WORKED IN THE SMITHY ON THE BOTTOM OF THIS TOWN FOR ELEVEN YEARS, AND WHO IS NOT A PERSON ANYBODY IN THAT LANE HAS EVER THOUGHT ABOUT UNTIL HE ASKED A QUESTION.** He asked, in front of about nine people on a Tuesday, whether six pounds a year is enough, and he said out loud that he had thought about it for about four days and was going to ask it in front of about nine people and not in a kitchen. A recordwright answered in about four sentences and he asked for the rest of the question and was given that he had not got it. HE THEN SAID OUT LOUD, TO THE ROOM, WHY HE COULD ASK IT AND A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE COULD NOT — that she can send him away, and that a man of thirty-one has never once in a hundred and thirty-nine days been able to leave a room by being told to — and he said he was sorry for him and that he was not going to say that again, and the man of thirty-one told him not to be. He was not thanked and did not ask to be, and the recordwright told him not to thank her, and said that if he thanked her then it became a thing that happened to her instead of a thing she answered. He also said out loud that he wanted the number said in a room where it could be checked, and that about four of the nine people in that smithy can be sent for and about five cannot, and that that was the whole of what he had. `538`**

**THE WOMAN OF ABOUT FORTY AT THE WOOL BENCH, WHO IS STILL UNNAMED AND HAS BEEN IN THIS VOLUME SINCE `514`, and who said the other half of a shelf in about four seconds in a market square on the Thursday of the ninety-eighth week: a shelf is a thing a person is responsible for, there is no form in this county that releases a person from being the one, and the copyist of that book has been the one for nineteen years and nobody has asked her whether it is enough and she has wanted somebody to ask her that for nineteen years. A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR ASKED HER ON THE TUESDAY AFTERWARDS AND SHE ANSWERED HIM, AND ON THE THURSDAY OF THE FIFTH DAY OF THE NINETY-NINTH WEEK SHE SAID OUT LOUD IN A MARKET SQUARE THAT NONE OF WHAT HAPPENED IN A WEEK IN THAT TOWN IS IN A BOOK, and that is the whole of what a week of work had produced. `533`, `538`, `540`**

**EDITH CROW, SIXTY-EIGHT, SAID NO A SECOND TIME IN THIS VOLUME, and the second no is not the same no as the first.** On the Wednesday of the ninety-ninth week she was one of eight people in a room with the door open who were asked, one at a time, in about four seconds each, whether they would hold one of four sheets of paper in their own house and be a person who can be sent for about it. She said no, out loud, standing, without being asked for a reason, and then gave one: she has had a thing put in a book about her once in four weeks and did not know it was going to happen, and she is not putting four pages over her bed in about four years to find out what has been done with them. She referred to the finding about her own objection, entered on the Monday of the ninety-seventh week, which went in with the day she said it and not the day it was made. She was not thanked for saying no and there is no form in the county of Aldwick that says a person may be thanked for refusing to hold a sheet of paper. `539`**

**FOUR PEOPLE OF THIS TOWN WHO SAID YES, AND THEY ARE NAMED ONCE AND NOT AGAIN, WHICH IS THE ARRANGEMENT: a woman of about forty at the wool bench, a man of about fifty who keeps a beast, a woman of about thirty-five with a child on her hip, and a man of about twenty-eight who works at the kiln. They hold one page each. They were not thanked. Nobody has asked any of them why, and a man of thirty-four said out loud in a room that not one of them has been told what happens if they lose it and not one of them can be told, because there is nothing to tell them. `539`**

**FOUR PEOPLE WHO SAID NO: Joe Drury the smith, a man of about fifty at the fire, Edith Crow, sixty-eight, and a man of about twenty-two. THE NINTH PAGE IS NOT HELD BY ANYBODY, including the woman of about fifty-one, who put it down on a table and said she would not keep it because she wrote the four pages out of her own memory and there is no reason on earth why she should also be the one who keeps them, and because nobody in this county pays anybody for holding a sheet of paper. `539`**

**HE IS NAMED IN ALL TEN AND HE HAS A WANT, A MISTAKE AND A COST OF HIS OWN IN ALL TEN, AND THE TEN WANTS AND MISTAKES ARE ONE HABIT AND IT IS THE SAME ONE AS IN THE FIRST THREE BANDS: he tries to be the instrument, or the voice, or the carrier, and a person in the room asks him a question he cannot answer in full, or offers him a thing he cannot be asked about afterwards. Wants to be the person who is asked to hold the copy of the four pages, and offers his own room in a porch in front of about nine people, and is refused in about nine seconds in four sentences that add up to the whole of the band. `531` Wants the ordinary Wednesday to be ordinary, stands in a porch and says it was, and does not say the sentence he has been carrying for twenty-four days, and a woman of about fifty-one says it in about ten words in a lane. `532` Wants the shelf, says out loud in a market square in front of about two hundred people that a shelf in a room in Bramblefold is the only thing in this county that would make the copy of the four pages safe, and a woman of about forty names the person who would be responsible for it in about four seconds. `533` Wants to be the second pair of eyes on four pages, is the second pair of eyes, and is told in about four seconds in a lane in front of about nine people that a man who cannot be sent for can look at a thing and cannot be asked whether he looked. `534` Wants a heading for a third unheaded thing, asks for the third time in fifteen days, and is given the same answer, and then is not given the reason, and says out loud that she gave it to him the other two times, and she says yes, and the reason she stopped is that a reason given once is a reason and a reason given twice is a habit. `535` Wants a woman of sixty-one to keep going, asks her to in a porch in front of about nine people on the grounds that the two towns are still remembering that morning differently, and she tells him that a person who is the reason a thing keeps going is a person who cannot stop. `536` Wants the list to be a finding, asks a recordwright to enter it as one, and is refused in about four sentences, and the refusal is the fourteenth case in nineteen years of the one who has not asked, and he is not a person of this county. `537` Wants to be the one who asked, stands in a smithy for about four minutes waiting to ask whether six pounds a year is enough, does not ask it, and a man of thirty-four asks it instead and tells the room why he could and the other could not. `538` Wants to be offered the list of the four and to take it, says yes in about four seconds to a thing he had not thought about, and a woman of about fifty-one says no on his behalf in about nine words before he has finished. `539` Wants the answer to be about the four pages, says out loud in a market square in front of about two hundred people that the man came for the four pages, and is corrected in about four seconds by the man himself. `540`**

**HIS COST IS THE SAME SHAPE IN ALL TEN AND IT IS NOT THE SAME THING TWICE, AND IN THIS BAND THE COST IS THE SAME SENTENCE SIX TIMES: A THING HE HOLDS CANNOT BE CHECKED. It was said to him in four sentences in a porch at `531`, in about four seconds in a lane at `534`, as a refusal to head a column at `535`, as the reason a list is not a finding at `537`, as nine words on his behalf at `539`, and as a correction in front of about two hundred people at `540`. AND THE ONE SENTENCE HE SAID OUT LOUD IN THIS BAND THAT HE HAD NEVER SAID BEFORE IS A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR'S, AND IT IS THE BAND'S FINDING: *a thing is safe because somebody holds it, and a thing is safe because nobody may take it, and those look the same from the outside.* HE HEARD IT IN A ROOM AT `537` AND SAID YES TO IT IN ABOUT FOUR SECONDS AT `539` AND WAS REFUSED ON HIS BEHALF. `531`–`540`**

**HE WAS REFUSED SIX TIMES IN FOUR WEEKS — AT `531`, AT `534`, AT `535`, AT `537`, AT `539` AND AT `540` — AND HE UNDERSTOOD EACH ONE INSIDE ABOUT FOUR SECONDS, AND AT `538` HE WAS THE ONLY PERSON IN THAT SMITHY WHO DID NOT ASK THE ONE QUESTION HE HAD BEEN CARRYING SINCE THE FOURTH WEEK, AND A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR WHO MENDS PLOUGHS ASKED IT AND SAID OUT LOUD THAT SHE CAN SEND HIM AWAY. Nobody thanked him and nobody was sorry for him.**
