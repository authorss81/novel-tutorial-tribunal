## 3. WHAT WAS DONE TO `535`, AND WHY IT IS THE ONE CHAPTER THAT WAS REWRITTEN

**The rework keeps every beat the card asked for and the band summary certified** — the week read in a chancel in about eleven minutes; a dead man of about eighty-four in the ground column for the ninth week running; the boy's distinction between a thing that happened and a person asking; the three men at the fire and the fold and the holdings and the ground behind the meeting-house; the man of thirty-one's want, the third asking in fifteen days, the same answer; the recordwright ruling nothing and heading nothing and writing one line with the boy's name at the foot of it; the four ruled things and two of them on a board anybody may rub out; a penny out of twenty-eight pounds; and the closing inventory.

**And it changes the shape of the scene, because the shape was the defect.** The boy is now already at the board when she arrives instead of being sent for, the two columns are described where they are being talked about, the two columns that were the width of a gap and longer than a gap are physically on the page, the reason that was given at `520` and again at `530` is **not given a third time** and the chapter says why in a woman's mouth, and the reason she gives is *a reason given once is a reason and a reason given twice is a habit*.
