## 1. Running in Bramblefold after 540

**EVERY LINE IS A THREAD, WHICH MEANS A PERSON IS STILL DOING IT. NOTHING IN IT HAS BEEN RESOLVED, AND THE FOURTH BAND ADDED SIX AND CHANGED THREE.**

1. **The struck leaf.** Forty-three names, one stroke, four words that name no order, a hundred and forty-one years old. **READ, once, on a Saturday, in about eleven minutes, with the terms fixed on a sheet three days before it. What came out of the room was a sheet of paper and four people striking out, in public and with their own hands, the four names they are each descended from. NOTHING HAS COME OF IT IN TWENTY-THREE DAYS.** The claim is not carried and the leaf is not struck back. `511`, `514`
2. **The claim.** In respect of the holding numbered six, a half-acre at the bottom of Kiln Lane, and of the fold, and of the ground behind the meeting-house, by Abel Tarn, weaver, sixty-two. **Nothing is carried. The leaf and the claim are two different things and a band may not merge them.** `510`
3. **The two objections, and the finding about them.** The name of Harl Wenlock, forty-four, against the claim; the name of Edith Crow, sixty-eight, against the page going back in at all. Both have been told. She said NO to two thirds of the finding and that went in too, and **on the Wednesday of the ninety-ninth week she said NO A SECOND TIME, to holding one of four pages, and gave a reason: that she has had a thing put in a book about her once in four weeks and did not know it was going to happen.** `517`, `523`, `539`
4. **FOUR RULED THINGS WITH NO HEADING ON THEM, and there were three a fortnight ago.** The space at the back of the book, two inches by nine, one name in it. Two columns on the end wall of a smithy. And the back of a form in a leather wallet nine miles off. **TWO OF THE FOUR ARE ON A BOARD THAT ANY PERSON IN THAT TOWN MAY RUB OUT WITH A RAG, and a recordwright said so out loud in a smithy, and about two hundred people read that board on a Thursday and not one of them can check a word of it.** `510`, `520`, `530`, `535`

6. **THE PROHIBITION ON ASKING ANYBODY IN THE COUNTY OF ALDWICK ABOUT THE ORIGINAL, staged and not broken.** A recordwright said out loud in a lane in front of about nine people that it may be broken in a room with the door open with about nine people in it, and only if one of the nine asks her first, and that she has said in front of nine people that the answer would be no so that she cannot be asked privately in a fortnight and say something else. **A person must be asked and the answer may be no, and the answer was no. THE ENTRY IS THE ONLY THING THAT MAKES HER THE ONLY PERSON WHO CAN BE SENT FOR, AND SHE HAS SAID OUT LOUD THAT IT IS ON A LEAF THAT IS NOT THE PAPER OF THE BOOK.** `519`, `534`
7. **THE ARCHIVE LORDS.** Nine of them in four counties, about four hundred shelves between them, a keeper against the number of a shelf and a day. **A man of thirty-four from a town four days off named the trade in a porch at `531` and said the name sounded like a boast and that he chose it and would rather have chosen a duller one.** He keeps a shelf and has kept one for about eleven years and has watched two of them stop being kept, and both times it was ordinary. **HE SAYS OUT LOUD, AT `531`, `533` AND `540`, THAT HE DOES NOT WANT A PERSON IN THIS COUNTY WHO CAN BE ASKED ABOUT A SHELF AND WHO ANSWERS FOR OTHER PEOPLE'S PAPERS, AND NOBODY IN THIS COUNTY BELIEVED HIM, AND HE IS RIGHT AND HE IS NOT A VILLAIN AND THE BAND DID NOT MAKE HIM ONE.** `531`, `533`, `540`

No single person can hold it, and that is a finding and not a power, and there is no form in the county of Aldwick that says a person may be asked whether they still have it, and a man of thirty-four said out loud that he has found out it can be started and has not found out that it works.** `539`, `540`

SHE SAID HER FOUR SENTENCES ONE LAST TIME IN A SMITHY ON A TUESDAY, said that it was a room and that rooms are where people are held to, and has not said anything else in this county.
