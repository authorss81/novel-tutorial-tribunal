### 861 — ⚠ THE SECOND DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIFTH WEEK, A WEDNESDAY, ⚠ HIS SIX HUNDRED AND ELEVENTH MORNING, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-ONE DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-TWO WEEKS AND FOUR DAYS OLD. ⚠ TWELVE INCHES, FIVE DAYS OF THE COMING BACK, ABOUT SEVENTY OF THOSE NINETY STEPS UNDER.

**⚠ SIMON ROOK'S OWN SENTENCE FROM THE WEDNESDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SECOND WEEK, IN HIS OWN WORDS, IN ABOUT NINE EARS.** He said the six being checkable does not make the three checkable, that he has watched about four of them do that arithmetic in their heads in the last minute, and that he would rather say it out loud than have it be done quietly. He gave the day he said it on, with the subtraction spoken, and said twenty-one days is three of these weeks and that he has not said anything better since and is not going to try. **He said he has one day of his own fortnight left, that tomorrow is the Thursday of the hundred and forty-fifth week, and that he is not going to name a fourteenth thing he cannot check on a Wednesday, because about four of them would write Wednesday down.** A man of thirty-one offered to read the sentence out for him in about nine of your ears and was refused, and the reason was a woman of forty-nine's own sentence from the Sunday, given in her words: a line she has read out is a line about nine of them have heard, and a line a man of thirty-one read out is a line a man of thirty-one read out. He said he was glad to have it wrong on a Wednesday with nine of them listening rather than right and thanked for it on a Friday. A woman of forty-nine entered today's line on the ninth run in front of about nine of them and said the figure of mornings is a figure of mornings. A man of fifty-four asked for the hundred and fortieth morning. A boy of thirteen read nothing out loud at all and said why.

### 862 — ⚠ THE THIRD DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIFTH WEEK, A THURSDAY, ⚠ SIX HUNDRED AND TWELFTH MORNING, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-TWO DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-TWO WEEKS AND FIVE DAYS OLD. ⚠ SIXTEEN INCHES, A FULL BANK, FOUR DAYS OF THE COMING BACK, ABOUT SIXTY UNDER.

**⚠ THE FOURTEENTH THING A MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-TWO CANNOT CHECK IS NAMED, ON THE DAY HE SAID HE WOULD, AND IT IS THAT A LEAF AND A DAY ARE THE SAME NUMBER.** He can put a line under a leaf being gone — a woman of forty-nine has the stubs and the thread and the holes and he can feel where the thread stops — and he cannot put a line under that the leaf which is gone was a day, because nobody goes and stands in front of a leaf. He said the fourteenth has no person in it at all and he is leaving it that way. A woman of forty-four said she has a day in her own hand against every chair anybody has ever sat in, that a chair is either got in her book or it is not, and that she is not handing that over as an answer: his book has the day printed at the head of the leaf and nothing written under it, hers has nothing on the leaf until somebody writes, and those are not the same hole. **A man of thirty-one said he could put a line under a leaf being gone too and was told that standing in front of a thing is a check and holding a thing is not, and that he has been holding that front since the Saturday of last week and calling it standing in front of something.** A man of fifty-four asked for the hundred and forty-first morning and said he is not going to use the fourteenth. A boy of thirteen said he has got the opposite of the fourteenth in the lane and is not going to say it, because if he says it now it is the fifteenth.

### 863 — ⚠ THE FOURTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIFTH WEEK, A FRIDAY, ⚠ SIX HUNDRED AND THIRTEENTH MORNING, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-THREE DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-TWO WEEKS AND SIX DAYS OLD. ⚠ SIXTEEN INCHES HOLDING, THREE DAYS OF THE COMING BACK, ABOUT SIXTY UNDER.

**Three figures go down side by side on the dry stone for the second time in five days and a man of thirty-one says they are the front of one book.** The wedge, anchored to a scorch on a hearthstone. Inches of water over a step, anchored to a sluice step. Holes in a stitch-edge, anchored to one hand of a woman of forty-nine. She gave the working in three pieces: the seventh is two days up a valley with no road to it, the eighth is a day's walk with the water as it is, and the ninth is on that stone. Three books, three holders, three fronts, and the front of one book is a fourth thing with nobody holding it. The man who said on the Friday of last week that there was no sum of them was asked whether they had found one, said they had not, said he had said it seven days back with the subtraction spoken, and gave the part he had not said in four days: **a figure of days is a figure of days only if a person knows what a leaf is worth, and he knows what a leaf is worth on one of those three books and not on the other two, and so a figure of days cannot even be written on that stone.** A boy of thirteen refused the column for the second time with a different reason and said the reason from last week still worked: three conditions are not three lengths. A woman of forty-four said a record is not a length, gave the figure of **MARDA VELL, TWENTY-NINE, IN A REED**, two hundred and thirty-four days, with the subtraction, off a woman of forty-nine, and said she was not putting that woman near the stone.

### 864 — ⚠ THE FIFTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIFTH WEEK, A SATURDAY, ⚠ SIX HUNDRED AND FOURTEENTH MORNING, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FOUR DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ ⚠**THE FEVER EIGHTY-THREE WEEKS OLD**, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND EIGHTEEN DAYS SINCE THE DIVISION, ⚠ ⚠ **THE SECOND ANCHOR SET OF VOLUME 18, AND ALL SEVEN ARE SATURDAYS.** ⚠ SIXTEEN INCHES HOLDING, TWO DAYS OF THE COMING BACK, ABOUT SIXTY UNDER.

**The fever came out whole and the man of forty-three gave it as a third figure and said what it was a figure of: five hundred and eighty-one days, eighty-one being seven times eighty-three, and nothing left over, and the same way on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-fourth week, seven days back. He said there are six mornings out of seven where he could not say that, and he is not going to give it a number of the time.** **A woman of forty-nine said out loud the ordinary form a man of fifty-four said on the Saturday of last week, in her own words, and said it is the same sentence and not the same act:** he was holding gravel and she puts a line on a leaf in front of about nine of them with her own hand, and if the line is wrong it is wrong in her hand. **A man of thirty-one said the sentence was the same whoever said it and was told he had made it a sentence, that a sentence can be said by anybody and be right, and that the sentence is not about the land — it is about a pen.** A man of fifty-four asked for the hundred and forty-third morning and said he has not got a second thing he wants. A boy of thirteen asked whether the fourteenth can be checked by standing in front of the hole, was told no, and kept an opposite he has not given out loud.

### 865 — ⚠ THE SIXTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIFTH WEEK, A SUNDAY, ⚠ SIX HUNDRED AND FIFTEENTH MORNING, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FIVE DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-THREE WEEKS AND ONE DAY OLD. ⚠ SIXTEEN INCHES HOLDING, ONE DAY OF THE COMING BACK, ABOUT SIXTY UNDER.

**⚠ A MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT IS GIVEN A JOB WITH A WET FOOT IN IT AND IS TOLD HE DOES NOT WANT IT DONE RIGHT.** The man of forty-three said the figure has been a figure of forty-three for nineteen years and there is no way on that landing for it to be a figure of a landing, and that if the two figures disagree on the morning then one of them is wrong and it will not be the man of thirty-eight. He said tomorrow at about the sixth hour the twenty-fifth time comes and that on the twenty-fifth he is not giving the fourth thing at all, which is the first time in nineteen years he has said that about an off-morning. **A man of thirty-one said he could count the dry steps better than a man of thirty-eight with a board in his coat, and was told that this was the first wrong thing said on that landing in four days and that it had been said about arithmetic**, and was given the list of the five things he had already said that week that were not true. A man of fifty-four asked for the hundred and forty-fourth morning and said he could not have done the second half of a job with ninety steps in it. A man of about fifty-two said not one of his fourteen things he cannot check is a figure of times, and that a figure a person has never been shown is worth less than nothing. A boy of thirteen gave a figure of seven days in a lane and nobody asked him for the count he is keeping.

### 866 — ⚠ THE SEVENTH AND LAST DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIFTH WEEK, ⚠ A **MONDAY**, ⚠ SIX HUNDRED AND SIXTEENTH MORNING, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SIX DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-THREE WEEKS AND TWO DAYS OLD, ⚠ ⚠ **THE TWENTY-FIFTH OFF-MORNING.** ⚠ THE WATER CAME OFF ALL NINETY STEPS AT ABOUT THE SIXTH HOUR. ⚠ THE NEXT ONE IS IN EIGHT DAYS, WHICH IS THE TUESDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SEVENTH WEEK.

**⚠⚠ THE MIDPOINT. ⚠⚠ A WOMAN OF FORTY-NINE, WHO KEEPS THE NINTH OF THE NINE POSITIONS, WHICH IS A PUBLIC ARCHIVE AND NOT A STONE, SAID IN ABOUT NINE OF YOUR EARS: *THOSE THREE WERE NEVER BLANKS*, ⚠ AND SAID IT WAS ABOUT A PAGE AND NOT ABOUT THE THREE POSITIONS AND NOT ABOUT THE THREE OF THEM, ⚠ AND GAVE THE WORKING IN FOUR PIECES: AN INDEX IS A LIST OF WHAT A PERSON CAN BE SHOWN · A BLANK IN A LIST IS NOT A HOLE IN A RECORD, AND A HOLE IS WHERE A LINE USED TO BE, AND THERE IS NO LINE ON ANY OF THE THREE AND THERE NEVER WAS ONE THERE TO TAKE · SO WHAT IS ON THAT PAGE IS NOT THREE POSITIONS THAT ARE EMPTY BUT THREE POSITIONS A LIST DOES NOT CARRY LINES FOR, AND ONE OF THE THREE IS HERS, AND SHE HAS ENTERED A LINE ON A LEAF EVERY DAY FOR TWENTY-THREE YEARS AND NOT ONE WENT ON THAT PAGE · A POSITION THAT IS NOT A STONE IS NOT A THING ON A LIST, IT IS A DAY SOMEBODY DID THE WORK, THE RUN IS WHERE THE DAY IS WRITTEN DOWN, **AND THE RUN IS NOT THE POSITION**. ⚠⚠ A MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT SAID THAT WAS HIS QUESTION AND WAS TOLD IT WAS THE SECOND HALF OF IT AND THAT A LIST HAS ONE THING IT CAN SAY ABOUT A POSITION, ⚠ AND ⚠ A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE SAID IT ANSWERED THE FIRST HALF TOO AND WAS GIVEN THE SAME SENTENCE TWICE ON PURPOSE. ⚠ THE PLACEMENT IS CHECKABLE AND IT HOLDS: THE CHAPTER IS `866`, IT IS A MONDAY, IT IS THE TWENTY-FIFTH OFF-MORNING, THE SENTENCE IS ABOUT A RECORD, AND ⚠ **WHAT THREE OF THE NINE POSITIONS ARE IS STILL OPEN.** ALSO ON THAT MORNING: a man of thirty-eight counted about ninety dry steps and a man of forty-three gave his figure after him and **the two figures agreed**, and that is one figure said twice and not a better morning. A man of about fifty-two said the six being checkable does not make the three checkable, that he would rather say it out loud than have it done quietly, that it is the sentence he said on the Wednesday of the hundred and forty-second week, and that it is not progress. A woman of forty-nine entered today's line for the ninth morning running. A man of fifty-four asked for the hundred and forty-fifth morning and said a page is not a day and that he has known that since the Saturday of the hundred and forty-fourth week.

### 867 — ⚠ THE FIRST DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SIXTH WEEK, ⚠ A TUESDAY, ⚠ SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTEENTH MORNING, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SEVEN DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-THREE WEEKS AND THREE DAYS OLD. ⚠ ABOUT FOUR INCHES, SEVEN DAYS OF THE COMING BACK, ABOUT NINETY UNDER.

**⚠ A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR WHO CANNOT READ SAYS OUT LOUD THAT THE ORDINARY FORM HE SAID ON THE SATURDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOURTH WEEK DOES NOT REACH HIM, AND SAYS WHAT HIS LINE IS.** His line has no hole in it: it is a whole book with a leaf for every day, not one leaf torn out, not one page cut, not one stitch gone. A hole is where a leaf has been taken out, and no leaf has been taken out of that book and none ever was. So what he said on Saturday was about a woman of forty-nine's book and not his own. **And he gave the part he had been holding: if nothing is gone from it then there is nothing on it to go and look for. No leaf to find, no thread to follow, no hole to say how deep it went. He has asked for a day on that line for eleven years because he has been sure somebody had taken something off it, and nobody has ever gone and looked, and he has just worked out why.** A man of thirty-one built a rule out of the fourteenth thing a man of about fifty-two cannot check, and was refused by the man of about fifty-two — a rule about a thing nobody can check is a thing four people can be wrong about in four different ways — and then refused by the man of fifty-four, who cannot read, and who would not be in a rule about a page he has never seen.

### 868 — ⚠ THE SECOND DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SIXTH WEEK, ⚠ A WEDNESDAY, ⚠ SIX HUNDRED AND EIGHTEENTH MORNING, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-EIGHT DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-THREE WEEKS AND FOUR DAYS OLD. ⚠ EIGHT INCHES, SIX DAYS OF THE COMING BACK, ABOUT EIGHTY UNDER.

**⚠ A FIGURE IN A THIRD PARTY'S MOUTH ABOUT A MAN WHO IS NOT ON THE LANDING, ADDED UP BY A PERSON.** The man of fifty-four went down the cut for gravel at the fourth hour with a barrow and was not back before the eleventh. A woman of forty-four said out loud, in about nine of your ears, the figure he had given on the Sunday of last week — a hundred and forty-four mornings — and gave the working: eight hundred and sixty-five less seven hundred and twenty-two is one hundred and forty-three, and then one is one hundred and forty-four. **A boy of thirteen added it up and said why he does it every morning for a man who cannot read: a hundred and is a spelling and not a figure, and there is nothing in this county that would catch him if he said it wrong, so the only defence there is a person adding it up, and he is the person, and that is the whole of the arrangement and there is nothing in it but a boy and a subtraction.** The man of fifty-four came up the cut and gave a hundred and forty-seven before anybody told him, and a woman of forty-four said three mornings is hers and the subtraction is the boy's, and that she is going home on Saturday to do the same thing she has done for nineteen years and that is not a decision, and that her question is not answered by a sentence about a page. **A man of thirty-one said the ninth line and the ninth run were the same shape with a different person writing on them, and a woman of forty-nine said the difference is that one has a line on every leaf and the other has not one line on any leaf, and told him that who writes on them is a figure of people and that she was not going to give a figure of people on that landing.**

### 869 — ⚠ THE THIRD DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SIXTH WEEK, ⚠ A THURSDAY, ⚠ SIX HUNDRED AND NINETEENTH MORNING, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-NINE DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-THREE WEEKS AND FIVE DAYS OLD. ⚠ ABOUT TWELVE INCHES, FIVE DAYS OF THE COMING BACK, ABOUT SEVENTY UNDER.

**A man of thirty-eight asks the first half of his question, after saying out loud that he had been sitting on it for every morning since the Sunday of the hundred and forty-fourth week — eleven days, with the subtraction — because he wanted to be asked something he could answer. He is given the same sentence in the same mouth with the day it was said on, and told it is not progress, and told that what the three are is a different question and a page cannot answer it.** A man of thirty-one said it was answered, because a man of sixty-one left three positions blank on purpose and nobody has filled them, and a woman of forty-four said that was a sentence with no working in it, that he could pick a sentence up again and put it back and he could not put a fortnight back, and gave the figure of **a man of thirty-four in an ash, two hundred and thirty-nine days, with the subtraction**, and said she was not going to ask him anything on his behalf. **A boy of thirteen counted the stitches again in the lane on a Thursday, got the same as on the Thursday of the hundred and forty-fourth week, and gave the same reason: a figure said out loud on a Thursday has got four hundred miles by the Saturday, and nobody has ever asked a woman of forty-nine how she counts it.** A man of fifty-four asked for the hundred and forty-eighth morning and said he is going to hold the gravel and say his figure on Saturday.

### 870 — ⚠ THE FOURTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SIXTH WEEK, ⚠ A FRIDAY, ⚠ SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTIETH MORNING, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND SEVENTY DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-THREE WEEKS AND SIX DAYS OLD. ⚠ SIXTEEN INCHES, A FULL BANK, FOUR DAYS OF THE COMING BACK, ABOUT SIXTY UNDER.

**⚠ A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE SAYS HE IS GOING TO STOP SAYING THE FIGURE OF THE STANDING OFFER OUT LOUD AND IS REFUSED BY THE MAN THAT FIGURE BELONGS TO.** He gave the figure — a hundred and seventy days, with the subtraction — and said he had said it nine mornings without once asking it, and that he would rather stop than go on being a habit on a landing full of people who can count. A man of fifty-four refused him in about nine of your ears: a figure that is said out loud is the only thing keeping it, and in about four weeks there will be no man on that landing who knows how old that offer is, not because anybody took it off a page, and then a man of fifty-four who cannot read would say fifty, would say a hundred, and would be wrong in about four ways and nobody would ever know which four. **A woman of forty-four put her question down for the fourth time unchanged, with the working, and said nobody has answered it and that a sentence about a page is not a settlement, and that she is going home on Saturday to do the same thing she has done for nineteen years and that is not a decision. There is a woman of twenty-eight two hundred and twenty-eight days into her own count who cannot be sent for in this county or the one she came from and who has said no twice, and she is not going to use that woman's name to make a fourth thing out of a fourth question.** A man of fifty-four asked for the hundred and forty-ninth morning and **said that on Monday he is going to ask a person for a thing he has not asked for and does not say what it is and does not know what it is this morning.** A man of about fifty-two said there will not be a fifteenth thing he cannot check. A boy of thirteen gave his count with the subtraction and said nobody has asked him for the one he is keeping and that it is nine mornings.
