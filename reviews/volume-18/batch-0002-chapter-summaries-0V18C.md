### 851 — ⚠ THE FIRST DAY OF VOLUME 18, ⚠ THE SIXTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-THIRD WEEK, A SUNDAY, ⚠ HIS SIX HUNDRED AND FIRST MORNING, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-ONE DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-ONE WEEKS AND ONE DAY OLD.

**The volume opens from a run being entered on.** A man of fifty-four who cannot read put four strokes and a half a barrow on the leaf for the day in a day-book that came up the cart road four miles from the bakehouse on Saturday and cost him fourpence, which he said out loud twice so that nobody would call it a gift. A boy of thirteen read the printed day at the head of that leaf out loud twice, gave his own count of a hundred and ninety-eight days with the subtraction in it, and said one is a figure of a day and not of a week. The man of forty-three gave about four inches and seven days of the coming back, gave the figure of steps first, and **refused the fourth thing on the Sunday and said he meant Monday**, because a figure he does not have is worse on a Sunday than on a Saturday. A man of fifty-four asked for a day against the ninth line for the hundred and thirtieth morning and was told what the ninth line is: a run of days four hundred miles off with a leaf for every day, and **nothing missing from it, no leaf torn out, no page cut, a whole book with not one line ever entered on any leaf**, and a man of fifty-four who cannot read had believed for eleven years that something had been taken off it. A man of thirty-one said he wanted to put the first line ever on that line, and a man of fifty-four gave the working of why it could not be him, and he took it back in the same breath in the shape it was given to him in. He then said a figure of about eleven years for how long the line has been empty, and a boy of thirteen told him in one sentence that a figure of years with no day on the end of it is the same as no figure, and the figure was taken back and not replaced. He is nobody's, has been in this county two hundred and ninety-seven days, the cut across that palm is a hundred and sixty-six days old, and the standing offer is a hundred and fifty-one days old and unanswered.

### 852 — ⚠ THE SEVENTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-THIRD WEEK, A MONDAY, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-TWO DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-ONE WEEKS AND TWO DAYS OLD.

**The fourth thing comes back and its run is settled by a mouth.** The man of forty-three gave about eight inches, six days of the coming back and about eighty of the ninety steps under, on the morning he said on Saturday he would give it on. He said it was nothing for nineteen mornings, from the second day of the hundred and forty-first week to yesterday, and that a boy of thirteen counted nineteen off the post at the top of that cut and got the same. **He named the last morning he gave that figure out loud as the first day of the hundred and forty-first week, a Tuesday, and said out loud that on the Wednesday of that week he had told about four of them it was the Monday, and the Monday was the day before, and he had been wrong by one day for about four of these weeks.** A woman of thirty-six walked the last four miles of the cart road with a satchel because she would not put it in a cart, and in the satchel was a wedge of burnt leaves. She named the run: a bound sequence of day-leaves, a leaf for a day, one line on the leaf and nothing else, nineteen years of it, and the front of it is gone. She anchored the figure of leaves to **a scorch on a hearthstone at the head of her valley the length of her forearm**, said how deep the burn went cannot be told because the leaves that would have told it are the leaves it took, and **gave the directions to that hearthstone in her own mouth, in days, on purpose** — two days up from the bakehouse, one down if the water is out, and no road to it. A man of thirty-one said the burning happened in one night and a woman of thirty-six told him nobody knows, that a bound run does not burn in a night, and that there is a morning in the middle of it nobody alive was awake for. He took it back in the same breath and then said he wanted to be the one who carries that wedge four hundred miles, in about nine of your ears, because a want said late is a want that has been growing, and a woman of thirty-six told him a person who carries a thing arrives with a story about the thing. He is in this county two hundred and ninety-eight days, the cut is a hundred and sixty-seven days old, and a man of fifty-four is at one hundred and thirty-one mornings.

### 853 — ⚠ THE FIRST DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOURTH WEEK, A TUESDAY, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-THREE DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-ONE WEEKS AND THREE DAYS OLD.

**The eighth run is under water and the figure of inches is given with its anchor.** About twelve inches, five days of the coming back, about seventy of the ninety steps under, and the man of forty-three gave the figure before the inches for the first time and said why: about four of them had spent two days looking at the front of a thing and not at the thing. A man of forty-four came up the cart road from the low gate end and did not come to the counter and said why, and **gave the distance to the sluice step in days on purpose** — a day's walk with the water as it is, two days in a dry week. He said about eleven inches of water stand over the first of his leaves at this hour and that the step is the anchor and he is not going to say a figure without an anchor on it in about nine of your ears. A man of thirty-one said that was the same water that took those leaves, and the man of forty-four gave the working: the two are not joined by anything a man can walk, and if they were he would have dried a run in a fortnight instead of nineteen years. **A run that is put in water goes into water, and it is the same condition and not the same water.** A woman of twenty-nine in a reed a hundred yards along that drain said what the condition is — the thing gets less every day and nobody can see it get less because it is slow — and said she was speaking about a reed and not about a compact and declined to speak for him. A man of thirty-one said he wanted the run lifted out and dried and was given the working: a wet bound thing in a satchel is not a run, and the eleven inches of head on that step are what keep the low drain on the other side of that landing clear, and about nine of them live on the low side. He took it back and said a man of forty-four had told him in about nine ears that he had heard the want twice. The reed is at two hundred and twenty-four days.

### 854 — ⚠ THE SECOND DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOURTH WEEK, A WEDNESDAY, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-FOUR DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-ONE WEEKS AND FOUR DAYS OLD, ⚠ AND THE FIRST MORNING OF A FULL BANK AT SIXTEEN INCHES.

The man of forty-three gave both figures and said a full bank holds at sixteen until it comes off, and said he had said that on the second day of this week and would say it on the fifth, and invited about four of them to check. A woman of thirty-six entered today's line on the last leaf of the wedge in about nine of your ears, said the line would be a bad line because there is nowhere to put a good one, and **said she was not going to call it a thing anybody may rely on, because about four of them would have it in a book by Thursday, and that entering a day is a smaller thing than that.** A man of about fifty-two said he can put a line under a leaf being gone and not under how many leaves are gone, and gave the figure of what he cannot check as **thirteen, named on the Thursday of last week, a figure of a woman**, and said he had not named her because she named herself first, and gave the end of his own fortnight as **the Thursday of the hundred and forty-fifth week, which is eight days off**. A man of thirty-one said he wanted the wedge on the long table under the charters. A woman of forty who keeps the first of the six stones said she was not going to give a working from a thing she had never done, and told him the fourth line of her own charter is still not published and nobody has told her where it goes. A man of about fifty-two gave the rest: **a description outgrows a wedge, because a wedge is a shape and a description is a sentence, and a sentence is much easier to carry.** A man of thirty-one said the seventh run goes back nineteen years and a woman of thirty-six told him he had taken a figure of her ringing and made it a figure of a book — you cannot add years to a hole and you cannot date a hole. He took it back and said three wants had gone on the ground in about four days and that three is a figure of wants and not of days.

### 855 — ⚠ THE THIRD DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOURTH WEEK, A THURSDAY, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-FIVE DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-ONE WEEKS AND FIVE DAYS OLD, ⚠ THE SECOND MORNING OF A FULL BANK.

The bank held at sixteen and the man of forty-three said the word holds out loud because about four of them had been waiting three days to see whether he would. **A woman of forty-nine got down off a public cart with a bundle of about four inches on her, set it on the counter herself, and named herself and her own age in about nine ears before anybody asked.** She is the ninth of the nine positions, the ninth is a public archive and not a stone, and the run is a bound sequence of day-leaves with one line on a leaf for a day — and she said at once that it is **not** the ninth line. The gap is at the front: somebody drew the threads and took the leaves away and used them, and she has the stubs, the thread and the holes, and she is not going to say how many. A man of about fifty-two said he could put a line under the gap, the drawn thread and the empty holes, and could not put one under whether the missing leaves are the ones this run lost or somebody has been in the middle of it. **A boy of thirteen said he had counted the stitches in that gap on Tuesday in the lane, twice, and got the same both times, and was not going to give the count**, and gave the reason: a figure said out loud on a Thursday has got four hundred miles by the Saturday, and nobody has ever asked her how she counts it. A man of fifty-four asked for it anyway and said why. A woman of forty-nine gave the same refusal and said the boy's reason was better than his. A man of thirty-one said the word destroyed and a woman of forty-nine told him that is a word for a thing that had a whole and does not have it now, that taking is not destroying, and that one of the two words is a fact and the other is a feeling. He took it back, and then asked her to stay nine days, and she said no in one sentence, using a sentence a man of about fifty-two gave about nine of them on the Wednesday, and said she was not going to be thanked for the no and not going to be told she was right to give it.

### 856 — ⚠ THE FOURTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOURTH WEEK, A FRIDAY, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-SIX DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND TEN DAYS SINCE THE DIVISION, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-ONE WEEKS AND SIX DAYS OLD, ⚠ THE THIRD MORNING OF A FULL BANK.

The bank held at sixteen with two days of the coming back and about sixty of the ninety steps under, and the man of forty-three said tomorrow at about the sixth hour the water comes off again and **that is the twenty-fourth time**, and that he has given that number for twenty-four mornings and nobody has asked him to stop. **Three figures went down side by side on the dry stone — a figure of leaves, a figure of inches of water over a stone step, a figure of holes in a stitch-edge — and a man of about fifty-two put his hand flat on the stone and said there was no sum of them, pointing at his own sentence from the Wednesday of the hundred and forty-second week and saying he has not said anything better since, and that he has six days of his own fortnight left and will not name a fourteenth thing he cannot check.** A boy of thirteen wrote the three figures on a board and refused to add them, because three is a figure of figures and not a figure of anything on that stone, and adding them would make a fourth thing and the fourth thing is a figure of him, and **he gave no figure of how many were standing there and said that was the only figure in the week he would not give.** A woman of forty-four put her question down for the third time, unchanged, with the working: can a day be put down for a thing that happened before anybody thought to write it, and is that day the same as a day put down at the time. A man of thirty-one said it is the same, that a day is a day, and that it is the answer to her question, **and a man of fifty-four said that is my sentence, that he has said it on that bank for eleven years and it has never once been an answer to that woman's question, and that a sentence can be right and be no use at all.** A man of thirty-one took it back and said a woman of sixty-four had told him he could not tell a rule from an instruction, and that he had just done the same with a sentence out loud on a bank. He then asked a woman of forty-four to have a day-book and said he would pay for it, and she said no to the book and not to the wanting, and said her question is not a day's work, and that she knows the price of a book that is entered in because she has been charging it for nineteen years.

### 857 — ⚠ THE FIFTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOURTH WEEK, A SATURDAY, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-SEVEN DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ **THE FEVER EIGHTY-TWO WEEKS OLD AND THAT IS A WHOLE NUMBER OF WEEKS**, ⚠ THE FOURTH MORNING OF A FULL BANK, ⚠ **AND THE FIRST ANCHOR SET OF VOLUME 18.**

The man of forty-three gave both figures, said tomorrow at about the sixth hour about ninety of those steps can be walked and that is the twenty-fourth time, and **said out loud that the fever had been a whole number of weeks since the third day of this week and that he did not know it until the seventh hour.** **At the ninth hour a man of fifty-four who cannot read said out loud, in about nine ears, the ordinary form of a run with a hole in the front of it, gave the working, and said twice that it was not his and would not give it a name:** a run with a hole in its beginning is still entered on today and today's line is not a gap, and a position may be checked from the day its record starts and may not be checked before that day. A man of thirty-eight asked what it was called and **said the word the right of refusal out loud on that landing**, and a man of fifty-four told him it was spoken, that a man of sixty-four said a sentence in a room on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-second week and he was in the room, that he cannot read or spell it, and that he was not going to give a thing he has heard spoken a name a man of thirty-eight gave it that morning, and that about four of the nine were not going to hear it said by him. The man of thirty-eight did not argue and asked for the working. **A man of thirty-one then said the form closes a gap and a man of fifty-four said it closes nothing, that it says where the gap is and what may be done on the far side of it, and then said the three things it does not do:** it does not put a day on the book four hundred miles off, it does not say who holds the pen, and it does not let a person be checked from the beginning, only from the day their record starts. A man of thirty-one asked for it on a leaf and a man of fifty-four said no, and gave the working by saying that in about four weeks the name anybody puts on that leaf will be the name of the man who can write.

### 858 — ⚠ THE SIXTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOURTH WEEK, A SUNDAY, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-EIGHT DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-TWO WEEKS AND ONE DAY OLD, ⚠ **THE TWENTY-FOURTH OFF-MORNING.**

**The water came off that bank at about the sixth hour and all ninety of those steps were walkable by about the seventh, and the man of forty-three gave the hour, the count and the number of the time, and said there was no figure of inches and no figure of steps, and that the next one is in eight days, which is the Monday of the hundred and forty-fifth week.** **A woman of forty-nine entered today's line on the ninth run on the dry stone in front of about nine people, read it out herself, and would not let a man of thirty-one read it for her**, and said a line she has read out is a line about nine of them have heard, and a line he read out is a line he read out, and that a witness is a figure of ears and not a figure of a reader. A man of thirty-one said it was the first entry ever made on that run, and **a woman of forty-nine told him it was the first entry anybody had watched, and that she has entered a line on it every day for twenty-three years** — first ever and first seen are not the same thing. He took it back, said he had the difference and would not put a name on it because a name on it would be a sentence. A man of thirty-eight then asked the second half of his question one more time and **was given the same answer with the day it was said on: a man of sixty-one read the arrangement's own index out on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-third week and left three of the nine blank on purpose, in about nine of your ears, and that is not a discovery this morning and nobody is to make it one**; the first half he has been asked for twelve days and does not know, and a man of thirty-one does not know, and that is the whole of the answer there is. A boy of thirteen read a printed day out loud twice and said the water coming off at the sixth hour is a figure of water and the twenty-fourth time is a figure of times, and neither of them is a figure of a day, and a day is a leaf.

### 859 — ⚠ THE SEVENTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOURTH WEEK, A MONDAY, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY-NINE DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-TWO WEEKS AND TWO DAYS OLD, ⚠ THE FIGURE OF STEPS GIVEN BEFORE THE INCHES FOR THE SECOND MORNING RUNNING.

About four inches and seven days of the coming back and about ninety of the ninety steps under. **The ninth run and the ninth line went side by side on the dry stone and a woman of forty-nine said they are not the same book:** the ninth run is hers, with one line on a leaf for a day and a gap in the stitching at the front; the ninth line is the arrangement's own record, also a run of days, with a leaf for every day and not one line entered on any of them; a woman of sixty-four has a name in ink on its index and a man of fifty-four has asked for a day against it for a hundred and thirty-eight mornings. A man of thirty-one said the ordinary form makes a position checkable, **and a woman of forty-four said it makes a record checkable, and that those are two different days, and that about four of them had been using one of them for a week and calling it the other** — a chair is either got in her book or it is not, and that is the only kind of record she has ever had. A man of thirty-one then asked her to write the ordinary form in her book and she said no, and said her book is twenty-nine chairs, and that in about four weeks a man of thirty-one would have written the right of refusal into a keeper's book of chairs and about nine people who were not in that room would read it and find out what it was for from the page, and that if it went under no chair it would not be in the book. **A boy of thirteen offered to write it down and was told no**, and told where she had got the refusal from — a woman of sixty-four handed it to her on the Saturday of this week and said it was for the person it was most likely to be wasted on — and that he is the only person on that landing who reads a sentence out in three pieces for a man of fifty-four who cannot read, and that if he wrote it down in about four weeks he would be the person who wrote it, and a boy of thirteen who writes down what he is told is a clerk.

### 860 — ⚠ THE FIRST DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIFTH WEEK, A TUESDAY, ⚠ FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, ⚠ THE FEVER EIGHTY-TWO WEEKS AND THREE DAYS OLD.

About eight inches and six days of the coming back and about eighty of the ninety steps under, and **the man of forty-three put the inches back in front of the figure and said it was not a correction and that a thing about four of them had got used to is not a thing he wants to hand about nine of them.** **A man of fifty-four asked for a day against the ninth line for the hundred and thirty-ninth morning, and then asked for the index of that line to be read out in about nine of your ears by anybody who has it, in any shape at all, and said the second thing was smaller and available, and that he is not going to keep asking a person for a thing they have told him the shape of.** A woman of forty-nine said the index can be read out, that a woman of sixty-four heard it read out on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-third week, and that **nobody is to tell a man of thirty-eight this morning he was the first to want it**; that she does not hold the ninth line and keeps the ninth run; and that a day cannot be got, because the name in ink on the index is a woman of sixty-four who has never held that seat, and a day she wrote against her own name in a different place is not a day on that line — **and that this is not a refusal, it is a shape, and a shape does not refuse anybody at all.** A man of thirty-one offered to write the smaller ask on a leaf and a man of fifty-four said no, and gave the working by way of what he had said to a woman of forty-four about a boy of thirteen: a thing he said to somebody else on a Friday is a thing he has to live by on a Tuesday. He will do it in marks, four strokes and a figure, and a boy of thirteen will read it out, and that is the whole of the arrangement. A boy of thirteen read it out and said a figure is a figure and not a letter, and that he was not going to pretend to him that he read a sentence, and that a sentence and a question are not the same shape and a mark is a third shape nobody on that landing has a word for yet. A man of thirty-one said a record does not have to be a book to be one and then did not say it, because a man of fifty-four had said it first, and said he had been refused four times in about four days and was not going to be told that is patience. **The standing offer is a hundred and sixty days old and unanswered, and he said he is going to go on saying that figure out loud and not going to ask it.**
