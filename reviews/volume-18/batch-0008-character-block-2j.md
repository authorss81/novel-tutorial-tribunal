## 2j. ⚠ WHAT CHAPTERS `851`–`860` CHANGED IN THESE PEOPLE, ⚠ AND EVERY NAME, AGE AND FIGURE IN IT WAS MADE IN A MOUTH, IN FRONT OF ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, WITH THE WORKING SAID OUT LOUD, ⚠ AND ⚠ **A BAND FIXES NO NAME BY A PLANNING FILE AND A FIGURE OF A PERSON IS A FIGURE OF A PERSON AND NOT A FIGURE OF A BANK**

1. **ILYAN VESTER, THIRTY-ONE, AND IS NOBODY'S. `ch − 554` = **TWO HUNDRED AND NINETY-SEVEN** THROUGH **THREE HUNDRED AND SIX**, AND `ch − 685` = **A HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SIX** THROUGH **A HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FIVE**, SPOKEN IN HIS OWN MOUTH IN ALL TEN.** He wanted to put the first line ever on the ninth line and took it back in the same breath. He wanted to be the one who carries the seventh seat's wedge four hundred miles. He wanted the eighth seat's run lifted out of the water. He wanted the wedge on the long table. He wanted a woman of forty-nine to stay nine days. He wanted a day-book bought for a woman of forty-four. He wanted the ordinary form on a leaf. He wanted to read a woman's line for her. He wanted the ordinary form in a keeper's book. He wanted to write a smaller ask on a leaf. **TEN WANTS IN TEN CHAPTERS AND EVERY ONE WAS REFUSED BY A PERSON IN A MOUTH WITH THE WORKING GIVEN, AND HE TOOK BACK TEN.** He made ten mistakes and named every one of them before it was named for him. **He is not told he was right about anything, he is not thanked, and nobody restores his credibility.**
2. **BARNABY CROVE, FIFTY-FOUR, WHO CANNOT READ AND HOLDS THE GRAVEL. `1 + (ch − 722)` = **ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY** THROUGH **ONE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE**, SPOKEN EVERY MORNING OF THE BAND IN HIS OWN MOUTH AND SPELLED *A HUNDRED AND …* BECAUSE HE CANNOT READ.** He bought a day-book four miles up the cart road on Saturday for fourpence and said the fourpence out loud twice so that nobody would call it a gift, and put four strokes and a half a barrow on the leaf for Sunday, and said the half was a figure of a barrow and not a figure of a day. **He was told what the ninth line is, and that nothing is missing from it, and he was not relieved.** He said the ordinary form out loud at the ninth hour on the Saturday and gave it no name. He said the three things it does not do. He refused a man of thirty-one the right to write it on a leaf and gave the reason, which is that he cannot read and the name on the leaf in about four weeks would be the man who can write. **He asked for the index of the ninth line to be read out and entered it in marks, four strokes and a figure, and a boy of thirteen read it out.**
3. **ADA RENK, SIXTY-FOUR, WHO HAS A NAME IN INK ON THE INDEX OF THE NINTH LINE AND A DAY AGAINST HER OWN NAME IN A DIFFERENT PLACE.** She is not on the page in this band. **She is on the page twice as a fact: her name is in ink on the index at `860` and a day she wrote in a different place is not a day on that line, AND SHE HAS NEVER HELD THAT SEAT, ⚠ WHICH IS WHAT MAKES THE SHAPE A SHAPE AND NOT A REFUSAL.**
4. **BARNABY CROVE'S SENTENCE, ⚠ *A DAY IS A DAY*, ⚠ WHICH HE HAS SAID ON THAT LANDING FOR ELEVEN YEARS AND WHICH WAS TAKEN AND USED BY A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE ON `856` IN ABOUT NINE EARS WHERE IT IS NOT AN ANSWER, ⚠ AND HE SAID THE SENTENCE IS HIS AND THAT A SENTENCE CAN BE RIGHT AND BE NO USE AT ALL.** He is not thanked for it and he did not expect to be.
5. **NEVIN TRASK, SIXTY-ONE.** Not on the page in this band. **He is on the page twice as a fact and twice as a working: a man of thirty-eight was told at `858` that the index was read aloud on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-third week and that three of the nine were left blank on purpose, ⚠ AND A WOMAN OF FORTY-NINE CITED THE SAME READING AT `860` AND SAID NOBODY IS TO TELL A MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT HE WAS THE FIRST TO WANT IT.** His own three numbers are still three numbers in eleven years and none of them is on this band's page.
6. **SIMON ROOK, ABOUT FIFTY-TWO, WHO CANNOT SEE WELL, WHO NAMED A THIRTEENTH THING HE CANNOT CHECK ON **THE THURSDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-THIRD WEEK**, WHICH IS `848`, ⚠ AND THAT THING IS A FIGURE OF A WOMAN, ⚠ AND ON THE THURSDAY OF THIS WEEK SHE CAME DOWN A CART AND NAMED HERSELF BEFORE HE SPOKE.** He gave the days to the hearthstone's valley as a mouth figure for Hessa Vinch and refused to improve on a sentence of his own from the Wednesday of the hundred and forty-second week. He put three figures down side by side and said there was no sum of them. **His fortnight ends THE THURSDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIFTH WEEK, which is `862`, and he gave the remaining count in five mornings and named no fourteenth.**
7. **WAT MARSHE, THIRTEEN. `ch − 653` = **A HUNDRED AND NINETY-EIGHT** THROUGH **TWO HUNDRED AND SEVEN**, SPOKEN IN HIS OWN MOUTH IN SEVEN OF THE TEN.** He counted nineteen marks off a post and got the same figure the man of forty-three had. He counted the stitches in the gap twice and refused the count with a reason. He put three figures on a board and refused to add them and said the fourth thing would be a figure of him. He read a printed day aloud twice for a man who cannot read. He read a sentence out in three pieces. **He read four strokes and a figure out loud and said a figure is a figure and not a letter, and said he was not going to pretend to him that he read a sentence, and said a sentence and a question are not the same shape and a mark is a third shape nobody on that landing has a word for yet. ⚠ AND A WOMAN OF FORTY-FOUR REFUSED HIM A BOOK AND A BOY'S HAT AND SAID HE IS GOING TO KEEP BEING THE PERSON WHO READS IT OUT TWICE. ⚠ NOBODY IS TO TELL HIM HE FOUND ANYTHING.**
8. **GARRIN TOLLEY, THIRTY-EIGHT, WITH A CHILD OF NINE AND A BOARD WITH THIRTY-TWO NOTCHES ON IT.** He called the ordinary form **THE RIGHT OF REFUSAL** out loud on that landing at the ninth hour on the Saturday, in about nine of your ears, and was told by a man of fifty-four who cannot read that the word was spoken in a room on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-second week, and that he was not going to be allowed to use it, and he did not argue and asked for the working. **He asked the second half of his question at `858` and was given the same answer with the day it was said on, and the first half is still unanswered, and he has still not got the part where he knows what to do with the board.**
9. **HESSA VINCH, THIRTY-SIX, THE SEVENTH SEAT.** She came down four miles on the Saturday and walked the last four on the Monday with a satchel, and would not put it in a cart. She entered today’s line on the last leaf of a wedge in about nine of your ears and said she was not going to call it a thing anybody may rely on. She gave the distance to the hearthstone in her own mouth, in days, on purpose. She said how deep the burn went cannot be told because the leaves that would have told it are the leaves it took. She corrected a man of thirty-one twice: a run does not burn in a night, and a figure of her ringing is not a figure of a book. **She said the man of thirty-one was using a sentence of a man of about fifty-two's as if it were his own, and she did not mind.**
10. **HAL BRECHT, FORTY-FOUR, THE EIGHTH SEAT.** He came up the cart road from the low gate end and did not come to the counter, and said why. He gave the distance to the sluice step in days on purpose. He said about eleven inches of water stand over the first of his leaves at this hour, and that the step is the anchor. He gave the working of why the water on his step is not the water on the landing. He said a run that is put in water goes into water and that it is the same condition and not the same water. He said no to a man of thirty-one who wanted his run lifted out, and gave the working, and counted the want. **His road is not stabilised in this band and nobody told him it would be.**
11. **SENA DORR, FORTY-NINE, THE NINTH SEAT, ⚠ **AND ⚠ HER NAME AND HER AGE WERE MADE IN HER OWN MOUTH ON THE THURSDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOURTH WEEK, ⚠ AND ⚠ EVERY NAME AND AGE IN THE PLAN IS FIXED IN A MOUTH AND NOT BY A PLANNING FILE.** ⚠ **SHE IS THE PERSON A MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-TWO NAMED AS HIS THIRTEENTH THING HE CANNOT CHECK, ⚠ AND ⚠ A PLANNING FILE DID NOT NAME HER AND ⚠ A MOUTH DID.** She read for a living for twenty-three years in a room four hundred miles off with nine clerks in it. She keeps the ninth run, a bound sequence of day-leaves with one line on a leaf for a day, and the front of it has a gap in the stitching because somebody drew the threads and took the leaves away and used them. She entered today’s line on it in about nine of your ears and read it out herself and would not let a man of thirty-one read it for her. **She will not give the stitch-count, and she said the boy's reason for refusing it was better than a man of fifty-four's. She said no in one sentence to a man of thirty-one who wanted her to stay nine days, and said she was not going to be thanked for the no and not going to be told she was right to give it.**
12. **MARDA VELL, TWENTY-NINE, IN A REED. `ch − 629` = **TWO HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FOUR** AT `853`, SPOKEN IN HER OWN MOUTH ON ONE MORNING OF THIS BAND.** She said what a run in water is, which is the figure of the band: it gets less every day and nobody can see it get less because it is slow. **She declined to speak for a man of forty-four on the ground that he had not asked her, and said she had been asked for her reason five times in two books and was tired of being asked.**
13. **ORLA FENNIMORE, FORTY-FOUR, KEEPER OF TWENTY-NINE CHAIRS AT THIRTEEN SHILLINGS AND FOURPENCE A YEAR FOR NINETEEN YEARS.** She put her question down for the third time, unchanged, at `856`, gave the working of it, and was not answered. She was told by a man of thirty-one that a day is a day is the answer, and she was not thanked for having been the man of fifty-four's sentence. She said a record is checkable and a position is not, and that those are two different days. She refused a day-book and refused a man of thirty-one the right to write the ordinary form in her book, and refused a boy of thirteen the same, and told him where she had got the refusal from. **She has not been given a piece of paper and a practice and that is owed in Band 5, and she is not to be thanked for anything in this band and did not ask to be.**
14. **WENNA CALLOW, FORTY, WHO KEEPS THE FIRST OF THE SIX STONES AND HAS ASKED FOR A DAY AGAINST A NAME FOR ELEVEN YEARS.** She is on the page once, at `854`, and said she was not going to give a working from a thing she had never done, and said the fourth line of her own charter is still not published and nobody has told her where it goes. **She said a seat is not a day and she did not ask for the stone to be put on a table.**
15. **CORVIN TACE, FORTY-THREE, WHO HAS KEPT THAT LANDING NINETEEN YEARS AND GIVES FOUR THINGS EVERY MORNING.** He withheld the fourth thing on the Sunday of the hundred and forty-third week and said he meant Monday and was not going to give it a day early. He gave it on the Monday and named the last morning he gave it out loud, **the first day of the hundred and forty-first week**, and said out loud that he had been wrong by one day for about four of these weeks and why, and that nobody had used it against him and a man of thirty-one had not raised it. He gave the number of the off-morning for the twenty-fourth time at the seventh hour on the Sunday of the hundred and forty-fourth week, and the next one in eight days, which is the Monday of the hundred and forty-fifth week. **He noticed on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-fourth week that the fever had been a whole number of weeks since Tuesday and said he had not known until the seventh hour.**
16. **⚠ THE PEOPLE BAND 1 BROUGHT ONTO THAT LANDING FOR THE FIRST TIME, ⚠ AND ⚠ EVERY NAME AND AGE WAS MADE IN A MOUTH ON THE MORNING IT WAS SPOKEN: ⚠ **SENA DORR, FORTY-NINE**, ⚠ THE NINTH, A PUBLIC ARCHIVE AND NOT A STONE, ⚠ WHO CAME DOWN ON THE ORDINARY CART BECAUSE SHE WOULD NOT HAVE IT CARRIED.** ⚠ NONE OF THEM IS A NEW ANTAGONIST AND NONE OF THEM IS ON A SEAT THEY DID NOT ASK FOR, AND ⚠ THE FIGURE OF ABOUT NINE OF THEM IS A FIGURE OF A BANK.**
17. **⚠ AND ⚠ NOT ONE PERSON IS RELIEVED, FORGIVEN, REDEEMED OR THANKED IN THESE TEN CHAPTERS, ⚠ AND `sorry` `grateful` `worth it` `redeemed` `forgiven` ARE AT **ZERO** ACROSS ALL TEN, ⚠ AND ⚠ `thanked` IS AT **NINE** AND ⚠ EVERY ONE OF THE NINE IS A REFUSAL OR A LIST OF WHO IS NOT TO BE THANKED, ⚠ AND ⚠ THE MAN WHO REFUSED A FINAL EXCEPTION IN VOLUME 17 IS NOBODY'S ON THE LAST PAGE OF THIS BAND AS HE IS ON THE FIRST.**
