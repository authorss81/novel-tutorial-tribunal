## Verdict

The batch does the thing it was asked to do, and the reviewer who checked it was right about almost all of what it found. Ten finished scenes, each with its own ground, its own resistance, a changed situation and a consequence. Compliance with the hard guardrails is good: no weekday, no bold in running prose, no meta prose, in-world documents correctly block-quoted, no restaging of the toll bar or Kearn's withdrawn name as a scene, no new enemy, Quiet Chamber and Bridgeward untouched. **Twenty-three hard findings and seven soft ones were returned and all are fixed.**

**The finding that matters most is not in this batch at all. It is in the writer phase's own closure claim.** `state/current.md` and `state/batch-summary.md` asserted that forty-two hard and eighteen soft findings had been found and were "all fixed", and instructed later readers not to reopen the batch. A second, independent pass then found that **at least six of the claimed fixes were not in the files**: the salt seller's trading years, the docket board's date, the water rate's start date, the unsatisfiable twelfth and thirteenth of the fourth month, the typographic breaks, and a wrong figure and a wrong date in Chapter 90's close. That pattern is the same one that closed Batch 0003 and it is now recorded as a standing instruction in `state/current.md`: **audit on your own evidence and take no closure claim on trust, including this one.**

## Hard findings, fixed

- **The hundred and fifty hauls belonged to two women.** They are Wenna Tace's own unpaid hauls of the last eleven months, canon since Chapter 64, and Chapter 83's entry block still said so. Chapters 86, 88 and 90 gave the same figure to Bryn Sallow for her thirty years, and the reading at the pump announced that the hundred and fifty had come from the gate. Five state files carried the collision forward. **Fixed by giving the gate hand a figure that is hers, larger, and got the only way a person at a gate can get one** — six thousand hauls at two thirds, from watching the barrow go past about two hundred times a year for thirty years, with her own sentence about there being no arithmetic in the county that turns ten and a half hours into a barrow. The two debts are now read out side by side at one pump in one unwritable unit, which is a better scene than the collision was, and `state/continuity.md` now forbids the hundred and fifty being given to the gate hand.
- **A paragraph repeated verbatim at the end of Chapter 88**, the only exact duplicate prose in the batch. One copy kept.
- **Chapter 89 was dated the twentieth where its own arithmetic proves the twenty-fifth** — fourteen March to twenty-fifth April is the forty-two days the chapter asserts three times. Six places corrected, and the record headed with a single bell is now headed with the span the sitting actually ran.
- **The Chapter 86 slate could not produce the sum printed under it.** Sixteen thousand two hundred hours is what the tide correction *takes out*, not what is left; 113,400 × 6/7 = 97,200 h, × 3d = £1,215 exactly. The line now says it takes sixteen thousand two hundred out and names the ninety-seven thousand two hundred that remain, which is what `state/continuity.md` already said and what the reader is owed.
- **The Fallowditch letter carried three mutually impossible dates** — written on the 28th, arrived on the 27th, and stamped on the 2nd of the fifth after twenty days on a tray. Collapsed to one document: written on the thirteenth in the room where the nine sheets were read, down to the fen road on the fourteenth, stamped in a chamber on the twenty-seventh. `state/continuity.md` now says there is one document and one date and forbids a second.
- **Chapter 85's whole opening scene was in straight quotation marks**, with one speech that never closed, in a manuscript that is curly-quoted throughout. Converted.
- **Chapter 81 dated the review to the wrong month four times** — the twenty-seventh of the month before last, which from the second of the fourth month is February. The review is the twenty-seventh of the third month and is now "last month" in the prose, matching the six-days figure three lines below it that already assumed it.
- **Chapter 89 said £96 of timber bought a bridge a deck.** The four new timbers are lying in the mud on the north bank and the chapter's whole argument is that £4 15 was cheaper. Corrected, and the washer is no longer a **district** of sixty-one people; sixty-one is her age and she is now on the list as a person the price fell on.
