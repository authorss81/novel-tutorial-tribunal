## 2. The two resolution lines, checked against the text

`outline/series.md` assigns Volume 02 exactly two resolution lines. Neither is softened here.

### 2.1 *"The appeal survives as a durable right, and it survives by costing somebody."*

**DELIVERED, AND NOT SECURE.**

The mechanism is visible, ordinary, and unpaid by the person it protects, and all three halves are on the page:

- **Visible and ordinary.** A page kept in a room over a pump, kept by a person, read aloud twice a week by a boy of nineteen who is not paid (Ch 83, Ch 100). A rate on a barrel lid, read at the sixth bell on the first of every quarter (69, 88, 100). A definition of what a load is, in a bench of four's own book (97). A rule that any person of the district may ask for the book to be read aloud to them and is not to be asked why (90). A bench of three eleven miles off with a room of its own and a boundary that is not going to be done this spring (84).
- **Unpaid by the person it protects.** Chapter 97: the ninth bridge's district pays £44 16s a year, out of £410, and the year as set out at the review leaves £44 4s, and **the charter costs twelve shillings more than what is left**, and £105 — a hand under a deck — is in a different line and cannot be moved into the charter's line without a minute of a bench four miles away. Chapter 89: a public answer cost **£4 15 for twenty-two names in four months**, entered against a road fund that is not the district asked. The holder is paid about **£1 11 a quarter** for keeping nine sheets and a page.
- **It survives by costing somebody, and the somebody is named.** Chapter 99, Maren Kett, in a room, on the twenty-fourth of the sixth: four of the ten things cost somebody an hour a day or a day a week or four days of a man's sweeping; one cost a woman a pitch at the bottom of a slipway; one cost a recordwright a shop, two rooms and a business; **one cost a man of thirty-one the only thing his body ever gave back.** Not one of the ten is a ruling. **Not one would survive a district being squeezed.**
- **The finding of the volume, delivered as a consequence and not as a ruling.** Chapter 100, the sixth bell, on a mud bank: *a page is for keeping the record of a duty, and I did not find that out from the old thing. I found it out from a man with a bad hand… and from a clerk who entered the cost of a sitting against the fund of a district four miles away, and from a woman who said out loud that there is no day in this district that is a day.* Nobody improved it.

**It is not secure, and the text says so in four separate places:** the holder may not be a party, may not appeal, may not appear in support of one, and may not be named in one (68, 96, 100); a rate in a bench's own book cannot be carried by anybody (96); and Meridith Vale has said out loud, in advance, with a date, that in the spring he will write the charter **below a hand under a deck** (97).

**It is *not* "delivered and not secure" in the sense of a hedge.** It is this: the right exists, it is in use, it cost eight named people real things, and it will not survive being squeezed. That is a definite answer, and the volume gives reasons for it in every direction.

### 2.2 *"Sera Quill recovers a fragment of the original Quiet Chamber terms."*

**DELIVERED — as a fragment, not the clause.**

Entered under licence, dated and signed, on the facing leaf beside the empty slot and **not** on the removed page, which is not on the shelf and has not been since before she was born (Ch 94, line 81):

> **[  ] OF THE ENTRY IS A DUTY OF THE WARD, AND [  ]**

- **It is a fragment, not the clause.** Chapter 94, line 83: *I enter this as one quarter of a sentence. I am not entering it as a clause. It is not the clause. It does not restore any right of anybody to answer no, and the first line did not restore it, and this does not restore it, and it was not restored by anything in four months and it is not restored tonight.*
- **It is ten words.** Counted on the text: OF · THE · ENTRY · IS · A · DUTY · OF · THE · WARD · AND. The number **nine** appears nowhere in the manuscript or the state record for this fragment; that claim is correct and is confirmed.
- **The printed form gives away eleven words and the leaf gives ten, and the two are never the same length.** Chapter 93, line 41: *AND THE KEEPING OF IT IS A DUTY OF THE WARD* — AND · THE · KEEPING · OF · IT · IS · A · DUTY · OF · THE · WARD = **eleven**. Chapter 94, line 65: *they are eleven words against ten, and the difference is in the middle.* ✓
- **She does not use the form to fill the crease.** Chapter 94, lines 85 and 87: *A form is a copy made by a person who had to leave a volume and put down what they could carry, and a copy is not a witness, and a witness is a person, and a person may be examined, and a form cannot be examined by anybody ever.* And she read the leaf for three days with the form face down under the weight of the volume, turned it over on the fourth, and entered the order of the work, *because a clerk in twenty years who finds the comparison first will think the form came first, and it did not.* ✓
- **Read as a duty and not as a place**, and the consequence lands on a person: *the person standing nearest in four miles is a woman of twenty-nine with a barrow* (94, line 134).
- **The historical question is not answered.** Nobody named the First Witness, nobody identified a responsible person, and the authorship question and the Shale Mirror were not touched. Chapter 93 establishes that the Quiet Chamber's terms were *entered by hand four hundred years ago* and **were never printed in this city**; what has been printed, three times in four years, is a *form for lodging a matter* in that chamber, made by a shop filling a space in a docket. The archive work is about paper and not about a person, and the volume says so on the page.

**The right of refusal is not restored** and the text says so four times (94 line 83, 94 line 154, 100 line 84, 100 line 190). **The removed page and her missing duplicate remain two events on two dates** (95, lines 31 and 39, with both dates entered and the reason entered twice). **Her family claim of six years is unfiled, unadvanced, and unresolved** (95; she wrote two letters with no reason in them and did not open the book on the sixteenth).

### 3.1 The continuity line — counted against the chapters, not the summary

**Ten appearances. Seven refusals. No eleventh appearance has crept in. Nobody named. The holder has not read it.** All four verified on the text.

Appearances, by the volume's own numbering: 6th at Ch 70 (line 95, *"the finding of the sixth appearance"*; and line 79, *"it was the sixth time"*); 7th at Ch 80 (line 149); 8th at Ch 85 (line 63, and Ch 90 confirms the count forward); 9th at Ch 90 (line 107, *"It is the ninth time"*); 10th at Ch 100 (line 9, *"That is the tenth time"*). That is five in Volume 02 and the volume's own arithmetic requires five in Volume 01, which is what Volume 01 supplies (Ch 1, 2, 7, 39, 49 — Ch 49 line 71 calls itself the fifth and says the four before it all said *stated*). **Ch 13 carries the line in bold on a document; the manuscript's own count at Ch 39 ("a cadence Ilyan had heard three times") and Ch 49 ("heard four times") does not include it. That is a Volume 01 matter and is out of this audit's scope, but it is the reason the count closes at ten and not eleven, and it is recorded here so a later pass does not "correct" it.**

**The holder has still not read the line under the fifth term of the copy in her own room** (Ch 100, line 26; Ch 94, line 138; Ch 90, line 113; Ch 85). Her reason is the whole of the best small mechanism in this case: she reads a charter in order and a charter ends at the fifth term. **Nobody in this case will tell her and nobody did.** ✓

**One hard defect in the refusal count.** Chapter 70, line 87, says of the refusal in that chapter: *"this was the sixth time and it was the same words"* — while the same chapter calls it, at line 95, *"the finding of the sixth appearance."* The appearance count and the refusal count have been conflated.
