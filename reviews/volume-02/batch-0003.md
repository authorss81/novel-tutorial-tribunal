## Hard findings, fixed

- **Chapter 80, the fund figure contradicted itself by one line.** Rell said the district "has four pounds and sixteen" where Sedge had said forty-four pounds and four shillings one line earlier, where the disposition computed forty-four pounds and four shillings, and where four state files record forty-four pounds and four shillings. Re-derived in farthings: 393,600 − (201,600 + 14,400 + 43,008 + 92,160) = 42,432 farthings = **44 lb 4 s**, short of the props by 49,728 = **51 lb 16 s**. The disposition is right. Rell's figure was the charter's separate cost of forty-four pounds and sixteen, run into the wrong mouth. Corrected to forty-four pounds and four shillings, and `state/continuity.md` now carries an explicit warning that the two figures sit four lines apart in the same document and must not be run together.
- **Chapter 80, the same line contradicted itself on hours.** Sedge says *four hours a day*; Ilyan says *four hours a day*; Rell says *an hour a day*, in the same exchange, in the breath before the record Sedge is about to ask to have entered. A half day is four hours, and four is what the guild actually took. Corrected.
- **Chapter 80, a three-day date drift on the batch's most human beat.** Sedge, on the twenty-seventh: "I shall tell him at the second bell tomorrow with my own mouth" — the twenty-eighth. Ilyan, in the last scene, dated the first of the fourth month: "I am going to be standing here when Oren Sedge tells him at the second bell tomorrow morning" — the second. Both cannot be true, and if Sedge had kept to the twenty-eighth then Blore would have been informed four days before the batch ended, contradicting `state/open-threads.md` and the Batch 0004 prompt, which both hold that he does not know yet, and which the Chapter 0082 card depends on. **Fixed by removing the date, not the intention.** Sedge now says it will not go in a letter — a letter from a trade comes at the change and a man on a deck reads it standing up and takes it for a summons about something else — and that he will say it at the second bell the first morning he can get down to the cut banks and stand in front of the man. Ilyan's line now promises to be standing at the gate when Sedge goes down, with no date. The ethic of the beat, a guild man telling a man in his own mouth rather than a clerk reading it out of a page, is intact and slightly better for being undated. `state/continuity.md` now states that Blore has not been told, that no date may be pinned to the telling, and that he is not to be informed in a scene.
- **Chapter 77, an off-by-one in the enumeration.** "The other six of the seven" was followed by five reasons, one reason, and one non-answer — seven items for six benches. Secondary: the middle reason (three people, no money, one out on a boundary) is Fallowditch's own, in the same paragraph that had just given Fallowditch's reason to four others. Now four for Fallowditch's reason, one repeating the hundred-and-sixty-year-old book it named the first time, and one non-answer: four plus one plus one is six, and those five plus Fallowditch's own withdrawal are the six of the seven who answered with reasons, which is what `state/continuity.md` and `state/open-threads.md` already said. Both state entries now carry the working so the count cannot drift again.

The charter was made at the ninth bell on the seventeenth; the scene is the thirtieth. That is Volume 01, Chapters 21–40, which name weekdays as canon and consistently — the levy, the count book, eight chapters. The guardrail begins at Chapter 41. **No canon prose and no summary was altered**; instead `state/continuity.md` now bounds the guardrail explicitly, records that Chapters 21–40 name weekdays by design, and states that the Chapter 0031 entry is a fact about a canon chapter and not a licence to copy forward.
- **The re-entry guard was stronger than the evidence supported.** This is the finding that plausibly explains the other three. `state/current.md` asserted that "an independent review pass has already been run against this batch and is closed", listed forty hard and nineteen soft findings, and instructed **"Do not reopen any of these."** That pass ran *inside the writer phase* (`logs/batch-0003.log:1051`–`1054`); it was the writer marking its own work. The pipeline's review phase had committed nothing and `reviews/volume-02/` did not exist. A reviewer reading that guard as authoritative has a standing instruction to suppress findings, which is the most likely reason a self-audit that had already caught a Chapter 77 device breach did not catch a fund figure, a doubled line, a date drift, or a miscount. **Rewritten.** It now says a self-audit was run and is not a review, records what that pass fixed, states that the paragraph is not authority over any later reader, names the four hard and four soft findings the review phase went on to return, and tells the next reader to audit Chapters 71–80 on its own evidence. `state/batch-summary.md`'s review section is scoped the same way and now carries the review phase's ten findings in full.
