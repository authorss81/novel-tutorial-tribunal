## 2c. WHAT CHAPTERS 821–830 CHANGED IN THESE PEOPLE, AND EVERY NAME, AGE AND FIGURE IN IT WAS MADE IN A MOUTH, IN FRONT OF ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, WITH THE WORKING SAID OUT LOUD

**A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE WHO SAID OUT LOUD, FOR THE FIRST TIME ON THAT BANK, THAT HE WANTS SOMEBODY ELSE TO CARRY THE FIGURES, AND WHO SAID THAT A THING WITH A NAIL IN IT WOULD TAKE THE ELEVENTH HOUR OFF HIM, AND WHO SAID THAT A LESSER MAN THAN HIM WOULD HAVE SAID YES ON THE CHILD ALONE. ⚠ A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE WHO SAID OUT LOUD, AT `826`, THAT **IT WORKS**, IN ABOUT NINE EARS, FOR THE FIRST TIME IN FIVE DAYS, ⚠ AND WHO NAMED THE COST OF THAT SAYING: HE HAS SAID IN ABOUT NINE EARS, FOUR TIMES, THAT HE IS THE MAN EVERY FIGURE COMES OUT OF, AND A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR SAID AT `824` THAT FOUR IS TOO MANY.**

**A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR IN AN ASH WHO CAME UP A BANK UNASKED AT `824` TO SAY WHAT A THING HE HAD NOT READ HAS TO DO WITH HIM, WHICH IS NOTHING, ⚠ AND WHO SAID AT `826` THAT A LINE IN THAT THING MEANS THERE IS NO SUCH THING AS BEING WRONG ABOUT THIS, ⚠ AND WHO NAMED THE SHAPE OF IT OUT LOUD RATHER THAN NAMING IT A THREAT, AND SAID HE COULD NOT GET PAST THE SHAPE AND HAD BEEN TRYING SINCE THE EIGHTH HOUR. ⚠ A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR IN AN ASH WHO PUT A CONDITION ON A WOMAN OF SIXTY-FOUR, HEARD HER SAY NO, AND DID IT ANYWAY, AND WHO AT `826` REPEATED BACK WHAT SHE SAID TO HIM NINE FEET OFF HER AND DID NOT SOFTEN IT.**

**A WOMAN OF TWENTY-EIGHT WHO ANSWERED A BOY OF THIRTEEN'S QUESTION BEFORE THE MAN WHO HAD HAD IT TWO DAYS, ⚠ AND WHO SAID WHAT IT COST HER AND WHO SHE HAD NOT TOLD, ⚠ AND WHO ASKED FOR NOTHING IN RETURN, ⚠ AND WHO AT `829` NAMED A QUESTION THAT HAD BEEN GOING ROUND THAT BANK AND SAID SHE HAD SAID NO TO BEING ASKED IT THREE TIMES. ⚠ A WOMAN OF TWENTY-EIGHT WHO SAID SHE IS NOT GOING TO BE THE ONE WHO SAYS NO TO A SICK CHILD, IN FRONT OF ABOUT NINE OF THEM, AND ASKED A QUESTION INSTEAD, AND WHO HAS STILL NOT SAID THE ANSWER TO THE SECOND OF HER TWO THINGS IN ABOUT NINE EARS.**

**A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR WHO CANNOT READ, WHO ASKED THAT A THING BE READ A LEAF AT A TIME AND GAVE THE REASON, ⚠ AND WHO PUT ON THE PAGE, FOR THE FIRST TIME IN NINETEEN YEARS, THE REASON HE HAS NEVER SAID NO TO A PERSON — BECAUSE A YES FROM FOUR HUNDRED MILES IS NOT A YES, AND A YES IS THE MAN WHO COULD HAVE SAID NO — ⚠ AND WHO HEARD A FOURTH LINE HE COULD NOT READ BY THE SOUND OF THE PAPER, ⚠ AND WHO SAID THREE LEAVES IS A MORNING AND FOUR IS NOT, AND DECIDED BEFORE THE LEAF WAS OPENED. ⚠ A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR WHO HAS ASKED FOR A HUNDRED AND NINE MORNINGS AND HAS BEEN GRANTED NOTHING AND REFUSED NOTHING, ⚠ AND WHOSE NAME IS ON THE FIRST LEAF OF A THING THAT CAME OUT OF THE DARK, ⚠ AND WHO WAS OFFERED THE ONE THING HE HAS ASKED FOR A HUNDRED MORNINGS FOR, AND TOOK NOTHING AND LOST NOTHING BY IT.**

**A MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT WHO SAID *NOT THIS WEEK* AND NOT *NO*, HEARD HIMSELF SAY THE DIFFERENCE, AND TOLD ABOUT NINE OF THEM HE HAD NOTICED IT HIMSELF, ⚠ AND WHO IS THE ONLY ONE OF THE FOUR OF THEM WHO MIGHT HAVE THE ANSWER TO A BOY OF THIRTEEN'S QUESTION AND DID NOT GIVE IT.**

**A MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-TWO WHO SAID OUT LOUD, FOR THE FIRST TIME IN ELEVEN YEARS, THAT A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE IS THE ONLY PERSON IN THE ROOM WHO GETS TO DECIDE WHEN A THING HAS BEEN SAID ENOUGH, ⚠ AND WHO NAMED A FIGURE OF FIVE FOR THE THINGS HE CANNOT CHECK, ⚠ AND WHO SAID ON A SUNDAY HE IS GOING TO READ OUT THE EIGHT MORNINGS OF HIS OWN LIFE ON WHICH HIS OWN SHEET OF DEAL HAD A MARK PUT ON IT, AND THAT THOSE EIGHT ARE NOT A BOY OF THIRTEEN'S EIGHT, AND THAT NEITHER ONE IS A FIGURE OF ANYBODY.**

**A BOY OF THIRTEEN WHO ASKED HOW A MARK GETS MADE, WAS NOT ANSWED FOR TWO DAYS, SAID HE WOULD ASK AGAIN AND WOULD NOTICE WHO DID NOT ANSWER, ⚠ AND WHO GAVE A FIGURE OF EIGHT TWICE ON PURPOSE AND WOULD NOT SAY WHICH EIGHT MORNINGS, ⚠ AND WHO IS TAKING HIS QUESTION FOUR HUNDRED YARDS UP A CART ROAD TO THE ONLY PERSON ON THAT ROAD WHO HAS EVER BEEN IN THE WAY OF A MARK, AND IS NOT GOING ALONE, AND IS NOT GOING TO ASK A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE TO COME, BECAUSE IF HE COMES IT IS A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE ASKING AND NOT A BOY OF THIRTEEN ASKING. ⚠ A BOY OF THIRTEEN WHO GAVE A FIGURE OF FOUR THAT NOBODY ASKED HIM FOR, NAMED ALL FOUR, AND SAID IT IS A FIGURE OF FOUR PEOPLE AND NOT A FIGURE OF THIS BANK.**

**A WOMAN OF TWENTY-NINE IN A REED WHO SAID THAT WHAT A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE CAME BACK DOWN FOUR MILES WITH WAS NOT AN ANSWER AND WAS NOT NOTHING, ⚠ AND WHO STOPPED A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE IN ABOUT NINE EARS AND MADE HIM TAKE TWO HALVES OF A SENTENCE APART, ⚠ AND WHO SAID **THE GAME WAS A BRIDGE AND IT WAS NOT A SOURCE**, ⚠ AND WHO SAID US IS FOUR PEOPLE AND IS NOT GOING TO BE FIVE BECAUSE A THING ON A FLAT HAS A NAIL IN IT, ⚠ AND WHO SAID SHE IS A FIGURE OF ONE PERSON IN A REED AND IS NOT A PERSON WHO CAN BE ASKED TO BE CHECKED.**

**A WOMAN OF SIXTY-FOUR WHO GAVE THE WHOLE OF NINETEEN YEARS AWAY AT `815` AND WHO AT `826` AND `830` GAVE IT BACK TO HERSELF, — THE NINETEEN YEARS ARE MINE, THEY ARE NOT EVIDENCE, THEY ARE NOT A THING THE ARRANGEMENT CAN HOLD OR NOT HOLD — ⚠ AND WHO SAID THE NINETEEN YEARS SHE GAVE AWAY IN THE SAME WEEK AND THE ELEVENTH LINE OF A THING ON A FLAT ARE NOT THE SAME THING, ⚠ AND WHO SAID SHE BELIEVES THE NINTH LINE AND WILL KEEP BELIEVING IT, ⚠ AND WHO SAID SHE IS NOT AFRAID OF THE ELEVENTH LINE AND IS ALSO TIRED OF IT, ⚠ AND WHO SAID WHAT SHE ACTUALLY HAS IS THAT NOBODY IN THIS COUNTY CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT ANYTHING, WHICH IS NOT ON A LEAF. ⚠ THE WORD IS AT **ZERO** OCCURRENCES IN ALL TEN CHAPTERS AGAIN.**

**A MAN OF FORTY-FOUR WHO ASKED FOR A THING TO BE READ WITHOUT BEING ASKED WHETHER HE WANTS IT, ⚠ AND WHO SAID THE THING TO ARGUE ABOUT IS THE ROAD AND NOT A MAN, ⚠ AND WHO SAID IF ABOUT NINE OF US SPEND A WEEK ON A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE AND NONE OF IT ON A FLOOR, THEN AT THE END OF THE WEEK THE FLOOR IS EXACTLY WHERE IT IS, ⚠ AND WHO GAVE FOUR TERMS FOR GOING WITH A BOY OF THIRTEEN UP A CART ROAD AND SAID THEY ARE NOT NEGOTIABLE ON THE FLAT. ⚠ HIS MISTAKE IS THE FIFTH OF THE SAME FIFTEEN FIGURES HE CARRIES, AND IT IS THE WEDNESDAY ONE.**

**A WOMAN OF FORTY WHO SAID THE THING THAT CAME DOWN IN THE NIGHT IS THE ANSWER TO A QUESTION SHE ASKED TWENTY-EIGHT DAYS AGO, GAVE THE DATE OF THE ASKING WITH THE SUBTRACTION SPOKEN, ⚠ AND SAID I DID NOT WANT IT TO ARRIVE TODAY AND THAT IS NOT A REASON AGAINST IT, ⚠ AND WHO STILL WILL NOT NAME A ROOM AND WAS ASKED THREE TIMES IN ELEVEN YEARS AND DID NOT.**

**A WOMAN OF FORTY-FOUR WHO DECLINED TO PUT A THING WITH FOUR LEAVES IN HER APRON, GAVE THE REASON, — PUTTING IT THERE WOULD BE ME DECIDING THAT IT IS THE SAME KIND OF THING AS THE PAPER IN MY OTHER POCKET, AND IT IS NOT — ⚠ AND WHO SAID IT IS NOT A THING SHE IS GOING TO BE THE KEEPER OF, AND THAT SHE IS A KEEPER OF CHAIRS AND A LEAF IS A THING AND NOBODY CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT A LEAF. ⚠ THE CRUMB OF BREAD IS IN ONE POCKET AND THE PAPER IS IN THE OTHER AND SHE HAS CHECKED.**

**A MAN OF SIXTY-ONE WHO READ FOUR LEAVES IN ABOUT NINE EARS ON THREE MORNINGS, ONE LEAF A MORNING, AT A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR'S REQUEST, ⚠ AND WHO READ THE NINTH LINE TWICE, ⚠ AND WHO SAID HE WILL READ THE FOURTH WHOLE OR NOT AT ALL AND THAT THERE IS NO FIFTH WAY, ⚠ AND WHO SAID HE IS READING IT BECAUSE A WOMAN OF TWENTY-EIGHT ASKED HIM WHETHER HE WAS GOING TO READ IT ON HIS OWN — AND THAT SHE DID NOT ASK HIM TO READ IT, SHE ASKED HIM NOT TO READ IT ALONE, AND THAT HE HEARD THE DIFFERENCE. ⚠ AND WHO HAS TWO NUMBERS IN ELEVEN YEARS AND STILL WILL NOT WRITE IT IN HIS BOOK.**

**A MAN OF FORTY-THREE WHO FOUND A THING ON THE FLAT OF THAT LANDING AND SAID NOBODY SAW IT COME IN BECAUSE THE BANK WAS UNDER ALL NIGHT, ⚠ AND WHO FOUND A PRESSED MARK AT THE BOTTOM OF IT AND SAID HE HAS SEEN FORTY-ONE OF THOSE IN NINETEEN YEARS AND THAT IT IS THE ONLY FIGURE ON THAT FLAT HE CAN PUT HIS HAND ON, ⚠ AND WHO SAID HE WILL NOT SAY WHAT HE THINKS THE MARK IS FOR, ⚠ AND WHO GAVE THE FIGURE FOR THE NEXT TIME AS A FIGURE WITH A RATE ON IT AND SAID AT `830` THAT IT IS DERIVABLE FROM THE RATE AND DOES NOT NEED HIM, AND THAT HE IS NOT SURE HE LIKES THAT.**
