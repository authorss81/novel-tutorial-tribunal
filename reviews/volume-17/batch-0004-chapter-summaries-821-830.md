## 821–830 — VOLUME 17, BAND 0003, CHAPTERS 821–830, TEN PARAGRAPHS, ONE PER CHAPTER, AND EVERY FIGURE IN THEM TAKEN FROM THE CHAPTER IT IS IN AND NOT FROM ANY OTHER FILE

**821 — A FRIDAY, THE FOURTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINTH WEEK, HIS FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FIRST MORNING, FOUR HUNDRED AND TWENTY-ONE DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, THE FEVER SEVENTY-SIX WEEKS AND SIX DAYS, THE BANK ABOUT TWELVE INCHES OVER THE TOP STEP AND THE NEXT TIME ALL NINETY STEPS CAN BE WALKED IN FIVE DAYS. A MAN OF FORTY-THREE FINDS A FOLDED THING WITH A NAIL THROUGH THE CORNER OF IT ON THE FLAT OF THAT BANK AT ABOUT THE SIXTH HOUR AND SAYS NOBODY SAW IT COME IN, BECAUSE THE BANK WAS UNDER TWELVE INCHES ALL NIGHT AND NOBODY HAD BEEN ON IT. A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR WHO CANNOT READ ASKS FOR IT **A LEAF AT A TIME** AND GIVES THE REASON, WHICH IS THAT FOUR LEAVES IN ONE MORNING IS A MORNING IN WHICH NOBODY CAN SAY NO, AND HIS FIGURE IS **A HUNDRED MORNINGS**, ONE MORE THAN NINETY-NINE, THE ELEVENTH MORNING OF A RUN THAT BEGAN ON THE FIRST DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-EIGHTH WEEK, `811`. A MAN OF SIXTY-ONE READS THE FIRST LEAF IN ABOUT NINE EARS: AN EXCEPTION, THREE THINGS THAT CAN BE DONE, A REFUSAL ENTERED IN A BOOK, A BOOK THAT CAN BE CORRECTED, AND AT THE FOOT OF IT A WORKING ABOUT A ROAD WITH **HIS OWN NAME ON IT**. HE SAYS A YES FROM FOUR HUNDRED MILES IS NOT A YES. A WOMAN OF FORTY SAYS IT IS THE ANSWER TO A QUESTION SHE ASKED TWENTY-EIGHT DAYS AGO AND THAT SHE DID NOT WANT IT AND STILL WILL NOT NAME A ROOM. A MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-TWO CHECKS TWO FIGURES AND CANNOT CHECK NINE DAYS, BECAUSE THERE IS NO LINE UNDER HIM AT THE TOP END OF THAT CART ROAD. A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE SAYS OUT LOUD, FOR THE FIRST TIME, THAT HE WANTS SOMEBODY ELSE TO CARRY THE FIGURES, AND NAMES THE THIRTY-THIRD OF THE SAME MISTAKE.**

**822 — A SATURDAY, THE FIFTH DAY OF THAT WEEK, HIS FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SECOND MORNING, THE FEVER **SEVENTY-SEVEN WEEKS OLD EXACTLY**, THE BANK SIXTEEN INCHES, A FULL BANK, FOUR DAYS OF THE COMING BACK, NEXT TIME IN FOUR DAYS. A MAN OF FORTY-FOUR ASKS FOR THE SECOND LEAF TO BE READ AND SAYS HE IS NOT GOING TO BE ASKED WHETHER HE WANTS IT. A BOY OF THIRTEEN, AT **A HUNDRED AND SIXTY-NINE DAYS**, GIVES A FIGURE OF **FOUR** THAT NOBODY ASKED HIM FOR: FOUR PEOPLE ON THAT BANK WHO COULD HAVE SEEN THAT THING COME IN ON FRIDAY NIGHT AND DID NOT, HE NAMES ALL FOUR WITH THE REASON EACH COULD HAVE SEEN IT, HE SAYS IT IS A FIGURE OF FOUR PEOPLE AND NOT OF THIS BANK, AND HE SAYS HE HAS BEEN KEEPING THREE DIFFERENT FOURS IN THREE PLACES SINCE WEDNESDAY. A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE ADMITS HE READ THE SECOND LEAF ALONE AT THE EIGHTH HOUR WITH THE WATER TO HIS ANKLES AND TOLD NOBODY, AND NAMES THAT AS THE THIRTY-FOURTH OF THE SAME MISTAKE. A MAN OF SIXTY-ONE READS THE THREE LINES THAT ANSWER A QUESTION: NINE DAYS, AN ENTRY IN A BOOK NAMING THE PERSON AND THE DAY AND THE THING REFUSED, AND A CORRECTION THAT REQUIRES NOBODY'S CONSENT. A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR SAYS THERE IS A FOURTH LINE BECAUSE HE HEARD THE PAPER TURN.**

**823 — A SUNDAY, THE SIXTH DAY OF THAT WEEK, HIS FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-THIRD MORNING, THE FEVER SEVENTY-SEVEN WEEKS AND ONE DAY, A FULL BANK HOLDING FOR THE SECOND MORNING, NEXT TIME IN THREE DAYS. A MAN OF SIXTY-ONE READS THE THIRD LEAF: **ONE CHILD OF SIX**, FOUR MILES UP THAT CART ROAD, WHO HAS NOT KEPT DOWN WHAT SHE HAS EATEN SINCE THE FOURTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SIXTH WEEK, **TWENTY-THREE DAYS**, WITH THE WORKING IN THE DOCUMENT, TREATABLE IN NINE DAYS, AND A LINE AT THE FOOT SAYING THE PERSON ON WHOM IT IS DONE **SHALL NOT BE ASKED TO CONSENT**. A WOMAN OF TWENTY-EIGHT, AT **A HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-ONE DAYS**, SAYS SHE DID NOT COME UP FOR THE READING, SAYS SHE IS NOT GOING TO BE THE ONE WHO SAYS NO TO A SICK CHILD IN FRONT OF ABOUT NINE OF THEM, AND ASKS A QUESTION INSTEAD: WHO WRITES DOWN THE DAY IT WAS DONE. A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE WALKS TWO STEPS TOWARD THAT CART ROAD AND IS STOPPED BY A MAN OF FORTY-THREE, WHO SAYS THE WATER IS OVER THE TURN UNTIL THE SECOND DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTIETH WEEK, AND GIVES THE WORKING. HE ACCEPTS IT, SAYS THE MAN OF FORTY-THREE IS RIGHT, AND SAYS THAT IS THE THIRTY-FOURTH OF THE SAME MISTAKE AGAIN.**

**824 — SEVENTH AND LAST DAY OF THAT WEEK, A MONDAY, HIS FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FOURTH MORNING, THE FEVER SEVENTY-SEVEN WEEKS AND TWO DAYS, A FULL BANK FOR THE THIRD MORNING, AND THE WATER COMES OFF ALL NINETY TOMORROW FOR THE **TWENTIETH** TIME, EIGHT DAYS ON FROM THE MONDAY. A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR IN AN ASH, **A HUNDRED AND NINETY-FOUR DAYS** IN, COMES UP THAT BANK **UNASKED** AND SAYS WHAT THE FOURTH LEAF HAS TO DO WITH HIM, WHICH IS **NOTHING**, BEFORE THE LEAF HAS BEEN READ, AND SAYS HE IS THE ONLY ONE OF THE FOUR OF THEM WHO LOSES SOMETHING IF IT IS ACCEPTED, AND THAT HE IS SAYING IT BEFORE ANYBODY WORKS IT OUT. A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR, AT **A HUNDRED AND THREE MORNINGS**, SAYS THREE LEAVES IS A MORNING AND FOUR IS NOT, AND SAYS HE WILL DECIDE ON THE SECOND DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTIETH WEEK AND IS TELLING BOTH LOTS NOW SO NOBODY CAN SAY HE WAS AMBUSHED. A WOMAN OF FORTY-FOUR SAYS THE FOURTH LEAF IS NOT GOING IN HER APRON AND IS NOT THE SAME KIND OF THING AS THE PAPER IN HER OTHER POCKET. A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE NAMES A MISTAKE **THAT IS NOT THE FENCE** AND SAYS HE IS GOING UP THAT CART ROAD AT THE SEVENTH HOUR TOMORROW.**

**825 — THE FIRST DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTIETH WEEK, A TUESDAY, HIS FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FIFTH MORNING, THE FEVER SEVENTY-SEVEN WEEKS AND THREE DAYS, THE WATER **OFF ALL NINETY** AT ABOUT THE SIXTH HOUR, THE **TWENTIETH** TIME SINCE THE SATURDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND NINETEENTH WEEK, NEXT TIME IN FOUR DAYS, GIVEN AS **A FIGURE WITH A RATE ON IT**. A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE WENT UP FOUR MILES AT THE SEVENTH HOUR AND CAME BACK DOWN AND SAYS A CHILD OF SIX IS SITTING UP AND THAT **NOBODY HAS TREATED HER** AND THAT HE LOOKED FOR A LINE WITH A DATE ON IT AND IT WAS NOT THERE. A WOMAN OF TWENTY-NINE IN A REED, AT **A HUNDRED AND NINETY-SIX DAYS**, SAYS THAT IS NOT AN ANSWER AND IS NOT NOTHING, THAT HE WENT TO CHECK A DESCRIPTION AND NOT TO CONTRADICT A DOCUMENT, AND THAT **US IS FOUR PEOPLE AND IS NOT GOING TO BE FIVE BECAUSE A THING ON A FLAT HAS A NAIL IN IT**. A MAN OF SIXTY-ONE SAYS THE FOURTH LEAF WILL BE READ WHOLE OR NOT AT ALL ON THE SECOND DAY OF THAT WEEK, AND THAT HE IS READING IT BECAUSE A WOMAN OF TWENTY-EIGHT ASKED HIM WHETHER HE WAS GOING TO READ IT ON HIS OWN. A BOY OF THIRTEEN GIVES A FIGURE OF **EIGHT** TWICE ON PURPOSE AND WOULD NOT SAY WHICH EIGHT MORNINGS, AND SAYS IT IS A COUNT OF TIMES AND NOT A CLOCK.**

**826 — THE SECOND DAY OF THAT WEEK, A WEDNESDAY, HIS FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SIXTH MORNING, THE FEVER SEVENTY-SEVEN WEEKS AND FOUR DAYS, ABOUT FOUR INCHES BACK ON THE FLAT AND THE NEXT TIME IN THREE DAYS. A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR SAYS HE DECIDED **BEFORE THE LEAF WAS OPENED**. A MAN OF SIXTY-ONE READS THE FOURTH LEAF WHOLE — ELEVEN LINES, COUNTED TWICE — AND READS THE NINTH LINE TWICE: **NO DAY IS ENTERED, NO PERSON IS NAMED IN ANY BOOK, NO PERSON MAY BE SENT FOR**; AND THE TENTH: ANY QUESTION ANSWERED IN THE OPEN; AND THE ELEVENTH: **NO ANSWER MAY BE QUESTIONED, AND THE ONLY REMEDY FOR A PERSON WHO DOES NOT ACCEPT AN ANSWER IS THAT THE PERSON REMAINS.** A WOMAN OF SIXTY-FOUR SAYS THE NINETEEN YEARS SHE GAVE AWAY IN THE SAME WEEK AND THAT ELEVENTH LINE **ARE NOT THE SAME THING**, THAT SHE BELIEVES THE NINTH LINE, AND THAT WHAT SHE ACTUALLY HAS IS THAT NOBODY IN THIS COUNTY CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT ANYTHING, **WHICH IS NOT ON A LEAF**. A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR IN AN ASH, **A HUNDRED AND NINETY-SIX DAYS** IN, SAYS LINE ELEVEN MEANS **THERE IS NO SUCH THING AS BEING WRONG ABOUT THIS**, THAT IT IS NOT A THREAT **BECAUSE NOTHING HAS BEEN TAKEN OFF HIM THIS MORNING**, AND THAT IT IS **A THING THAT IS ONLY GOOD IF YOU SAY YES**, AND THAT HE COULD NOT GET PAST THE SHAPE. A WOMAN OF TWENTY-EIGHT SAYS SHE IS GOING TO LOOK AT THE SECOND OF HER TWO THINGS TODAY. A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE SAYS, OUT LOUD AND FOR THE FIRST TIME IN FIVE DAYS, **THAT IT WORKS**, AND THAT A LESSER MAN WOULD HAVE SAID YES ON FRIDAY MORNING ON THE CHILD ALONE, AND ASKS ABOUT NINE OF THEM TO REMEMBER HE SAID IT.**

**827 — THE THIRD DAY OF THAT WEEK, A THURSDAY, HIS FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SEVENTH MORNING, THE FEVER SEVENTY-SEVEN WEEKS AND FIVE DAYS, ABOUT EIGHT INCHES AND TWO DAYS, NEXT TIME IN TWO DAYS. A MAN OF FORTY-THREE SAYS A THING HE DID NOT SAY ON THE MORNING THE LEAF WAS READ: THERE IS **A PRESSED MARK AT THE BOTTOM OF IT, THE KIND A BAILIFF'S STAMP LEAVES**, AND HE HAS SEEN **FORTY-ONE** OF THEM IN NINETEEN YEARS, AND IT IS THE ONLY FIGURE ON THAT FLAT HE CAN PUT HIS HAND ON, AND HE WILL NOT SAY WHAT HE THINKS IT IS FOR. A BOY OF THIRTEEN, AT **A HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FOUR DAYS**, ASKS **HOW DOES A MARK GET MADE** AND IS NOT ANSWED, AND SAYS HE WILL ASK AGAIN ON FRIDAY. A MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-TWO NAMES **THE FIFTH THING HE CANNOT CHECK** SO THAT ABOUT FOUR OF THEM CAN COUNT THEM, THE FIFTH BEING THAT MARK. A MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT SAYS HE HAS A BOARD IN THE INSIDE OF HIS COAT WITH THIRTY-TWO NOTCHES, THAT HE IS THE ONLY ONE WHO MIGHT HAVE THE ANSWER, THAT HE IS NOT GIVING IT, **NOT THIS WEEK**, AND THAT HE HEARD HIMSELF SAY THE DIFFERENCE. A MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-TWO SAYS OUT LOUD, FOR THE FIRST TIME IN ELEVEN YEARS, THAT A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE IS THE ONLY PERSON IN THE ROOM WHO GETS TO DECIDE WHEN A THING HAS BEEN SAID ENOUGH. A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE AGREES, SAYS WHAT HE BUILT AND WHERE: **A POST AT A CART ROAD, ABOUT FOUR HUNDRED YARDS UP, AT THE TURN, WITH A FIGURE OF THE WEATHER AND THE ROAD AND THE CARTS ON A BOARD** — A FIGURE OF THINGS AND NOT OF ANYBODY — AND SAYS HE IS NOT GOING TO LET IT BE A MONUMENT THIS WEEK.**

**828 — THE FOURTH DAY OF THAT WEEK, A FRIDAY, HIS FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-EIGHTH MORNING, THE FEVER SEVENTY-SEVEN WEEKS AND SIX DAYS, ABOUT TWELVE INCHES AND THREE DAYS, NEXT TIME IN ONE DAY, GIVEN AS A FIGURE WITH A RATE ON IT SO THAT ANYBODY CAN WORK IT OUT. A BOY OF THIRTEEN, AT **A HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FIVE DAYS**, ASKS HIS QUESTION AGAIN AT THE NINTH HOUR, AND **A WOMAN OF TWENTY-EIGHT ANSWERS IT BEFORE THE MAN OF THIRTY-ONE WHO HAD IT TWO DAYS**: A MARK IS MADE BY A PERSON GIVEN A THING TO HOLD, THE SMALLEST THING IN THIS COUNTY, **A STAMP WITH A HANDLE**, IN A ROOM WITH ABOUT NINE CLERKS IN IT FOUR HUNDRED MILES OFF, BY A PERSON WHO IS DOING THE WORK OF THE BOOK AND DOES NOT KNOW WHAT THE MARK IS FOR. SHE SAYS WHAT IT COST HER, AND THAT SHE HAS **NOT TOLD A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR** AND THAT IT IS NOT BECAUSE OF THE THING ON THE FLAT. A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE SAYS WHAT HE BUILT IS **A MAP**, AND SAYS IN THE SAME BREATH THAT A GAME IS A THING A PERSON PLAYS TO FIND OUT WHETHER A WORLD WORKS — AND A WOMAN OF TWENTY-NINE IN A REED, AT **A HUNDRED AND NINETY-NINE DAYS**, STOPS HIM AND MAKES HIM TAKE THE HALVES APART, AND SAYS **THE GAME WAS A BRIDGE AND IT WAS NOT A SOURCE**. HE SAYS IT BACK, SAYS HE HAS BEEN CALLING IT A WINDOW FOR TWO HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FOUR DAYS, GIVES THE NAME OF THE WOMAN WHO SIGNED ABOUT NINE HUNDRED RECORDS IN A COUNTRY THAT IS NOT THIS ONE, AND SAYS HE IS NOT TAKING IT TO A ROOM FOUR HUNDRED MILES OFF THIS WEEK.**

**829 — THE FIFTH DAY OF THAT WEEK, A SATURDAY, HIS FIVE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-NINTH MORNING, THE FEVER **SEVENTY-EIGHT WEEKS OLD EXACTLY**, A FULL BANK, FOUR DAYS, NEXT TIME IN FOUR DAYS. A MAN OF FORTY-FOUR SAYS THE THING THEY OUGHT TO BE ARGUING ABOUT IS THE ROAD, AND THAT IF ABOUT NINE OF US SPEND A WEEK ON A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE AND NONE OF IT ON A FLOOR, THEN AT THE END OF THE WEEK THE FLOOR IS EXACTLY WHERE IT IS. A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE SAYS NO, AND SAYS THAT A MAN OF FORTY-FOUR HAS JUST MADE HIS ARGUMENT FOR HIM AND DOES NOT KNOW IT, AND GIVES THE WANT WHOLE: TO STAND ON THAT BANK AND BE WORTH ABOUT NINE OF THEM LISTENING TO **FOR A DIFFERENT REASON THAN THE ONE HE HAS GOT**. A WOMAN OF TWENTY-EIGHT, AT **A HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-SEVEN DAYS**, SAYS A QUESTION HAS BEEN GOING ROUND THAT BANK, THAT IT HAS BEEN ASKED OF HER TWICE, THAT SHE HAS SAID NO TWICE, AND NAMES IT: **WHICH OF HIS STANDING AND THE TRUTH OF THE CRISIS IS IT BETTER TO LOSE.** ABOUT NINE OF THEM ARE ASKED OUT LOUD AND **NOBODY ANSWERS**, AND A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE NAMES THE NOTHING: IT IS NOT AGREEMENT, **NOBODY THERE HAS STANDING TO WEIGH THE TWO**, AND IT IS THE MOST CONVENIENT ANSWER AVAILABLE TO EVERY ONE OF THEM. HE SAYS HE WILL NOT ASK IT AGAIN UNTIL SOMEBODY WHO IS NOT HIM ASKS IT.**

**830 — THE SIXTH DAY OF THAT WEEK, A SUNDAY, HIS FIVE HUNDRED AND EIGHTIETH MORNING, FOUR HUNDRED AND THIRTY DAYS AFTER THE SETTLEMENT, THE FEVER SEVENTY-EIGHT WEEKS AND ONE DAY, A FULL BANK FOR THE SECOND MORNING, AND THE **NEXT TIME IS IN FOUR DAYS AND THE FIGURE IS DERIVABLE FROM THE RATE AND DOES NOT NEED HIM.** A WOMAN OF SIXTY-FOUR SAYS **THE NINETEEN YEARS ARE MINE AND ARE NOT A LEAF**, THAT SHE IS NOT EVIDENCE, THAT SHE IS GOING TO KEEP BELIEVING THE NINTH LINE, AND THAT NOBODY IN THIS COUNTY CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT ANYTHING. A BOY OF THIRTEEN, AT **A HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SIX DAYS**, SAYS HE IS **TAKING HIS QUESTION TO THE LOW GATE** ON THE MONDAY, NOT AT THE NINTH HOUR AND NOT TO ABOUT NINE OF THEM, BECAUSE THE MAN THERE WHO STOPPED A CART ON A TUESDAY ELEVEN YEARS AGO IS THE ONLY PERSON ON THAT ROAD WHO HAS EVER BEEN IN THE WAY OF A MARK, AND THAT HE IS NOT GOING ALONE AND IS **NOT GOING TO ASK A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE TO COME.** A MAN OF FORTY-FOUR GIVES FOUR TERMS AND THEY ARE NOT NEGOTIABLE. A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE SAYS THE TERMS ARE RIGHT, SAYS HE IS NOT ADDING HIMSELF, AND SAYS HE WILL BE AT THE POST ON MONDAY SAYING A FIGURE OUT LOUD SO THAT ABOUT FOUR CAN CHECK IT. A MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-TWO SAYS HE IS GOING TO READ OUT, ON MONDAY, **THE EIGHT MORNINGS ON WHICH HIS OWN SHEET OF DEAL HAD A MARK PUT ON IT**, WHICH HE HAS KNOWN FOR ELEVEN YEARS, AND THAT THOSE EIGHT ARE **NOT** THE BOY'S EIGHT, AND THAT NEITHER IS A FIGURE OF ANYBODY. A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE SAYS THE OFFER IS AT **A HUNDRED AND THIRTY DAYS** AND HAS NOT BEEN ANSWERED BY A ROAD, OR A CHILD, OR A BRIDGE, OR BY A WOMAN WHO IS NOT FRIGHTENED OF THE ELEVENTH LINE, AND THAT HE IS NOT GOING TO STOP SAYING THE FIGURE. NOTHING IS SETTLED.**
