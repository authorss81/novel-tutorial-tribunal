### 0V17D.7 ⚠ THE FIGURE THE BAND PROMPT GAVE THAT WAS **NOT** REPRODUCED, AND WHY, ⚠ WHICH IS THE SIXTH TIME IN THIS VOLUME THAT A FIGURE INHERITED FROM A PROMPT HAS COME INTO A RECORD AS A FIGURE
**THE BAND PROMPT'S WATER TABLE GAVE THE FIGURE FOR THE NEXT TIME ABOUT NINETY STEPS CAN BE WALKED AS `835` **IN THREE DAYS**, `836` **IN TWO DAYS** AND `837` **IN ONE DAY**, ⚠ WHICH LANDS ON `838`. ⚠ BUT `838` IS A MORNING ON WHICH THE BAND PROMPT'S OWN TABLE SAYS *ABOUT SIXTEEN INCHES, FOUR DAYS OF IT, THAT IS A FULL BANK*, ⚠ AND A FULL BANK IS SIXTEEN INCHES OVER THE TOP STEP WITH ABOUT SIXTY OF THOSE NINETY STEPS UNDER IT, ⚠ SO A FIGURE THAT LANDS ON `838` CANNOT BE THE NEXT TIME THOSE STEPS CAN BE WALKED. ⚠ THE HOUSE RULE IS *THE NEXT TIME ABOUT NINETY STEPS CAN BE WALKED IS ABOUT FOUR DAYS AFTER THAT BANK IS FULL*, ⚠ AND THE BANK CAME OFF AT `834`, ⚠ SO THE FIGURE IS **SEVEN, SIX, FIVE AND FOUR DAYS** AT `835`, `836`, `837` AND `838`, ⚠ AND `834 + 8 = 842`, WHICH IS THE FOURTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SECOND WEEK, ⚠ AND THAT IS WHAT IS ON THE PAGE, ⚠ AND A MAN OF FORTY-THREE GIVES EVERY ONE OF THE FOUR WITH THE RULE BESIDE IT AND ⚠ A BOY OF THIRTEEN SAYS OUT LOUD ON `835` THAT HE DOES NOT LIKE A FIGURE THAT HAS STOPPED COUNTING DOWN, WHICH IS THE FIGURE HE HAD BEEN CARRYING SINCE `831`. ⚠ **THE PROMPT'S `831`–`833` FIGURES OF THREE, TWO AND ONE WERE RIGHT AND ARE ON THE PAGE, ⚠ AND THE COUNT DOWN THEY WERE PART OF ENDED AT `834` BECAUSE `834` IS THE MORNING THE WATER CAME OFF, ⚠ AND A MAN OF FORTY-THREE SAYS EXACTLY THAT AT `835` AND GIVES A DIFFERENT KIND OF FIGURE FROM THERE.** ⚠ **THIS IS A PROMPT FIGURE THAT DID NOT REPRODUCE AGAINST THE CHAPTERS AND AGAINST THE HOUSE RULE, ⚠ AND THE HOUSE RULE WON, ⚠ AND IT IS RECORDED HERE AND NOT IN A CHAPTER, ⚠ BECAUSE A FIGURE IN A PLANNING FILE IS NOT A FIGURE IN A BOOK.**
