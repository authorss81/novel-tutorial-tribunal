HE ADMITS HE HAD SIX HOURS TODAY IN WHICH HE COULD HAVE SAID SOMETHING TO A MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT AND DID NOT, AND NAMES THE COST AS A FIGURE OF A DAY AND NOT A FIGURE OF A PERSON. HE PUTS THE RIGHT OF REFUSAL OUT OF BAND BY BAND: NOT BY THE ZONE, NOT BY EIGHTY YARDS OF LIME, NOT BY A WOMAN OF SIXTY-FOUR PAYING TWICE, AND NOT BY HIM PUTTING A QUESTION UNDER A LINE THAT A THING ON A FLAT WROTE. HE GIVES THE STANDING OFFER AT A HUNDRED AND THIRTY-EIGHT DAYS AND SAYS HE IS GOING TO KEEP SAYING THE FIGURE.**
