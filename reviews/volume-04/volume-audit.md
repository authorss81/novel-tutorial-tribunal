## Verdict

**Volume 04 PASSES WITH CARRIED DEFECTS.**

It passes on the volume's own terms, which `PHASE_SYSTEM.md` defines: *a volume that closed its immediate promise while opening a larger question is a pass.* All three of the volume's promises are delivered in the chapters, at the chapters, with the outline's own figures. The central pressure is on the page in fifty consecutive chapters and is priced in loads on the last page but one. The climax is staged as ordinary legal work in a room and paid for in about nine minutes. The resolution is delivered in terms, with the figures, by the man who owns the thing he is describing, and his network survives and he says so in his own mouth. The volume opens the question the series asked it to open, on a page, and does not answer it and does not compress it into one word. There is no new final enemy, the planned ending is untouched, and no System panel declares any choice correct.

**That is a pass of the volume's spine. It is not a pass of the record, and it is not a clean pass of the prose.** Nine prose defects and one device breach sit inside finished canon. Eight of the nine are carried deliberately, on the standing precedent that a volume close creates one artifact and does not reopen a finished batch's prose, and this audit holds that each of the eight is carried correctly and says so item by item in §9. **The ninth is not carried, because nothing carries it: Chapter 151 line 77 names a weekday, in the first chapter of the volume, and three separate files report that a plural-safe weekday scan over the fifty chapters returns zero.**

**A named weekday is not a typo. It is a foreign calendar inside a world whose calendar is the grain year counted in weeks and days with bells inside a day, and `outline/volume-04.md` line 144 forbids it in terms and line 197 forbids it again in the guardrails.** It is three words, it is in Chapter 151, and it is in the mouth of a woman of the district on the second day of the fortieth week. A reader meets it on the first page of the volume.

**The next phase is therefore a Volume 04 repair phase and not a Volume 05 phase.** §10 names five repairs, each with its chapter, its line and the wording it proposes, and each of the five is inside a line that already exists, so that no chapter-and-line citation in any state file moves. `PHASE_SYSTEM.md` §Volume audit forbids an audit from rewriting a volume, and this audit rewrote nothing; it recommends, with a wording, which is the mechanism the repair phase exists for.

**Four things the audit found that the record does not carry, all of them in the records and none of them in the spine:**

- **A weekday at `151:77`.** Unrecorded. A guardrail breach in finished canon, in the volume's first chapter.
- **The leg goes four times in the volume, not once.** `state/current.md` §7 says *it goes once in the volume, unremarked, in Chapter 190, for about four hours, and nothing in it clears the leg except a carried notice, once, four years ago.* The chapters say otherwise at `152:113`, `155:5` and `155:9`, `173:65` and `190:87`–`89`. The close's own sentence is contradicted by four chapters of the volume it is closing.
- **A twenty-second day-count instance at `156:85`,** in the same italic page-heading idiom as `155:87` and `158:111`, with the figure equal to the chapter's Shelf day. FINDING 1's count of twenty-one is short by one, and it is the seventh time a list in this project has been short.
- **Two false claims in the records, both of the class this project exists to catch.** A state file's own working for a carried prose defect at `158:77` uses a load count of *two hundred* that does not appear anywhere in Chapter 158, whose own figure is *forty*; and `state/character-state.md` lines 38 and 52 restate a count of the people who know he cannot read, which the close and the guardrail both bar from being restated, and do so with a figure that does not reconcile with the close's own statement that the count did not move.

**None of the four touches a delivered promise, a delivered climax, or the resolution, and none of them is a reason to doubt the volume. They are reasons the record is not yet the size or the shape it says it is, and they are named here so that a Volume 05 writer does not inherit them as canon.**
