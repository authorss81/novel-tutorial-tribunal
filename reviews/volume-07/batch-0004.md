  standing at a top gate for nothing. The records no longer overstate it.

  sets the threshold at three or four in ten; the delivered band printed the day's own weight at
  both ends in nine chapters out of ten and said nothing. Measured and now at **four in ten**
  (`334`, `335`, `336`, `338`). Six closings that carried a second copy of a figure the chapter
  had already printed in its third line were cut. The opening frame is seven of ten and is
  **not** cut, because the place rule at `316:7` means seven of these chapters have no figure and
  must say why.
- **Five of ten ran the same scene shape.** The measured count is three, and the three are not
  the same scene.
- **Ages and dates drifted inside single chapters, and one was resolved by inventing a
  character.** The market town's woman is sixty-one throughout. The barwoman is one person and
  not two. `336`'s impossible wet-day date is fixed and the other two of the three were right.
- **`332:23` put Tarin Keel in the county for four months.** Shelf day 165 is Chapter 290 and
  Chapter 332 is Shelf day 207: 207 − 165 = 42. Six weeks, in the chapter and in the record.
- **The Batch 0005 hand-off stated Chapter 340's own day as four and derived 64,091 from it.**
  The own day is eight. `PROMPT.md:67` now carries the ring and the working that produces the
  answer, and the second hand-off error of this kind is named.
