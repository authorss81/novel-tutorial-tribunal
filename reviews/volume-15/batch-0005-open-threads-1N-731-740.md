## 1N. THE THIRTEEN THREADS OF 731–740, AND EVERY ONE IS A PERSON DOING A THING

1. **A MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT PUT HIS OWN HAND IN TWO OF THE NINE OPENINGS IN ONE WALL AND SAID THERE IS NOTHING IN ANY OF THE NINE, AND HE IS NOT GOING TO PUT IT IN A THIRD, AND HE IS NOT GOING TO CALL THEM FIELDS OR ANYTHING ELSE, AND A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR PUT HIS HAND IN FOUR OF THEM AND SAID IT WAS EMPTY AND HE WAS TELLING IT BY HAND BECAUSE HE CANNOT READ. `731`, `732`**
