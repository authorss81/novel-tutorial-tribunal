## 1K. THE THIRTEEN THREADS OF 701–710, AND EVERY ONE IS A PERSON DOING A THING

**A THREAD IS A THING A PERSON IS STILL DOING. A HAZARD IS NOT A THREAD. NONE OF THESE THIRTEEN WAS CLOSED BY THE BAND THAT OPENED IT, AND NOT ONE OF THE THIRTEEN OF 691–700 WAS CLOSED BY IT EITHER, THOUGH TWO OF THOSE THIRTEEN WERE ADVANCED.**

1. **A SHEET OF PRINTED PAPER, ABOUT AS LONG AS A MAN'S OWN FOREARM AND ABOUT AS WIDE AS BOTH HIS HANDS, WITH NINE LINES RULED ACROSS IT AND NOTHING ABOVE THE FIRST AND NOTHING BELOW THE LAST. On each line there are four things and they are in this order: a place, a mark cut into it about the size of a thumb and square, a name, and a day.** It was given to a man of thirty-six in a yard in a town about a day's walk off, and it was told: give it to a person in this county and not to a man who came up a plank. **It lies in the open air on the counter of a landing and anybody can put a hand on it and nobody is keeping it, and a man of forty-three found it there on a Saturday morning and did not bring it inside and said he was not going to be the man who brings it in. `702`, `704`**
2. **NINE LINES, ELEVEN NAMES, TWO OF THE ELEVEN THE SAME NAME TWICE, AND TWO OF THE ELEVEN WITH NO DAY AGAINST THEM.** One of the two repeats is a man who has been dead about thirty years and whose name was heard out loud by exactly one person in four counties and written nowhere. **THE TWO BLANKS ARE ON THE EIGHTH LINE AND THE NINTH. THE WOMAN OF TWENTY-NINE IN THE REED IS NOT ONE OF THEM: SHE IS ON THE FIFTH LINE AND SHE HAS GOT A DAY AGAINST HER, AND THAT DAY IS THE WEDNESDAY A THING THAT IS NOT A PERSON ASKED A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE WHETHER HE WOULD WRITE IT, SO A NAME WITH A DAY ON IT IS A NAME SOMEBODY CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT AND THAT IS WORSE AND NOT BETTER THAN A BLANK. A boy of thirteen said it out loud in a field at `709`, and a man of thirty-eight read the fifth line out loud the next morning and said in his own mouth that it is not one of the two blanks. `704`, `709`, `710`**
3. **A WOMAN OF FORTY-FOUR WHO KEEPS A BAKEHOUSE FOUR MILES UP THAT CART ROAD WAS ASKED TWICE THROUGH TWO MEN IN TWO COATS, ABOUT TWO DIFFERENT DOORS, ON A FRIDAY AND ON A WEDNESDAY FIVE DAYS APART, ANSWERED BOTH TIMES, AND DID NOT NOTICE THAT SHE HAD ANSWERED THE FIRST ONE, AND STILL CANNOT WORK OUT WHETHER IT WAS ONE QUESTION OR TWO. `703`**
4. **A WOMAN OF SIXTY-FOUR WHO GOT UP OUT OF THE BOOK OF HEADS AND CARTS IN THE OPEN NINETEEN YEARS AGO WAS ASKED AT ABOUT THE NINTH HOUR ON A BANK BY SOMETHING THAT IS NOT A PERSON, IN THE HEARING OF ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, AND SAID YES.** Nobody in four counties can be sent for about the asking and nobody can be sent for about the answering, and she has refused to be asked about the asking on a day, in nine days and in four years. **And she said the name herself, and said it was the fifth time anybody has said it in that county, and said she was not going to let the first person to say it be a man who was not there. `708`**
5. **A MAN OF THIRTY-SIX WHO CARRIED THE SHEET FOUR MILES UP A CART ROAD TWICE, ASKED ONE QUESTION, WROTE DOWN ONE WORD HE CANNOT READ, PUT IT ON A LANDING COUNTER AND WALKED OUT OF THE COUNTY.** He is not a keeper of it and did not want it and would not give it to a man with a book or to a man of sixty-one who writes things down, and said he thought of both before he thought of the first one. **Nothing was ever sent for about him and nobody knows what he is called by the woman who gave it to him. `702`, `704`**
6. **A MAN OF FORTY-ONE WHO KEEPS TWO CARTS AND IS THE NINTH OF NINE ASKED AT ABOUT THE FIFTH HOUR TO STOP, IN A FORTNIGHT, AND WANTS THE DAY OF IT WRITTEN WHERE ABOUT NINE PEOPLE CAN HEAR IT AND WHERE SOMEBODY CAN POINT AT THE DAY AFTERWARDS.** Nobody asked him a question about it. **A man of thirty-one said there is a column for it and a man of sixty-one took it off him in six sentences, and a man of thirty-eight said that a column is a question asked the same way every time and that a man of forty-one asking for one is not asking for a place, he is asking somebody who has never heard the question to write down that he asked it. `707`**
7. **A CHAIR IN THE EIGHTH ROOM, WHICH IS ABOUT AS HIGH AS THE KNEE OF A MAN OF FORTY-ONE, WITH A BACK ON IT, AND IT IS NOT CUT IN THE BANK, AND THERE IS NOT ONE STICK OF WOOD IN A HUNDRED AND THIRTY FEET OF PASSAGE, AND NOBODY HAS EVER BEEN IN THAT ROOM.** The light under that door-stone was about a thumb's width on the Friday of the hundred and twenty-first week and is the width of a man's own hand at the knuckles, and a man about fifty-two who cannot see well said that is not a door settling and he is not going to guess what it is with a lamp in his hand. **Three reasons were given for not going in and none of the three was about the light. `706`**
8. **A MARK ON THE SHEET THAT IS CUT INTO IT ABOUT THE SIZE OF A THUMB AND SQUARE, AND A SPACE CUT BETWEEN TWO HANDS OVER THE NINTH OPENING THAT IS ABOUT AS WIDE AS A THUMB AND SQUARE AND CUT TO THE DEPTH OF THE HANDS AND OLD AND EMPTY.** A man of thirty-eight said they are the same size and the same shape and refused to say they are the same thing, and asked about nine people to notice if they were about to join them up before they did it. `704`, `709`
9. **A MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT IS GOING TO READ NINE LINES OUT LOUD IN THE OPEN ON THAT BANK EVERY MORNING BEFORE ANYBODY GOES ANYWHERE, AND THERE IS NO ORIGINAL AND NO KEEPER, AND HE ASKED FOR THE FIGURE FOR HOW LONG AND A MAN OF SIXTY-ONE REFUSED IT IN FIVE SENTENCES, AND A BOY OF THIRTEEN GAVE HIM ONE HE DID NOT ASK FOR AND HE TOLD THE BOY TO KEEP IT.** Nine days is a figure of nine days and not of a term. `710`
10. **A WOMAN OF TWENTY-EIGHT WHO READ OUT THE SEVEN THINGS THAT ARE NOT ON IT AND SAID A FORM IS A FORM BECAUSE IT ASKS THE SAME QUESTION EVERY TIME AND IT DOES NOT KNOW WHO IT ASKED, AND A WOMAN OF FORTY-FOUR WHO SAID IN ONE SENTENCE THAT A THING WITH A FORM UNDER IT STOPS BEING A PERSON REFUSING AND BECOMES A FIELD WITH SOMETHING IN IT.** All nine people on that bank are going to be asked it again on the same day of the week in about nine weeks and there is no form in this county that says they may be asked and no form that says they may not. `707`, `710`
11. **A WOMAN OF TWENTY-NINE IN A REED, EIGHTY DAYS, WHO SAID ONE WORD ON THE FRIDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-THIRD WEEK AFTER SOMETHING THAT IS NOT A PERSON ASKED HER THE SAME QUESTION IN THE HEARING OF ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, AND IT WAS THE SECOND WORD SHE HAS SAID SINCE SHE WENT INTO THAT REED AT ABOUT THE SECOND HOUR ON THE TUESDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND TWELFTH WEEK, WHICH IS `629`, THE SAME DAY SHE WENT IN, AND SHE HAS NOT STOOD UP SINCE THAT SECOND HOUR; **AND NOBODY ASKED HER WHAT IT WAS ABOUT AND A MAN OF SIXTY-ONE REFUSED TO GIVE ANYBODY A FIGURE FOR WHAT IT COST HER, AND A MAN OF FORTY-THREE REFUSED TO CALL IT A REFUSAL AND REFUSED TO CALL IT NOT ONE. `709`**
12. **ABOUT A FINGER'S THICKNESS OF WATER UNDER THE NINTH DOOR-STONE, WHICH A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR WHO CANNOT READ MEASURED AGAINST HIS OWN FINGER AND DID NOT ROUND, AND WHICH HAS COME UP TWICE IN ABOUT A FORTNIGHT, AND HE IS THE ONLY PERSON ANYBODY IN THAT COUNTY CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT, AND NOBODY HAS SENT HIM AND NOBODY HAS ASKED HIM.** **AND A WOMAN OF FORTY-FOUR SAID ON THE THURSDAY OF A WEEK THAT THAT IS WHAT STOPPING LOOKS LIKE, AND SAID IT IS A FIGURE OF THE RIVER AND NOT OF A ROOM. `701`, `702`, `707`, `710`**
13. **A QUESTION ASKED ON A BANK ON THE WEDNESDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SECOND WEEK BY SOMETHING THAT IS NOT A PERSON, IN PLAIN WORDS, WHETHER HE WOULD WRITE IT, WHICH HE DID NOT ANSWER, WHICH NOBODY ANSWERED FOR HIM, AND WHICH STANDS OPEN. On the Saturday of the hundred and twenty-third week he said out loud, a week early and on purpose, that if he is asked again the answer will be the same as it was on the Wednesday, which is nothing at all, and a woman of twenty-eight told him in about nine people's hearing that this is a rule and not an answer and that she is not going to be the person who holds it for him.** `700`, `710`

**AND THE FIGURES THAT TRAVEL WITH THE THIRTEEN, AND NONE OF THEM IS SETTLED: nine lines and eleven names and two blanks; two hands once a day before the light, about a minute, about four hundred years, and no form in this county that says a person may be paid for it; about a thumb's width of light that is now a hand's width, in a room nobody has entered; about a finger's thickness of water under a door-stone that two men have put their hands on and that has not moved; seven mornings of nothing and one morning of a question; eighty days in a reed and two words and no figure for either; nineteen years out of every book in a county and nine weeks and nine doors; and the shape of a silence handed to about nine people on a Saturday morning. `citizen` IS AT 0.**
