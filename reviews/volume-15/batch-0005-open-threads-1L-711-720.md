## 1L. THE THIRTEEN THREADS OF 711–720, AND EVERY ONE IS A PERSON DOING A THING

**A THREAD IS A THING A PERSON IS STILL DOING. A HAZARD IS NOT A THREAD. NONE OF THESE THIRTEEN WAS CLOSED BY THE BAND THAT OPENED IT, AND NONE OF THE THIRTEEN OF 701–710 WAS CLOSED BY IT EITHER, THOUGH THREE OF THOSE THIRTEEN WERE ADVANCED: THE SHEET AND THE READING, THE WOMAN OF FORTY-FOUR ASKED TWICE THROUGH TWO DOORS, AND THE WOMAN IN A REED.**

1. **A SHEET OF PRINTED PAPER THAT HAD NINE LINES AND ELEVEN NAMES ON IT AT THE END OF THE PREVIOUS BAND AND HAS TWELVE NAMES ON IT NOW, THE TWELFTH IN INK AND NOT IN THE PRINT, ON THE NINTH LINE, UNDER A NAME THAT HAS NO DAY AGAINST IT, AND NOBODY IN FOUR COUNTIES CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT WHO WROTE IT.** A man of thirty-eight read the count out loud and gave the working before he read one of the names, said he had not read the sheet on the Wednesday because he was a hundred and thirty feet under that bank with his own lamp, and said he was not going to say that was the reason and was not going to say it was a coincidence. **And a woman of sixty-four said out loud on the last morning of the band that somebody is going to write another name on it, a week early on purpose, and that nobody is going to be sent for about it. `716`, `720`**
2. **A READING OF TWELVE NAMES OUT LOUD IN THE OPEN ON A BANK BEFORE ANYBODY GOES ANYWHERE, EVERY MORNING, ABOUT NINE MINUTES, NO ORIGINAL AND NO KEEPER, AND A MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT WHO HAS ASKED FOUR TIMES FOR A FIGURE FOR HOW LONG AND HAS BEEN REFUSED FOUR TIMES, AND A BOY OF THIRTEEN WHO IS KEEPING THE COUNT AND GAVE IT OUT LOUD TWICE.** The count is a count of times and not a clock, and it is seven at `717` and ten at `720`, and one morning of the seven had no reading on it. **A woman of forty said out loud that it is a practice and not a settlement, and gave the reason, which is that a settlement is a thing that holds when the person who was doing it stops, and nobody on that bank has stopped anything, and she said she was not going to say what would make it one because about nine of them had been putting a finger at that since the Thursday and not one of them had put it on the same one. `711`, `717`, `720`**
3. **A DAY THAT ABOUT NINE PEOPLE HAVE HEARD READ OUT, WHICH IS NOT A DAY IN A BOOK AND NOT A DAY ON THE SHEET AND NOT A DAY ANYBODY CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT AND NOT A DAY ANYBODY CAN POINT AT, GIVEN IN NINE SENTENCES BY A MAN OF SIXTY-ONE WHO REFUSED THE TENTH AND REFUSED TO WRITE ANY OF THE NINE DOWN.** And it cuts both ways, and the sentence that makes it cut is a man of fifty-four's: a thing about nine people have is a thing about nine people can be sent for about. **A man of forty-one wants a day written down where somebody can point at it afterwards and has given three ways he has tried and none of them is a form in this county, and is not going to put it on the sheet because he would be asking it once, and a column that gets asked once is not a column, it is a favour. `719`, `720`**
4. **A MAN OF FORTY-FOUR WHO KEEPS A BAKEHOUSE FOUR MILES UP THAT CART ROAD, ASKED TWICE THROUGH TWO MEN IN TWO COATS ABOUT TWO DIFFERENT DOORS, WHO HAS NOW SAID OUT LOUD THAT BOTH MEN USED THE SAME NINE WORDS AND NEITHER OF THEM KNEW ABOUT THE OTHER, AND THAT IF IT WAS ONE QUESTION SOMETHING COUNTED HER ONCE AND IF IT WAS TWO THEN NOTHING COUNTED HER AT ALL, AND THAT SHE WOULD RATHER NOT KNOW, AND SHE STILL WILL NOT SAY WHICH OF THE TWO COST MORE.** She has three askings and two answers and nobody has asked her a question about any of them. `713`, `719`
5. **A WOMAN OF SIXTY-FOUR WHO IS OUT OF EVERY BOOK IN THIS COUNTY FOR NINETEEN YEARS AND SAID YES AT ABOUT THE NINTH HOUR, AND WHO CAME UP A BANK AND ASKED TO BE TOLD WHETHER HER NAME WAS ON A SHEET OF PRINTED PAPER, AND WAS TOLD IT WAS NOT BEFORE ANYBODY HAD READ IT, AND HAS A NAME IN INK ON THE NINTH LINE WITH NO DAY AGAINST IT.** She is still a name nobody can be sent for about and she said so herself, and she said the difference between the two things costs nothing, and she said the First Witness's name at `708` and a man of thirty-one said it at `719` and she gave what that cost and it is hers and she is not relieved. `715`, `716`, `719`
6. **A WOMAN OF TWENTY-NINE IN A REED, EIGHTY-THREE DAYS, ON THE FIFTH LINE WITH A DAY AGAINST HER, WHO IS ONE OF THE THREE OF THE FIVE THAT A WOMAN OF FORTY WORKED OUT AND REFUSED TO NAME.** A day on the fifth line is what makes her a name somebody can be sent for about, and a man of fifty-four who cannot read said that to look at the place is to go along the edge of that drain about a hundred yards and stand about twenty feet off a reed and look into it at a woman lying on her side. **She has said nothing since one word on the Friday of the previous band, and nobody went nearer, and nobody asked her for a second word. `712`**
7. **A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR WHO CANNOT READ AND IS THE ONLY PERSON ANYBODY IN THAT COUNTY CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT FOUR THINGS, AND WAS ASKED A FIFTH THING BY SOMETHING THAT IS NOT A PERSON ON A BANK AT ABOUT THE NINTH HOUR, AND HAS NOT ANSWERED IT, AND HAS SAID OUT LOUD THAT WHAT HE SAID WAS NOT A REFUSAL AND WAS THE REASON HE HAD NOT GOT AN ANSWER.** He cannot read, and he cannot tell whether the nine words he has been given are the nine words that are on the sheet. **A woman of forty-four refused the asking in nine sentences and the eighth of them is that she is not going to give him the right of refusing and is not going to take it off him. `718`, `720`**
8. **THREE THINGS AND NO WORD FOR THE THIRD: A DOOR THAT WILL NOT OPEN, A DOOR THAT IS SHUT AND HELD, AND A MAN WHO CANNOT READ. A woman of forty gave the count, said the first two have been there about nineteen years and that neither of them is the right of refusing, and refused to pick one, and nobody has picked one since.** `718`
9. **NINE BOARDS WITH NO ORIGINAL AND NO KEEPER, FOUR OF WHICH CARRY A JOB WITH A SPACE ABOUT AS WIDE AS A THUMB WHERE A NAME GOES AND FIVE OF WHICH HAVE NO JOB ON THEM AT ALL, AND EIGHT OF THE NINE ARE IN FOUR COUNTIES AND NOBODY IN THIS COUNTY KNOWS WHERE, INCLUDING THE MAN OF SIXTY-ONE WHO KEPT THEM.** Two of them were on one counter beside the printed sheet for about an hour and NEITHER WAS READ, one of them by a man of fifty-four who cannot read who said out loud that he was not asking anybody to read it to him, and one of them by a man of thirty-eight who was given it by a man of thirty-one at the end of the hundred and twenty-first week and has never read it. **A man of thirty-eight asked about nine people to notice if they were about to join a board and a sheet up before they did it, and said he was not going to say they were the same thing and was not going to say they were not the same thing either. `717`**
10. **ONE COLUMN AND NOT TWO: A DAY AGAINST A NAME IN A BOOK OF HEADS AND CARTS HAS MEANT THE DAY A HEAD CAME THROUGH A LANDING FOR ELEVEN YEARS AND THE SAME COLUMN ON A SHEET PRINTED FOUR HUNDRED MILES OFF HAS MEANT THE DAY A PERSON WAS ASKED FOR FOUR DAYS, AND A MAN OF SIXTY-ONE GAVE TWO REASONS WHY HE WILL NOT SAY WHICH OF THE TWO IS THE REAL ONE AND SAID IT WAS TWO REASONS AND NOT THREE.** A woman of forty said it is one column in two places and that there is no form in this county that says which one you are reading, and that every day she has asked for since before any of them came up that plank has meant the first one. `713`
11. **A NAME IN THE MOUTHS OF ABOUT NINE PEOPLE ON A BANK WHO DID NOT HAVE IT IN THEIR MOUTHS ON MONDAY, AND ONE MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT WHO HAS TO STAND ON THAT BANK BEFORE ANYBODY GOES ANYWHERE AND DECIDE EVERY MORNING WHETHER HE SAYS IT OUT LOUD, AND HAS NOT SAID IT.** A woman of sixty-four said she would rather not have a figure of mornings for it and is not going to make one up, and that she is not relieved and nobody there is relieved. `719`
12. **A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR IN AN ASH, EIGHTY-TWO DAYS, WHO WAS ASKED NOTHING IN ANY OF THEM, AND WHOSE BOARD, THE SEVENTH, IS A REMAINDER AND NOT A COPY.** He was not in this band and he was quoted in two mouths and he was not asked anything. `711`–`720`
13. **A QUESTION ASKED ON A BANK ON THE WEDNESDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SECOND WEEK BY SOMETHING THAT IS NOT A PERSON, IN PLAIN WORDS, WHETHER HE WOULD WRITE IT, WHICH HE DID NOT ANSWER, WHICH NOBODY ANSWERED FOR HIM, AND WHICH STANDS OPEN.** He said out loud on the Saturday of that week that the shape of his answer was nothing at all and a woman of twenty-eight told him that is a rule and not an answer, and at the end of this band he said nothing has asked him anything since that Wednesday, that he is not going to arrange to be asked, and that he is not going to say the shape of it a second time. `700`, `710`, `719`, `720`

**AND THE FIGURES THAT TRAVEL WITH THE THIRTEEN, AND NONE OF THEM IS SETTLED: about nine minutes a morning and ten readings and one morning with no reading on it; eleven names and then twelve; nine days and two blanks and then three; five of the eleven and three of those five with a day; four nights on a counter and nobody who can be sent for about who wrote on any of them; one column with two meanings in it and a man of sixty-one who will not say which is real; two boards and a sheet on one counter and none of the three read; eight of nine boards in four counties and not one of them located; nine words on a sheet and a man who cannot read them and has not answered; three things and no word for the third; a fortnight and a Wednesday and no day; and the shape of a silence handed to about nine people nine days after it was given and still not answered. `citizen` IS AT 0.**
