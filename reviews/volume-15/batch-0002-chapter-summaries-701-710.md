**701 — NOTHING WHATEVER HAPPENS ON THAT BANK FOR A SECOND MORNING RUNNING ABOUT A QUESTION THAT WAS ASKED ON WEDNESDAY, AND A BOY OF THIRTEEN COMES DOWN A CART ROAD AND GIVES A COUNT OF A QUESTION AND AN ANSWER HEARD FROM OUTSIDE A DOOR FOUR MILES OFF.** The third day of the hundred and twenty-second week, a Thursday, his four hundred and fifty-first morning, the fever fifty-nine weeks and five days, one hundred and forty-seven days in the county of Kell, and the water about twelve inches over the top step, three days since it came off all ninety, with the next time the ninety steps can be walked given as the Tuesday of the week after this one. A man of thirty-one says out loud that he is the reason nothing is happening and that about nine of them came up that bank for it. A woman of forty takes it off him in about nine sentences — a sheet is not a name, a name is a person you can be sent for about, and a sheet with a column in it is a field — and refuses to thank him. A boy of thirteen gives forty-eight days, eleven minutes, nine words and one word, with the subtraction spoken and nobody having asked him, and says it sounded like yes and he is not going to say it was, because he was outside a door. A man of fifty-four who cannot read puts his own hand in the gap under the ninth door-stone and says the water is an inch and about the thickness of his own finger, that it has come up twice in about a fortnight, that he is the only person anybody can be sent for about it, that nobody has sent him and nobody has asked him whether he would move it, and he has not moved it. A woman of forty-four says that is what stopping looks like and it is the first thing anybody in that county has had that is not a person, and will not be thanked for the ninth time this fortnight. A man of sixty-one gives three counts and refuses a fifth reason for refusing to write a boy of thirteen down. A man of thirty-one says he wants to walk four miles up the cart road to a bakehouse, works it out in four sentences in front of about nine people, and then does not go, and gives no reason.
