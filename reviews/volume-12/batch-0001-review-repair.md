## 0R. THE REVIEW REPAIR OF VOLUME 12 BATCH 0001, CHAPTERS 551–560, and the twenty-nine findings of the external review and what each one was

**A REVIEW WAS RUN AGAINST THE BAND AND IT FOUND FIFTEEN BLOCKING DEFECTS, TWELVE SERIOUS AND A SET OF MINOR, AND EVERY BLOCKING ONE AND EVERY SERIOUS ONE THAT WAS A FACT WAS REPAIRED IN THE CHAPTERS AND IN THE STATE FILES IN THE SAME PASS. THE CHAPTERS ARE THE CANON AND A STATE FILE THAT DISAGREES WITH A CHAPTER IS A STATE FILE THAT WAS WRITTEN FROM A CHAPTER THAT WAS WRITTEN AGAIN.**

**THE FIFTEEN BLOCKING, in order, and this is the whole list and every one of them was a figure, a date, an attribution or a claim the body of a chapter did not support.** 1. **THE TITLE OF `560` CARRIED *NINE HUNDRED SHEEP* AFTER THE BODY WAS SWEPT TO SEVEN HUNDRED, which is finding twenty-five for the fifth time in this repository and the worst of the five, because the sweep had been made and the sweep had missed the one line nobody re-reads.** 2. **THE MONEY CHAIN AT `552` NAMED THE BALANCE BEFORE THE FARE WAS PAID** — 243d less 3d is 240d, which is one pound, and the chapter printed the post-fare figure while the 42d was still in his hand; the chapter now counts the fare off the top, hands it over, and names what is left. 3. **THE RISING WAS NINE DAYS OUT AT `553` AND THIRTEEN, and one chapter later the same book says twelve, and the state sweep had listed 554 to 560 and omitted 553 entirely; the boy now says he has never seen the stone and cannot say.** 4. **THE WATER OFF THE DRAIN WAS THE NINTH DAY FROM `558` FOR A MORNING THAT IS THE TENTH, and the sentence that said so also said it was the second day after the rising, which is `568` and is the tenth; the chapter, the title and the state file were all cut to the tenth.** 5. **THE OLD MAN'S QUESTION WAS DATED SIX DAYS BEFORE `559` AND IS EIGHT.** 6. **FOUR OFF-BY-ONE DAY-COUNTS AGAINST THEIR OWN DATE LINES, in `555` twice and `559` once.** 7. **THE DRAIN WAS MARCH IN FOUR CHAPTERS AND APRIL IN TWO, AND THE TWO APRILS WERE THE NOTICE AND THE ANTAGONIST, which means one line of a public record was false and the band is about a public record; the drain is March everywhere and the notice's fifth line now says the first week of March.** 8. **THE NOTICE DATED THE TAKING-UP TWO DAYS LATER THAN THE WALL DOES AND GAVE THREE DAYS WHERE THE WALL GIVES FOUR.** 9. **THE DYKER HAD FOUR ACRES AND HIS BROTHERS ELEVEN IN ONE CHAPTER AND SIXTEEN ACRES IN THE NEXT, and the state file had followed the wrong one.** 10. **THE JOURNEY WAS FOUR DAYS DOWN AND SIX WEEKS OF WATER IN THE CHAPTER THAT SETS IT UP, for a crossing that takes one day and a carrier who says nine weeks.** 11. **THE BAND CLIFF'S LOAD-BEARING ARITHMETIC DID NOT CLOSE: a water-clerk told a woman on a step that a ditch fills in without the rate in about eleven years, and four chapters later the ditches are full after two years of non-payment, and the man who proves the covenant is right is contradicted by the only other person with standing to know; `555` now says two years, and `560` and the panel say two years, and the two agree.** 12. **THE PANEL SAID THE CLERK HAD NOT BEEN ASKED ABOUT A PERSON IN FOUR YEARS IN A CHAPTER WHERE HE SAYS NINETEEN, TWICE.** 13. **THE TITLE OF `558` SAID THE CORRECTION WAS FOUR LOWER AND THE CHAPTER SAYS TWO.** 14. **TWENTY-FOUR MILES FOR A TWENTY-ONE-MILE WALK, IN THREE CHAPTERS, AGAINST THE CHAPTER THAT WALKS IT.** 15. **THE PANEL'S CLAIM ASSERTED THAT THE HUNDRED HAD AGREED TO BE GOVERNED, WHICH IS THE ONE THING THE VOLUME EXISTS TO DENY; ITS BURDEN SAID *TWO YEARS* AGAINST FOUR IN THE SAME CHAPTER; IT SAID *FOUR FAMILIES* AGAINST ELEVEN HOUSEHOLDS; AND IT SAID *FOUR YEARS* AGAINST NINETEEN. ALL FOUR WERE WRONG AND ALL FOUR WERE THE CHECKABLE CLAIMS IN THE BAND'S MOST QUOTABLE ARTEFACT.**

A man said he had asked two people things in nine days on the third day of his arrival. A hinge sentence at `560` did not on its face say the thing the whole chapter turns on, and now it does. A girl at a gate delivered a thesis and was dropped in a line, and she now has a name. Two distance lists were short of two distances, and both went into `state/continuity.md` the day they were found. **AND THE TWO FINDINGS THAT ARE NOT DEFECTS AND WERE RECORDED AS REFUSALS: the review thought `560` reads as a settlement being narrated rather than a climax, and thought the panel re-instruments the man at the moment he stops instrumenting himself.
