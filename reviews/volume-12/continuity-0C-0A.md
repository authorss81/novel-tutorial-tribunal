## 0C. WHAT CHAPTERS 571–580 ADDED TO THE GROUND, AND EVERY FIGURE IN IT IS ONE A PERSON SAID OUT LOUD

**THE MIDPOINT GROUND. Six acres of the sally bed on the top side with nothing in the square, twelve shillings and sixpence a year, one hundred and fifty pence, a hundred and forty yards of ditch with a foot of sallow, about a yard of it to a barrow-load and about nine loads a day alone, about sixteen days for a year of it (about nine yards of it a day, and the chain closes: about a hundred and forty-four loads over about a hundred and forty yards), seven days out and about nine to go at `580`. Forty-four acres of long grazing in the sixth year with about nine acres of new sward sowed in March, in season to Owain Bray who paid 2s 1d on two hundred and ten acres (£21 17s 6d). Ten acres of the low meadow standing, about twenty-one gone this year, a fortnight with a scythe and about nine men begun Tuesday `573`, six days late Monday `579`, two acres down and the first swath not to be put back. THE SENTENCE: the hand that pays the rate holds the season and a holder cannot say a true thing about a man in that room (`578`, said back by Garr Ryley by the ninth hour). THE DECISION: stay nobody’s until the year turns (`580`, in a yard). THE ASKED-AND-UNSAYED: whether Bray meant the three boards out of his own purse, thirteen days carried and fourteen at `580`, asked `579`, no answer that day nor on `580`. `571`–`580`

**THE MORNING LIST WITH NINE PARCELS.** At the Saturday reading `577` nine people put their own ground on the wall against the challenges, the first Sabra Lyne’s four acres above the lane with the willow stump, hay standing. Hester Nunn’s entry is parcel, acres and day with no name top or bottom. Five of six challenges now carry parcels; one still names none. `577`

**HESTER’S THREE, PAID.** Common seen with her own eyes (nine hundred yards of rise, eleven feet of top, four of eleven tents up, nine hundred pounds by flat boat); nine foot/fifty-eight things read, two in the green book (the rising day, the empty square); day named and question asked by another man in a room of thirty, four wrong in four seconds, nine silent, nobody bound. `575`

**A NEW DISTANCE ENTERED ON THE DAY, PER INDEX RULE 15: about four hundred yards from the parlour door of the Green Paw to the north end of the low meadow (`572`).**

**NO NEW PLACE WAS INVENTED IN THIS BAND. Coldcombe’s yard still holds three boards. The camp moves nine hundred pounds by flat boat while the water is up about nine weeks. Sera Quill’s hand arrived (four lines, refusal, unread, `574`).**
