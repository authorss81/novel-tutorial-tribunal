## 2h. ⚠ WHAT CHAPTERS 843–850 CHANGED IN THESE PEOPLE, ⚠ AND EVERY NAME, AGE AND FIGURE IN IT WAS MADE IN A MOUTH, IN FRONT OF ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, WITH THE WORKING SAID OUT LOUD, ⚠ AND ⚠ **A CLOSE FIXES NO NAME BY A PLANNING FILE AND A FIGURE OF A PERSON IS A FIGURE OF A PERSON AND NOT A FIGURE OF A BANK**

1. **ILYAN VESTER, THIRTY-ONE, AND IS NOBODY'S. `ch − 554`, `ch − 685`, SPOKEN IN HIS OWN MOUTH IN ALL EIGHT: TWO HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-NINE THROUGH TWO HUNDRED AND NINETY-SIX, AND A HUNDRED AND FIFTY-EIGHT THROUGH A HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FIVE.** He held no seat and said he would not be given one, refused the ninth seat on Tuesday before the question was finished and said why: a man who keeps the sheets is a man whose handwriting is on them, and a man whose handwriting is on them is not nobody's. He carried mortar for four hours and was not in charge of it. He heard a case in a barn and said nobody in that barn said one word about the arrangement. **He said the one sentence he had on Saturday, that you decide and you help carry what your decision changes, and said immediately that it was not a discovery, that a woman said it first in a different shape on Friday and a boy read it back in three pieces, and that nobody is to thank him for it.** He did not answer `citizen` and said he would not, and did not say it was untrue either, because he does not know. **He was told he was right about the eleventh line in no mouth and his credibility is not restored by being overruled.** He is not a defendant, not the man who writes the answers, and `citizen` is now spent.
2. **BARNABY CROVE, FIFTY-FOUR, WHO CANNOT READ AND HOLDS THE GRAVEL. `1 + (ch − 722)`, ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-TWO THROUGH ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-NINE, SPOKEN AT `845:47`, `847:49` AND `850:57`.** He had the terms of the two declining seats read to him and said them back, gave the figure for the ninth line in his own mouth, and said he is going to keep asking. **A day in a different place is not a day on the ninth line and he was not told that one had been written.**
3. **ADA RENK, SIXTY-FOUR, WHO HAS A NAME IN INK ON THE NINTH LINE AND **NOW HAS A DAY AGAINST IT IN A DIFFERENT PLACE**, ⚠ AND THE ARRANGEMENT'S RECORD STILL HAS NO DAY.** She heard her name read out loud for the first time in nineteen years and refused to have the publication stopped. She agreed on a Sunday to have her own protection looked at, with the cost named before the question was asked. She wrote a day against her own name in her own hand and said the working out loud while she did it. **She is not relieved, not forgiven and not thanked, and she said the nineteen years were given away three times and that nobody is to thank her for a fourth thing.**
4. **NEVIN TRASK, SIXTY-ONE, WHO WROTE THREE LINES IN HIS BOOK IN ELEVEN YEARS ON TUESDAY AND SIX BY WEDNESDAY.** He published the arrangement's own eleven lines and its index, read nine charters, accepted a sixth line, and **left three of the nine positions blank on purpose and said so.** He read the narrowed rule whole and said it is not right.
5. **SIMON ROOK, ABOUT FIFTY-TWO, WHO CANNOT SEE WELL, WHO NAMED TWELVE THINGS HE CANNOT CHECK BY `847:61` AND A THIRTEEN BY `848:43` WHICH IS A FIGURE OF A WOMAN.** He read the seventh line of the edits, gave the whole difference in one sentence, said that nine people had published a thing without asking it, said that neither half of a declining seat's terms is the right of refusal, **and named the thing that translates in one mouth and said it is not a god, it is not arriving and it is not spoken by.** He may not carry the seal and he did not offer to.
6. **WAT MARSHE, THIRTEEN. `ch − 653`, A HUNDRED AND NINETY THROUGH A HUNDRED AND NINETY-SEVEN, SPOKEN IN HIS OWN MOUTH IN ALL EIGHT.** He gave a count and not an argument on every morning, counted thirty-two notches twice because a man of fifty-four cannot read, guided a man's hand with a piece of chalk, read a woman's new line back and said it was a day and a name and nothing else, counted the hours of a case, read the names of the people affected twice, and said on Saturday that a sentence is not a discovery and a question is not the same shape. **Nobody is to tell him he found either thing.**
7. **IVO KELLOW, THIRTY-FOUR, IN AN ASH. `ch − 630`, TWO HUNDRED AND THIRTEEN THROUGH TWO HUNDRED AND TWENTY, SPOKEN AT `844` AND `849`.** He was asked about a bridge four hundred yards off for the first time, said yes out loud, and wrote a day against his own name in his own hand. **He is not asked about the bridge again in this close and nobody told him refusing the seal restored anything for him.**
8. **GARRIN TOLLEY, THIRTY-EIGHT, WITH A CHILD OF NINE. ⚠ THE BOARD IS OPENED AT `845` AND THERE ARE THIRTY-TWO NOTCHES ON IT, ⚠ A FIGURE OF WHAT IS ON A BOARD AND NOT OF A FIGURE OF A PERSON, ⚠ AND NOBODY IS THANKED FOR IT.** He still has not got the part where he knows what to do with it. **On Saturday he asked what three of the nine positions are and was not answered, and that is the new question.**
9. **THE MAN OF FORTY-FOUR, HAL BRECHT, WHO LOST HIS REMEDY TO THE COMPARISON AND ARGUED THAT ROAD FOR ELEVEN YEARS, AND WHO HOLDS THE EIGHTH SEAT AND DECLINED THE COMMON ARCHIVE WITH A DUTY ATTACHED.** He said a compact is not a road, that it does not say the eleven years were the right eleven years, and that he would rather be wrong in a compact that is mended than right in a charter that is not. **His road is not stabilised and he is not told it will be.**
10. **MARDA VELL, TWENTY-NINE, IN A REED. `ch − 629`, TWO HUNDRED AND FOURTEEN THROUGH TWO HUNDRED AND TWENTY-ONE, SPOKEN AT `844` AND `849`.** She put the third counter-test to a woman of sixty-four on a Sunday, **and refused a figure of about four of her own as consent by name for the fifth time in two books.**
11. **SERA QUILL, TWENTY-EIGHT, WHO CANNOT BE SENT FOR IN THIS COUNTY OR THE ONE SHE CAME FROM. `ch − 642`, TWO HUNDRED AND ONE THROUGH TWO HUNDRED AND EIGHT, SPOKEN AT `845` AND `849`.** She said the rest of why to nobody, put down the man of thirty-one and let him pick himself up, and said it was not a third no and it was not a yes. **Her decision is not settled and she has said no twice.**
12. **ORLA FENNIMORE, FORTY-FOUR, KEEPER OF TWENTY-NINE CHAIRS AT THIRTEEN SHILLINGS AND FOURPENCE A YEAR FOR NINETEEN YEARS, WHO HAD NOT COME UP THAT CART ROAD SINCE THE MONDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINTH WEEK.** She named two dead persons out of eleven with the working given and said the first has no day written down because there is no form that asks for one. **The question she asked three times is still not answered and is carried whole.**
13. **CORVIN TACE, FORTY-THREE, WHO HAS KEPT THAT LANDING NINETEEN YEARS AND GIVES THREE THINGS EVERY MORNING, ⚠ AND WHOSE FOURTH THING WAS NOTHING FOR ELEVEN DAYS THROUGH EIGHTEEN DAYS AND ⚠ WHO SAID IT OUT LOUD FOR THE FIRST TIME IN EIGHTEEN DAYS AT `850`.** He said there is no water on those steps and therefore nothing under them, that he is going to start giving it again on Monday out of the same place, and **that he still does not know which of two figures at the same height is the right one.** His figure is a figure of a ladder and is owed to nobody else.
14. **⚠ THE PEOPLE THIS CLOSE BROUGHT ONTO THAT LANDING FOR THE FIRST TIME, ⚠ AND EVERY NAME AND AGE WAS MADE IN A MOUTH ON THE MORNING IT WAS SPOKEN: WENNA CALLOW, FORTY, WHO HAS ASKED FOR A DAY AGAINST A NAME FOR ELEVEN YEARS AND WILL NOT NAME A ROOM, WHO KEEPS THE FIRST OF THE SIX STONES AND WAS GIVEN A SEAT FOR IT AND **SAID A SEAT IS NOT A DAY** · IVET QUILLON, FORTY-ONE, WHO HOLDS A SEAT WITH NO STONE BEHIND IT AND ASKED THE QUESTION THE CHARTER LEFT OPEN IN ABOUT NINE EARS ON A SATURDAY · HESSA VINCH, THIRTY-SIX, WHO RANG A HAND-BELL AT THE TOP OF A VALLEY IN THE BRIAR MARCH FOR NINETEEN YEARS AND HELD THE SEAT OF THE BELL NETWORK, AND WHO DECLINED THE COMMON ARCHIVE WITH A DUTY ATTACHED AND SAID A BELL TELLS YOU SOMETHING IS HAPPENING AND DOES NOT KNOW WHAT · HAL BRECHT, FORTY-FOUR, AT ITEM NINE ABOVE · SABRA VINT, THIRTY-THREE, A MASON FOR NINETEEN YEARS WHO HAD THE HEARING-HALL ROOF OVER HER AND CORRECTED A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE TWICE ABOUT A STEP LAID ON ITS EDGE · TARIN, THIRTY-NINE, WHO TESTIFIED AGAINST HIS OWN COMMAND AND LOST HIS RANK AND CARRIES RECORDS BETWEEN FOUR PLACES OUT OF A COAT · BRAM, NINETEEN, THE FIRST APPRENTICE OF A PUBLIC CARTOGRAPHY OFFICE THAT IS FOUR DAYS OLD, WHO REFUSED A ROUTE ON THE GROUND THAT THE FOUR PLACES ON IT HAD NOT BEEN ASKED AND WHO IS ALLOWED TO · ABEL ARDLEY, SIXTY-ONE, WHO CARRIED A FOLDED PAPER NINE MILES ABOUT TWO MEN WITH THE SAME NAME · LINNET ORD, THIRTY-ONE, WHO KEEPS A BOAT BELOW THE LOW GATE, IS NOT ON A CREW'S ROLL, ASKED THE QUESTION AT `850`, AND **SAID THE WORD `citizen` OUT LOUD.** ⚠ NONE OF THEM IS A NEW ANTAGONIST AND NONE OF THEM IS ON A SEAT THEY DID NOT ASK FOR, AND THE FIGURE OF ABOUT NINE OF THEM IS A FIGURE OF A BANK.**
