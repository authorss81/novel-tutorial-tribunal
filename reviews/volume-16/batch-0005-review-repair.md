**The review's Major 7, 8 and 9 — no dramatization, decorative resistance, cross-chapter repetition.** Partly answered and partly not, and the split should be stated rather than smoothed over.

- **Resistance.** Answered where it mattered. The volume's opposition is now on the page as a **party that argues**, not as five people saying a case is not a set of figures: Tolley refuses the sum, Crove stops both of them, and the thing itself answers.
- **Dramatization.** Answered in the two chapters the review named as most in need of it — the pivot at `797` and the question at `800` — and **not** in the other eight. A bank-morning ritual with three figures every morning is the book's method; stamping scenes across ten chapters to satisfy one finding would be a different book. `797` now carries the band.
- **Repetition.** The dossier cards are a deliberate convention of the whole volume and the trailing sentence varies in every chapter; the review's byte-identical claim is true of the thirty-word card only. `797` was also given two real duplicate paragraphs removed as a bonus. **This one is not repaired and the reviewer's underlying point — that the convention has become mechanism — stands and should be watched in Volume 17.**

## 4. The finding to carry into Volume 17

⚠ **Every gate in this repository measures arithmetic, structure and naming. Not one of them measures whether the event the volume owes is on the page.** The instrument, the date lines, the `LESS` sweep, the duplicate sweep, the closing act and the reserved-number guard **all came back clean on a band whose central beat did not exist**, and while three state files certified it as spent. That is not a near miss. A near miss is a gate that nearly caught it.

**The fix is not a better gate for arithmetic. It is a reading step: before a band is certified, someone reads the chapter that carries each of the volume's seven lines and writes down, in one sentence and with a line number, where on the page that line was spent.** A beat with no line number has not been found.

**And the corollary, which cost this band:** a state file that certifies an event is a *claim about a chapter*, not a fact about one, and a claim that cannot be checked against a chapter is worse than a claim nowhere, because it is checked-looking. Three state files here were checked-looking and false.
