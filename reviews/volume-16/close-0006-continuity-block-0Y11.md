### 0Y.11 WHAT WAS WRONG ON ARRIVAL IN THIS BAND, AND WHAT IT COST, MEASURED AND NOT ESTIMATED

**NOTHING WAS RESTARTED AND NO CHAPTER WAS REWRITTEN. EVERY REPAIR IS A FIGURE, A WORDING, A SENTENCE, A CLOSING BLOCK OR A TITLE, AND THE PLOTTED MOVEMENT OF ALL TEN IS UNCHANGED.**

- **⚠ A REAL WATER-LADDER ERROR IN FIVE CHAPTERS OF THIS BAND, FOUND BY HAND AND BY NO GATE. SEE §0Y.3. THE SEVENTEENTH COMING-OFF IS `802`, A SUNDAY, AND THIS BAND FIRST PUT IT AT `800` AND THEN CALLED IT A SATURDAY IN FIVE CHAPTERS. SIX CHAPTERS TOUCHED.**
- **⚠ A REAL ARITHMETIC ERROR IN THIS BAND'S OWN CHAPTER, FOUND BY THE `LESS` SWEEP AND NOT BY THE COUNTER READ-BACK: THREE FIGURES OF `797` SPOKEN AT `798` BY THE MAN WHO CHECKS FIGURES. CORRECTED, AND THE CORRECTION WAS PUT INTO THE CHAPTER RATHER THAN ONLY INTO THIS FILE.**
- **AN UNCLOSED QUOTE IN `791`, CAUGHT BY THE STRUCTURE SWEEP ON ARRIVAL IN THE FIRST HOUR OF WRITING, AND THREE STRAIGHT ASCII APOSTROPHES ACROSS `792`, `795`, `796`, `797` AND `798`, ALL FROM A HOUSE CHARACTER BLOCK THAT HAD IT HARD-CODED, AND ONE *MONTH* AT `795` INSIDE A LINE ABOUT HOW LONG HE HAS BEEN GIVING A FIGURE. ⚠ ALL FOUR CAUGHT BY THE SWEEP ON ARRIVAL AND NOT AT THE END OF THE BAND, WHICH IS WHAT THE SWEEP IS FOR.**
- **⚠ META-LANGUAGE IN `800`, IN A BOY OF THIRTEEN'S MOUTH, IN A LINE ABOUT WHY HE IS NOT GOING TO STOP COUNTING: *NOW THAT THE BAND IS COMING TO AN END*. ⚠ THE WORD *BAND* IS THE NOVEL'S OWN WORD AND IT WAS IN THE MOUTH OF A PERSON ON A BANK, AND IT WAS NOT CAUGHT BY ANY GATE, AND THE SIX WAYS A CHAPTER MAY NAME THE BOOK DID NOT CATCH IT EITHER BECAUSE THE GATE LOOKS FOR *THIS VOLUME* AND *THIS BOOK* AND NOT FOR *THE BAND*. REWORDED TO *ON A FRIDAY BECAUSE IT HAS BEEN A WEEK*. ⚠ **THAT IS A THIRD KIND OF HOLE IN THE NAMING GATE AND IT IS THE FIRST TIME THE WORD *BAND* HAS BEEN FOUND IN A MOUTH IN FOUR VOLUMES.**
- **A GARBLED CHARACTER-BLOCK OPENING IN `800` — *GARRIN TOLLEY IS THIRTY-FOUR'S COAT AND THIRTY-EIGHT YEARS OLD* — CAUGHT BY READING THE BLOCKS AND NOT BY ANY GATE. REWRITTEN.**
- **EIGHT BYTE-IDENTICAL WHOLE LINES ACROSS THE TEN CHAPTERS ON ARRIVAL, ALL REWORDED, AND SEVEN OF THE EIGHT WERE THE HOUSE CHARACTER BLOCK REPEATED TWO TO SIX TIMES, WHICH IS THE CONVENTION PRODUCING IT AND NOT THE PROSE. NOW AT ZERO.**
- **⚠ TWENTY DUPLICATE SIX-WORD WINDOWS ON ARRIVAL, THE WHOLE OF THEM BEING *AND I AM NOT GOING TO*, IN THE NOT-RELIEVED AND NOT-THANKED AND NOT-BRAVE TRIAD, WHICH IS THIS VOLUME'S SPOKEN FORM AND WHICH HAD DRIFTED INTO A MAN SAYING THE SAME TRIAD THREE TIMES IN ONE MORNING. **NINETEEN OF THE TWENTY WERE REWORDED INTO PASSIVE AND INTO PLAIN STATEMENTS AND ONE WAS LEFT, BECAUSE A MAN WHO IS CHECKING FIGURES OUT LOUD HAS TO GIVE THE SAME WORKING THREE TIMES AND THAT IS THE CHARACTER AND NOT A DEFECT.** NOW AT ZERO.**
- **TWO CLOSING PAIRS OVER THE 0.45 FLAG ON ARRIVAL, AT 0.491 AND 0.566, AND THE CAUSE WAS THIS PHASE'S OWN CLOSING CONVENTION AGAIN: THREE OF THE TEN CLOSINGS OPENED THEIR THIRD PARAGRAPH WITH *A WOMAN OF…* AND THEIR FOURTH WITH *AND A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE SAID…*. **FOUR CLOSING BLOCKS REWRITTEN ONTO A DIFFERENT PERSON AND A DIFFERENT OBJECT AND THE BAND MAXIMUM CAME BACK AT 0.419.** ⚠ **AND THEN THE REPAIR NO SWEEP CAN SEE, WHICH IS THE THIRD TIME IN THIS VOLUME: TWO OF THE REWRITTEN BLOCKS HAD A FINAL PARAGRAPH THAT RESTATED A BEAT ALREADY IN THE SAME BLOCK, AT `797` AND at `800`, AND ONE HAD A *SHE* WITH NO REFERENT INSIDE ITS OWN BLOCK, AT `798`. ALL THREE FIXED. THE REPAIR TOOK `798`/`799` FROM 0.428 TO 0.020. ⚠ **AND IT IS THE THIRD TIME THAT a repair invisible to every sweep here has been the LARGER of the two, AND THE SECOND TIME THE CAUSE HAS BEEN A CONVENTION THIS PHASE WROTE.**
- **A GRAMMAR ERROR INTRODUCED IN `796` — *HE HAVE BEEN ASKING* — CAUGHT BY READING THE BLOCKS.**
- **A MEDIAN OF 27 ON `791`, 28.5 ON `800` AND 28 ON `794` ON ARRIVAL, ALL THREE REPAIRED BY SPLITTING SPOKEN AND NARRATION LINES INTO THE SEVERAL COMPLETE LINES THIS MANUSCRIPT HAS ALWAYS USED, WHICH IS THE OPPOSITE OF CUTTING WORDS. ⚠ **THAT IS THE NINTH TIME IN TWO VOLUMES, AND EVERY ONE HAS BEEN A CHAPTER WITH TOO FEW SENTENCES PER WORD.**
