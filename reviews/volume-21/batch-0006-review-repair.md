Source: `logs/batch-0005.review.log`. This is the record of what was changed in the
chapters and in state after that review. Band 5 was not restarted. No plot was moved,

## Fixed in the chapters

### A speaker asked the lead five hours before he arrived — fixed (`1042`)

Sena Dorr arrived at the fourth hour and put her asking to Ilyan at `1042:31`; Ilyan
came up at the ninth hour at `1042:33` and answered at `1042:45`. This is the band-4
defect recurring, and it was the one blocking finding.

She now **stands at the wall from the light and waits**. Her arrival paragraph says so,
Ilyan's arrival says he "asked for nothing until the woman at the wall spoke", and her
asking follows his figures. Nothing else in the chapter moved: her descriptor, his
descriptor, his want-and-cost speech, the headshake, the refusal, her answer and the
closing beat all stand as they were, in the same order, on the same lines.

Her asking also no longer sounds like a demand for that morning. She said "with nobody
else on the steps" and the descriptor said "with the landing empty", while the boy and
Corvin were both on the stone. Both are now *a morning* — "on a morning when nobody
else is on the steps", "said out loud on a morning when the landing was empty" — which
is what she has wanted since `1033`.

Her hour stamp was dropped rather than moved. She is the one visitor in the band who
arrives before the morning has a shape: she is a fixture of that morning at the bottom
of the road, waiting. Stamping her at the fourth hour put a numeral ahead of the lead's
ninth in prose order for no gain.

### Three sentences of the closing sweep paragraph were verbatim across the band — fixed

The byte-identical *line* gate passed, because the ritual question is the only whole
line that repeats and the volume wants it word for word. Underneath that, three
sentences inside the sweep paragraph were shared:

- the fortieth step gone round and the iron gone over with the broom head — **7 chapters**
- the dry strip swept up to its edge and what lay on it lying there at the seventh hour
  and at the eleventh — **6 chapters**

  counted — **8 chapters**

Every morning's version of all three is now written fresh, in each of the ten chapters,
keeping the facts: the fortieth step goes round and not underfoot, the chair goes round
the same way, the iron in the seam is crossed with the broom head and never with
fingers; the strip is swept to its edge and no further; what is on it at the seventh
hour is on it at the eleventh.

Two further verbatim pairs went with them: the sweep's own opening sentence, shared by
`1041` and `1049`, and the two-lengths sentence, shared by `1045` and `1048`. A third,
*nothing came of that, and he shrugged*, shared by `1045` and `1046`, was reworded in
`1046`.

**Left alone on purpose.** These still repeat across the band and are not defects:

### The ordinary form was carried off the wall in `1049` — fixed

On `1048` the woman of forty-four said, in a mouth, *I said it from this wall and it
stays on this wall, and I will not have it carried off in a pocket.* On `1049` the boy
of thirteen said the form unbidden about his own yoke, and Corvin Tace answered *you
said the saying right and you said it yourself*. Owed line 9 only requires the form to
stay unnamed, and it did — but her claim was contradicted on the page two mornings
later, which is the kind of thing a careful reader files and never forgets.

The boy now owns his own want in his own words: leaning the yoke against the wall would
not make it his, it would only put his wood where he could look at it and could not get
at it. Corvin's reply no longer treats it as a saying to be confirmed — *that is you
settling it with yourself and not with me*. The form is said once in the band, from
the wall, and stays there.

### Two pronouns had no clear antecedent — fixed

`1050:77` opened *And he set down what he was carrying* with Barnaby gone at line 13 and

### Trap 7 was thin on `1042` — the mistake is now owned in his mouth

`1042` had a want and a cost and no wrong judgment. Ilyan's refusal now names the glad
part as the part of the morning he would take back if the taking back were his to give,

keeps the refusal from reading as virtue.
