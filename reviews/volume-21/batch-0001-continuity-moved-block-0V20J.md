## 0V20J. ⚠ THE GROUND OF VOLUME 20 BAND 5, CHAPTERS `991`–`1000`, *THE THING HE PUT DOWN*, ⚠ **THE THREE THINGS THIS BAND PUT ON THE LANDING AND WHAT EACH ONE IS**, ⚠ AND THE FOUR FIGURES IT MAY NOT PRINT

⚹⚾ **WHAT IS ON THAT DRY STRIP ABOVE THE TOP STEP, MEASURED ON THE PAGE, IS **TWO** PIECES OF ASH. ⚠ THE FIRST WENT UP AT `981` WITH A GIVER IN IT, BECAUSE A MAN SAID OUT LOUD THAT HE PUT IT THERE. ⚠ THE SECOND WENT UP AT `991:61` WITH **NO** GIVER IN IT, BECAUSE HE SAID NOTHING, AT `991:67`, AND WALKED DOWN THAT BANK. ⚠ BESIDE THEM ON THE LANDING ARE THE IRON IN THE SEAM OF THE FORTIETH STEP AND THE CHAIR, AND ALL FOUR ARE DESCRIBED IN THE SAME WORDS ACROSS CHAPTERS, ⚠ **WHICH IS THE LEFT-THING CLASS AND IS NOT THE CARRIED-OBJECT CLASS AND MAY BE DESCRIBED THE SAME WAY AGAIN.** ⚹⚾**

⚹⚾ **THE THING WENNA PYLE SAID IT WAS, AT `992:49`, IS *A HAFT*, ⚠ THE NOTCHES ARE FOR A WEDGE, ⚠ AND THE ONLY EYE ON THAT LANDING IS AT THE HEAD OF A BARROW. ⚠ IT IS NOT WHAT IT WAS AND THE NAME CANNOT BE TAKEN OFF THE WOOD WITHOUT A HAND ON THE WOOD. ⚹⚾**

ANY FIGURE OF HOW FAR ANYBODY WALKED TO LEAVE OR FIND A THING — **ZERO** IN EVERY WORDING, ⚠ AND AT `991` HE WALKS DOWN THAT BANK AND **NOT** ONE FIGURE OF IT IS PRINTED. ⚹⚾**

⚹⚾ ⚠ **AND THE FIGURE OF HOW MANY THINGS ARE LEFT ON THAT LANDING AND NOT CLAIMED WAS GIVEN **ONCE**, AT `995:41`, IN A MOUTH, WITH THE WORKING GIVEN AT `995:45` AND THE REASON IT CANNOT BE CHECKED GIVEN AT `995:57`, ⚠ AND IT IS **NOT PRINTED IN A TITLE**, ⚠ AND IT IS **NOT ADDED** TO THE LADDER OR TO ANY RUN OR TO ANY DATE LINE, ⚠ AND IT IS **NOT GIVEN AGAIN** IN ANY OF THE TEN MORNINGS, ⚠ AND IT IS A FIGURE OF THINGS AND OF **NOBODY** AND IT IS OWED TO NOBODY. ⚹⚾**
