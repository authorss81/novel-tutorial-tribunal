## 6. WHAT THE SEVEN LINES DID

The seven lines are `outline/volume-21.md` §1, and §1 says itself that **none of them
is a `series.md` line** and that every one is marked with what it is derived from. A
controller may overturn any one of the seven by writing a Volume 21 block into
`series.md`, and this file then stands against that block and not against these seven.
**Here is what the fifty chapters did to each of them.**

### 6.1 Central pressure — a name said out loud that cannot be unsaid

The line: a name was said out loud, in the mouth of the man who put the thing down, on
a thing he does not work at, and it cannot be unsaid or taken off, because a name comes
off a piece of wood by somebody laying a hand on the wood, and there is not one person
on that landing going to lay a hand on it.

**What the fifty did: they made the reason mechanical instead of moral.** §3.2 says the
name is not on the wood at all — it is in the mouths — and therefore a hand on the wood
would not remove it either. The fifty mornings prove it by arithmetic rather than by
promise: **no hand went on that wood on any of the fifty mornings, and every one of the
fifty says so in its own words**, and the volume's whole second substance is that the
name survives everyone, including the man who cannot read it and including the man who
said it. Band 1 found the ordinary form already in use by somebody who did not invent
it. Band 4 took a name off the landing inside a woman's head with nobody carrying it.
Band 5 had the woman who knew the wood **confirm** it out loud — and a confirming makes
it owed and not given.
