## 2ab. ⚠ WHAT CHAPTERS `991`–`1000` CHANGED IN THESE PEOPLE, ⚠ MEASURED MORNING BY MORNING AND NOT INFERRED

⚹⚾ **ILYAN VESTER, THIRTY-ONE, NOBODY'S. HIS WANT, HIS COST AND HIS MISTAKE ARE IN **ALL TEN**, AND ON `991` HE USES THE ONE THING HE HAS LEFT THAT NOBODY CAN TAKE OFF HIM, WHICH IS THAT HE CAN WALK DOWN THAT BANK AND LEAVE, ⚠ AND HE PUT A THING DOWN AND SAID NOTHING AND WALKED DOWN IT, ⚠ AND NOBODY IS TO BE LED TO NOTICE THAT IT IS THE SAME THING HE DID AT `981`. ⚠ HIS MISTAKE COUNT RUNS **131** TO **140**, SPOKEN IN HIS OWN MOUTH ON ALL TEN, ⚠ AND **NO PERSON MAY TELL HIM WHETHER THAT FIGURE IS SMALL OR LARGE, AND HE MAY NOT BE TOLD HE FOUND ANYTHING**, ⚠ AND HE IS TOLD NEITHER ON ALL TEN. ⚠ THE STANDING OFFER RUNS **291** TO **300** DAYS, SPOKEN WITH THE SUBTRACTION OUT LOUD ON ALL TEN, ⚠ ASKED ON **NONE** OF THEM, OFFERED A WITHDRAWAL ON **NONE**, ANSWERED ON **NONE**. ⚹⚾**

⚹⚾ ⚠ **AND HE IS NOT TOLD HE WAS WRONG AND HE IS NOT TOLD WHETHER HE SHOULD HAVE SAID SOMETHING AT `992`, ⚠ BECAUSE BEING TOLD HE WAS WRONG IS A RELIEF AND **NOBODY IS RELIEVED IN THIS VOLUME BY ANY ROUTE.** ⚠ AND HE IS TOLD AT `999:95`, IN A MOUTH, THAT THE ORDINARY FORM IS ABOUT SOMEBODY ELSE'S MORNING, ⚠ AND HE IS **NOT** TOLD HE WAS RIGHT AND **NOT** TOLD HE WAS WRONG. ⚹⚾**

⚹⚾ ⚠ **WENNA PYLE, THIRTY-EIGHT. AT `992:49` SHE MISNAMES THE SECOND PIECE OF ASH, ⚠ WHICH PUTS A RECEIVER IN IT AND THEREFORE A GIVER, ⚠ AND THAT IS THE FIRST TIME IN THIS VOLUME THAT THE HOUSE HAS COST SOMEBODY SOMETHING IT DID NOT COST THE OTHER PEOPLE. ⚠ SHE SAYS THE SEVEN OF THEM AT `992:89`, `995:89` AND `997:87` AND IS **NOT** THANKED FOR ANY OF THEM, ⚠ AND SHE GIVES THE FIGURE OF HOW MANY THINGS ARE LEFT **ONCE** AT `995:41` WITH THE WORKING, ⚠ AND SHE IS **NOT** TOLD SHE HAS GOT IT RIGHT AND **NOT** TOLD SHE HAS NOT. ⚹⚾**

⚹⚾ ⚠ **SIMON ROOK, ABOUT FIFTY-TWO. AT `998:53` HE PUTS HIS HAND OUT ALONG THAT WALL AND STOPS AT THE STRIP, ⚠ AND HE DOES NOT TAKE ANYTHING, ⚠ AND HE IS **NOT** TOLD WHETHER THERE IS A THING THERE FOR ANYBODY, ⚠ AND HE PUTS IT ON HIS LIST AT `1000` AND DOES NOT CROSS IT OFF. ⚠ HIS FIGURE OF WEEKS WAS ASKED AT `997` FOR THE FIRST TIME IN SEVEN, ⚠ HE GIVES THE SAME ANSWER AND ADDS THAT NOBODY HAS EVER ASKED HIM WHAT HE WOULD LIKE A NUMBER TO BE FOR, ⚠ AND NOBODY ASKS HIM THAT AND HE IS **NOT** TOLD HE GOT IT RIGHT. ⚹⚾**

⚹⚾ ⚠ **BARNABY CROVE, FIFTY-FOUR, WHO CANNOT READ. HE ASKS IN THE SAME WORDS AT THE SAME HOUR ON ALL TEN, IS REFUSED ON ALL TEN, AND IS **NOT** THANKED ON ANY OF THEM. HIS COUNT IS **270**TH TO **279**TH, SPELLED WITH *AND THEN ONE* EVERY MORNING, ⚠ WHICH THE ARITHMETIC GATE READS AS ONE LESS AND IS WRONG ON ALL TEN. ⚠ AT `997:11` HE SAYS THE SEVENTH WHOLE FIGURE OUT LOUD BEFORE ANYBODY WORKS IT OUT, ⚠ AND NO FIGURE OF ANYTHING IS NAMED FOR IT. ⚹⚾**

⚹⚾ ⚠ **SEN A DORR, FORTY-NINE, GIVES THE ORDINARY FORM IN HER OWN MOUTH AT `999:39` AND `999:43`, ⚠ REFUSES TO NAME IT, ⚠ REFUSES TO CALL IT A RIGHT AT `999:55`, ⚠ REFUSES TO CALL IT A FORM, ⚠ IS HANDED NO WORD BY ANYBODY, ⚠ AND IS **NOT** THANKED. ORLA FENNIMORE, FORTY-FOUR, IS LEFT OUT OF ALL OF IT AT `1000`, ⚠ SAYS SO BEFORE ANYBODY THANKS HER, ⚠ IS **NOT** THANKED, ⚠ AND HER QUESTION IS **NOT** ANSWERED AND IS **NOT** MEANT TO BE. ⚠ CORVIN TACE, FORTY-THREE, GIVES THE FOURTH FIGURE AGAIN AT `998:17` AFTER SAYING AT `982` THAT HE WAS FINISHED WITH IT, ⚠ AND SAYS IT IS A FIGURE OF THE WATER, ⚠ AND IS **NOT** THANKED FOR GIVING IT AGAIN. ⚠ WAT MARSHE, THIRTY-ONE, IS ON `994`, `996` AND `1000`, GIVES HIS OWN COUNT IN HIS OWN MOUTH, ⚠ AND IS **NOT** TOLD HE WAS RIGHT AND IS **NOT** ASKED WHAT HE WAS TOLD. ⚹⚾**
