## 1B. Running in HASK after 620, opened by Chapters 611–620, and every one of them is a person still doing a thing

**EVERY LINE BELOW IS A THREAD. NOTHING IN IT WAS RESOLVED BY TEN CHAPTERS, AND THE BAND LANDED NO APPOINTMENT THAT BINDS ANYBODY, NO REMEDY, AND NO ANSWER TO ANY QUESTION IT ASKED.**

1. **THE COUNT IS TEN AND IT HAS MOVED FOUR TIMES IN TWENTY DAYS, AND THE COUNT FOLLOWS THE CHAIR AND NOT THE PERSON.** Eleven on the Tuesday of the hundred and eighth week, ten from the Thursday of it, nine from the Saturday of the hundred and ninth week when a woman came back from a fair and sat down, ten since the Wednesday of the hundred and tenth week when a woman of twenty-nine stood up out of the reed in the open. **A second count exists and is nine, and it is a count of people this town has not been able to ask and not a count of chairs, and no book in that town holds it.** `611`, `612`, `616`, `620`
2. **THE REED IS HELD BY NOBODY.** A woman of twenty-nine said yes on the Thursday of the hundred and eighth week in about nine seconds because nobody asked her, and stood up out of it in the open on the Wednesday of the hundred and tenth week and took it back, and the count went to ten, and there is no column for a reason and about eight of the people who have been in that book could not say why. `603`, `616`
3. **THE QUESTION OF `610` — whether a person who has said yes to a chair may take the yes back when they find out what is on the chair — WAS ATTEMPTED BY AN ORDINARY PERSON IN THE OPEN AND FAILED, AND IT IS NOT SETTLED AND IT IS NOT ANSWERED.** `610`, `616`
4. **THE QUESTION ASKED ONCE AT `620` AND NOT ANSWERED: WHETHER THE SAYING OF NO IN THE OPEN HAS TO BE WRITTEN DOWN THE WAY THE SAYING OF YES IS, AND IF IT IS NOT, WHO DECIDED THAT, AND WHEN, AND ON WHAT DAY.** Nobody in that town answered it. **The woman who could answer it best has not come up that plank since the sixth day of the hundred and ninth week, and a man of thirty-one said on the Sunday that he will be on that deck on the Tuesday, and nobody went and got her on the Sunday.** `620`
5. **THE SHEET OF DEAL WITH TWENTY-NINE LINES, ONE COPY NAILED INSIDE THE AFTER THWART OF THE GULL AND ONE KEPT AT A LANDING NINE MILES UP THE WATER, READ AGAINST EACH OTHER ONCE A WEEK IN THE OPEN, AND IF THE TWO DO NOT AGREE THEN THE THING IS NOT TRUE.** **It is the first thing in that town anybody can be sent for about, and it is ten days old, and a woman of forty-one with two carts has already stopped coming up the plank because of it.** `617`, `618`
6. **A SECOND LINE WAS ASKED FOR AND PUT ON THE SHEET, THE ELEVENTH THING OF ITS KIND IN THAT TOWN AND NEVER READ: THE LAST QUARTER MILE OF THE CART ROAD AND THE PLANK, WHICH HAS NO CHAIR.** A man of sixty-eight asked for it because the plank is why nine of them came up, and nobody in that town has read it. `617`
7. **A DAY AGAINST A NAME IS BEING ASKED FOR BY ABOUT NINE PEOPLE AND REFUSED BY ABOUT FOUR, AND THE REFUSAL IS NOT A MISUNDERSTANDING.** A woman of forty wants the day; a woman of forty-one says that if the day went on a sheet she would have to say what the load was in the same morning, and a man of fifty-four would have had to say his barrow and a half on the Saturday instead of the Monday. `618`
8. **THE PENNY DOES NOT GO ON THE SHEET, AND A MAN OF SIXTY-ONE SAID WHY OUT LOUD BEFORE ANYBODY ELSE COULD WORK IT OUT, WHICH IS THAT HE DOES NOT WANT A ROOM, AND ABOUT FOUR PEOPLE IN THAT TOWN NOW KNOW THAT THE MAN WHO TAKES THE MONEY WOULD RATHER NOBODY COUNTED IT.** `618`
9. **A WOMAN OF FORTY-ONE WITH TWO CARTS STOPPED COMING UP THE PLANK ON THE FRIDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND TENTH WEEK AND NOBODY SENT FOR HER, AND IT IS NOT A BETRAYAL, AND THE MAN OF THIRTY-ONE SAID OUT LOUD THAT HE IS NOT GOING TO GO AND ASK HER WHY BECAUSE HE PUT HER ON A FORM THAT DOES NOT EXIST YET.** `618`
10. **THE GRAVEL OF A CAUSEWAY IS EIGHT HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FOUR PENCE AND THE WHOLE LANDING FOR A YEAR TAKES A THOUSAND AND FORTY, AND THE ROAD IS STILL A PLANK ELEVEN FEET LONG.** `614`, `615`
11. **WREN SAYER, GONE UP THE WATER IN A LIGHTER ABOUT ELEVEN YEARS AGO, NOT HEARD OF SINCE, THE LAST LINE OF THE SHEET AND THE ONLY NAME ON IT THAT NOBODY IN THAT TOWN CAN BE CHECKED AGAINST, AND ONE OF THE OTHER THREE OF THE FOUR PEOPLE WHO WERE IN THAT WEIGH-HOUSE. HIS NAME WAS SAID OUT LOUD ON THAT DECK AT `619` FOR THE FIRST TIME IN NINETEEN YEARS, BY HIS BROTHER, TO A WOMAN FROM ANOTHER COUNTY, AND THE MAN WHO SAID IT DID NOT KNOW WHETHER SAYING IT WAS A THING HE WAS DOING FOR HIS BROTHER OR A THING HE WAS DOING FOR HIMSELF.** `617`, `619`
12. **A THING THAT WAS SAID IN A WEIGH-HOUSE NINE MILES UP THIS WATER BY SOMETHING THAT WAS NOT A PERSON IS NOW A THING A MAN SAYS IN A ROOM IN THE MIDDLE OF THE AFTERNOON, AND ANYBODY WHO HAS BEEN TOLD IT CAN SAY IT AGAIN, AND NINE PEOPLE WENT THROUGH THE DOOR OF A HOUSE ON THE LOW SIDE OF THAT LANDING ON THE THURSDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND NINTH WEEK, AND THE WOMAN OF SEVENTY-ONE WHO KEEPS IT HAS NOT WORKED OUT WHICH OF THE NINE AND IS NOT GOING TO.** `612`, `619`
13. **A WOMAN OF SEVENTY-ONE HAS BEEN THE INSTRUMENT FOR ELEVEN YEARS AND WAS NEVER ASKED, AND TOLD ONE PERSON, AND ASKED HIM NOT TO PUT IT IN A BOOK, AND HE IS NOT CARRYING IT ABOUT.** `612`
14. **A WOMAN OF THIRTY-FOUR FROM THE COUNTY OF ASHENFIELD IS IN THAT TOWN AND WANTS TO BE ASKED WHETHER SHE WANTS A CHAIR SHE HAS HAD FOR FIFTEEN YEARS, AND SAID ON THE SUNDAY THAT IF A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE ASKS HER ON A TUESDAY SHE WILL STAND THERE AND BE ASKED IT HERSELF, SO THAT THERE ARE TWO OF THEM AND ONE CAN BE SENT FOR.** `619`, `620`
15. **HESTON BRAKE, FORTY-ONE, PUT HIS OWN NAME AGAINST A CHAIR IN ANOTHER COUNTY, HAS NOT TAKEN IT OFF, CAME NINE MILES UP THE WATER TO SAY SO, AND IS NOT SORRY AND HAS NOT BEEN FORGIVEN.** `619`
16. **A COALITION WAS NAMED OUT LOUD, WAS SAID NOT TO BE THEIRS, AND WAS OWNED BY THE MAN WHO NAMED IT, AND ABOUT NINE PEOPLE IN THAT COUNTY NOW HAVE A WORD FOR A THING THAT HAS NOT HAPPENED YET.** `613`
17. **THE MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT TOLD HIS OWN CHILD OF NINE TWICE THAT IT IS NOT THEIR BUSINESS, AND THEN TOLD HER IT AGAIN AT `619` BY SAYING HIS OWN BROTHER’S NAME OUT LOUD FOR THE FIRST TIME IN NINETEEN YEARS, AND HE TOLD HIS OWN CHILD OF NINE TWICE ON THE THURSDAY OF THE HUNDRED AND NINTH WEEK.** `610`, `619`
18. **THE MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR IS STILL NOBODY’S, AND THE FIRST THING IN THAT TOWN ANYBODY CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT HAS HIS HAND ON IT AND HIS NAME AT THE TOP OF NOTHING, AND HE SAID SO OUT LOUD.** `617`
