## 1. THE FINDING OF THE FIRST CLASS, AND IT IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS MOST

**THE VOLUME'S RESOLUTION AS THE TOP AUTHORITY WRITES IT WAS HALF ON THE PAGE, AND THE HALF THAT WAS MISSING IS THE ONE THE WHOLE VOLUME WAS BUILT FOR.**

THE SECOND WAS NOWHERE.** The words *Bench*, *Veyra* and *Continuity Hearing* appeared **zero times across Chapters 601–650** when this phase began. A `grep` for `\bthe Bench\b` and `Veyra` over the whole of Volume 13 returned only the word *bench*, and every occurrence of that is the physical seat carried up to the head of the plank and set against the gunwale of the Gull.

**AND THE CONSEQUENCE WAS NOT COSMETIC. **THE REPAIR, AND IT IS IN `650` AND NOWHERE ELSE, BECAUSE `650` IS THE LAST CHAPTER OF THE VOLUME.** A man of forty-three who keeps a landing nine miles up that water nineteen years comes up the plank with a sheet of deal in his coat, and says where it was before anybody is asked: **on the counter at that landing on the Thursday, under a stone, with nothing on the outside of the stone and nothing on the inside of it, and about nine people in that yard and about four of them walked past it.** A man of sixty-one reads it out loud, because about four people on that bank can read and about nine cannot, and he is the only person in that room who reads a thing out loud with the working:

**THEN HE READS IT A SECOND TIME AND PUTS IT FACE DOWN ON A BARREL THAT HAS NO BOARD ON IT, AND SAYS OUT LOUD THAT HE READ IT TWICE AND IS NOT GOING TO READ IT A THIRD TIME IN FRONT OF ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, BECAUSE THE FOURTH HOUR IS NOT THE HOUR A THING GETS READ BETTER, AND SAYS THAT THERE IS NO COLUMN IN THAT BOOK FOR A HEARING AND NO COLUMN FOR A NOTICE AND HE IS NOT GOING TO CUT ONE WITH A KNIFE THIS WEEK.**
