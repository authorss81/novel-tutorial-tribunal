## 3. What is true at the end of Chapter 600, and §0F is later and is above

The famine did not happen: fifty-four acres of hay off ground in season in that hundred, of which the ten on the low meadow gave about six in a fortnight and four in five passes that are a hay of the following year and are named as such. The fifty-eight have a place: about eight hundred acres of nobody's common and two hundred and twenty feet of the top of a rise, with no licence, no form, no column and no day on it, and a foot of water that went over the low side of that swell on the second day after the rising morning and will do it again. A man of thirty-one is partially anchored: a year of his future options from the crossing of the tally stone to the crossing after it, and from that morning a permanent count from the water. ## 5. What is true at the end of Chapter 610, and it is short because the rest is in §0F

1. **HE IS STILL NOBODY'S.** There is no column in the county of Kell with his name at the top of it, no form that renews him, no rate on him, and he is not one of about thirty men, because there is no hundred in that county either. **He is anchored to a river and a block of limestone nine miles up the water and he is standing nine miles down the water from that block, and the anchoring is partial by the chapters and not by omission.** `601`, `605`, `610`
2. **A CASE IS OPEN AND IT IS NOT RESOLVED.** Ten of twenty-nine chairs have a name against them and nobody in them. One of them, the reed, is held by a woman of twenty-nine who said yes in the open on `603` and cannot be put out of it by any form, and **she has asked out loud whether a person may take a yes back when they find out what is on the chair, and nobody answered, and it was asked once.** `610`
3. **A DOCUMENT THAT BINDS NOBODY HAS BEEN USED ONCE BY A PERSON WHO READ IT, IN ANOTHER COUNTY, TO DO A THING HE HAS NOT TAKEN BACK.** It is the first time in his life that anything he wrote has been held up as a precedent by a stranger who had read it. **It is uncomfortable and it is not an answer and nobody asked it to be used.** `609`
4. **THE RIGHT OF REFUSAL IS UNRESTORED AND GIVEN TO NOBODY IN ANY COUNTY.** A woman of seventy-one said out loud in about nine people's hearing that a person who cannot be asked is not a refusal, and the sentence she was most afraid of is that in about four years there will be a form in that county that says a man who does not answer has refused. **That form does not exist and nobody in this band moved one step toward making it.** `602`
5. **NOBODY WAS RELIEVED, FORGIVEN, REDEEMED OR THANKED, AND `thanked` IS 26 ACROSS 601–610 AND ALL TWENTY-SIX ARE NEGATIONS, twenty-four of them on the page and one in a title and one in a closing.** `601`–`610`
