## 1h. What 621–630 changed

**NOBODY IN VOLUME 13 BAND 0003 WAS RELIEVED, FORGIVEN, REDEEMED OR THANKED. NOBODY IN IT IS A VILLAIN. THE PEOPLE OF IT ARE AT `state/continuity.md` §0H, and the ground is the same ground as Bands 0001 and 0002: a set of twenty-nine chairs on a lighter in a town of about eleven hundred people nine miles down the water, in the county of Kell, and the only person anybody in that town can be sent for about a chair is a woman of forty-four who keeps a book for thirteen shillings and fourpence a year, and who has not come up that plank since the sixth day of the hundred and ninth week.**

**ILYAN VESTER, THIRTY-ONE, NAMED IN ALL TEN, WITH A WANT, A MISTAKE AND A COST OF HIS OWN IN ALL TEN.**

- **Wants to know how a record that anybody can be sent for about behaves once it is in two places, and finds out inside a fortnight that it goes where it goes, and that the rule he is standing behind is what makes it go.** `621`
- **Wants to say no in the open about a chair and find out what happens to a person who says no in this town, and the answer is that nothing whatever happens, which is the finding and not the comfort.** `622`
- **Wants to know who in that town is against the copies, and is told by a woman of forty in front of about nine people that he could not have found her, because he has spent nineteen days asking people somebody can be sent for about, and that about nine of the nine he asked cannot be sent for about either.** `623`
- **Wants to be in a room, and is nine feet off with a withy knife for the second time in three days, and is told about it afterwards by three people who each tell it a different way.** `624`
- **Wants to put his hand on eleven copies of a record he wrote, and is told no in front of about nine people by the man who gave the rule, and writes the best sheet of deal in four counties on a Friday evening and it is not the one that goes.** `625`
- **Is in the room, and says the true thing about one half of the split and not one word about the other, and a woman of thirty-four tells him in about nine people's hearing that it is the fifth time in nineteen days and that about four of them are glad of it and about nine of them cannot tell.** `626`
- **Spends a day on his knees in a front room copying out a thing that is about him in a hand that is not his, is not allowed to write one line of it, and at the end of the day eleven sheets exist and none of them has his hand on it.** `627`
- **Says out loud that he is not going after a woman of thirty who has gone four days up the water, and why, and that he has now done the same thing twice in ten days about two different people, and both times he was right and both times he said it out loud in advance.** `628`, the first time at `618`
- **Starts to say that it is nobody's fault, gets about four words out of it, and stops, and a man of sixty-one says the same nine words and does the sum instead.** `629`
- **Opens his mouth to answer a boy of thirteen's question and is told no by a woman of twenty-six in front of about nine people, and holds the no, and it is the first time in nineteen days that he has been told no by a person in that town and said nothing anyway.** `630`

**HIS MISTAKE IS STILL THE CARRIER, AND IT IS NOW NINE VARIATIONS IN EIGHTY CHAPTERS, AND IN THIS BAND IT IS THE SAME MISTAKE SEVEN TIMES IN THIRTY DAYS AND HE KNOWS IT EVERY TIME.** **WHAT IS NEW IS THAT FOR TWO DAYS OUT OF THREE HE WAS NINE FEET OFF WITH A KNIFE WHILE THE THINGS WERE BEING SAID, AND A WOMAN OF TWENTY-SIX TOLD HIM IN FOUR SENTENCES WHAT THAT MAKES OF A PERSON, AND HE WAS TOLD IT TWICE IN THREE DAYS AND ANSWERED NEITHER TIME ON THE PAGE.**

**HIS COSTS IN THIS BAND, AND EVERY ONE OF THEM IS A THING THAT STAYS:** sixty-seven days in the county of Kell on a clock from the evening of `553` through seventy-six; a year of his own future from the crossing of that block of limestone to the crossing after it, half paid, and a permanent count from one water; **a thirty-first line on a sheet in two places that is about him and that he did not write, did not hear written, and cannot take off, and a thirty-second line about a woman of seventy-one that he put on because she asked him to and that he has not told her is on; and a hearing about every record in the world that was asked for in a yard nine miles up that water over a division he caused and that is not his decision and is not his fault.** STAGE UNCHANGED: **CO-WITNESS. HE IS CO-WITNESS AT `501` AND HE IS CO-WITNESS AT `630`.**

**WHAT HE CAN NO LONGER DO. HE CANNOT LEAVE THE COUNTY OF KELL BEFORE THE WATER GOES OVER THAT BLOCK OF LIMESTONE. HE CANNOT STOP KNOWING WHERE THAT ONE RIVER IS. HE CANNOT PUT A THIRD HAND ON A WALL, AND AT `625` HE COULD NOT PUT A HAND ON ELEVEN COPIES OF A THING HE WROTE HIMSELF, BECAUSE A RECORD MADE BY A PERSON THE RECORD CANNOT REACH IS THE SAME THING AS THE PERSON IN ABOUT FOUR YEARS. HE CANNOT ASK A PERSON A QUESTION ON THEIR BEHALF. HE CANNOT ANSWER A QUESTION IN THAT TOWN WHEN A PERSON IN THAT TOWN SAYS HE MAY NOT, AND HE HELD THAT NO.** `630`
