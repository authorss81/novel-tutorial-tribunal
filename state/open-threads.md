# Open Threads

## Immediate threads after Volume 02 Batch 0002

1. **The public maintenance review.** The fourth morning bell of the third month after the ruling, at the span, on public notice, callable early by any party on a named reading outside the band the keeper has kept, and not postponable. **It is 49 days from the eighth of the month, and about the twenty-seventh of the third month, and it is in five weeks at the end of Chapter 70.** Ilyan must be produced at it with his report and the two copies of his own sequence, to say out loud what the record cost and who paid it, and may not send somebody with his name on it. **His five entries are now named in the record with their sizes, and the fifth is the carrier's clause, which is the only one anybody can call out and ask to see him.**
2. **The charter of the district of Halloway, and whether it survives.** Nine sheets, five terms, four of them a bridge's, a fifth with a person in it, made on the seventeenth, five days old at the end of the batch and working. **It has no standing outside one poor district, it has no rate that it can pay out of anything but a pump, and the third sitting of the bench is on the twenty-eighth of the month and nobody knows what is on its agenda and the convenor has said in writing that she does not either.** Its survival as a durable right is Chapters 71–100 and **is not delivered.**
3. **The appeal, now used twice and running.** The ninth-bridge appeal was heard on the sixteenth and disposed of in three parts: the limit (this span, not widened), the receipt (a bench that receives it may not enforce it), and the **duty to answer**. The first appeal under the new charter was sworn by the gate hand and heard on the twenty-first and **refused on a rule that is in the charter, and partly won, and the thirty years is entered as owed and not paid.** The machinery is in use and is not clean: serving a district took nine hours in a clerk's office and there is still no clause for it.
4. **The eleven-day answers, and the one that said yes.** Nine of eleven benches answered. Seven said they hold nothing. **The standing authority of preservation answered that it holds every instrument printed in this city for four years, in a series running by plate, where any person may read any of it at a counter on a day, and that no person has ever asked.** That is a new fact about the C—2 chain, obtained by asking a question nobody had thought of asking, and it is not the same as confronting the office. **Ilyan has still not chased the C—2 chain and has still not spent the sealed preservation-index item.**
5. **Wen Lask's question.** *Does your paper say anything about a road with nobody in it.* Returned on the twenty-ninth from a good road where the toll has never been short, the loads go through, and the last name in the witness line is her father's and she has written over it twice. **Nobody can answer it, the court has no power over that road, and the honest answer from every door in Orison is that the paper says something about a person.** This is the volume's new question and it is now one step further along.
6. **The four props and the account.** The old four went back on the same cart on the eighteenth for four pounds, no new ones will be bought this year, the account is carried with a deficiency of eleven pounds four in the quarter, and the frame on the fourth bridge now says *fourth bridge, four words, asked and answered* with a date. **Anhalt has been asked what he wants for the first time and the answer has been entered, and he is still not a party, and there are no new props on a bridge that has wanted four for twenty-nine years.**
7. **Anhalt's thirty-four days.** He asked for one thing for himself — that it be on a page in a book and not on a nail that he has been the man who was not on the bridge for thirty-four days. **It is on a page, in a clerk's hand, on the back of a slip, in a wet lane, and it is going up the fen road in a cart in the morning.**
8. **The holding of the page.** Wenna Tace keeps the page, swears entries in it, and **may not appeal, appear in support of one, or be named in one.** She has two entries. She is paid a quarter of a haul rate by the district and the district's rate is a third of a haul rate, and she is the only person in that district who can read, and the rate on the pump is read aloud at the sixth bell on the first of every quarter by her.
9. **The carrier's clause, live.** A district can ask for the terms, and he has to walk four miles to take them, and the appeal is stayed if he refuses. **He has carried them once for nothing and will be asked again, and the reason it cannot be argued down is that if he says no then four hundred people have no door and the only way to have a door is to be a man who can be found.**
10. **Bryn Sallow's thirty years.** Over a hundred thousand hours, entered as owed by a district that never kept hours, at a rate the four who owe it set themselves, two of whom live on Weir Lane. **She has refused to have them counted and the number is entered as a shape and not a figure, and the only person producing the hours is a man from a bridge whose own relief depends on it.**
11. **Stage 2, and the fourth layer.** The practice is now a practice: the afterimage clears inside a burden of his own and does not clear when he rests, and it is a four-hour limit and not a cure, and it is load-bearing for somebody else's claim. **The token was used once and the third layer resolved into a rendering: a gate from two hundred feet, at the same height, every time, and a rendering has a vantage and a vantage is a place he cannot find. The fourth layer is a door, and the vantage has not moved.** Four people have heard him say the leg is not his: Sera Quill, Harrow, Roke, Ansa Rell. Tarin worked it out and said it first. **The sixth is the gate hand, who has now said the other half of it — *you have made my thirty years into a job for a man who has a leg* — and still does not know that the leg is hers.**
12. **The continuity line.** It appeared for the **sixth** time, on the twenty-second, under the fifth term of the copy in the holder's page, and the word changed again: *continuity preserves the record against the holder.* **Harrow refused for the fourth time, in the same words, entered it as an unentered line, named nobody, and said he will refuse on the seventh.** **The holder does not know.**
13. **The word on the form.** EXCEPTION is at the top of a form in nine districts within eleven days and is in no instrument. It came from a hearing at the ruling. **It cannot be stopped, because a form is a habit with a seal on it and a court has no power over a form.**
14. **The ward.** Eleven of fifty-five duties performed in a hundred and ninety years, five at Halloway and none of them done by a body, and a count that returned five places. **Nobody has yet asked whether a duty can be refused by the person doing it, which is the question the word *ward* now makes obvious and which the whole of the next volume's argument is standing on.**
15. **The five words and the count.** The bank stood on the twenty-eighth, a hundred and ninety people were behind a rope on the third, two hundred and forty behind one on the thirty-first, about two hundred on a flat on the seventeenth. **A bench of four in one district struck out its household column at a ratepayer's request and entered the loss of accuracy against itself, and a child of fourteen is a number in a record and is not named in any page of this court, and must not be.**
16. **The rate.** Half a farthing a bucket at a standpipe on the low water, in chalk, on a barrel lid, the first tax in nineteen years, in a district where nobody is on the traveller book either, and it is the same money the clean instrument wanted and less of it only because the gate is shut.
