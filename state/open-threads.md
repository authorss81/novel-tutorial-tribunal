# Open Threads

## Immediate threads after Volume 02 Batch 0003

1. **The page when the holder cannot keep it.** This is the question the batch ends on and it is Batch 0004's engine. **The fifth term wants a person in the district who can read, and there is one, and she is twenty-nine and hauls about two hundred loads a year and is paid a quarter of a pound more for keeping nine sheets and a page, and there is no procedure in this city for what happens when the holder cannot do it.** A bench of four can be asked and nobody has ever asked. **The whole of the district's law now depends on whether a hauler's back holds out until somebody who has never done it tries.** Nobody has ever put a person in an instrument and this city has spent three months finding out that it cannot.
2. **The appeal's survival as a durable right.** The carrier clause is used once and working. **Harrow's fourth procedure, invented at the review, is the first time anyone has written down how a person becomes the door for four hundred people, and it cannot be used, because the only district with a carrier is the only district that does not need one.** The charter still costs a poor district forty-four pounds and sixteen a year out of a fund with a bridge in it, and the pump rate is set at exactly eleven pounds four a quarter and not a unit more, and nobody can appeal either. **A right that costs money and has no standing outside one bench is a favour with a seal on it.**
3. **The third sitting of the bench of four at Halloway** has been and gone; the bench now has a haul rate, two debts in its book and neither paid.
4. **Two debts and no money.** The thirty years is a figure of one thousand four hundred and seventeen pounds and ten shillings, entered wrongly and refused and un-overruled. The hundred and fifty hauls of the last eleven months are entered as owed and not paid. **The district's road fund is two hundred and forty a year and is fed by cartage on a shut road, and the arrears came out of a barrow-huler's own wanting and are the largest single want of the people the charter was read to.**
5. **Wenna Tace's second barrow.** Written on a page for the first time in her life. Not asked for, not paid for, and the money that would buy it is owed to her by her own district.
6. **The continuity line, seven appearances, five refusals**, refused in the same words, nobody named, and the holder has still not read the line under the fifth term of the copy in her own room. **Harrow has said he will refuse on the sixth and on the seventh, and the volume's seventh refusal is still ahead.**
7. **A form with EXCEPTION at the top of it** in nine districts, and **the finding of this batch: the word is not a label, it is a claim that a rule is temporary, and a form that says so can be used against the person who relies on the rule.** It cost a man of thirty-four a season of four months and a roof. Nobody can stop the printing.
8. **A short copy of the charter is already in circulation.** A drover carried a copy down a lane on the twenty-seventh of the month before with no seal and no clerk, and it is missing the second half of the fourth term, and it was in a district for four days before the only complete copy arrived in a cart. **Every copy of a term in this city stops where a person begins. The thing that gets left off is always the part with a consequence in it.**
9. **A public answer is a price, and one desk is a monopoly.** Nine benches answered in public in eleven days; six of the seven *no*s have answered again with reasons; Fallowditch withdrew its answer in writing because of a woman called Adela Cray. **And the only *yes* in the city is a yes about paper: the standing authority of preservation has said twice that it can prove a rule was printed and cannot hold a person.** Nahl's own finding, in her own book and in no record, is that anybody who needs a page sworn can now name the one desk that said yes, that the leverage is real and cannot be used, and that in about a year somebody will try anyway and there will be a form about it.
10. **The Nine Yards.** Sixty-one people, nine gates, no district, paying Nethergill's rate for a bar they do not own, and a matter now on a docket that concerns them and in which they are not a party. The four pounds and six went back into the slot. **The finding is that a place can only be given a page by making somebody a body, and the only candidate is the carrier, and a holder is not a carrier.**
11. **The four new props** go up the fen road to the fourth bridge, and the old four come out, and **that deck is open in the weather until the new timbers arrive, and the man who keeps that bridge is the man sitting on it, and the court did not ask him and took no view.** A bridge that has wanted four props for twenty-nine years is being propped by other bridges' timbers for the last time.
12. **T. Blore, twenty-two.** Eleven months at the west end under a deck, one of two seasons, and the second hand is gone at the ninth bridge for this year, which is four hours a day and not one. He is nineteen miles off and **still does not know, and Sedge has not told him and committed to no date: it is not going in a letter, because a letter from a trade comes at the change and a man on a deck will read it standing up and take it for a summons.** **The telling is Chapter 0082 and it is face to face in Sedge's own mouth, with no messenger and no page in it, and nothing before that chapter may inform him.** He is the only person in this case's record named as having lost something in it, and nobody has written to him and there is no line that pays a man for eleven months.
13. **Corva Silt is a second head.** She knows that Ilyan Vester cannot read. It is not on a page. She named the four roles he is entered in and said she would not be that second head for free and would not refuse either.
14. **The Bridgeward, two steps along and no further.** Wen Lask's question has now been asked in public twice and answered once, and the answer is entered as not being the answer, and the road is a good road and has not been touched. Nobody has gone and looked and nobody is going to.
15. **The rendering, the vantage, the room, the door, and the hand.** The vantage is a room with four people in it and one of them is a gate hand he has never seen at a table. It has not moved. His left hand is a scar at the base of his thumb. The token has been used four times and the fourth took the words.
16. **Abel Kearn's wall and a survey in Fallowditch.** He took his name off a board rather than be produced and he has a crack in his mother's wall that a timber merchant paid for and would not be repaid, and he is waiting for a mechanism, and there is no clause anywhere in this city that lets a person carry a sworn page four miles to a room.
17. **Dessin Nahl's finding** — *the only district in this city with a carrier is the only district that does not need one* — is in her own book with a date on it and in no record but hers, and nobody in this case has read it.
