## 0. Two hazards for whoever writes next, not threads

## 1. Running, and the four that changed shape in 421–430

1. **A clerk of thirty is four hundred miles off on his own two legs with three sheets, having refused the office's charge, and he is to be at the seat.** The office's man went out of this city on the same road on the same day and nobody in this city knew. His own count of his nine years is carried once, at `401:39`, and **may not be resolved by a batch.**
2. **Tarin Keel is walking north after him, and Ilyan said out loud that he was not going to stop him.** He is not in this city. He walked past the plate two miles out eleven days before Chapter 430 and nobody knows.
3. **Hessa Marl is a person in a record four hundred miles off, she cannot say no, her name is at the top of a page, and her own no is in a different line of a different book.** She is not on any list and not off any list. **The form for being struck out does not reach her and the form for striking her out is a removal, not a declination.**
4. **Kerwin Dace is four hundred miles off with a return that has one column and no name in it**, and he does not know what the money is for and is not going to be the one who asks.
5. **A run has no column, and a carter's boy of nineteen gave it one.** Unchanged, and not this band's mechanism. He was not in this band and was not asked for anything and said nothing.
6. **THE ROLL HAS NO COLUMN, AND A KEEPER OF ONE HAS NOW HAD ONE. This thread did not exist before this band and it is now its centre.** A roll counts doors; a roll in the office's one column is a register; a register goes up a hill; the woman who kept it for eleven years unpaid cannot now tell anybody whether it is right. The roll comes back down in about three weeks, in the ordinary way, on a cart, and nobody is at the cart.
7. **THE BOY OF SIXTEEN PUTS PEOPLE IN BOOKS BY BEING THE ONE STANDING IN A DOORWAY, AND NOBODY HAS ASKED HIM ABOUT IT.** He has worked out that he is the only person who could put a person of this place into a book, that no form asks a person of sixteen whether he may, and that naming the man of the ward would make the whole fortnight be because of who he knows. He refused. **The thread is open. It is not a power and it is not a rank and nobody has thanked him.**
8. **The return with one column has a clerk in it and a woman in it and a word at the end of her line that is the office's.** A person who comes back the same is a thing; a person who comes back different is a different thing; the column has room for the first and not the second, and the office has not asked which she is.
9. **THE CO-WITNESS LINE.** Two names, one of a place and one not of it, and neither can take the other off. The price was named in advance by the man who is not of the place: the person of the place will be asked about him for as long as there is a road and a form and an office, and he cannot name one person who could stop it. **The price is unpaid and is not going to be paid back.**
10. **The office's answers.** Six clauses, all correct, and a bar that is to remain up. The office has not changed its mind by one line and has never been wrong about anything it has printed in this city.

## 2. Opened by this band, and none of it closed

1. **The south side of the Low Road**, which has no name in any office document and is the only way past the ninth marker on foot.
2. **The plate at the ninth marker**, which is up, which nobody in this city can take down, and which is barring a road with nothing behind it.
3. **The removal of a woman from a city she has never left**, in a book four hundred miles off, in a line she never filled.
4. **The carrier's wage** for a cart with a return in a coat and a roll in oilcloth, paid out of four shillings a year that has never been paid, for which there is no line on any form in the county.
5. **The printed definition of a person of the place**, and the boy of sixteen whom it does not reach, who is not on a list and is not off a list.
6. **The word *co-witness***, said in a room by a man who is not one, and the rule that it is a piece of work and not a rank and cannot be revoked by either of the two people on the line.
7. **Four of the six things of a civic crossing, and not the fifth:** a named traveller who has gone and come back, a route open on one side, a place kept, and a count that went up a hill. **No exit guarantee**, because the fifth row says a person so conveyed is not to be asked about the city afterwards, and nobody has asked, and one person will not.
8. **A paper from four hundred miles off** that is right about the work and wrong about the plan, and nobody has answered the plan.
9. **The unanswered question of the volume:** what did the First Witness sign, and which people are still governed by that signature. It is not raised in this band. It is not to be raised by gesture, by a look at the sky, or by a panel.

## 3. What this band did not do, and it is the whole list

It did not restore the right of refusal, and nobody has one. It did not pay, spend, accept or resolve the twelve shillings, and it did not touch them, and it did not blank the line for the three hundred names, which is still the correct figure. It did not answer, sum or partition the hundred and forty. It did not name the boy of eight out loud in any room. It did not hang the bell, rebuild the porch or pay for the corridor. It did not use the word *arbiter*. It did not let a position, a record, a plate, a stone, a roll, a seal, a road, a sheet or a seat answer, speak, reply or render. **It did not make Marrow a villain and it did not make him absent; he is right about the roads, and the office is still correct, and the succession has not begun.** It did not spend a second panel. It did not invent a rate per head; it is the band in which the house says out loud, by a man who counts halls, in a room, with the working, that a government which divides a number of doors by a number of people has decided what a person is. It did not make Hessa Marl the co-witness and it did not make her lose anything the band needs. It did not confess anything and it resolved no affection. It did not relieve, forgive, redeem or thank anybody.
