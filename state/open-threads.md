# Open Threads

## Immediate threads after Batch 0004

1. **The full hearing.** It is set for the fourth morning bell, which is also the day the local arrangement ends and the day the load-held day would have ended anyway if the guild had signed. The three remedies return — amend the charter, break the span, or hold the hearing open — and the maintenance-review condition and the Ro household's legal access, housing, and work are all set for it. Chapters 41–50. The charter is still unamended. Appeal, enforceable exit, and durable housing and work protection are still unresolved and not addressed.
2. **The keeper's pause clause.** The gap is now measurable, explained, and priced. A named keeper has described a span, named an inspection, seen a figure, and had no clause that lets her stop the load. The guild is ordered to bring a costed pause proposal to the full hearing, and the cost of the pause — the stopped load, the unpaid hour, the schedule line that does not exist — is the district's strongest argument and has not been answered.
3. **The emptied west seat.** The joint is not wearing; it is being emptied by water under a bedded course. It borrows one and a half fingers on a light load and up to three on a heavy one and has never been observed to accumulate. The pale line is the permanent set and is a different number. The stone and the lime are bought and are at a yard off the fen road; the load-held day was won and then lost; the seat is unfixed and the fix needs three weeks of cure the case does not have.
4. **The keeper's pause burden has never been priced.** The district's position is coherent: a span that cannot be stopped is cheaper to maintain, and the district cannot fund a power it has neither created nor voted for. A full hearing that cannot answer who pays for a stopped load will not restore the clause.
5. **The arriving court clerk.** A new court record hand takes the case. Ilyan's standing is lapsed and cannot be renewed except by a new ruling; he enters the full hearing as a witness to his own attached observation, with no constituency. The one lever he has not spent — the sealed item in the preservation index — is a lever he has been told not to spend, by the advocate whose family it would expose.
6. **The fifth mark in the count book.** A cut margin mark for a load that walked and did not come back, appearing in a guild working book inside a hearing field with forty people watching, in a book with three hands in it, none of which cut it. Tarin held the book under a signed court entry so that the record survived its own anomaly. Nobody knows what it is. It has not been explained and must not be.
7. **The standing preservation authority.** Print run 114 was authorized by an office with a stamp and no person. Its own index shows eleven items taken into preservation in the week the current working plate was executed, all routed C—2, of which two are named in class and neither carries a name. Preservation and enforcement share one signature. Ilyan has been warned, by the clerk it applies to, that going on after the office makes records stop coming to everybody.
8. **The C—2 chain.** One documented step deeper and still no responsible person: the maintenance-review condition is in the authority's index for the week the plate was executed, and a sealed continuity request is beside it, and both are routed to a desk with a stamp, no clerk, and no incoming index after the rerouting date. The pre-arrival paper carrying Ilyan's private Earth sequence carries the same route with a receipt inside the gap. Two small districts cannot produce their own maintenance terms for the same reason. The desk still has no name.
9. **The two items in the index, kept apart on purpose.** Ilyan has the class, the week, and the routing of both and has not put them next to each other in anything filed, because the second is Sera's family. She declined to ask what is in it and the print-shop clerk entered the refusal. It stays untouched unless the full hearing forces it.
10. **The Ro household's new position.** A party under objection in the ward's common register, with a sworn tenancy, a portable work record, a stable crossing hour, a lapsed-but-continued relief resting on the court's own procedural principle, and no signed acknowledgment. The register has a column for objection and no column for leaving. Tamsin's account exists only as a copy with five terms written on it, in a guild's book, and cannot be entered as court evidence without her consent, which she has given on four conditions of her own.
11. **The common clause at the fen crossing.** Draft eleven circulates unadopted with the witness question open and a code with no office in a district column. Three districts will meet it again. A rule that works on the ninth bridge and does not work on a joint that does not come back is a rule waiting to fail somewhere else.
12. **Tamsin's fifth term.** Her account may not be used to cure a refusal. The strongest evidence in the case is also the most dangerous, and the terms that protect it are in a guild's hand and travel with a copy.
13. **The quiet lane reason.** An inspector offered Neris her daughter's account in place of a signature. Nobody has acted on it and the offer is on nobody's record. It is a template, and templates travel, and print run 114 proved that.
14. **The two measurements, still separate.** The pale line the joint has taken and kept, and the fingers one load borrows and gives back. Any scene in the next batch that reports drift must keep them distinct.
15. **The Ninth bridge's local arrangement.** Dusk to the fourth morning bell, four named sources, a pump two days short, a load-fee no household pays, no district copy of anything, and an end date chalked at cart height.

## Relationship and political promises

- Ilyan and Sera work under a stricter boundary than before and the boundary is holding. She refused him the archive in public, corrected him in public and was right, named the one lever he has and forbade it, and said the one warm thing in the batch in a lane. Trust remains conditional, specific, and revocable. No romance beat has occurred and none is planned in this volume.
- Ilyan and Tarin are a working alliance with a real disagreement about witness use. Tarin refused the bell account as a witness to Ilyan's theory in front of witnesses, held the count book out of a marshal's hands under oath, and refused Bram's line book as a constituency before Ilyan could misuse it.
- Tamsin and Neris have three times refused the uses that would strengthen the case at their own expense. Tamsin's account is the only testimony in the case that describes how the bridge failed and it is in a guild's book under five terms she set. Neris's reason for protecting her is known to Ilyan alone and is not to be repeated in public.
- Oren Sedge's guild has won two arguments with its own hands and refused a third, has no money, and is carrying the span on an arrangement that ends on the morning of the hearing. It has no mandate from six crews and is not going to get one by asking.
- Sel Harrow has reappeared, has ruled on a distinction he invented, and is a probable witness in the full hearing. Maren Kett has been right more often than not and has still lost nothing. Dain and Vale remain institutional actors and not culprits, and Vale has now said the office's own limit aloud in front of witnesses.
- The lowland conservancy has withdrawn; the ward seats are empty; the district will not name a constituency; Hesk Ardan is a man with nine carts and a book and no mandate; the Cinder Ward will not be a body; and the Ro household is a party under objection. Ilyan has nobody behind him and the record says so.
- Nadia remains an independent Earth witness; every exchange must carry a cost and no rescue shortcut.
- The final hearing must resolve the global constitutional conflict without unanimity, a new enemy, or a reset.
