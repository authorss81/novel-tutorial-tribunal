## 1F. THE THIRTEEN THREADS OF 651–660, AND EVERY ONE IS A PERSON DOING A THING

**A THREAD IS A THING A PERSON IS STILL DOING. A HAZARD IS NOT A THREAD. NONE OF THESE THIRTEEN WAS CLOSED BY THE BAND THAT OPENED IT AND NONE WAS ADVANCED BY THE REPAIR PASS; THE REPAIRS CHANGED DATES AND COUNTS AND NOT ONE OF THESE THIRTEEN.**

1. **THE CROWN IS ABOLISHED AND THE RECORD IS A BOARD LEANING AGAINST A BARREL.** A single Crown of about nine people was abolished on the Monday of the hundred and fourteenth week by about nine people at a barrel in the open, and it binds nobody, and it was abolished anyway, and **what is left at that barrel is a barrel with no board on it, nine names under a board about four feet by a foot and four inches, no Crown over it, and a bench against the gunwale of a boat that a man of thirty-one sat on twice.** `649`, `651`, `660`
2. **THE NOTICE IS STILL LYING FACE DOWN ON A BARREL.** A sheet of deal with about four inches of writing on the front of it and a seal on it came up that cart road under a stone on a counter nine miles up that water on the Thursday, was read aloud twice and not a third time, and is lying face down on a barrel nine miles down that water with the stone back on the counter where it came from. **Nobody has taken it inside, nobody has burned it and nobody has put a mark on it.** `650`, `651`, `658`, `660`
3. **THE LOCAL HEARING IS THE THIRTY-SECOND DAY AND NOBODY GRANTED IT AND NOBODY REFUSED IT.** Asked for in a yard nine miles up that water on the Monday of the hundred and eleventh week, to be read in one room, the ones that disagree with each other especially. **Thirty-two is six hundred and sixty less six hundred and twenty-eight and the subtraction is spoken, and nobody has written it down.** `628`, `658`, `660`
4. **THE WORLD HEARING IS DECLARED AND PUBLISHES NO DAYS.** A Bench four hundred miles off has declared a Continuity Hearing for every record there is in all of Veyra, and names no room, no county and no keeper, and says every person in every county may be spoken about at it and no person may be sent for about it. **A body that does not publish its days cannot be late, and there is no figure for it and there is not going to be one.** **AND NOBODY HAS SAID OUT LOUD THAT THE TWO HEARINGS ARE THE SAME THING, AND THE WORLD HEARING HAS NOT SETTLED THE LOCAL ONE.** `650`, `658`
5. **THE FOUR LINES ARE IN THE WRONG BOOK.** A man of sixty-one has put four lines about a gallery on the leaf after the last head of a book of heads and carts, under the name of a man of sixty-six who is a sinker, and has said out loud that it is the wrong book and that there is no right one and that the reason he used it is that it is the only book in the county of Kell that anybody can be sent for about. **AND A WOMAN OF FORTY-FOUR HAS SAID THAT THE OTHER BOOK IS ALSO WRONG, AND HAS MADE NO FIFTH REFUSAL, AND HAS SAID THERE IS NOTHING TO BE DONE ABOUT IT.** `653`, `657`
6. **NINE WORDS ARE IN ABOUT NINE HEADS AND NOBODY CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT ANY OF THEM.** *Nine feet, and then it is somebody's*, heard standing in a gallery about nine years ago by a man of sixty-six who does not know who said it and will not be the man who says. **Two people have refused to write it down — a woman of forty-four in four sentences, because a thing with no person on either side of it is a thing that has not happened yet, and a woman of twenty-eight in nine sentences, because there is no body in that gallery.** `659`, `660`
7. **THE BEAM IS MOVING WITH NOBODY IN THE ROOM.** About nine feet long, on its side, in the roof of a gallery about nine feet high about nine feet past a narrowing, going along about two inches and coming back, with no cord, no water under it that nine people can feel and no draught nine people can feel. **A man of sixty-six will not be the man who tells you what moves it, and a man of thirty-one said the whole of a thing instead of half of it and was wrong, and about four people in about eleven are still sure it is a machine.** `656`
8. **THE MAN OF SIXTY-SIX IS THE ONLY PERSON IN FOUR COUNTIES ANYBODY CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT THE WORD *LOOM*, AND THERE IS NO FORM THAT SAYS HE MAY BE ASKED WHAT IT MEANS OR THAT HE MAY NOT.** He said it once, in about four hundred years, in a gallery, and he said he got it from a man he dug with when he was about twenty-five who was not of that county and is not going to be found. **AND HE IS ALSO THE ONLY PERSON WHO CAN BE SENT FOR ABOUT THE NINE WORDS, AND HE IS FOUR MILES OFF A MILL IN A COUNTY NOBODY IS GOING TO.** `656`, `659`
9. **THE MILL STOPPED FOR A DAY AND A HALF.** About eleven people in four houses eat out of it and one of the eleven is her mother, and a hand mill is about nine hours of a day, and the figure of what that cost was refused out loud and not written anywhere, and **the woman who keeps it came down on the Thursday of a week in which she had said on the Thursday she would not come down again.** `659`
10. **THE MAN IN THE ASH HAS BEEN IN IT THIRTY DAYS AND HAS NOT STOOD UP, AND HE ASKED ONE QUESTION HE WAS NEVER ASKED, AND AT THE END OF THE BAND HE SAID ONE WORD.** *Is the water over the stone*, asked on the Tuesday of the hundred and sixteenth week and answered in four words. *Next*, said on the Friday of the same week from the doorway of the ash, and nobody asked him what he meant and he did not say it again. **About nine people on that bank walked past that doorway about nine times on the Friday and nobody stopped at it.** `657`, `660`
11. **THE WOMAN IN THE REED HAS BEEN IN IT TWENTY-EIGHT DAYS AND HAS NOT STOOD UP.** She said one word — *Both* — and did not stand up to say it, and **no chapter of 651–660 gives a day-count for her after `657` and none was invented.** `657`
12. **ABOUT FOUR PEOPLE IN ABOUT ELEVEN ARE SURE THE BEAM IS A MACHINE, AND THE FINDING OF THE BAND IS THAT NOTHING DOWN THERE ANSWERS TO ANY NAME.** A man of thirty-one named a thing once, flat, under about ninety steps of bank, and nothing whatever happened, and **the cost of the naming went to a man of fifty-four who cannot read and who said the price out loud himself before anybody asked him.** `656`
13. **THE BAND'S OWN QUESTION WAS ASKED ONCE ON A FRIDAY MORNING AND WAS NOT ANSWERED.** *If a thing down there is held up by a person doing a job, and the bottom nine steps are held up by two people about once a year, and a chamber is held up by two hands once a day, then who is holding the part in the gallery up.* About nine people heard it and about four understood all of it, and a man of thirty-one said he has not answered it and is not going to. `660`

**AND THE FIGURES THAT TRAVEL WITH THE THIRTEEN, AND NONE OF THEM IS SETTLED: the water is on the first three steps of about ninety and the board is on its stick, and the next time those steps can be walked is the next time that block of limestone comes out, and nobody in four counties has a figure for that either; a year in that county is a day of a river and a man of forty-three has two crossings in his own hand out of nineteen; about eleven people in four houses eat out of a mill four miles off; about nine people in four counties cannot be sent for about, and the man of thirty-one has said out loud that his figure is a figure of about nine days and not of a county; and a man of thirty-one is nobody's, and `citizen` is at 0.**
