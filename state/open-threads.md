# Open Threads

## Immediate threads after Volume 02 Batch 0001

1. **The public maintenance review.** The fourth morning bell of the third month after the ruling, at the span, on public notice, callable early by any party on a named reading outside the band the keeper has kept, and not postponable. **It is roughly seventy days out at the end of Chapter 60 and no earlier unless a keeper's band is read outside it.** Ilyan must be produced at it with his report and the two copies of his own sequence, to say out loud what the record cost and who paid it, and may not send somebody with his name on it. **The appeal he filed on the twenty-third is pending and must be heard or disposed of before that date.**
2. **The appeal, used once.** It was filed on the twenty-third under term six, notice was served on every named party and one seal, and **it worked.** The condition disputed is whether a review held at one span may be received in a bench that has not adopted the instrument. **The second is in Court 61–70's hands, and whatever happens to it will be the first time a right nobody had used is used against the settlement rather than for it.**
3. **The Bridgeward.** A region with no district, a toll taken for eight years, a keeper, a good road, and eleven dead names entered as the witnesses on a form copied from the ninth bridge. **Nobody can challenge it because a challenge must be served on every party named and every party named is dead.** It works, the toll has never been short, and a woman of seventy-one collects it. Bram's line book is the only record. **Ilyan has not chased it and has said why: the preservation office, and the price of going on after it.** This is the volume's new question and it is the hardest one in the book, because the answer is *it is fine*.
4. **The four paid hours, ended.** Four hours a day, eight days, in chalk, by name, at a pump, ending on the twenty-eighth with the district's reason on the reverse of the board. **The reason is the ninth bridge's tenth term, in three places at once.** The balance of the tide is entered as unpaid and Bryn Sallow refused to count it, because counting it would make it a wage and a wage wants a rate and a rate wants a body. **Nobody has yet offered her a body, and that is the whole of the next forty chapters.**
5. **Hanne Kolm's schedule, sworn at Halloway.** The only sworn instrument in this city sworn outside the district it was sworn in, about a burden its author has never held and cannot pay, on a page that nobody will accept. **The court neither accepts nor refuses it. The instrument's defect is the point: *the entry is open to the ward* does not say the ward's clerk holds it, and eleven benches have been asked whether they have anything that can be sworn four miles from the road it was sworn on, and eleven have answered that they have not.**
6. **The hearing on the twenty-ninth, set and not held in this batch.** Mr Vale's question in writing: whether a district may pay a person who has no address. The court's own notes say it can write a clean instrument in an afternoon and cannot write the other thing, and that four benches have tried in nineteen years and produced eleven valid instruments and none that was affordable, and that the court is afraid. **The missing thing is named as a piece of paper that can be sworn in a room with no address in it.**
7. **Ansa Rell and the eleven benches.** She wants one city-wide maintenance and access rule before the end of the month, she is right that it is cheaper and she is right about the need, and her instrument will be procedurally valid and place its burden where nobody is named. **She is not defeated and is not a villain. Her defeat produces a difficult ally and belongs later in the volume.**
8. **The halloway print and Instrument H-9.** The gate sheet is still on the board with the seal on it and the rain on it. Clause two is struck in chalk. **The settlement is still in four districts and eleven benches, and the fourth paragraph of the sheet is still a two-hundred-year-old word meaning *registered*.**
9. **Stage 2 and what it is doing to him.** The left leg, the two layers of the low water, and a chain in his right hand four hundred yards away. It comes back in a house with the door shut and goes again on a bank. **The blackglass token is the same kind of thing and is warm, and touching it takes the smell away for about fifty minutes and brings it back with a third layer. He has understood that the passage and the afterimage are one operation and that he was not asked. He has not resolved it and has said he is not going to chase it.**
10. **The fifth mark in the guild's count book.** Still unexplained. Tarin held it under a signed court entry. Nobody knows who cut it, nothing was named, and Tarin would testify under oath that he cannot account for it and would not enjoy it.
11. **Wist Anhalt.** His four words are nailed to the ninth bridge's frame, the props go back on the same cart before the review or the district pays, and he is not a party. **Nobody has asked him whether he wanted it.** He sent two hands unasked in Volume 01 and has not been spoken to since.
12. **The two measurements, still separate, and a third.** The pale permanent set, the borrow-and-return band, and the figure that exists only under a standing load. **Three districts now quote them differently and all three were in the room.**
13. **The C—2 chain, the preservation authority, and its wage.** Print run 114, eleven items in one week, two named in class, a desk with a stamp and no clerk. **Untouched by this batch, and Ilyan has now said out loud that he is not going to chase it.**
14. **The Quiet Chamber.** The original page is still missing and the slot is empty and the thread is cut. The one surviving line says a refusal's entry is **open to the ward**, and Sera has read it again in Chapter 60 and found that she had been reading a place into a sentence because a place was what she had. **That is a consequence of the line and not a recovery of the clause, and it is close to the shape of the volume's resolution. The next batch must not be able to top it.**

The continuity line has spoken five times and the word has changed once, from *stated* to *entered*. **It did not appear at all in Volume 02's first movement, and its absence is now a thing a reader can notice.**

- Can Ilyan belong to Veyra without being absorbed into its preferred story?

Sera named the difference in a corridor and told Ilyan to be frightened of it; he was not, and the batch has now shown the difference acquiring a district.

- **Is a right with no receiving office a right?** Term six and term seven are both on one bridge in one ward, and the first person who needed either was four miles down a fen road.

## Relationship and political promises

- Ilyan and Sera are professionally close and personally unaddressed. She is licensed, she is not his advocate, she corrected the volume's framing twice, she read the facing leaf again and found she had been wrong in a lane, and she said on a step a month ago that if it becomes affection it will be a separate entry and it will be dated. **No romance beat has occurred and none is planned for the rest of this volume.** Her family claim is separate, unfiled, and needs a page that may not exist, and she has still forbidden him from spending the sealed preservation-index item.
- Ilyan and Tarin: Tarin held the rope at a field on a bank, told Ilyan to sit down and was right, and carried him off a step when the leg went. He has still not been asked to testify at anything and would testify under oath that he does not know who cut the mark.
- Ilyan and the Ro household: the charter protects them and nobody has asked them for anything since the ruling. Neris got a second notice about her own household in a month and asked for the arithmetic to be entered while the woman who can check it was standing up. **Tamsin came down a fen road on her own and was not called, and asked a gate hand three questions about a chain.**
- Ilyan and Wenna Tace: she is the person in the volume who has the most use for him and the least use for his name, and she told him to read a sentence to a boy of nine rather than to be interesting about himself.
- Ilyan and Corva Silt: she is the mechanism and she is thirty-four and she cried about the water on the seventh day of an instrument she printed at the fourth bell, and she is the person who most needs somebody to be annoyed with her about it in a month.

- Oren Sedge's guild is unpaid, out of money, has given away its only safety equipment, and has carried a count on a propped deck for three weeks while the seat cures. Its six crews have not voted for any of it. **The props came out on the twentieth day and went on the same cart, and the pay line is at the ward rate, and the guild is still on the levy rate out of its own empty account.**
- Sel Harrow opened a field at a culvert, said twice that the court has no power over the gate, entered a burden schedule with a woman's own hours and no rate against it, entered Ilyan's *his left leg is not his* without asking for an explanation, and discharged a field in nine minutes because a field is not a place to keep sitting in.

- Tovin Dain is paying for stopped hours the district cannot route and was right in Chapter 57 about why a burden assigned to nobody is assigned to the person with the least standing, every time, without exception, in nineteen years.
- Meridith Vale has a question of his own that nobody has answered: whether a district may pay a person who has no address. It was put in writing and the sitting is on the twenty-ninth. **He is nineteen years in the post and he entered the reason his own office could not pay a wage on the back of a slip in his own hand, and this is the question that came back to him.**
- The ward seats are still empty and the bench still says *WARD — VACANT*. The ward still has no address. **The ward is now, for the first time, the thing a city has formally asked a court about.**
- Nadia remains an independent Earth witness. Every exchange must carry a cost and there is no rescue shortcut. **The blackglass token and the afterimage are the same kind of operation and the Earth end of that is the Shale Mirror question, which has not been touched and must not be resolved here.**
