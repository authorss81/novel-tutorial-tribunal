## 2. Running in that city, untouched for forty-eight days, and he does not know

He left on the sixth day of the eighty-sixth week and has not been back. The office of Regent of the Shelf is not held and the second seat is vacant; the four hurdles, nine links, plate and box are still up for a reason nobody asked for; **Hessa Marl's book of the visits has no office to keep it in and she has said out loud that she does not know what happens to a book when there is no office;** Fenna Rusk's roll of eleven pages is under a stone behind a chandler's shop and she has not been able to say whether it is right once in forty-eight days; **the declaration of continuity governs about a hundred and forty people who are dead**; twenty-two wards, nine silent, and no line anywhere for a ward to say no in; **a form with his name at the foot of the fifth page and a woman's name under it, with *beside* struck through and *under* written over it**; Renn Calloway, the bar, and a south side with no name anywhere; Tarin Keel walking north; Kerwin Dace's stamped return; Sef, sixteen; Bess Low, who has never once been counted. **And a sheet with a head on it is in a book at a counter with a clerk of about thirty and a bad cough, and she shut the book, and nothing in that city has moved, and he does not know and cannot be sent for.** That city is forty-one miles off and the counter at Ostrey is eleven.

**AND WHAT THE VOLUME CLOSE ADDED TO THIS LIST, WHICH IS THREE THINGS IT EXAMINED AND DELIBERATELY LEFT ALONE, so that a later band does not mistake an examined question for an oversight.** **(1) The four towns of the compact are carried at THREE distances — *four miles and eleven and thirty-two* — in `498`'s title, in `498`'s body and in `state/continuity.md`.** It is carried identically in three places, no table controls it, and inventing a fourth in a close would be the same error this file has logged ten times. **A Volume 11 planner who needs four towns and four distances may set the fourth; a planner who needs three may drop a town. What a planner may not do is print the phrase again and leave it.** **(2) *Six weeks* is printed about twenty times in this band's narration for a stay of thirty-nine to forty-eight days.** It is the volume's recorded convention (hazard 8), it means the run of the matter and not elapsed days, it is used with the same force throughout 451–500, and the chapter that argues for it in dialogue is `476`. **A volume close is not the place to tighten a convention the whole volume stands on, and the distinction that matters is between a convention that has been examined and a figure that has not: this one has been examined, and the record of the examination is the reason it is safe to keep.** **(3) The gates have been standing open since the sixth hour of the fourteenth day and were *opened five days early* — a phrase that first appears at `481:35` and is repeated at `494:11`.** It is used twice and consistently and nothing in the volume states what the routine date was, so there is nothing to check it against and nothing to repair. **It is recorded here as an unexaminable figure rather than a verified one, which is a third category this file has not had before.** **(4) AND THE FOURTH, WHICH IS THE SAME SWEEP AS FINDING EIGHTEEN AND THE OPPOSITE VERDICT: after the eight crowd uses of *four hundred* were cut, the sweep returns four bare uses and all four are counts of a unit, not of a place, a date, a distance, a sum or a population — *a man of sixty-one has been asked a question in a public square **four hundred times** in forty years* (`466:117`), *asked for his view in a square **four hundred times** in nineteen days* (`471:41`), *he had said **four hundred times** in twenty-six days* (`478:109`), and *in about **four hundred** lines* (`489:104`). They are consistent with one another, they assert nothing that can be put next to the roll, the calendar or the fund, and they are the same rhetorical round number doing the same work in three bands. EXAMINED AND KEPT, and this is the second time this file has had to record a figure that is deliberately left in place; the test that separates the two classes is whether the figure can be contradicted by anything, and a count of occasions cannot be.**

## 5. What 491–500 closed, and what Volume 11 inherits

**CLOSED IN THIS BAND, and by closed it means that a person did a thing and it is in a document and the document binds nobody:** an instrument and a decision were named as two different things, out loud, by a person with no standing; a list of works nine years old was read all the way through for the first time and a second winding bar was found on it at the tail; the roll for the autumn was made and stands at 1,074 and the whole of the argument of this volume is 44 pence a quarter on it; the north heel went and a board entered the fact with a day on it and called it nothing; the House of this town was called for the first time in nine years on one question and the answer was that it can find out what a thousand people think; a request for a charter was drawn up with a head and two lines and an objection to the fourth of the six was entered under the head; an exit was written with a handle on the inside; a compact of six lines was made with four towns that are not upon a road of the Shelf and the sixth line is the exit and every burden in it was named by a person who was in the room; a sentence a man said in a market square was struck out of a sheet in front of nine people with the objection entered at the foot with his name against it; a request went four hundred miles with a witness line standing open; and a page came back with two names in a witness line.
