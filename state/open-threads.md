## 5. What 601–610 did not do

**The right of refusal was not restored and was given to nobody in any county, and a woman of seventy-one said out loud in about nine people's hearing that a person who cannot be asked is not a refusal, and the band refused to make a category out of the coincidence.** A settlement of nine hundred words in six hands with nothing at the top of it still binds nobody and has now been used once by a stranger who read it. The empty square, Hester Nunn's parcel with no name, the woman's unnamed hand, the boards nobody put in, the three hundred words under the oak bar and the mark of two letters and a number at the bottom of the back of the settlement are none of them that, and none of them was advanced. The midpoint of Volume 12 was not re-spent and no chapter of 601–610 puts six acres back in season or stages a second unsayable choice. A woman of twenty-nine was not asked whether she wanted a chair and said so out loud, and the volume's question about asking is one the man of thirty-one had not noticed he was still failing. Nobody was relieved, forgiven, redeemed or thanked, and `thanked` is 26 across the ten chapters and all twenty-six are negations. No villain. No new final enemy. One System panel in ten chapters, at `609`, and it is a record of a citation and not a choice and it is four sentences. The name spoken once at `600` is in a town nine miles down the water and was not addressed and was not answered. Sera Quill does not appear. The page that came back four hundred miles is not mentioned. `citizen` is at 0 and the question it stands for is not answered, and it was not answered in this band either.

**AND THE THREE THINGS THE BAND DID THAT NO CARD ASKED FOR, WHICH ARE NOW FIXED BY THE CHAPTERS.** A woman of seventy-one refused to put her no on a dead man's line, and in saying so described a form that does not exist and would be made in about four years.
