# Open Threads

## Immediate threads after Batch 0001

1. **The provisional stay and review:** The Ro household can use the ninth bridge for one review cycle, with review at the seventh morning bell after the Chapter 10 ruling. The stay expires unless the Bench issues a new ruling. The current charter is not amended.
2. **The missing maintenance condition:** Oren’s saltglass workroom plate contains a review route absent from the active public charter. Harrow’s physical replacement of the working plate preserves the original in a locker but does not restore the term. Who removed it, and why?
3. **The copied precedent:** The eastern district has mounted its petition on a public blackglass backing and uses the old language to make an unlisted household’s refusal a shared danger. The petition is a precedent witness, not the Ro household’s consent.
4. **The Quiet Chamber gap:** A surviving public fragment says, “the governed may answer no”; the current Bench says, “the governed may petition for correction.” The original page’s binding sleeve is empty and its removal is recorded before Ilyan arrived. Sera’s private duplicate was present before the first night field and disappeared during or after it. The two dates, the remover, and the chain of custody remain unresolved.
5. **The privacy breach:** Neris’s flood account and the names in it were preserved after Ilyan filed them without permission. The copy is disputed and not renewable by him; the household may offer narrower facts. The harm to the unnamed people in the account still needs acknowledgment.
6. **The First Defendant status:** Ilyan’s tester signature and private sequence are public evidence in an authorship claim. The status is not proof of guilt. The right-of-refusal language and the bridge record may be connected.
7. **The continuity voice:** The phrase “Continuity preserves the record before remedy” intrudes beneath formal translation. Ilyan cannot identify whether it is an older Bench layer, an archive intelligence, or another institution.
8. **The Shale Mirror and Earth:** The fragment beneath Morrowlight’s room translated the report; a separate null passage moved Ilyan. Nadia’s independent records and the reason the game carried private wording remain unresolved.
9. **The nine anchors:** Their locations, functions, and custodians are not yet known. No final enemy has been introduced beyond the seeded First Witness.
