# Open Threads

## Immediate threads after Batch 0005 — Volume 01 is complete

1. **The public maintenance review.** The fourth morning bell of the third month after the ruling, at the ninth bridge, on public notice, callable early by any party on a named reading outside the band the keeper has kept, and not postponable. Ilyan must be produced at it to answer for the cost of his own record. The west seat is bedded and has eleven days of Puck's three weeks of cure to run before it, and nine days before the props come out; the fixes are bought, staffed, and dated.
2. **The appeal.** Any person affected may challenge the ruling before the review date in a public sitting, on notice to every named party. **The appeal does not suspend access, housing, or work, and that is written into the charter.** Nobody has appealed yet. The district's objection to the refusal of *break the span* is expressly preserved and invited to appeal, and Harrow's own distinction between a reopening and a scheduling is preserved for appeal against his own ruling. **The appeal is unused and is the first thing Volume 02 can put weight on.**
3. **The Halloway print.** Two days old at the moment it arrived, same words, same order, different column, and *lawful traveler* meaning *proper* at a low-water culvert in a poor district with a sanitation complaint in front of its own bench. Kett is going to the room under protest. Nobody can stop it. **This is the Volume 02 reversal and it is already in motion.** A settlement that fixes a bridge will be read as a rule about a city, and the phrase that does the damage is in the ninth bridge's own access condition, where it has meant *registered* for two hundred years.
4. **The template bites, and the record keeps count.** Ilyan's name is on a citable precedent and cannot be un-entered, and it is the fourth thing in this case he has put into a public Veyran record that he cannot take back. The first two were his Earth reproduction sequence. The third was the precedent itself. The fourth is whatever he does next.
5. **The keeper's pause is in the charter and has never been used.** The first time a keeper rings that bell for a real reading, somebody will be standing on a bridge at four in the morning, and a carter will quote a sentence. The clause is bounded by the loads standing and the span is released if the deck does not clear, and the ward rate is payable at the moment of the work — and nobody has yet proved any of it outside a hearing field.
6. **The under-deck watch is two hands and a wage.** It is the cheapest item in the ruling and the one that finds things. It runs on a district day-book line out of road funds and it is the first line in this city that has ever been paid on this bridge. It will be the first line cut.
7. **The maintenance-review condition is restored and its original wording is not recovered.** The ruling says so expressly and does not supply it. The sealed plate in the court locker is a load record, not a keeper's plate; its edge carries **S. Kest**; it and the older witness index are one document cut once. **That pairing is a result and is not to be restaged.** The wording may yet be recovered from somewhere the ruling did not reach.
8. **The fifth mark in the guild's count book.** Still unexplained. A cut margin mark for a load that walked and did not come back, appearing inside the Chapter 39 hearing field with forty people watching, in a book with three hands in it. Tarin held it under a signed court entry. Nobody knows who cut it, no district objected, nothing was named, and Tarin would testify under oath that he does not know and would not enjoy it.
9. **The standing preservation authority and its wage.** Print run 114, eleven items in one week, all routed C—2, two named in class, neither carrying a name, a desk with a stamp and no clerk and no incoming index after the rerouting date. **Ilyan did not chase it.** Vale's warning stands: go on after the office and the only thing that changes is that records stop coming, to everybody. The question is the wage, not the sin.
10. **The two items in the preservation index, still kept apart.** The class, the week, and the routing of the sealed continuity request are known and are in no filed document, because the second one is Sera's family. She declined to ask what is in it and the print-shop clerk entered the refusal. It stays untouched unless something forces it.
11. **The Quiet Chamber.** The original page is still missing, removed before Ilyan crossed. The one surviving line on the facing leaf says a refusal is entered against a burden and not against a person, and the entry is open to the ward. **That is one line of a removed page and it is not the refusal clause.** It explains why a ward register cannot be repaired by adding a column and why the sworn column is a different instrument from the sworn tenancy book. The historical right of refusal is unresolved. Sera's duplicate is still missing, and it is still a second event on a second date.
12. **The relief's own durability.** The protection is written not to be conditional on the ninth-bridge record, and a reopening is exactly the instrument that suspends every relief entered under a record. The settlement has answered the question in the abstract. It has not been tested.
13. **The draft eleven clause at the fen crossing.** Still unadopted, with the witness question open and a code with no office in a district column. The ninth bridge has now adopted something that four districts may copy, which makes Prenn's clause both easier and harder to argue for, and two districts still cannot produce their own maintenance terms for the same reason.
14. **Two measurements, still separate.** The pale line the joint has taken and kept, and the fingers one load borrows and gives back. **And now a third, new, and different number: a standing load, which came back most of the way and held at one.** Any later scene that reports drift must keep all three apart and must say which kind of load produced the figure.
15. **The fourth bridge and Wist Anhalt.** He gave the ninth bridge his four props and objected in four words, and he is owed an answer that the ruling gives: the props are replaced or paid for out of the district's road funds before the review, and the ninth bridge's settlement may not spend the fourth bridge's safety in a document that does not name it. He sent two hands unasked to help pack the seat. Nobody has asked him whether he wanted to.

## Long mystery threads

- Who removed the right of refusal from the original Compact, and what did the Warden's Codification edit?
- Is the First Witness merely a custodian, or does it deliberately maintain a singular version of continuity? The continuity line has now spoken five times and the word has changed once, from *stated* to *entered*.
- Was the Earth game a reflection, a bridge, or a partial authorship attempt?
- How can Ilyan belong to Veyra without being absorbed into its preferred story?
- What does a responsible right of refusal look like when the cost falls on neighbours? A sworn column in one ward is an answer for one ward, and it took twenty days, a stolen page, and a landlord's twelve years of silence to produce.
- Why does a good local maintenance settlement become a district-wide weapon when its terms are copied, and what is the difference between a precedent and a template that Sera named in a corridor and Ilyan did not yet understand?
- What is the relationship between Sava Kest, the C—2 routing code, the bridge plate, and Ilyan's pre-arrival report?
- Can a chain of lawful acts be interrupted at a named step, and who has the standing to name the step?
- What is a standing authority for the preservation of records in a period of continuity preserving, when nobody can name the office or the wage behind it?
- What wrote a mark in a guild's count book inside a field, and what does the Bench's own translation do with a line nobody entered?

## Relationship and political promises

- Ilyan and Sera are closer and further apart than at any point in the volume. She is licensed, she is not his advocate, she will correct him in front of a copy, and she has said that if it becomes affection it will be a separate entry and it will be dated. **No romance beat has occurred in Volume 01 and none is planned in Volume 02's first movement.** Trust remains conditional, specific, and revocable, and the revocability is now written into a license.
- Ilyan and Tarin: Tarin said nothing in the field this batch, held the boundary, let Tamsin inside it without an order, and would testify under oath that he does not know who cut the mark. He has not yet been asked to testify at anything.
- Ilyan and the Ro household: the household is protected by a charter and the protection is not bought from them. Neris has made her own name public three times and knows what it cost. Tamsin spoke because she chose to, at the span, with the rope, and has not been thanked by anybody except Harrow.
- Oren Sedge's guild has signed in a keeper's name and is unpaid and out of money and has given away its only safety equipment to a bridge it does not have standing on. Its six crews have not voted for any of it.
- Sel Harrow invented a rule of procedure twice in public, said so both times, preserved every objection against his own rulings, and refused for the third time to rule on a line in his own translation. He is the court's witness in any appeal, and the appeal is unused.
- Maren Kett argued for one legible rule, was right, and has been proved right about its cost by a print he did not see coming. He is going to stand in a poor district's room under protest, with his written instruction produced and read.
- Tovin Dain argued for breaking the span, won the refusal of it by supplying the condition that removed the ground from under his own argument, and is now paying for stopped hours the district cannot route. He has been right more often than not and has still lost the money.
- Meridith Vale paid the pump's shortage, entered the reason his office cannot pay a wage on the back of a slip in his own hand, and said out loud in front of witnesses that the man who built the register gate is the man who cannot pay the toll. He is not a villain and has never been one.
- The ward seats are still empty and the bench still says *WARD — VACANT*. The ward still has no address. Hanne Kolm's column records refusals and burdens and no addresses at all, and the ward is, for the first time, the holder of a sworn instrument.
- Nadia remains an independent Earth witness. Every exchange must carry a cost and there is no rescue shortcut.
- The final hearing must resolve the global constitutional conflict without unanimity, a new enemy, or a reset.
