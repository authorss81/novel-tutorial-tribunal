# Open Threads

## Immediate opening threads

1. **The bridge case:** Ilyan must keep Tamsin Ro alive and open a hearing before the ninth bridge’s charter chooses who may cross. The immediate rescue does not settle Neris Ro’s access to housing or work.
2. **The First Defendant status:** The opening case record attaches Ilyan’s tester signature to an authorship claim and reproduces his private Earth sequence. “First Defendant” is a legal status, not proof of guilt.
3. **The null passage:** Why could the Bench recognize Ilyan’s Earth defect report as a challenge without obtaining informed consent, why was the legacy emergency clause preserved, and why did it consume the ninth bridge’s reserve?
4. **The Quiet Chamber gap:** Sera finds that the official record of withdrawal and refusal is incomplete before Ilyan arrived; a surviving page says “the governed may answer no.”
5. **The Shale Mirror:** Why does a Veyran record contain language from Ilyan’s private Earth test report, and what did Morrowlight know about the shard’s anomaly?
6. **The missing anchors:** Nine original anchors support the Loom; their locations and custodians are not yet known.
7. **The continuity voice:** A cadence inside the opening System translation preserves the tester record “before remedy.” Ilyan cannot yet identify it or separate it from ordinary Bench continuity.

## Long mystery threads

- Who removed the right of refusal from the original Compact, and what did the Codification edit?
- Is the First Witness merely a custodian, or does it deliberately maintain a singular version of continuity?
- Was the Earth game a reflection, a bridge, or a partial authorship attempt?
- How can Ilyan belong to Veyra without being absorbed into its preferred story?
- What does a responsible right of refusal look like when the cost falls on neighbors?

## Relationship and political promises

- Ilyan and Sera begin as adversarial collaborators and must not become a reward-based romance. Their first serious break comes when Ilyan uses Neris’s private statement without permission; Sera suspends representation until a concrete correction and changed boundary are in place, and conditional cooperation does not erase the breach.
- Tamsin and Neris retain independent agency. Their immediate safety does not automatically settle their legal status, housing, work, or consent to public testimony.
- Nadia remains an independent Earth witness; every exchange has a cost and no rescue shortcut.
- Sable Venn’s Null Cartographers offer a genuine alternative to the Bench without becoming automatically correct.
- Ansa Rell, Ors Halm, and Iven Marrow embody institutional pressures that remain morally legible after defeat.
- The final hearing must resolve the global constitutional conflict without unanimity, a new enemy, or a reset.
