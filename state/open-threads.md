# Open Threads

## Immediate threads after Batch 0002

1. **The ninth-bridge stay and review:** The Ro household may use the ninth bridge for one review cycle, with review at the seventh morning bell after the Chapter 10 ruling. The current charter is not amended; enforceable maintenance review, appeal, exit, and durable housing/work protection remain unresolved.
2. **The Saltwheel Road arrangement:** The Cinder Ward’s local arrangement names a route, work, hours, warning line, water route, public review, and exit condition. It was renewed for one additional marked interval by a fresh choice, not automatically. The copied petition remains a dangerous precedent witness, and the district may seek a new order after the review.
3. **The missing maintenance condition:** Oren’s sealed saltglass workroom plate contains the maintenance-review route absent from the current public charter. The local road’s broken drain and altered load behavior show why omission matters physically. Who removed the bridge term remains unknown.
4. **The copied precedent chain:** Eastmere’s petition and Merefen’s Saltwheel extract show that public wording can be lawfully reused without the original parties’ consent. Authorship, adoption, enforcement, and consent remain separate questions.
5. **The Quiet Chamber losses:** The original page’s empty sleeve is in archive custody and records removal before Ilyan arrived. Sera’s private duplicate disappeared during or after the first night field. The dates, remover, and chain remain unresolved.
6. **The C—2 / Sava Kest route:** A formal continuity request has a valid office seal and ambiguous C—2 code. An older witness index names Sava Kest while the active list and levy ledger remove the name, and the old bridge plate bears the same name in a second location. A second paper carrying Ilyan’s private Earth sequence uses the same code and bridge-plate route. The Chapter 17 date comparison showed the local petition was built from the public wording **before** the Quiet Chamber margin was rewritten, so the copier and the person who tidied the record afterward are different people. The person, office, or process behind the routing is unknown.
7. **The privacy breach:** Neris’s flood account and the names inside it remain preserved after Ilyan filed them without permission. The court copy is disputed and is not evidence unless renewed; the names were not consenting witnesses. The household’s privacy harm still needs acknowledgment.
8. **The pre-arrival private sequence:** The second paper’s receipt predates the bridge notice and Ilyan’s arrival. How his Earth wording entered Veyran law, and who or what routed it, remains the immediate mystery.
9. **The continuity voice:** “Continuity preserves the record before remedy” and its clipped variants recur in formal records. The source remains unidentified; it is not yet a named First Witness reveal.
10. **The First Defendant status:** Ilyan’s tester signature and public Earth sequence remain attached to the bridge and copied-road records. The status is not proof of guilt, and his name now travels with public burdens.
11. **The refused hold:** The Bench preserved the levy ledger and refused to hold local enforcement. The district knows the distinction is on the record and collects anyway. A later filing must not quietly assume this one succeeded.
12. **Acknowledgment inferred from continued use:** The common register infers consent from a person’s continued use of a road, so the ward can be made to sign by being left no alternative. This survives the pause and the local arrangement, and it is the shape the next batch’s institutional pressure should push on.
13. **The nine anchors:** Their locations, functions, and custodians remain unknown. No new final enemy has been introduced.
