## 3. Held, still not opened, and none of it was ever this volume's question

**About nine hundred refusals on a shelf eleven inches wide eleven inches from where a woman of twenty-eight sits, which she has not opened and has written down eleven times that she is not going to, and a second letter has gone out of a market town in her hand with nothing on the outside of it, and nothing has been said about whether the range was opened. The four letters and a stroke are not read out in either volume. **THE PAGE THAT CAME BACK FOUR HUNDRED MILES IS STILL IN THE INSIDE POCKET OF HIS COAT AND IS NOT MENTIONED IN ANY CHAPTER OF 551–570.** What did the First Witness sign, and which people are still governed by that signature: shut, and `First Witness` is at 0 across 551–600 until `600`. A man of thirty-three gave up a season, a woman of forty-one refused a stranger's number in a doorway, a woman of forty-one refused a stranger's request for her name, and a woman of twenty-eight was refused twice in the open in three days, and NONE OF THOSE IS THE RIGHT OF REFUSAL AND NOBODY IN EITHER COUNTY HAS BEEN GIVEN IT.
