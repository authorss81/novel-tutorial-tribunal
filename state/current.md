## Where Volume 02's first movement ends

A poor district four miles below the weighhouse has printed the ninth-bridge settlement, lawfully, two days after a ruling, and used **lawful traveler** — the charter's own two-hundred-year-old word for *registered* — to shut a low-water culvert. **The instrument is well made and it is unjust, and that is the finding.** A refuse hauler used the same sentence honestly and got eleven months of arrears out of a district bench, and the sentence beside it in the same charter is what traps her, because a barrow is a load. A gate hand of forty-four has thirty years at that gate, a page with her own hours on it and no rate against them, and four days of a four-hour wage that the district has now refused to renew on the ground that a district which pays a favour has learned to rule by habit. A sixty-one-year-old landlord has sworn a schedule four miles outside her own district because a refusal had nowhere else to go, and the court has entered that it neither accepts nor refuses it. **Neither of Volume 01's two new instruments reaches anybody four miles away: the appeal runs to a person affected by a ruling "on this span" and the exit is entered in a register kept in the ward. The first appeal in the record was filed by Ilyan, who is a party by the ruling, and it works perfectly.**

Ilyan is **Stage 2, Seam Reader**. He perceives the assembly of a rule inside a hearing field and the limit holds: he sees how a rule is put together and not why its authors wanted it, and a coherent rule can still be unjust. The cost is named and accumulating — a gate hand's leg from the hip, two layers of the low water in his nose in a lane with hay in it, and a chain on a gate in his right hand four hundred yards away — and it changed a decision before it changed his body. **The spent blackglass token is warm, and touching it takes the low water away for fifty minutes and brings it back with a third layer, and he has understood that the passage that brought him and the afterimage are one operation. He has not resolved it and has said he is not going to chase it.**
