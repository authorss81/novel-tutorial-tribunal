### 3. The money, with the working in the sentence

- **1,041 rated households** in the roll of Halloway for the year, unmoved in eleven years. **The figure of 1,060 is wrong and is entered as wrong in a market**: 19 tenements are not 19 households, the row holds about twenty-three people, and the true number is somewhere between **19 and 23** and is not knowable. `454`
- **Eighty lots of five acres, £2 a lot = £160.** **The Four Hundred was divided and sold in the week of `hall = 0`.** The Fen floods, was not divided, and lies.
- **The water rate is twopence a quarter on 1,041 rated households: 1,041 × 8d = 8,328d = £34 14s a year, and that is the whole fund.** **A sluice keeper at £6 a year is £6 of £34 14s.**
- **The scouring costs £14, found by a levy of 3½d a lot on the eighty lots: 14 pounds = 280d; 280 ÷ 80 = 3½d.** **£14 of £34 14s is two fifths.**

### 4. What the band paid, and what it cost

1. **He told a room of about thirty people in that city that he wanted a town where his name is not on anything, and that it was the sixth want whose price he had said out loud, and the price was that he is not going to get it.** `451`
2. **He gave away in four seconds, in a room, the one fact that would have let Wenna Crale leave — that Halloway is not upon a road of the Shelf — and she said he should not have said it in a room, and the cost was eleven days he could have had.** `451`
3. **He read a notice out in a market square to forty-one people because a man he had met ninety seconds earlier asked him, and afterwards said out loud that reading a notice is what makes a man somebody's man in a town.** `452`
4. **He put a wrong figure into a market on a Tuesday and had to take it apart in the same square on the same day, and the correct figure is that he does not know it.** `454`
5. **He wrote out, for nineteen houses, a sentence about what they are owed, that they had not asked for, and a woman of thirty-eight read his own words back to him and struck the fourth paragraph out in her own hand and handed him the paper.** `455`
6. **The band's one panel, at `456:73`–`456:79`, four fields, one decision, nothing chosen, nobody told. The person who answers it is not him: Wenna Crale turns the board over before he can.**
7. **He said out loud, in a room of sixteen, the date the right of refusal was taken out of the law, and a woman asked him how he knows, and there is not one thing in Halloway that backs him.** `456`
8. **He offered to carry the objection of nineteen houses and was told, in a lane, in front of about eleven people, what a witness is, in the words he had said himself in another city a week before, and he said the breaking of it out loud in the lane.** `457`
9. **A miller of thirty-nine refused credit to a woman of thirty-four in a shop with nine people in it, correctly, in front of a stranger who had offered to pay it, and named the reason: he does not know her.** `458`
10. **He was right, twice, loudly, and the rightness did the damage. A copyist refused his number for the best reason anybody had heard in nine days; a clause four is alive in a fair copy; and the word in it is *household*, and that is the word that shuts the door.** `458`, `459`
11. **The House of Halloway declines to enter the fenrow on the roll, in the fenrow's own name, in front of about two hundred people, in a minute that is correct and self-contradictory in the same sentence — and Ilyan says he has no standing and says nothing, and asks for the minute to say so.** `460`
