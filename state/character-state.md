**THE BAND'S FINDING, AND IT IS THE WHOLE OF THE BAND'S NEW INFORMATION ABOUT ILYAN: HE IS ENTERED IN THE BOOK OF ORDERS AT ALDWICK IN A CLASS BY HIMSELF, WITH A NAME OVER THE CLASS, AND THE NAME IS AN EXTERNAL WITNESS. A man of fifty-four of the county made the entry on the Wednesday of the second day of the hundredth week, nine miles off, on the second line of a form whose first line is a person of the county, and the second line has a head over it that has been there longer than he has been in that office and that neither of them knows who wrote. A person in that office may be asked about him, and no person in this county can ask him anything, and there is no form in the county of Aldwick that says what a man has to do to stop being one. **HE SAID OUT LOUD, IN THE PORCH, THAT HE DOES NOT KNOW, AND THAT HE HAS LOOKED AND THAT HE HAS BEEN ELEVEN YEARS IN AN OFFICE THAT KEEPS TWO BOOKS AND CAN MAKE A RETURN AND NOTHING ELSE. `541`** AND THE KEEPER OF THE BOOK SAID THE SENTENCE THE WHOLE BAND IS BUILT ON, IN A PORCH, IN FRONT OF ABOUT NINE PEOPLE: **A HEADING IS A PROMISE ABOUT WHAT GOES IN IT.** Four words in a margin of a book in this building, a head over them, no order named and no day, and that has been kept for a hundred and forty-one years by nobody meaning to keep it. `541`, `550`

**THE PROHIBITION ON ASKING ANYBODY IN THE COUNTY OF ALDWICK ABOUT THE ORIGINAL WAS ASKED ABOUT AND BROKEN, EXACTLY ONCE, IN A ROOM WITH THE DOOR OPEN, IN FRONT OF ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, AND THE ASKING HAPPENED FIRST AND WAS A QUESTION AND NOT A NOTICE.** She had written it twenty-four days earlier, on the Thursday of the ninety-sixth week, and had said out loud in a lane at `534` that it might be broken only in a room with the door open with about nine people in it and only if one of the nine asked her first, and that she had said in front of nine people that the answer would be no. **THE REASON SHE SAID YES WAS NOT THAT ANYBODY HAD CONVINCED HER OF ANYTHING AND SHE SAID SO OUT LOUD FIRST: it is that the entry says nobody IN THE COUNTY OF ALDWICK may be asked, and not that nobody may be asked, and the person who asked was not of this county, so there was never anything in it standing between him and the question, and a rule a person can break because they came from somewhere else is a rule about a place and not about a person. `543`** She did not strike it out, because a strike is a thing this county has done once in a hundred and forty-one years with a heavier ink and no order named. **AND SHE SAID OUT LOUD, IN THE ROOM, THAT THE WHOLE OF THE PROTECTION HAD BEEN A QUESTION AND NOT A PERSON, because nobody in this county has the original and nobody in this county has seen it.**

**ABEL TARN, SIXTY-TWO, A WEAVER, IS THE PERSON DESCENDED FROM THE FIRST NAME ON THAT LEAF AND HE SAID THAT NAME OUT LOUD IN A ROOM FOR THE FIRST TIME IN HIS COUNTY'S KNOWLEDGE, AND SHE WROTE IT DOWN, AND HE WAS TOLD BEFORE SHE WROTE IT.** He said the leaf of descent is ruled in the same four columns and filled from the eldest at the top, so the first name on it is the eldest of that household, and it is his grandfather's, and his father told him it was struck before he was born and told him it was struck on a Tuesday, and that is the whole of what his house has ever had to say about it. **AND WHEN A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE ASKED FOR THE LEAF TO BE STRUCK BACK IN THAT ROOM IN FRONT OF ABOUT NINE PEOPLE, HE REFUSED HIM IN ABOUT FOUR SENTENCES, AND THE FOURTH OF THEM WAS THAT HE IS THE LAST OF A NAME AND HE HAS BEEN THE LAST OF A NAME FOR NINE YEARS AND HE CAN STAND IT ANOTHER NINE. `548`** HE IS RIGHT AND HE IS NOT A VILLAIN.
