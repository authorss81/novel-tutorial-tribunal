## 1. What 551–560 changed, and it is a new county and a new substance and the same man

### 1a. What 551–560 changed

**THE BAND'S FINDING, AND IT IS THE EXACT INVERSE OF VOLUME 11'S, AND IT WAS SAID OUT LOUD BY THE PERSON IT HAPPENED TO. In Bramblefold the record was in a locked cupboard and nine people could be sent for about it, and the whole of Volume 11 was a man learning what that costs. In Withermere the record is a sheet of parchment about two men long on the north wall of a weigh-house, in the open, read by about nine people every morning of their lives for about two hundred and thirty years, and it has three columns — the ground, the taking, and the year — and no column with a person's name at the top of it, and it never has had one. A water-clerk of fifty-eight said the whole of it in about four sentences: a man nobody may ask is not a free man, and a man everybody may ask is not a free man either, because then the asking is the work and the man is only the thing being worked on. `554`, `559`**

**ILYAN VESTER, THIRTY-ONE, NAMED IN ALL TEN, WITH A WANT, A MISTAKE AND A COST OF HIS OWN IN ALL TEN, INCLUDING THE CHAPTERS WHERE HE IS RIGHT AND GETS NOTHING.**

- **Wants a letter entered at the back of a book four hundred and eleven years old so that a person could be sent for about it, and is refused in about four seconds, and the reason is the best one anybody has given him in a hundred and fifty-one days: in a hundred years somebody will want to know what the woman was owed and there will be no way on this earth to ask her. `551`**
- **Wants to know what a carrier's fare is and says the number out loud, and wants to work his passage, and is refused in about four seconds on the ground that a man in his crew in the water is a man he has to answer for. `552`**
- **Wants to be useful on a boat and counts the loads of a stone out loud, and is told, in front of about four people, by a boy of nineteen, that the number is only so that the other man knows it was heard. `553`**
- **Wants to read a wall and stands in the way of a cart of wool and turns it twice, and a carter tells him he is no use apologising to, because a man who cannot be sent for is a thing a person would have to carry for a year. `554`**
- **Wants to keep a stranger's six acres in a family, offers twelve shillings and sixpence out of his own purse, and is refused in four sentences. `555`**
- **Wants to see a boundary and puts his hand on a gatepost that comes out of the ground. `556`**
- **Wants a record to be an instrument, calls it a remedy out loud in a building with the door open, is told before it goes up that it will change nothing, and then watches a man of forty-four come nine miles to correct one line of it in public. `557`**
- **Counts a camp out loud and is corrected, and the correction is worse than his number. `558`**
- **Offers to be the one anybody can be sent for and is refused in four seconds, and then offers, for the first time in a hundred and fifty-nine days, to be in a room, and is accepted in four seconds. `559`**
- **Is given a choice about four doors and takes the four doors in about nine seconds, and nobody thanked him for it, and there is no form in the county of Kell that says a man may be thanked for anything. `560`**

**HIS MISTAKES ARE ONE HABIT AND IT IS THE OLD ONE — HE MAKES HIMSELF THE CARRIER — AND IT IS BROKEN ONCE, IN THE SAME CHAPTER THAT IT IS REFUSED TWICE, AND THAT IS THE FIRST TIME IN TWELVE VOLUMES. `559`**

**HIS COSTS IN THIS BAND, and every one of them is a thing that stays:** a bed at the top of a joiner's shop, paid to the end of the week, given back on the last day of the week. **One pound and seventeen pence, which is sixty-five days and two thirds of a day, and a woman who keeps an inn of four beds corrected the division for him.** A leg that stopped working on a bank at the fifth hour of the afternoon on the first day of the hundred and second week, and a boot with no sole on it. **A hand on a wall in a town where a man who has lived there for six years cannot take it back.** A number about a camp that put a man of about seventy and a boy of nine on the wrong side of it, and neither of them can be asked about it. **FOUR DOORS HE HAS PROMISED TO KNOCK ON TONIGHT.** `551`, `553`, `557`, `558`, `559`, `560`

**AND THE STAGE DID NOT ADVANCE. HE IS CO-WITNESS AT `501` AND HE IS CO-WITNESS AT `560`.** What changed is a turn inside the stage and it is the first in twelve volumes: he stopped offering to be the instrument and offered to be in a room. The panel at `560` gave him a choice between four of the eleven doors and all eleven, and he took the four closes, and the two of them agreed that if they do not both go then neither of them goes, and **the cost the volume is going to price is not paid here and is NOT to be paid before Band 0005: a year of his future options and a permanent change in how he experiences time.**

**SERA QUILL, TWENTY-EIGHT, FOUR HUNDRED MILES OFF, DOES NOT APPEAR. HER HAND ARRIVES ON A SHEET AT `551`, FORWARDED BY HER, IN FOUR LINES, AND SHE SAYS IN WRITING THAT SHE IS NOT COMING AND THAT SHE IS NOT ASKING HIM FOR ANYTHING AND THAT IF HE GOES SHE WANTS IT WRITTEN DOWN SOMEWHERE THAT SHE DID NOT ASK. `citizen` is at 0 across 551–560. She is the only person in this manuscript who has refused to use intimacy as leverage, and she did it in a letter, at a distance of four hundred miles, in four lines, and it is the reason he is in a fen town and not a bed at the top of a joiner's shop. She is not resolved in this volume and her refusal is not withdrawn. `551`**

**IVEN ASHCOMBE, FIFTY-EIGHT, THE WATER-CLERK OF WITHERMERE AND OF THE HUNDRED, PAID FOUR POUNDS A YEAR OUT OF A RATE OF TWO SHILLINGS AND A PENNY THE ACRE, WITH HIS NAME ON A BOARD OUTSIDE HIS OWN DOOR AND A PROMISE ON IT THAT ANY PERSON IN THE HUNDRED MAY ASK HIM ANYTHING AND HE WILL ANSWER IT, AND NO QUALIFICATION ON THE BOARD AND NONE UNDERNEATH.** He kept that promise on a Friday in a market in front of about thirty people about the worst thing anybody has ever asked him, and could not have done otherwise, and said so out loud himself. **HE HAS NEVER BEEN ASKED A QUESTION ABOUT A PERSON IN NINETEEN YEARS AND HE SAID THAT OUT LOUD AT `554` AND IT IS THE TRUEST THING ANYBODY SAYS IN THE BAND.** He refuses to say a name in his own building and gives the reason that a keeper's name in a story is a thing a man's wife is told about at a pump. He says the whole of a spring in about nine seconds when asked and then goes out to cut a ditch. He stands at the edge of a camp for eleven minutes and does not cross onto it. He says he can hold the water either way and not both ways, and that a decision about the level of a fen is not taken by the people who happen to be in a room. He accepts a stranger's offer to be in the room in about four seconds and says he has never been able to use anything like him. **HE IS RIGHT ABOUT MOST OF IT AND HE IS NOT COMFORTABLE AND HE IS NOT A VILLAIN AND HE HAS A WIFE WHO WILL BE TOLD ABOUT A STORY AT A PUMP.** `552`, `554`, `555`, `557`, `558`, `559`, `560`

**OWAIN BRAY, FIFTY-FOUR, A GRAZIER OF A FARM FOUR MILES SOUTH, WITH ABOUT SEVEN HUNDRED SHEEP. HE IS THE OPPONENT OF THIS BAND AND HE IS A RULE WITH A MAN IN IT.** He took up eleven seasons on two hundred and ten acres in one week in April and paid the rate and the penny on every one of them in four days and did not go in at night and did not lie to one of the eleven. **HE IS RIGHT ABOUT THE COVENANT AND HE PROVED IT IN ABOUT NINE SENTENCES IN FRONT OF ABOUT THIRTY PEOPLE, AND THE PROOF IS THAT THE DITCHES HAD NOT BEEN CUT FOR TWO YEARS BECAUSE THE RATE HAD NOT BEEN PAID, AND THE LOW MEADOW WENT UNDER, AND HE HAD SEVEN HUNDRED SHEEP AND NOWHERE TO PUT A THIRD OF THEM. A COMMON THAT FAILS IS NOT STOLEN. IT IS MAINTAINED BY SOMEBODY.** And then he says out loud, before anybody asks him, that he is also wrong about one thing: the drain he cut in April on his own ground, which he could not see the end of, and which he agrees to take out at his own cost before the rising, and which he says will put the water onto a camp of fifty-eight people on a common that belongs to nobody. **HE LOSES A SEASON OF KEEP DOING IT. HE IS NOT A VILLAIN, IS NOT A CRIMINAL, IS NOT A LIAR, AND IS NOT GOING TO BE EITHER, AND THE BAND SAYS ALL FOUR OUT LOUD.** `554`, `560`

**HESTER NUNN, FORTY-ONE, SIX ACRES OF THE SECOND CLOSE OFF THE DYKE, TWELVE SHILLINGS AND SIXPENCE A YEAR, TWO CHILDREN, A MAN WHO IS AT SEA OR IS DEAD AND HAS NOT BEEN DECIDED.** She wrote a letter to an address four hundred miles off because she did not know who to write to. **She asked a plain question at a stone step in a market in front of about thirty people, and when she was told the true of it she said the worst of it out loud — that he is doing it properly — and then refused a stranger's twelve shillings and sixpence in four sentences, and the fourth was that a season bought with a stranger's money is still a season bought from her. SHE IS NOT A SYMBOL AND SHE IS NOT A VICTIM IN A SCENE; SHE IS THE QUICKEST PERSON IN EVERY ROOM SHE IS IN.** `551`, `555`

**ORWIN SLEE, FORTY-FOUR, A CARRIER OF LETTERS AND SMALL PARCELS ON THE WATER, FOUR BOATS AND A BOY OF NINETEEN, OFF THIS WATER FOR EIGHT MONTHS OF THE YEAR.** He names his fare out loud on stones. He refuses a stranger's labour in about four seconds for a reason he gives twice. He says a man who is off the water at Withermere is a man in a town of about eleven hundred who is told to his face that he is a stranger. He has carried a man down in the water-season who said the whole of it in one sentence on his own deck: *they will not ask me anything here.* **HE IS NOT A SERVANT AND IS NOT A FRIEND AND IS NOT AN INSTRUMENT, AND HE IS THE ONLY PERSON WHO GOT A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE OUT OF ONE COUNTY AND INTO ANOTHER, AND HE SAYS SO WITHOUT SAYING IT.** `552`, `553`

**REUBEN SEARLE, NINETEEN, ON THE POLE. SOMEBODY'S, AND NOT YOURS. HE TELLS A MAN WHAT THE HUNDRED CALLS A SEASON AND THAT IT IS ON NO PAPER AND HE HAS BEEN IN FRONT OF THE SHEET FOUR HUNDRED TIMES, AND HE NAMES A TALLY STONE UNASKED, AND HE TELLS A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE TO ASK HIM ABOUT THE CLERK INSTEAD. HE IS NOT A DEVICE AND HE IS NOT A MESSENGER AND HE IS THE ONLY PERSON IN THE BAND WHO GIVES ILYAN A SENTENCE HE DID NOT ASK FOR.** `553`, `557`

**BETT SEARLE, TWENTY-EIGHT, OF THE COME-OVERS, A DAUGHTER OF SIX ON HER HIP AND A BROTHER ON THE BOATS.** She keeps the count of fifty-eight people in eleven tents on about eight hundred acres of nobody's common and will not have it counted by a stranger. **She corrects a man of thirty-one from sixty to fifty-eight in front of about nine people and names the two that were subtracted.** She has one sheet of paper in a sack under a churn with a figure and a day on it and no name on it and no copy anywhere in the county, and she says the true of it in one sentence: there is no form that says how many people a licence covers, because the licence is the number, and the number is the season. **She walked four miles to hear a man of fifty-four say a thing about her common and said it was the first true thing anybody had said to her about it since the day she came off the high ground. `558`, `560`**

**HOARE RYLEY, FIFTY-ONE, A DYKER, WITH FOUR ACRES OF HIS OWN AND ELEVEN BETWEEN HIS TWO BROTHERS, ALL ON THAT WATER.** He puts a stranger off nine acres in about four seconds and says he is not to be sorry at all. He pulls him out of a ditch with a crowbar. He says the sentence the volume's engineering rests on: a rule that governs a parcel and not a water is a rule that can be kept while somebody else drowns. He gives the reason nine men have not said it out loud since March, and then says the reason had not meant to come out. He says the number out loud on a bank at the end of a day because that is what a man does, and nobody had asked him to. **HE IS RIGHT AND HE IS AFRAID, AND BOTH OF THOSE ARE ON THE PAGE. `556`, `559`**

**ILSA RUNDLE, ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT, WITH A BARROW AND A BOY OF ABOUT FOUR, AT A GATE ON A TOWPATH.
