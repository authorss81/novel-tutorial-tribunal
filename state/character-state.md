# Character State — end of Chapter 430

**This block supersedes every block below it. Nothing below the rule has been edited in this phase.** Chapters 1–430 are canon. **Nobody in this file has been relieved, forgiven, redeemed or thanked, including the office, which has read everything and answered it correctly.**

**A NOTE ON NAMES.** These people were carried for two bands as *a woman of twenty-eight*, *a man of fifty-two*, *the boy of sixteen* and *the clerk of forty*, and the batch-0003 repair gave them names, because a cast carried only as numbers is not a cast. The theme is untouched: **a name in a record is still a different thing from a name in a mouth**, and Sef gives his name to one person in one room and asks that it not be written down. The one repair to a fact: the ground-floor clerk is **twenty-nine** and always said so in his own mouth, so the age label *clerk of thirty* that appeared twice in the delivered band has been dropped in favour of the stated age.

## 1. The people this band moved

**ILYAN VESTER.** Mid-thirties. Not of this place; cannot be sent for; cannot be struck out, because the only form that takes a name out of a return is filled at the seat by a person of the place. Bad leg, bad ear, cracked steel pen, cold bitter tea, counts exits, asks what a rule considers a person. Twenty-nine days in this city. He put four people in books in that time, one of them by carrying a paper about her into a building himself, and he knows it and has not said it out loud. **He said the price of putting his own name on a line out loud, in his own mouth, to a person who had not asked, with a man who could act in the room, before he did it, and it did not stop him, and he could not name one person who could stop it, and neither of them said so.** He is a co-witness; it is a piece of work and not a rank; there is no form for it. `427:61`, `427:71`

**ILYAN'S THREE THINGS THIS BAND, WHICH ARE THE ENGINE.** He had a divisor worked out from three weeks of boards that would have filled the office's count form in ten seconds, and he did not say it, and the taste of it stayed with him all afternoon — a man can be made into an arithmetic by a stranger with a pen. `422:47` He had a sentence ready that would have cleared Sef out of the room in a minute, and it would have cost the boy nothing, and he did not say it. `428` He offered his own name to a third line of a return and was refused, and the refusal was correct, and both facts were the same fact. `429:85`

**FENNA RUSK.** Forty-four. Keeps a roll of doors, eleven years, four shillings a year, unpaid: 4,004 days, £4 4s. **She is now a thing in a column and not a person in a book, and a thing cannot be struck out.** She said yes to keeping a place and no to a number of persons in the same breath, in her own room, in about nine people's hearing, and the clerk wrote both on one line and the line was longer than the form. Her roll went up the hill in a coat and in oilcloth; he copied eleven pages fair and put the copy in the press and read the first page back to her, and she said *that is not my hand*. **What she lost is the only thing she was: she can no longer say whether a roll is right, and she said so in her own mouth, and she declined to put a figure on herself out loud, which is the only time in the band that a person refuses a figure.** She has a lamp on and a bare table. She has stopped saying that she will not be thanked. `424:139`, `426:113`, `426:117`, `426:103`

**HESSA MARL.** Twenty-eight. Nine doors a day, 441 doors in 49 days on a slate. She is the only person anybody can be asked about a door in this ward and **she cannot say no.** She refused the number of doors three times and then read her own figure out in a row in front of twenty people, and refused to let the roadkeeper write it down as a count of his road. She was entered as removed from this city, in the line for a person removed and not the line for a person applying, off a paper Ilyan carried in himself. **Her own no is in a book four hundred miles off. She is the only person in this city who is in both.** She went out past the ninth marker on foot at the fourth hour and came back up the same four yards of mud at the last of the light, and the finding is two words: *it works.* She is on the second line of a return as *a person conveyed to the ninth marker and returned, the same*, and the last word on that line is the office's and not hers. **She has not been paid anything and has not asked and says a person who has not asked has not asked.** She has told Ilyan he will not stand in the room, because he will be asked about it and she will be asked about him. `422:67`, `424:43`, `425:75`, `429:123`

**SEF.** Sixteen. At a door on the low row at that hour since the eighth morning of a fever. Carried a statement about a road from four doorways in the ordinary way, and a clerk filled a line off the paper, and now a form has been offered to him with two questions in one line. **He refused to choose between the answers and refused to name the man of the ward, and the reason he gave is the whole of him: if he says the name, then the fortnight was because of who he knows and not because of what he said.** He worked out that he is the only person in this city who could put a person of this place into a book, and that no form asks a person of sixteen whether he may. **He is not on a list and is not off a list**, and the office's printed definition of a person of the place does not reach him. He gave his name to Ilyan in a room and asked that it not be written down. He is not a power and not a rank and nobody has thanked him. `428:27`, `428:57`, `428:97`, `430:87`

**RENN CALLOW.** Fifty-eight. Keeps the Low Road twenty-nine years, unpaid, bad leg, a lamp he carries with the wick out. Eleven chalk strokes on a bench at a crossing, none of them a name or a figure, and one more that is not a stroke of anything. He walked a mile and a half down a row to ask a question and was given a number about a woman's work, and said out loud, in front of twenty people, that he knew the difference and that he was the one who had to know it. He walked past the ninth marker for the first time in four years, having said on the fifth day of the seventy-ninth week that he did not go past it alone. **He said that a road with one keeper and one decider in the same body is held and not kept, and that this morning there are two of them and neither of them is him. He is not on the line. He has not been thanked and has stopped expecting it and went to look at the water under the ninth weir.** `422:75`, `427:111`, `430:103`

**DARRIN SKELL.** Fifty-two. Counts halls and doors for twenty years; 900 doors in the ward, counted by himself, and four figures about halls he has never used. He refused the office a number of persons in a room, out loud, and said the day that number exists this city has decided what a person is and it will not be his room that decided it. **He did the division himself, out loud, with the working, nobody having asked him, and admitted that it was his and that he did not enjoy it.** He said the word *co-witness* and is not one. He said the second half of the bar: a person may go and may not, neither is a victory, and a woman of sixty-eight in a bed at the top of this city is counted in the answer anyway. `424:73`, `424:91`, `425:89`, `427:97`

**TAM FERROW.** Twenty-nine. Three years on the ground floor, copies fair. He read eight things out of the press in fourteen days and did not apologise for one of them. He put both of Fenna Rusk's answers on one line. He copied the plate into the press, and eleven pages of a roll into the press, and read the first page back to a woman who could not see it. **He put his own name on the first line of the return, because on the first day of the week he said out loud in a room that it is going to be him they ask, and he had nine days to think about it and got nothing better.** He wrote the word *the same* on the second line because a person of this place asked him to in a room, and there was no paper in front of him with a reason on it, and he would not make one up. He has never put two names in one line before `427`. **He paid for the cart out of four shillings a year he has never had, and he does not know what a cart costs from four hundred miles, and there is no line on any form for it.** `429:13`, `429:109`

**OTT DRAY.** Fifty-four. Carries sealed paper for the office: thirty-one sheets in nineteen years and he has never been asked what was in one. He brought four hurdles and a plate in and took them out the same afternoon, and told a row of nine people the difference between a sheet and a plate. He would not take a cart round four hurdles on a ledge a foot and a half over the water with a load that is not his, and he waited about eight hours on a tail-board and was the only one who saw a person go out and come back. **He has been in the country forty years and has never once seen the first kind of road, and this afternoon it was a woman in boots with mud on them and there was nothing on it, and he said he was not going to say it again.** He took the return and the roll up the hill and went round the hurdles on the south side, which is four yards of mud, because a cart does not go round four hurdles, and said nothing about it to anybody. `421:33`, `425:59`, `429:127`

**BESS LOW.** Sixty. A shawl over a dress mended at the cuffs twice. Reads what arrives: a dispute, a count form, six clauses, and a paper from four hundred miles off that is right about four things and wrong about the plan. She got up off the wall once, in `423`, and put three things on a table to prove she had been in the room — a bell not hung in a county four hundred miles off, a porch gone in a valley of sixty people, a corridor standing with a bell unpaid on it — and went back to the wall. She said she had been in this city nine years and had never once been counted. Nobody thanked her. `423:93`, `428:109`

**NELL FISK.** Thirty. Walked two miles to read a plate out loud on the flat, standing in the open, with the wind off it, twice, because Ilyan asked. `421:47`

**BEVIN TARR.** Cuts lines and dies for seals: four thousand lines, paid for two dies at four shillings the hundred, and has never read one of them. Came up the mile and a half with a plate under his arm four times in ten days, because he cannot leave a plate, and was not sent for. Said *huh* out loud twice in four days and did not apologise for it either time. `429:25`, `430:55`

**KERWIN DACE.** Thirty-eight, of the seat, prints the sheets. Went out of this city in `422` with a schedule of payments and a return with one column and no name in either, having said in a room that he was not asking anybody to stop him. He stopped two miles out at his own office's timber, walked round it, and said out loud that neither of the two plates the office has put on that road has ever named the south side of anything. He said he does not know what the money is for and has not asked and is not going to be the one who asks. **Not in this city. His return is still open.**

**TARIN KEEL.** Forty-six. Walking north after the office's man, on his own two legs, with three sheets, having refused the office's charge of conveyance. He said the sentence against that wall in a room on the first day of the month — *a government which reads what you write to it is a government which has not heard you* — and on the fifth day of that week a clerk of twenty-nine said it out loud in a room in about thirty people's hearing, because he was not here. **He walked past a plate and four hurdles two miles out on his way north eleven days ago and nobody in this city knows it.** `430:65`

**SERA QUILL.** Twenty-eight, four hundred miles off. Sent a paper into a tray, unwaxed, with an address in a hand nobody here knows. Right about the work, wrong about the plan. **Nobody thanked her. Her own family's claim of six years is unfiled and no chapter has asked her about it and nobody is going to.** `428:127`

## 2. The people who were not moved, and carry unchanged

Ansa Rell, sixty-eight, in a bed at the top of this city, in no register, the gap still a gap. The boy of eight, better and cannot be discharged, at the back of a room with a barrow between his knees, never named out loud in any room. The carter's boy of nineteen with an arm in a splint mending, who gave a road its first figure and was asked for nothing in this band. The woman of thirty with a head on her knee. The nine hundred doors of the ward. The about hundred and forty people who are dead.

## 3. And nobody is produced

**The produced-person count is zero and the test that holds it is in the prose.** Dray was on a cart between two towns and in a stable in this city before the band opened. Fenna Rusk had been in her room behind a chandler's shop for eleven years. Ferrow had been on the ground floor of the black building for three years. Sef had been at a door since the eighth morning of a fever. Calloway had been at his crossing for twenty-nine years. **Every one of the eight people who walked two miles out to a post had said out loud beforehand why they were going and by which route.**
