**SERA QUILL** (28, recordwright, **four hundred miles off**). **HER ANSWER ARRIVED IN FULL IN THIS BAND AND NOT BEFORE, and all four pages have now been read out, four lines of them in a market square on the thirty-first day and the other three in a room over a nail shop on the forty-third day, and the copyist who read them entered no line of them because no body is behind them.** She is right about the shelf and he is right about the lane. **Her three other pages say: a hundred and eighty forms in six years and a half across nine buildings, and the hinge in a hundred and eighty forms is always the same person and that person has never once been the person the form is about; a shelf eleven inches wide in the room where she sits, with about nine hundred refusals in it, eleven inches from where she sits, and she has not opened it and has written down eleven times in six years that she has not and nobody has ever asked her why; A LANE WORKS, and the one time in six years and a half she has seen a thing open without asking anybody's character was in a four hundred yard lane in a market town of four thousand people with about eleven people in it, and she has put all three of the things that town did in a paragraph in a round hand and has not put his name in any of them; and you can build me a shelf and I can build you a lane and neither of us can build the other one's thing, and there is no form in this county and none in the nine buildings she has worked in that would let either of them try, and this is written to a body and he is not the body, and she has not signed it with her name.** **And a letter of about nine hundred words has gone out of this town in her own hand, folded in four, with nothing on the outside of it, and no mark on it says where it came from.** She is not redeemed by any of this and her family's claim of six years is no longer only hers. `477`, `485`, `489`
