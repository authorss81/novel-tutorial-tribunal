# Character State After Batch 0001

## Ilyan Vester

- **Status:** First Defendant; early Stage 1, Case Opener. He can notice contradictions and, with a named advocate, phrase one narrow challenge or exception request. He cannot assign another person’s burden, force consent, or command a ruling.
- **Physical state:** Sleep-deprived, bruised from the crossing, carrying ordinary strain after handling the active plate. No supernatural protection or combat ability has appeared.
- **Current burden:** His identity, signed Earth report, and post-hearing testimony remain attached through the seventh-morning-bell review. He may not conceal or reassign the costs of that record.
- **Immediate want:** Keep the Ro household safe through the review, prove the missing maintenance condition, and find a route home without becoming Veyra’s author.
- **Moral change:** He learned that a useful fact is not made safe by being filed. His first voluntary burden is accepted, but his first use of another person’s private words caused real harm.
- **Public position:** The eastern district and nearby parties now know his name. His presence is both evidence resource and political liability.

## Sera Quill

- **Status:** Provisional advocate, limited scope. She resumes representation of Ilyan only after the flood-account dispute is recorded; she is not yet formally licensed.
- **Immediate want:** Complete the bridge case without sacrificing the Ro household’s privacy and preserve the Quiet Chamber trail connected to her family appeal.
- **Relationship boundary:** She works with Ilyan because the case has consequences and because he accepted a concrete boundary. She does not trust him personally, forgive the breach, or give him control over witnesses.
- **Current possession:** Custody of the court’s copied, disputed account. Neris retains the original flood account. Sera’s private duplicate of the surviving public Quiet Chamber fragment is missing; the original archive page’s empty sleeve has a removal record dated before Ilyan’s crossing.

## Tarin Keel

- **Change:** He has enforced neutral perimeters, explained escape conditions, and protected witnesses without allowing Ilyan to direct the hearing. He continues to distrust unchecked outsider judgment and may challenge Ilyan’s next proposal.
