The woman of about fifty-one over a milliner's with the copy of four pages. The four who hold four pages, and the fifth sheet nobody holds. The Keeper of returned names, sixty-one, with thirty-two letters. The Archive Lords, nine of them, about four hundred shelves between them. **AND ALL OF IT IS STILL RUNNING: the right of refusal is unrestored and given to nobody; the struck leaf is not struck back; about nine hundred refusals on a shelf eleven inches wide are not opened and nothing has been said about whether they were; the four letters and a stroke have not been read aloud in any of the fifty chapters; the page that came back four hundred miles is in the inside pocket of a coat and is not mentioned; about nine hundred tests are signed by a woman named at `600` for the first time and no more is said of her.**

**And from earlier volumes:** the office of Regent of the Shelf, not held; Fenna Rusk's roll of eleven pages under a stone; Renn Calloway and the eleven chalk strokes; Tarin Keel walking north; Kerwin Dace; Sef, sixteen; Ansa Rell, sixty-eight, in a bed, in no register; the boy of eight; about a hundred and forty dead, governed as a category by a signature that is not a person's; the bell, the porch and the corridor; the twelve shillings and the three hundred blank names untouched, the blank correct; **the first missing guarantee of the original Compact, recovered in Volume 11 and untouched since; the nine anchor positions, six restored, the seventh burned, the eighth under the Red Basin, the ninth under Orison, all untouched.**
