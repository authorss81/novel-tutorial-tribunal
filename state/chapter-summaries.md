**661 — A LIST OF NINE JOBS IS READ OUT LOUD BY A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE WHO SAYS HE CAN DO NONE OF THEM, AND A MAN OF SIXTY-SIX IS ASKED TO GO DOWN A THIRD TIME AND SAYS NEITHER YES NOR NO.** The fifth day of the hundred and sixteenth week, a Saturday, his four hundred and eleventh morning, his one hundred and seventh day in the county of Kell, and the fever fifty-four weeks old. **652 — THE SIXTH READING OF TWO COPIES AGREES, AND A MAN OF SIXTY-SIX COMES DOWN THE BANK WITH A PICK AND A LAMP HE PUTS DOWN BEFORE HE SAYS ANYTHING, AND HE SAYS WHAT OPENING A BOARD IS.** The third day of the hundred and fifteenth week, a Thursday, his ninety-eighth day, and **nobody on that bank says the number of days he has been in Hask, and the chapter says so.** A man of thirty-eight goes up the water in the Gull with a sheet of deal in a coat and brings it down and says the two copies agree and that nobody has ever once asked him to do it and nobody is going to. **654 — ABOUT NINETY STEPS GO DOWN AND A STONE DOOR ABOUT FIVE FEET HIGH TAKES TWO PEOPLE TO SWING, AND THERE IS A HAND CUT IN A LINTEL WITH FOUR FINGERS ON IT AND A LINE CUT UNDER IT IN NINE WORDS, AND THE CHAMBER IS A DAY.** The fifth day of the hundred and fifteenth week, a Saturday, his one hundredth day, and the fever is fifty-three weeks old.
