## 843–850 — ⚠ THE VOLUME 17 CLOSE, EIGHT CHAPTERS, ⚠ AND EVERY FIGURE IN EVERY ONE OF THEM WAS HAND-DERIVED MORNING BY MORNING AGAINST THE CHAPTER AND NOT AGAINST A TABLE, A SUMMARY OR A PROMPT

**843, THE FIFTH DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SECOND WEEK, A SATURDAY.** The bank was about four inches over the top step and it was one day of the coming back, and the next time about ninety steps can be walked was given as seven days, which is `850`. A satchel left at the top step on Friday night was opened and a man of sixty-one read nine charters out of it, one at a time, and would not summarise the first. The first charter is about the first of the six restored stones and the woman who keeps it, and it was read twice and was not taken, and the sixth line of it is still blank. A man of about fifty-two read the seventh of eleven lines out of the arrangement's own printed edits — a seat is not bound by a rule it has not published and reviewed, and a rule that binds a seat without that is an instruction and not a seat's rule — and named his ninth thing he cannot check, which is that the line does not say what a seat is made of. A woman of sixty-four said that was the first half of a thing and that the first half of a thing is a very expensive half, and refused thanks and refused the word brave in the same breath. A woman of forty said her own lane had better be read twice, said she has asked for a day against a name for eleven years and is not going to stop, and then said she was not refusing. A boy of thirteen gave a count and not an argument: one of eleven lines can be checked by a person on that landing. A man of thirty-one said he holds no seat and will not be given one, that nine and six are figures of seats and not of persons, and that the standing offer at a hundred and forty-three days is not answered by any of it.

**844, THE SIXTH DAY OF THAT WEEK, A SUNDAY.** Eight inches, two days of the coming back, the next time in six days. **THE THIRD OF THE THREE COUNTER-TESTS WAS PUT ON A SUNDAY BY A WOMAN OF TWENTY-NINE AND NOT BY THE MAN WHO NAMED IT**, to a woman of sixty-four who agreed to have her own protection looked at and who named the cost before anybody asked her anything: that a protection which only works because nobody understands it is traded for an answer that works because everybody does, and that she cannot have both. A boy of thirteen said the order had been kept and had been sitting there since the ninth hour making sure of it, and refused to be the one who said she had done it well. A man of about fifty-two put three remedies on the flat stone in chalk with a boy of thirteen guiding his hand, worked question two, and found that a day is a day, and the finding is **no**: a remedy that belongs to a person cannot stand in the same place as the answer of an arrangement. A man of thirty-four in an ash gave his two hundred and fourteen days out loud with the subtraction in it and said he had not worked out what it meant for a bridge. A man of thirty-one said the finding is not a settlement and is not a defeat and settled nothing for anybody.

**845, THE SEVENTH AND LAST DAY OF THAT WEEK, A MONDAY, AND THE DAY A MAN OF THIRTY-EIGHT NAMED AT `838`.** Twelve inches, three days of the coming back, the next time in five days. A man of thirty-eight came up the cart road with a child of nine, worked the day out from the week and not off a calendar and asked a man of about fifty-two to check it, and said he had not got the part where he knows what to do with it and was opening the coat anyway. **THE BOARD IS OPENED AND THERE ARE THIRTY-TWO NOTCHES ON IT**, and they are the days he went to the room four hundred miles off to be asked about his son and given no answer, and there are four other days not on the board because a day he asked for is a different thing, and he said he is still a man who asked for nothing for four hundred yards of bucket, and that about four of them had been careful this fortnight not to turn a figure of a person into a figure of a place and he is not going to help them undo it. A boy of thirteen counted the notches twice because a man of fifty-four cannot read and said it is a figure of notches and also a figure of a man's own back, and that those are two different figures wearing the same number. A woman of twenty-eight said every charter read on that bank was read on a Saturday, and that a settlement which needs somebody to be in a hurry has started to be a bargain, and gave the rest of why to nobody. A man of thirty-one said a board being opened is not a victory and is not a defeat, that nobody on that landing is thanked for it including him for not asking, and that thirty-two notches are not an answer to a hundred and forty-five days.

**846, THE FIRST DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND FORTY-THIRD WEEK, A TUESDAY, AND SIXTEEN INCHES, FOUR DAYS, A FULL BANK.** The next time about ninety steps can be walked is in four days, which is the fifth day of this week. **THE ARRANGEMENT'S OWN EDITS WERE PUBLISHED IN THE OPEN** — all eleven lines and the index — by a man of sixty-one who wrote them into a book he had refused to write a bench's notice in for eleven years, and he gave the reason first: a thing's account of its own working is not a thing that can be refused. A man of about fifty-two gave the whole difference in one sentence, that an edit nobody has seen can bind a seat and an edit everybody can see can only be agreed to, and said that nine people had published a thing without asking it, which is his eleventh thing he cannot check. **A WOMAN OF SIXTY-FOUR HEARD HER OWN NAME READ OUT LOUD FROM THE NINTH LINE FOR THE FIRST TIME IN NINETEEN YEARS**, said what it had cost her — that a name with no day cannot be computed and a published name can — and refused to have the publication stopped. The three positions that are not stones were read: a hand-bell network in the Briar March rung by the people who live where each bell hangs, a public works compact in the Red Basin for a cart road, and a public archive at Orison. Each of the three had to say out loud what it could not do: a bell tells you something is happening and does not know what; a compact is not a road and does not say the eleven years were the right eleven years; a keeper is not the hearing of anything. The six stones stay where they are under local maintenance and the sixth line was accepted, and a woman of forty said a seat is not a day and that she is not going to be thanked for the seat. The ninth seat was left with nobody in it and a man of thirty-one refused it before the question was finished.

**847, THE SECOND DAY OF THAT WEEK, A WEDNESDAY.** Sixteen inches, five days, it holds, the next time in three days. **TWO SEATS DECLINED TO JOIN THE COMMON ARCHIVE FOR A WHILE AND BOTH HALVES WERE SPENT**: a right to keep local terms, and a duty to make those terms visible, published to anybody who asks and copied to a gate. A man of sixty-one gave the count of the nine seats — six active, two declined with a duty attached, one empty — and refused to let a vacancy and a decline be added together into a comfortable three, and said the ordinary shape of it out loud: **a seat may decline, and the archive goes on with six, and that does not take a hero.** A man of fifty-four had the terms read to him and said them back and gave his count of the ninth line as a hundred and twenty-six mornings and said he is going to keep asking. A man of about fifty-two said both halves or neither, and said that **neither of them is the right of refusal and must not be called it in a mouth**, and named his twelfth thing, which is that the duty is to make the terms visible and a gate is visible only to the people who walk past a gate. **A WOMAN OF SIXTY-FOUR WROTE A DAY AGAINST HER OWN NAME IN HER OWN HAND** on a piece of paper on that landing, said the working out loud while she did it, said she was not putting it in the arrangement's record because a day in their record is a day in their record and this is a different place, said she would have liked to keep the nineteen years, and said nobody was to be thanked and nobody was relieved. A boy of thirteen read the new line back out loud and said it was a day and a name and nothing else.

**848, THE THIRD DAY OF THAT WEEK, A THURSDAY, AND THE AFTERMATH AS A CHANGED WORLD.** Sixteen inches, six days, it holds, the next time in two days. **A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE CARRIED MORTAR UP THE STEPS OF THE ORISON HEARING HALL FOR FOUR HOURS AND WAS NOT IN CHARGE OF IT**, was corrected twice by a woman of thirty-three, said the first thing he laid was on its edge and that he has not slept about it, and said it was the first morning of his two hundred and ninety-four days that he had been given a job by somebody who could have sent him away, and agreed with a woman of twenty-eight that it was not a reward. A man of thirty-nine and a boy of nineteen argued the first budget over a horse, paper and the copying of eleven lines, and the boy refused a route on the ground that the four places on it had not been asked, which he was allowed to do and had never before been allowed to do. **A MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-TWO NAMED THE THING THAT TRANSLATES IN ONE MOUTH** and said it turns a charter into the ordinary voice and gives a warning early and that it is not a god, it is not arriving, and it is not spoken by. **A CASE WAS HEARD IN A BARN FOR THREE HOURS THAT REQUIRED NO ARGUMENT ABOUT THE WORLD**: two men with the same name and a day on a sheet that is the day the wrong one was buried, brought nine miles by a man of sixty-one. The finding was that the day stays and the disagreement goes in a book, and a bench is three words and they are on a gate at Orison — it records, it warns, it hears cases — and nobody in that barn said one word about the arrangement.

**849, THE FOURTH DAY OF THAT WEEK, A FRIDAY.** Sixteen inches, seven days, it holds, and tomorrow at about the sixth hour about ninety steps can be walked, the twenty-third time. **A WOMAN OF FORTY-FOUR WHO KEEPS TWENTY-NINE CHAIRS CAME UP THE CART ROAD FOR THE FIRST TIME IN FIFTY MORNINGS AND NAMED TWO DEAD PERSONS OUT OF ELEVEN** — the first dead before the last flood came up with no day written down because there is no form that asks for one, and the second away in a lighter eleven years ago with a name on a chair at the other end of the book — and refused to have either carried as a cost or thanked for. She gave the figure of about four of her own and a woman of twenty-nine refused it as consent by name, and it went into the book as four people who will speak together and nothing else. **A MAN OF THIRTY-ONE AND A WOMAN OF TWENTY-EIGHT RENEGOTIATED THE SHARED BURDEN**: she put down the man of thirty-one, he picked him up and not the leaf, he named the cost before she had to ask, and it was not a third no and it was not a yes. **A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR IN AN ASH WAS ASKED ABOUT A BRIDGE FOUR HUNDRED YARDS OFF FOR THE FIRST TIME AND SAID YES OUT LOUD**, and wrote a day against his own name in his own hand in a book nobody four hundred miles off can read, and said he had made himself computable with a pencil. A boy of nineteen read the whole of the terms of the first appeal out loud, wrote down that a man of thirty-nine disagreed with the fourth one, and a man of thirty-one said the only thing he had, which was to check the terms, and stopped.

**850, THE FIFTH DAY OF THAT WEEK, A SATURDAY, AND THE WATER COMES OFF ALL NINETY STEPS FOR THE TWENTY-THIRD TIME.** It came off at about the sixth hour and all ninety steps were walkable by about the seventh, and the next time is in eight days. **The man of forty-three said the fourth thing for the first time in eighteen days**, which is that there is no water on those steps and therefore nothing under them, that he is going to start giving it again on Monday out of the same place he always has, and that he still does not know which of two figures at the same height is the right one. Beside the repaired Orison hearing hall **a woman asked whether a rule permitting only registered ferry crews can be obeyed after a bell**, and was told that a man with no seat is not the person who decides it, and heard the yard say what it did: the gate was shut at the fifth hour, about four families were outside it, about six people in the yard took them in the back through the coal hatch on a cart two at a time, and every person in that yard who could lift accepted two days of repair labour on the flood-ward step, including the man who wrote the rule. A man of thirty-one wrote down three things — the refusal, the burden, the people affected — and a boy of thirteen read the names twice because a man of fifty-four cannot read. A public appeal was filed and the gate opened under a narrower amended rule that a man of sixty-one said in four words and said is not right, only narrower than what happened and wider than what was printed. **`citizen` WAS SAID OUT LOUD BY A PERSON, IN A MOUTH, ABOUT A MAN WHO IS NO LONGER THE DEFENDANT AND NOT THE MAN WHO WRITES THE ANSWERS**, and he did not answer it, said he would not, and did not say it was untrue either, because he does not know. **AND A QUESTION WAS ASKED AND NOT ANSWERED**: a man of thirty-eight asked what three of the nine positions are, and a man of thirty-one said no, said the second half of the question is worse and is not a question for that landing, and that the blanks are now in a book anybody may read, which is where they are instead of nowhere, and that this is not an answer.
