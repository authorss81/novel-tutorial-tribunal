## VOLUME 09, CHAPTERS 441 TO 450 — the volume's turn and its close

**441 — The exception, in writing, five lines.** A fifth sheet under the writ, unsealed, dated the day the writ came down, die-cut foot: Iven Marrow, Regent of the Shelf, the third head Bevin Tarr has cut. (1) A person not of the Shelf may be named in an instrument, and the office does not require a person able to hold the office to be of a city on a road — a sentence the room had heard the day before. (2) The second seat at the council, **four pounds a year** quarterly. (3) The instrument lodges on the ceasing of the holder, and the roads pass. (4) **The ninth marker is a place of passage out of this country, and the keeper of the box is its keeper.** (5) Nothing a finding. Skell: eighty shillings a year against forty-four in eleven years, unpaid. Ilyan says out loud, in a room, that he wants line four and has wanted it for one hundred and ninety-one mornings. Hessa Marl says the three parts she can say and refuses, aloud, to say whether she would count a person going out. **He does not answer in the room and says so.** `441`

**442 — Thirty-one wards asked for a person; the first answer is a counter.** The office asks every ward for one person, does not ask for a function, checks nothing — and grants that a person may decline to answer, which nine people call the right of refusal and two of them say is **a permission and not a right: a person who may decline to answer is a person the office has decided does not have to be told anything.** Six-day window; a ward that sends none has not declined. The first answer, in the evening, is Darrin Skell, sent by a hall and not by a man. Ilyan says *function* out loud before he has said it to himself and is made to say it again standing up. **Nobody in the room can say how a form asks for a person**; Fenna Rusk says a form asks for two, Skell says that is how a form asks for two people, and Ilyan says he does not know. `442`

**443 — The two-line form, and the burden line in his own hand.** Six pages at a penny a page, set in the press window, nobody sent for it. Line 2: a ward may name one other person who may be asked concerning the first **and not concerning a road**. Line 3, the burden line, in his own hand, in a room where it could be read: where the person first named is one the office does not recognise, the burden of the recognition falls on the person named in the second line. Foot 1: **Ilyan Vester, in his own hand.** Foot 2: empty, because he does not get to choose. **Panel (the band's one):** a form with one line cannot ask a ward for a person, and the second line is the only part of it that is a person; the burden names a person of the place who does not get to be asked first and does not get to refuse; nothing chosen, nobody told. Nell Fisk reads all six pages aloud in the rain, twice, and works out in four minutes that the empty line is not a question. Bess Low says she knows what a bill is standing next to one. `443`

**444 — Four wards answer; the office asks her; she says his name.** Two carts and a third: plate yard (Skell; Renn Calloway beside), low row (Fenna Rusk; herself), nine bridges (Nell Fisk; Skell), by the rail (Bess Low; herself). Skell: four in the first lines, three in the second, two wards naming the same person twice. Then the office's answer to one sentence — up the **first** day of the week, down the **fifth**, five days: it may ask a person of this place about anything in the form in which they are named, and **it has asked the person named in the second line at the seat, in a room there, who placed the name of the person not of this city beside their own.** She asks him not to leave the room. He stays. She says four things, and then says his name, in her own mouth, with him standing in it. The answer goes up that evening: one line, and the line is a name. `444`

**445 — The night, and a barrow.** Nine men come up the Low Road in the dark with a bar of sawn ash nine feet long, nine links of chain and a written authority, and about eleven people are in four yards of mud on the south side with a lamp. Ilyan says the wrong true thing — the hurdles are the office's and there is nothing here that stops it — and a man of about nineteen puts a barrow in the way of it. **What holds the crossing is not the draft and not the office: a barrow going to work goes up the side that has never been named in any document, and about forty people and two carts follow it in forty minutes, and the nine men stand there and take nothing down.** At the change of the light a rider reads four lines into the middle of the floor: an instrument of succession lodged at the fourth hour, naming one person, and the holder ceased at the same hour — and the third line takes the roads. `445`

**446 — Iven Marrow comes up the road on foot, and is refused line by line.** Sixty-one, wet to the knee, went round the four hurdles in the mud, and asked for the mud to be entered and not the walking. He wrote the third line because a road held by whoever is standing nearest it killed four off one cart in a winter. He asks for the one answer he has had twice in forty years and is told that the one instrument he has is a piece of paper naming a man four hundred miles off. **Ilyan accepts line 1 in full and declines lines 2, 3, 4 and 5, in the offer's own words, out loud, in a room, and asks that it be entered in those words and not his.** A frightened room says he has shut the crossing; he does not deny it. Marrow names the right of refusal, taken out of the law about a hundred and forty years ago and not put back. **Then he asks the sixth question — somebody has to hold the roads — and Ilyan says *I do not know*, says he has no remedy, and asks the clerk to enter that he could not do it in a day and not in two years.** The writ is silent on a person who is named and declines. `446`

**447 — Twenty-two of thirty-one, and the count nobody expected.** Last day of the six. **Twenty-five names in the first lines → twenty-four persons (one of the twenty-five is the keeping of a box); twenty-two names in the second lines → twenty-one persons (Skell is in two); nine wards sent nothing and are on no paper; the office writes twenty-two on the foot of the form and calls it a settlement.** The second foot line was filled on Saturday evening in a fair hand that is not his, with *placed* struck through and *put* written over it — the ward at the end of the low row, three times in two days, and she was not asked. Bess Low finds the thing the draft does: **a ward can be silent and be protected by nothing**, and there is no line in six pages for a ward to say no in, and the man who wrote it says out loud that he left it out because he is afraid of what a refusal would be asked to carry. Marrow: twenty-two of thirty-one is a road administration. `447`

**448 — The terms, and the bar stays up.** Six clauses, all correct. The instrument is entered and not withdrawn; the writ has **no provision for a person who is named and declines**, and **the office is not held**; until the council of the Shelf fills it, **no bar upon any road in this city may be taken down and no plate concerning any road in this city may be put up**; the twenty-two wards noted and not checked; **clause five is the sheet of the fifth day of the eighty-third week word for word — the sums are noted and not pressed for, and not one line of the six pays anything.** Ilyan admits in the room that he wanted the bar down for five weeks and got it by accident, and that he broke Skell's instruction in the last three words of his own sentence. Marrow is defeated by a refusal, refuses the room's villain, and says a road with no office on it is decided by whoever is standing nearest it and that he has no doubt the council will sit. Cope enters his standing: named in an instrument, declining entered, on the fifth page of a city's form with a woman of forty-four under him, and **no line anywhere to take him off it.** `448`

**449 — A declaration of continuity, signed by something that is not a person.** Marrow goes down the road before the light, round the mud. In the tray: her answer to the seat, noted and entered; and a sheet that did not come from the office, with a seal that is not the office's and a date in a reckoning this city does not use. Four clauses: the office is not held and this declaration does not fill it; **this declaration is made by the First Witness, which is not of this city, does not hold the office, has not been asked by this city about anything, and is not a person**; **it governs every person entered in a register of the Shelf, whether or not they are in the city where the register is kept, and whether or not they are alive, and after they have died in the same way that it governs them before**; nothing a finding. At the foot, a die mark pressed and not written — a word, and then *Witness* — and Bevin Tarr says he did not cut it. **A clerk copies the same clause of the eighty-third week word for word and, in the same afternoon, asks the office a question the office can only answer by asking the man who wrote the form, because the form has only his name and a blank beside it.** `449`

**450 — The road in the morning.** Second hour, Friday, his two hundredth morning. Four hurdles, nine links, a plate with a name at the head and none at the foot, a box, a slate, and about two hundred crossings a day going up four yards of mud on a side that has no name in any office document in the world. Hessa Marl reads her slate out: **fifteen days, three thousand crossings, three thousand farthings = 750d = 62s 6d = five pounds two shillings and sixpence**, against nine visits in the office's book, and a crossing is not a visit. Ilyan does the working in the rain. Fenna Rusk carries a lamp a mile and a half, makes him say his number out loud, and says the roll is under a stone and she has not been able to say whether it is right once in nineteen days. **They stand in the mud and say the co-witness line out loud on a road: neither can take the other off it, neither can pay the other back, and he has stopped trying to.** The bar is up because nobody has the power to take it down. Fever twenty-three weeks and six days. `450`
