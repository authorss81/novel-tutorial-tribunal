## VOLUME 09, CHAPTERS 431 TO 440 — what each one is

**431 — The roll comes down, opened, and cannot be certified.** Cart in at second hour, oilcloth re-tied, knot reversed. Dray: ordinary way, three yards visited in nineteen years, never let in on the third. Ferrow: his knot done the other way round. Thirty people hear eleven pages read; pages in order, folds across — Fenna Rusk folds down the gutter. She says twice she cannot say whether figures are right. Ilyan carried it up the row unasked; wants a paper it came back unopened and will ask rooms for a week. Fever 21w 1d. `431`

**432 — A market no becomes a ward's legible no.** Count form back with office third line requiring signed declining with ward named. Skell refuses sixth time before two hundred on the flat, will not sign: a ward of nine hundred doors cannot be asked or answer back. Ilyan chose the market knowing it cannot be kept out of a book. Form returns *Declined. The ward of this city*, no name. Fever 21w 2d. `432`

**433 — The office names a person about a road.** Dace's schedule back stamped Cray Crossing, day after sending. Fifth row entered against Tarin Keel — walked out the **fourth day of the eighty-second week** at own charge, refused conveyance, after saying his sentence in a room on the **first day of that week** — on office's motion, no paper from this city. Four reply lines: received, not conveyed/paid/removed, entered of own knowledge, never to be asked about city or road. Ilyan wanted refusal in a book; got protection instead. `433`

**434 — The co-witness answered as a description.** Ferrow's one-sentence question (which name is person/thing) answered in four correct lines: both in column, distinction not required/paid, neither entered, line filled at marker by a person of the place — plus a note that the outsider said the cost out loud before eight witnesses *because a register ought to hold it*. Ilyan can no longer call the mud a thing he did in a room. Three go to the marker; mud keeps nothing. `434`

**435 — The box, and the one who cannot say no.** Box up north side by fourth hurdle: farthing a visit, one-line keeper form, book of visits without names, nothing a finding. Panel (band's one): kept place is cheapest to ask; burden names Hessa Marl; choice her or a paid bar with a person. Ilyan asks her on the record to refuse it, naming the price; she takes it — box at cart hour, nine doors mornings, crossings on her own slate, name in the line Saturday before thirty. Skell: 3,276 visits = 819d = 68s 3d. `435`

**436 — The sum, the error, the refusal.** Claim £4 4s noted; payable only if entered in a nonexistent keepers' register; declining entered in same book — printed five days before she declines. Skell corrects himself: 4,004 = 11 × 364; 11 × 4s = 44s = **£2 4s**; £4 4s is twenty-one years' wage, ten undone, his error since first of week. Ilyan withholds the entering-sentence and leaves without asking. Fenna Rusk refuses before nine; declining goes up unsealed. `436`

**437 — Sent for on a headless sheet.** First sending-for in the volume: summons with wax/seal, no head, mid-sentence, a boy of sixteen to answer concerning a road. Wray Cope, 34, person appointed in disputes register — the dispute reached him in a tray on the **fifth day of the eighty-second week** — in the city, round the south side. Sef sets terms, answers the return line, refuses safe/unsafe and the name, gives four doorways in order — putting Ferrow at one. Ilyan withholds his ready sentence. Hessa Marl declares her box line before thirty. Fever 22w 0d. `437`

**438 — The unsent return.** Alone over a saddler's: Ilyan copies the head, writes his name, four margin lines (what he is, four people in books unnamed, a margin number from three weeks of boards held since the fourth day of the eighty-second week, why unsent). **Dated that morning**, folded, in his coat. Tells thirty people, withholds the number twice; room wants it. Calloway: four office things on the north side and he keeps the road — what is he for? Ilyan: wrong question; no one in ninety thousand can be asked about putting or taking. Ferrow minutes the unsent number he never heard. `438`

**439 — Eleven pence and eleven names.** Bowl outside a Tallowgate shop, second hour to light: eleven pence in, eleven names on Ferrow's line with hours — written before collecting; tenth pence collected, spare penny off-page, Bess Low's name without penny or promise. Bell 4 pounds = 960d; 960 ÷ 11 = 87 remainder 3. Skell: subscription/list/roll going up a hill. Bess Low, sixty, nine years uncounted, refuses to be one of eleven, reads shortfall of one aloud. Ilyan's plea to lift names puts the one in public. `439`

**440 — The writ, and which sentence.** Head: office of Regent one office. Four lines, all correct: one office undivided; passes on ceasing to named instrument or second seat at council; roads/keeping/bar/taking-down pass with it; nothing a finding. Calloway: ninth reply, first unasked; the dispute went up on the **fifth day of the eighty-second week**, the day the office gave it to him. He asks Ilyan which sentence — mud (*for the road*, said in four inches of mud on the second day of the eighty-third week) or margin (*will not be in a book*, said Sunday at the change of the light) — and Ilyan picks the first, stating it cannot be kept, then asks Cope to leave a sentence out and is refused on the record. Question now who holds the roads. `440`
