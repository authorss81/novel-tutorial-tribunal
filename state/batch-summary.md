## Goal and outcome

The fifth prose batch of Volume 03, and the batch `outline/series.md` gives the breach, the link and the charter to. **There is no monster. Nothing is attacked. Ilyan links a ferry family, a glassmaker and a guild officer, and every one of the three says yes to being asked and none of the three says yes to the same thing.**

**The three are Sorrel Gannock, thirty-six, a flat boat at the hard; Hollis Thrale, sixty-one, a fire at Thornglass; and Bettany Rook, fifty-one, the harbourmaster.** They are the three people Chapter 140 closed on, nobody has asked any of them for anything, and not one of them can see the other two. The batch's spine is that **the flat refuses first, correctly, and the refusal is what makes the three possible** — because a body that stands behind a record about a road is standing behind a thing nobody on that coast can stand behind, so the people who cross the flat cannot be in it, and the claim has to be carried by three people who were not on the road at all.

**Stage 3, Witness Broker, is arrived in Chapter 147 and it is a duty and not a power.** He asks three people for permission to have their accounts put in a record, one of them says no at first, he does not persuade her and does not try again in the same words, and the whole of the profession turns out to be **the separation and not the joining** — which is the thing he was wrong about for a month and wrong about in a net loft at the north end on the third of the sixth tide. **The permanent cost is paid in a person: three names are in a record with his own at the foot of the third sheet, the record is not sealed and is not in a locked room, and the finding is that the broker's fee is that somebody can send for the broker's own paper.**

**The resolution is a revocable passage charter on paper, made out of three people's own accounts in their own words, with the three named and the burden named and the burden on a person and not on a harbour.** It is the first thing on this coast ever made that way and nobody in the shed says so. **The revocation costs a person a day and a standing and seventy faces and no answer, and it cannot be done for anybody else, and that is what makes it a charter and not a licence and not a notice.** It is worked out in five mouths over one day and nobody who works at it is a bench, a court, a villain or a hero.

**The volume closes on the System recognising a tester signature from Earth belonging to Nadia Kestrel, in a Veyran record, and saying nothing about what it means, and on a woman of twenty-four asking in an ordinary voice who was standing there when somebody taught a copy of this place to let a person say no. Nobody answers her. Nothing on that coast answers her.**
