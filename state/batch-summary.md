# Batch 0001 Summary — Volume 01, Chapters 1–10

## Goal and outcome

Ilyan Vester crossed from Earth through a one-use legacy null passage and became the First Defendant in Orison’s ninth-bridge case. He kept Tamsin Ro alive with Oren Sedge’s crew, entered a formal challenge through Sera Quill, and secured only a provisional stay. The bridge is usable by the Ro household for one review cycle, with Oren’s maintenance burden, Ilyan’s attached record, and the district’s disagreement all named. The current charter is unchanged. Appeal, enforceable exit, durable housing/work protection, and the maintenance condition remain open for the full hearing.

## Key events

- The Earth report was never merged, but its signed refusal sequence was recognized as an external challenge. The Shale Mirror fragment answered beneath Morrowlight’s room; the living-person crossing came from a separate legacy null passage. The passage was non-consensual, one-use, assigned no automatic burden, left no return route, and spent the ninth bridge’s emergency reserve while worsening an existing drift.
- Tamsin, age ten, was rescued from beneath the bridge with a repeatable clamp-and-pin brace. Neris Ro, her mother and a dye-yard tally worker, named the household’s access, housing, and work needs while refusing public use of her private flood account.
- The challenge fields were accepted in the Chamber of Passage. Ilyan accepted a bounded public witness duty; his identity and report became public, and the duty ended with the first night field. Sera signed as a provisional advocate, initially for the record rather than Ilyan’s innocence.
- Oren’s old saltglass workroom plate preserved a maintenance-review condition missing from the current public charter. A one-bell maintenance exception held only while the named crew performed the inspection. An eastern-district petition copied the old language and made an unlisted person’s refusal a danger to every lawful traveler.
- Ilyan filed Neris’s private flood account without asking. The Bench preserved the copy; Sera halted the access request and suspended representation. The account is disputed, not evidence unless renewed, and the names it contains were not offered by the people named. Neris later offered only the warning-bell fact; Tamsin chose to testify that the bell rang before the collapse.
- The first one-bell night field exposed a “playable” technical term and a continuity line, “Continuity preserves the record before remedy.” Harrow physically replaced the active maintenance plate with the current certified duplicate; the original saltglass evidence was sealed, not magically rewritten.
- Sera’s surviving public fragment says, “the governed may answer no,” while current Bench text says, “the governed may petition for correction.” The original Quiet Chamber page’s binding sleeve is empty, and the inventory records that original’s removal before Ilyan’s crossing. Sera’s private duplicate, present the night before the first field, disappeared during or after that field. The two dates and the remover are unresolved.
- Ilyan reached early Stage 1, Case Opener: with Sera, he can frame a narrow request for a hearing or exception. He still cannot choose another person’s burden or grant himself authority.

## Relationship and practical changes

Ilyan moves from treating the bridge as a puzzle to accepting a public, continuing obligation. Sera resumes only limited provisional representation after the correction; trust is conditional and specific. Tarin becomes a cautious working ally through neutral perimeters and practical decisions. Tamsin and Neris retain separate voices and consent boundaries. Harrow remains a procedural gatekeeper rather than a solved villain. The eastern district becomes a serious political and physical pressure.

## Batch ending

The practical recovery is complete for this cycle: Tamsin and Neris cross together, the repaired load holds, and the review date is public. The meaningful final event is the archive discovery: the original Quiet Chamber page’s removal was recorded before Ilyan arrived, while Sera’s later duplicate is also missing, leaving the full hearing with two dates and a visible but incomplete clue about the right of refusal.

## Next-batch pressure

The eastern petition has copied the provisional wording into a district dispute. Chapters 11–20 must show that a local remedy can become a weapon when its burdens and consent travel farther than its original place. Sera must pursue the missing Quiet Chamber trail without treating it as a shortcut to acquittal, and the Ro household’s housing/work needs must remain active.
