# Batch 0005 Summary — Volume 03, Chapters 141–150, *The Breach, the Link, and the Charter*

**THE LAST TEN CHAPTERS OF VOLUME 03. The next phase is a volume close and not a batch, and this file is the record the volume close has to read. There is no committed review behind this batch and no agent may claim one: the phase prompt forbade writing one into `reviews/`.**

## Goal and outcome

The fifth prose batch of Volume 03, and the batch `outline/series.md` gives the breach, the link and the charter to. **There is no monster. Nothing is attacked. Ilyan links a ferry family, a glassmaker and a guild officer, and every one of the three says yes to being asked and none of the three says yes to the same thing.**

**The three are Sorrel Gannock, thirty-six, a flat boat at the hard; Hollis Thrale, sixty-one, a fire at Thornglass; and Bettany Rook, fifty-one, the harbourmaster.** They are the three people Chapter 140 closed on, nobody has asked any of them for anything, and not one of them can see the other two. The batch's spine is that **the flat refuses first, correctly, and the refusal is what makes the three possible** — because a body that stands behind a record about a road is standing behind a thing nobody on that coast can stand behind, so the people who cross the flat cannot be in it, and the claim has to be carried by three people who were not on the road at all.

**Stage 3, Witness Broker, is arrived in Chapter 147 and it is a duty and not a power.** He asks three people for permission to have their accounts put in a record, one of them says no at first, he does not persuade her and does not try again in the same words, and the whole of the profession turns out to be **the separation and not the joining** — which is the thing he was wrong about for a month and wrong about in a net loft in the sixth of a tide. **The permanent cost is paid in a person: three women's names are in a record with his own at the foot of the third sheet, the record is not sealed and is not in a locked room, and the finding is that the broker's fee is that somebody can send for the broker's own paper.**

**The resolution is a revocable passage charter on paper, made out of three people's own accounts in their own words, with the three named and the burden named and the burden on a person and not on a harbour.** It is the first thing on this coast ever made that way and nobody in the shed says so. **The revocation costs a person a day and a standing and seventy faces and no answer, and it cannot be done for anybody else, and that is what makes it a charter and not a licence and not a notice.** It is worked out in five mouths over one day and nobody who works at it is a bench, a court, a villain or a hero.

**The volume closes on the System recognising a tester signature from Earth belonging to Nadia Kestrel, in a Veyran record, and saying nothing about what it means, and on a woman of twenty-four asking in an ordinary voice who was standing there when somebody taught a copy of this place to let a person say no. Nobody answers her. Nothing on that coast answers her.**

## What the batch delivers, in order

## The three things the outline gave this batch, and where each one is

- **The link, not a fight.** Nothing is attacked, nothing is a monster, nobody is a hero of the water, the coast is not saved, and nobody on this coast gives Ilyan standing. He gets no party, no bench, no body, no office, no party of friends and no remedy he chose alone. **He also gets nothing new to do to the world: Stage 3 is a duty with a consequence and its consequences are responsibility for the safety of the three, responsibility for keeping their accounts apart, and a permanent liability on his own record.**
- **The revocable charter.** Chapter 149, on paper, in three hands, with the three named and the burden named and the burden on Bettany Rook by name and not on an office. **Revocation is real, available to any one person and not to a body, and it costs a day and a standing and seventy faces and no answer, and it cannot be exercised on anybody's behalf.** The cost was worked out in Chapter 148 by the five people who would pay it and by nobody else, and the first person on the coast to think about using it is Wend Garrow, who did not build it and does not use it.
