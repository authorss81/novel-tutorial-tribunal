> **WHAT IT IS NOT. It is not a summary of the novel, and it is not evidence.** Every count in it is a pointer to the place the count is kept, and where a count is printed here at all it is printed with the file and section that carries it. **A count in these files that carries no line number beside it is unverified, however confident the sentence around it is, and a line number here is a convenience and not a citation — sections are addressed by their heading, because a heading does not move when a paragraph above it is edited and a line number does.** **The sizes and line counts in the table below were measured on the day this index was written, and they are the reason for this file rather than a claim about the story: re-measure them rather than believing them.**
>
> **THE STANDING RULE, inherited from the Volume 03 close and not improved since: re-run the grep against the chapter files, and do not re-read the list.** A list in this project has been checked against the previous list instead of against the chapters six times in two hundred chapters, three separate lists were each short once, and the Volume 04 close found one short by ten.
