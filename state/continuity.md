SERA QUILL DOES NOT APPEAR IN ANY CHAPTER OF 601–610 AND HER TWO LETTERS ARE UNREAD. THE FIRST WITNESS IS NAMED TWICE IN THE TEN CHAPTERS, BY A PERSON WHO WAS IN THE ROOM, AND IS NOT ADDRESSED AND IS NOT ANSWED. A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR FROM A TOWN FOUR DAYS OFF WHO IS RIGHT ABOUT THE ARCHIVE LORDS WAS NOT USED. THE STRIKE PROPOSED AT `604` WAS NOT CARRIED OUT AND WAS NOT REFUSED. THE QUESTION ASKED AT `610` WAS NOT ANSWED.**
