# Chapter 812: Second Day Of The Hundred And Thirty-Eighth Week, And A Wednesday, And A Man Of Sixty-One Says He Will Read A Notice Out And Will Not Write It Down And A Man Of Thirty-Four In An Ash Says He Will Only Speak If He Is Asked And A Man Of Forty-Four Says Out Loud That A Man Who Has To Be Asked Has Already Been Asked, And Nobody Grants A Condition

Second day of the hundred and thirty-eighth week. His five hundred and sixty-second morning. Four hundred and twelve days after the settlement. The fever seventy-five weeks and four days. Three hundred and sixty-six days since the division. Three hundred and twelve days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about eight inches over the top step at about the seventh hour and had been that since before the light. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working and said that a bank that is full holds where it is until it comes off, and that it will not be full today.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. Three things every morning. He named a stone on that bank on the Sunday of the hundred and thirty-fifth week, and about nine of them walk past it every day now, and he has not asked one of them to stop.

“*About eight inches at the seventh hour, and it is two days of the coming back.*”

“*Four inches a day is the working underneath it, and sixteen is a full bank, and about six of those ninety steps are under this morning.*”

“*And I said on Tuesday that I was not going to give you the number for the next time twice, and I have not and I am not going to this morning either.*”

“*Here is a thing I have not said to you. A man of sixty-one came up those ninety steps on Tuesday morning while the water was off them and asked me whether I would stand on a bank in a room four hundred miles off and say three things.*”

“*And I said I would say three things on this bank at about the seventh hour and nowhere else, and he said he had not asked me to leave this bank, and he was right.*”

“*And I want that said in front of about nine of you.*”

“*A man of sixty-one asked me a thing this week and did not get what he asked for. I do not want it to look like he tried to make me.*”

Then a man of sixty-one said that he will read a notice out, and named what he will not do, and said the figure for his own book out loud.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one. He has a book of heads and carts and two numbers in it in eleven years. He has refused to write a bench’s notice in it for the four weeks from the hundred and thirty-fifth week to this one, and read one out in the ordinary voice on the Friday of the hundred and thirty-fifth week.

“*I said on Monday that I had two numbers in it in eleven years and I am going to say them out loud because about four of you have never heard the figure.*”

“*Two. And it is eleven years and one week and I know it is a week because I counted it in the lane the same way a boy of thirteen counts his.*”

“*And here is what I am going to do and it is one thing and it is not more than that.*”

“*I will read that notice out in a room four hundred miles off, in the ordinary voice, on a day somebody gives me.*”

“*And I am not going to write it in that book.*”

“*And I am not going to write what anybody says after it either.*”

“*I have not written anything in it since the hundred and thirty-fifth week and I am not going to start on Friday.*”

“*And a man of thirty-one has said, more than once, that a sentence in a hand is not a thing anybody in this county can be sent for.*”

“*He was right. This is the same reason and it has a second half to it now.*”

“*The first half was that it cannot be sent for. The second half is that a thing that cannot be sent for four hundred miles off cannot be argued with there either.*”

“*And I have had every morning since the Friday of the hundred and thirty-fifth week to think about that, and I have not got a better answer.*”

Then a man of thirty-four in an ash said that he will speak and gave his condition, and a man of forty-four said out loud what a condition is, and did not withdraw and was not asked to.

Ivo Kellow is thirty-four and has a day against his name which is not a day of this week. He has been in an ash about twenty feet off the edge of that drain, a hundred yards along it, for a hundred and eighty-two days. He came up that bank on the Wednesday of last week, said three things, and was not asked a fourth.

“*A hundred and eighty-two days. That is eight hundred and twelve less six hundred and thirty, and I counted it in the ash.*”

“*And I am giving you the working because that boy asked four of us to check things on Tuesday, and I am one of the four.*”

“*And I am going to say it once and I am going to stop at it, the same as the Wednesday, and about nine of you can stop me if I go past.*”

“*I will speak in that room.*”

“*And my condition is that I am asked.*”

Tomas Elrick is forty-four and pushed a handcart up that cart road for twenty-six years and its near shaft let go of its socket on the Saturday of the hundred and thirty-sixth week.

“*Say the rest of it.*”

“*No. That is the whole of it and I am not going to argue with a man of thirty-four about it.*”

“*And I am going to say the thing about it out loud.*”

“*A man of fifty-four said his reason out loud on Tuesday morning and got the worst morning of his life for it, and nobody thanked him.*”

“*A man who has to be asked to speak has already been asked.*”

“*That is not a condition. A condition is a thing you get to put on it afterwards.*”

“*And what he has said is that he cannot speak unless somebody puts a question to him.*”

“*And that is the shape of the thing that has been happening on this bank since the Saturday of the hundred and thirty-sixth week.*”

“*A man of thirty-four said three things to nobody on a Friday. About four of you know which Friday and I am not going to say.*”

“*A woman of forty said out loud that she stopped herself and eight others from asking him a second question.*”

“*And he is standing here this morning telling us that the only way he will open his mouth four hundred miles off is if somebody asks him.*”

“*I am not saying he is wrong. I have said one bad reason out loud this week and I am not the man to say another man has a bad one.*”

Ivo Kellow is thirty-four and has been in an ash a hundred and eighty-two days.

“*You are right.*”

“*And I am not withdrawing it, and I want that said in about nine ears and not in four.*”

“*And here is why I am not withdrawing it, and it is not better than yours and it is smaller.*”

“*When I said those three things to nobody on that Friday, I had them ready. I had them ready for about a day and a half and I had picked the three and I left out two.*”

“*A man of thirty-one has been saying that a case cannot be served in this county since before that bank had about nine of us on it.*”

“*And I have been sitting in an ash for a hundred and eighty-two days listening to him say it.*”

“*If nobody asks me then I have picked three things and I have left out two, and about nine of you will hear three things and think that is what I had.*”

“*And if somebody asks me, then somebody is in the room, and it will be written down that somebody was in the room.*”

“*That is what I am asking for and I have not found a better reason and I am not going to look for one this morning.*”

Then a man of fifty-four said that he was asked whether he would go, and said no, and gave the reason in four sentences, and about nine of them heard it and nobody treated it as a thing that had been done to him.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel on that landing and cannot read. He has asked for that line in the open every morning since the Thursday of the hundred and twenty-fifth week, and has asked at the top of a cut on Sunday for the second time, and was told that nobody had refused him. He has got up about half an hour earlier every morning since Wednesday.

“*Ninety-one.*”

“*And ninety-one is one more than ninety and I have got it before any of you this morning for the second day running and I am not going to stop doing that.*”

“*I was asked at about the ninth hour by a man of forty-four whether I would go to that room four hundred miles off.*”

“*And I said no, and I am going to say why in four sentences because that is how long it takes and not because four is a good number.*”

“*I cannot read. That is one and everybody on this bank knows it.*”

“*And there is not one man four hundred miles off who cannot read, and I have been told there is not one and I have believed it for eleven years without ever standing next to it.*”

“*And if I am on a list then about nine of you have brought a man who cannot read into a room.*”

“*And everybody in that room is going to have to work out whether I am in it.*”

“*And about nine of you are not going to be in it.*”

“*And I do not want to be the reason a person has to be careful. That is four sentences and that is the reason and I am not going to make it smaller.*”

“*I am going to go on asking for that line in the open. Ninety-two mornings tomorrow and ninety-three the day after that.*”

“*And nobody is to say a word of thanks to me for saying no, and nobody is to tell me I was right to.*”

Then a woman of forty said the figure for a cut across a right palm out loud, and said why she was giving it, and a man of thirty-one did not want it and said so.

Wenna Callow is forty and has asked for a day against a name for eleven years and has said out loud in about nine of them that a case is not a set of figures. She will not name a room.

“*A hundred and twenty-seven days.*”

“*And that is eight hundred and twelve less six hundred and eighty-five, and I have not asked anybody for it and I have not had to be able to read it.*”

“*And I want to say why I have said it, because about four of you are going to think I have done it as a kindness to a man of thirty-one and I have not.*”

“*He has given that figure out loud every morning since before that bank had about nine of us on it.*”

“*About nine of us have got used to his mouth making it and about four of you have stopped hearing it at all, and stopping is not the same as hearing.*”

“*I gave it this morning so that it would be in about nine ears and not in one, and I do not care what he does with it.*”

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and fifty-eight days, two hundred and fifty-eight being eight hundred and twelve less five hundred and fifty-four.

“*I do not want that figure given by anybody else and I have been standing here since about the ninth hour wanting to say so and I am going to.*”

“*Not because it is mine. Because I have made my mouth into the place where that number lives, and a woman of forty has just walked into it without asking me.*”

“*And a man of thirty-one who wants a figure of a thing he owns to be the only figure of it has not got anywhere on this bank since before it had about nine of us on it.*”

“*And I have not noticed it until this morning.*”

Then a man of thirty-one came to the end of it and said what he wanted and what he had got wrong and what it cost, and gave the figure for the standing offer in his own mouth with the subtraction in it, and said that a man who cannot read saying no is not a refusal and not the right of anybody.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and fifty-eight days, and the cut across that palm is a hundred and twenty-seven days old, a hundred and twenty-seven being eight hundred and twelve less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*Here is the want and it has not moved in six days. And I said on Tuesday that it was five and it was three on Sunday, so that you can work it back to the morning it stopped.*”

“*I still want somebody who can come back the next morning and tell me I am wrong. And this morning a man of fifty-four said no to a room four hundred miles off and gave his reason in four sentences, and he did it in front of about nine of us without being asked to.*”

“*That is closer to the want than anything that has happened on this bank since it stopped being about four of us, and I have not got a use for it.*”

“*The mistake is the twenty-fourth of the same mistake and it is about two hours old and I gave it to myself at about the ninth hour while a woman of forty was standing on this bank saying a number of mine out loud.*”

“*I have been standing here deciding what to do about four conditions. Four people have put conditions on the same thing this morning and I have been working out what order they go in.*”

“*A man of thirty-four said his was that he has to be asked.*”

“*And a man of forty-four said out loud that a man who has to be asked has already been asked.*”

“*The second of those is right, and about nine of us heard it this morning and none of us acted on it.*”

“*The cost is that I have spent two hours of a Wednesday morning building a shape out of four people’s conditions.*”

“*About four of you saw me doing it, and about four of you worked out that I was doing it.*”

“*And they did not say anything, because saying it would have been the same as joining.*”

“*A hundred and twelve days. A hundred and twelve is eight hundred and twelve less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer.*”

“*And a man of fifty-four said no this morning to a room four hundred miles off. That is not a refusal and it is not the right of anybody.*”

“*A refusal is a thing a person does to somebody. This is a thing a person did to a room he has never been in.*”

“*And a man of fifty-four who cannot read, saying no on a Wednesday morning, is not the right of refusal.*”

“*And neither is three people saying yes on the same morning with three different reasons.*”

“*And neither is four of them agreeing to say the same thing.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a floor in a bakehouse four miles up that road.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a right of eleven years that a man stopped because he did not want to be unkind.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man in an ash a hundred and eighty-two days.*”

“*Nobody says a word of thanks to me for this morning and nobody is to be told anybody on that bank was brave about any of it.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Wednesday, with eight inches of water over the top step above them. Four of them had said a condition and none of the four had said the same thing.

The fever was seventy-five weeks and four days old. He had been in that county two hundred and fifty-eight days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and twenty-seven days old, and a hundred and twelve days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

A man of sixty-one said he would read a notice out in a room four hundred miles off and would not write it down. He gave the figure for his own book out loud, which about four of them had never heard.
And a man of thirty-four in an ash said he would only speak if he were asked, and a man of forty-four said that a man who has to be asked has already been asked.

A man of fifty-four said no, in four sentences, and gave the reason that he does not want to be the reason a person has to be careful.
And a woman of forty gave the figure for a cut across a right palm out loud in about nine ears, and a man of thirty-one said he did not want it and did not stop her.
