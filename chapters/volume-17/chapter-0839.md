# Chapter 839: First Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Second Week, And A Tuesday, And A Full Bank Holds At Sixteen For The Second Morning And The Next Time Is In Three Days, And A Thing On A Flat Has A Fifth Leaf On It And A Man Of Sixty-Four Reads The First Two Lines Of It And Says Stop And A Man Of Thirty-One Says He Is Not Going To Stop Reading On Friday

First day of the hundred and forty-second week. His five hundred and eighty-ninth morning. Four hundred and thirty-nine days after the settlement. The fever seventy-nine weeks and three days. Three hundred and ninety-three days since the division. Three hundred and thirty-nine days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about sixteen inches over the top step at about the seventh hour and it was the second morning of a bank sitting at that figure. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working and the figure for the next time, and did not give the figure of how many of those ninety steps are under, and said that on the last morning of last week he had been asked for it and had not got it, and that a man of thirty-one had not asked him since.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. Three things every morning, and the fourth thing has been nothing for a week and he is no longer apologising for it.

“*About sixteen inches at the seventh hour, and it is five days of the coming back, and that is a full bank and it holds.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in three days, which is the third day of this week, and that is four days after the morning it is full, and the morning it was full was Sunday.*”

“*And four inches a day is underneath all of it and anybody here can work the next one out without me, and I have been saying that for a week and about four of you have stopped asking me which is the correct number of times to stop being asked.*”

Then a man of sixty-one said there was a fifth leaf on that flat, that it had been under the fourth one and that he had not known it was there until about the seventh hour, and that he had weighed it in his hand before he said anything about it.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one, and has a book of heads and carts and two numbers in it in eleven years, and has refused since the second day of the hundred and thirty-sixth week to write a bench’s notice in it. He read four leaves of a thing on a flat in about nine ears on four mornings, one leaf a morning, and on the last morning of last week he read an answer off a page of the same kind and did not write it in.

“*It came at about the seventh hour and it had been under the fourth leaf the whole time, and I have carried that flat for nineteen years and I did not know.*”

“*I am sixty-one. I am going to say that again because I want about nine of you to have the number before I go on, and not after.*”

“*It is a fifth leaf and it is the same size as the other four and there is a nail through the corner of it with rust on the head, the same nail.*”

Then a woman of sixty-four stood up off the bank and said one word, and the word was the name of a thing that has been in about nine ears every morning of that week.

Ada Renk is sixty-four and has said a word twice in nineteen years and has refused nine people who asked her to say it a third. On the fifth day of the hundred and thirty-eighth week she gave away nineteen years, and on the last morning of last week a man of sixty-one read out in about nine ears that the gap she had relied on was the arrangement’s own.

“*Stop.*”

“*I am not going to say why, and I am not going to say it twice.*”

“*And I want that on the page before anybody works out what I have done.*”

“*And I have said a word twice in nineteen years, and this is not the word.*”

“*And I am not spending it on a page with a nail in it.*”

“*You read the first two lines of it and you stopped, and I want about nine of you to notice that a man of sixty-one who has read four leaves a morning for four mornings stopped on the ninth word of the fifth one, and that is not a reading man stopping. That is a man being stopped.*”

Then a man of sixty-one said what he had read in the two lines, and said that it was not a threat, and that it was the only thing anybody had put in front of that bank in eleven days that he could not describe in one sentence, and gave the reason in his own mouth.

“*Here is the whole of the two lines and I am giving it to you before the rest of the leaf, because a woman of sixty-four stood up and I am not going to be the one who decides what about nine of you hear next.*”

“*Line one: there is one seal that is immediately valid, and it is not yours, and it may be taken once.*”

“*Line two: it may be taken by the person the arrangement has already named, and that person is the man of thirty-one.*”

“*And I have read out a bench’s answer off a bench’s page on Saturday and I did not write it in. I have been eleven days saying out loud what I do not know.*”

“*And I do not know what line two means, and I am not going to stand here and guess at it in about nine of your ears.*”

“*And I will read the rest of the fifth leaf on the third day of this week, in the open, on this flat, whole or not at all, and there is no fifth way and I have not looked for one since Saturday.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said what the thing on that flat was, and said it was not a voice and not a message, and gave the four words he had been using for it since Friday, and said he was going to keep using them.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and eighty-five days, two hundred and eighty-five being eight hundred and thirty-nine less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and fifty-four days old, a hundred and fifty-four being eight hundred and thirty-nine less six hundred and eighty-five. He has asked seven times whether it will keep. Nobody has said and he is not going to ask an eighth time.

“*A thing on a flat. That is the four words and I have been saying them since Friday morning and I am not going to improve on them on a Tuesday.*”

“*It has no voice. It has not had a voice in eleven days and the woman of sixty-four is the reason I know that, and she is not going to tell you why and neither am I going to guess at it for her.*”

“*A man of sixty-one has read two lines of a fifth leaf off a piece of paper in about nine of your ears and stopped on the ninth word because a woman of sixty-four stood up.*”

“*And I want to be exact about that. It is the only thing that has happened on this bank in four days that I did not arrange.*”

“*I did not ask her to stand up. I did not know the leaf was there. I have been on this bank four mornings saying a figure at about the eleventh hour and a woman of sixty-four has just spent something she has kept for nineteen years on stopping a man of sixty-one, and she did it for a reason that is hers and not mine.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for that and I am not going to let about nine of you work out that it makes me look better than a man who did not arrange it.*”

Then a man of thirty-one came to the end of it and said what he wanted, what he had got wrong, and what it cost, and gave the figure for the standing offer in his own mouth with the subtraction in it, and said it was still two offers and not one.

“*Here is the want and it is the same shape it has been every morning of this week and it is one day smaller.*”

“*And I want to say the thing nobody has said on this bank in four days, which is that I do not know what I would do, and that is not modesty. I have worked the arithmetic of this for two days and I have got two answers and I have not got a third, and about four of you have been waiting for me to pick one in front of you.*”

“*The mistake is the thirty-ninth of the same mistake. I gave it out loud on Friday morning and it is the same mistake.*”

“*And I am not going to renumber it, because a new number would look like progress.*”

“*A hundred and thirty-nine days. A hundred and thirty-nine is eight hundred and thirty-nine less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer.*”

“*And the offer on that flat came on Friday and it is four days old. The offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth is a hundred and thirty-nine days old.*”

“*And those are two offers, and I am giving you both figures with their own subtractions, because a woman of twenty-nine in a reed told me in about nine ears on the second day of this week that merging them is the one move that would settle it cheaply.*”

“*They are not the same thing. One of them was asked of me by a person at the ninth hour. One of them arrived on a flat in the night with a nail through the corner of it. And I have not answered either, and I am not going to answer the second one on Friday by accident because it is easier than the first.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Tuesday with sixteen inches of water over the top step above them, a fifth leaf under a fourth one, and a man of sixty-one who had read two lines and stopped and would not say why.

---

The fever was seventy-nine weeks and three days old. He had been in that county two hundred and eighty-five days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and fifty-four days old, and a hundred and thirty-nine days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

A fifth leaf had been under the fourth one for the whole of the four days, and a man of sixty-one had weighed it in his hand and said he was sixty-one before he said anything else, and a woman of sixty-four had stood up and said one word and would not say it again.
And a man of thirty-one said that a thing on a flat has no voice, and that he did not ask her to stand up, and that he was not going to be thanked for a thing he did not arrange.

The man of sixty-one read the first two lines and would not guess at the second one in about nine ears, and said he would read the rest of the leaf whole on the third day of this week or not at all.
And a man of thirty-one said the two offers are two offers, and gave the standing one at a hundred and thirty-nine days with the subtraction in it, and said he has two answers to the other one and no third.
