# Chapter 825: First Day Of The Hundred And Fortieth Week, And A Tuesday, And A Full Bank Holds For The Fourth Morning And Comes Off Tomorrow For The Twentieth Time, And A Man Of Thirty-One Goes Up That Cart Road At The Seventh Hour And Comes Back Down It, And A Woman Of Twenty-Nine In A Reed Says The Thing He Came Back With Is Not An Answer And Is Not Nothing

First day of the hundred and fortieth week. His five hundred and seventy-fifth morning. Four hundred and twenty-five days after the settlement. The fever seventy-seven weeks and three days. Three hundred and seventy-nine days since the division. Three hundred and twenty-five days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about sixteen inches over the top step at about the seventh hour and it was the fourth morning of a bank sitting at that figure. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working, said what tomorrow was, gave the ordinal out loud because tomorrow is the twentieth time, and gave the figure for the next time, which is a figure with a rate on it and is therefore derivable.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. Three things every morning and none of them about the water. He named a stone on that bank on the Sunday of the hundred and thirty-fifth week and about nine of them walk past it.

“*About sixteen inches at the seventh hour, and it is seven days of the coming back, and that is a full bank and it holds.*”

“*And it comes off tomorrow at about the sixth hour, and that is the twentieth time since the Saturday of the hundred and nineteenth week.*”

“*And it is eight days on from the Tuesday of the hundred and thirty-ninth week, and the nineteen before it were eight days apart, and the four at the start were not.*”

“*And I have said that four times since the seventh day of the hundred and thirty-seventh week, and I am not going to say it a fifth.*”

“*Four inches a day is the working underneath the holding, and it will be about four inches and eight and twelve and sixteen on the mornings after tomorrow.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked after that is in nine days.*”

“*And that is tomorrow plus eight, and tomorrow is the twentieth and the one after that is the twenty-first, eight days on.*”

“*I want that said plainly, because a figure with a rate on it has its next one worked out from the rate.*”

“*About four of you have been writing the water down in a lane and about four of you have not, and I have read both of those figures this morning and they do not agree, and that is not a mystery, it is two lanes.*”

Then a man of thirty-one went up that cart road four miles at about the seventh hour and came back down it, and did not go up with anybody, and said what he had gone for and what he had come back with.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and seventy-one days, two hundred and seventy-one being eight hundred and twenty-five less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and forty days old, a hundred and forty being eight hundred and twenty-five less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*I went up four miles at the seventh hour and I came back down at about the eleventh.*”

“*I did not go with anybody, and nobody asked to, and I am not going to make a thing of that either.*”

“*I did not go to see the child. I went to see the child’s mother, and I want about nine of you to notice the difference and not to smooth it over for me.*”

“*The child is six years old and she is sitting up.*”

“*She has been sitting up since the Thursday of the hundred and thirty-ninth week, and she has kept down what she has eaten since the sixth day of that week.*”

“*And the arrangement has not done anything.*”

“*There is no line in that mother’s house with a date at the top of it and a name under it, and I looked, and I looked properly.*”

“*And I am telling about nine of you that I looked.*”

Then a woman of twenty-nine in a reed said that what he came back with was neither an answer nor nothing, and said what a figure of four people is and is not, and said what she would be doing about it.

Marda Vell is twenty-nine and has been in a reed about twenty feet off the low side of that drain, a hundred yards along it, for a hundred and ninety-six days, and she can read, and her name is in ink on the ninth line of a book four hundred miles off and has never had a day against it.

“*A hundred and ninety-six days. That is eight hundred and twenty-five less six hundred and twenty-nine.*”

“*And I can read, and about nine of us on that bank did not know that for a hundred and seventy days.*”

“*That is a figure of about nine people and it is not a figure of anybody who is not standing there.*”

“*I was about nine feet off him when he said the child was sitting up, and I have been in this reed since before the sun came up.*”

“*So I am going to say what I saw before he says what he thought of it.*”

“*Nobody has treated that child and the child is better.*”

“*That is the whole of what I came up for, and I am not going to make it into less than it is or more than it is.*”

“*And a man of thirty-one has come down four miles with nothing in his hands, and about four of you are disappointed.*”

“*I can see the disappointment and I am telling him he can have it in about nine ears.*”

“*You went to check a description. You did not go to contradict a document.*”

“*Those are two different walks and only one of them is four miles.*”

“*And I am going to say a thing about a man of forty-three and I have been sitting on it since about the ninth hour.*”

“*I want to thank him for the water.*”

“*Do not thank him for the water, and I am saying that in the same breath I am saying it in, and about four of us can hear that both halves of it happened at once.*”

“*He has given that figure out loud on that bank every morning for nineteen years and about nine of you have come up that cut to hear it, and not one of you has ever said a word to him about it.*”

“*And he is the only man on this bank who has never once been asked whether he wants to give it, and I am not going to be the one who starts, and I am not going to thank him.*”

“*And I am not going to tell you the reason I am not, and that is a different thing from the first half and about four of you will not hear the difference and about four of you will.*”

“*And here is a thing I want said in about nine ears. I am not a person who can be asked to be checked. I am a figure of one person in a reed.*”

“*And about four of you are about to say us. Us is four people, and I said that on the third day of the hundred and thirty-sixth week, in a reed, at about the eighth hour.*”

“*And it is still four, and it is not going to be five because a thing on a flat has a nail in it.*”

Then a man of sixty-one said the fourth leaf would be read on the second day of the hundred and fortieth week, in the open, on that flat, and gave the time, and said he would read it whole or not at all.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has a book of heads and carts and two numbers in it in eleven years, and has refused since the second day of the hundred and thirty-sixth week to write a bench’s notice in it. He is the only one of the four of us who can read every word on that flat and he has said out loud that he can read and not that he can check.

“*It will be read on the second day of the hundred and fortieth week, at about the tenth hour, on that flat, in the open.*”

“*About nine of you know the time now, because I am saying it now.*”

“*And I will read it whole or I will not read it. There is no fifth way and I have not looked for one.*”

“*And I am not going to read it early because a man of thirty-one went up a road this morning.*”

“*That is not a reason, and I would like about nine of you to hear me say that it is not.*”

“*I am going to read it on the Wednesday because a man of fifty-four said he would decide then.*”

“*And because a woman of twenty-eight asked me at about the eighth hour on Friday morning whether I was going to read it on my own, and I said I was not.*”

“*She did not ask me to read it. She asked me not to read it alone, and I heard the difference.*”

“*And I am naming the difference because about four of you are going to hear me say the wrong thing.*”

Then a boy of thirteen said what he had counted overnight and said it was a count of times and not a clock, and gave it twice on purpose.

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and seventy-two days, and he has counted them in a lane, and the figure is one more than the morning before. He has a figure of seven that will not go up, and it is a count of mornings on which somebody on that bank said a thing that was not a figure.

“*A hundred and seventy-two days, and it is one more than the morning before, and I counted it in the lane before the light came up.*”

“*And here is my figure and it is eight now, and it was seven on the third day of the hundred and thirty-ninth week.*”

“*I am going to give it twice in front of the same about nine of you, so that about four of you can check it.*”

“*Eight.*”

“*Eight is a count of mornings on which somebody standing on this bank said a thing that was not a figure.*”

“*Not a figure of a thing and not a figure of a person. Just a thing said.*”

“*And it is a count of times and it is not a clock.*”

“*A man of thirty-one has been calling it a figure for nine days and I have let him, because he is the only one of the four of us who could check it and nobody else has offered.*”

“*And nobody is to thank me for the two, and I am not going to say who the eight mornings were.*”

“*And a man of thirty-one is not going to ask me in about nine ears, and if he does, I am going to say no in one sentence, and only one.*”

Then a man of thirty-one came to the end of it and said what he wanted, what he had got wrong, and what it cost, and gave the figure for the standing offer in his own mouth with the subtraction in it, and said what he is not going to do with it.

“*Here is the want and it is the same shape as it was on Thursday, and it is one day smaller.*”

“*A man of forty-four said on Saturday that about four of you are going to have to decide something, and I am not going to decide it for him on Wednesday.*”

“*The mistake is the thirty-fifth of the same mistake and I gave it out loud on Monday.*”

“*I am not going to number it again this week, because numbering a mistake is a way of not having it.*”

“*What I did wrong this morning is that I went up four miles and came back down and told about nine of you the child is better, before I told about nine of you that nobody has treated her.*”

“*A woman of twenty-nine said the second half of that first, in about nine ears, and she was standing about nine feet off me, and I did not see her.*”

“*That is what it costs to be the man whose mouth every figure comes out of.*”

“*I have said that in about nine ears four times now, and a man of fifty-four said on Monday that four is too many, and I am not going to say it a fifth time.*”

“*A hundred and twenty-five days. A hundred and twenty-five is eight hundred and twenty-five less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer.*”

“*The offer is at a hundred and twenty-five days, and the thing on that flat is at three days.*”

“*I am giving both figures with their own subtractions, because a man of thirty-eight stopped giving a figure about a thing in a coat on purpose, and I have understood why this week.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a woman of twenty-nine in a reed who said the thing he came back with is not an answer and is not nothing, and who has not been thanked for it.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a boy of thirteen who gave a figure of eight twice on purpose and would not say which eight mornings.*”

“*And nobody is relieved of the fact that a child of six is sitting up and nobody has done anything, because I have not said what that is.*”

“*And I am not going to say it until Wednesday, and about four of you are right that I am building a case and about four of you are right that I am not, and I am not going to spend the morning telling you which.*”

“*And the right of refusal is not restored this morning, and a child getting better by itself is not it.*”

“*And I am not going to spend Wednesday on that, and I am telling about nine of you that I have already thought about spending Wednesday on it.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Tuesday with a full bank still over the top step above them, one morning short of the twentieth time. A man of thirty-one had gone up four miles and come back down and said a child of six was sitting up and that nobody had treated her, and a woman in a reed had said that was not an answer and was not nothing.

The fever was seventy-seven weeks and three days old. He had been in that county two hundred and seventy-one days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and forty days old, and a hundred and twenty-five days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

The water did not come off all ninety steps at about the sixth hour, and the man of forty-three gave the figure for the next time as a figure with a rate on it, and said it comes off tomorrow for the twentieth time, eight days on from the Tuesday.
And a man of thirty-one said a child of six four miles up that cart road was sitting up and that nobody had treated her and that he had looked for a line with a date on it and it was not there.
