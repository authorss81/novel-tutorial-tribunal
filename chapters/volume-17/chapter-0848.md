# Chapter 848: Third Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Third Week, And A Thursday, And Sixteen Inches And Six Days Of The Coming Back And It Holds And The Next Time Is In Two Days, And A Man Of Thirty-One Carries Mortar Up The Steps Of The Orison Hearing Hall And Is Not In Charge Of It, And A Man Of Thirty-Nine And A Boy Of Nineteen Argue About The First Budget, And A Case Is Heard That Does Not Require An Argument About The World

Third day of the hundred and forty-third week. His five hundred and ninety-eighth morning. Four hundred and forty-eight days after the settlement. The fever eighty weeks and five days. Four hundred and two days since the division. Three hundred and forty-eight days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about sixteen inches over the top step at about the seventh hour and it was six days of the coming back, and it holds. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working and the figure for the next time. He said that if anybody had told him on Saturday morning that he would be saying holds three days running, he would have asked them for a figure they could not produce.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and gives three things every morning. The fourth thing is a figure of how many of those ninety steps are under, and it has been nothing for sixteen days.

“*About sixteen inches at the seventh hour, and it is six days of the coming back, and it holds. A boy of thirteen asked me this morning what happens if it holds for so long that everybody stops noticing it, and I told him that is the most dangerous sentence anybody can say about a bank.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in two days, and that is the fifth day of this week. Two days, and I have not looked at the water-stone, and the slate says what I say and not the other way round.*”

Then at about the seventh hour a man of thirty-one carried mortar up the steps of the Orison hearing hall for four hours, and was not in charge of it, and did the job he was given, and was corrected twice by a woman of thirty-three.

Sabra Vint is thirty-three and has been a mason for nineteen years and has the hall roof over her because the hall roof came off in the wind two years ago and nobody had money until Wednesday. She is not on any seat and has said she does not want one and has been asked once.

“*You are carrying it like that and you will have your back on the stones by the fourth load. There. Over there. And you are to put the stone down flat and not on its edge, because a step that is laid on its edge in October is a step that a person finds in February.*”

“*And I am not being unkind to you and I do not care that you are the man who opened a portion on Friday. You are a pair of hands this morning and I have got about six of those and about four of them are mine.*”

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and ninety-four days, two hundred and ninety-four being eight hundred and forty-eight less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and sixty-three days old, a hundred and sixty-three being eight hundred and forty-eight less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*Twenty-two years of that, and I have watched about four steps laid in my life. I laid three of them, and one of the three is the one she found this morning, and it was on its edge. I have not slept about it and I am not going to pretend I have.*”

“*And I have been in this county two hundred and ninety-four days. This is the first morning of those days that I have been given a job by somebody who could have told me to go away. I am not going to say what it has done to me, because a woman of twenty-eight is going to say it is not a reward.*”

Then at about the eleventh hour a man of thirty-nine and a boy of nineteen argued about the first budget in a room with a door shut, which was a thing nobody had expected. The budget was set, and about four of them were not satisfied with it.

Tarin is thirty-nine and testified against his own command and lost his rank. He is a field witness carrying emergency records between regions, and has not been in a room with a door shut for two years, because he was not sure there was one he was allowed to be in.

“*I have carried records between four of these places for two years and there has been no money for it. I have been doing it out of a coat. Put a figure against the coat in the budget and I will carry anything you like.*”

“*And the first thing I will carry is a copy of every one of those eleven lines, to every region that declined on Wednesday. A woman of thirty-six put her terms on a gate, and a gate is not in Red Basin, and a man in Red Basin is never going to walk to Briar March to read it.*”

Bram is nineteen and is the first apprentice of the public cartography office, which is four days old and has a table and a stool and a window with a broken catch. He is allowed to refuse a route if the people who depend on it have not consented.

“*I have a table and a stool and a window, and I have made a map of a landing. It is the best map anybody has made of it. A man of thirty-nine is asking for money to ride a horse between four places, and I am asking for paper.*”

“*And I want it written down that I said no to his route this morning. Not because the route is wrong. Because the four places on it have not been asked, and I have been told I may refuse a route on that ground, and I have been refusing things since I was fifteen and this is the first one that was allowed.*”

Then a woman of about fifty-two said what the thing that translates is, in about nine ears, in one mouth, and said what it is not, and nobody on that landing argued with him about it.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well, and has a palm and a forearm, and has named twelve things he cannot check. On Wednesday morning he named a thirteenth, which is a figure of a woman, and said it was the last one he was going to name before the end of the fortnight.

“*There is a thing that translates. Everybody in this county calls it the System, and I am saying the word once in about nine of your ears, and then I am not going to say it again today.*”

“*It turns a charter into the ordinary voice. It gives a warning when a thing is about to happen and it is early. That is all it does now, and that is all it is going to do as far as I can see, and it does it without a room and without a door and without anybody in it deciding to.*”

“*And it is not a god, it is not arriving, and it is not spoken by. I know that because I have watched about four of you this fortnight look at it the way people look at weather, and a thing you look at like weather is a thing you have stopped asking questions of.*”

Then a case was heard at about the second hour, and it was about two men with the same name in two valleys, and it took three hours, and nothing anybody said about the world decided it.

The man who brought it is called Abel Ardley and is sixty-one, and the other man is called Abel Ardley and is dead, and the day on the sheet is the day the second one was buried. The bench that hears it has no door shut and is two planks and a bench in a barn.

Abel Ardley is sixty-one and has come nine miles with a folded paper in a coat, and has not asked anybody to help him carry it, and is not a defendant and has not been one.

“*I have brought this nine miles because a man of thirty-one was on a landing and told about four of us that a person may refuse a specific rule. I do not know whether that has anything to do with my business, and I have come anyway, because I did not know where else to go.*”

“*My Abel died four years back in a hard year. There is a day against the name on a sheet that came from a clerk four hundred miles off. The day on that sheet is the day we put my Abel in the ground, and it is not his day. It is mine.*”

“*And I have been to two rooms about it and in both of them a man read the day out and asked me a question about a debt. I have not got a debt. I have got a dead son and a name on a piece of paper that does not know which of us it means.*”

Then a man of thirty-one heard the case for an hour and a half and said what the answer was and what the answer was not, and said that nobody in that barn had said anything about the world all morning, and that that had been the point.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and holds no seat and has said so on this landing in about nine ears on three mornings, and said the finding in the ordinary voice.

“*The day on the sheet stays. That is the finding and I am not going to dress it up. The bench that hears this can record that a man of sixty-one says the day on the sheet is the day he buried a son of the same name, and that the sheet does not agree, and the two of those are both true and only one of them is arithmetic.*”

“*And it can record that Abel Ardley of the low valley is not the Abel Ardley of the high valley, and that both of them are on a sheet which does not say which. That is a thing a clerk four hundred miles off can put right in about a minute, and has not put right in four years.*”

“*Nobody in this barn has said one word about the arrangement, and I want that said. About four of you came down that road expecting to, and there is going to be a story afterwards that we settled something. We did not settle it. We wrote down a disagreement in a book, and a man of sixty-one is going home nine miles with it.*”

“*And here is what a bench is now, in three words, and the three are on a gate at Orison under a roof that a woman of thirty-three put back on. It records. It warns. It hears cases. There is no fourth word and there is no room, and the whole of what it does is those three, and about four of you have been waiting seventeen days for somebody to say that in a mouth.*”

Then a boy of thirteen kept a count of the case out loud and gave the count, and said what it was and was not, and said that nobody was thanked for it, including the man who carried the paper.

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and ninety-five days, and the figure is one more than the morning before, and he counted it in the lane before the light came up.

“*A hundred and ninety-five days, and it is one more than the morning before, and I counted it in the lane before the light came up.*”

“*Here is my count for the morning and it is a figure of hours and not of anything else. Three hours and a bit, and about four of them talked and a man of sixty-one did most of it, and the man of thirty-one spoke for about a minute and a half of the whole three hours.*”

“*And a man of about fifty-two named a thirteenth thing he cannot check this morning, and it was a woman. I am not going to say which woman, and I am not going to pretend that a figure of thirteen is better than a figure of twelve.*”

“*And nobody is thanked. Not the man who walked nine miles, and not the man who carried the mortar, and not the man of thirty-one who wrote a disagreement in a book. A book with a disagreement in it is the ordinary Tuesday of this county and I would like it said like that.*”

About nine of them were on that landing at about the fifth hour of a Thursday with a full bank holding over the top step. A roof and eleven steps were being mended in Orison, a budget was set in a room with a door shut, and a man’s dead son was written down as a disagreement in a book in a barn.

---

The fever was eighty weeks and five days old. He had been in that county two hundred and ninety-four days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and sixty-three days old, and a hundred and forty-eight days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

A man of thirty-one carried mortar up the steps of the Orison hearing hall for four hours and was not in charge of it, and said that it was the first morning of his two hundred and ninety-four days that he had been given a job by somebody who could have sent him away, and agreed with a woman of twenty-eight that it was not a reward.

A man of thirty-nine and a boy of nineteen argued the first budget over a horse, paper and the copying of eleven lines, and the boy refused the route on the ground that the four places on it had not been asked, which he was allowed to do and which he had never before been allowed to do.
A man of about fifty-two named the thing that translates in one mouth and said what it is and is not, and that it is not a god, it is not arriving, and it is not spoken by.

A case was heard in a barn for three hours about two men with the same name, and a day on a sheet that is the day the wrong one was buried. The finding was that the day stays and the disagreement goes in a book.
Nobody in that barn said one word about the arrangement, and a boy of thirteen counted the hours and said nobody was thanked, not the man who walked nine miles and not the man who carried the mortar.
