# Chapter 829: Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Fortieth Week, And A Saturday, And Twelve Inches And Three Days Of The Coming Back With The Bank Full Tomorrow, And The Fever Is Seventy-Eight Weeks Old Exactly, And A Man Of Forty-Four Says The Road Is The Thing To Argue About And A Man Of Thirty-One Says His Own Standing Is The Thing To Argue About, And About Nine Of Them Are Asked Which Of The Two Matters More And Nobody Answers

Fifth day of the hundred and fortieth week. His five hundred and seventy-ninth morning. Four hundred and twenty-nine days after the settlement. The fever seventy-eight weeks old. Three hundred and eighty-three days since the division. Three hundred and twenty-nine days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about twelve inches over the top step at about the seventh hour and had been since the middle of the night. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working, and gave the figure for the fever out loud before about nine of them had worked it out, and said why on a Saturday, and gave the figure for the next time.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. Three things every morning, and he has given the third whole number of weeks of the fever out loud twice now, and both times it was a Saturday, and about four of you noticed that both times.

“*About twelve inches at the seventh hour, and it is three days of the coming back, and about seventy of those ninety steps are under this morning.*”

“*And it will be a full bank tomorrow at the seventh hour, and a bank that is full holds at sixteen until it comes off.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in five days, and that is five days after the morning it is full, and the morning it is full is tomorrow.*”

“*And here is a figure, and I am giving it before any of you have worked it out, because I did it on Friday night and I have been sitting on it since.*”

“*Seventy-eight weeks. Five hundred and forty-six days.*”

“*That is eight hundred and twenty-nine less two hundred and eighty-three, and it comes out at seventy-eight weeks and no days and no half.*”

“*And that is the third whole number of weeks this bank has had since the fever started.*”

“*The first two were on the fifth day of the hundred and thirty-eighth week and on the fifth day of the hundred and thirty-ninth week, and about four of you have been counting them since Wednesday and I have watched you do it.*”

Then a man of forty-four said the thing they ought to be arguing about is the road, and a man of thirty-one said the thing they ought to be arguing about is his own standing, and neither of them would take the other’s.

Tomas Elrick is forty-four and pushed a handcart up that cart road for twenty-six years, and its near shaft let go of its socket on the Saturday of the hundred and thirty-sixth week. Two lengths of ash, a wedge and an old nail are still in that socket and were put there on the first day of the hundred and fortieth week. On the Saturday of the hundred and thirty-eighth week he said it would be easier and safer if there was one person who could say yes to that road, and that it was not about the nine of them standing there.

“*The thing we ought to be arguing about is the road.*”

“*Not the mark, and not the child, and not the fourth leaf, and not a man of thirty-one’s standing in a room four hundred miles off.*”

“*Four houses at that low gate have had nothing come down that road since the Tuesday before a man of fifty-eight walked down it and stood on this bank and said so.*”

“*It is eleven years. A floor is lime and gravel and cannot be laid with lime alone.*”

“*And if about nine of us spend a week on a man of thirty-one and none of it on a floor, then at the end of the week the floor is exactly where it is, and about nine of us will have spent a week.*”

“*I do not know a cleaner way of losing.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said no, and said why, and said that the man of forty-four had just made the argument for him.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and seventy-five days, two hundred and seventy-five being eight hundred and twenty-nine less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and forty-four days old, a hundred and forty-four being eight hundred and twenty-nine less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*No, and I am going to say why, and a man of forty-four has just made my argument for me and does not know it.*”

“*He has said that if about nine of us spend a week on a man of thirty-one and none of it on a floor, then at the end of the week the floor is exactly where it is.*”

“*And that is true of the floor. And it is also true of me.*”

“*And it is the reason I am not going to be the man we spend the week on, and I would rather say that at about the ninth hour on a Saturday than have about four of you work it out by Wednesday.*”

“*Here is the want, and it is the fifth shape in five days, and I am going to give it to you whole.*”

“*A woman of twenty-eight told me on Tuesday that about four of you are waiting for me to change my mind, and I am not going to keep making you wait for a shape.*”

“*I want to stand on this bank and be worth about nine of you listening to, for a different reason than the one I have got.*”

“*The mistake is the thirty-sixth, and I am not numbering it.*”

“*It is the one a woman of twenty-nine in a reed named on Friday in about nine ears, and I am not going to get away with it by saying it in a different order.*”

“*A hundred and twenty-nine days. A hundred and twenty-nine is eight hundred and twenty-nine less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer.*”

Then a woman of twenty-eight said she was going to be asked something and had been asked it twice already by about four people and had said no both times, and said what the question was.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from. She said her own name on that bank on the Tuesday of the hundred and thirty-ninth week, having come up four miles to say it. She is at a hundred and eighty-seven days, and the figure is eight hundred and twenty-nine less six hundred and forty-two.

“*A hundred and eighty-seven days, and that is eight hundred and twenty-nine less six hundred and forty-two.*”

“*There is a question going round this bank. About four of you have asked it and two of you have asked me twice, and I have said no twice.*”

“*And I am going to say what it is, so that about nine of you stop asking it in about nine ears.*”

“*The question is this. If a man of thirty-one’s standing is the thing that has to be argued about, and the truth of the crisis is the thing that has to be answered, then which of the two is it better to lose.*”

“*And I am not going to answer it, and I am not going to be asked it again this week by anybody, including him.*”

“*I said on the second day of the hundred and fortieth week that I had two things to look at, and that both had been given to me so that about four of you could check them.*”

“*I looked at the second one on the second day of the hundred and fortieth week. I have not said the answer and I am not going to say it on a Saturday morning in front of about nine of you.*”

Then about nine of them were asked, out loud, in about nine ears, which of the two matters more, and nobody answered, and a man of thirty-one said what that was.

“*About nine of you have just been asked, and about nine of you have said nothing, and I want the nothing said out loud.*”

“*I am going to name it, because a man of fifty-four said on Monday that the reason he asks is so that he can be wrong in his own mouth.*”

“*And a woman of twenty-nine said on Thursday that us is four people and is not going to be five.*”

“*The nothing is not agreement. I have watched about nine of you not answer things for a week, and I know what not-answering looks like on a face.*”

“*The nothing is that nobody on this bank has standing to weigh the two.*”

“*And that is the true of it, and it is also the most convenient answer available to every one of us, and about four of you knew that before I said it.*”

“*And I am not going to settle it this morning, and I am not going to ask it again until somebody who is not me asks it.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man of forty-four who said the road is the thing to argue about, and who has been arguing about a road for eleven years, and who has not been thanked for that on any morning of the last eleven years.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man of thirty-one who said his own standing is the thing to argue about, and who said a man of forty-four had just made that argument for him.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a woman of twenty-eight who said the question is going round and has said no to being asked it three times, and who has not answered it in front of about nine of them.*”

“*And nobody is to be told that about nine of them saying nothing was brave, or that a man of thirty-one naming it was brave.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for naming it.*”

“*And the right of refusal is not restored this morning, and a full bank at sixteen is not it, and neither is a man of thirty-one giving up his standing, and I am not going to say that either of those is it.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Saturday with sixteen inches of water over the top step above them, and had been asked which of two things matters more, and had said nothing, and a man of thirty-one had said the nothing was not agreement and that nobody on that bank had standing to weigh the two, and that it was the most convenient answer available to every one of them.

---

The fever was seventy-eight weeks old exactly. He had been in that county two hundred and seventy-five days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and forty-four days old, and a hundred and twenty-nine days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

A man of forty-four said the road is the thing to argue about and said that a week spent on a man of thirty-one leaves a floor exactly where it is, and a man of thirty-one said that was also true of himself and that he would rather say so on Saturday than have it worked out by Wednesday.
And a woman of twenty-eight said the question had been going round that bank, had been asked of her twice, and that she had said no twice, and named it.

About nine of them were asked which of the two matters more, and nobody answered, and a man of thirty-one said out loud that the not-answering was not agreement, and that nobody there had standing to weigh the two, and that it was the most convenient answer available to every one of them.
And he said he is not going to ask it again until somebody who is not him asks it.
