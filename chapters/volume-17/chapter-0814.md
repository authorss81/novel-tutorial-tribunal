# Chapter 814: Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Thirty-Eighth Week, And A Friday, And A Woman Of Sixty-Four Who Came Up That Bank On Her Own Two Feet Says She Will Speak And Gives A Condition And Gives One Part Of Nineteen Years Away And Does Not Give The Part She Was Asked For, And A Woman Of Forty-Four Had Four Names On A Piece Of Paper That Nobody Asked Her For And Put It In Her Apron

Fourth day of the hundred and thirty-eighth week. His five hundred and sixty-fourth morning. Four hundred and fourteen days after the settlement. The fever seventy-five weeks and six days. Three hundred and sixty-eight days since the division. Three hundred and fourteen days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working and said the two things that follow from a full bank, and about nine of them did not write the second one down because he said it on Tuesday.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. Three things every morning, and he gives them at about the seventh hour and not at any other. He has not asked about the stone he named on the Sunday of the week before last.
