# Chapter 836: Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Forty-First Week, And A Saturday, And Eight Inches Of Water And Two Days Of The Coming Back, And The Fever Seventy-Nine Weeks Old Exactly, And The Comparison Is The Cost, And A Bridge Goes Down About Two Feet Onto A Man Who Went In Alone At The Sixth Hour Because He Is The One Who Checks

Fifth day of the hundred and forty-first week. His five hundred and eighty-sixth morning. Four hundred and thirty-six days after the settlement. The fever seventy-nine weeks old. Three hundred and ninety days since the division. Three hundred and thirty-six days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about eight inches over the top step at about the seventh hour and it was two days of the coming back. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working and the figure for the next time, and then gave the figure for the fever out loud before about nine of them had worked it out, and said why on a Saturday, and said he was not going to make a rule of it.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He has not given the figure of how many of those ninety steps are under since Monday. He has given a whole number of weeks of the fever out loud twice before, and both times it was a Saturday, and about four of them have been counting the Saturdays as well as the weeks.

“*About eight inches at the seventh hour, and it is two days of the coming back, and about ninety of those steps are dry at the sixth hour and are dry still.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in six days, and that is six days forward from this morning with nothing counting down, and a boy of thirteen does not like that and he is right not to like it.*”

“*And here is a figure, and I am giving it before any of you have worked it out, because I worked it out on Friday night and I have been sitting on it since.*”

“*Seventy-nine weeks. Five hundred and fifty-three days.*”

“*That is eight hundred and thirty-six less two hundred and eighty-three, and it comes out at seventy-nine weeks and no days and no half.*”

“*That is the third time I have given a whole number of weeks out loud on this bank. It has been a Saturday every time, and I have not made a rule of it, and I am not going to start on a Saturday.*”

Then a man of about fifty-two went into a drain at about the sixth hour before the light and put a rod down through a silted sill, and a bridge came down about two feet, and a man of thirty-four in an ash said what that cost him before anybody asked him to say it.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has a book of heads and carts and two numbers in it in eleven years. He read all four leaves of a thing on a flat in about nine ears on three mornings, one leaf a morning, and the third figure of the fourth leaf is a figure of the bed of a drain at an abutment.

“*Four foot six below the top of the sill. That is what is written, and I read it on the Friday at the seventh hour and about four of you heard it and about four of you have not thought about it since.*”

“*And I am not going to say what I think of it. I am sixty-one and I read leaves and I do not stand in drains.*”

Tomas Elrick is forty-four and pushed a handcart up that cart road for twenty-six years, and he went down to that bridge at about the seventh hour with two of the people who were going to dig there and had not yet started, and he found the sill clear and the drain empty of people, and he came back up and said so in about nine ears.

“*Nobody in the drain. Nobody on the deck. And I said last night that that deck is not to be walked, and I said it in about nine of your ears, and about four of you heard me and about four of you did not.*”

“*A man of about fifty-two put that rod in at about the sixth hour this morning, before the light, on his own, and he has told me why and I am not going to argue with him about it in front of about nine of you.*”

“*The bed is down. That is the short of it and the man of thirty-one has already said the rest of it badly and I am going to say it in a different order.*”

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well. He put a mark on one of two sheets of deal, four lines under him, eleven years ago, because he was the only one of nine who was not asked what the line was for. He has named six things he cannot check. He went into that drain at about the sixth hour this morning and came out of it at about the seventh.

“*I went at the sixth because the water comes back at four inches a day and the drain has to be clear before it is under, and about nine of us agreed last night that it would be at the seventh.*”

“*And I want that said in about nine ears before anybody works out that I did not wait. I did not wait, and I have told you why.*”

“*I am the one who checks. And about nine of you have not worked out that checking and going are two different things, and I have not told you that until this morning because I have been the one who checks for a week and it has been convenient for everybody.*”

“*Here is what the rod found. The bed is at six foot six below the top of that sill. It is written as four foot six.*”

“*Four foot six and two foot is six foot six. That is the whole of the working and a man of about fifty-two can do it in his head, and a man of about fifty-two has just done it in his head with a wet arm.*”

“*And that sill is two feet deep. There is two feet of air under the toe of a two-foot timber and it has been standing there on nothing for long enough that the silt had grown over the gap.*”

Nobody on that bridge said anything for about a minute. Then a man of thirty-four in an ash said what the drain had cost, in about nine ears, and he said it about nine feet off a man of thirty-one and did not soften it.

Ivo Kellow is thirty-four and has been in an ash about twenty feet off the low side of that drain, a hundred yards along it, for two hundred and six days, and he has a day against his name which is not a day of this week. He came up that bank unasked for the fifth time this week and said what he said on Friday was going to happen.

“*Two hundred and six days. That is eight hundred and thirty-six less six hundred and thirty.*”

“*On Friday at the seventh hour I told about nine of you that I was going to be standing in the water when that bridge was found out about, and I am not standing in the water, and I said that in a wrong order and I am not going to improve on it.*”

“*Here is the cost and it is mine and it is said in my own mouth.*”

“*The drain is my remedy. It is a woman of twenty-nine’s remedy too, and she gave me half of it on the second day of this week, and she has not taken it back.*”

“*And the drain is the thing that found that bridge out. Not the rain and not the water and not a person coming in the night. Eighteen inches deeper for about nine hundred yards, and the toe of that sill went.*”

“*I asked for the drain on the seventh day of last week and I asked for it again on the fourth day of this week, at about nine of your ears, twice, and I did not think about the bridge once.*”

“*Not once. And I want that said in about nine of your ears and I want it said on Saturday and not discovered on Monday by a man of thirty-one with a figure in his mouth.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said what the comparison had found, and that the local remedy did not survive it, and that it did not survive because the other one was correct on a figure from a long time ago, and a man of about fifty-two said what his hand was and refused to let anybody make it into anything.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and eighty-two days, two hundred and eighty-two being eight hundred and thirty-six less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and fifty-one days old, a hundred and fifty-one being eight hundred and thirty-six less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*The comparison is finished and here is what it found, and I am going to say it in the wrong order first because that is what I do and about four of you have stopped forgiving me for it.*”

“*The local remedy survives.*”

“*No. That was the wrong order. The local remedy does not survive being compared, and it does not survive because the other one is right.*”

“*The arrangement can answer that bridge in nine days. And it will answer it on four foot six, because four foot six is the figure it was given, and it will build a sill two feet deep on a bed that is not there.*”

“*And to build a sill on a bed that is not there, you fill the bed up. You put about nine hundred yards of drain back to the level the old figure says it was.*”

“*And if the drain goes back up, the water stands in a reed again, and the reed is a woman of twenty-nine’s, and the ash is mine, and the answer to the bridge is the un-doing of the answer to the reed.*”

“*That is what a precedent is when you are standing in it. It is not a person deciding anything. It is a figure being right, this week, about a Tuesday eleven years ago.*”

Then a woman of twenty-nine in a reed gave her own count, and said what the comparison had done to her half of it, and said she was not going to argue about the bridge with a man of thirty-four who had already said it in a better order than he has ever said anything.

Marda Vell is twenty-nine and has been in a reed about twenty feet off the low side of that drain, a hundred yards along it, for two hundred and seven days, and she can read, and she has said that us is four people.

“*Two hundred and seven days. That is eight hundred and thirty-six less six hundred and twenty-nine.*”

“*I gave him half of it on the second day of this week and I have not taken it back and I am not going to, and that is not generous, it is arithmetic.*”

“*A drain that is deeper here and not deeper there is not a drain. It is a pond with a hole at the end of it, and I knew that on the second day of this week, and I have not said it out loud since, because a man of thirty-four was saying what he was going to lose and I was not going to be in about nine ears twice in one morning.*”

“*So the deep part is at the top and the shallow part is at the bridge, and the water goes to the shallow part, and it always did, and I knew it and I asked for it anyway.*”

“*And I am not going to argue with him about the bridge. He said it at the seventh hour on Friday and he has said it better today than he has said anything all week, and about four of you noticed and about four of you did not.*”

Then a man of about fifty-two said what his hand was, and said it was a palm, and said it was not going to be made into anything, and a man of thirty-one said the whole of what was left of the morning.

“*It is my right hand. Palm and forearm, and the palm is the worst of it and the forearm is the part I cannot make go away by not looking at it.*”

“*It is a palm. That is all it is. Do not make it into anything.*”

“*A man of thirty-one carries a cut across the same hand, and it is a hundred and fifty-one days old, and he gives that figure out loud every single morning.*”

“*And about four of you have just put the two of us together, and I can see you doing it.*”

“*Do not. That palm is not going to be the second thing that happens to it on this bank.*”

“*And I am not going to be a lesson, and there is nothing owing to me for it either.*”

“*And I am not going to be asked how it happened in about nine of your ears this morning. I have said how it happened. It was a bridge, at the sixth hour, on my own.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said what he had wanted, what he had got wrong, and what it had cost, and gave the figure for the standing offer in his own mouth with the subtraction in it, and said that nobody on that bank had lost anything this week by saying no to anything.

“*Here is the want and it has not moved in five days and I am not going to pretend it has.*”

“*I want a person to put that rod in. I said on Friday that I was not going to be the one, and I was not the one, and I was also not the one who said out loud that a man of about fifty-two had to go.*”

“*Here is the mistake and it is the thirty-eighth of the same mistake and I am not numbering it.*”

“*I decided last night that it would be at the seventh hour. I decided it, I did not say it out loud, and a man of about fifty-two was in a drain an hour before that because of it.*”

“*That is the whole of the mistake, and it is the same fence a man of about fifty-two named on the third day of last week, and I have been standing behind it for five days while calling it mine.*”

“*And the cost is a man of thirty-four with two feet of air under a sill and a woman of twenty-nine with a pond with a hole at the end of it, and both of them knew on Friday, and neither of them said the word to me first.*”

“*A hundred and thirty-six days. A hundred and thirty-six is eight hundred and thirty-six less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer, and a bridge going into eight inches of water at the seventh hour on a Saturday is not an answer either.*”

“*And I want it said in about nine ears, because a woman of sixty-four taught me that in this week. Nobody on this bank has said no to anything this week. Nobody on this bank has lost anything this week for saying no to anything.*”

“*A man of thirty-one has a cut across his palm and it is a hundred and fifty-one days old and it came from a thing that happened two hundred and eighty-two days ago, and it is not from this week, and I am saying so because about four of you have been quietly putting it next to a bridge.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man of about fifty-two who went into a drain alone at the sixth hour and got two feet of air and a palm, and who has said it is a palm and not anything.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man of thirty-four in an ash who said in about nine ears that he had asked for the drain twice and had not thought about the bridge once.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a woman of twenty-nine in a reed who said she had known since the second day of this week that the deep part would send the water to the shallow part, and who asked for it anyway.*”

“*And nobody is to be told that any of the three of them was brave about it. A man of about fifty-two was in a drain. That is a place, and a place is not a virtue, and I am not going to stand here and turn a wet arm into a thing to be admired.*”

“*And the right of refusal is not restored this morning. A disclosed zone is not it. A lime line is not it, a precedent is not it, and a man of thirty-four saying I did not think about the bridge once is not it either, and I said that this morning at the seventh hour and I am saying it at the eleventh.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Saturday, with eight inches of water over the top step above them, and a bridge four hundred yards from an ash lying in about eight inches of rising water with a sill in it and two feet of air under the toe.
A man of about fifty-two was on the top step with a palm and a forearm. A man of thirty-four in an ash had said out loud that the drain he asked for twice was the thing that found it out.

The fever was seventy-nine weeks old, five hundred and fifty-three days of it, with no days and no half over. He had been in that county two hundred and eighty-two days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and fifty-one days old, and a hundred and thirty-six days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

A man of about fifty-two went into a drain at about the sixth hour before the light, on his own, and put a rod down through a sill that had silt grown over the gap under it, and found the bed at six foot six where a figure from eleven years ago says four foot six.
And a bridge came down about two feet, and a man of thirty-four in an ash said in about nine ears that the drain he asked for twice was the thing that found it out and that he had not thought about the bridge once.

And he said that to build a sill on a bed that is not there, you have to fill the drain back up to where the old figure says it was.
