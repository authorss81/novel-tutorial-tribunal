# Chapter 844: Sixth Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Second Week, And A Sunday, And Eight Inches And Two Days Of The Coming Back And The Next Time Is In Six Days, And The Third Of The Three Is Put To A Person Who Agrees To Have Her Own Protection Looked At With The Cost Named Before The Question Was Asked And It Is Not The Man Of Thirty-One Who Asks, And The Answer Comes Back No

Sixth day of the hundred and forty-second week. His five hundred and ninety-fourth morning. Four hundred and forty-four days after the settlement. The fever eighty weeks and one day. Three hundred and ninety-eight days since the division. Three hundred and forty-four days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about eight inches over the top step at about the seventh hour and it was two days of the coming back. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working and the figure for the next time, and said that eight days ago he had given no figure of inches at all because there were none, and that a bank coming back at four inches a day is the most predictable thing on that landing, and that he has never once said that out loud in nineteen years.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and gives three things every morning. The fourth thing is a figure of how many of those ninety steps are under, and it has been nothing for twelve days.

“*About eight inches at the seventh hour, and it is two days of the coming back, and it is four inches higher than it was yesterday at the same hour, and I said on Saturday that I would not count the steps and I am not going to count them today either.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in six days, and that is the fifth day of the hundred and forty-third week. Six days forward with nothing counting down to it, and I do not think a boy of thirteen likes that, and I have not asked him.*”

Then at about the tenth hour a woman of twenty-nine who lives in a reed about twenty feet off the low side of that drain asked a question on a Sunday, and said out loud whose protection it was. She said she was not the arrangement, and that she has never been in a room four hundred miles off in her life.

Marda Vell is twenty-nine and has been in a reed about twenty feet off the low side of that landing, a hundred yards along the same drain, for two hundred and fifteen days, and the figure is eight hundred and forty-four less six hundred and twenty-nine. She can read, and she has said that us is four people, and she is one of the four and has said so.

“*I am going to ask this in the ordinary voice and about nine of you are going to hear it, and I am not going to ask it to the arrangement because the arrangement is four hundred miles off and does not come when it is asked.*”

“*There is a third of the three that a man of thirty-one named out loud on the seventh day of this week, and he said it was a claim and not an assignment. I have been carrying it for four days, and I am putting it to somebody today, because I have not carried anything this long without wanting it done.*”

“*The first two are done. A string and a bridge, and about fifteen yards and about four hundred, and both of them said no and both of them were right. This is the third, and it is not a string and it is not a bridge. It is this: whether a remedy that belongs to a person can stand in the same place as the answer of an arrangement.*”

“*A gap. A name in ink with no day against it. A day that nobody in this county can be sent for. Those are the three and I have said all three out loud and I am not going to pretend they are one thing.*”

Then a woman of sixty-four said the cost before anybody asked her a question, and said that the order of those two things was the whole of what the test was, and refused to be thanked for it and refused to be called brave in the same breath.

Ada Renk is sixty-four and has a name in ink on the ninth line of a book four hundred miles off and has never had a day against it. She has said a word twice in nineteen years and has refused nine people who asked her to say it a third, and she has given those nineteen years away three times and is not going to do it a fourth.

“*A woman of twenty-nine has put a thing to a person and she has not asked me anything yet, and I am going to answer before she asks, because that is the only part of this I care about.*”

“*The cost. Here it is, in about nine of your ears, and it is mine and not hers and she is not going to be able to argue me down off it.*”

“*For nineteen years the reason that nobody can be sent for me is that the arrangement cannot work me out. A name with no day against it is not a person it can compute anything about. It is a name, sitting there, with nothing either side of it, and a thing that cannot compute you cannot be sent for you.*”

“*And if about nine of you look at that in the open this morning, then it has been looked at. It will have been said out loud and it will have been written on a stone, and a thing nine people know is a thing that can be carried to a room four hundred miles off by one person who heard it from another.*”

“*So the cost is this and it is not small. I am trading a protection that only works because nobody understands it, for an answer that works because everybody does. And I cannot have both of those, and I am not going to be asked to have both of them.*”

“*And say the order out loud, because a man of thirty-one wrote it down on the Monday of last week and about four of you have not read it since. The cost is named before the question is asked. If anybody in about nine of your ears thinks the cost was named after, they are to say so now and not on Wednesday.*”

“*And here is the agreeing, and I am going to say it as plainly as I know how, because about four of you have been waiting for a woman of sixty-four to be brave and I am not going to do that.*”

“*I agree to have my own protection looked at. Not looked for, not looked into, and not looked at by a room. Looked at, here, on this bank, in about nine of your ears, and written on a stone, with the cost said out loud first.*”

Then a boy of thirteen said that the order had been kept, and said what he had done about it, and refused to be the one who said the woman of twenty-nine had done it well.

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and ninety-one days, and the figure is one more than the morning before, and he counted it in the lane before the light came up.

“*A hundred and ninety-one days, and it is one more than the morning before, and I counted it in the lane before the light came up.*”

“*The order was kept. That is all. The cost was named at about the tenth hour and nobody had asked anything before the tenth hour, and I have been sitting here since the ninth hour making sure of it, because a boy of thirteen is the only person on this landing with nothing else to do on a Sunday.*”

“*And I am not going to say she did it well. She said no three times on the seventh day of this week when a man of thirty-one handed her something and she was right and I have written down that she was right, and doing that again is not a thing I am going to be noticed for.*”

Then a man of about fifty-two put the three remedies on the flat stone in chalk and had a boy of thirteen guide his hand, and asked the three questions out loud, and gave the working, and named the thing he could not do.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well, and has a palm and a forearm, and has named nine things he cannot check and a tenth on this morning, and he cannot see the edge of a stone well enough to write on it without a hand on his.

“*I am going to put three things on this stone and ask three questions about them, and I am going to be bad at the writing, and a boy of thirteen is going to tell me where the edge is, and I am going to say that out loud so that nobody afterwards can say I did not know.*”

“*The gap. The name in ink with no day. The day that nobody in this county can be sent for. That is three remedies and none of them is mine, and one of them is a number, and I am not going to add them up this morning either.*”

“*Question one. Can the arrangement answer the ground underneath the gap? Yes. It can, and a man of sixty-one read that out of a bench’s own page a fortnight ago, and it said that the interval was two hundred and six days, and that the answer would be the arrangement’s own.*”

“*Question two. Can the arrangement answer the ground underneath a name in ink with no day? No, and here is the working. The moment it answers, it has to put the answer somewhere, and the only somewhere it has is the name, and the moment a thing is put next to a name it is a day against a name, and that is the whole of what a day is.*”

“*Question three, and this is the one that matters. Can the two stand in the same place? No. And the working for that is question two, and I am not going to give you a second working because there is not one.*”

“*And here is my tenth thing I cannot check, and it is the one I mind most. I cannot check whether any of the three of them is still protecting anybody. I can put a line under the arithmetic and I cannot put a line under whether it works on a person, and I have known that since the Saturday of this week and I have not said it in about nine of your ears until now.*”

Then a man of thirty-four who lives in an ash a hundred yards along the same drain said one thing, and it was the only thing he said, and he gave the figure for his own days out loud with the subtraction in it, and nobody asked him about a bridge.

Ivo Kellow is thirty-four and has been in an ash about a hundred yards along the low side of that landing, on the same side as the reed, for two hundred and fourteen days, and the figure is eight hundred and forty-four less six hundred and thirty. He has not asked this landing for anything in fifty mornings, and he was not asked about the bridge this morning.

“*I am the man in the third remedy, or I am the man the third one was written about, and I do not know which and I have not worked out which and I am not going to pretend to about nine of you that I have.*”

“*Two hundred and fourteen days. That is eight hundred and forty-four less six hundred and thirty, and I am saying it because a man of about fifty-two has said out loud that a thing on a stone is not a person. A person in an ash is not a thing on a stone, and I would like both figures on this landing this morning.*”

“*And I am not going to say anything else. About four of you have been waiting for me to say what the third one means for a bridge four hundred yards from where I am standing, and I have not worked it out, and I would rather say that than say a thing that sounds like working it out.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the whole of what the test had cost and what it had found, in his own mouth, on a bank, in about nine ears, and said that he was not the one who had asked and had said so a fortnight ago, and gave the figure for the standing offer with the subtraction in it.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and ninety days, two hundred and ninety being eight hundred and forty-four less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and fifty-nine days old, a hundred and fifty-nine being eight hundred and forty-four less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*On the seventh day of this week I named this third one and said it would not be me asking. A woman of twenty-nine asked it. I am going to say the working of that in about nine of your ears, because I would like it written down that a man of thirty-one said a thing out loud and then did not do the thing he had said.*”

“*And I am not going to take the finding and make it tidy. The finding is no. A remedy that belongs to a person cannot stand in the same place as the answer of an arrangement, and the working for it is four words long, and the four words are that a day is a day.*”

“*That is not a settlement and it is not a defeat, and it settles nothing for a woman of sixty-four. She has a name in ink on the ninth line and she still has no day against it this morning. She told us the cost before she was asked, and she is not going to be thanked for it.*”

“*Here is what it did do, and it is a small thing and I am going to say it anyway. A man of about fifty-two can now put a line under three of the four of these. A boy of thirteen can check the two he wrote on a stone this morning with his own hand. About four of you have been carrying a question for a fortnight, and it turns out to have a four-word answer.*”

“*A hundred and forty-four days. The offer asked at the seven-hundredth is a hundred and forty-four days old, and a hundred and forty-four is eight hundred and forty-four less seven hundred. None of this morning is an answer to it, and I am not going to let a woman of sixty-four pay a fourth time for somebody else’s morning.*”

About nine of them were on that landing at about the twelfth hour of a Sunday with two days of the water coming back, and three remedies written on a stone in chalk, and a cost that was named before the question was asked, and an answer of four words that settled nothing and cost a woman of sixty-four the only thing she had left to trade.

The fever was eighty weeks and one day old. He had been in that county two hundred and ninety days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and fifty-nine days old, and a hundred and forty-four days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

The third of the three counter-tests was put on a Sunday, by a woman of twenty-nine and not by the man who had named it, to a woman of sixty-four who agreed to have her own protection looked at and named the cost before anybody asked her anything.
The cost was that a protection which only works because nobody understands it is traded for an answer that works because everybody does, and that she cannot have both.

A man of about fifty-two wrote three remedies on a stone in chalk with a boy of thirteen guiding his hand, and worked out that a day is a day, and found that a remedy belonging to a person cannot stand in the same place as the answer of an arrangement.
A man of thirty-four in an ash gave his figure out loud and said he had not worked out what it meant for a bridge, and a man of thirty-one said the finding is not a settlement and is not a defeat and that the answer of four words settled nothing for anybody.
