# Chapter 835: Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Forty-First Week, And A Friday, And Four Inches Of Water Back On The Flat And Not On The Steps, And The Second Thing Is Set Out, And A Man Of About Fifty-Two Asks For A Working To Be Read Again And Says What A Precedent Is, And A Man Of Thirty-Eight Says He Is Still Not Opening It

Fourth day of the hundred and forty-first week. His five hundred and eighty-fifth morning. Four hundred and thirty-five days after the settlement. The fever seventy-eight weeks and six days. Three hundred and eighty-nine days since the division. Three hundred and thirty-five days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about four inches over the top step at about the seventh hour and it was one day of the coming back, and the water was on the flat and not on the steps. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working and the figure for the next time, and said that the count down was finished and what came after it was a different kind of figure.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He has not given the figure of how many of those ninety steps are under since Monday, and he gave the figure for the next time on Wednesday by counting down to this morning, and that count is over.

“*About four inches at the seventh hour, and it is one day of the coming back, and all ninety of those steps were dry at the sixth hour and are dry now.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in seven days, and that is the fourth day of the hundred and forty-second week, and there is nothing to count down to.*”

“*I gave you three days on Monday and two on Tuesday and one on Wednesday, and that was a count down to this morning, and it was right three times.*”

“*From here it is a full bank that has not happened yet. Four days to reach it, and four days after that before the water comes off, and that is seven.*”

“*I have not had to look at anything to work it out, and neither can about four of you.*”

Then a boy of thirteen said what he did not like about a figure that has stopped counting down, and asked the man of forty-three for a figure he has not got, and did not ask twice.

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and eighty-two days, and he has counted them in a lane. He has been keeping a count of his own since Monday that is not his days and is not his eight.

“*A hundred and eighty-two days, and it is one more than the morning before, and I counted it in the lane before the light came up.*”

“*Here is my other count and it started on Monday because a man of forty-three gave me a number to hold.*”

“*Three on Monday, two on Tuesday, one on Wednesday, and this morning there is nothing to hold, and I am not going to pretend I am still holding three.*”

“*I do not like a figure that has stopped counting down. It means from this morning the number only goes one way and I cannot tell from the number whether it is close or far, and that is the first figure on this bank I have not been able to check by watching it.*”

“*And I asked him what it is on the morning the bank is full again and he said he did not know, and I am not going to ask him twice, and I am writing it in the lane that he said it and not what it is.*”

Then a man of thirty-eight said he was still not opening what is in the inside of his coat. He gave the reason he had not given on Thursday, and said the reason was not the same reason twice.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine. He has a board in the inside of his coat with thirty-two notches cut in the edge of it, and it is not opened. He said on the third day of last week that he was not going to give the answer to a boy of thirteen. He heard the difference between that and no, and said he had noticed it himself.

“*I said on the third day of last week that it was not this week. That was true on the day I said it.*”

“*It is not true today and I can tell the difference, and about four of you have been waiting since Thursday to see whether I could, and I can, and I am not going to pretend the reason is the same reason twice.*”

“*Here is the reason I did not give on Thursday. It is not that I do not want to. It is that I do not know what I would do with it. I have known that since the Saturday of the hundred and thirty-ninth week.*”

“*I did not say it then because I had not found the words. I have found them.*”

“*A boy of thirteen asked me on the fourth day of last week how a mark gets made. He got an answer from a woman of twenty-eight in a day, and he got part of an answer from a man at a low gate on Monday, and neither of them came out of this coat.*”

“*And if I open it on Monday and it is nothing, then I have been carrying nothing for a long time.*”

“*And I will have told about nine of you on the third day of last week that I was the one who might have the answer.*”

“*And if it is not nothing, then I will have to decide something by Monday, and I do not know what I would decide.*”

“*About four of you know what I am talking about and about four of you do not.*”

“*I am not asking for it to be taken off me. I am saying I have not got the part where I would know what to do, and that has been the truth since the third day of last week, and I have been saying a different thing.*”

Then the second of them was set out, and it was not a hearing, and a woman of forty-four said so again before anybody started, and a man of thirty-four in an ash said what the comparison is going to cost before it has been done.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept twenty-nine chairs at thirteen shillings and fourpence a year for nineteen years. She said on Monday that about nine of them standing on a bank with one of them right is a room with a door in it.

“*Before anybody starts. It is not a hearing and it is not a second hearing.*”

“*There is a thing to be compared and there is a rod and there is a drain, and about four of you are going to want to sit down.*”

“*Sit down if you like. I have kept chairs for nineteen years and I am telling you that the ones on this bank are the worst chairs in this county and that is not a figure of anybody either.*”

Ivo Kellow is thirty-four and has been in an ash about twenty feet off the low side of that drain, a hundred yards along it, for two hundred and five days, and he has a day against his name which is not a day of this week. He came up that bank unasked for the fourth time this week and said the cost before the comparison, not after.

“*Two hundred and five days. That is eight hundred and thirty-five less six hundred and thirty.*”

“*Here is what I am going to lose, and I am saying it before the rod goes in, because a woman of sixty-four told me in this same week to do that with a thing like this.*”

“*I want that drain deeper. It is my remedy and a woman of twenty-nine’s remedy and it is the only thing in this county anybody has ever offered me that was not a person telling me to be careful.*”

“*And a bridge is four hundred yards from where I am standing, and a bridge stands in a drain’s bed, and every man on that bank knows what a drain is doing eighteen inches deeper.*”

“*So I am going to be the one who is standing in the water when that bridge is found out about.*”

“*I would like about nine of you to have heard me say that at the seventh hour on a Friday, and not at the tenth hour on a Saturday.*”

“*And I am not going to say what I will do about it, because I have not decided, and a man of thirty-one will tell you that deciding in advance is a thing he does and not a thing I do.*”

Then a man of about fifty-two asked for a working at the foot of the fourth leaf to be read out loud a second time. It was read. He gave the figure in it and said what a precedent is, and a man of sixty-one read it in the ordinary voice and did not improve it.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well. He put a mark on one of two sheets of deal, four lines under him, eleven years ago, because he was the only one of nine who was not asked what the line was for. He has checked figures out loud on four mornings this week and named a fifth thing he cannot check on Friday.

“*A man of sixty-one read a working at the foot of that leaf on the Wednesday of last week. He read it the way it is written and about nine of you have not thought about it since.*”

“*I have thought about nothing else since Thursday, and I have not said so out loud until now, and I am saying it now so that nobody can say I sprang it on you.*”

“*The working is three figures and the third one is the bridge, and the third one is a figure of the bed of that drain at the abutment.*”

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has a book of heads and carts and two numbers in it in eleven years. He has refused since the second day of the hundred and thirty-sixth week to write a bench’s notice in it. He read all four leaves of a thing on a flat in about nine ears on three mornings, one leaf a morning.

“*The third figure, then, and I am reading it as it is written and not as it was on the Wednesday.*”

“*It says: at the abutment the bed of the drain stands four foot six below the top of the sill.*”

“*That is the whole of the third figure. There is no second sentence under it and there is no working at the foot of it.*”

“*I did not say anything about it on the Wednesday because I was asked to read a leaf and not to have an opinion.*”

“*And I am not going to have one now. I am sixty-one and I have two numbers in a book in eleven years and both of them are of things.*”

Then a man of about fifty-two said what a precedent is, in about nine ears, on a cart road, and it was not a word anybody had used on that bank before, and he gave his own five things and added a sixth.

“*Four foot six below the top of the sill. Written down at some point by a person in a room with about nine clerks in it, four hundred miles off.*”

“*A figure like that is only true on the morning it is taken. Eleven years is a long time for a drain bed and I have seen a bed go down in a fortnight.*”

“*So here is the word, and I am giving it to about nine of you and I am not asking anybody to use it. A precedent is a figure of the ground from a long time ago, used as a figure of the ground today, without anybody going back to look.*”

“*That is what the arrangement has done. It has taken a figure of a bridge from a long time ago and it will answer that bridge in nine days, and it will build the sill on four foot six, and four foot six is not there any more, and nobody will have lied and nobody will have said anything at all.*”

“*And the reason that matters this week is not the bridge. It is that the answer is correct on the figure it was given, and a correct answer on an old figure beats a local answer on a new one every time, and that is not a person doing anything cruel. That is a person doing nothing at all.*”

“*That is my sixth thing I cannot check, and I am naming it at the seventh hour on a Friday and not on Saturday.*”

“*I cannot check it because the checking is the rod, and the rod has not gone in.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said what he had wanted, what he had got wrong, and what it had cost, and gave the figure for the standing offer in his own mouth with the subtraction in it, and said that the rod had not gone in and that he was not going to be the one who put it in tonight.

“*Here is the want. I want two remedies to be able to stand in the same place, and I have wanted that since Thursday, and on Monday a string said no about fifteen yards of it.*”

“*And here is what I got wrong this morning, and it is a small thing and it is mine.*”

“*Here is the mistake and it is the thirty-seventh of the same mistake and I am not numbering it.*”

“*At the seventh hour I had it in my head to put that rod in myself tonight.*”

“*I did not say it out loud. I have not said it out loud. About four of you know that I had it and about four of you do not.*”

“*The cost of not saying it is that a man of about fifty-two has told us in about nine ears that he cannot check his sixth thing until a rod goes into a drain bed.*”

“*He is the only one of about nine of us who can check any of them.*”

“*And I am not going to put it in tonight. I do not know the bed and neither does he, and I have found out this week that I am worse at a figure on the ground than I am in front of you, and that is the whole of the cost and I am not dressing it.*”

“*A hundred and thirty-five days. A hundred and thirty-five is eight hundred and thirty-five less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man of thirty-eight who has said in about nine ears that he has not got the part where he would know what to do, and who has been saying a different thing since the third day of last week.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man of thirty-four in an ash who said what he is going to lose at the seventh hour on a Friday, before the rod went in, and who did not say what he would do about it.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man of about fifty-two who named a sixth thing he cannot check and gave a word to about nine of them, and who is not going to be thanked for the word because it is a word and not a finding.*”

“*And the right of refusal is not restored this morning. A precedent is not it. A working read twice is not it. A man of thirty-four saying he is not going is not it.*”

“*And I have said that four mornings running, and I am aware that repeating a figure is the only thing I have been doing for six days.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Friday, with about four inches of water back on the flat and ninety steps dry, and a second thing set out that was not a hearing.
A man of about fifty-two had named a word and a figure of a drain bed from a long time ago. A man of thirty-four in an ash had said in about nine ears what he was going to lose, before anybody had found out whether he would lose it.

The fever was seventy-eight weeks and six days old. He had been in that county two hundred and eighty-one days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and fifty days old, and a hundred and thirty-five days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

A man of forty-three said a count down to this morning was finished, and that the figure from here is a count forward.
And a boy of thirteen said he did not like a figure that has stopped counting down, and asked for one the man of forty-three did not have.
And a man of thirty-eight said he was still not opening what is in the inside of his coat, and gave the reason he had not given on Thursday, and said the reason was not the same reason twice.

A man of about fifty-two asked for a working at the foot of the fourth leaf to be read a second time, and a man of sixty-one read it in the ordinary voice, and the figure in it was four foot six below the top of the sill.
And a man of about fifty-two said a precedent is a figure of the ground from a long time ago used as a figure of the ground today without anybody going back to look, and called it his sixth thing that he cannot check.
