# Chapter 815: Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Thirty-Eighth Week, And A Saturday, And The Fever Is Seventy-Six Weeks Old Exactly, And A Man Of Forty-Four Who Pushes A Handcart Says Out Loud That It Would Be Easier And It Would Be Safer If One Person Could Say Yes To A Road, And A Woman Of Sixty-Four Says What One Vote Sounds Like From The Inside, And The Cost Is Hers And It Is Named

Fifth day of the hundred and thirty-eighth week. His five hundred and sixty-fifth morning. Four hundred and fifteen days after the settlement. The fever seventy-six weeks old. Three hundred and sixty-nine days since the division. Three hundred and fifteen days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about sixteen inches over the top step at about the seventh hour, and it was a full bank and it held there. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working, and said that this was the second morning of four on which nothing would happen on that bank. Then he gave the figure for the fever out loud, before about nine of them had worked it out, and said why on a Saturday.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. Three things every morning. He named a stone on that bank on the Sunday of the hundred and thirty-fifth week, and about nine of them walk past it every day now.

“*About sixteen inches at the seventh hour, and it is a full bank, and it is five days of the coming back.*”

“*A bank that is full holds at sixteen until it comes off, and about six of those ninety steps are under this morning, and I have said about six.*”

“*And it is the second morning out of four where nothing happens on this bank, and I have had four mornings like this in nineteen years and none of them had about nine of us standing on it.*”

“*And here is a figure and I am going to give it before any of you have worked it out, because I did it on Wednesday night and I have been sitting on it since.*”

“*Seventy-six weeks. Five hundred and thirty-two days. And that is eight hundred and fifteen less two hundred and eighty-three, and it comes out at seventy-six weeks and no days and no half.*”

“*And that is the second whole number of weeks this bank has had, and the first one was a fortnight ago on the Saturday of the week before last.*”

Then a man of forty-four said a thing about a road. He said it would be easier and it would be safer if one person could say yes to it, and it was not about the nine people standing on that bank.

Tomas Elrick is forty-four and pushed a handcart up that cart road for twenty-six years and its near shaft let go of its socket on the Saturday of the hundred and thirty-sixth week. He said his worst reason out loud and said the worst reason on that bank was his. On Wednesday he said out loud that a man who has to be asked has already been asked.

“*I am going to say a thing and I told a woman of forty-four on Wednesday that I was not going to improve on it out loud again.*”

“*And I am going to say it anyway, and about four of you are going to find it worse than what I said on Saturday, and I have thought about that for three days and I would rather say it than not.*”

“*If about nine of us all said the same thing this morning, then there would be one thing said, and it would be easier than what is happening on this bank.*”

“*And a single thing said by nine people at once is not nine people saying it. That is the part I want somebody to hold on to.*”

“*And it would be safer.*”

“*And I want to say why I know that word. It is not because a man of thirty-one has been using it about a case since before that bank had about nine of us on it. That is a different word in a different mouth.*”

“*I know it because I have been on the other end of it.*”

“*Eleven years ago the four houses at the low gate had a right to send one handcart down that road on a Tuesday morning, fill it off that landing, and take it up again.*”

“*And a Tuesday a man who is not one of the four houses stopped it. He said the road was being measured and he could not let a cart down it till he had said so to somebody.*”

“*And he has said so to nobody.*”

“*And he has not been unkind once since. That is the whole of what he did and he did it kindly.*”

“*And that is why four houses at the low gate have had nothing since the Tuesday before a man of fifty-eight walked down that road to stand here and say so.*”

“*And it would be easier and it would be safer if there was one person who could say yes to that road. Not one of us. Somebody with a book of heads and carts four hundred miles off and two numbers in it in eleven years.*”

“*And I am going to say this next part out loud, because I said the last part out loud and about four of you have been carrying it about all week.*”

“*That is not about the nine of you standing on this bank. It is not about this bank and it is not about my cart and it is not about a woman of twenty-eight on the low side of a drain.*”

“*It is about a road and a floor and eleven years.*”

“*And I have wanted a person to be able to say yes to it since the Saturday of the hundred and thirty-sixth week, and I did not know that was what I wanted until about the eighth hour this morning.*”

Then a woman of sixty-four said what one vote sounds like from the inside, gave the whole of it, named what it cost her, and did not say the other part.

Ada Renk is sixty-four and has a name in ink on that ninth line and has never had a day against it. She came up that bank on her own two feet on the Monday of the week before last, and said that nobody had asked her and nobody was going to.

“*I have been standing here since about the ninth hour and I am going to say one thing and then I am going to sit down, which is a thing I do not do.*”

“*A man of forty-four has just said it would be easier and it would be safer if one person could say yes to a road.*”

“*And he is right. He is exactly right and he has been carrying that since the Saturday before last and I have been carrying it since about a year before he was born.*”

“*I know what one voice sounds like from the inside. I was in it.*”

“*It was not loud and it was not unkind, and there was not one person in it who had done anything wrong.*”

“*And about nine of us agreed with each other about everything, every morning, for nineteen years, and not one of us was cruel.*”

“*And at the end of it I got up out of a book of heads and carts in the open, and it cost me one thing a day of them for the rest of my life.*”

“*And there is not one of you I would call cruel, and there is not one of you who would have got me out of that room.*”

“*That is what I am telling you, and it is the hardest thing anybody has said on that bank since the Saturday of the hundred and thirty-sixth week.*”

“*The ease of it is the prison. Not the cruelty. The ease.*”

“*And I have said about nine of you that not one of us was cruel in that room, and now I have said the other half of it.*”

“*And I am going to be treated differently from Monday and I know that, and a woman of forty asked me one question on Monday and did not ask it twice, and I want that on the bank before anybody thinks about how to handle me.*”

“*And I am not going to say why I could not attend and nobody is to ask me twice.*”

“*And I am not going to say the word. I have said it twice in nineteen years and I am not going to say it a third time on a Saturday morning because it would land.*”

“*Nobody is to thank me for this. And nobody is to be told that I was brave about it, and nobody is to be told that a man of forty-four was brave about his cart either.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said that he had wanted, since before that bank had about nine of us on it, the shape of the thing that has just been said twice on that bank.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and sixty-one days, two hundred and sixty-one being eight hundred and fifteen less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and thirty days old, a hundred and thirty being eight hundred and fifteen less six hundred and eighty-five. He has asked seven times whether it will keep. Nobody has said and he is not going to ask an eighth time.

“*I have been standing on this bank since before it had about nine of us on it, building a thing where about nine of us are careful with each other, and I have been calling it care.*”

“*And a woman of twenty-nine called it a fence, and a man of thirty-four gave three things to nobody, and a woman of forty stopped eight people asking him a second question.*”

“*And all of that is one vote. About nine of us, and not one of us cruel, and not one of us able to say no without the whole shape moving.*”

“*A single unanimous vote would be easier and safer than what is actually going to happen here.*”

“*And what is actually going to happen here is that about nine of us take nineteen years and come out of it changed, and nobody in it is cruel.*”

“*And I have spent every morning since before that bank had about nine of us on it putting a fence round the one thing I wanted.*”

“*And the thing I wanted was somebody who would come back the next morning and tell me I am wrong, and there has not been one voice on that bank since I came to it, and I have not noticed.*”

Then a boy of thirteen said a figure, said what it was for, and said that he was not going to add to it.

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and sixty-two days. He counted them in the lane this morning and it is one more than yesterday.

“*A hundred and sixty-two days. That is eight hundred and fifteen less six hundred and fifty-three.*”

“*And it is seven this morning, the mornings when somebody on this bank said something that was not a figure, and I said on Friday that I was not going to let it go up by one every morning.*”

“*I am not going to add to it and I want to say why, because it is the first time I have had a figure that I have wanted to go up and I am not going to let it.*”

“*A woman of sixty-four and a man of forty-four have both said a thing this morning that was not a figure. It would go to nine.*”

“*And nine is a whole thing, and a whole thing is a figure of this bank.*”

“*So it stays at seven. And about four of you can check that, which is what a figure is for.*”

Then a man of thirty-one came to the end of it and said what he wanted, what he had got wrong, and what it cost. He gave the figure for the standing offer in his own mouth with the subtraction in it, and said that the thing he has wanted since before that bank had about nine of us on it is the thing a man of forty-four has just said he wants.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and sixty-one days, and the cut across that palm is a hundred and thirty days old.

“*Here is the want and it has not moved in nine days, and it was seven days on Thursday, and I am giving you the run every morning so that about four of you can work it back to the Sunday it stopped.*”

“*And I am not going to say the want this morning. I am going to say where it is, because a man of forty-four has said where his is and it is the same place.*”

“*He wants one person who can say yes to a road.*”

“*I want one person who can come back and say I am wrong.*”

“*Those are the same want, with a road at the end of it and a man of forty-three at the end of it.*”

“*And I have been standing here since before it had about nine of us on it, telling about nine of you that mine was the second one.*”

“*The mistake is the twenty-seventh of the same mistake and it is about an hour old and a woman of sixty-four gave it to me and she was not doing it for me.*”

“*I have said I do not know four times in nine days, and I made a woman of forty count them.*”

“*And I said the word out loud as though it were an answer. It is not an answer. It is a thing I say when somebody has taken the ground out from under a sentence I was going to give.*”

“*And I have done the thing a woman of twenty-nine named on the Thursday of last week, for nine days, and told nobody.*”

“*Which is that the fence went up around a thing I wanted and I said it out loud so that I could not do it afterwards.*”

“*And the fence is still up and it is higher than it was on Monday.*”

“*The cost is that this morning, for the first time since I came to that bank, I want what a man of forty-four wants.*”

“*And about four of you have heard both of us say it, and that is the worst morning I have spent on that bank, and I would not have spent it if he had not spoken first.*”

“*A hundred and fifteen days. A hundred and fifteen is eight hundred and fifteen less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer.*”

“*And the right of refusal is not restored this morning and it is not given to anybody, and this is the morning it was going to look most like it.*”

“*A woman of sixty-four has just said out loud that the ease of one voice is a prison, and that about nine of us agreeing with each other kindly is the old thing.*”

“*That is not the right of refusal. That is a woman of sixty-four telling nine people what she has been inside.*”

“*And it costs her, and it has cost her nineteen years, and it has not cost anybody else anything at all.*”

“*And a man of thirty-four in an ash has said he will speak if she speaks, and she said no, and he is going to do it anyway, and that is not it either.*”

“*He told about nine of us on Friday morning that he could see himself using her and was going to do it anyway. A man who can see that and does it anyway has not refused anything.*”

“*And four of us agreeing to say the same thing is not it.*”

“*And a boy of thirteen asking nine people for one thing and getting four answers is not it.*”

“*Nothing was refused this morning. Nothing was consented to.*”

“*About nine of us were kind to each other, and two of them gave away the whole of what they knew about a thing that took nineteen years, and a man of forty-four wanted a person at the end of a road and did not get one.*”

“*And that is a Saturday and not a settlement.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a right of eleven years at the low gate, or of four houses at that gate that have had nothing since that Tuesday.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a woman of sixty-four who will be handled differently from Monday, or of a man of thirty-four who can see himself.*”

“*Nobody says a word of thanks to me for a morning where I found out what I have been building.*”

“*And nobody is to be told that I was brave about a morning where I found out what I have been building, because I do not think it was one of those.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Saturday, with a full bank about sixteen inches over the top step above them. And a man of thirty-one said he had wanted, since before that bank had about nine of us on it, the same thing a man of forty-four wanted, and named that as the worst morning he has spent on that bank.
