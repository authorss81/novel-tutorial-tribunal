# Chapter 828: Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Fortieth Week, And A Friday, And Eight Inches And Two Days Of The Coming Back, And A Boy Of Thirteen Asks His Question Again At The Ninth Hour And A Woman Of Twenty-Eight Answers It Out Loud, And A Man Of Thirty-One Says The Thing He Built Was A Map And Not A Source, And A Woman Of Twenty-Nine Says He Is Not Going To Be Allowed To Say It In That Order

Fourth day of the hundred and fortieth week. His five hundred and seventy-eighth morning. Four hundred and twenty-eight days after the settlement. The fever seventy-seven weeks and six days. Three hundred and eighty-two days since the division. Three hundred and twenty-eight days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about eight inches over the top step at about the seventh hour, and it was two days of the coming back, and about eighty of those steps were under this morning. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working, said the bank will be full in two days, and said the next time, and gave it as a figure with a rate on it and said so.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. Three things every morning, and on this morning he gave the reading and the working and the figure for the next time and then stopped, which is the first time in nineteen years the fourth thing has been nothing.

“*About eight inches at the seventh hour, and it is two days of the coming back, and about eighty of those ninety steps are under this morning.*”

“*And it will be a full bank in two days at the seventh hour, and it will hold there until the day after that, and the day after that is the twenty-first morning.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in six days, and that is six days after the morning it is full, and the morning it is full is the day after tomorrow.*”

“*And that is a figure with a rate on it, so anybody can work the next one out from the rate without asking me, and about four of you have already done it in the lane and about four of you have not.*”

Then at about the ninth hour a boy of thirteen asked his question again, and a woman of twenty-eight answered it out loud in about nine ears, and said what it had cost her to answer it and who she had not told.

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and seventy-five days, and he has counted them in a lane. He asked at the ninth hour on the third day of the hundred and thirty-ninth week how a mark gets made, was not answered on that Thursday, and asked again on the fourth day of the hundred and fortieth week.

“*A hundred and seventy-five days, and it is one more than the morning before, and I counted it in the lane before the light came up.*”

“*And here is the question again, and about four of you said you would try, and I am not blaming anybody who did not.*”

“*The same question. How does a mark get made.*”

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from. She said her own name on that bank on the Tuesday of the hundred and thirty-ninth week, and she came up four miles on that Tuesday to say it. She is at a hundred and eighty-six days, and the figure is eight hundred and twenty-eight less six hundred and forty-two.

“*A hundred and eighty-six days, and that is eight hundred and twenty-eight less six hundred and forty-two.*”

“*A man of thirty-one has had that question two days and has not answered it, and I have had it one day and I am going to answer it, and about nine of you are going to hear the answer before you hear his.*”

“*A mark is made by a person who has been given a thing to hold, and the thing to hold is the smallest thing in this county.*”

“*It is a stamp with a handle.*”

“*It is not made in a room with the door shut. It is made in a room with about nine clerks in it, four hundred miles off.*”

“*And it is made by a person who is doing the work of the book and does not know what the mark is for.*”

“*And that is the answer, and it took me a day, and it is not a good answer and it is the true one.*”

“*Here is what it cost me to say it out loud in about nine ears, and I am going to say it in about nine ears and not to him.*”

“*That is the first time in this county that a person has said out loud how a mark gets made.*”

“*And I have been at a hundred and eighty-six days, and a man of fifty-four has asked for a hundred and seven mornings, and nobody has ever told him where the mark comes from.*”

“*And I have not told a man of fifty-four. I am not going to, and it is not because of the thing on the flat.*”

“*About four of you are going to think that is a reason and it is not.*”

“*It is because I do not know what a man of fifty-four does with an answer he has had for nineteen years, and I am not going to put that on him at about the ninth hour on a Friday.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said what the thing he had built was, and said it in the wrong order on purpose, and a woman of twenty-nine stopped him in about nine ears and made him say the two halves apart.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and seventy-four days, two hundred and seventy-four being eight hundred and twenty-eight less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and forty-three days old, a hundred and forty-three being eight hundred and twenty-eight less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*A mark is made by a person who is given a thing to hold, and a woman of twenty-eight has said that out loud in about nine of your ears before me.*”

“*About four of you are going to want to know what I think of that.*”

“*I think it is the first true thing anybody has said on that bank since the thing arrived, and I am not going to improve on it by saying it after her.*”

“*Here is what I am going to say, and I am going to say it in the wrong order, and I want about nine of you to notice that I know I am doing it.*”

“*The thing I built at that cart road is a map. It is a map of what the water and the weather and the carts do on that road.*”

“*About four of you have read it, and it has not been wrong in a week.*”

“*And I built it because I came out of a game, and a game is a thing a person plays to find out whether a world works.*”

Nobody on that bank said anything, and a woman of twenty-nine in a reed said the next part out loud, and made him take the two halves apart.

Marda Vell is twenty-nine and has been in a reed about twenty feet off the low side of that drain, a hundred yards along it, for a hundred and ninety-nine days, and she can read, and she has said that us is four people.

“*A hundred and ninety-nine days. That is eight hundred and twenty-eight less six hundred and twenty-nine.*”

“*Stop there, and say the second half on its own.*”

“*You have just said that a game is a thing a person plays to find out whether a world works, and you said it after the first half, and about nine of you heard it in that order.*”

“*That order is the whole of what a man of thirty-one is, and you have done it twice this week with two different things.*”

“*A man of about fifty-two caught the first one on Thursday and I am catching the second one now.*”

“*Say it the other way round. A world is not a thing a game was made to test.*”

“*A game was made to see whether a world could be entered, and I am not going to say the rest of that in about nine ears, because a boy of thirteen is standing here.*”

“*And I am a figure of one person in a reed, and I am about to say something about four of you have been waiting three days to hear, and about four of you would rather I had not.*”

“*The game was a bridge and it was not a source.*”

Nobody on that bank said anything for about a minute. Then a man of thirty-one said it back, and said what it cost him to have it said by somebody else, and said what he was going to do with it, and said that it was not for this bank.

“*I am not going to improve on that either.*”

“*A game is a bridge. And I came over it, and it was built by a person I have never met.*”

“*She signed about nine hundred records in a building with a bad coffee machine, and her name is Nadia Kestrel.*”

“*And it was not a source. It was not made of this.*”

“*It was made by people about two hundred miles from here who thought they were making a thing to look at, and it was the only door.*”

“*And I have been calling it a window for two hundred and seventy-four days.*”

“*That is what it costs to have it said by somebody else. It costs me the word window, and I have been using that word since the first morning I stood on this bank.*”

“*And I am not going to give her name to a room four hundred miles off this week.*”

“*She is not one of the four of us who are going to speak, and she has not asked to be, and I have not asked her.*”

“*And I am not going to put what she said on that counter, and I am not going to give it to a man of thirty-eight’s board.*”

“*And I am not going to put it anywhere except in about nine ears on a bank on a Friday morning, and that is where it is going to stay unless about four of you can tell me a better place.*”

Then a man of thirty-one came to the end of it. He said what he wanted, what he had got wrong, and what it cost, and gave the figure for the standing offer in his own mouth with the subtraction in it, which is the eleventh morning of that run and which he is not going to stop.

“*Here is the want and it has moved shape four times in four days, and I am not going to give you the shapes.*”

“*A woman of twenty-eight answered a boy of thirteen’s question before I did, and that is the shape.*”

“*The mistake is the thirty-sixth, and I am not going to number it as a new one.*”

“*A woman of twenty-nine in a reed named it in about nine ears before I did, and I am not going to pretend I got there first.*”

“*What I got wrong this morning is that I had it and I did not have it ready, and there is a difference and it is the whole of the difference.*”

“*A hundred and twenty-eight days. A hundred and twenty-eight is eight hundred and twenty-eight less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer.*”

“*And the standing offer and the thing on that flat are still two offers and I gave both figures yesterday with their own subtractions, and I am not going to merge them, and if about four of you merge them that is your figure and not mine.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a woman of twenty-eight who answered a question in about nine ears before the man who had it, and who said what it cost her and who she had not told.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a woman of twenty-nine in a reed who said that a game was a bridge and not a source, and who has said that us is four people and that it is not going to be five.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a boy of thirteen who asked a question twice and got an answer on the second asking and has not said thank you to anybody, and is not going to.*”

“*And nobody is to be told that a woman of twenty-eight saying a thing before a man of thirty-one was brave about it, and nobody is to thank me for the word window, and I am not going to be thanked for it.*”

“*And the right of refusal is not restored this morning, and a game that was a bridge is not it, and I am not going to tell about nine of you what it is.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Friday with twelve inches of water over the top step above them, and a boy of thirteen’s question answered at the ninth hour by a woman who had had it a day, and a woman in a reed who had said a game was a bridge and not a source, and a name given once out loud in about nine ears and not put anywhere else.

---

The fever was seventy-seven weeks and six days old. He had been in that county two hundred and seventy-four days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and forty-three days old, and a hundred and twenty-eight days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

A boy of thirteen asked at the ninth hour how a mark gets made, for the second time, and a woman of twenty-eight answered it before the man of thirty-one who had had it two days, and said it is made by a person with a stamp in a room with about nine clerks in it four hundred miles off.
And she said what it cost her to have said it out loud, and that she has not told a man of fifty-four, and that it is not because of the thing on the flat.

A man of thirty-one said the thing he built at the cart road is a map, and said in the same breath that a game is a thing a person plays to find out whether a world works, and a woman of twenty-nine in a reed stopped him and made him take the halves apart.
And she said the game was a bridge and not a source, and he said it back, and gave the name of the woman who signed about nine hundred records in a country that is not this one, and said he is not taking it to a room four hundred miles off this week.
