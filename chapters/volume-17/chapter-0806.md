# Chapter 806: Third Day Of The Hundred And Thirty-Seventh Week, And A Thursday, And A Woman Of Twenty-Nine In A Reed Who Has Been In It One Hundred And Seventy-Eight Days Says Four Sentences About A Bank And About A Man Who Was Not Asked A Fourth Question And Is Not Going Down Two Hundred Yards Of Ground, And A Man Of Forty-Four Says He Has Been Waiting Nine Years For A Barrow And Has Not Got One

Third day of the hundred and thirty-seventh week. His five hundred and fifty-sixth morning. Four hundred and six days after the settlement. The fever seventy-four weeks and five days. Three hundred and sixty days since the division. Three hundred and six days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about sixteen inches over the top step at about the seventh hour, four days of the coming back, and it was a full bank.
And the man of forty-three gave the reading and the working and said that a full bank holds where it is until it goes.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. Three things every morning. He named a stone on that bank on the Sunday of the week before last and about nine of them walk past it every day now and he has noticed that.

“*About sixteen inches at the seventh hour, and it is a full bank, and it is four days since it came off.*”

“*And about a dozen and a half of those ninety steps are under this morning and I have said a dozen and a half because that is what I know and not what I could guess at.*”

“*Four inches a day is the working underneath the holding, and a full bank holds at sixteen until it goes.*”

“*And it went on the Sunday of the week before for the seventeenth time and it goes again on the Monday that closes this week for the eighteenth.*”

“*And the sixth thing I said on Wednesday morning was that there were about nine of us where there had been about four on Saturday. I said I did not do that. I want to correct about a fifth of it and I have had two days to think about it.*”

“*I did not invite them. I did not stop them. And I have given three things every morning for nineteen years to about four people and there was a morning last week when I gave three things and did not need to repeat them.*”

“*And that has not happened to me in nineteen years and I have not said that out loud until this morning.*”

Then a woman of twenty-nine came up that bank at about the ninth hour out of a reed about twenty feet off the edge of that drain a hundred yards along it.
And she had been in that reed one hundred and seventy-seven days, and she said four sentences and about nine of them did not interrupt her.

Marda Vell is twenty-nine and can read and came up that bank on her own on the Thursday of the hundred and thirty-sixth week and on the Friday of last week. And her name is in ink on that ninth line and has never had a day against it. And she has said that four of them are her people and nobody is going to be sent for about any of the four.

“*I am going to say four sentences and I have said them in my head since Wednesday afternoon and I am not going to be interrupted.*”

“*And if I am then I will say them again tomorrow and it will take me until about the twelfth day.*”

“*One. There were about four of you on that bank on Saturday and there are about nine of you now, and it was not the water and it was not a thing coming apart.*”

“*And I have been in a reed a hundred and seventy-seven days watching who walks onto it and about four of you were walking onto it out of the water and about five of you have come up that road for other reasons and I have watched all nine of you and I am not saying it is good.*”

“*Two. A man of thirty-four came up that bank yesterday on his own two feet and said three things and nobody asked him a fourth.*”

“*And a woman of forty said out loud that handing three things to nine people is not the same as asking him, and she was right and nobody has said so this morning.*”

“*Three. A man of thirty-one said he was not going to go and look at a piece of ground two hundred yards off that landing.*”

“*And he said that out loud on a bank in about nine ears so that he could not do it afterwards.*”

“*And about four of you heard that as carefulness and it was not carefulness. It was a man putting a fence up around a thing he wanted to do.*”

“*Four. I have been asked one question in four weeks and it was about ground, and I have not been asked anything this morning either.*”

“*And I am telling you that I am still here and I still have not been asked, and that is the fifth sentence and I said I was going to say four.*”

Then a man of forty-four said he had been waiting nine years for a barrow and had not got one, and said what he had done with the nine years, and about four of them did not say anything to him about it.

Tomas Elrick is forty-four and pushed a handcart up that cart road for twenty-six years and its near shaft let go of its socket on the Saturday before last, and it was carried up ninety steps on Tuesday morning by two men who were not asked.

“*On Saturday I said I had not asked anybody for anything in nine years that I can remember.*”

“*And I have been thinking about that for two days and I want to be more careful with it than I was.*”

“*I have not asked. That is true and I am not going to take it back. What I have done is been about two hundred yards off that landing, about three times a week, for nine years.*”

“*And I have not asked, and I have stood in the same place each time.*”

“*And this morning a handcart came up ninety steps that I did not have to lift.*”

“*And about four of you watched two men do that and one of them has a bad knee and did not mention it.*”

“*And I have had nine years of standing about two hundred yards off and about four of you have had four days of watching me.*”

“*That is not a thing anybody has done to me. I want that said by me and not by somebody else this morning. And I am not going to stand here and put that on a Thursday morning because I have got nine years and a Thursday morning is not the size of it.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open, eighty-fifth morning, and gave the figure before it was given and said what he is going to do about his own morning at about the ninth hour.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel on that landing and cannot read. He has asked for that line in the open every morning since the Thursday of the hundred and twenty-fifth week. And asked at the top of a cut on Sunday for the second time.

“*Eighty-five mornings and I have got it before you have said it and that has been the shape of this week and I am not going to pretend I have not noticed it.*”

“*I am going to do something at about the ninth hour this morning. A man of thirty-eight said on Wednesday he was going to stand on this bank at the ninth hour instead of the eleventh.*”

“*And a man of thirty-four came up yesterday and I have had four mornings of thinking about two hundred yards of ground I have never walked in nineteen years of holding gravel on this landing.*”

“*So I am going to walk about two hundred yards. That is all. And I am telling you now because a man of thirty-eight told us on Monday that he had a figure and then went and worked out that it was a day from yesterday and got it right out loud.*”

“*And I am not going to be a man of fifty-four with a wrong figure in my hand at about the eleventh hour.*”

“*And I am not going to ask anybody to come with me and I am not going to say why I am not.*”

“*And about four of you can work out that a man of thirty-one did the same thing on Tuesday about a barrow and neither of us said a word to the other about it.*”

Then a boy of thirteen gave his own figure and a figure of nobody in particular.
And a man of thirty-eight stood on that bank at the ninth hour and did not leave and did not talk.
And a man of about fifty-two said what a day is when a thing has stopped.

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and fifty-three days.

“*A hundred and fifty-three days and I counted them in the lane and it is one more than yesterday.*”

“*And I am not giving a count this morning. I am going to say why and I have been working on it since Tuesday night in the lane and it is not a thing I am proud of.*”

“*The fourth count went up by one every morning for five mornings and I worked out in the lane on Tuesday that it was a figure of a bank and not a figure of the four of us.*”

“*And I stopped it at nine. And since Tuesday it has gone up again every morning and I have not said so.*”

“*And yesterday a man of thirty-four came up that bank, which is the reason, and a man of thirty-eight has been standing on it at the ninth hour.*”

“*So it went up by two on Wednesday and by one yesterday and it would go up again this morning if I gave it.*”

“*And it is a figure of about four of us and about four of us have grown to about nine.*”

“*And I do not know what the number is supposed to be about and I am not going to carry a number I cannot say what it is of.*”

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine and a board in the inside of his coat with thirty-two notches cut in the edge of it. And he stood on that bank at about the ninth hour on Thursday and did not leave and did not talk.

“*That is what I did and I told them on Wednesday I was going to do it and I have not got anything to say about it and I am not going to pretend I have.*”

“*A man of fifty-four is going to walk about two hundred yards at about the ninth hour this morning. I am not going to say anything about that either. I said on Monday that I was not going to say the want about myself twice in a week and it is Thursday and I have not said it and I am not going to.*”

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well and put a mark on one of two sheets of deal four lines under him eleven years ago because he was the only one of nine who was not asked what the line was for. And he stopped checking figures out loud on Tuesday after three mornings and said on Thursday that a day is where a thing was and not that the thing was right.

“*I said on Tuesday that I would give three and then stop and I am stopped, and I am going to say one thing about stopping and then I am finished.*”

“*A day is what a thing was on. That is all a day is. It is where a thing was on the day somebody wrote it.*”

“*And it does not say the thing was right and it does not say anybody agreed and it does not say the thing is there now.*”

“*And I have been putting a figure on a day for eleven years and about four of you have been reading that as me saying the day was right.*”

“*And I have never once said that and I have never been asked whether I meant it.*”

“*About four hundred miles is the distance to that line and I cannot check it because there is no line under me four hundred miles off.*”

“*And that is not a figure of a distance, that is a figure of what I cannot do.*”

“*And I have been giving it out loud for eleven years as though it were the first.*”

Then a man of thirty-one came to the end of it and said what he wanted, what he had got wrong, and what it had cost, and gave the figure for the standing offer in his own mouth with the subtraction in it, and said that a woman of twenty-nine had caught him in four sentences.
And gave the figure for an offer that has not been answered.
And said that a woman of twenty-nine had caught him in four sentences and he had been caught on Monday and Saturday and Wednesday and he was not going to pretend the four of those were four different things.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and belongs to nobody, and has been in this county two hundred and fifty-two days, two hundred and fifty-two being eight hundred and six less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and twenty-one days old, a hundred and twenty-one being eight hundred and six less six hundred and eighty-five. He has asked seven times in about a fortnight whether it will keep and nobody has said, and he has not asked since Tuesday, and about four of them have noticed that he has stopped.

“*A woman of twenty-nine has just told me in about nine people’s hearing that I put a fence up around a thing I wanted to do, and she is right.*”

“*And she said it on her second morning up this bank in a fortnight.*”

“*And I am going to say the rest of what she said because about four of you are waiting for me to argue with it.*”

“*She said a man of thirty-four gave three things to nine people and that is not the same as asking him.*”

“*And she said I said out loud on a bank that I was not going to look at that ground so that I could not do it afterwards.*”

“*And she said about four of you heard that as carefulness and it was not carefulness.*”

“*Here is what I wanted and I am going to say it before the mistake, because she gave it to me first and I am not going to get the order wrong this morning.*”

“*I wanted her to have said it to me and not to about nine of us.*”

“*And I am a man of thirty-one and that is what I wanted and I have been on this bank four weeks saying that a case cannot be served and today a woman of twenty-nine served it on me in four sentences in the open and there is nobody I can send it back to.*”

“*The mistake is the nineteenth of the same mistake and it is three hours old and it is the same one as Monday and Saturday and it is this: I have been arranging for people to tell me things in front of about nine of you, because a thing said to me in private is a thing I can hold.*”

“*And a thing said to me in the open is a thing I have to stand next to while about four of you decide what to make of it.*”

“*A woman of sixty-four did it to me on the Tuesday of the week before last and I got a word out of it. A man of thirty-four did it to me on Tuesday morning by going down. A boy of thirteen did it to me on Sunday and I have been saying the word I do not know to about four of you for four days as though it were an answer.*”

“*And it is not an answer, it is a thing I say when somebody has taken the ground out from under a sentence I was going to give.*”

“*And nobody is going to let that go and nobody is going to thank me for saying so and nobody is relieved of it.*”

“*And it is the same mistake four mornings running now and it is not going to stop because I have said it out loud on a bank with sixteen inches of water over the top step of it.*”

“*And the cost is that a woman of twenty-nine has had to say four sentences to about nine of you to hand me a thing I could have been asked for, and about four of you are going to spend this week being careful with me, and I have done that to about four of you twice already.*”

“*A hundred and six days. A hundred and six is eight hundred and six less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer.*”

“*And a man of fifty-four is about to walk about two hundred yards at about the ninth hour this morning because of me and I am not going to watch him do it.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man of forty-four and nine years of his, or of a woman of twenty-nine and a hundred and seventy-seven days of hers. Nobody is to be thanked and nobody is to be told they were brave about any of it.*”

“*And about four of you are going to work out that a man of thirty-one arranged for most of the last four days of this bank and I have said it out loud before about nine of you and I am not going to unarrange it.*”

“*And the right of refusal is not restored this morning. A woman of twenty-nine has not been asked anything for four weeks and that is not a refusal, because nobody asked her and a refusal is a thing a person does. A man of thirty-four said three things and was not asked a fourth. A man of fifty-four has not asked at the top of that cut since Sunday. And I have put a fence up around a thing I wanted and called it out loud.*”

“*None of those is it. And the one that looks most like it is not me not going down two hundred yards of ground, because I do not want to go.*”

“*And that is the opposite of a refusal and it is the same as not going to a bank because the water is over the top step of it.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Thursday, with a full bank about sixteen inches over the top step above them.
And a man of fifty-four was walking about two hundred yards along the edge of that drain and was not being watched by anybody.

The fever was seventy-four weeks and five days old. He had been in that county two hundred and fifty-two days, and a man of thirty-four had said three things to about nine people on Wednesday and had not been asked a fourth question.

A woman of twenty-nine said four sentences about a bank that has grown from about four people to about nine, and said that a man of thirty-one had built a fence around a thing he wanted to do and called it out loud so that he could not do it afterwards.

And a man of about fifty-two said that a day is where a thing was on the day somebody wrote it and does not say the thing is there now, and had been giving four hundred miles out loud for eleven years as though it were the first.
