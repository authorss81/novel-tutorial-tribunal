# Chapter 850: Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Third Week, And A Saturday, And The Water Comes Off All Ninety Steps At About The Sixth Hour For The Twenty-Third Time, And A Resident Beside The Repaired Orison Hall Asks A Man Of Thirty-One Whether A Rule Permitting Only Registered Ferry Crews Can Be Obeyed After A Bell, And The Answer Is A Refusal And Two Days Of Repair Labour And A Public Appeal, And A Word Is Said Out Loud That Has Not Been Said Here, And A Question Is Asked And Not Answered

Fifth day of the hundred and forty-third week. His six hundredth morning. Four hundred and fifty days after the settlement. The fever eighty-one weeks old. Four hundred and four days since the division. Three hundred and fifty days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water came off that bank at about the sixth hour and all ninety of those steps were walkable by about the seventh. The man of forty-three gave the hour and the count and the number of the time, and then said the thing he has not said for nineteen mornings, and said that he does not have it right and is going to start again anyway.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and gives three things every morning. The fourth thing is a figure of how many of those ninety steps are under, and it has been nothing for eighteen days.

“*It came off at about the sixth hour and all ninety of those steps are walkable now, and there is no figure of inches to give you this morning, and that is eight days after the last time I had none.*”

“*It is the twenty-third time. The last one was eight days ago at about the sixth hour, and the one before that was eight days before that, and the next one is in eight days, and I know that without the water-stone and I have not looked at it.*”

“*And here is the fourth thing, and it is the first time I have said anything about it in eighteen days, and it is not a figure. There is no water on those steps this morning, so there is nothing under them, and that is the only reason I can say anything, and I would like about nine of you to notice what it is that I am saying and what I am not.*”

“*I am going to start giving it again on Monday, out of the same place I always have. A man of about fifty-two told me on the seventh day of the hundred and fortieth week that I gave two figures at the same height on a Saturday and a Sunday, and I did not know which was right then. I still do not know. I am going to give it anyway.*”

Then at about the tenth hour, beside the repaired Orison hearing hall, a woman asked a man of thirty-one a question in the ordinary voice, in about nine ears, and the question was about a rule and a bell and a gate, and it was not a question about the world.

Linnet Ord is thirty-one and keeps a boat on the water below the low gate, and is not on a ferry crew’s roll, and came up the road on Thursday and asked to be asked things in public, and there are about four people in this county she does not know the names of.

“*You are the man who read that notice out on the last day of the hundred and fortieth week, so I am going to ask you and not the six of you, because the six of you have a room and I have a yard.*”

“*Here is the rule as it is printed. When the water is coming, the gate into this yard is opened for registered ferry crews, and only for them. The yard is where the shelter is. There is one bell before the water, and I have a boat, and I have pulled four people out of that yard in a fortnight, and I am not on the roll.*”

“*And here is the question. Is that rule to be obeyed? Because the bell went at four and the water was coming at six and my boat was in, and a roll with my name off it is a roll, and I want to know whether I was to break a rule that morning or keep a rule that shut four families out of a yard.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said he was not the person who decides that, and said what the yard had done instead, and said the two halves of what had happened out loud, and named the people affected, and did not make a speech about any of it.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and ninety-six days, two hundred and ninety-six being eight hundred and fifty less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and sixty-five days old, a hundred and sixty-five being eight hundred and fifty less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*No, and I am not going to do the second half of that sentence for you, and I am going to say why in one breath so that about four of you do not decide afterwards that I was being clever.*”

“*I hold no seat, I am not on any roll of anything in this county, and the six seats that keep the archive have not asked me anything and I have not asked them for anything. If I say a rule may be obeyed, that is a man with no seat deciding that a rule is a rule.*”

“*What the yard did is this. The gate was shut at about the fifth hour and about four families were outside it. About six people in the yard decided that the gate stays shut for the crews, and that the four families would be taken in the back, through the coal hatch, on a cart, with two of them on it at a time.*”

“*That is a refusal of a specific rule, in about nine of your ears, and it is not lawful and I am not going to tell anybody here that it is. And they accepted two days of repair labour on the flood-ward step, every person in that yard who can lift, including the man who wrote the rule, and that was the whole of what was asked of them and it was asked out loud.*”

“*I am going to write three things down before I stop talking, and I am going to write them on a page and not in my head, and a boy of thirteen is going to read them back because I have been the mouth for every figure on this landing for twelve days and that is one too many.*”

“*One: the refusal. The gate was kept shut for the crews and about four families were taken in the back through the coal hatch on a cart, two of them at a time, at about the fifth hour, after one bell.*”

“*Two: the burden. Two days of repair labour on the flood-ward step, asked out loud, taken by every person in that yard who can lift, and one of those persons wrote the rule that was refused.*”

“*Three: the people affected. About four families and a boat, and the names are on a list in a hand that is not mine, and a boy of thirteen is about to read them and a man of fifty-four cannot read and is standing on this landing.*”

Then a boy of thirteen read the names of the people affected out of a list, and read them twice because a man of fifty-four cannot read, and said that four is a figure of families and that the yard is not that figure.

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and ninety-seven days, and the figure is one more than the morning before, and he counted it in the lane before the light came up. A man of fifty-four holds the gravel on that landing and has asked for a line a leaf at a time for a hundred and twenty-nine mornings.

“*A hundred and ninety-seven days, and it is one more than the morning before, and I counted it in the lane before the light came up.*”

“*Four families, twice, and the names are on a list in a hand that is not mine, and I am reading them because about four of you cannot read the list and a man of fifty-four cannot read anything.*”

“*And four is a figure of families outside a gate. It is not a figure of how many people were in that yard, because I do not know that, and I am not going to stand here and give a figure of about nine of them for a yard that held more than that and less than something else.*”

Then a public appeal was filed at about the second hour, and a gate opened under a narrow amended rule at about the fifth hour, and nobody said that the rule had been right, and nobody was thanked.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and read the appeal whole, and read the amended rule whole, and wrote both in a book he has not written in for eleven years until Wednesday, and said what the amendment is in four words.

“*The amended rule says: the gate is opened for registered ferry crews, and for any other person named on a list kept in the yard, and a person is added to that list by any two people in the yard who are not on it.*”

“*That is the amendment. I have read it twice and I have not softened it. It does not say crews. It does not say a roll. It says a list in a yard, and about four of you can add a name to it, and that is a different thing from the thing that was printed on Thursday.*”

“*And it is not right. It is narrower than what happened and wider than what was printed, and a woman of sixty-four is going to say that the first half of a thing is a very expensive half, and she said that on Saturday last week about a different thing, and she is right about this one too.*”

Then a woman said a word out loud on that landing that has not been said there, and said it about a man who is not the defendant and not the man who writes the answers, and he did not answer it and said so.

Linnet Ord is thirty-one and keeps a boat below the low gate, and asked a question at about the tenth hour, and has no seat and no roll and no place in any of the nine, and came up the road on Thursday.

“*One more thing, and I am saying it to you and not to the six, and then I am going down the road.*”

“*You are not the defendant in this. You were, in a room, a long time ago, and you are not now. You are not the man who writes the answers, and I have read what you wrote and it does not read like a man who writes the answers.*”

“*So you are a citizen, and I am saying that word because nobody on this landing has said it in about nine of your ears, and I have been here four days and it has been going round like a word for a thing somebody used to be.*”

“*And I am not saying it to be kind to you. I am saying it because you are the only man I have spoken to this week who did not tell me what I was allowed to do, and I would like the word to be on the ground before you go and be somebody important somewhere else.*”

Ilyan Vester, thirty-one, said that he was not going to answer the word, and said why, and said that about four of them had heard it and that was the whole of what a word does.

“*I am not going to answer it, and I want that said out loud rather than left for about four of you to work out.*”

“*I have been in this county two hundred and ninety-six days and I have been nobody’s for all of them, and a woman has just given me a word in the ordinary voice, and I am not going to say thank you for it and I am not going to say I have earned it.*”

“*And I am not going to say it is not true either, because I do not know. Since the Monday at the end of the hundred and forty-first week, which is twelve days, I have been telling about nine of you the difference between a thing that is true and a thing that has been said. I am not going to be the man who lets his own sentence go the day it costs him something.*”

Then a man of thirty-eight asked the question, in about nine ears, and it was about three things on a page, and nobody on that landing answered it, and he said that was the correct number of people to not answer it.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and opened what is in the inside of his coat on the seventh day of the hundred and forty-second week, and there are thirty-two notches on it, and he has still not got the part where he knows what to do with it.

“*I have got one question and it is not a good one, and I am asking it in about nine of your ears and I have not slept about it.*”

“*On Tuesday a man of sixty-one read the arrangement’s own index out on that landing and left three of the nine positions blank, on purpose, and said he left them blank on purpose, and a man of thirty-eight did not sleep on Wednesday because of it.*”

“*What are the three. That is the whole of my question. Three of the nine positions that a man of thirty-one has said for eleven days are not on the ground, and a man of about fifty-two has said he can check six and not three, and I am asking what they are.*”

Ilyan Vester, thirty-one, said that he could not answer it and would not try, and gave the reason, and did not give a better reason than that.

“*No. I have been asked that for eleven days, on this landing and on a cart road and in a room, and I do not know, and I am not going to know by standing here.*”

“*And the second half of the question is worse than the first half. The second half is who put them there. That is not a question for this landing and it is not a question for me, and I am not going to pretend to about nine of you that a man who cannot check three of the nine is going to find them on a Saturday.*”

“*What I can say is that a settlement was made in the four days from Saturday to Wednesday with three of the nine positions that nobody can account for, and that the blanks are in a book that a man of sixty-one wrote and that anybody may read, and that is where they are now instead of nowhere. That is not an answer and I am not going to call it one.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the one sentence he had, and said it was not an answer to the three blanks, and said where he had got it, and a boy of thirteen said out loud that it was not a discovery and refused to let about four of them put it in a book.

“*It is not an answer to the three of the nine, and I am not going to stand here and let about four of you take it for one.*”

“*Here is the whole of what I have got, and it is a sentence and not a finding. You decide. And you help carry what your decision changes.*”

“*I did not find that this morning and I did not work it out. A woman of sixty-four said it in a different shape on Friday and a boy of thirteen read it back in three pieces, and the third piece was the part I have just said, and about four of you were standing here when he said it.*”

“*And it is in a mouth on a landing, and a man of thirty-eight has three blanks in a book that nobody can fill, and one of those things does not fix the other one, and I am going to walk down that landing and not say anything else today.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for the sentence, and the woman who said it first is not going to be thanked for it by me, and a boy of thirteen is not going to be told he found it, and that is the last thing I have got this morning and it is not a reward.*”

Wat Marshe is thirteen and read a sentence back in three pieces on the Friday of this week because a man of fifty-four cannot read. He said his figure at the seventh hour and this is the second time he has given it today, and he said the same thing about this sentence that he said about the other one on Friday.

“*That is not a discovery and I am not going to be the man who says it is. A woman of sixty-four said it on a Friday in a different shape, I read it back in three pieces because a man of fifty-four cannot read, and the third piece is what he has just said out loud.*”

“*And a man of thirty-eight has asked what three of the nine are and nobody has answered him, and about four of you are about to decide that a sentence has answered it. It has not. It is a sentence and a man of thirty-eight is a question and the two of them are not the same shape.*”

About nine of them were on that landing at about the sixth hour of a Saturday with all ninety cut steps dry under them for the twenty-third time. A gate was open under a narrower rule, a word was on the ground that had not been on it, three blanks sat in a book that nobody could fill, and the amended rule was nailed to a gate in Orison with about four names on a list beside it, and a cart road went up out of that yard past the hearing hall and on toward a bakehouse four miles off that nobody in this county had been asked about this week.

The fever was eighty-one weeks old. He had been in that county two hundred and ninety-six days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and sixty-five days old, and a hundred and fifty days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

The water came off all ninety steps at about the sixth hour for the twenty-third time. The man of forty-three said the fourth thing for the first time in eighteen days: that he is going to start giving it again on Monday, out of the same place, and does not know which of two figures at the same height is right.
Beside the repaired Orison hearing hall a woman asked whether a rule permitting only registered ferry crews could be obeyed after a bell, and was told that a man with no seat is not the person who decides that, and heard the yard say what it had done.

The gate was shut at the fifth hour and about four families were outside it, and about six people in the yard took them in the back on a cart through the coal hatch two at a time, and accepted two days of repair labour on the flood-ward step, every person who could lift, including the man who wrote the rule.
A public appeal was filed and the gate opened under a narrower amended rule, and a man of sixty-one said in four words what the amendment is, and said that it is not right, only narrower than what happened and wider than what was printed.

A word was said out loud by a person, about a man who is no longer the defendant and not the man who writes the answers. He did not answer it and said he would not, and did not say it was untrue either, because he does not know.
And a man of thirty-eight asked what three of the nine positions are, and a man of thirty-one said no. He said the second half of the question is worse and is not a question for that landing, and that the blanks are in a book anybody may read, which is where they are now instead of nowhere, and that this is not an answer.
