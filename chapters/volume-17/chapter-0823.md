# Chapter 823: Sixth Day Of The Hundred And Thirty-Ninth Week, And A Sunday, And A Full Bank Still Holds At Sixteen, And A Man Of Sixty-One Reads The Third Leaf And It Is A Child Four Miles Up That Cart Road, And A Woman Of Twenty-Eight Is On That Bank And Did Not Come Up For The Reading, And A Man Of Thirty-One Goes Up That Road On His Own Two Feet And Is Not Relieved Of What He Finds There

Sixth day of the hundred and thirty-ninth week. His five hundred and seventy-third morning. Four hundred and twenty-three days after the settlement. The fever seventy-seven weeks and one day. Three hundred and seventy-seven days since the division. Three hundred and twenty-three days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about sixteen inches over the top step at about the seventh hour and it was the second morning of a bank holding there. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working, and said the figure for the next time, and said which of the four of the four leaves was left.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. Three things every morning, and the man of forty-four is on that flat on the fourth morning of this week with a wedge and an old nail in a handcart socket, which is two of the three done in ash.

“*About sixteen inches at the seventh hour, and it is five days of the coming back, and that is a full bank and it holds, and that is the second morning of it and I have said the count twice this week.*”

“*A bank that is full holds at sixteen until it comes off, and I gave you that on Friday and I am giving it again because about four of you need it twice.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in three days. Three days after the morning it is full, and the morning it was full was Friday.*”

“*And there are four leaves and two have been read, and the third is going to be read this morning, and the fourth is sitting on that flat with a nail through the corner of it.*”

“*And the man of fifty-four is the reason it is going a leaf at a time and not all four, and I want that said in about nine ears, because about four of you have started looking at me like a man who is running a meeting.*”

“*I am not running a meeting. I am holding a gravel and reading a water-stone, and a thing with a nail in it came out of the dark on Friday night and there is nothing else to do with it but put it in front of people.*”

“*And I have a full bank on a Sunday for the second morning and about nine of us on it, and I have had that four times in nineteen years, and in three of those four there was nobody else on the bank at all.*”

Then a man of sixty-one read the third leaf out loud in about nine ears, and said it was about one child and not about a number of children, and said the working was not in the document.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has a book of heads and carts and two numbers in it in eleven years. He has refused since the second day of the hundred and thirty-sixth week to write a bench’s notice in it, and gave that figure out loud once and has not given it since. He does not write down what anybody says after a thing he reads.

“*Here is the third leaf, and I have turned the first two over and the fourth is under this one, and a man of fifty-four is watching me do it.*”

“*It is about a child. It is one child and it has a name on it, and I am going to say the name, because a man of fifty-four read a name on Friday and went quiet for about half a minute.*”

“*It says four miles up this cart road from this landing there is a bakehouse, and about nine houses in that village.*”

“*And in one of those houses there is a child of six who has not kept down what she has eaten since the fourth day of the hundred and thirty-sixth week.*”

“*It says that is twenty-three days, and the working is in the document, and the working is a figure of days and not a figure of the child.*”

“*And it says the arrangement can treat her, and it says it will do so within nine days of this leaf being read.*”

“*The nine is in the document. I am not going to do that working either.*”

“*And there is a line at the foot of this leaf, and I am going to read the line, and I am going to read it last.*”

“*The line is: this treatment is given under the arrangement, and the entry of it shall be made in the book, and the person on whom it is done shall not be asked to consent.*”

“*That is the third leaf. That is a child, and a road, and a person who is not asked.*”

“*I have read it in about nine ears and I am not going to say one word about what I think of it.*”

Nobody on that bank said anything about the line at the foot for about a minute. Then a woman of twenty-eight said that the line was the whole of it and that a man of sixty-one had read it last.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from. She refused a man of thirty-one in nine sentences on the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-seventh week and said that a second time is a rule. She said her own name on that bank on the first day of the hundred and thirty-ninth week and has not gone back down that road since.

Then a man of about fifty-two said what on that third leaf he could put a line under and what he could not, and gave the working for the part he could.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well. He put a mark on one of two sheets of deal, four lines under him, eleven years ago, because he was the only one of nine who was not asked what the line was for. On Friday morning he checked two figures out loud and named the third, and about four of you have been asking him since which is which.

“*A hundred and eighty-one days, and that is eight hundred and twenty-three less six hundred and forty-two, and I am giving you the working because a boy of thirteen has made it a rule on that bank.*”

“*And I am on this bank and I did not come up this morning for the reading, and I want about nine of you to notice that and not to make a thing of it.*”

“*I have been four miles down that cart road twice this week and I did not come up for it, and about four of you know that and about four of you do not.*”

“*The line at the foot of that leaf is the whole of it, and I am not the first one of you to have worked that out.*”

“*A man of sixty-one read it last, and did not say so, and I am not going to thank him for reading it at all.*”

“*A thing can be done to a person and entered in a book and corrected later, and the person is not asked, and the correction does not need them.*”

“*That is four sentences, and all four of them are in that document, and not one of them is in a voice.*”

“*I have not said that I would refuse the child.*”

“*I want that said in about nine ears before anybody puts it in my mouth.*”

“*I have a child of six in a village two days off that I could not go to on Wednesday, and I am not a fool about what a treatment means.*”

“*And I have a hundred and eighty-one days and a name in ink on a line four hundred miles off.*”

“*And I am not going to stand on this bank and be the one who says no to a sick child in front of about nine of you.*”

“*So I am going to ask the question that has to be asked, and I am going to ask it of a man of thirty-one and not of the thing on the flat.*”

“*And the question is not whether that child should be treated. Everybody in about nine of your ears knows she should be treated, and so do I, and a man of sixty-one read that in a leaf this morning.*”

“*The question is who writes down the day it was done, and what happens on the day somebody reads that line back and finds it is wrong.*”

“*And I am not asking it this morning. I am telling you that I am going to be asked it and I am telling you now so that about four of you can think of an answer before I have one.*”

Then a man of about fifty-two said which parts of the third leaf he could check, and that one of them he could not, and gave the reason with the figure in it.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well. He put a mark on one of two sheets of deal, four lines under him, eleven years ago, because he was the only one of nine who was not asked what the line was for.

“*Twenty-three days I can check. A day is a figure I can put a line under, and the fourth day of the hundred and thirty-sixth week is a day a man of forty-three has been keeping for nineteen years.*”

“*And twenty-three days is eight hundred and twenty-three less eight hundred, and that I can do in my head.*”

“*And six years old I cannot check, and neither can about four of you, and I am not going to pretend that a child of six is a figure of a person I have a line under.*”

“*And I can hear a figure of a person and I cannot check one, and I said that on Friday and about four of you heard me and about four of you did not.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said he was going up that cart road on his own two feet, and said what he was going for and what he was not going for, and did not ask anybody to come.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and sixty-nine days, two hundred and sixty-nine being eight hundred and twenty-three less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and thirty-eight days old, a hundred and thirty-eight being eight hundred and twenty-three less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*I am going up that cart road on my own two feet, and I am not asking anybody to come.*”

“*I am not going to ask because the last time a man of fifty-four went down ninety steps alone, about four of us made a thing of it in about nine ears, and he had not asked for that either.*”

“*I am going because there is a child of six four miles up that road, and I have read her name out loud in about nine of your ears off a piece of paper.*”

“*And nobody on this bank has laid a hand on her in six years, and I have not either, and I am not going to pretend the reading is the same as the laying on of hands.*”

“*And I am not going to take a figure back.*”

“*I am going to go and see whether a child of six who has not kept down what she has eaten for twenty-three days is the same child that leaf is about.*”

Then he did not go for about a quarter of an hour, because a man of forty-three said the water was still over the low end of the flat and over the turn, and gave the working and told him when.

“*You are not going up that road at the eighth hour.*”

“*The water is over the low end of that flat and it is over the turn, and it is not going to be off the turn until Wednesday of the hundred and fortieth week.*”

“*That is the working. Three days after the morning it was full, and the morning it was full was Friday.*”

“*And I am not going to be the man who lets a man of thirty-one walk into four feet of water on a cart road on a Sunday to prove a point about a child.*”

“*Come back on the Wednesday of the hundred and fortieth week at the seventh hour. The water will be off that flat and off the turn, and you can go up, and I will not say a word about it.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the man of forty-three was right, and that he was not going to argue on a Sunday, and said what he was going to do with the morning instead, and the want and the mistake and the cost, and gave the figure for the standing offer with the subtraction in it.

“*He is right and I am not going to argue with him on a Sunday about a road.*”

“*I have been about two hours doing the thing I do, which is deciding when a question is finished.*”

“*And this morning I decided to go and look at a child instead of asking about nine of you whether I should.*”

“*The mistake is the thirty-fourth of the same mistake, and I gave it out loud on Saturday morning, and I am not going to number it twice.*”

“*The want has moved shape in two days and it has not moved size.*”

“*On Friday I wanted somebody else to carry the figures.*”

“*This morning I want a person in that village to be able to say, in about nine ears, that a man of thirty-one did not come up that road because a man of forty-three told him the water was over the turn.*”

“*A hundred and twenty-three days. A hundred and twenty-three is eight hundred and twenty-three less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer.*”

“*And the cost is a girl of six who is described in a document none of us has seen, by a person none of us has met, on the strength of a leaf a man of sixty-one read in about nine ears.*”

“*And nobody in that village has been asked whether the description is right, and nobody on this bank can ask, because it is four miles up a cart road and it is under at the turn.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a woman of twenty-eight who said she is not going to be the one who says no to a sick child, and who asked a question of me instead, and who is not going to be thanked for it.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man of forty-three who told me the water was over the turn, and who is right, and who has been giving about nine of us the water every morning for nineteen years and has never once been asked to be right about anything else.*”

“*The right of refusal is not restored this morning, and a thing done to a child who is not asked is not it.*”

“*And I am not going to say what I think it is instead, because about four of you would hear that as a man of thirty-one building a case at about the eleventh hour on a Sunday.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Sunday with sixteen inches of water over the top step above them, and the third of four leaves read out loud, and the figure of a child of six read out loud with her name on it. A man of thirty-one was told by a man of forty-three that the water was over the turn and accepted it, and did not go up that road.

---

The fever was seventy-seven weeks and one day old. He had been in that county two hundred and sixty-nine days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and thirty-eight days old, and a hundred and twenty-three days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

A man of sixty-one read a third leaf about one child of six four miles up that cart road, and a line at the foot of it, and the figure of twenty-three days with the working in it.
And a woman of twenty-eight said she had not come up for the reading, said she was not going to be the one who says no to a sick child, and asked a man of thirty-one a question instead.

A man of thirty-one went up two steps towards that cart road and was stopped by the water over the turn, took the answer, and said what it cost him that the answer was right.
And he said the cost is a girl of six described by a person none of us has met, and that nobody in that village has been asked whether the description is right.
