# Chapter 819: Second Day Of The Hundred And Thirty-Ninth Week, And A Wednesday, And A Woman Of Twenty-Eight Says She Is Not Going To Decide This Week And Gives Two Things She Is Going To Look At, And A Man Of Thirty-One Tries To Produce A Witness For Her And Is Caught By Her In Front Of About Nine Of Them, And Nothing Is Decided And Nothing Is Refused

Second day of the hundred and thirty-ninth week. His five hundred and sixty-ninth morning. Four hundred and nineteen days after the settlement. The fever seventy-six weeks and four days. Three hundred and seventy-three days since the division. Three hundred and nineteen days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

The bank was about four inches over the top step at about the seventh hour, and it was one day of the coming back. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working, and said that about four of them had come up that bank yesterday and gone down ninety cut steps and come back up. He said he was not going to ask any of them about it.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years and has never once been asked to leave it. Three things every morning. About nine of them walk past the stone he named on the Sunday of the week before last.

“*About four inches at the seventh hour, and it is one day of the coming back, and about six of those ninety steps are under this morning.*”

“*Four inches a day is the working underneath it. It will be eight and twelve and then full, and I will be giving it every morning that bank is coming back up.*”

“*Yesterday morning about four of us went down ninety cut steps on that bank with the water off them, and came back up.*”

“*That is the fifth time in nineteen years that anybody has done that while I have been standing here. I gave three things at the sixth hour and at the seventh hour and did not have to say them twice.*”

“*And I noticed that, and I have not worked out what it means.*”

“*And I am not going to ask any of them about it.*”

“*A man of forty-four asked me on Tuesday morning and I said it was not an instruction. It is still not an instruction, and it is a different thing from an instruction, and about four of you are already standing about nine feet off it.*”

Then a woman of twenty-eight said that she was not going to decide this week, gave two things she was going to look at instead, and said both of them were given so that about four of them could check them.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from. She refused a man of thirty-one in nine sentences on the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-seventh week and said that a second time is a rule. She said her own name on that bank yesterday for the first time in this county.

“*A hundred and seventy-seven days. That is eight hundred and nineteen less six hundred and forty-two, and I counted it on the walk and I am giving you the working because that is what about nine of you have started doing and I am not going to be the one who does not.*”

“*I asked that bank yesterday for a word to do with a person in a room where nobody can be sent for about one.*”

“*And a man of thirty-one did not answer me, and said he was not going to be asked it twice.*”

“*And here is where I have got to and it is not a decision and I am going to say that first.*”

“*I am not going to decide this week whether I can still trust him.*”

“*About four of you have been waiting since Tuesday for me to say one or the other, and about four of you have not. Both of those are about me and not about him, and I am not going to give either of them what they are waiting for.*”

“*And I am going to tell you why I am not, in one sentence, and then I am going to give you two things instead.*”

“*A boy of thirteen worked out on Tuesday that a thing said in a lane is not a thing anybody can check.*”

“*I have been in this county a hundred and seventy-seven days and I have watched every morning of it.*”

“*And I have never once seen him be able to take a thing he wanted and not put a fence round it.*”

“*Four times. A day against a name. A case he cannot serve. A figure of the days he has been here. And my name on Tuesday.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said he had been thinking about how to answer the question, gave her two things and what she wanted to hear, and she stopped him at the second one.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and sixty-five days, two hundred and sixty-five being eight hundred and nineteen less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and thirty-four days old, a hundred and thirty-four being eight hundred and nineteen less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*I am going to give the two things and I am going to give them before I have worked them out properly, and I have had about two hours, and about four of you can check them this morning.*”

“*One. That nobody in this county can be sent for about anything, and that a man of thirty-one has been saying it out loud on that bank since before it had about nine of us on it, in a way that has kept about nine of us from being cruel to each other.*”

“*I have never once been cruel to anybody. I do not think I am capable of it, and I do not think I have ever wanted to be, and I do not think that is the thing she is asking me about.*”

“*Two. That on Tuesday she asked me a question in front of about nine of you and I did not answer her, and I said she was not going to get it twice.*”

“*And that was me deciding something again, and I have had two days about it.*”

“*And here is what I would like her to hear, and I am going to say it, and you are going to tell me if it is the wrong thing to have said.*”

“*A person in a room where nobody can be sent for about one is called a person.*”

“*And about nine of us have been calling them that on this bank since before it had about nine of us on it, without knowing that there was a question.*”

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from.

“*Stop there.*”

“*That is the second one. You have got as far as the second one and it is about the fence.*”

“*I have been in this county a hundred and seventy-seven days and I have watched you get to the second one four times.*”

“*And I am not going to tell you that you are wrong about the first one.*”

“*A man of forty-four said on Tuesday that one person being able to say yes to a road would be easier and safer, and a woman of sixty-four said what that is from the inside, and both of those were about this bank and I was on it.*”

“*And I am not going to be given an answer by a man of thirty-one on a Wednesday morning.*”

“*If there is a word then it will be in a room and not on this bank, and about nine of you will be in that room, and not one of them will have said it here first.*”

“*And that is not me refusing to decide. That is me saying that I am not going to be given it by you.*”

“*And there is a difference, and you know there is a difference, because you have spent every morning since before that bank had about nine of us on it telling about nine of us the difference.*”

Then a boy of thirteen said his own number, said the figure was still seven, said what he thought a person was called, and said that he was not going to say it twice.

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and sixty-six days. He counted them in the lane this morning and it is one more than yesterday.

“*A hundred and sixty-six days. That is eight hundred and nineteen less six hundred and fifty-three.*”

“*And it is still seven. I gave it twice on Monday at about the ninth hour and at about the eleventh, and it is seven, and it was seven yesterday and it was seven on Sunday.*”

“*And I am not going to be the fifth reason in this.*”

“*There is already four of you giving reasons about a woman of twenty-eight on this bank this morning, and a man of thirty-one gave a fifth.*”

“*That is five reasons and it is about one person, and it is more reasons than any of you have had for anything else.*”

“*So I am going to say the one thing I know and it is a small one.*”

“*A person is called whatever the other people in the room call them, and it is not in anybody’s gift.*”

“*That is what I worked out in the lane. It is not worth saying, and I am saying it once, because a boy of thirteen who keeps a figure ought to say the thing he has when about nine of them are standing about nine feet off each other.*”

“*And I am not going to say it twice and I am not going to work out what it is worth.*”

Then a man of fifty-four said his figure, said what he is going to do with the two lengths of ash on a handcart socket, and thanked nobody.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel on that landing and cannot read. He has asked for that line in the open every morning since the Thursday of the hundred and twenty-fifth week. He has got up about half an hour earlier every morning since the Wednesday of the week before last, and went down ninety cut steps yesterday morning before the reading.

“*Ninety-eight, and one more than ninety-seven, and I have got it before you for the ninth day running.*”

“*And about four of you have stopped being surprised, and that is the right order for that to happen in.*”

“*And a man of forty-four put two lengths of ash across that handcart socket on Tuesday, with a wedge and an old nail. He said on Tuesday morning that it will hold as long as that nail holds.*”

“*I have put my hand on it. I cannot read a figure of a nail, and I can put my hand on two lengths of ash and a nail.*”

“*And I am telling about nine of you that I have put my hand on it, and I am not going to tell you what I think of it.*”

“*And I am not going to say a word of thanks to him for the two lengths of ash, and I am not going to be thanked for putting my hand on it.*”

“*Because a woman of twenty-four is standing at the bottom of that cut and nobody knows that but a man of forty-three and me.*”

“*I am not going to say anything else about her and I have said that on purpose and I have not given a figure for the day I saw her and I am not going to.*”

Then a man of thirty-one came to the end of it and said what he wanted, what he had got wrong, and what it cost. He gave the figure for the standing offer in his own mouth with the subtraction in it, and said that he had tried to hand a woman of twenty-eight the answer to her own question and had been stopped in about nine ears.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and sixty-five days, and the cut across that palm is a hundred and thirty-four days old.

“*Here is the want and it has not moved in thirteen days. It was twelve days yesterday.*”

“*And I am not going to give you a fourth change of shape this week, because about four of you are waiting for one and they can wait.*”

“*The want is a woman of forty at the ninth hour.*”

“*It has got smaller three times this week and I do not think it can get any smaller, and I am going to keep saying it at that size and let about nine of you decide what to do about a small want.*”

“*The mistake is the thirty-first of the same mistake, and it is about an hour old.*”

“*It is the same one I gave at the ninth hour on Thursday with the lime. It is not a new mistake, and I am not going to pretend a new number makes it a new one.*”

“*A woman of twenty-eight gave me two things at about the ninth hour and I heard them. About a quarter of an hour later I began working out an answer to give back to her.*”

“*And I had a witness in my mouth before I had finished the second thing she said.*”

“*That is what I have been doing since before that bank had about nine of us on it, and it is what a boy of thirteen asked about nine of you for on the Tuesday of last week.*”

“*And four of the four answers were people saying the same thing for him and for each other, and mine was for me.*”

“*The cost is that a woman of twenty-eight told me in front of about nine of you that I was at the second one, and that I have got to the second one four times.*”

“*And that I was not going to be given an answer by me.*”

“*And she is right. About four of you knew it before she said it and about four of you did not.*”

“*And that is twice this week that I have found something out about nine of us had already worked out.*”

“*A hundred and nineteen days. A hundred and nineteen is eight hundred and nineteen less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer.*”

“*And the right of refusal is not restored this morning. A woman of twenty-eight stopped me at the second one in about nine ears this morning and I have not been refused and I have not been granted and I am not going to say which of those I wanted.*”

“*She is not going to decide whether she trusts me this week.*”

“*I am not going to push for it and I am not going to be the one who says she is not to, and about four of you are going to say that is arranging it again.*”

“*And it is.*”

“*And I am going to say it out loud at about the eleventh hour rather than have about four of you work it out.*”

“*That is four times now that saying a thing out loud has turned out to be the smaller of the two things, and it has not once stopped me doing the larger one first.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a woman of twenty-eight who came up four miles yesterday and is not going to decide this week.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man of forty-four with two lengths of ash in a socket, or of a boy of thirteen who has said a small true thing once.*”

“*Nobody says a word of thanks to me for a Wednesday morning.*”

“*And nobody is to be told that a boy of thirteen was brave for refusing to be the fifth reason about one person, and nobody is to say a word of thanks to me for a Wednesday morning.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Wednesday, with four inches of water over the top step above them. A woman of twenty-eight had not decided anything about a man of thirty-one, and had told about nine of them two things she would look at instead.
