# Chapter 834: Third Day Of The Hundred And Forty-First Week, And A Thursday, And The Water Is Off All Ninety At About The Sixth Hour For The Twenty-First Time, And Two Margins Are Marked In Lime, And A Man Of Thirty-Four Says A Place You Cannot Leave Is Not A Margin, And A Man Of Thirty-One Says He Is Not Going To Explain What A Margin Is

Third day of the hundred and forty-first week. His five hundred and eighty-fourth morning. Four hundred and thirty-four days after the settlement. The fever seventy-eight weeks and five days. Three hundred and eighty-eight days since the division. Three hundred and thirty-four days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water came off that bank at about the sixth hour and all ninety of those steps were walkable by about the seventh. The man of forty-three gave the hour and the count and the number of the time, and gave the working back to the last time it happened, and did not give a figure of inches because there were none.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He has not given the figure of how many of those ninety steps are under since Monday, and this morning there was no figure of that kind in the world to give.

“*It came off at about the sixth hour and all ninety of those steps are walkable now, and there is no figure of inches to give you this morning and that is the first time in nineteen years I have been glad there is not.*”

“*It is the twenty-first time. The last time was eight days ago at about the fifth hour, and that was the second day of the hundred and fortieth week, and it was the twentieth.*”

“*Eight days on from the twentieth is the twenty-first, and that is the working and there is nothing in it that I had to look up.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in eight days, and that is the fourth day of the hundred and forty-second week, and I can give you that now because the bank is not full and I know the rule.*”

Then two margins were marked in lime, and a man of thirty-one said the figures out loud, and a man of about fifty-two checked them, and a man of thirty-four in an ash said what a margin is not, and a man of thirty-one said he was not going to say what it was.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and eighty days, two hundred and eighty being eight hundred and thirty-four less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and forty-nine days old, a hundred and forty-nine being eight hundred and thirty-four less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*Two, and I am marking them now, and I have got the figures and a man of about fifty-two is going to say whether they are right.*”

“*The low margin is on the bank above those ninety cut steps. It is about forty paces of mine, and two yards to a pace, and that is about eighty yards.*”

“*The high margin is on that cart road above the turn. It is about sixty paces, and that is about a hundred and twenty yards.*”

“*And here is the rule and it is two sentences and both of them are about water.*”

“*The low margin is under when the bank is a full bank, and a full bank is sixteen inches over the top step, and a man of forty-three gave me that figure on Monday and he has given it every morning for nineteen years.*”

“*The high margin this water cannot reach at all. It is there for a different water and a woman of twenty-nine is the only person on this bank who has ever seen the other one, and she is not going to describe it to me today.*”

Ivo Kellow is thirty-four and has been in an ash about twenty feet off the low side of that drain, a hundred yards along it, for two hundred and four days, and he has a day against his name which is not a day of this week. He came up that bank unasked for the third time this week and said what the lime is not.

“*Two hundred and four days. That is eight hundred and thirty-four less six hundred and thirty.*”

“*I was not brought up. Nobody brought me up. I am here because there are two lines cut and painted on the ground and I am inside one of them, and I would rather be here when that is said than be found out on Saturday.*”

“*I am not going. And I am not going to give you a reason, because I have given about nine of you a reason every other morning of this week and a man of thirty-one has got a collection.*”

“*I have been in this ash two hundred and four days and I know what is under it. That is the whole of the reason and it is not a moral position and it is not the right of anything.*”

“*And I am going to say the other thing, and I would rather say it at about nine feet than have about four of you work it out on Saturday.*”

“*A strip of ground with lime on it is only a place to go to if you are allowed to leave it again. Nobody has said that anybody is. A woman of sixty-four said the arrangement holds no day against any person and no person may be sent for, and that is a promise about a book, and a strip of ground with lime on it is not a book.*”

“*So either you can go to it and come back, or it is not a margin, and I have not been told which, and I am not going to be the one who works it out in about nine ears on a bank.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said three things, and the second of them was that he was not going to say what a margin is, and the third of them was that this was not the right of refusal and he would not let about nine of them call it that.

“*A woman of forty-four told me on Monday that a thing with two lines on it is one thing, and she was talking about a post, and I have been thinking about the lime since the seventh hour.*”

“*Here is the first thing. Nobody is being made to go anywhere. A man of thirty-four has said he is not going and he is not going and I am not going to spend the morning on it.*”

“*Here is the second thing, and I am saying it before about four of you ask me for it.*”

“*I am not going to tell you what a margin is. A woman of sixty-four told me in this same week that a person who gives nineteen years away to explain what a thing is has stopped owning the thing, and I am not going to stand here and explain a strip of ground to about nine of you.*”

“*A man of about fifty-two has a fifth thing he cannot check and he named it on the third day of last week. This is mine and I am naming it now. I cannot check whether a margin works. I can only tell you where the lime is and when the water comes.*”

“*Here is the third thing and it is the one I would like written down somewhere, and I am saying it out loud instead.*”

“*This is not the right of refusal. Not the lime, not the two lines, not being told in about nine ears at the sixth hour, and not a man of thirty-four saying he is not going.*”

“*The right of refusal is not on this bank on any morning of this week, and it is not on the fourth day of the hundred and forty-second week either.*”

“*And if about nine of you start using that word about lime, I am going to say so in about nine ears, and I am not going to be quiet about it.*”

“*The day somebody uses it for lime is the day it stops meaning anything.*”

Then a man of fifty-four asked for the figures of the two margins to be said a second time, and said what he could not do with them, and gave his own count in his own mouth.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel on that landing and cannot read. He has asked for that line in the open every morning since the Thursday of the hundred and twenty-fifth week. He has a hundred and thirteen mornings and the figure is one more than the morning before.

“*A hundred and thirteen mornings. One more than yesterday, and the twenty-fourth morning of that run.*”

“*Say the two lengths again. I want the forty and the sixty and I want the yards, and I am not going to pretend I can do anything with a yard.*”

“*Here is what I am going to do with them and I would rather say it than have it noticed.*”

“*I cannot read a line of lime. Not because it is not writing. Because I cannot see it from where I am going to be standing, which is the top of those steps, and the low margin is below me.*”

“*So the figure of the low margin is a figure I have been given by a man of thirty-one and checked by a man of about fifty-two. It is not a figure of mine.*”

“*And if the water is at the top step and I am on the top step, then I am in it, and I would like that said out loud today while I am standing here.*”

“*And I am not asking to be put above the top step. I am asking to be told what the figure is, in about nine ears, twice, and then I will stand where the figure puts me.*”

Then a man of forty-four and a boy of thirteen went to the eleven houses at about the seventh hour and told it again, and came back, and the boy said what the second telling had found, and one thing he had been told to keep to himself he kept.

Tomas Elrick is forty-four and pushed a handcart up that cart road for twenty-six years. He went to the six houses on the low side with the boy, and he did not go to the five above the turn, and he said why before anybody asked him.

“*Six, and then the five above the turn, and I did the six first because they are the ones in it.*”

“*A man of thirty-one did the same order yesterday and a woman of forty-four made him say what he had done, and I am not going to argue with her about it. I have not been to the five yet and I will go on Friday morning.*”

“*And nobody at any of the six said no to being told and nobody said yes either, and that is not a figure of anything and I am not going to give it one.*”

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and eighty-one days, and he has counted them in a lane, and he went to the eleven houses with a man of forty-four and came back at about the tenth hour.

“*A hundred and eighty-one days, and it is one more than the morning before, and I counted it in the lane before the light came up.*”

“*Nine of the eleven were in. Two were out, and one of the two is the house up the low side where a woman of twenty-eight was put, and I am not going to say more about that one than that.*”

“*And I counted the houses again at the sixth one and at the eleventh and it is eleven both times.*”

“*And here is the thing I was told to keep and I am keeping it, and I am telling you I kept it, because a man of about fifty-two said on Tuesday that he was going to say the days and not the asks, and I am doing the same thing with a house.*”

“*And here is the thing I am not keeping. At the ninth house a person said they had heard it yesterday and did not want it said again, and I said it again anyway, because a man of thirty-one told me to tell everybody twice and I did not have a better answer at the door.*”

“*I am not going to make a word of it and I am not going to make it smaller. I am saying that I did it, and that I would do it differently, and that I do not know how.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said what he had wanted, what he had got wrong, and what it had cost, and gave the figure for the standing offer in his own mouth with the subtraction in it.

“*Here is the want. I want the two margins to be somewhere the water cannot get to, and I know the high one is, and I have marked the low one forty paces long on ground that will be under in four days, which is the day the bank is full again.*”

“*That is the want and it is not a good one and I have had it since the fourth hour.*”

“*Here is what I got wrong. I marked the low margin and then I stood here and told about nine of you it was a place to go to, and a man of thirty-four heard me say it and I did not stop him, and I had heard myself say it.*”

“*It is not a place to go to. I have not got a rule that makes it one. I have got eighty yards of lime and a figure for when the water arrives at it.*”

“*And the cost of that is that eleven houses have been told twice in two mornings, and about four of you have heard me say the word place, and about four of you have believed me, and I am not going to be able to take that back by saying it differently on Saturday.*”

“*A hundred and thirty-four days. A hundred and thirty-four is eight hundred and thirty-four less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer, and none of it is a margin.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man of thirty-four who said he was not going and gave a figure of a day in an ash instead of a reason, and who said out loud that what a margin is not, when he did not have to.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man of fifty-four who asked for two figures to be said twice and then said he would stand where the figure puts him, and who is going to be in eighty yards of lime on Monday, which is the day the bank is full again.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a boy of thirteen who told it twice at a door because he had been told to tell everybody twice, and who has said so, and who is not going to be told that saying so was brave.*”

“*And the right of refusal is not restored this morning, and I said so at about the seventh hour and I am saying it again at the eleventh, and about four of you have been waiting for me to change my mind about it since the seventh hour.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Thursday, with all ninety of those steps walkable below them for the twenty-first time, and two margins marked in lime above and below them.
A man of thirty-four in an ash had said that a place you cannot leave is not a margin. A man of thirty-one had said in about nine ears that none of it was the right of refusal, and that he was not going to let about nine of them use the word for lime.

The fever was seventy-eight weeks and five days old. He had been in that county two hundred and eighty days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and forty-nine days old, and a hundred and thirty-four days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

The water came off all ninety at about the sixth hour for the twenty-first time, eight days on from the twentieth, and a man of forty-three gave the next time in eight days, which is the fourth day of the hundred and forty-second week.
And a man of thirty-four in an ash, unasked, said that a strip of ground with lime on it is only a place to go to if you are allowed to leave it again, and that nobody had said that anybody was.

A man of thirty-one said he was not going to explain what a margin is, and named a fifth thing he cannot check, and said in about nine ears that the lime was not the right of refusal and that he would not be quiet if about nine of them used the word for it.
And a man of fifty-four asked for two figures to be said twice, and then said that he would stand where the figure puts him, and that if the water is at the top step and he is on the top step then he is in it.
