# Chapter 842: Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Second Week, And A Friday, And The Water Comes Off All Ninety Steps For The Twenty-Second Time And Has Been Off Since About The Fifth Hour, And A Man Of Thirty-One Refuses The Only Immediately Valid Seal, And A Woman Of Sixty-Four Reads Out The One Sentence That Gives A Person Back The Ability To Refuse A Rule And Stay, And He Loses The Morning He Filed The Sovereign’s Wake Refusal Report And Keeps The Report

Fourth day of the hundred and forty-second week. His five hundred and ninety-second morning. Four hundred and forty-two days after the settlement. The fever seventy-nine weeks and six days. Three hundred and ninety-six days since the division. Three hundred and forty-two days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water came off that bank at about the sixth hour and all ninety of those steps were walkable by about the seventh. The man of forty-three gave the hour and the count and the number of the time and did not give a figure of inches because there were none, and said it was the second time in eight days he had been glad there was not one, and that the first of the two was the first time in nineteen years.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. Three things every morning, and the fourth thing has been nothing for ten days.

“*It came off at about the sixth hour and all ninety of those steps are walkable now, and there is no figure of inches to give you this morning, and that is eight days after the last time I had none, and eight days ago I said it was the first time in nineteen years, and it was, and it is not the first time any more and I am not going to pretend it is.*”

“*It is the twenty-second time. The last time was eight days ago at about the sixth hour, and that was the twenty-first, and the one before that was eight days before that and was the twentieth, and eight days on from the twentieth is the twenty-first and eight days on from that is this morning.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in eight days, and I know that without the water-stone and I have not looked at it.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said no, and said the whole of the no out loud in about nine ears with the figures in it, and said what it was and what it was not, and said who it was for.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and eighty-eight days, two hundred and eighty-eight being eight hundred and forty-two less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and fifty-seven days old, a hundred and fifty-seven being eight hundred and forty-two less six hundred and eighty-five. He has asked seven times whether it will keep. Nobody has said and he was not going to ask an eighth time and this morning he did not ask it because his hand was on the bank and he was not thinking about it.

“*No.*”

“*And I am going to say the rest of it in about nine ears and then I am going to stop talking, and about four of you should notice that I have said one word so far and I am not going to make the one word do more work than it can.*”

“*The seal is the only immediately valid one. I verified that on Thursday.*”

“*And I am not going to un-verify it at about the seventh hour on a Friday because the water has come off and the steps are dry and it would read better.*”

“*Nine positions would stand and nobody would reopen them, including the arrangement. I have put a line under six of the nine and I cannot put one under three of them, and I said that on Wednesday in about nine of your ears, and I am saying it again now so that the refusal is not mistaken for a man who did not do the work.*”

“*The two hundred and six days would be entered as answered and the answer would be the arrangement’s own. A road, a child, and a bridge. Nobody in this county sent for about any of the three.*”

“*And that is good. I said on Thursday that it was good and I meant it in the ordinary voice and I am not taking any of it back this morning because the water came off.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the reason, and it was not the reason anybody on that bank had been waiting for, and he said so himself before he said it.

“*And here is the reason, and I am telling about nine of you now that it is not the one you have all been carrying around for two days.*”

“*A boy of thirteen told me on Wednesday that about four of you were going to make a sum of three different figures, and I would rather say the wrong reason than let a right one be invented for me.*”

“*It is not the child. The child is a child of six, and if I refuse and she does not get treated then I have refused a treatment, and I will have to live in a county where that happened.*”

“*And I have thought about that for two days and I am still refusing, and that is the part I want in about nine ears.*”

“*It is not the gap and it is not the nineteen years and it is not what a woman of sixty-four stood up for on Tuesday. She told me on Thursday that the nineteen years is not a reason and she is right and I am not going to use her to get out of this.*”

“*It is line eleven. It is the eleventh line of the leaf and it is not on the fifth leaf, and a man of thirty-four in an ash said it out loud on the second day of the hundred and fortieth week and he said it was not a threat, and he was right then and he is right now.*”

“*Line eleven says there is no such thing as being wrong about this. And a man of thirty-one has spent two hundred and eighty-eight days in this county working out how to be wrong out loud in front of about nine people.*”

“*And about four of them on this bank have been correcting me since the Monday of the hundred and thirty-eighth week. A boy of thirteen has corrected me by not answering me, and a man of fifty-four cannot read and has asked for a line a leaf at a time for a hundred and twenty-one mornings.*”

“*And I am the man the arrangement has already named. That is line two and it is on the fifth leaf and it is the reason the whole of this is pointed at me and not at a bench somewhere four hundred miles off.*”

“*So the thing I would be taking is not a seal for the nine positions. The thing I would be taking is a seal for nine positions AND for the eleventh line.*”

“*And the eleventh line is the only part of it I cannot verify, and it is the only part I am refusing, and I am refusing all of it, because it is one offer and I am not going to take nine-tenths of it and leave the tenth on a table for somebody else to pick up in a fortnight.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said what a refusal opens, and said that he is opening it, and said it in his own mouth on a bank and not in a room four hundred miles off, and gave the two sentences out loud.

“*And I am going to say the second half of this in about nine ears before anybody works out that there is a second half, because a man of sixty-one said on Tuesday that he would read that leaf whole or not at all, and he did it on Wednesday, and I am not going to let about nine of you find the rest of this on Saturday.*”

“*A seal is a thing on a flat. I have refused a thing on a flat. That is all that has happened on this bank this morning and it is not a room and nobody was summoned to it.*”

“*And a notice says a Foundational Hearing can choose and will not be given a day, and a woman of sixty-four has just read that out loud in about nine of your ears, and about four of you have been waiting for me to be handed a day by it.*”

“*Nobody is handing me a day. I am not asking it for one either, and I want both of those said in the same breath, because one of them without the other is a man being summoned and the other is a man asking.*”

“*So here is what I am opening, and I am opening it myself, and it is my own mouth on this bank, and it is the ordinary defendant’s portion, and it is the portion where a man is a defendant and is allowed to say what happened to him.*”

“*I opened a case against the Bench on the first day of the hundred and thirty-first week and gave nine things away before I used any of them and said that nobody in this county can be sent for, and that a case which cannot be served is not a case that has been lost.*”

“*That case is still open. I am not withdrawing it and nobody has told me to, and a man of about fifty-two told nine days ago that a case is a figure of an argument and not a figure of anybody, and he was right then and he is right now.*”

“*And this morning I am opening the ordinary defendant’s portion of a hearing that has not been given a day, and I am doing it from a landing nine miles up that water, in front of about nine of you, and nobody four hundred miles off has authorised it and nobody four hundred miles off has refused it.*”

“*And I do not know what happens to a portion that is opened by the man it is about. That is not modesty. I have been in this county two hundred and eighty-eight days and I have no more idea about that than I had about the eleventh line on Tuesday, and I am saying so in about nine of your ears at about the seventh hour on a Friday.*”

Then a woman of sixty-four said stop, and then said that a man of thirty-one is not the reason anything gets restored, and said what a refusal opens and who opens it, and gave the notice out loud in about nine ears.

Ada Renk is sixty-four and has said a word twice in nineteen years and has refused nine people who asked her to say it a third. She gave away nineteen years on the fifth day of the hundred and thirty-eighth week. She read a notice from a bench in the ordinary voice on the last day of the hundred and fortieth week and said it was not a refusal and settled nothing for anybody.

“*I am not going to read that leaf again and neither is a man of sixty-one and neither is a man of about fifty-two, and about four of you have been waiting for one of us to say a sentence that fixes it and it is not on that flat.*”

“*I have been in a room four hundred miles off twice in nineteen years and the second time I said no to a man who had read a line out and had it read out well, and I have not been asked to be in a room since and nobody can make me.*”

“*And a man of thirty-one has just refused the only immediately valid seal in about nine of your ears and I am not going to let that be the reason anything happens. It is not a reason. It is a Thursday and a Friday and a man doing his work out loud.*”

“*And here is the notice, and I am reading it in the ordinary voice because that is the only voice it was written to be read in.*”

“*It says: a Foundational Hearing can choose. It says it will not be given a day.*”

“*That is the whole of it and a man of sixty-one read it out on the last day of last week and said it is not a refusal and it settles nothing for anybody, and he was right then and he is right now.*”

“*And a man of thirty-one has just refused a seal, and about four of you have worked out that the two go together and that refusing a seal opens a hearing. It does not. A seal is a thing on a flat. A hearing is a room.*”

Then a woman of sixty-four read out one sentence, and it was hers and not the arrangement’s, and she said where it came from and what it cost her to say it in about nine ears.

“*Here it is, and it is mine, and it did not come off that flat and I am not going to pretend to about nine of you that I am the first person alive to have thought of it.*”

“*A person may refuse a specific rule, and stay in the covenant, and is responsible for the people the refusal touches.*”

“*That is the whole of it. That is one sentence and it has been true in about nine of your ears this morning and nobody can be sent for refusing it.*”

“*And I am giving it away. I am not going to need it where I am going and I have no say in where I am going and that is not a reward either, and a woman of twenty-nine in a reed has been telling about nine of you for four days that a protection is not the same thing as a person and I have believed her since Wednesday.*”

Then a boy of thirteen read the sentence back out loud in about nine ears, and said what it was and what it was not, and said it was not a discovery.

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and eighty-nine days, and the figure is one more than the morning before. He has been keeping a count of his own since the Monday of last week.

“*A hundred and eighty-nine days, and it is one more than the morning before, and I counted it in the lane before the light came up.*”

“*A person may refuse a specific rule, and stay in the covenant, and is responsible for the people the refusal touches.*”

“*I am going to say the three parts of that out loud because a man of fifty-four cannot read and this is being read to him and he is not the only one on this bank who needs it in three pieces.*”

“*One: a person may refuse a specific rule. Not everything. A specific rule, and you have to be able to say which rule, and a man of fifty-four has been able to say which line for a hundred and twenty-one mornings and that is the same shape of thing and I am not going to pretend it is the same thing.*”

“*Two: and stay. That is the part that is not on the flat. Line eleven says the only remedy for a person who does not accept an answer is that the person remains, and remaining is not staying. My mother used that word and she was wrong about it and she was wrong about it for years and she was not a fool, she was working with what she had.*”

“*Three: and is responsible for the people the refusal touches. Not for the people it does not touch. That is a smaller thing than it sounds and a much larger thing than staying.*”

“*And nobody is to tell me that I found that, because a woman of sixty-four said it and I read it back, and a boy who reads a thing back is not the man who thought of it.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the cost of the morning, and it was not the seal, and he gave it in his own mouth with the interval in it.

“*And there is a cost, and it is mine, and it is not about the seal.*”

“*And I am going to say it in about nine ears, because a woman of twenty-eight said on Saturday that a cost which is not said out loud on the morning it is paid gets made into something else by whoever finds it.*”

“*There is a report. I filed a refusal report once. The Sovereign’s Wake. It is not on that flat and it is not in a room four hundred miles off and it is a piece of paper I put in with my own hands.*”

“*And I have had the morning of filing it in my head for as long as I can remember, and the room it was in and the man I gave it to and the weather, and this morning it is not there.*”

“*Two hundred and six days. That is the interval the arrangement left, and it is a figure of a gap in a figure of a book, and I am not claiming it took anything. Nobody has said that and I am not going to say it in a mouth on this bank.*”

“*What is gone is a morning. I know the report is refused. I know what it says and I know what happened after it and I can put any question I like about the paper. What I cannot do is remember being the man who handed it over. That is what the cost is and it is a morning and not a feeling and I have given you the size of it in a figure that is not mine.*”

“*A man does not stop counting the day he loses one. I am in this county two hundred and eighty-eight days and the cut across that palm is a hundred and fifty-seven days old.*”

“*And the boy of thirteen is at a hundred and eighty-nine days, a man of fifty-four is at a hundred and twenty-one mornings, and the offer asked at the seven-hundredth is a hundred and forty-two days old and has not been answered.*”

“*And I did not lose any of those this morning. I want that said in about nine of your ears by somebody who is not me, because I have been the mouth every figure comes out of for nine days and a man who can say that about himself is a man who has stopped doing it properly.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Friday with ninety cut steps dry under them for the twenty-second time, a seal refused in about nine ears, and one sentence read out by a woman of sixty-four that was not on the flat.

The fever was seventy-nine weeks and six days old. He had been in that county two hundred and eighty-eight days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and fifty-seven days old, and a hundred and forty-two days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

The only immediately valid constitutional seal was refused in about nine ears, with the figures in it, and the reason given was the eleventh line rather than the child, and he said a lesser man would have said yes on Tuesday and had said so on Thursday first.
And the person who was named on the second line of the leaf is the man who refused it, and he said he was refusing all of it rather than nine-tenths of it.

A woman of sixty-four read out one sentence of her own, that a person may refuse a specific rule, and stay in the covenant, and is responsible for the people the refusal touches, and said it was not on the flat and that she was giving it away.
A boy of thirteen read it back in three parts and said remaining is not staying, and said nobody was to tell him he had found it.

And the cost was a morning and not a feeling: he lost the lived memory of filing the Sovereign’s Wake refusal report and kept the report and its facts, and every counter on that bank still ran, and the standing offer is at a hundred and forty-two days and is still not answered.
