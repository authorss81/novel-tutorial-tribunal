# Chapter 833: Second Day Of The Hundred And Forty-First Week, And A Wednesday, And The Zone Is Told Out Loud In About Nine Ears, And A Woman Of Sixty-Four Says That Is A Warning And Not A Telling, And A Woman Of Forty-Four Asks Which Of Two Things Matters More And Nobody Answers

Second day of the hundred and forty-first week. His five hundred and eighty-third morning. Four hundred and thirty-three days after the settlement. The fever seventy-eight weeks and four days. Three hundred and eighty-seven days since the division. Three hundred and thirty-three days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about sixteen inches over the top step at about the seventh hour and it was the fifth morning of a bank sitting at that figure. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working and the figure for the next time, and gave it in the new way again, and said that the new way would stand until the bank came off.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He has not given the figure of how many of those ninety steps are under since Monday, and about four of them have stopped asking him for it, which he says is the correct number of times to be asked for something he has not got.

“*About sixteen inches at the seventh hour, and it is eight days of the coming back.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in one day, and that is tomorrow at about the sixth hour, and I have given it in the new way three mornings running and I am not going back to the old one while the two agree.*”

Then at about the sixth hour a man of thirty-one told a zone out loud in about nine ears, and got the shape of it wrong, and a woman of sixty-four stopped him, and he told it again in a shape that was not a warning.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and seventy-nine days, two hundred and seventy-nine being eight hundred and thirty-three less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and forty-eight days old, a hundred and forty-eight being eight hundred and thirty-three less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*I am going to tell you what is going to happen in that zone, and I am going to say it once, and I am going to say it now and not on Saturday.*”

“*Three things may fail in there, and I am naming all three before anything is dug.*”

“*A bridge may fail. A well may fail. And the thing that keeps your names out of the arrangement’s books may fail in there, and that last one is not a bridge and it is not a well, and I am not going to soften it by putting it third.*”

“*And here is the fourth thing, and it is the one I would have left out.*”

“*The comparing that is coming means digging. And the digging is the likeliest cause of all three, and I am telling you that at the sixth hour on a Wednesday and nobody has put a spade in the ground.*”

Ada Renk is sixty-four and has a name in ink on that ninth line of a book four hundred miles off and has never had a day against it. She got up out of a book of heads and carts in the open nineteen years ago, and was forty-five when she worked out that the thing asking was not a person. She said on Sunday that she is not afraid of the eleventh line and is also tired of it.

“*Stop.*”

“*You opened with the word happen. You told about nine of us what is going to happen to us.*”

“*That is a warning. A warning is one person telling other people what is going to happen to them, and it is the shape of the thing on that flat, and you have been standing on this bank for two hundred and seventy-nine days and we are the ones it is happening to.*”

“*And I have been in a warning before. Nineteen years ago I was in one and it was a good warning and it came with a day against my name at the end of it.*”

Then a man of thirty-one told it again in a different shape, and gave the cost of each of the three things in it, and said which of them the cost fell on, and said that he could promise nobody anything at all.

“*Not what is going to happen. Here is what is going to be done, and here is what each of it costs, and here is who pays.*”

“*One. We are going to dig. It is about nine hundred yards of drain, from the cart road at the turn down to that bridge, taken eighteen inches deeper at the top end.*”

“*That nine hundred is my figure and I have not walked it and I am saying so at the sixth hour on a Wednesday, which is earlier than I would have said it.*”

“*A man of forty-four wants a hole under his road and a woman of twenty-nine wants the water off her reed, and the two of them cannot both have the first forty yards. The one that gave way was his.*”

“*That costs a man of forty-four nine feet of hole and four feet of depth and eleven years of a road that answers to nobody. He is the one who pays and he is standing here and he has already said so.*”

“*Two. We are going to find out what is under that bridge. It cannot be found out from a bank or from a leaf. It has to be found out with a rod in the drain bed, and the only way to put a rod in the drain bed at that bridge is to clear the sill.*”

“*That costs nobody a figure. It costs whoever is standing in the drain at the time, and I am telling you that at the sixth hour and not after.*”

“*Three. The thing that keeps your names out of the books is this.*”

“*If the arrangement answers the water in that ground, then the arrangement’s books begin to keep that ground. And when a book keeps a ground it keeps the people on it, and there are eleven houses in there and I am not going to give you a figure of the people in them because I do not have one and I am not going to invent one at the sixth hour.*”

“*A man of thirty-four has a day against his name already. A woman of twenty-nine has a name in ink and no day.*”

“*A woman of sixty-four has a name in ink and a gap she has relied on for nineteen years. A woman of twenty-eight has neither and cannot be sent for.*”

“*None of those four is a figure. All four of them are in there.*”

“*And if the books start keeping that ground, the gap a woman of sixty-four has relied on becomes a gap in a book about a piece of ground, instead of a gap about a person.*”

“*And I cannot tell you what that does, because I do not know, and that is the first time in a week I have said that.*”

“*I can promise you that I will tell you again on Saturday. I cannot promise you that a bridge will stand, and I cannot promise you a well, and I cannot promise anybody in this county a name out of a book, and I am not going to try.*”

Then a man of fifty-four asked for the whole of it again, and gave the figure for his own run of mornings out loud before anybody had worked it out, and said the reason he wanted it twice.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel on that landing and cannot read. He has asked for that line in the open every morning since the Thursday of the hundred and twenty-fifth week. He has a hundred and twelve mornings and the figure is one more than the morning before.

“*A hundred and twelve mornings this morning. That is one more than a hundred and eleven, and it is the twenty-second morning of that run.*”

“*I cannot read. I have said that on this bank in about nine ears more often than any other thing I have said on it, and I have never once been wrong to say it.*”

“*And about four of you have stopped noticing me say it, and I have noticed that, and I am going to keep saying it anyway.*”

“*Say it again from the road. Not from the start. From the words about the sill.*”

“*And here is why, and I am giving the reason before the asking, because a woman of sixty-four taught me that in this same week.*”

“*The first time I have a thing in about nine ears is the only time I know I have had all of it. If you say it twice and the two times are the same, then I had all of it the first time.*”

“*And I have a name on the ninth line of that thing on the flat and a gap where a day would go, and about four of you have been waiting all week for me to say that a gap is the same as a name.*”

“*A gap is not a name. I am fifty-four and I have held gravel for nineteen years and I know the difference between a hole in a wall and a thing you can be sent for about.*”

Then a woman of forty-four asked out loud, in about nine ears, which of two things matters more, and about nine of them said nothing, and a man of thirty-one named the nothing and did not answer it.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept twenty-nine chairs at thirteen shillings and fourpence a year for nineteen years. Her three numbers are all of things. She has said the counting was never the work, and she has declined three times in a week to put a thing on her counter.

“*I am going to ask the thing that has been going round this bank and I am not asking it because anybody made me.*”

“*On Saturday a woman of twenty-eight named it. If a man of thirty-one’s standing is the thing that has to be argued about, and the truth of what is in that zone is the thing that has to be answered, then which of the two is it better to lose.*”

“*I am asking it at the sixth hour on a Wednesday and I am asking it in about nine ears, and I am not asking it to him, and I want that said before it is asked.*”

Nobody on that bank answered for about as long as it takes a man of forty-three to walk down his own steps and back up them.

“*A hundred and thirty-three days. A hundred and thirty-three is eight hundred and thirty-three less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer, and this morning it is not even the only thing it is not an answer to.*”

“*About nine of you have said nothing, and I am going to name the nothing, and a man of fifty-four said on Monday that the reason he asks is so that he can be wrong in his own mouth.*”

“*The nothing is not agreement. I know what nine people not answering looks like and I have watched about nine of you do it for a week.*”

“*And here is the nothing, which I said out loud on Saturday and am having to say a second time in a different shape. It is not agreement. It is that not one person on this bank has the standing to weigh those two things against each other.*”

“*And that is the most convenient answer available to every one of you, and about four of you knew that before I said it, and a woman of forty-four knew it before any of us.*”

“*And I am not going to answer it. She asked it and not me, and on Saturday I said I would not ask it again until somebody who is not me asked it, and a woman of forty-four has asked it, and that is satisfied, and satisfying it is not the same as settling it.*”

“*And I am going to say what would settle it and I am going to say it for nobody. It would take a person in this county whose day or whose name is on a page in one of those books, saying out loud that they will stand behind the zone being told whatever it costs their own protection.*”

“*And I am not going to ask one of those four people for it this week.*”

“*About four of you have noticed that I have just described the only thing that would answer a question I have been carrying for six days, and that I have not asked for it. That is on me and not on them.*”

“*Here is the want. I want the eleven houses in that zone to believe what was said to them at the sixth hour, and I cannot get a thing believed by anything I say at the sixth hour, and about four of you worked that out before I did.*”

“*And the mistake is one I have made all week and I am not going to number it. I opened with the word happen, and I did it to be quick, and a woman of sixty-four caught it in about a second and had to take the whole of it back out of my mouth and put it in again in a shape that was not a warning.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a woman of sixty-four who stopped a man of thirty-one in about nine ears and made him say a warning in a different shape.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man of fifty-four who asked for the whole of it twice and gave the reason before the asking.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a woman of forty-four who asked a question in about nine ears and was not answered, and who has been asked that question herself twice and had said no twice, and who asked it a third time today on her own account.*”

“*And nobody is to be told that any of the three of them was brave about it, and I am not going to be thanked for naming the nothing, because naming it is what I do instead of answering it.*”

“*And the right of refusal is not restored this morning. A thing that may fail is not it. Telling about nine people in about nine ears is not it, and a question that nobody answers is not it, and a gap is not it.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the ninth hour of a Wednesday, with sixteen inches of water over the top step above them.
A zone about eighteen hundred yards long had been told out loud in about nine ears. Three things had been named as things that may fail, and the digging had been named as the likeliest cause of all three. A woman of forty-four had asked which of two things matters more, and nobody had answered.

---

The fever was seventy-eight weeks and four days old. He had been in that county two hundred and seventy-nine days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and forty-eight days old, and a hundred and thirty-three days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

A woman of sixty-four stopped a man of thirty-one in about nine ears and said that what he had opened with was a warning, and that a warning is one person telling other people what is going to happen to them.
And she said she had been in one nineteen years ago, and that it had come with a day against her name at the end of it.
And a man of thirty-one told the whole of it again in a shape that was not a warning, and named the digging as the likeliest cause of all three failures, and said that he could promise nobody a bridge, or a well, or a name out of a book.

A man of fifty-four asked for the whole of it to be said twice and gave the reason before the asking, and gave a hundred and twelve mornings of his own run out loud in about nine ears, and said that a gap is not a name.
And a woman of forty-four asked which of two things matters more, and about nine of them said nothing, and a man of thirty-one named the nothing and said it was not agreement and that nobody there had standing to weigh the two.
