# Chapter 845: Seventh Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Second Week, And A Monday, And Twelve Inches And Three Days Of The Coming Back And The Next Time Is In Five Days, And A Man Of Thirty-Eight Arrives On The Day He Named A Fortnight Ago And Opens What Is In The Inside Of His Coat And There Are Thirty-Two Notches On It And Nobody Is Thanked For It

Seventh day of the hundred and forty-second week. His five hundred and ninety-fifth morning. Four hundred and forty-five days after the settlement. The fever eighty weeks and two days. Three hundred and ninety-nine days since the division. Three hundred and forty-five days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about twelve inches over the top step at about the seventh hour and it was three days of the coming back. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working and the figure for the next time. He said that this was the last day of a week and that tomorrow a new one began, and that a new week had never once in nineteen years changed what his three things were, and that a woman of twenty-eight would probably call that the least interesting sentence he has ever spoken.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and gives three things every morning. The fourth thing is a figure of how many of those ninety steps are under, and it has been nothing for thirteen days.

“*About twelve inches at the seventh hour, and it is three days of the coming back, and it is four inches higher than it was yesterday at the same hour. In about four days it will be a full bank, and it will then hold at that figure until it comes off.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in five days, and that is the fifth day of the hundred and forty-third week. Five days forward, and there is nothing counting down to it, and I have said that three times this week and I am not going to say it a fourth.*”

Then at about the eighth hour a man of thirty-eight came up that cart road with a child of nine, and stood at the top of that landing, and said that this was the day he had named, and gave the working for it out loud in about nine ears.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and has a board in the inside of his coat with thirty-two notches cut in the edge of it. On the seventh day of the hundred and forty-first week he said he would open it on the seventh day of the hundred and forty-second week. He said he would open it if he had the part where he would know what to do with it, and that he did not have that part.

“*I worked this off the week this bank is in and not off a calendar, and a man of about fifty-two can check it, and I would rather he did than that anybody should say afterwards that I had got it wrong and got it wrong helpfully.*”

“*It is the seventh day of the hundred and forty-second week, and that is the day I named on the seventh day of the week before, and I said seven days then, and seven days from that morning is this morning.*”

“*And I have not got the part where I know what to do with it. I said on that Monday that I would not open it without the part, and I have had nine days and I have not found it, and I am opening it anyway, and I want that said before it is opened rather than after.*”

Then a man of thirty-eight took a board out of the inside of his coat and held it up so that about nine of them could see it, and did not hand it over, and said what was on it out loud, and said why he had never said it before.

“*Thirty-two notches, cut in the edge, one a day, and I have been carrying it since the year my boy was born. I have never told about nine of anybody what they were, and the reason I am telling now is that about nine of you are standing here.*”

“*They are the days I went to the room. Thirty-two days, and no working, because this one is not a figure of anything anybody keeps a count of. It is a figure of a man’s back, and I have said that four times this morning already.*”

“*And I did not cut a notch for the day I asked to see the boy. There are four of those and they are not on the board. A notch on that board means a day I was sent for, and a day I asked for is a different thing, and I have never wanted the two mixed in.*”

Then a man of thirty-eight said what he was going to do with it, and said that he did not know, and said what he was not going to let it be used for, and said the thing about four hundred yards of bucket out loud in about nine ears.

“*I have still not got the part, and I am going to say that again because a boy of thirteen will write it down. I would rather it went in his hand as the second half of a thing than as a footnote at the bottom of a page.*”

“*Here is what I am not going to do with it, and it is one thing said twice. I am not going to let thirty-two be put in a charter, and I am not going to let it be read out as what a place cost, because it is not what a place cost, it is what one man’s back cost, and about four of you have been very careful this fortnight not to turn a figure of a person into a figure of a place and I am not going to help you undo that on a Monday.*”

“*And I am still a man who asked for nothing for four hundred yards of bucket, and that is not a complaint. It is the only sentence I have got that is worth anything. A man of thirty-one has been standing on this landing for eleven days putting figures in about nine of our ears, and he has not once asked me for a figure, and I have noticed.*”

Then a boy of thirteen counted the notches twice out loud because a man of fifty-four cannot read, and said the figure was thirty-two both times, and said what it was and was not a figure of.

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and ninety-two days, and the figure is one more than the morning before, and he counted it in the lane before the light came up. A man of fifty-four holds the gravel on that landing and has asked for a line a leaf at a time for a hundred and twenty-four mornings.

“*A hundred and ninety-two days, and it is one more than the morning before, and I counted it in the lane before the light came up.*”

“*Thirty-two. I have counted them twice and I am going to say it twice, because a man of fifty-four cannot read and this is being read to him, and because a figure that is only said once on a landing gets turned into a different figure by about four people before the light goes.*”

“*And I want to say what thirty-two is, because about four of you have already made it into something. It is a figure of notches. It does not count the days for anybody else, it does not count a room four hundred miles off, and it does not count how many people were asked anything. That last one is a sentence a man of about fifty-two said a fortnight ago, and I have carried it since.*”

“*It is a figure of a board, and that is all it is a figure of on its own. A man of thirty-eight has told us in about nine of our ears that it is also a figure of his own back. Those are two different figures wearing the same number, and I am not going to pretend I can tell you which one it is on a Monday morning.*”

Then a woman of twenty-eight said the thing nobody on that landing had said, and said it about the rate of a settlement, and said that she was not going to say the rest of it in about nine of your ears, and gave the figure for her own days with the subtraction in it.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and cannot be sent for in this county or in the one she came from, and has been in this county two hundred and three days, and the figure is eight hundred and forty-five less six hundred and forty-two. She has said no twice, and she was not asked again in the last four mornings, and she has not been thanked for that either.

“*Two hundred and three days, and the working is eight hundred and forty-five less six hundred and forty-two. I have said it out loud because a boy of thirteen counts on a Monday, and a woman of twenty-eight is not going to be the one person on this landing who is worse at keeping a figure than he is.*”

“*Here is what nobody has said, and I am going to say it once and then leave it. Every charter that has been read on that bank was read on a Saturday, and today is a Monday, and I want the difference out loud before it is a difference anybody has noticed.*”

“*A charter read on a Saturday is a thing people are frightened of, and a charter read on a Monday is a thing that has to be done, because on Monday there is a week in front of it and everybody can see what it is going to cost. I have watched about four of you get gentler with it every day and I do not think one of you has noticed.*”

“*And I am not saying that is wrong, I am saying what it is. A settlement which needs somebody to be in a hurry is a settlement that has started to be a bargain. I am not going to be hurried, and I am not going to say the rest of why in about nine of your ears. That is not a threat, it is me telling you what I have left.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said what a board being opened is and what it is not, in about nine ears, and said that nobody on that landing was thanked for it and that he was not going to be the man who started, and gave the figure for the standing offer with the subtraction in it.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and ninety-one days, two hundred and ninety-one being eight hundred and forty-five less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and sixty days old, a hundred and sixty being eight hundred and forty-five less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*A board is open, and I am going to say four things about that in about nine of your ears. None of them is a congratulation, and a man of thirty-eight has not come up that road to be congratulated and would not know what to do if he were.*”

“*One: a board being opened is not a victory and it is not a defeat. It is a thing that was shut and is now not shut, and that is a smaller event than about four of you have been carrying around since Saturday.*”

“*Two: nobody on this landing is thanked for it, and I am going to be exact about the list. Not the man, and not the boy who counted, and not me for not asking. A man of thirty-eight walked up a cart road on his own on the day he named, and that is the whole of the credit in it.*”

“*Three, and this is the one I want written down in a boy’s hand. I have been asked eleven times this fortnight to open things. I have opened one, on a Friday, in my own mouth, and it cost me a morning, and I have not been asked to open anything since. I am not going to treat a man of thirty-eight opening a coat as an example of anything.*”

“*And four, and it is the last thing I am going to say about a figure this morning. A hundred and forty-five days. The offer asked at the seven-hundredth is a hundred and forty-five days old, and a hundred and forty-five is eight hundred and forty-five less seven hundred, and nothing that happened on that bank this morning has changed it by a single day, and I am not going to let thirty-two notches be mistaken for an answer.*”

About nine of them were on that landing at about the eleventh hour of the last Monday of a week. Three days of the water were coming back, a board was held up in a coat and not handed over, and a figure of thirty-two was said twice out loud because a man of fifty-four cannot read.

---

The fever was eighty weeks and two days old. He had been in that county two hundred and ninety-one days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and sixty days old, and a hundred and forty-five days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

A man of thirty-eight arrived on the day he had named and worked it out from the week, and opened what is in the inside of his coat, and there were thirty-two notches on it, and nobody was thanked for it.
He said the notches are the days he went to the room four hundred miles off to be asked about his son and given no answer. He said there are four other days not on the board, and that he is still a man who asked for nothing for four hundred yards of bucket.

A boy of thirteen counted them twice and said the figure was thirty-two both times, and said it is a figure of notches and also a figure of a man’s own back, and that those are two different figures wearing the same number.
A woman of twenty-eight said every charter read on that bank was read on a Saturday, and that a settlement which needs somebody to be in a hurry has started to be a bargain, and gave the rest of why to nobody.
