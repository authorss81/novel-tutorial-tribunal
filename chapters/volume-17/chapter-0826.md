# Chapter 826: Second Day Of The Hundred And Fortieth Week, And A Wednesday, And About Four Inches Of Water Back On The Flat And Not On The Steps, And The Fourth Leaf Is Read Whole At About The Tenth Hour And It Is The Price And Not A Threat, And A Man Of Sixty-Four Says The Nineteen Years She Gave Away Are Not The Same Thing As This, And A Woman Of Twenty-Eight Says She Is Going To Look At The Second Of Her Two Things Today

Second day of the hundred and fortieth week. His five hundred and seventy-sixth morning. Four hundred and twenty-six days after the settlement. The fever seventy-seven weeks and four days. Three hundred and eighty days since the division. Three hundred and twenty-six days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

The bank was about four inches over the top step at about the seventh hour, and it was one day of the coming back, and about ninety of those steps were dry at the sixth hour and are dry still. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working and the figure for the next time.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. Three things every morning, and the third of them this week was a mark at the bottom of a piece of paper, and that is two of the three about a thing that is not the landing.

“*About four inches at the seventh hour, and it is one day of the coming back, and about ninety of those steps were dry at about the sixth hour and are dry still at the seventh.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in three days, and that is three days after the morning it is full, and the morning it is full is the second day of this week.*”

“*And that figure is the same as the days of the coming back for about three mornings and then it is not, and I am not going to explain that to about nine of you.*”

Then at about the tenth hour a man of fifty-four said he had decided, and a man of sixty-one read the fourth leaf whole on that flat in about nine ears, and read it once and then read the two lines that matter again.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel on that landing and cannot read. He has asked for that line in the open every morning since the Thursday of the hundred and twenty-fifth week. He has a hundred and five mornings and the figure is one more than the morning before.

“*I said on Monday that I would decide today and I have decided and I want about nine of you to know that I decided before he opened it and not after.*”

“*Read it whole. He said he would read it whole or not at all, and he has said that out loud on three days now.*”

“*And I am going to stand about nine feet off while he reads it and I am not going to be about nine feet off the reading. I am going to be about nine feet off the reading and I am going to be able to hear all of it.*”

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has a book of heads and carts and two numbers in it in eleven years, and has refused since the second day of the hundred and thirty-sixth week to write a bench’s notice in it. He wrote a woman of twenty-eight’s name on a page in a column nineteen years ago and has not gone back to that page since, and he does not write down what anybody says after a thing he reads.

“*Here is the fourth leaf. It is the last one. There is nothing behind it.*”

“*It is one page and it is eleven lines and I have counted them twice, because I have a book of heads and carts and a habit, and a man of fifty-four is standing about nine feet off me waiting to see whether I count twice.*”

“*I count twice.*”

“*The first eight lines are the three things. The road answered in nine days, the child treated in nine days, the bridge open in nine days. Those are the first eight lines and I have read them already and you have heard them.*”

“*Line nine is the price and I am going to read it and then I am going to read line nine again, and about four of you are going to be annoyed with me for that, and I am doing it anyway.*”

“*Line nine: the arrangement holds no day against any person, and no day is entered, and no person is named in any book of the arrangement, and no person may be sent for about a thing in any book of the arrangement.*”

Nobody on that bank said anything for about a minute.

“*Line ten: any person in this county may at any time put a question to the arrangement, and the arrangement shall answer it, and the answer shall be given in the open.*”

“*Line eleven: no answer of the arrangement may be questioned, and no person may be asked to explain why they did not accept an answer, and the only remedy for a person who does not accept an answer is that the person remains.*”

“*That is the whole of the fourth leaf and that is the whole of the thing.*”

“*I am going to read line nine again.*”

“*The arrangement holds no day against any person, and no day is entered, and no person is named in any book of the arrangement, and no person may be sent for about a thing in any book of the arrangement.*”

Then a woman of sixty-four said that the nineteen years she gave away in front of about nine of them in this same week were not the same thing as that, and gave the two apart.

Ada Renk is sixty-four and has a name in ink on that ninth line of a book four hundred miles off and has never had a day against it. She got up out of a book of heads and carts in the open nineteen years ago and was forty-five when she worked out that the thing asking was not a person. She has said the word twice and refused nine people, and her protection for nineteen years was a gap and not a wall.

“*I gave away nineteen years on the fifth day of the hundred and thirty-eighth week, in front of about nine of you, and I have not been thanked for it, and I am not going to be thanked for what I am going to say now either.*”

“*And what I am going to say now is that the nineteen years and that eleventh line are not the same thing, and I want about nine of you to hear the difference from me before you hear it from a thing with a nail in it.*”

“*In those nineteen years I was in a book of heads and carts and there was a day against my name, and I got up out of it in the open, and from that morning there was a gap where the day had been.*”

“*And the gap was not put there by me. Nothing in this county can put anything in a book four hundred miles off, and I have relied on that every day for nineteen years, and about four of you have known that and about four of you have not.*”

“*And line eleven says the only remedy for a person who does not accept an answer is that the person remains.*”

“*Remains. In the county. Not sent for, because line nine says not sent for, and I believe line nine.*”

“*And I have believed a line before, and I have relied on a gap for nineteen years, and I am telling about nine of you that the shape of line eleven is a shape I know and that I am not frightened of it in the way about four of you are.*”

“*What I am is tired of it.*”

Then a man of thirty-four in an ash said what he thought line eleven was, out loud, at about nine feet off a woman of sixty-four, and did not soften it.

Ivo Kellow is thirty-four and has been in an ash about twenty feet off the edge of that drain and a hundred yards along it for a hundred and ninety-six days, and he has a day against his name which is not a day of this week. He said on the second day of the hundred and thirty-ninth week that he will speak in a room four hundred miles off only if he is asked, and did not withdraw it when he was told that a man who has to be asked has already been asked.

“*A hundred and ninety-six days. That is eight hundred and twenty-six less six hundred and thirty.*”

“*Here is what line eleven is, and I am going to say it nine feet off a woman of sixty-four and I am not going to soften it, because she told me on the sixth day of the hundred and thirty-eighth week what to do with a thing like this.*”

“*Line eleven is: there is no such thing as being wrong about this.*”

“*There is no day, and no name in a book, and no sending for.*”

“*And in exchange there is no way of saying the answer was wrong, and no way of putting your own answer in instead, and the only thing a person can do is be wrong and stay.*”

“*And a man of thirty-four has a day against his name and has been in this ash for a hundred and ninety-six days.*”

“*And line eleven means the day never comes off and never comes on, and I get to be wrong about it for the rest of my life at four inches a day of my own choosing.*”

“*And I am not saying line eleven is a threat. About four of you were waiting for me to say that word.*”

“*It is not a threat, because nothing has been taken off me this morning, and I still have a day against my name, and I am still in this ash.*”

“*It is a thing that is only good if you say yes.*”

“*And I am aware that that is the same shape, and I have been trying to get past the shape since the eighth hour and I cannot.*”

Then a woman of twenty-eight said she was going to look at the second of her two things today, in front of about nine of them, and said what the second one was, and said she had told a man of thirty-one on Wednesday not to produce it for her.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from. She said her own name on that bank on the Tuesday of the hundred and thirty-ninth week. She is at a hundred and eighty-four days, and the figure is eight hundred and twenty-six less six hundred and forty-two.

“*A hundred and eighty-four days, and that is eight hundred and twenty-six less six hundred and forty-two.*”

“*I said on the second day of the hundred and fortieth week that I was not going to decide this week whether I can still trust a man of thirty-one, and that I had two things to look at and had been given them so that about four of you could check them.*”

“*I have looked at the first of them. I have not looked at the second until this morning.*”

“*The second one is this: whether a man of thirty-one has said one true thing this week that he did not have to say.*”

“*And I am not going to tell about nine of you whether the answer is yes or no, and I am telling about nine of you that I have been asked twice since the Wednesday by about four people and I have said no both times.*”

“*And I am saying this in about nine ears and not to him, and a man of thirty-one told me on the Wednesday that he was not going to produce it for me, and he has not, and I noticed.*”

Then a man of thirty-one came to the end of it and said what he wanted, what he had got wrong, and what it cost, and gave the figure for the standing offer in his own mouth with the subtraction in it, and said that the thing on the flat is good and works and that he had not said so out loud until now.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and seventy-two days, two hundred and seventy-two being eight hundred and twenty-six less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and forty-one days old, a hundred and forty-one being eight hundred and twenty-six less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*Here is the want and it is the same shape and it is one day smaller again, and I have given you that four mornings running and I am not going to keep it up much longer and I do not know what I will do when I stop.*”

“*The mistake is the thirty-fifth of the same mistake and I gave it out loud on Monday and I am not numbering it again this week.*”

“*What I did wrong this morning is that I read line nine off a man of sixty-one’s mouth and I felt something, and about four of you saw me feel it, and I am not going to pretend to about nine of you that I did not.*”

“*I have been standing on this bank for five days waiting to be able to say no to a thing, and a thing came out of the dark on Friday night and it is good, and about four of you have been watching me want it.*”

“*A road answered in nine days. A child treated in nine days. A bridge open in nine days. And a woman of sixty-four who has had a gap for nineteen years who is not frightened of it.*”

“*I am going to say the next part out loud and I have never said it out loud.*”

“*It works.*”

“*That is what I have wanted since Thursday. I wanted somebody else to carry the figures and this thing carries them, and it is not cruel, and it is not lying, and about nine of you can see the road and the child and the bridge in it.*”

“*And a lesser man than me would have said yes on Friday morning and would have been right to, on the child alone, and about four of you are going to remember that I said that, and I am saying it now so that nobody has to decide whether I said it.*”

“*A hundred and twenty-six days. A hundred and twenty-six is eight hundred and twenty-six less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer.*”

“*And the standing offer and that thing on the flat are two offers, and I have said so on two mornings, and about four of you have been holding them together anyway, and I am not going to do it again.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a woman of sixty-four who said the nineteen years and the eleventh line are not the same thing and who said she is tired of it.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a man of thirty-four in an ash who said line eleven means there is no such thing as being wrong about this, and who said he could not get past the shape of it, and who has not been thanked for it.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of a woman of twenty-eight who looked at the second of her two things this morning in about nine ears and not to me.*”

“*And nobody is to be told that saying it works was brave, because I did not say it because it was the brave thing. I said it because a woman of sixty-four was not frightened of the eleventh line and I have been frightened of it since Friday.*”

“*And the right of refusal is not restored this morning. A gap that nobody can put a day in is not it, and a day that cannot be taken off a person is not it, and both of those are on a piece of paper and not in a mouth.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Wednesday with four inches of water back on the flat and ninety steps dry, and the fourth leaf read whole at about the tenth hour, and line nine read twice. A woman of sixty-four had said the nineteen years she gave away in the same week were not the same thing as the eleventh line, and a man of thirty-four in an ash had said that the eleventh line means there is no such thing as being wrong about this.
