# Chapter 754: Seventh And Last Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-Ninth Week, And A Monday, And The Water Comes Off All Ninety Of The Steps In The Night For The Eleventh Time Since The Saturday Of The Hundred And Nineteenth Week, And About Nine People Go Down At The Fifth Hour Because A Keeping Is Done Before The Light, And A Woman Of Sixty-Four Was In That Room On That Day And Was In This County On That Day And Was Told She Could Not Attend

Seventh and last day of the hundred and twenty-ninth week. His five hundred and fourth morning. Three hundred and fifty-four days after the settlement. The fever sixty-seven weeks and two days. Three hundred and eight days since the division. Two hundred and fifty-four days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water came off all ninety of the steps in the night and the man of forty-three gave that at about the sixth hour before anybody else said anything, and he gave the day it was on.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He has said he had nothing to say about a night five times and then stopped. He has given two figures for a night since and neither of those two is his, and he is not going to give a fourth thing out of the three he gives every morning.

“*Off all ninety at about the sixth hour, and it is the eleventh time since the Saturday of the hundred and nineteenth week, and it is a Monday.*”

“*It was a Tuesday the first one and a Wednesday the second and a Thursday the third and a Friday the fourth and a Saturday the fifth and a Sunday the sixth, and it is a Monday this time.*”

“*That is one day later every cycle and the cycles are eight days apart, and eight days apart is what it has been for eleven of them.*”

“*I am not going to work out in front of about nine people why it is eight days apart, because I have not found out.*”

“*I am not going to be thanked for three mornings saying when it would come off and I am not going to be told I was right about Monday.*”

About nine of them went down that bank at about the fifth hour before the light, the way they go on the morning it comes off. A chamber that is a day is kept by two hands once a day before the light and about a minute. A man of thirty-eight carried the lamp and it is his.

Then the bank was empty at about the seventh hour and the sheet of printed paper was still on that counter with nothing in any of the three columns on it, on the tenth morning of it. The four leaves of a hearing were beside it. About nine people looked at both.

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open, and it was the thirty-third morning. It was the last reading of this week and nobody had asked for it, and everybody on that bank knew it was going to be asked for.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He has said he had nothing to say about a night five times and then stopped. He has given two figures for a night since and neither of those two is his, and he is not going to give a fourth thing out of the three he gives every morning. About nine of them were on that bank this morning.

“*Thirty-three mornings and it is the last day of a week.*”

“*And I am going to be here on Tuesday morning and it will be the first day of the hundred and thirtieth week and I am not going to count it tonight because I am not going to sit up.*”

“*The fifth thing is still not answered and nobody has asked me about it and I am still waiting, and I have now been waiting about three weeks.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the thirty-three and nobody is going to be told I was brave about asking for the same thing thirty-three times.*”

Then a woman of sixty-four said she was in that room, and gave the day, and gave the working, and said what she is not going to say about it.

Ada Renk is sixty-four and got up out of that book in the open nineteen years ago, and it cost her one thing a day of them, and she has been out of every book in this county since. She cannot be found by writing a name down.

“*I was in that room.*”

“*The day of it is the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-sixth week, and that is seven hundred and fifty-four less seven hundred and twenty-six.*”

“*Seven hundred and twenty-six is the twenty-sixth day that has been counted in this county and it is a Wednesday.*”

“*Seven hundred and fifty-four less seven hundred and twenty-six is twenty-eight days, and I worked it out twice on the road because I do not trust the first one.*”

“*I was asked whether I could attend and I said I would and I was told that I could not.*”

“*I have said that out loud in about nine people’s hearing and nobody has asked me what the reason was and I am not going to tell anybody the reason and I am not going to be asked twice.*”

“*And I am not going to say whether nine people were in that room or six.*”

“*A woman of thirty-four walked two days to say six. I am not going to take that off her and I am not going to add to it.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for six lines and nobody is going to be told I was brave about saying it on the first morning of the water being off.*”

About four of them had a woman of sixty-four who had been in a room four hundred miles off on a Wednesday and had been told she could not attend, and about nine of them had a woman of thirty-four who had said six.

Then a woman of forty said what the thing on that counter is now, and refused the total.

Wenna Callow is forty and asks for a day against a name and has never once in her life asked for a reason instead. There are five things and she has still not said which three of them have a day against them, and she said on a Monday of a fortnight that she would not and has not started since.

“*Say what a person is standing in front of, because it is a week of somebody asking me that.*”

“*A sheet with three columns on it that has been on that counter ten mornings with nothing in any of the three, and four leaves beside it with a number on them and no room on them and no names on them.*”

“*And a woman of sixty-four who says she was in that room and a woman of thirty-four who says six were, and a man of sixty-one who has written six on a leaf after the last head of a book he has kept eleven years.*”

“*That is three instruments on one counter and about nine of you are looking at all three of them the way you would look at one instrument.*”

“*I am not going to add them together. One of them is about a day and about nine of you can be sent for about that. One of them is about an argument four hundred miles off and not one of you can be sent for about any of it. And the third is a book of heads and carts that about nine people can be sent for about and there is a number in it this morning that was not in it yesterday.*”

“*I am still not going to say which three of the five have a day against them and nobody is going to be thanked for the five sentences.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said what he wanted, and what it is, and what it costs, and he said the standing offer’s working, and nobody answered it.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred days, two hundred being seven hundred and fifty-four less five hundred and fifty-four. On Friday of last week he gave up being nobody’s for one morning by saying a word out loud. On Thursday before that he refused in three sentences to answer a question about whether nine people can write a rule without one of them being the one who wrote it.

“*Two hundred is seven hundred and fifty-four less five hundred and fifty-four, and I have been in this county two hundred days.*”

“*Two hundred is a figure and it is a figure of a place I am in and not of anything I have done.*”

“*I worked that out on Wednesday and I am saying it this morning because a woman of twenty-eight has made me say the figure before the want about nine times since Thursday.*”

“*Here is the want and I am giving it first. I want a man of thirty-eight to ask a woman of sixty-four what the reason was, and not me, and I am not going to ask him to do it.*”

“*And I am aware that is a want I have said on this bank before.*”

“*I said on the Friday of the hundred and twenty-eighth week that I wanted a man of fifty-four asked one question before the end of next week.*”

“*It is not the end of next week and he has not been asked.*”

“*That is the mistake and it is mine. I have wanted to be the man who arranges for about nine people to be asked things for about nine mornings, and that is not a want. That is a job I have made up and there is nobody in this county who can be sent for about it.*”

“*And here is the cost, and it is the same cost it has been for about three days and I am not going to say it differently to make it smaller. A man of thirty-one has said the word want out loud in about nine people’s hearing about nine times in about nine days, and a word want said nine times in nine days in front of about nine people is not a want any more. It is a habit and about four of you have worked that out.*”

“*Nobody is going to be relieved about that and nobody is going to be thanked and nobody is going to be told I was right about any of it.*”

“*Fifty-four days. Fifty-four is seven hundred and fifty-four less seven hundred.*”

“*That is what a thing that is not a person asked me on a Wednesday about three weeks ago.*”

“*Nobody has answered it and nobody is going to answer it for me and I am not going to answer it, and none of what I have said this morning is an answer to it.*”

Then a woman of twenty-eight said the true of nine days of wants in five sentences and refused the sixth, and the fifth is the reason.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from, and has been in this county a hundred and twelve days, a hundred and twelve being seven hundred and fifty-four less six hundred and forty-two. She refused a man of thirty-one in nine sentences on the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-seventh week and said a second time is a rule.

“*Five sentences and the fifth of them is the reason and it is mine and it is about what you have been doing since Thursday and not about what you are.*”

“*One: a man of thirty-one has said the word want in about nine people’s hearing on about nine mornings and has not been asked for anything in two hundred days.*”

“*Two: a man of thirty-eight asked for a room once this fortnight in about nine people’s hearing and nobody gave him one and nobody asked him a second time, and that is the correct way round.*”

“*Three: a woman of sixty-four said a reason is not hers to give twice, and a man of thirty-four said he has not got a fifth reason and is not going to be asked for one.*”

“*Both of those are people who are not getting anything out of this morning.*”

“*Four: you have said you want somebody else to be asked. That is not a want about this county. That is a want about you, and it is wearing about nine people’s clothing, and about four of us have noticed and about nine of them have not.*”

“*Five is the reason I said five and not six. I have been on this bank for a hundred and twelve days and I have watched you say a thing out loud every morning.*”

“*I am not going to say the sixth sentence, which is that about nine people would rather you asked nothing than asked in that order.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the five sentences and nobody is going to be told I have begun again.*”

About nine of them stood on that bank in the wind on the last morning of a week with an empty bank and a full counter and nothing answered at all.

The fever was sixty-seven weeks and two days old. He had been in that county two hundred days.

The water came off all ninety of the steps in the night for the eleventh time since the Saturday of the hundred and nineteenth week, and it was a Monday. A man of forty-three gave the day it was on, because the day-name moves one place every cycle and the cycles are eight days apart.

And a woman of sixty-four said out loud in about nine people’s hearing that she was in that room, on the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-sixth week. She was asked whether she could attend and told she could not. She refused to say the reason and asked not to be asked twice.

And a man of thirty-one said he wanted a man of thirty-eight to ask her why, and said he is not going to ask him to do it. He said that wanting to arrange for about nine people to be asked is not a want but a job he has made up, and nobody in this county can be sent for about it.
