# Chapter 797: First Day Of The Hundred And Thirty-Sixth Week, And A Tuesday, And Nobody Gives Her Figure, And A Man Of Thirty-One Says He Does Not Know Whether The Thing That Asks In The Voice Of A Minute Is One Thing Or Nine, And A Woman Of Sixty-Four Says She Will Say The Word If He Will Say What It Is Not

First day of the hundred and thirty-sixth week. His five hundred and forty-seventh morning. Three hundred and ninety-seven days after the settlement. The fever seventy-three weeks and three days. Three hundred and fifty-one days since the division. Two hundred and ninety-seven days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was about twelve inches on that cut at about the seventh hour, three days of the coming back, and the man of forty-three gave the reading and the working and the day because it is the first morning of a week.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. Three things every morning. He named a stone on that bank on the Sunday of the week before last and about four of them walk past it every day.

“*Twelve inches at the seventh hour, and it is three days since it came off, and about the first forty of those ninety steps are under.*”

“*Four inches a day is the working, and a bank that is full holds at sixteen until it comes off, and I have given you both halves all week.*”

“*And the next time all ninety of them can be walked is in five days, and that is the Sunday of this week, and it will be the seventeenth time.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open, seventy-sixth morning, and asked out loud at the top of that cut, and asked for the whole of it this time.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel on that landing and cannot read. He has asked for that line in the open every morning since the Thursday of the hundred and twenty-fifth week, and went down ninety cut steps to have it read to him at the bottom of them on the Saturday of last week.

“*Seventy-six mornings, and this is the first morning of a week and I am asking for the whole of it.*”

“*Not the ninth line. The sheet. All of it, read out, in the open, from the top of that cut, and I have never asked for that in seventy-six mornings and I have thought about it since Saturday.*”

“*And I am not asking for it because I cannot read it. I have not been able to read it for seventy-six mornings and that has not been the problem.*”

“*The problem is that a man of thirty-eight read the ninth line out loud seventeen mornings running and stopped on Monday because a man asked him to, and a man of thirty-eight was right to stop, and now about four of you have not heard it read at all.*”

“*So I am asking for the whole of it this morning so that it is in the open whether anybody reads it or not. That is my doing and not his.*”

Then the sheet was read out in the open from the top of that cut, all of it, and a man of thirty-eight read it and gave the count and the number of names and said what the count is this morning.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine and a board in the inside of his coat with thirty-two notches cut in the edge of it. He gave the count every morning for seventeen mornings and did not give it yesterday.

“*A man of fifty-four asked for the whole of it this morning and I have read the whole of it and I did not give a figure yesterday and I am not giving one today, and both of those are mine.*”

“*The number of names is four. That is the eighteenth morning and I am giving it because a man of fifty-four asked out loud at the top of a cut for the whole of the sheet and I am not a man who stops reading when it is asked for.*”

“*The board has been in this coat ninety-nine days, and ninety-nine is seven hundred and ninety-seven less six hundred and ninety-eight, and I gave that figure eighteen days running and I gave the last one yesterday and it was the last one.*”

“*Not because I have opened it. I am not opening it this week and I have not opened it in ninety-nine days and about four of you know what is on it and about nine of you do not, including me.*”

Then a woman of twenty-eight was not on that bank, and nobody gave her figure, and about four of them noticed that nobody gave it, and a man of thirty-one said the thing that is the middle of his week.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and forty-three days, two hundred and forty-three being seven hundred and ninety-seven less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and twelve days old, a hundred and twelve being seven hundred and ninety-seven less six hundred and eighty-five, and he has asked five times in about a fortnight whether it will keep and nobody has said.

“*Nobody gave a figure for a woman of twenty-eight this morning. A man of sixty-one stopped on Sunday and I stopped on Sunday and about four of you noticed.*”

“*And I want to be exact about why, because I have been the one giving those figures for about four weeks and I did not decide to stop this morning.*”

“*I decided to stop on Sunday in front of you, and then I had to live with it over two days.*”

“*Here is how I know. A man of about fifty-two said on Saturday that a figure a man repeats becomes a thing he is standing behind.*”

“*A boy of thirteen said on Sunday that the mornings about four of us repeat a figure is a figure of about four of us and not of anybody.*”

“*A woman of forty said on Monday that a case is not a set of figures.*”

“*Three people, three mouths, four days, and I had been doing it since a Friday three weeks ago.*”

“*Three people told me the same thing in three different mouths in four days and I had been doing it since a Friday three weeks ago.*”

“*And here is what I do not know, and I am going to say it on the first morning of a week with about nine of you standing here.*”

“*There is a thing that has been asking in the voice of a minute for about nine weeks. I have known it was there since the Friday of the hundred and twenty-ninth week and I have not named it and I have a reason for not naming it that I gave on the first day of this week and I am not withdrawing the reason.*”

“*My reason was that about four of you would have it and a woman standing in four feet of water who came two days and was asked nothing would not, and that has not changed in four days.*”

“*And I have been carrying a second thing this week and I am going to put it down. I do not know whether that is one thing or nine. I have been talking to it as if it is one, because that is what a person does, and about four of you have let me.*”

“*A man of thirty-eight has a man in a reed and a woman in a reed and a man in an ash. A man of sixty-one has four figures. I have thirteen things with no names in them.*”

“*That could be one thing with nine mouths or nine things, and I have not got a way of telling them apart, and I am not going to stand on this bank and find out which one it is by asking about nine of you what it sounds like.*”

Then a woman of sixty-four said she would say the word if he would say what it is not, and he said what it is not, in nine lines, and she said the word once.

Ada Renk is sixty-four. She got up out of a book of heads and carts in the open nineteen years ago and it cost her one thing a day of them, and she has a name in ink on that ninth line and has never had a day against it.

“*I said on Sunday that I would read out the line above the one I gave you and I was not going to and I am not, and this morning I am offering something else and it is a trade and I am saying it is a trade.*”

“*You say what that thing is not. Out loud. In front of these people. Nine lines, no more, and I have counted them in my head on the walk up.*”

“*And then I will say the word, once, and it will be in about nine ears and not nine hundred as it should be, and I will be sixty-four when I say it and I have been waiting nineteen years and about four of you would tell me that is a reason to wait and it is not.*”

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and has been in this county two hundred and forty-three days. The cut across that palm is a hundred and twelve days old and nobody has said whether it will keep.

“*It is not a person in this county and I have never been able to say that it is.*”

“*It is not a clerk. There are nine clerks in that room four hundred miles off and I have never seen one of them and I have not got a figure for any of them.*”

“*It is not the hearing. That happened. It was held. It is not a thing that is asking anything.*”

“*It is not my own handwriting and it is not my own asking, and I have said for a fortnight that everything said on this bank is said because of me and I am not withdrawing that.*”

“*It is not something a woman of sixty-four can be protected from by not saying a word, and I know that because she read nineteen words out on Sunday and about four of us now have them and nobody can take that back off us.*”

“*It is not a thing that can be refused. I have wanted it to be that for about nine weeks and a man of fifty-four said on a Friday that a man who is not allowed is not a man who has refused, and he is right and it is not one.*”

“*That is eight. I said nine and I have eight and I am not going to invent a ninth to make the trade even.*”

“*And I am not saying what it is, and about four of you have been waiting nine weeks for me to say it.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of me not having said it, and nobody thanks me for nine lines in place of it.*”

Ada Renk said the word once, out loud, on a Tuesday morning, in about nine people’s hearing, and did not say it twice.

“*First Witness.*”

“*That is the word, and it is said once this morning and then it is not said again today, and nobody is going to ask me about it.*”

“*I have not said it out loud in nineteen years, and I have said it in a morning where a man of thirty-one is the only person who could make it cost somebody.*”

“*And it cost a woman of twenty-nine in a reed, and it cost a man of thirty-four in an ash, and it cost a woman of thirty-four four hundred miles off, and it cost me, and it has cost this county nineteen years of a line with a name on it and no day against it.*”

“*Nobody thanks me for a word and nobody is told I was brave about it.*”

“*A man of fifty-four said that sentence to a man of thirty-one on a Friday. I have said it back to him twice since and I will say it a third time today.*”

Then a woman of forty-four gave three counts and said she is not going to weigh a word, and a boy of thirteen gave two, and a man of thirty-one gave the want and the mistake and the cost.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs at thirteen shillings and fourpence a year for nineteen years, and she is not a keeper of anything anybody can be sent for about. She said that a word spoken on that bank this morning is not hers to weigh.

“*One: a sheet of printed paper with three columns on that counter fifty-three mornings with nothing in any of the three.*”

“*Two: four leaves of a hearing on that counter forty-seven mornings with a number on them and no room and no names.*”

“*And three: ninety-two mornings of this bank, and ninety-two is seven hundred and ninety-seven less seven hundred and five.*”

“*And a word was said out loud on this bank this morning for the first time in nineteen years, and I am not going to say whether it was the right time, because I have said for a fortnight that weighing is somebody else’s morning.*”

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and forty-four days. He carried five counts for about nine weeks, put the sixth down on the Thursday of the week before last, and has been counting the mornings about four of us repeat a figure.

“*A hundred and forty-four days, and I counted them in the lane and it is one more than Monday.*”

“*And my second count is nineteen and it stopped on Monday because a man of thirty-eight stopped on purpose, and I have started a third count and I am not telling these people what it is.*”

“*I am counting the mornings about four of us do not say a figure when one of us could. That is four so far and today is four, and that is a figure of about four of us and not of anybody, and I learned that on Sunday.*”

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and has been in that county two hundred and forty-three days. Nobody on that bank has said whether the cut across that palm is going to keep, and he has asked five times.

“*The want has not moved in six days, and I am going to keep saying it has not moved until it does, and I am not going to know which morning that is.*”

“*I want somebody to be asked who can say no to me, and come back the next morning, and be asked again.*”

“*The mistake is the thirteenth of the same mistake and it is two days old, and I have given you a new one without meaning to.*”

“*I said on Monday that I have been turning people into figures, and then this morning I stood on this bank and traded nine lines about a word with a woman of sixty-four, and about four of you watched a man and a woman divide up the saying of a thing between them.*”

“*Ninety-seven days. Ninety-seven is seven hundred and ninety-seven less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer, and I have a word on this bank now that I did not have on Friday and I am further from an answer than I was on Friday.*”

“*The cost is that a woman of sixty-four said a word out loud for the first time in nineteen years, because I could not say it.*”

“*She is sixty-four, and about four of you heard it, and it is in nine ears and not nine hundred.*”

“*She did that so that this morning would have one thing in it that I did not do.*”

“*Nobody is relieved of having been traded with this morning, and nobody says a word of thanks to me for a trade, and nobody is told I was brave about one.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of the first morning of that week, and a word had been said once on it that had not been said in nineteen years.

The fever was seventy-three weeks and three days old. He had been in that county two hundred and forty-three days.

A man of fifty-four asked for the whole of a sheet to be read out loud at the top of a cut, so that it would be in the open whether anybody read it or not, and said that was his doing.

Eight things were said about what that is not, and a ninth was not invented to make a trade even.

A boy of thirteen said his count of the mornings about four of us do not say a figure was seven, and that it is a figure of about four of us and not of anybody.
