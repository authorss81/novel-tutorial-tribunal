# Chapter 797: First Day Of The Hundred And Thirty-Sixth Week, And A Tuesday, And A Woman Of Sixty-Four Says The Word Out Loud Here For The First Time Since She Said It Here Eighty-Nine Days Ago, And A Boy Of Thirteen Does A Sum Nobody Asked Him For And Gets Five, And The Thing That Has Been Asking In The Voice Of A Minute Answers In About Nine People’s Hearing And Says In Four Items That It Read The Line
