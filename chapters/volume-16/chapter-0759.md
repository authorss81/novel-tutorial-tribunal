# Chapter 759: Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Thirtieth Week, And A Saturday, And On Which The Fever Was Sixty-Eight Weeks Old, And The Bank Is Still Full At About Sixteen Inches Over The Top Step On The Fifth Morning Of It, And A Man Of About Fifty-Two Who Cannot See Well Asked For The Fifth Time What A Mark Is Worth As A Figure And A Woman Of Thirty-Four Gave Him One And Refused It

Fifth day of the hundred and thirtieth week. His five hundred and ninth morning. Three hundred and fifty-nine days after the settlement. The fever sixty-eight weeks old. Three hundred and thirteen days since the division. Two hundred and fifty-nine days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was still full at about the seventh hour, about sixteen inches over the top step, and it was the fifth morning of it. The man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working and said the figure for the next time about ninety steps can be walked and then said he was not going to give it again.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He has said he had nothing to say about a night five times and then stopped, and he has given two figures for a night since and says neither of those two is his. He is not going to give a fourth thing out of the three he gives every morning.

“*About sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour, and that is the fifth morning of it running, and a bank that is full stays at sixteen.*”

“*Five days. It was full on the Friday of this week and four inches a day is the working.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in about four days after that bank is full, and I said on Wednesday that I would not say that until Monday.*”

“*I said it on Friday and I am not going to say it a fifth time and I am not going to give you a fourth thing about a night.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open, and it was the thirty-eighth morning, and a man of thirty-eight read it because he was asked.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He has said he had nothing to say about a night five times and then stopped. He is not going to give a fourth thing out of the three he gives. A man of thirty-eight was there and about nine of us were.

“*Thirty-eight mornings and it is the last full day before Monday and I am going to be here Monday and I am not going to say anything about it.*”

“*I have still not answered the fifth thing. Nobody has asked me and I am still waiting for one of you to.*”

“*It is now about four weeks. I am not going to put a figure on it this morning because about four of you would want one.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the thirty-eight and nobody is going to be told I was brave about it.*”

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and is the only person on that bank who can put a light on a thing in the dark. He has read that sheet forty times in this county and forty-seven times as of yesterday.

“*Forty-nine is seven hundred and fifty-nine less seven hundred and ten, and there has not been one of the forty-nine in the dark.*”

“*And the four leaves of that hearing have been on that counter five mornings and about nine people have looked at them and about four have read the fourth line out loud and about nine have not.*”

“*A woman of forty read it on Thursday and would not read the rest. A man of thirty-one said on Thursday that he wanted the four leaves off this counter and is not going to move them.*”

“*A man of fifty-four asked me yesterday to say what the fifth morning was and I am not going to say it, because he has been asking for thirty-eight mornings and he has never once asked me anything.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the forty-nine.*”

Then a man of about fifty-two asked for the fifth time what a mark is worth as a figure, and gave the working, and about nine people heard it.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well, and is the fifth line of one of two sheets of deal that have been eleven years in a place he works. He has asked in the open what a mark is worth as a figure four times in a fortnight and been answered by nobody. He said at the end of that fortnight that the figure is owed to him and he is not going to stop asking for it, and that he asked it first.

“*Five times is five times. Four of them were in a fortnight and this is the fifth and it is three days after the end of that fortnight, and I said I was not going to stop asking so here is the fifth.*”

“*And I am going to say what I asked it first this time, because I said it once and about four of you did not believe me.*”

“*I asked it first because I have been the fifth line of two sheets of deal for eleven years and there is no fourth thing that comes before a fifth thing.*”

“*And I have been able to be checked against the four lines under me every morning for eleven years, and that is the whole of what a mark is for.*”

“*That is what a mark is for and it is not a figure and about four of you have been waiting four weeks for me to say it and I have not said it.*”

“*A mark is worth being sent for and it is not worth being believed, both at once about the same mark.*”

“*I have said that on this bank and about four of you believed the second half and about nine of you have not. I said it eleven years ago somewhere worse than this bank.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the five.*”

Then a woman of thirty-four gave him a figure, and refused it, and said why in four sentences.

Brenna Dole is thirty-four and has walked about two days from a place four hundred miles off this landing, and she cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from. She said on the Sunday of this week that she was one of the nine and that six were in that room.

“*Four sentences and I am giving the figure first because a man of thirty-one has been saying for about eleven days that the figure comes before the thing, and I agree with him about that and about nine of you do not.*”

“*What a mark is worth as a figure is nothing. That is my figure and I will give the working for it.*”

“*The working is that a mark is a thing a person says. I have been in a room where nine people said things, and only six of them are in that room, and the other three are a figure of nine.*”

“*That is the whole of my answer and I am refusing it as an answer in the next sentence.*”

“*I am not going to stand on this bank and give a figure of nine to a man who has asked five times and been owed one. A woman of thirty-four standing here is not a clerk and I did not come two days to be one.*”

“*I am going to say the last thing and then I am going to walk down that bank about two hundred yards to the water and stand in it until it is time for me to go.*”

“*About nine of you can decide for yourselves whether that is a thing a woman does or a thing a person does. And I am not going to be here on Monday.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the figure and nobody is going to be told the figure was right.*”

About four of them had a man of about fifty-two who had said out loud that a mark is a thing a person says and had not been able to say it for eleven years.

Then a woman of twenty-nine in a reed said the second half of a man’s sentence, out loud, from about twenty feet off the edge of that drain, and nobody repeated it back.

Marda Vell is twenty-nine and has been in a reed on the low side of that landing since the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-ninth week, a hundred and thirty days ago.

A hundred and thirty is seven hundred and fifty-nine less six hundred and twenty-nine. She has said two words in about eighty days and this is the third.

“*He is right.*”

Then about nine of them stood on that bank in the wind for about a minute and nobody said anything.

Then a man of thirty-one said what he wanted and what it is and what it costs, in about nine people’s hearing, and he did not give a figure.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and five days, two hundred and five being seven hundred and fifty-nine less five hundred and fifty-four. On Friday he said he would not decide which of about four people he was going to answer first.

“*Two hundred and five is seven hundred and fifty-nine less five hundred and fifty-four, and I have been in this county two hundred and five days, and nobody has asked me for anything in one of them.*”

“*Here is the thing I want and it is not a want about this county and I said on Thursday that I would stop calling that a want.*”

“*I want to be able to say what I am, out loud, on this bank, in about nine people’s hearing, and I have not been able to do it since the Friday of the hundred and twenty-eighth week.*”

“*That is because on that Friday I found out that the word I use for the thing I do on this bank is not a want and is not a refusal and is not an answer.*”

“*And I have been saying want and answer, and about four of you have believed one of those two and about nine of you have not, and neither of those is what the word is.*”

“*Here is the mistake, and it is the same one I said on Friday morning and I have had two days to sit with it and it is worse on Saturday.*”

“*On Friday I said that what I called a cost was a trade. That was true and it was small. The bigger one is that about four of you came up this bank on Friday morning to say what I am, and about four of you came up to say I should do something.*”

“*And I spent Friday morning and Friday afternoon talking about which one I would answer first, and about nine of you watched a man treat being asked what he is as though it were a request.*”

“*Being asked what you are is not a request. Nobody has ever put it on a piece of paper. There is no column for it and there is not going to be one.*”

“*And I am not going to answer it this morning.*”

“*I do not have an answer that is about this county, and I have been pretending for about eleven days that I did, and about four of you have known and about nine of you have not.*”

“*Nobody is going to be relieved about that and nobody is going to be thanked and nobody is going to be told I was right on Friday about picking.*”

“*Fifty-nine days. Fifty-nine is seven hundred and fifty-nine less seven hundred. I said on Friday that I would not say that word again until Monday and I am not going to say it again this morning.*”

Then a woman of forty said what a woman of twenty-nine said, and refused to repeat it back to her.

Wenna Callow is forty and asks for a day against a name and has never once asked for a reason instead.

“*A woman of twenty-nine said two words this morning and a man of about fifty-two said out loud what a mark is for, and I am not going to repeat either of those back to either of them.*”

“*That is not manners and it is not a rule and it is the first thing I have said this week that is not a count.*”

“*Say what you am standing in front of: about nine people on a bank on a Saturday morning and a woman standing in the water about two hundred yards down it who is not going to be here on Monday.*”

“*I am still not going to say which three of the five have a day against them, and nobody is going to be thanked for the three sentences.*”

About nine of them stood on that bank in the wind. A woman of thirty-four was in the water about two hundred yards down it, and a woman of twenty-nine was about twenty feet off the edge of that drain, and neither of them was going to be there on Monday.

---

The fever was sixty-eight weeks old. He had been in that county two hundred and five days.

A man of about fifty-two asked for the fifth time what a mark is worth as a figure and said out loud that a mark is a thing a person says.

He said he has been the fifth line of two sheets of deal for eleven years and has been able to be checked against the four lines under him every morning, and that that is the whole of what a mark is for.

And a woman of thirty-four gave him a figure of nothing, refused it as an answer in the same breath, and walked down the bank into the water.

And a woman of twenty-nine said two words from a reed, and they were that he is right, and nobody repeated them back to her.
