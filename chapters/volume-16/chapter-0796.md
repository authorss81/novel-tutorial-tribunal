# Chapter 796: Seventh Day Of The Hundred And Thirty-Fifth Week, And A Monday, And A Man Of Thirty-Eight Answers The Question He Was Asked On Friday Evening And Says He Will Stop, And A Woman Of Forty Asks Whether Anybody Here Can Refuse A Thing Put In Front Of Them By Somebody Four Hundred Miles Off

Seventh day of the hundred and thirty-fifth week. His five hundred and forty-sixth morning. Three hundred and ninety-six days after the settlement. The fever seventy-three weeks and two days. Three hundred and fifty days since the division. Two hundred and ninety-six days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was about eight inches on that cut at about the seventh hour, two days of the coming back, and the man of forty-three said the figure and gave the day-names in order because it is the last day of a week.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. Three things every morning. He named a stone on that bank on the Sunday of last week and about four of them walk past it every day and about nine of them do not.

“*Eight inches at the seventh hour, and it is two days since it came off, and about the first seventy of those ninety steps can be walked.*”

“*Four inches a day is the working and I have given that every morning this bank has been full and twice this week at the hour instead of after it.*”

“*And the next time all ninety of them can be walked is in six days, and that is the Sunday of next week, and the day-names run on from the Saturday I gave you two days ago.*”

“*And the days run Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday, Monday, and then it starts again on the Tuesday, and I have said that three times and I am saying it for the last time this week.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open, seventy-fifth morning, and asked out loud first, and asked for it at the top of the cut again because that is where he asked from first.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel on that landing and cannot read. He has been asking for that line in the open every morning since the Thursday of the hundred and twenty-fifth week, and went down ninety cut steps to have it read to him at the bottom of them on the Saturday.

“*Seventy-five mornings, and I am asking from the top of that cut again, and I want that said out loud before I ask, because I went down on Saturday and I have thought about it for two days.*”

“*I went down because I had been standing above it. That was the reason and I have said it, and I am going back up there this morning on purpose.*”

“*And I am not going to say the figure for the not being asked. A man of thirty-one said on Sunday that it is a number I can start trading on and I have been not asked for about three weeks and nobody has offered me anything for it, and that is because nobody wants it, not because I have held it well.*”

Then the man of thirty-eight answered the question he had been given on Friday evening, and it was not about the sheet, and about four of them did not expect it.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine and a board in the inside of his coat with thirty-two notches cut in the edge of it. He said on Friday that somebody had asked him a question and he would answer it on Monday.

“*On Friday evening a man asked me whether I would stop reading that line out loud in the open if he asked me to, and I said I would answer him on Monday, and here is Monday.*”

“*And the answer is that I would, and I have been thinking about it for two days, and that is the answer and not the other one.*”

“*Eighty-six is seven hundred and ninety-six less seven hundred and ten, and I am not going to say that figure today because I have said it for seventeen mornings and a man of thirty-one said on Sunday that a number a person repeats becomes a thing they are doing instead of a thing they are counting.*”

“*He is right and it is the second time he has been right at me and about four of you have noticed both.*”

“*So I am not giving the count today. I am giving the number of names, which is four, and it is the sixteenth morning of that, and I am giving it because a man of thirty-eight who can be asked should be able to stop.*”

“*The board has been in this coat ninety-eight days, and ninety-eight is seven hundred and ninety-six less six hundred and ninety-eight, and I have given that figure on every morning of the seventeen before this one and I am giving it today because it is the last one I am going to give.*”

“*Not because I have opened it. Because a figure a man repeats for ninety-eight days is a figure he is standing behind, and I have not got anything behind this one but a coat.*”

Then a woman of forty said out loud that a case is not a set of figures, and said how long she has been saying it, and asked a man of thirty-one a question in front of about nine people.

Wenna Callow is forty and has asked for a day against a name for eleven years and has never once asked for a reason instead. She went down that bank on the Monday of last week and asked a woman in a reed one question about ground and got one word back.

“*I have said since Wednesday that a case is not a set of figures, and I said it to myself on Wednesday and I am saying it out loud this morning for the first time, and I am about nine days late.*”

“*Wednesday was five days ago. The nine days are before that. I had it for nine days and I had nowhere to put it, and the nowhere was the cart road and it is the same nowhere it has always been and I am not going to call that a reason.*”

“*Here is what I have watched this week. A man opened a case on Wednesday out of nine things. He has added four more since. Thirteen things. And not one of them is a person who can be asked whether the case is a case.*”

“*A woman of sixty-four read out nineteen words on Sunday that were better than all thirteen of them, and she did it because she was asked to do nothing, and he is the one who stands on this bank every morning.*”

“*So I am going to ask you a question in front of these people and about four of them are going to want me not to, and I am asking it anyway.*”

“*The right of refusal. Not yours, not mine, and not the one a man of thirty-eight has just given up this morning.*”

“*Theirs. A woman of twenty-nine in that reed. A man of thirty-four in that ash. A woman of thirty-four four hundred miles off. A woman of sixty-four with a name in ink and no day.*”

“*Has anybody in this county got a way of refusing a thing that a person four hundred miles off can put in front of them? Because that is the case, and you have been building it out of paper.*”

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and forty-two days, two hundred and forty-two being seven hundred and ninety-six less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and eleven days old, a hundred and eleven being seven hundred and ninety-six less six hundred and eighty-five, and he has asked five times in about a fortnight whether it will keep and nobody has said.

“*I do not have one and I have not got one.*”

“*And I have not got one because I have spent ten days making sure that nobody in this county has to refuse anything, which I have been calling a margin, and which is a strip of ground with somebody at both ends of it, and which is not a right and is not a refusal and I have said that out loud on this bank once already.*”

“*And a man of thirty-eight has just given up a thing in about two days that I have been asking him for since the Friday of a fortnight ago, and about four of you have gone quiet, and I am not going to be relieved about any of that.*”

Then a woman of forty-four gave three counts, and a boy of thirteen gave his own and gave the second figure, and a man of thirty-one gave the want and the mistake and the cost and said the word *figure* out loud as a thing he has been doing.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs at thirteen shillings and fourpence a year for nineteen years, and she is not a keeper of anything anybody can be sent for about. She said on the Wednesday of this week that what to do about her three numbers is somebody else’s morning, and she said it again this morning about a figure a man of thirty-eight has stopped giving.

“*One: a sheet of printed paper with three columns on that counter fifty-two mornings with nothing in any of the three.*”

“*Two: four leaves of a hearing on that counter forty-six mornings with a number on them and no room and no names.*”

“*And three: ninety-one mornings of this bank, and ninety-one is seven hundred and ninety-six less seven hundred and five.*”

“*And a man of thirty-eight has stopped giving a figure this morning and a woman of forty has said that is the case, and I have got three numbers and they are all of the same kind.*”

“*They are all of things. That is what my three numbers are. They are not of people and they never were, and I have said that every morning for a fortnight and nobody has argued with me about it because I have not let anybody get past the first one.*”

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and forty-three days. He carried five counts for about nine weeks, put the sixth down on the Thursday of last week, and has been counting the mornings about four of them repeat a figure since Saturday.

“*A hundred and forty-three days, and I counted them in the lane and it is one more than Saturday.*”

“*And my second count is nineteen, and it is about four of you, and it is not a figure for anybody. It is a figure for about four of you, and that is a different thing and I did not know it was different until Wednesday.*”

“*And a man of thirty-eight has stopped, so it is going to be nineteen and I am not going to keep counting a thing that has stopped on purpose.*”

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and has been in this county two hundred and forty-two days. The cut across that palm is a hundred and eleven days old and nobody has said whether it will keep.

“*The want is the same want. I want somebody to be asked who can say no to me, and come back the next morning, and be asked again.*”

“*The mistake is the twelfth of the same mistake and it is two days old, and a woman of forty has said it out loud this morning and said it better than I said it on Saturday, and I am not going to give you a new one.*”

“*I have been standing on this bank for ten days turning people into figures. I did it to myself first and then I did it to about nine of you by asking, and a man of thirty-eight has been made into a figure of eighty-six mornings by a man of thirty-eight, and a boy of thirteen has been made into a figure of about four of you by a boy of thirteen.*”

“*Ninety-six days. Ninety-six is seven hundred and ninety-six less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer.*”

“*And the cost is that a man of thirty-eight is going to stop reading that line out loud in the open in about nine people’s hearing, on Monday, and about four of us wanted it and one of us is sixty-four.*”

“*Nobody thanks me for that and nobody is relieved of it, and nobody gets told I was brave about a fortnight.*”

“*And the right of refusal is not restored this morning, and I am not saying that it was.*”

About nine of them were on that bank at about the eleventh hour of the last day of that week, and one of them had stopped doing a thing he had done for seventeen mornings, and a boy of thirteen had stopped counting it.

---

The fever was seventy-three weeks and two days old. He had been in that county two hundred and forty-two days.

A man of thirty-eight said he would stop reading the line out loud in the open if he was asked to, and had two days to decide and had decided.

A woman of forty asked a man of thirty-one, in front of about nine people, whether anybody in this county has a way of refusing a thing a person four hundred miles off can put in front of them.

And he said he did not have one, and that the right of refusal was not restored that morning and he was not going to say that it was.
