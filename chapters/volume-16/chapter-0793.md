# Chapter 793: Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Thirty-Fifth Week, And A Friday, And A Full Bank On The Fourth Morning Of It, And A Man Of Thirty-Four In An Ash Is Walked Down To The Edge Of That Drain And Is Not Asked A Question And Says Three Things Nobody Asked Him For

Fourth day of the hundred and thirty-fifth week. His five hundred and forty-third morning. Three hundred and ninety-three days after the settlement. The fever seventy-two weeks and six days. Three hundred and forty-seven days since the division. Two hundred and ninety-three days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was still about sixteen inches over the top step at about the seventh hour and it was seven days of it, and that is the fourth morning of a bank sitting at that figure, and the man of forty-three said so before anybody asked him.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. Three things every morning. He has offered to hold the end of anything about nine of them have measured and it has not been taken up.

“*Seven days of it now, and that is the fourth morning of a full bank, and a full bank holds where it is until it goes.*”

“*Tomorrow morning those ninety steps can be walked, and that is the fifth day of this week, and it will be the sixteenth time, and I said on the Monday that the next one would be a Saturday.*”

“*And I have given the day-names in order twice in three weeks and about four of you have them right now, and I am not going to give them a third time unless somebody asks me for one.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open, seventy-second morning, and he said the number out loud again and said what he wanted done with it.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel on that landing and cannot read. He has asked for that line in the open every morning since the Thursday of the hundred and twenty-fifth week. He asked about the fifth thing on the Monday of last week in the ordinary voice and about four of them did not answer him.

“*Seventy-two mornings.*”

“*And I gave the figure for the not being asked out loud yesterday for the first time, and it is a week and a half, and nobody answered it, and that was the point of saying it.*”

“*Here is what I want done with it. I do not want it argued with. I do not want one of you to tell me I have been patient. I want it written on nothing, which is what has happened to it, and I want that to have been my doing.*”

“*I have been not asked for about a fortnight and a half and I have not been asked by anybody, and I would like that to be a thing I did.*”

Then the man of thirty-eight read the line and gave the count and the number of names and said that he is not going to say the fourth name out loud today.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine and a board in the inside of his coat with thirty-two notches cut in the edge of it. He has said the number of names out loud every morning since the Friday of last week and gave the reason once.

“*Four names, and it is the thirteenth morning, and I am not going to say the fourth one today and about four of you will notice and I do not care.*”

“*Eighty-three is seven hundred and ninety-three less seven hundred and ten, and I read the ninth, because a man of fifty-four asked for the ninth and nobody has asked me for a second line.*”

“*The board has been in this coat ninety-five days, and ninety-five is seven hundred and ninety-three less six hundred and ninety-eight, and there is a boy on this bank who can see the notches on it and has not said so and I have not asked him.*”

Then a man of thirty-four in an ash was walked down to the edge of that drain on a Friday morning, and nobody asked him a question, and he said three things.

Ivo Kellow is thirty-four and has been in an ash on the low side of that landing a hundred and sixty-three days, and there is a day against his name on that ninth line, and nobody in this county has ever asked him one question.

“*Ivo Kellow. Thirty-four. A hundred and sixty-three days, and that is seven hundred and ninety-three less six hundred and thirty.*”

“*I have been in this ash since a Tuesday in a fortnight before the water came up to the second step and I have not been asked anything by anybody in this county in all that time, and about four of you have walked past me and looked down.*”

“*And a man of thirty-one came down here yesterday and told me he could not use a day against my name, and he is right, and he is the first person who has come down here and said a true thing about me.*”

“*I am not going to be asked a question this morning. I know what I would say to one and I have had a hundred and sixty-three days to work out what it is, and if somebody asks me for it now it will be a thing I have said because I was asked.*”

“*So I am going to say three things to nobody, on my own, and then I am going to go back up the bank, and about four of you can write them down or not.*”

“*One: I was in that room. I will not say which day I was there and I will not say what I said, and both of those are mine and not the county’s.*”

“*Two: I have a day against my name and I have not spent it, and I have not spent it because nobody would tell me what spending it is for.*”

“*Three: I am not the only one. There is somebody about two hundred yards off that landing in a place with water in it and she has not got a day and she is not in a tree, she is in the ground, and I have never been able to say that out loud in this county and I have just said it.*”

Then a woman of forty said that nobody is to go and ask him, and a man of thirty-one said he is not going to, and gave the reason in four lines.

Wenna Callow is forty and has asked for a day against a name for eleven years and has never once asked for a reason instead. She went down that bank on Monday of last week and asked a woman in a reed one question about ground and got one word.

“*He has just told us there is somebody in the ground about two hundred yards off that landing and I am not going down there today and I am not going to ask him a second question about it.*”

“*He said three things to nobody on purpose and about four of you heard all three, and the whole point of saying a thing to nobody is that it cannot be asked after.*”

“*If I go down there now and ask him one question then nothing he said this morning was said to nobody, and he will have said it to me, and I have not got a right to that and neither has anybody else on this bank.*”

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and thirty-nine days, two hundred and thirty-nine being seven hundred and ninety-three less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and eight days old, a hundred and eight being seven hundred and ninety-three less six hundred and eighty-five, and nobody has said whether it will keep.

“*I am not going down there and I am not going to send anybody and I have said so in front of these people before I have done it, which is the only way I have of not doing it later.*”

“*What he gave me this morning is a figure of a place and a thing in the ground, and I have been building a case out of figures of places since Wednesday.*”

“*And I am not going to put it in the case. It was not said to the case. It was said to nobody and about four of you were standing near enough to hear it and that is not the same as being given it.*”

Then a woman of forty-four gave three counts, and a boy of thirteen gave his own, and a man of thirty-one gave the want and the mistake and the cost and said the ninth thing on his list has now got two people in it and neither of them will be named.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs at thirteen shillings and fourpence a year for nineteen years, and she is not a keeper of anything anybody can be sent for about. She said on the Wednesday of this week that what to do about her three numbers is somebody else’s morning, and she said it again this morning about a man in a tree.

“*One: a sheet of printed paper with three columns on that counter forty-nine mornings with nothing in any of the three.*”

“*Two: four leaves of a hearing on that counter forty-three mornings with a number on them and no room and no names.*”

“*And three: eighty-eight mornings of this bank, and eighty-eight is seven hundred and ninety-three less seven hundred and five.*”

“*And I am not going to say what to do about a man in an ash today, and I said on Wednesday that it is somebody else’s morning and it is not mine, and a man in a tree is not a chair and he is not on my counter.*”

Wat Marshe is thirteen and has been in this county a hundred and forty days. He carried five counts for about nine weeks, put the sixth down on the Thursday of last week, and held nineteen minutes at the top of ninety cut steps on the Friday of last week.

“*A hundred and forty days, and I counted them in the lane and it is one more than yesterday.*”

“*And a man of thirty-eight says there is a boy on this bank who can see the notches on his board, and I have not said so, and I am not going to say so today either, and I would like it noticed that I have not.*”

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and has been in this county two hundred and thirty-nine days. The cut across that palm is a hundred and eight days old and nobody has said whether it will keep.

“*The want is unchanged. I want somebody to be asked who can say no to me and come back the next morning and be asked again.*”

“*The mistake is the ninth of the same mistake and it is one day old. I built a case on Wednesday out of nine things and one of the nine has now got a man in it, and I nearly put him in it this morning at about the tenth hour.*”

“*Ninety-three days. Ninety-three is seven hundred and ninety-three less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer and I am not going to answer it.*”

“*The cost is that I walked down about a hundred yards of that bank this morning to stand next to a man nobody had asked anything in a hundred and sixty-three days, and about nine of you watched me do it, and I did not ask him either, and I want it said that not asking him was the hardest thing I did today and I got it right.*”

“*And the ninth thing on my list has two people in it now and I have the name of neither, and I am not going to get either, and a case with a figure in it and no name in it is a case about a figure.*”

About nine of them stood on that bank at about the eleventh hour of a Friday, and a man of thirty-four had gone back up it, and about four of them had heard all three of his things.

---

The fever was seventy-two weeks and six days old. He had been in that county two hundred and thirty-nine days.

A man of thirty-four in an ash said three things to nobody, and one of them was that somebody is in the ground about two hundred yards off that landing and has no day.

A woman of forty said she was not going down there to ask him a second question, because then nothing he said would have been said to nobody.

And a man of thirty-one said not asking him was the hardest thing he did that day, and that the ninth thing on his own list has two people in it and the name of neither.
