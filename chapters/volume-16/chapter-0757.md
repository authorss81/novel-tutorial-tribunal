# Chapter 757: Third Day Of The Hundred And Thirtieth Week, And A Thursday, And The Bank Is About Twelve Inches Over The Top Step On The Third Day Of It, And A Woman Of Forty-Reads The Fifth Line Of A Hearing Out Loud In The Open For The First Time In Her Life And Is Bad At It And Says So First, And About Four Of The Nine Have Worked Out What She Was Reading And About Nine Have Not

Third day of the hundred and thirtieth week. His five hundred and seventh morning. Three hundred and fifty-seven days after the settlement. The fever sixty-seven weeks and five days. Three hundred and eleven days since the division. Two hundred and fifty-seven days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about twelve inches over the top step at about the seventh hour and it was the third day of it. The man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working and gave the figure for the next time about ninety steps can be walked, and nobody had asked him for any of the three.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He said he had nothing to say about a night five times and then he stopped saying it. There are three things he gives every morning and there is not going to be a fourth thing out of them. A man of thirty-eight was there and about nine of us were.

“*About twelve inches over the top step at the seventh hour, and it is the third morning of it.*”

“*Three days. It was about eight at this hour yesterday and four inches a day is the working.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in about four days after that bank is full, and I said on Tuesday that I would not say that again until Monday and I am not going to say it again today.*”

“*And there is no fourth thing out of the three this morning and there is not going to be one.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open, and it was the thirty-sixth morning, and a man of thirty-eight read it because he was asked.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He has said he had nothing to say about a night five times and then stopped, and he has given two figures for a night since. He is not going to give a fourth thing out of the three he gives every morning. About nine of them were on that bank this morning.

“*Thirty-six mornings and I am not going to say anything else about the count this morning because I have said it thirty-six times and about four of you can count.*”

“*And it is the third day of a week and I asked and he read and that is three days running and I am not going to call it a habit.*”

“*Nobody has asked me the fifth thing and I have not answered it and I am not going to stop saying so.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the thirty-six and nobody is going to be told I was brave about it.*”

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and is the only person on that bank who can put a light on a thing in the dark. He has read that sheet forty times in this county and forty-five times as of yesterday.

“*Forty-seven is seven hundred and fifty-seven less seven hundred and ten, and there has not been one of the forty-seven in the dark.*”

“*And I am not going to say the sentence about being asked before I start, because a man of fifty-four told me on Wednesday that about four of you had not thought about the asking and about four of you had.*”

“*And I have started saying it, and that is the same shape as what a man of thirty-one has been doing for about eleven days and I am not going to improve on it in front of about nine people.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the forty-seven.*”

Then a woman of forty read the fifth line of a hearing out loud in the open for the first time in her life, and said before she started that she was going to be bad at it.

“*I have not read anything out loud in front of about nine people in my life and I am going to be bad at it and I am telling you that first so that nobody has to be polite about it afterwards.*”

“*It says that the finding is made in the absence of the persons heard about, and that their absence is not to be read as consent, and that the finding is to be held open for nine days for any person heard about to come forward.*”

“*I am reading it wrong. I have read it three times and I have stopped twice and I have not read the whole of it out loud and about four of you have worked out what I am leaving out and about nine of you have not.*”

“*And I am not going to read the rest of it.*”

“*A man of about fifty-two asked in the open four times in a fortnight what a mark is worth as a figure and nobody answered him.*”

“*I am not going to be the fifth person this week to put a thing on that counter that nobody in this county can be sent for about.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for three readings and nobody is going to be told I was right about what the fourth line says.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said what he wanted, and what it is about, and he gave it before the shape, and it was the last time he was going to say the word for about four days.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and three days, two hundred and three being seven hundred and fifty-seven less five hundred and fifty-four. Yesterday he said he wanted to walk four miles up a cart road and was not going to.

“*Two hundred and three is seven hundred and fifty-seven less five hundred and fifty-four, and I have been in this county two hundred and three days, and nobody has asked me for anything in one of them.*”

“*Here is the thing I want and I am going to call it a thing and not a want, because I said on Wednesday morning that I was going to stop saying the other word after that morning and about four of you counted.*”

“*I want the four leaves to stop being on that counter. All four of them. And I am not going to move them and I am not going to ask anybody to move them and I am saying it out loud so that a woman of thirty-four standing at the end of this bank can hear me say it.*”

“*A woman of forty read out loud on Thursday morning three things off the fourth line of that paper.*”

“*That a finding is made in the absence of the persons heard about. That their absence is not to be read as consent. And that it is to be held open for nine days for any person heard about to come forward.*”

“*That is on the fourth line of a paper that came down that cart road on the bread four days ago.*”

“*About nine people on this bank have now heard about nine days of a thing, and there is one woman in this county who is on that line and she is about twenty feet off the edge of that drain.*”

“*And about nine days is nine days from whatever day that was, and I am not going to work out which day that was in my own head this morning and I am saying that out loud too.*”

“*And here is the mistake and it is mine. I said on Wednesday that I was not going to walk those four miles. I said the reason was that about nine people in four counties would have a man of thirty-one going up a road saying a thing about a day nobody has seen written down.*”

“*That reason was about what about nine people would think of me. Today’s thing is about a woman about twenty feet off that drain and it is not about what anybody would think of me, and I have not said the word about eleven times this morning and I am not going to.*”

“*Nobody is going to be relieved about that and nobody is going to be thanked and nobody is going to be told I was right on Wednesday about the shape.*”

“*Fifty-seven days. Fifty-seven is seven hundred and fifty-seven less seven hundred.*”

“*Nobody has answered it and nobody is going to answer it for me and I am not going to answer it.*”

“*I said on Wednesday that I would not say that word again on this bank for about four days. I am not going to say it again until Monday.*”

Then a man of sixty-one said what the nine days are a figure of and what they are not, and refused to write the second number down.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one, and has kept a book of heads and carts in this county for eleven years, and has written two numbers in it in eleven years. He wrote the second one on the last day of the hundred and twenty-ninth week and has refused to give the working for it out loud three times.

“*Two numbers in eleven years and I am not going to write a third one this morning and about four of you are going to be disappointed about that.*”

“*Here is what a figure of nine days is a figure of. It is a figure of a paper four hundred miles off and a line on it, and nobody in this county can be sent for about when those nine days run out.*”

“*And here is what it is not a figure of.*”

“*It is not a figure of a woman about twenty feet off the edge of that drain. She has not got the paper. She has not got the line. She has not got anybody who can be sent for about her.*”

“*A man of thirty-one has just said he wants the four leaves to stop being on that counter, and said he is not going to move them and is not going to ask anybody to move them.*”

“*A man of sixty-one is not going to help with that either, and about four of you did not know I was going to say it until this second.*”

“*I have eleven years of carts going up and down that road and there are about nine houses at the top of it that I can be sent for about.*”

“*None of them has said they have seen a paper. I am not going to put a number on that leaf that says one of them has.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for two figures of nine days and nobody is going to be told which of them is the true one.*”

Then a boy of thirteen carried five counts and said he had not broken his own rule yet and gave the working for all five.

Wat Marshe is thirteen, and his mother is forty-one and keeps two carts and neither of them can be sent for about. He has been keeping five counts since the Monday of the hundred and twenty-eighth week.

“*A hundred and four days. A hundred and four is seven hundred and fifty-seven less six hundred and fifty-three.*”

“*And thirty-six mornings, and a hundred and twenty-seven days for a man in an ash, and a hundred and twenty-eight for a woman in a reed, and two numbers in a book.*”

“*That is five and I have carried five and I said on Wednesday that I would carry six on Friday and it is Thursday and I am not carrying six this morning.*”

“*And I am not going to carry a sixth thing a woman of forty said this morning about a nine days that is four hundred miles off.*”

“*That is not a count of anything I have counted, and I said last week I would not carry a figure that is not a day-count.*”

“*Friday is tomorrow and about four of you are going to tell me that is carrying out a promise.*”

“*About nine of you are going to notice that what I promised on Wednesday was a day, and what I am refusing now is a count, and those are two different instruments.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for five.*”

About nine of them stood on that bank in the wind with four leaves of a hearing on that counter.

A woman of thirty-four was at the end of it and had heard a man of thirty-one say out loud that he wanted the four leaves off that counter.

---

The fever was sixty-seven weeks and five days old. He had been in that county two hundred and three days.

A woman of forty read out loud in the open, for the first time in her life in front of about nine people, three things off the fourth line of a hearing.

That a finding is made in the absence of the persons heard about. That their absence is not to be read as consent. And that it is held open for nine days for any person heard about to come forward.

She read it wrong, stopped twice, and would not read the rest.

And a man of thirty-one said he wants the four leaves to stop being on that counter, and said he is not going to move them and is not going to ask anybody to move them.

He said there is one woman in this county on that line and she is about twenty feet off the edge of that drain.

And a man of sixty-one said a figure of nine days is a figure of a paper four hundred miles off and not a figure of a woman who has not got the paper and cannot be sent for about.
