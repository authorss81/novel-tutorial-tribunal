# Chapter 758: Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Thirtieth Week, And A Friday, And The Bank Is Full Again At About Sixteen Inches Over The Top Step On The Fourth Day Of It, And About Four Of The Nine Who Come Up That Bank Came Up It To Say What He Is And About Four Of The Nine Want Him To Say Yes To A Thing That Was Asked Fifty-Eight Days Ago, And He Says He Is Not Going To Decide Which Of Those Two He Is Going To Answer First

Fourth day of the hundred and thirtieth week. His five hundred and eighth morning. Three hundred and fifty-eight days after the settlement. The fever sixty-seven weeks and six days. Three hundred and twelve days since the division. Two hundred and fifty-eight days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was full again at about the seventh hour, about sixteen inches over the top step, and it was the fourth day of it. The man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working and gave the figure for the next time about ninety steps can be walked, and nobody had asked him for any of the three.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and about ninety steps of the work are under the bank he stands on. He has said he had nothing to say about a night five times and then stopped. He is not going to give a fourth thing out of the three he gives every morning. About nine of them were on that bank this morning.

“*About sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour, and it is the fourth morning of it, and a bank that is full holds at sixteen.*”

“*Four days. It was about twelve at this hour yesterday and four inches a day is the working.*”

“*And the next time anybody can walk about ninety steps there is in about four days after that bank is full.*”

“*I said that on Wednesday and said I would not say it again until Monday. I am not saying it again.*”

“*And I am not giving about nine of you a fourth thing out of the three I give every morning.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open, and it was the thirty-seventh morning, and a man of thirty-eight read it because he was asked.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He said he had nothing to say about a night five times and then he stopped saying it. He gives three things every morning and there is not going to be a fourth thing out of them. About nine of them were on that bank this morning.

“*Thirty-seven mornings.*”

“*And it is the fourth day of a week and the water is full and there are about nine more people on this bank this morning than there were on Monday and about four of them did not come up here to ask me anything.*”

“*I am still not answering the fifth thing and nobody has asked me and I am still waiting, and about four of you up here this morning came to say what a man of thirty-one is and not to say what I am.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the thirty-seven and nobody is going to be told I was brave about it.*”

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and is the only person on that bank who can put a light on a thing in the dark. He has read that sheet forty times in this county and forty-six times as of yesterday.

“*Forty-eight is seven hundred and fifty-eight less seven hundred and ten, and there has not been one of the forty-eight in the dark.*”

“*About four people came up this bank this morning to say what a man of thirty-one is, and about four came to say he should say yes to a thing.*”

“*That is about eight people and about nine of us and I am not going to add them.*”

“*A woman of twenty-eight told me on the Wednesday of a fortnight that a second time is a rule. There are about nine people on this bank who have now said the same sentence about him in about four days and I am not going to say that sentence myself.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the forty-eight.*”

Then about four of them said it, and they said it in four different orders, and a man of sixty-one counted them before he let any of them finish.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one, and has kept a book of heads and carts in this county for eleven years, and has written two numbers in it in eleven years.

“*Four things were said at me at once from four directions this morning and I counted them before I let any of them finish.*”

“*About four people coming up that bank to say what a man is is not the same thing as nine people coming up that bank to ask a man something.*”

“*One: you are the one who wrote it. Two: you are the one who should have refused it. Three: you should say yes to the thing that was asked on the Wednesday. Four: you should go up that cart road.*”

“*And about four of you said the fourth one and about four of you said the first one, and about nine of us are standing here and that is a figure of this bank on this morning and not a figure of this county.*”

“*A man of thirty-one has been in this county two hundred and four days and I am not going to write any of those four on the leaf after the last head.*”

“*I have written two numbers in eleven years and neither of those two was a person.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the count and nobody is going to be told I think any of those four is wrong.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said he was not going to decide which of the two he was going to answer first, and gave the working, and gave the reason.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and four days, two hundred and four being seven hundred and fifty-eight less five hundred and fifty-four. He gave up being nobody’s for one morning on the Friday of the hundred and twenty-eighth week by saying a word out loud in about nine people’s hearing.

“*Two hundred and four is seven hundred and fifty-eight less five hundred and fifty-four, and I have been in this county two hundred and four days, and nobody has asked me for anything in one of them.*”

“*About four of you came up this bank this morning to tell me what I am. I am going to say something about that before I say anything else and then I am going to not say anything else about it today.*”

“*A woman of twenty-eight told me on Wednesday that about nine people would rather I asked nothing than asked in that order. I have thought about that for a day and a half and here is what I have got, and it is not good, and I am saying it anyway.*”

“*If I answer the fourth one first, which is about four of you, and I go up that cart road, then about four of you will have got what you came for and about four of you will not.*”

“*And about nine of them will have watched a man nobody can be sent for about choose which of them he answered.*”

“*If I answer the third one first, which is about four of you, and I say yes to a thing that was asked fifty-eight days ago, then a woman of thirty-four standing at the end of this bank will have heard me say yes to a thing nobody in this county can be sent for about.*”

“*She came two days to say one sentence about a room and I will have answered a different thing in front of her.*”

“*And I am not going to choose. That is the sentence. I am not going to pick which of about four of you I answer first, because picking is the instrument.*”

“*About four of you would work out what I picked and about nine of you would not, and then about four of you would be the ones who got a man of thirty-one to do a thing.*”

“*Here is the mistake and it is mine and I worked it out on Wednesday and have not said it since. On the Friday of the hundred and twenty-eighth week I said a word out loud and I said the cost of it was that about four people can now say a man of thirty-one said that word. I said it was a cost. It was not a cost, it was a trade, and I have been calling it a cost for about four days because a cost is a word I know how to use.*”

“*Nobody is going to be relieved about that and nobody is going to be thanked and nobody is going to be told I was right on the Friday about it being a rule.*”

“*Fifty-eight days. Fifty-eight is seven hundred and fifty-eight less seven hundred. I said on Wednesday that I would not say that word again on this bank until Monday and I am not going to say it again this morning.*”

Then a woman of twenty-eight said what four people coming up a bank to say what a man is, is, in six sentences, and refused the seventh.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from, and has been in this county a hundred and sixteen days, a hundred and sixteen being seven hundred and fifty-eight less six hundred and forty-two. She refused a man of thirty-one in nine sentences on the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-seventh week and said a second time is a rule.

“*Six sentences and I am not going to say the seventh, and the seventh is the one I would say if I were about to say anything worse.*”

“*One: four people who came up a bank to say what a man is did not come up a bank to ask a man anything, and those are two different things and the difference is the whole of what has happened this week.*”

“*Two: about four of you want a man of thirty-one to go up four miles. About four of you want a man of thirty-one to say yes. Neither of those is a want about this county and both of them are a want about who gets to use him.*”

“*Three: a man of sixty-one counted four things at me this morning and said he was not going to write any of them down.*”

“*That is the first time in about eleven years that I have wanted to be in a book and could not be.*”

“*Four: I have been in this county a hundred and sixteen days. I have watched about nine people on this bank be kind to him about eleven mornings in about eleven days.*”

“*About four of those eleven were kind because he said a want out loud and about four were kind because he did not say one.*”

“*Five: that is eleven mornings and it is a figure of about eleven mornings on a bank in this county and it is not a figure of anything else and I am not going to let it be one.*”

“*Six, and it is why I said six. Nobody on this bank has asked me a question this week, and about four of you would rather he answered somebody else first, and I would rather he answered somebody else first too.*”

“*Those are the same thing and neither of us is going to say so on this bank.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the six sentences and nobody is going to be told I have begun again.*”

About nine of them stood on that bank in the wind with about four more than there were on Monday and a man of thirty-one who would not choose which of them he answered.

The fever was sixty-seven weeks and six days old. He had been in that county two hundred and four days.

About four people came up that bank on Friday morning to say what a man of thirty-one is, and they said it in four different orders.

About four of them wanted him to go up the cart road. About four of them wanted him to say yes to a thing that was asked fifty-eight days ago.

And a man of thirty-one said out loud that he was not going to decide which of those two he was going to answer first, because picking is the instrument.

Then he said that what he called a cost on the Friday of the hundred and twenty-eighth week was not a cost and was a trade.

And a woman of twenty-eight said in six sentences that people who came up a bank to say what a man is did not come up a bank to ask a man anything, and refused a seventh.
