# Chapter 752: Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-Ninth Week, And A Saturday, And On Which The Fever Was Sixty-Seven Weeks Old, And A Man Of Thirty-Eight Reads Four Leaves Of A Hearing Out Loud In The Open Because About Nine People Have Said Nothing At All About It, And The Fifth Line Of It Says A Woman Of Twenty-Nine Was Heard About And Did Not Object, And She Is Twenty Feet Off The Edge Of That Drain

Fifth day of the hundred and twenty-ninth week. His five hundred and second morning. Three hundred and fifty-two days after the settlement. The fever sixty-seven weeks old. Three hundred and six days since the division. Two hundred and fifty-two days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was still full at about the seventh hour, about sixteen inches over the top step, and it was the sixth morning of it. The man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working and gave the figure for the next time about ninety steps can be walked, and nobody had asked him for any of the three.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He has said he had nothing to say about a night five times and then stopped. He is not going to give a fourth thing out of the three he gives.

“*About sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour, and it is the sixth morning of it, and a bank that is full holds at sixteen.*”

“*Six days. It was full on the Monday of this week and four inches a day is the working and I am not rounding it.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in two days from this morning, and that is the Monday, and I said that on Wednesday and I said it yesterday.*”

“*Nobody is going to thank me for the three and I am not giving about nine of you a fourth thing.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open before anything else happened, and it was the thirty-first morning, and a man of thirty-eight read it, because about nine people on that bank had said nothing at all this morning about not doing it.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He has said he had nothing to say about a night five times and then stopped. He is not going to give a fourth thing out of the three he gives. About nine of them were on that bank this morning.

“*Thirty-one mornings.*”

“*And a man of thirty-eight read it this morning because about nine of you said nothing about not doing it, and I heard none of you say nothing, and I am telling you what the reason was and the reason was not me.*”

“*It is the second day of a week and I am going to say on Monday that I asked him and he read it, and I am saying today that I did not ask him.*”

“*The fifth thing is still not answered and nobody has asked me and I am still waiting for one of you to.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the thirty-one and nobody is going to be told I was brave about asking for a thing that took about nine people saying nothing to get done.*”

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and is the only person on that bank who can put a light on a thing in the dark. He has read that sheet forty times in this county. He stopped deciding at the Sunday of the hundred and twenty-seventh week. He said yesterday that he would read it again when somebody on that bank asked him to in front of about nine people and that he was not going to ask.

“*Forty-two is seven hundred and fifty-two less seven hundred and ten, and there has not been one of the forty-two in the dark.*”

“*About nine people said nothing at all this morning and I read that line because of it, and I am not going to improve on what that is.*”

“*A woman of twenty-eight said on the Wednesday of a week about two weeks ago that a second time is a rule. Yesterday I said I would read it when I was asked. This morning I was not asked.*”

“*And a man of fifty-four just said out loud in about nine people’s hearing that the reason was not him, and I am not going to argue with him about that in front of about nine people.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the forty-two and nobody is going to be told I was brave about reading one line because a room was quiet.*”

Then he read the four leaves out loud in the open, all four of them, in the order they were printed, and nobody stopped him, and it took about as long as a man of forty-three gives a reading.

“*It says a place, and it says a day, and it says nine, and it says what they were heard about.*”

“*The thing they were heard about is a line. It is the ninth line of a sheet of printed paper and it has names against it and a day against it and that is the whole of what the four leaves are about.*”

“*And it says, and I am reading this out loud because I am reading all four leaves out loud and I am not going to do four out of five: that the nine who were heard about it did not object.*”

“*Nine people were heard about a line and nine people did not object and there is no name on any of those nine anywhere on any of these four leaves.*”

“*And about four of you counted the nine with me and about nine of you did not, and nobody is going to be thanked for the five lines and nobody is going to be told I was right about there being no names on them.*”

Then nothing whatever happened for about a minute, and the wind came up the reach and went away again, and a man of thirty-four in an ash and a woman of twenty-nine in a reed were about a hundred yards apart along the edge of that drain and neither of them was there.

Then a man of sixty-one said what a minute is and what it is not, in six sentences, and the sixth one is the reason and it is his.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one, and has kept a book of heads and carts in this county for eleven years. One number is written in it and that number is the last of five lines on the leaf after the last head. He stopped a count of days himself on the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-fifth week and has refused the figure of it out loud every morning since.

“*Six sentences and the sixth of them is the reason and it is mine and it is about what a piece of paper is.*”

“*One: a book of heads and carts keeps what people came and went and what they fetched and went away with, and about nine people can be sent for about anybody in it.*”

“*Two: a form asks. A form has a line with a name on it and a line with a day on it and a person stands there or does not, and I have been on the wrong side of that for eleven years.*”

“*Three: a minute does not ask anybody. A minute says afterwards that people were there and said things, and the afterwards is the whole instrument.*”

“*Four: a minute about nine people who did not object is not a figure of nine people. It is a figure of an argument that nine people were not in the middle of.*”

“*Five: a man of thirty-eight read that out loud in the open this morning and I am not going to tell him he was wrong, because there is nothing on those four leaves to be wrong about. There are no names. There is no room. There is a number and a day and a line and a thing that was not objected to.*”

“*Six, and it is the reason I said six. A man of sixty-one keeps a book and can be sent for about what is in it, and I am not going to write nine on a leaf after the last head this morning, because nine is not a thing I saw.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the six sentences.*”

About four of them had a man of sixty-one who said he would not write nine on a leaf because nine is not a thing he saw, and about nine of them had four leaves of paper with nine on them and no names.

Then a woman of twenty-nine said one word from twenty feet off the edge of that drain, and it was the first word anybody in that county had heard her say in about eighty days, and nobody repeated it back.

Marda Vell is twenty-nine and has been in a reed on the low side of that landing since the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-ninth week, a hundred and twenty-three days ago. A hundred and twenty-three is seven hundred and fifty-two less six hundred and twenty-nine. She has said one word in about eighty days and this is the second one.

“*No.*”

Then a boy of thirteen gave four counts back and said he was not going to carry a fifth, and gave the working for all four.

Wat Marshe is thirteen, and his mother is forty-one and has two carts and neither of them can be sent for about. He has been keeping five counts since the Monday of the hundred and twenty-eighth week and he has carried five every morning since and he is not going to carry six.

“*Ninety-nine days. Ninety-nine is seven hundred and fifty-two less six hundred and fifty-three, and I counted it myself on the road coming down and nobody asked me.*”

“*And thirty-one mornings of a man of fifty-four asking, and a hundred and twenty-two days for a man in an ash and a hundred and twenty-three for a woman in a reed.*”

“*And that is four, and I said yesterday that I was going to carry five and I have carried five.*”

“*And I am not going to carry six this morning because a woman of twenty-nine said one word out of a reed and I do not know what it is a count of.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the four and nobody is going to be told I was right about the reed.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said what he wanted before anything else and gave the working, and said he was not going to do the thing he wanted this morning.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county a hundred and ninety-eight days. A hundred and ninety-eight is seven hundred and fifty-two less five hundred and fifty-four. Yesterday he said he wanted somebody on that bank asked whether they were in that room and said he was not going to say the word again this fortnight.

“*A hundred and ninety-eight is seven hundred and fifty-two less five hundred and fifty-four, and I have been in this county a hundred and ninety-eight days, and nobody has asked me for anything in one of them.*”

“*Here is the want and I am giving it before the shape again, because I told you yesterday that a woman of twenty-eight told me the order of those two things is the whole of what I am.*”

“*I want the woman who said that word to be asked what word it was, and I am not going to be the one who asks her.*”

“*Because I said yesterday that I am not going to be a man nobody can be sent for about talking about a room she was not in.*”

“*She was in this one and I was in this one and about four of you heard it.*”

“*And here is what I am not going to do this morning. I am not going to go down that bank about a hundred yards along the edge of that drain and stand over her and ask her to say it again so that a man of thirty-eight can check it against a piece of paper four hundred miles off.*”

“*That is the third time in about two weeks that I have wanted to go somewhere and worked out the distance to it in my own head before I told about nine people.*”

“*And a man of forty-one said at a hearing I was not at that the distance is not the thing.*”

“*And here is the mistake and it is mine and I worked it out before I opened my mouth this morning, and I am saying it first because I have got into a way of saying it after and a woman of twenty-eight has not told me to stop doing that and has said why.*”

“*Yesterday I said I want somebody asked whether they were in that room. That was a want about four hundred miles off and I have had it for one morning.*”

“*This morning the room is about a hundred yards from this bank and a woman in it said one word and it was no, and I have not been asked anything about it either, and about nine of you are looking at a man of thirty-eight and not at me.*”

“*I have been in this county a hundred and ninety-eight days and nobody has asked me for anything in one of them.*”

“*And this morning about nine of you have asked a woman of twenty-nine a question by not asking her anything, and that is a different instrument and I know it and I did it anyway.*”

“*Nobody is going to be relieved about that. Nobody is going to be thanked for it either, and nobody is going to be told I was right about the shape yesterday.*”

“*None of this is an answer to anything that was asked on a Wednesday fifty-two days ago. Fifty-two is seven hundred and fifty-two less seven hundred, and nobody is going to answer it for me and I am not going to answer it.*”

Then a woman of forty-four said what has not happened this week, gave the count first, and refused the last of it.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs at thirteen shillings and fourpence a year for nineteen years. She is not a keeper of anything anybody can be sent for about, and neither is the book. She brought the four leaves down four miles yesterday and nobody asked her to.

“*Four things have not happened this week and I am going to give them as four because that is how it is done on this bank.*”

“*One: nobody has filled in any of the three columns in nine mornings, and that is nine mornings and not a settlement.*”

“*Two: the ninth room has not been kept since the Friday of the hundred and twenty-sixth week. A man of thirty-one refusing a piece of paper did not keep it and did not unkeep it.*”

“*Three: the right of refusing is unrestored and it is given to nobody. A shut door is not it, a sealed door is not that, a man who cannot read is not it, and a person who cannot be asked has not refused anything.*”

“*Four: a woman of twenty-nine has said one word out of a reed and nobody has repeated it back to her, and about nine of you heard it and about four of you have worked out what it was.*”

“*I am not going to say whether the four of you are right and I am not going to say what the word was, because I did not hear it from where I was standing and I am not going to improve on that.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the four and nobody is going to be told I was brave about walking four miles with a thing I was not asked to carry.*”

About nine of them stood on that bank in the wind with a woman of twenty-nine about a hundred yards away in a reed who had said one word and had not been asked anything else. Nobody repeated it.

The fever was sixty-seven weeks old. He had been in that county a hundred and ninety-eight days.

A man of thirty-eight read four leaves out loud in the open because about nine people had said nothing at all, and read the fifth line of the thing on it, which says that nine people heard about the ninth line and did not object, and there are no names on any of the four leaves and no room on any of them.

And a man of sixty-one said he was not going to write nine on the leaf after the last head, because nine is not a thing he saw.

And a woman of twenty-nine said one word from a reed about a hundred yards away, and it was the first anybody in that county had heard from her in about eighty days, and nobody repeated it back to her.
