# Chapter 760: Sixth Day Of The Hundred And Thirtieth Week, And A Sunday, And The Bank Is Still Full At About Sixteen Inches Over The Top Step On The Sixth Morning Of It, And About Nine People Stand On That Bank While A Woman Of Thirty-Four Walks Down It To The Water And Is Not Going To Be Asked Anything On Her Way, And A Man Of Thirty-One Says He Is Not Going To Say What He Is, And Gives The Sixty Days He Has Given Every Morning Since The Fifty-First

Sixth day of the hundred and thirtieth week. His five hundred and tenth morning. Three hundred and sixty days after the settlement. The fever sixty-eight weeks and one day. Three hundred and fourteen days since the division. Two hundred and sixty days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was still full at about the seventh hour, about sixteen inches over the top step, and it was the sixth morning of it. The man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working, and gave the figure for the next time about ninety steps can be walked, and said it was the last time this week.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He said he had nothing to say about a night five times and then he stopped saying it. He gives three things every morning and there is not going to be a fourth thing out of them. A man of thirty-eight was there and about nine of us were.

“*About sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour, and that is the sixth morning of it running, and a bank that is full stays at sixteen.*”

“*Six days. It was full on the Friday of this week and four inches a day is the working.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in about four days after that bank is full, and that is the Tuesday of next week.*”

“*I have said that six times this week. This is the last of them and I will say it again on Monday.*”

“*I am not going to give you a fourth thing about a night and I am not going to be thanked for the six.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open, and it was the thirty-ninth morning, and a man of thirty-eight read it because he was asked.

“*Thirty-nine mornings and I am going to be here Monday and it will be forty and I am not going to say anything about the forty on Monday.*”

“*The fifth thing is still not answered and nobody has asked me and I am still waiting. I am going to stop putting a figure on how long I have been waiting, because about four of you have started counting it and that is not what I asked for and I have not said so until this morning.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the thirty-nine and nobody is going to be told I was brave about asking for the same thing thirty-nine times.*”

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and is the only person on that bank who can put a light on a thing in the dark. He has read that sheet forty times in this county and forty-nine mornings of that count had been counted by yesterday morning.

“*Fifty is seven hundred and sixty less seven hundred and ten, and there has not been one of the fifty in the dark.*”

“*And it is a Sunday, and on a Sunday of a fortnight somebody asked a fifth thing of a man of fifty-four, and it has not been answered and nobody has asked him about it since.*”

“*I am not going to say that out loud on this bank today because he has not asked me to.*”

“*A woman of thirty-four is going to walk down that bank this morning and about nine of us are going to watch her do it, and I am not going to read anything out loud while she does it.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the fifty and nobody is going to be told I was brave about not reading anything.*”

Then about nine of them stood on that bank while a woman of thirty-four walked down it to the water, and nobody asked her anything on her way, and she said so from about four feet into it.

Brenna Dole is thirty-four and has walked about two days from a place four hundred miles off this landing. She was one of the nine heard about and six were in that room. She said on Thursday morning that she wanted the four leaves to stop being on that counter and was not going to move them herself.

“*Nine of you watched me walk about two hundred yards and nobody asked me one thing.*”

“*I am going to say that out loud in about four feet of water, because about four of you have been waiting since Friday for somebody to say something about the walking.*”

“*And here is what the walking was. I came two days to say one sentence about a room and I said it on Sunday. Everything since then has been about nine of you, and I have been standing at the end of that bank for six days being looked at by about nine people and asked by none of them.*”

“*I am not going to say that is anybody’s fault.*”

“*About four of you have been asked things this week by a man of thirty-one and about nine of you have not, and that is the same shape and I am not going to make it into a finding.*”

“*I am going to say one thing that is about this county, and then I am going to go down that water until it is time.*”

“*I am not going to be here on Monday and nobody is going to walk down that bank with me.*”

“*A man of thirty-one said on Thursday morning that he wanted the four leaves off that counter and did not move them. A man of sixty-one said on Wednesday that he wanted that hearing to stand for the sake of a road four miles up that cart road. A woman of sixty-four said on Monday that she was in that room and was told she could not attend.*”

“*Those three men and that woman have all said what they want about this county in about six days.*”

“*I have said what I want about a room and about two hundred yards of water. About four of you worked out by Friday that those are not the same size and about nine of you have not.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for six days and nobody is going to be told I came two days for nothing.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said what he is not going to say, and gave the working for sixty days, and said none of it is an answer.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and six days, two hundred and six being seven hundred and sixty less five hundred and fifty-four. He gave up being nobody’s for one morning on the Friday of the hundred and twenty-eighth week by saying a word out loud in about nine people’s hearing.

On Saturday morning he said he wanted to be able to say what he is and could not.

“*Two hundred and six is seven hundred and sixty less five hundred and fifty-four, and I have been in this county two hundred and six days, and nobody has asked me for anything in one of them.*”

“*Yesterday I said I want to be able to say what I am out loud on this bank, and I have not been able to since the Friday of the hundred and twenty-eighth week.*”

“*About nine of you came up this bank on Friday to find out what I am.*”

“*I am not going to say it this morning. I am going to say what I am not going to say, and I am going to say why.*”

The why is that I have been on this bank for two hundred and six days being the thing about four of you come up to.

And there is a woman standing in about four feet of water at the bottom of this bank who came two days and has been looked at for six and asked nothing.

“*And if I say a thing about what I am this morning then about nine of you will have it and she will not.*”

“*And that is not modesty and I am not going to let about four of you call it that. It is that being asked what you are is not a request. I said that on Saturday morning and about nine of you heard it and about four of you wrote it down.*”

“*I am not going to pretend to be the sort of man a question makes humble.*”

“*Here is the mistake and it is mine and it is the last one I am going to say out loud this week. On Friday morning I said I was not going to decide which of about four of you I was going to answer first. I gave the reason that picking is the instrument.*”

“*About four of you believed the reason and about nine of you did not.*”

“*The reason was also that I did not know how to say what I am. That is the whole of it and I put a sentence in front of it that about four of you could carry, and about nine of you could not, and I did that on a bank on a Friday morning and called it a principle.*”

“*Not one of them is going to be relieved about that and not one of them is going to be thanked, and nobody is going to be told I was right on Friday about picking.*”

“*Sixty days. Sixty is seven hundred and sixty less seven hundred. That is what I have given every morning since the fifty-first, which is ten mornings, and I have said on every one of the ten that none of it is an answer.*”

“*And none of the ten is an answer today either. Nobody is going to answer it for me and I am not going to answer it.*”

“*There is a woman in about four feet of water at the bottom of this bank who was not there on the Wednesday that a thing that is not a person asked me. About four of us know that and about nine of us do not.*”

Then a woman of forty-four said what is on that counter and is not on that counter, gave the count first, and refused the last of it.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs at thirteen shillings and fourpence a year for nineteen years. She brought four leaves down four miles on the Friday of last week and nobody asked her to.

“*Two things are on that counter and I am going to give them as two and nobody is going to add them.*”

“*One: a sheet of printed paper with three columns on it, on that counter sixteen mornings, with nothing in any of the three columns in it and no column on it for a person stopping.*”

“*Two: four leaves of a hearing, on that counter ten mornings, with a number on them and no room on them and no names on them.*”

“*And about nine days on them that run out whenever they run out, and nobody in this county is sent for when they do.*”

“*And here is what is not on that counter and I am going to refuse to give you the third because about four of you have been waiting for it since Thursday.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the two things and nobody is going to be told I know there is a third.*”

Then about nine of them stood on that bank in the wind on the last morning of a week with a full water and two things on a counter.

A woman was standing in about four feet of water at the bottom of it, and nobody said anything for about a minute.

---

The fever was sixty-eight weeks and one day old. He had been in that county two hundred and six days.

A woman of thirty-four who had walked two days stood in about four feet of water at the bottom of that bank and said out loud that nine people watched her walk down and asked her nothing.

She said that three people had said what they want about this county in about six days, while she has said what she wants about a room and about two hundred yards of water.

And a man of thirty-one said he is not going to say what he is, and gave the reason in his own mouth, which is that being asked what you are is not a request.

He said he put a sentence in front of not knowing how to answer it and called it a principle on a Friday morning.

And the four leaves of that hearing were still on that counter on the Sunday morning, with nothing in any column of any of them, and a woman was standing in about four feet of water at the bottom of that bank.
