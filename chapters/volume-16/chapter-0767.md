# Chapter 767: Sixth Day Of The Hundred And Thirty-First Week, And A Sunday, And A Woman Of Thirty-Four Who Walked Two Days Comes Up About Two Hundred Yards Of That Bank Again, And Says She Did Not Know The Days Were Being Counted, And Nobody Thanks Her For It

Sixth day of the hundred and thirty-first week. His five hundred and seventeenth morning. Three hundred and sixty-seven days after the settlement. The fever sixty-nine weeks and one day. Three hundred and twenty-one days since the division. Two hundred and sixty-seven days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was still full at about the seventh hour, about sixteen inches over the top step, and it was the fifth morning of it. The man of forty-three gave the reading and the working and said the figure for the next time about ninety steps can be walked, and he did not say the day of the week on the word.

Corvin Tace is forty-three, has kept that landing nineteen years, and gives three things a morning out of three. He gave a figure once for why the cycles run eight days apart and took it back on the Monday of a fortnight ago, and he has not given another since.

“*About sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour, and that is a full bank on the fifth morning of it.*”

“*A bank that is full holds at sixteen until it comes off. Four inches a day is the working under it and I am not going to vary the wording of it this morning.*”

“*The next time those ninety steps can be walked is in three days from this morning, and I said that on Friday and I am saying it again and both of us were right.*”

“*Three things and then I stop. I am not going to add a fourth because a woman was on that bank on Friday and was not on it yesterday and is on it today.*”

Then a man of fifty-four who cannot read had the ninth line read to him in the open. It was the forty-sixth morning. He asked out loud first, and nobody thanked him for asking out loud, because nobody has thanked him for asking at all.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and cannot read, and he holds the gravel on that landing. He has asked out loud for that line before anybody speaks since the Tuesday of this week, and about four of you have got used to it and about nine of you have not, and he has noticed which is which.

“*Forty-six mornings.*”

“*Forty-six is one more than forty-five.*”

“*I have been asked nothing by anybody on this bank for about six weeks and I am going to keep saying that every morning until somebody asks me something, and that is not a want, it is a notice.*”

“*The fifth thing is not answered.*”

Then about two hundred yards of that bank was walked, by one person, from the water at the bottom of it, on a Sunday morning, with the water full and a bank that holds.

Nobody walked down with her. Nobody had been asked to and nobody offered.

Brenna Dole is thirty-four and has walked about two days from a place four hundred miles off that landing. She is one of the nine who were heard about. Six were in that room and three of those came in at the end and did not sit down. Nobody asked her anything on that day and she said one thing, about a road, and it is not on those four leaves.

She gave a figure of nothing to a man of about fifty-two about a mark on Saturday of last week and refused it in the same breath. She walked down about two hundred yards of that bank into the water on Saturday of last week and said she was not going to be there on Monday, and she was not.

“*About two hundred yards and I did it in one go this time and I am not going to tell anybody what that cost because I do not know and I am not going to guess at it in front of about nine of you.*”

“*I have been down there six mornings. I did not go home and I did not go off up that water and I did not ask anybody for anything, and about four of you have worked out that I was there the whole time and about nine of you have not.*”

“*Here is the one thing and I am going to say it and then I am going to stop talking this morning.*”

“*I did not know the days were being counted.*”

“*Nobody told me there were about nine days on that paper. Nobody told me there was a figure of days at all, and I came two days to say one sentence about a room, and I said it on the Sunday of a fortnight ago and nobody asked me a thing for six days after that.*”

“*I am not going to say that anybody in this county should have told me. I do not know who would have, and a woman of forty-four has said on that bank that nobody in four counties can be sent for about any part of it, and I have believed her for about six days.*”

“*And nobody is going to be thanked for me walking up that bank and nobody is going to be told I came two days for nothing.*”

Then a woman of forty-four asked her one question, and it was not about the room, and she said the reason out loud before she asked it.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs at thirteen shillings and fourpence a year for nineteen years. She has not asked anybody a question about that counter in six mornings and she said why on Thursday.

“*I am going to ask you one question and I am going to say why I have not asked it for six mornings.*”

“*The reason is that about four of them were waiting for me to ask you something about that room, and I did not want to be a second person doing that about a thing you already said on that bank.*”

“*And the question is not about the room. The question is this.*”

“*When you set off to come two days, did you know there was a figure of about nine days on that paper?*”

“*Not what you said in the room. Not whether you were one of the nine. Whether you knew about the nine days.*”

Then a man of thirty-eight said the count of that sheet with the working and said he would not open this coat today for a second reason, and gave the second reason.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and a woman of thirty-four came up about two hundred yards of that bank this morning while he was standing on it. He has read that sheet forty times in this county and has not read it since the Friday of the hundred and twenty-ninth week.

“*Fifty-seven is seven hundred and sixty-seven less seven hundred and ten.*”

“*On Wednesday I said a thing only about four of you are waiting for is a thing about four people and not about nine. That was about this coat and this board.*”

“*A woman of thirty-four has just come up about two hundred yards of that bank on a Sunday morning with a full water and a bank that holds, and she has told about nine of you that she did not know the days were being counted.*”

“*I am not opening this coat today, and the reason is not about four people any more. It is that there is a woman standing on this bank who has just said a thing about herself that about nine of you will write down, and I am not going to be doing something else at the same time.*”

Then a woman of forty said what she is not going to do with what has just happened, and she put it before anybody could say it was an answer to what she asked for on Thursday.

Wenna Callow is forty and asks for a day against a name, and she has never once asked for a reason instead. She has still not said which three of five things have a day against them. On Thursday she said out loud that she wants a day with nothing against it, and this morning she said she is not going to take what a woman of thirty-four said on this bank as that day.

“*A woman of thirty-four has just come up about two hundred yards of that bank and told about nine of you that she did not know the days were being counted.*”

“*On Thursday I said I wanted a day with nothing against it, and about four of you have been waiting since for somebody to hand me one.*”

“*I am not going to take what she just said as that day. She did not know there were about nine days and that is a thing that was done to her, and a thing that was done to her is not a thing I asked for.*”

“*I am not going to withdraw the want either. It is still what I want and it is still the only want I have said in eleven years and I have said it out loud in front of about nine of you.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the two of them standing on this bank saying two different things.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said that he had been waiting for her, out loud, which was the first thing he said, and he said afterwards that he had not planned to say it first.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s. He has been in this county two hundred and thirteen days, two hundred and thirteen being seven hundred and sixty-seven less five hundred and fifty-four. He has asked twice in about a fortnight that nobody say whether the cut across his right palm will keep.

“*Here is the want and I am going to say it before the mistake, and the mistake is that I said the want out loud before I had decided to say it.*”

“*I wanted to be on this bank when she came up it. That is the want and it is about me and it is about her and I do not want it to be about her.*”

“*Here is the mistake. I said that out loud, first, on a Sunday morning, to about nine people who have been looking at a woman walk up and down that bank since Saturday of last week.*”

“*A woman of forty said on Saturday that being asked what you are is not a request. This is a different thing and it is the same shape, and about four of you heard both of them this morning.*”

“*And here is the cost. I have been on this bank two hundred and thirteen days being the thing about four people come up to, and the woman who walked two days was never one of the about four, and I did not notice that until Friday morning of this week.*”

“*I have been the thing about four people come up to for about nine weeks and the one who came two days and was asked nothing has been outside that figure the whole time, and nobody on this bank told me and I did not ask.*”

“*Sixty-seven days. Sixty-seven is seven hundred and sixty-seven less seven hundred, and none of it is an answer and this morning it is not an answer to her either.*”

About nine of them stood on that bank while a woman of thirty-four stood at the top of about two hundred yards of it and said she did not know the days were being counted, and not one of the about nine said a word about that.

---

The fever was sixty-nine weeks and one day old. He had been in that county two hundred and thirteen days.

A woman of thirty-four walked up about two hundred yards of that bank by herself on a Sunday morning and said she did not know the days were being counted, and said she was not going to say anybody here should have told her, because she does not know who would have.

And a woman of forty-four asked her one question, which was not about the room, and said out loud why she had not asked one for six mornings.

And a man of thirty-eight said he would not open a coat with a board in it today because there is a woman standing on that bank who has just said a thing about herself.

And a man of thirty-one said he had wanted to be there when she came up, and said it out loud first, before he had decided to, and said that the woman who walked two days was never one of the about four, and that he did not notice until Friday.
