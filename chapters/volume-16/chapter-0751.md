# Chapter 751: Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-Ninth Week, And A Friday, And The Bank Is Still Full At About Sixteen Inches Over The Top Step On The Fifth Morning Of It, And A Man Of Thirty-Eight Does Not Read A Sheet Out Loud For The First Time In About Nine Days, And Says Why In Five Sentences, And A Piece Of Paper With A Hearing Written On It Is On That Counter And Nobody In Four Counties Can Be Sent For About Who Wrote It

Fourth day of the hundred and twenty-ninth week. His five hundred and first morning. Three hundred and fifty-one days after the settlement. The fever sixty-six weeks and six days. Three hundred and five days since the division. Two hundred and fifty-one days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

The bank was still full at about the seventh hour, about sixteen inches over the top step, and it was the fifth morning of it. The man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working and gave the figure for the next time about ninety steps can be walked, and nobody had asked him for any of the three.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He has said he had nothing to say about a night five times and then stopped. He is not going to give a fourth thing out of the three he gives every morning.

“*About sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour, and it is the fifth morning of it, and a bank that is full holds at sixteen.*”

“*Five days. It was full on the Monday of this week and I am giving you that working again because it is what I give.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in three days from this morning, and that is the Monday, and I said that on Thursday of last week.*”

“*I am not going to be thanked for it and I am not going to give you a fourth thing.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open before anything else happened, and it was the thirtieth morning, and he said it flat and short, and nobody had asked him for anything at all this morning.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He has said he had nothing to say about a night five times and then stopped. He is not going to give a fourth thing out of the three he gives every morning. About nine of them were on that bank this morning.

“*Thirty mornings.*”

“*Nobody on this bank has asked me a question since about nine days ago and I have not asked for one either.*”

“*It is the fourth day of a week and I am going to go on Saturday and on Monday and I am not going to say that on Saturday.*”

“*I am still not answering the fifth thing and nobody has asked me and I am still waiting.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the thirty and nobody is going to be told I was brave about any part of it.*”

Then the man of thirty-eight did not read the sheet out loud, and about four of them noticed and about nine of them did not, and he said why in five sentences before anybody asked him, which is what he does.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and is the only person on that bank who can put a light on a thing in the dark. He has read that sheet forty times in this county. He has been refused a figure for how long three times, asked for a fourth, and will not ask a fifth. He stopped deciding at the Sunday of the hundred and twenty-seventh week.

“*Forty-one is seven hundred and fifty-one less seven hundred and ten, and there has not been one of the forty-one in the dark.*”

“*And I have read that sheet forty times and I am not going to read it the forty-first time until somebody on this bank asks me to in front of about nine people, and nobody has, and I am not going to ask.*”

“*A woman of twenty-eight told me on the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-seventh week that a second time is a rule.*”

“*And I have been reading that sheet every morning since about nine days ago and I have not worked out what I was doing.*”

“*A man of fifty-four has asked for the ninth line thirty mornings running and I have read it thirty mornings running.*”

“*And that is not the same thing as somebody asking me to read it and me agreeing to do it.*”

“*I have not got to the bottom of the difference and I am not going to get there on a Friday.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the forty-one and nobody is going to be told I was brave about stopping.*”

About four of them had a man of thirty-eight who had stopped reading and about nine of them had the same sheet on that counter with nothing in any of the three columns in it, on the ninth morning of it.

Then a woman of forty-four who keeps a book of chairs came up that bank with a thing in her hand, and she was not asked to bring it, and she said so before she put it down.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs at thirteen shillings and fourpence a year for nineteen years. There are nine of those chairs and not ten, and she is not a keeper of anything anybody can be sent for about, and neither is the book.

“*I have not been asked to bring anything anywhere by anybody in this county and I am saying that first because about four of you are about to think I have.*”

“*It came down that cart road this morning on the bread and it was not addressed to me and it is not addressed to anybody, because there is nobody on it.*”

“*It is four leaves of the same thing and it has writing on it and the writing is a hearing.*”

“*It is not a form. I have seen about four forms in nineteen years and I know what a form looks like, and this is not one, and there is no column on it and nothing to fill in.*”

“*I am a woman of forty-four who keeps a book of chairs and I cannot tell you what a hearing is and I am not going to pretend I can.*”

“*And nobody is going to be thanked for the four miles and nobody is going to be told I was brave about carrying a thing I was not asked to carry.*”

Then about nine people looked at four leaves of paper on that counter, and a woman of forty said what she was standing in front of before anybody had said anything else about it.

Wenna Callow is forty and asks for a day against a name and has never once asked for a reason instead. She has still not said which three of five things have a day against them, and she said on a Monday of a fortnight that she would not and has not started.

“*Say what you are standing in front of.*”

“*Four leaves of paper on a counter, and nobody in this county can be sent for about who wrote them.*”

“*About nine of us can be sent for about the paper on this counter and not one word of it has changed in nine mornings.*”

“*A woman of twenty-eight said on the Wednesday of a week about two weeks ago that a second time is a rule. A man of thirty-eight has read the same sheet forty times and stopped this morning.*”

“*And about four of you have already worked out that these are two different instruments and about nine of you have not, and I am not going to add the two together.*”

“*I am still not going to say which three of the five have a day against them and nobody is going to be thanked for the four sentences.*”

Then a man of about fifty-two who cannot see well read four lines of it out loud, badly, and gave the count and said he was not going to give the fifth line back.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well, and is the fifth line of one of two sheets of deal that have been eleven years in a place he works. He has asked in the open what a mark is worth as a figure four times in a fortnight and has been answered by nobody, and the figure is owed to him and he is not going to stop asking for it.

“*Four lines and I am reading them wrong and I am going to read them wrong twice so that you have got them.*”

“*It says where the hearing was, and it says the day of it, and it says how many were heard about, and it says the thing they heard about.*”

“*Nine, and it says nine, and I counted the word twice and about four of you counted it with me.*”

“*It does not say the room. That is not me being bad at it. There is no room on it. There is a place and a day and a number of people and a thing and there is no room on it anywhere and I have looked.*”

“*And I am not going to read the fifth line out loud this morning because I have said four times in a fortnight what a mark is worth as a figure and nobody has answered me.*”

“*And I am not going to be the fifth line of a piece of paper in front of about nine people.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the four lines and nobody is going to be told I was right about there being no room on it.*”

Then a woman of twenty-eight said the true of all four of those in four sentences and the fourth one is the reason and it is hers.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from, and she has been in this county a hundred and nine days, a hundred and nine being seven hundred and fifty-one less six hundred and forty-two. She refused a man of thirty-one in nine sentences on the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-seventh week and said a second time is a rule. She has said that sentence about nine times since and has not begun again, and the count of the times she has said it and not said it carries no ordinal and is not restarted.

“*Four sentences and the fourth of them is mine and it is about what is on that counter and not about how it got there.*”

“*One: a form asks a person. I have not seen anybody this morning ask anybody anything and I have not seen one of those leaves ask anybody anything either.*”

“*Two: a form has a place where a person puts no, and a woman of forty-four just told you there is no column on this and there is nothing to fill in.*”

“*Both of those mean the same thing and neither of them is the same as what is on that counter.*”

“*Three: a finding is a figure of an argument. It is not a figure of anybody. Nobody in four counties can be sent for about what nine people said in a room they were not in.*”

“*Four is the reason and it is why I said it in four and not in nine. I said that sentence on the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-seventh week and I have said it about nine times since, and every one of those times was about something on this bank.*”

“*This is the first morning in about two weeks that it is about a piece of paper that has come from four hundred miles off, and I am not going to treat that as the same instrument because it is on the same counter.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the four sentences and nobody is going to be told I have not begun again.*”

About nine of them stood on that bank in the wind with four leaves of paper on that counter and about four of them had a woman of twenty-eight who had said a finding is a figure of an argument and not a figure of anybody.

Then a man of thirty-one said what he wanted, and what it cost, and what he had got wrong, and he gave the working for his own figure first.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county a hundred and ninety-seven days. A hundred and ninety-seven is seven hundred and fifty-one less five hundred and fifty-four. On Friday of last week he said the word amendment out loud in about nine people’s hearing and said he was not going to fill in any of the three columns on that paper, and on Thursday he said he was not going to answer a woman of sixty-four’s question and gave three sentences for it.

“*A hundred and ninety-seven is seven hundred and fifty-one less five hundred and fifty-four, and I have been in this county a hundred and ninety-seven days, and nobody has asked me for anything in one of them.*”

“*Here is the want and I am giving it before the shape, because I gave it after it last week and a woman of twenty-eight told me on the Wednesday of a fortnight that the order of those two things is the whole of what I am.*”

“*I want somebody on this bank to be asked whether they were in that room. Not me. I am in no room four hundred miles off and I cannot be sent for about one.*”

“*And if I say a thing about that finding it is a man nobody can be sent for about talking about a room he was not in, and that is the same instrument as the last thing I did on this counter.*”

“*And I am not going to say the word again this fortnight. I said it in about nine people’s hearing last week and about four of them can now say a man of thirty-one said it, and I am nobody’s again this morning and I have not decided that.*”

“*Here is the mistake and it is mine and I worked it out about four seconds ago and I am saying it before anybody finds it.*”

“*A man of thirty-eight has read one sheet forty times and stopped this morning.*”

“*And I have been saying the same four sentences on this bank every morning for about two weeks, and neither of those two things is anybody asking me to do it and me agreeing to do it.*”

“*A woman of twenty-eight said four sentences about a finding about nine minutes ago and one of them was that it is not the same instrument, and I have been treating this counter as one counter for about two weeks and it is not one counter.*”

“*And here is the cost. I have wanted five things in five days and got nothing out of any of them, and this morning I have found out that I have been standing in one place about two weeks and calling it work.*”

“*Not one of them is going to be relieved about that and not one of them is going to be thanked, and nobody is going to be told I was right on Friday of last week.*”

“*None of this is an answer to anything that was asked on a Wednesday fifty-one days ago. Fifty-one is seven hundred and fifty-one less seven hundred, and nobody is going to answer it for me and I am not going to answer it.*”

Then a man of sixty-one gave the count he gives, in the order he gives it, and did not add any of it to anything, and refused a fifth reason.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one, and has kept a book of heads and carts in this county for eleven years, and has written one number in it in eleven years. There are five lines on the leaf after the last head and a number is the last of the five. He stopped a count of days himself on the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-fifth week and has refused the figure of it out loud ever since.

“*A man of thirty-four has been in an ash on the low side of that landing for a hundred and twenty-one days. A hundred and twenty-one is seven hundred and fifty-one less six hundred and thirty.*”

“*A woman of twenty-nine has been in a reed for a hundred and twenty-two days, and a hundred and twenty-two is seven hundred and fifty-one less six hundred and twenty-nine, and I have not added them and I am not going to.*”

“*A man of thirty-one has been in this county a hundred and ninety-seven days and a woman of twenty-eight a hundred and nine, and I have not added those two to the other two either and I am not going to.*”

“*And there is a thing on that counter with nine in it and no room on it, and I have not added that to anything.*”

“*A man of sixty-one has not got a fifth reason for not adding it and is not going to be asked for one on this bank.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the four counts.*”

About nine of them stood on that bank in the wind. About four of them had four leaves of paper on that counter that nobody in this county could be sent for about. Nobody read the fifth line.

The fever was sixty-six weeks and six days old. He had been in that county a hundred and ninety-seven days.

A man of thirty-eight read a sheet of printed paper forty times in this county and did not read it the forty-first time, and said in five sentences that he will read it again when somebody on that bank asks him to and he is not going to ask.

A woman of forty-four who keeps a book of chairs brought down four miles four leaves of paper that were on the bread and were addressed to nobody, and said there is no column on it and nothing to fill in, and that she cannot tell anybody what a hearing is.

And a woman of twenty-eight said in four sentences that a form asks a person and a finding is a figure of an argument and not a figure of anybody, and that nobody in four counties can be sent for about what nine people said in a room they were not in.

And a man of thirty-one said he wants somebody on that bank asked whether they were in that room, and said he is not going to say the word again this fortnight, and said he has been standing in one place about two weeks and calling it work.
