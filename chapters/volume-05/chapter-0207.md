# Chapter 207: The Four Pages

The first day of the fifty-first week was a working day in Sallowby like every other working day, and the water-seat’s one room had nine people in it, which was four more than had ever been in it, and none of the four had been sent for and one of the four had walked up from Hollow Sallow with a barrow and left the barrow in the lane.

Hollis Nance had the table clear by the second light with a sheet on it and a pen and a pot, and he had ground the ink himself, which nobody does, and he said one sentence about it before anybody had said anything at all.

“I am writing this in my own hand and it is mine and not the Seat’s, and if it is wrong it is my hand on it and not a clerk’s, and I would like that said now in front of nine people and not afterwards.”

Nobody argued with it and nobody thanked him for it, and the four who had been in that room before the first shaking were three of the same men who had been in it then, and one of them was the man who had cut the marks, and his name is in eleven doors in this valley and in no instrument, and he is seventy-four and he has a boy of nineteen at the Anner who mends a winch.

“Ilya Kever,” said Garrow Lee, from the door. “The man with the string.”

“He is seventy-four and he has not been down to the water since the winter before the first one,” said Jory Vellan. “He came because somebody told him there was going to be a number and he did not want to hear it from a boy. He has a boy of nineteen at the Anner who mends a winch and that is the whole of what any of us know about him and it is not why he is here.”

“He came because he heard there was a number,” said Hollis Nance. “Nobody sent for him. He is here and he is the only man in this room who has done the thing that made the number necessary, and I would like him to be given the whole of it and not the half of it, and I have said that and I am going to write the order and I am not going to look up again.”

---

The trouble began at about four lines in and it did not stop for three hours, and every part of it was ordinary, and every part of it was somebody in that room wanting a different four pages.

The Anner’s four pages were about the road. Two roads, one over the causeway and one along the high road, and the high road adds a morning and a half each way and about nine carts a day for about a hundred and forty days, and the Anner would carry the whole of it and would rather not.

Hollow Sallow’s four pages were about the eleven houses, and the only sentence anybody could produce about the eleven houses in three hours was a sentence about a height, and the height everybody had was eleven.

The Seat’s four pages were about a date, and the date was the date in the standing order, and the standing order is three years old and was made by a man who has not been down to the water since the winter before the first shaking, and he did not come today either and nobody sent for him.

“You have all got a different four pages,” said Ilyan Vester, “and about six of the eight lines between you are the same line, and I am going to say the six out loud and then I am going to say the thing that is not in any of them, and then I am going to stop talking, because I am not a party to this and I cannot be and there is no form for making one, and if I go on I will end up being the man who wrote it and I have been the man who wrote a thing once.”

“You have not,” said Sera Quill. “You have been in a room where a thing was written four times and you were not asked for any of the four.”

“That is the first true thing anybody has said about me since we came nine days,” he said, “and I am not going to improve on it.”

---

The order was written at the third light on the second of the four pages and it came to four lines, and it is on a wall in Sallowby today, and it is four lines and all four of them are finished.

> AN ORDER OF THE WATER-SEAT OF SALLOWBY, THE ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-FIFTH DAY SINCE THE SECOND SHAKING, IN THE MATTER OF THE DAM ON THE ANNER WATER AND OF THE ELEVEN HOUSES OF HOLLOW SALLOW.
> ONE. THAT THE ANNER HOLD THE DAM ON THE ANNER WATER TO ELEVEN FEET ABOVE ITS OWN SILL UNTIL THE SLOW IS DOWN, AND KEEP THE SAME, AND THE ANNER IS THE PARTY BEARING THIS ORDER AND IS ANSWERABLE FOR THE HEIGHT.
> TWO. THAT THE CAUSEWAY UNDER THE SLOW BE CLOSED WHILE THE SLOW IS DOWN, AND THAT THE ANNER BEAR THE COST OF THE HIGH ROAD IN EVERY WEEK OF THE CLOSURE.
> THREE. THAT ELEVEN FEET BE THE HEIGHT, AND THAT THE ELEVEN HOUSES OF HOLLOW SALLOW BE BELOW IT, AND THAT NO PERSON OF HOLLOW SALLOW BE ASKED TO LEAVE ANY HOUSE BY THIS ORDER.
> FOUR. THAT THIS ORDER BE READ IN THE ROOM OF THE WATER-SEAT AND IN THE LANE AT HOLLOW SALLOW, AND THAT THE COPY IN THE LANE BE KEPT BY A PERSON OF THE LANE AND NOT BY THE SEAT.

Hollis Nance wrote it in a fair hand and it took about nine minutes and he read it out twice and the second reading did not change a word, and the difference between what it does and what anybody in that room wanted is in the four lines, and it is visible to anybody standing at that table who is not looking at the ink, because the third line is the shortest line on the page and it is the one that took the longest to agree.

“There is no hole in it,” said Ilyan Vester.

“No,” said Hollis Nance.

“There is no hole in it, and I want that said out loud in this room before we go any further, and I am going to say it now and nobody is going to be kind about it afterwards.” He had his hand flat on the table and he was not looking at the page. “There is a draft in another county with a hole in the third line about the size of a hand, and the hole is the most useful thing in that document, and every person in that room understood the hole and there was no argument about it at all. This is the other thing. This is four lines with nothing left out of them, and it is going to hold, and the reason it is going to hold is that it is complete.”

“Everything you have said is a reason why it is good,” said Garrow Lee.

“Yes,” said Ilyan Vester. “That is what I would like somebody in this room to notice.”

---

Nobody noticed it. That is the finding and it is the whole of Chapter 207 and it took three hours and nine people and there was nothing to notice.

They read the two copies out twice, at the second light and at the noon light, and the copies agreed, and the copy that went to Hollow Sallow was carried down by Ilma Treen because line four says a person of the lane keeps it and the only person of the lane in that room was the woman who had walked up with a barrow, and she put it in the barrow and took it down the hill.

And the Anner’s copy was taken by Jory Vellan, who read it once standing up, and said the two words that will do, and put the sheet down on the table, and went out, and was gone before anybody could ask him a question, and he was gone because he had stood in the doorway of a room in this town twenty-four hours ago and had not come in, and everybody at that table has spent the last day pretending that did not happen, and he is the only person in this basin who knows what the thing is going to cost and has never once said it out loud in a room that was writing it down.

And the seat’s press was locked and the keys are on a nail, and the four pages went in and the four pages came out, and a woman of about forty washed the pen and put it upside down on a board and turned it over again, and the first day of the fifty-first week was a working day.

Then the thing came up in its own two plain lines, in a room with nine people in it, in the flat white nothing that has put things in front of him in about a year and a half, and it did not say one word about the nine of them.

**THE ORDER OF THE WATER-SEAT OF SALLOWBY IS ENTERED AGAINST THE PARTY NAMED IN IT, AND ITS HEIGHT IS A HEIGHT ABOVE A SILL, AND NO SILL IS NAMED IN IT, AND A HEIGHT ABOVE NO NAMED SILL IS A HEIGHT ABOVE WHATEVER THE READER IS STANDING ON.**
**WHAT IS NOT DECIDED: WHETHER A DEED OF THIS BASIN IS ONE PAPER OR TWO, AND WHETHER A PERSON WHO HOLDS ONE HALF IS ANSWERABLE FOR THE WHOLE.**

He read it twice. It said nothing whatever about a village and nothing whatever about a dam and it did not take anything, and nothing came out of him in that room, and the matter it was about had been made an hour earlier by a clerk with a pen and it was on a wall in a market town in a basin that had never had an order on it before and never had had an order read in a lane.

And nobody in that room saw anything, and his leg was what his leg has been since a morning four hundred miles and some weeks behind him, and it had done nothing whatever in seven days, and it was going to go on doing nothing for as long as he was carrying the thing he was carrying, and that is not a rule and it is a fact about a body, and he has not told anybody in this valley one word of it and there is nobody here he is going to tell.

---

The day-count went on the page at the third light because it goes on the page at the third light, and the number is one hundred and fifty-five, and it is the fourth time in seven days that a number out of that water-book has been put at the top of a page in a valley four hundred miles from the sea, and three of the four were in his own hand and one of them was in a man of sixty-one’s mouth on a bank of clay, and neither of them knows the other’s number and they are both counting the same days.

That was the state of it at the third light on the first day of the fifty-first week.

The mine would not flood. The road over the causeway would be shut for about a hundred and forty days and the Anner would carry the high road and would rather not and would do it. The eleven houses would be below eleven feet. Nobody would be asked to leave a house, and that sentence was in the third line, and he had not asked for it and did not know it was in there until Hollis Nance read it out the second time, and it is the fourth time in about a year that somebody has tried to put a person in the middle of a sentence to protect somebody else, and the first three cost the person in the middle of it something, and this one will cost him the most, and he does not know that yet.

And the dam was going to hold, and the four men and the barrow were going to hold it, and they were going to hold it at eleven feet above a sill four miles up a river, which is a number, and a number is a height, and a height is a height above something.

Nothing in the four lines says what.

Hollis Nance put the key back on the nail it lives on and said, to nobody, that a clerk who files an order he is not allowed to make is still a clerk who has filed it, and that there was nobody in this basin he could hand that to, and that he had therefore put it in the press and shut it, and that the press is the only thing in this room that has ever been asked to remember anything.
