# Chapter 667: The Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Seventeenth Week, And A Friday, And On Which A Man Of Sixty-One Does A Sum He Refused To Do On The Thursday Of The Last Week And Does Not Refuse This Time, And Says Why, And The Reason Is Worse

Fourth day of the hundred and seventeenth week. His four hundred and seventeenth morning. Two hundred and sixty-seven days after the settlement. The fever fifty-four weeks and six days. Two hundred and twenty-one days since the division. One hundred and sixty-seven days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The man of forty-one said what it had cost him out loud at about the seventh hour on the Friday morning, before anybody asked him and before anybody had thought to. And about nine people on that bank heard it and about four of them had not known he had a Monday.

“*I have two carts and I had a load of stone going up that track on the Monday and it is not going up on the Monday. And it is going up on the Tuesday. And a stone that goes up on the Tuesday is a Monday that somebody else does not get.*”

“*I do not know whose Monday. That is the whole of what I have got and I have had it since Tuesday and I have not said it because there is no form in this county that says a man may be sent for about a Monday.*”

“*And I am not saying it to be thanked for it, and I am not saying it to be told it is a hard thing. And I am saying it because a woman of forty said no on the Thursday to nine people and eight of them went down instead of nine and the eighth went down on my back and that is the whole of where this has come from.*”

Nobody in that bank said a word back to him about the Monday.

Then the man of sixty-one did the sum, and he did it out loud with the working, and about four people on that bank understood the whole of it and about nine understood the total.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept the book of heads and carts eleven years, and there are four lines in that book on the leaf after the last head and he is not writing a fifth.

“*A hand mill is about nine hours of a day for the people who eat out of it. A day and a half of a stopped mill is about thirteen hours and a half, and a day of a stopped mill is about nine hours.*”

“*That mill has stopped four times that anybody has counted. Two of those were a day and a half and two of them were about a day. Thirteen and a half. And thirteen and a half, and nine, and nine, and that is forty-five hours.*”

“*And I am not going back to count the three descents before the Thursday of the hundred and sixteenth week. Because I do not have the figures for them and a figure I cannot get is a figure I do not print.*”

Then he did the division, and he did not refuse it, and he said the reason out loud before he gave the number, and the reason was worse than the last one and not better.

“*I refused this division on the Thursday of last week. I said a half of a loaf is not a loaf. And I said I was ashamed of the number. And I am not taking any of that back and I am not going to pretend I am a different man this morning.*”

“*And here is the reason I am doing it. On the Thursday a woman of forty said no in nine sentences about what happens in about four years. And about four of the nine people on that bank have been doing arithmetic in their heads every since. And a room with a true number in it that refuses to say the number does not get a made-up one, it gets a worse one, and I know what that is because I have been glad about numbers for about nine years.*”

“*So here is the number. Forty-five hours between about eleven people is about four hours each. Four hours of eleven of you is forty-four. Forty-five is an hour over that.*”

“*And an hour over is the same kind of thing as a quarter of an hour under and I am not going to stand here and tell you it is a different kind of thing because it is the nicer one.*”

About four people on that bank had the hour over. The rest of them had a man of sixty-one giving out a figure he had been ashamed of nine days earlier.

Then he said what the figure is a figure of, and he said the whole of it, and about nine people on that bank heard the sentence he has never said in eleven years of a book.

“*Forty-five hours is a figure of a mill.*”

“*It is not a figure of a woman of thirty-eight, because she came and she decided to come. And I have watched her do it four times and I am not going to put her four descents against her as a thing that was done to her.*”

“*It is not a figure of her mother, because her mother is one of the eleven and her mother has not been asked for anything and does not know that this sum was done and cannot be sent for about it and is not going to know.*”

“*And a figure of about eleven people in four houses is a figure of nobody. There is no line in this book that will take forty-five hours and set it against anybody’s name. And I have been keeping this book eleven years and that is the first number I have ever had that I could not put anywhere, and I have been glad about numbers for about nine years and I am not glad about this one.*”

Nobody in that bank said a word about that.

Then a boy of thirteen did a sum of his own, out loud, in front of about nine people. And nobody had asked him to and nobody stopped him, and it is the first number he has ever given anybody.

The boy is thirteen and he is not a head in the book of heads and carts because the penny a head is on everybody over sixteen. And he has been four miles up that track and four miles back with a barrow on four mornings running.

“*Four miles is about two hours with a barrow, and two hours is about eight hours in four days, and eight hours is a working day.*”

“*And a working day of a boy of thirteen is a figure of nobody and I have known that since the Saturday and I have not said it out loud before because I have never had a number for it. And now I have got one and I do not know what to do with it. And that is the same as the man of thirty-one and I am not going to pretend I am better than him.*”

About four people on that bank had the eight hours. The rest of them had a boy of thirteen who had gone to a number instead of to a person.

Then the man of thirty-one said what he wanted, and it was the wrong thing to want, and he said it out loud and in front of everybody, and a man of sixty-one told him what it was.

Ilyan Vester was thirty-one and he had been in that county one hundred and thirteen days. And one hundred and thirteen is six hundred and sixty-seven less five hundred and fifty-four. And he was nobody’s and there is no column in this county with his name at the top of it.

“*I want you to refuse the division. I want a true number to not exist in this bank. And I want it to be out loud that it does not exist, and then nobody in this county can be given it against anybody.*”

“*And I am saying that I want that because I have watched a man say a number in a room of about nine people for about nine days and every one of those nine has been carrying it. And one of them is in an ash and one of them is in a reed and neither of them knows it, and that is what I have been frightened of since about the ninth week.*”

“*And a man of sixty-one is going to tell me that I am asking for a thing that protects a number and not a person. And he is right, and I would like about four of you to have heard me ask for it before he says so.*”

“*It is not about the coalition. If it were about the coalition then nine people would owe a day and nobody would owe a name. And I am the one person in this bank who cannot be given a day at all. And I am not going to be the reason a man of sixty-one makes a true number sound better.*”

About four people on that bank had the whole of it. Nobody in that bank improved on it.

Then the man of sixty-one said the number of a day that has been going since the Monday of the hundred and eleventh week. And then he said the other thing, which was that a number of that kind is the last number he is going to add to.

“*It is the thirty-ninth day. Thirty-nine is six hundred and sixty-seven less six hundred and twenty-eight.*”

“*And on Monday it will not be in the thirties, and I am not going to stand on this bank on Monday and give you a new figure. Because a count of days is only a count of days while a person is in the place. And the place for that count is a room, and there is no room, and I am going to say the number as it stands and then I am going to stop.*”

Then about nine people on that bank stood about in the wind for about as long as it takes to pole a quarter mile. And nobody said that the refusal of a woman of forty had cost a Monday and a day and a half and a day and a half and a day and forty-five hours and a working day of a boy of thirteen, and about four of them could have said it and about nine of them did not.

---

The fever was fifty-four weeks and six days old. He had been in that county one hundred and thirteen days.

A man of sixty-one did a sum he had refused to do nine days earlier and did the division and gave the figure. And the reason he gave it is worse than the last one and not better. And the figure that came out of it is a figure of nobody and he said so: forty-five hours of a mill is not a figure of the woman of thirty-eight, who came and decided to come, and it is not a figure of her mother, who was not asked and does not know.

And about eleven people in four houses eat out of that mill and one of the eleven is that mother. And the refusing of a woman of forty on the Thursday is on a man of forty-one’s Monday and on a boy of thirteen’s four mornings and on forty-five hours of a mill.

And one of the eleven whom forty-five hours is a figure of is a woman who was never asked, and who does not know, and who cannot be sent for about it. And a man of sixty-one said out loud that this is what a figure of nobody is, and a boy of thirteen said the same about eight hours and a working day, and nobody in that bank said a word to either of them about it.
