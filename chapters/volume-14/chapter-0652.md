# Chapter 652: The Third Day Of The Hundred And Fifteenth Week, And A Thursday, And The Sixth Reading Of Two Copies Agrees, And On Which A Man Of Sixty-Six Who Is A Sinker Says Out Loud What Opening A Board Is

Third day of the hundred and fifteenth week. His four hundred and second morning. Two hundred and fifty-two days after the settlement. The fever fifty-two weeks and five days. Two hundred and six days since the division. One hundred and fifty-two days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The sixth reading of the two copies was done on the Thursday morning and the two agreed, and the count is of Thursdays and the fifth was the Thursday of last week.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and he is the only person who may carry a board about nine inches long with thirty-two notches cut in its edge. He went up the water in the Gull at about the fifth hour with a sheet of deal in a coat and came down and got in at about the second hour of the afternoon.

“*Thirty-two lines on both of them. The thirty-second is the thirty-second on both. The thirty-first is the thirty-first on both.*”

“*And it took about nine hours of a boat and a pole and about nine minutes of reading, and nobody has ever once asked me to do it, and nobody is going to.*”

Nobody in that yard thanked him for nine hours.

Then a man of sixty-six came down the bank in the middle of the afternoon, and about nine people saw him do it, and about four of them had not known he was within a day of that landing.

He had a pick about four feet long over his shoulder and a lamp in his other hand that was not lit, and he put the lamp down before he said anything, which is a thing a man does when he has decided he is not going to need it in the first hour.

Nobody on that bank thanked him for the walk down.

Then the man of thirty-one said what he wanted, and he said it out loud, and it was the first want of the day.

“*I am going to ask to go first down those steps, and I am saying it before anybody offers it and not after, because about four of you in this town have watched me wait to be asked and get it wrong.*”

About four people on that bank had that at once.

“*And I am not going to say why, because the why is that I have been nobody’s in four counties for ninety-eight days in a way I can check, and I have not been down anything in this county, and I would like one thing in this county that I know the bottom of.*”

About nine people on that bank had the ninety-eight. About four had the bottom.

Then the man of sixty-eight said no, and he said it in about four sentences, and the reason was about the man and not about the steps, and he did the sum on the rule out loud, and he said why he was doing it again.

Pell Varn is sixty-eight and keeps the last quarter mile, and gave that town its rule on the Thursday of the hundred and tenth week, which is thirty-five days ago, and thirty-five is six hundred and fifty-two less six hundred and seventeen.

“*No.*”

“*The first person down a wet flight of about ninety steps decides when the other eight turn round, and a person nobody can be sent for about deciding that is how nine people get hurt in a place with no form in it at all.*”

“*And I am not going to let a man I like decide it. That is four sentences and the last one is the reason and the reason is about you.*”

About four seconds went past. The wind came off the water and the lamp that was not lit rolled about an inch on the gravel.

“*And I said the same thing to you on the Thursday of the eleventh week about a board of notches, and it was twenty-six days then, and I did that sum out loud, and I am doing it out loud again because it is a different day and a rule that is thirty-four days old on a Wednesday is thirty-five days old on a Thursday.*”

“*You are not a step. You are a man who is in no book in four counties and I am not going to be the reason there is a form in this county that says a man may be sent for about a man standing in the dark.*”

Nobody on that bank said a word to him about it.

Then the man of thirty-one said nothing for about the length of a hundred paces, and then he said the true of one half of it, and there was no day-count in it, and it was the seventeenth time in that room.

“*A man of sixty-eight has just told me the true of a rule I would have got wrong if I had not been here, and I have held it, and I would like about four of you to notice that I held it in about four seconds instead of about four days.*”

“*And I am not going first. I am going fifth, and I would like it said that I asked for first and was told no in four sentences and that the no was right.*”

About four people on that bank had the fifth. The rest of them had the four sentences.

Then the man of sixty-six spoke, and he said what opening that board is, and he gave it as a figure of work, and he said what it is not a figure of.

“*It is not a lock. There is no key and there is no lock and there is not a keeper of it anywhere in four counties, and I am the one who is standing in front of you saying so.*”

“*It is a board about four feet by two feet on a stick, and the stick is about three feet, and the board is up so the steps drain.*”

“*It takes two people to get it up off its stick. It takes two people to get it back on. Not one and not three. I have watched two men try it and it is worse than it sounds and better than it sounds, and both of those are true, and one of them is a joke and I am not going to say which.*”

About four people on that bank had the two. About nine had the board.

“*It takes two because the board is about four feet by two feet and a man of sixty-six cannot get one end of it up off a stick without the other end coming down, and a man of thirty-eight can do it alone and a man of forty-one can do it alone, and that is the whole of the physics of it.*”

“*It is not a figure of people. It is a figure of two men on a bank on one day, and there is no form in this county that says a person may be paid for it, and I have never been paid for it, and I have not asked to be.*”

Then he said how long it has been and who the two have been, and he said the two names except one.

“*The two have been me and a woman who keeps a mill four miles off that landing. Her name is Aveline Marsh and she is thirty-eight. She has been in it about nine years and so have I.*”

“*We have been the two since about nine years ago and we are still the two. Nobody else has done it, and there is no form that says only we may, and there is no form that says anybody else may not, and that is a different thing and I am not going to let the room have it as the same thing.*”

About four people on that bank counted the two. The rest of them had the mill.

“*And it has not been done in about three years, and the reason is not a rule and I would like that said in a room with the door open.*”

“*The reason is that I am sixty-six and she is four miles off with a mill, and about eleven people in four houses eat out of that mill, and one of the eleven is her mother, and there is no third person that the two of us will agree on, and we did not go looking for one, and that is the whole of the three years.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked him for the three years.

Then the man of forty-three was asked about the steps, and he gave a figure and gave the working, and about nine people heard the figure and about four heard what it was a figure of.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept the landing nine miles up that water nineteen years, and he said the name of the man of sixty-six out loud, and it was the second name of the morning in that town and the first one anybody had ever said in a room there.

“*Ninety steps. I have counted them and I counted them again this week because a man of sixty-six asked me to.*”

“*The top thirty-one of them are cut into rock. The next forty-four are cut into gravel and clay and the clay holds about two years before it wants doing and it gets done about twice, and there is no form that says a man may be paid for it.*”

“*The bottom nine are always under. About four feet, and I have never seen them dry, and I have been going up that reach nineteen years and that is a figure of nineteen years of one man looking and not a figure of a depth.*”

About four people on that bank had the ninety split into three. About nine had the nineteen years.

Then the man of thirty-one asked the question he came to the bank to ask, and it was the wrong one, and the man of sixty-six answered it in about four sentences.

“*How deep does it go.*”

“*I do not know.*”

About four seconds went past and nobody filled them.

“*And I have been asked that by about four people in about nine years and I have given the same four sentences every time and I am going to give them now.*”

“*I have been down twice. The second time was forty-one years ago and I was twenty-five. The first time was before that and I was not in this county and I do not remember it.*”

“*The bottom of the steps is a floor and the floor is about nine feet back from the last step and there is a passage in it going along, and how far the passage goes I do not know, because I have been in it about nine feet.*”

“*I am sixty-six and I am a sinker and I dig holes for water and I know what a hole is, and I am telling you in about nine people’s hearing that I do not know what is under that bank, and I will not be the man who tells you, and if you write it down in a book I will not have said it.*”

About four people on that bank had the forty-one years. About nine had the pick over his shoulder.

Then the woman of forty-one with two carts was spoken of, and a woman of twenty-eight four hundred miles off, and a man of thirty-four in an ash, and none of the three came down that bank.

“*A woman of forty-one keeps two carts and came up that cart road with a load on the Monday and there is one wheel on the near side making a noise it was not making on the way down.*”

“*And a woman of twenty-eight is in this county and cannot be sent for about in it, and she was asked about the river yesterday and she said a river is not a hearing, and she has said out loud that she is not going down those steps, and nobody has asked her to.*”

“*And a man of thirty-four is in an ash on the low side of that landing and has not stood up in nine days, and he has never been asked anything by anybody in this bank, and about four of us have known that all along and about nine have not, and I am not going to make him into a reason for going down.*”

About four people on that bank had the ash. The rest of them had the pick.

Then the board was walked to, and the man of forty-one and the man of thirty-eight lifted it, and the man of sixty-six stood on the top step and did not go down, and a man of thirty-one stood at the fifth place with a lamp that was not lit.

Nobody on that bank thanked anybody for the two men.

---

The fever was fifty-two weeks and five days old. He had been in that county ninety-eight days, and he had not been in Hask since the Wednesday, and nobody on that bank said the number.

And a board about four feet by two feet was up on a stick at the head of about ninety steps, and it takes two people to get it up and two people to get it back, and the two have been the same two for about nine years, and one of them is sixty-six and the other keeps a mill four miles off that about eleven people in four houses eat out of.

And nobody has been down those steps in about three years, and the reason is not a rule, and a man of thirty-one asked to go first and was told no in four sentences by a man of sixty-eight who is not in a chair and cannot be corrected in one.

And a man of thirty-one said he would go fifth, and a man of sixty-six said he has been down twice and the second time was forty-one years ago, and that he does not know what is under that bank and will not be the man who tells you.
