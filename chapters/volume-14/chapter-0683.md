# Chapter 683: Sixth Day Of The Hundred And Nineteenth Week, And A Sunday, And On Which Nobody Goes Down Because A Bank Is At About Four Inches Over The Top Step, And The Cost Of The First Asking Arrives On The Ordinary Week Of A Woman Who Keeps A Bakehouse And She Says Out Loud That She Has Been Told Twice And Asked Once And Has Had About Nineteen Years Of Neither

Sixth day of the hundred and nineteenth week. His four hundred and thirty-third morning. Two hundred and eighty-three days after the settlement. The fever fifty-seven weeks and one day. Two hundred and thirty-seven days since the division. One hundred and eighty-three days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was about four inches over the top step at the seventh hour and the man of forty-three gave the reading and the convention with it and nothing else. And about nine people on that bank did not go down and said so themselves.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years and has stopped saying the pair of figures and has not said one since the Thursday of this week.

“*About four inches over the top step at the seventh hour. One day since it came off those steps, and I count from one morning to the next and not from a morning to itself.*”

“*And nobody is going down and I am not going to give you a reason for the pair of figures, because I have stopped saying the pair of figures and I am not going to start again on a Sunday to be able to explain a Sunday.*”

About four people on that bank had the four inches and about nine of them had nothing to do until the light went.

Then a boy of thirteen said a figure out loud, and he was talking about a man of thirty-one who is nobody’s and who stands on that bank about twice a week, because he says one every morning now and has done for about eleven days. And this one was a count of times.

The boy is thirteen and he is not a head in the book of heads and carts because the penny a head is on everybody over sixteen. And he walks four miles up that track with a barrow and four miles is about two hours.

“*Thirty days since I went down. Thirty is six hundred and eighty-three less six hundred and fifty-three, and that is a count of days and not a count of times. And I am going to say the other one out loud before anybody asks me for it.*”

“*I have said a figure out loud on about nine mornings since the Tuesday of the hundred and eighteenth week. That is a count of times and not a clock, and the reason I am saying both of them is that Ilyan Vester, who is nobody’s, has been telling everybody for about a fortnight that a count has to say what it is a count of.*”

About four people on that bank had the nine and the rest of them had the thirty.

Then she came down that bank at about the seventh hour with nothing on her cart. And nobody sent for her, and she had walked four miles down a road she has walked for nineteen years.

Hester Ollerton is forty-four and keeps a bakehouse four miles up that cart road. And about nine houses in that village take her bread, and one of the nine is a man of eighty-one who does not eat on a Thursday unless it is there in the morning.

“*It cost me a morning. That is the whole of it and I am going to give you the working because a man of thirty-one has been standing on a bank telling us for a week that a figure with nothing behind it does not go in a book.*”

“*He asked me at about the eighth hour on the Saturday and it was about a quarter of an hour, and my cart was standing at the head of that track with a load in it, and his two carts were on the road in front of it, and a cart cannot pass a cart on that road at the eighth hour because there is a wall on the north side of it for about forty yards.*”

“*So the forty loaves went up at about the tenth hour instead of about the seventh. An hour and a half up and an hour and a quarter down, and that is two hours and three quarters of a cart for one morning of bread, and it is the same figure it has been since the Friday of the hundred and eighteenth week and I have not improved it.*”

“*About nine houses got it a day late. About four of the nine cannot do a day without it. And the man of eighty-one has a Thursday in four days and he is one of the four. And I have known him since I was twenty-two and he is not going to tell anybody he did not get it and neither am I.*”

The nine houses went round that bank, and so did the four of them that cannot do a day without it and the man of eighty-one.

Then she said the part that was about herself and not about the bread. And she had said half of it on the Saturday and she said the whole of it now.

“*And a man of thirty-one did not ask me on the Friday and I would have said yes to being told, and I have said so out loud, and he asked me about a door instead, and that is the whole of the difference and I do not need him to explain it to me.*”

“*And that is not the cost I am going to talk about. The bread is a day late and it has been a day late before and I know what a day late is, I have been a day late my whole working life.*”

“*The cost is that you asked me. On the Friday of the hundred and eighteenth week I stood on this bank and told about nine people that nobody in this county has the second thing and that I have wanted it for about nineteen years. And the second thing is being asked.*”

“*And I said saying yes to being told is not the same as being asked, and I was right about that on the Friday, and on the Saturday a man came up my road and asked me about a door nine miles down a river instead of coming to my door and saying here is what it costs and here is what we do about it.*”

Nobody on that bank said a word, and nobody said the word sorry, and about four of them looked at the ground.

Then the man of forty-one said what he was going to do about it. And it was very short, and it was not an apology and it was not a defence either.

The man is forty-one and keeps two carts and asked a woman of forty-four one question on a cart road four miles from her own door yesterday morning. And he has been in and out of the ninth of nine doors twice a day for nineteen years.

“*I am not going to be sorry about it on this bank. I said on the Friday of the hundred and eighteenth week that if a person wants me to be sorry they can come and find me on a Wednesday. And it is a Sunday, and that is not the reason and I am not going to pretend it is.*”

“*And I am not going to stand here and say I did not know, because I did know. I have known since the Friday of the hundred and eighteenth week that she is the one person in four counties who wants to be asked. And I went up that road in the morning and asked her about a door, because a door is what I was going.*”

“*And there is no form in this county that says a man may not do that. And there is no form that says he may, and I would like both of those sentences left where they are.*”

Then a woman of forty-four said the count, and she gave it flat. And she said what each half of it was a count of, and nobody improved on it.

“*Being told: twice. Once on the Friday of the hundred and eighteenth week when a man of thirty-one told about nine people what my bread being a day late cost me. And once on the Saturday morning when a man of forty-one came up my road and asked me about a door.*”

“*Being asked: once. And it is now, and it is this, and it is a question about a door nine miles under a river and not about me.*”

“*And there have been about nineteen years of the other one. Nineteen years of nobody coming to that door and saying anything at all. And I am not going to stand on this bank and tell anybody which of those three is the one I would have given back.*”

Nobody on that bank said a word back to her, and about four people on that bank did not look at her and let her get down the bank on her own.

Then a man of about fifty-two who cannot see well said the whole of what he thought about it. And he was standing about nine feet off, and he said his reason first and he said it out loud before anybody had asked him for it.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well, and the fifth line of one of those sheets of deal is his and not the man of forty-three’s. And he has been that person for eleven years without knowing it was a thing to be.

“*I cannot see very well and I have not been down that flight and I am not going to. I said on the Sunday of the hundred and sixteenth week that a thing that is sent for is a thing people are sent for about, and I said on the Sunday of the hundred and eighteenth week that I have not been down that flight and am not going to. And I have not changed my mind since, and I am not going to change it on a Sunday about bread.*”

“*And here is what I have to say about it. I am saying it stood about nine feet off because I have stood about nine feet off for about nine days. And I have never once pretended to me that I was anywhere else.*”

“*A woman of forty-four is going to have her week made worse by a man going up her road and asking her a question instead of coming to her door and telling her. That is a fact about a person. It is not a figure. And there is no form in this county that says a person may be sent for about a woman’s bread being a day late.*”

“*And I know what being sent for about is. I have been the fifth line of two sheets of deal for eleven years and nobody has ever once asked me what my line was for, and there is nobody else on this bank who can tell you what that costs.*”

“*I am not going to be the one who goes to her door. There is no form that says I may and there is no form that says I may not. And that is not a thing anybody on this bank has an answer to this morning, and I am not going to be asked for one.*”

Nobody on that bank said a word to him and about four of them had the eleven years and about nine of them had the road.

Then a man of sixty-one said one thing about what happens next. And it was the thing he says every week, and this week it had a number in it for the first time.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept the book of heads and carts eleven years, and there are four lines in it on the leaf after the last head and there are not five, and he stopped counting the local hearing on the Friday of the hundred and seventeenth week.

“*Nobody in this county is going to be told about that by me. A woman of forty-four has had a number said out loud about her three times in nine days and I am not going to be the hand that puts it in a book, and a book with her in it is a column and a column is a thing people get put in inside about four years.*”

“*And I have watched that be right three times now and I am not going to be shown a better reason on a Sunday.*”

Then about nine people stood about on a bank with four inches of water over the top step, and the sun went, and the boy of thirteen went up that road with an empty barrow at about the fourth hour because it is what he does, and nobody asked him to and nobody told him not to.

---

The fever was fifty-seven weeks and one day old. He had been in that county one hundred and twenty-nine days.

About nine houses four miles up a cart road are a day late because a man of forty-one asked one question at about the eighth hour and her cart could not get past his on a road with a wall on the north side of it for about forty yards. About four of the nine cannot do a day without it and one of those four has a Thursday in four days.

And a woman of forty-four said out loud that she has been told twice in nine days and asked once. And that the once is a question about a door nine miles under a river and not about her, and that there have been about nineteen years of neither.
