# Chapter 660: The Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Sixteenth Week, And A Friday, And On Which The Water Is On The First Three Steps Of About Ninety, And A Year Has Turned With No Figure For It, And A Question Is Asked Once And Not Answered

Fourth day of the hundred and sixteenth week. His four hundred and tenth morning. Two hundred and sixty days after the settlement. The fever fifty-three weeks and six days. Two hundred and fourteen days since the division. One hundred and sixty days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was on the first three steps by about the fourth hour of the Friday morning, and the board was on its stick above the ninth.

About nine people were on that bank at about the eighth hour. About four of them had been down those steps three times in nine days. About nine had not.

Nobody on that bank thanked anybody for any of it.

A man of forty-three said what he could be checked on and what he could not, and he said it with the working, because a man of forty-three who keeps a landing is a man who is watched.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept the landing nine miles up that water nineteen years, and the block of limestone with a line cut round it is in that reach, and the water went over it on the Wednesday of last week at about the sixth hour.

“*About a foot and three inches at the sixth hour on the Wednesday. About two feet and nine inches at the eighth hour this morning. That is the working, and the working is the only thing I have.*”

“*I have been watching that stone for nineteen years and I have got two crossings in my own hand out of nineteen.*”

“*A man of sixty-one gave one of them a day in the hundred and fifteenth week, and a man of thirty-one gave the other one a count of days.*”

“*I have never had a figure for the gap and I am not going to invent one on a Friday morning with about nine people standing about.*”

“*I have been asked about it three times in about nine days by three different people. The answer is the same three words and they are that I do not know.*”

About four people on that bank had the two crossings. The rest of them had the water.

Then a man of sixty-one said what a year is, out loud, in about nine people’s hearing. He said it as a figure with its unit, and the unit is a stone.

“*A year in this county is the time between one crossing of that block and the next one after it. It is not a count of weeks and it is not a count of days and there is no figure for it in four counties.*”

“*It went over on the Wednesday of the hundred and fifteenth week. It is on three steps this morning.*”

“*A man of thirty-one has been in this county one hundred and six days, and one hundred and six is six hundred and sixty less five hundred and fifty-four. Neither of those numbers is a year.*”

About four people on that bank had the two clocks kept apart. About nine had a stone.

Then the things that had not moved were said out loud, one under another. A chapter that ends has to say what is still open or it looks like it has been settled.

“*A sheet of deal with a seal on it is lying face down on a barrel nine miles down this water. It was read out loud twice on the Tuesday of last week and not a third time.*”

“*Nobody has taken it inside, nobody has burned it, and nobody has put a mark on it. The stone is back on the counter where it came from.*”

“*A hearing about every record there is to be read in one room was asked for in that yard on the Monday of the hundred and eleventh week. It is the thirty-second day. Thirty-two is six hundred and sixty less six hundred and twenty-eight, and the subtraction is spoken, and nobody granted it and nobody refused it and nobody has written it down.*”

“*A single Crown of about nine people at a barrel was abolished on the Monday of the hundred and fourteenth week by about nine people in the open. It binds nobody.*”

“*What is left is a board about four feet by a foot and four inches leaning against a barrel with nine names under it and no Crown over it, a bench against the gunwale of a boat, and a barrel that is a barrel.*”

“*Nine words are in the heads of about nine people on a bank. A man of sixty-six has them in a place nobody can reach, and he is the only person in four counties anybody can be sent for about the word that means them.*”

Nobody on that bank said a word about any of the four.

Then a man of thirty-four was at the top of that bank in the doorway of an ash all day, and about nine people on that bank went past that doorway about nine times, and nobody stopped at it and nobody said a word at it, and about four of them knew whose mouth it was.

He has been in that ash thirty days. He is on no board and in no book and nobody can be sent for about him, and the last thing anybody said to him was a question he asked out loud on the Tuesday of this week and a man of forty-three answered in four words.

About nine people on that bank did not know that either. About four did.

Then the man of thirty-one said what he wanted, and he said it out loud, and it was the want he said in that bank on the Wednesday of last week with the number in it, and it was the same want and it had nothing in it now, and about four people in that bank noticed that and about nine did not.

Ilyan Vester was thirty-one. He has been in that county one hundred and six days. He is nobody’s. There is no column in the county of Kell with his name at the top of it, no form that renews him, no rate on him, and no hundred in that county either.

“*Last Wednesday I said out loud that I wanted a figure, and a man of sixty-one told me in front of about nine people that there is not one and is not going to be one.*”

“*And I am saying the same want again on a Friday, four days later, and it is the same want, and the difference is that on Wednesday I had a thing to be angry at and on Friday I have not got that either, and that is the whole of what has happened to me in four days.*”

Nobody on that bank said a word to him about that.

Then he said the question, and he said it once. Nobody in that bank had asked it before, and it was the only question of the week that had not been asked in that bank, and about four people heard it and about four understood all of it.

Nobody on that bank moved.

“*If a thing down there is held up by a person doing a job, and the bottom nine steps are held up by two people about once a year, and a chamber is held up by two hands once a day — then who is holding the part in the gallery up.*”

“*There is a part of a roof in that gallery going along two inches and coming back with nobody in the room, and I have had about nine seconds to hear myself say it and I said the part about the roof second instead of first, and I would like about four of you to notice that I moved it.*”

About four seconds went past and nobody filled them. The water went on coming past that landing. About nine people on that bank heard a question and about four of them understood all of it.

“*And I am not going to answer that. I would like it said that I have not answered it and that I am not going to.*”

“*I have been in four counties for one hundred and six days and I have got a beam, a groove and about nine words.*”

Then a woman of twenty-eight was standing about nine feet off with a bag in her hand. She did not look at him. About four people on that bank noticed that she did not look at him and about nine did not.

She said one thing, and it was about herself and not about the question.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and came up that cart road on the Monday of the hundred and thirteenth week and has been in that county eighteen days and cannot be sent for about in it or in the county she came from.

“*I asked a question on the Tuesday of the hundred and fifteenth week in a yard nine miles down this water and nobody answered it. A man asked a different one just now and nobody answered that either.*”

“*I am not going to help him with mine by being near his.*”

“*That is two sentences and about four of you have the whole of them and about nine of you have a woman standing about nine feet off.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked her for standing nine feet off.

Then the ninth person said one word, and it was the last word said on that bank that day, and it came from the top of the landing from a man who has been in an ash for thirty days and has not stood up once since.

Ivo Kellow is thirty-four. He has been in that ash since the second day of the hundred and twelfth week, and he has not stood up, and he is on no board and in no book, and nobody can be sent for about him.

“*Next.*”

About four seconds went past and nobody filled them. About nine people on that bank had a word and about four of them had the whole of it. Nobody asked him what he meant, and he did not say it again.

---

The fever was fifty-three weeks and six days old. He had been in that county one hundred and six days.

The water is on the first three steps of about ninety and the board is on its stick. The next time those steps can be walked is the next time that block of limestone comes out, and nobody in four counties has a figure for that either.

And a year turned on the Wednesday of last week with no figure for it. A man of thirty-one counted one hundred and six days, and neither number is a year.

And a sheet of deal with a seal on it is still face down on a barrel. A hearing about every record there is is the thirty-second day and nobody granted it and nobody refused it. A Crown that binds nobody is a board leaning against a barrel.

And nine words are in the heads of about nine people, and a man of sixty-six is the only one anybody can be sent for about.

And a part of a roof under about ninety steps of bank is going along two inches and coming back with nobody in the room.

And a question was asked once on a Friday morning in about nine people’s hearing, and about four people understood all of it, and nobody answered it.

A man of thirty-one said he was not going to answer it and had not answered it. A woman of twenty-eight standing about nine feet off did not look at him. A man of thirty-four in an ash said one word, and nobody asked him what he meant.
