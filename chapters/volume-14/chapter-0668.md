# Chapter 668: The Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Seventeenth Week, And A Saturday, And On Which The Thing That Is Turning Is Put To About Nine People As A Question, And The Man Who Has Been Down Twice Says The Same Four Sentences He Has Said Since The Monday Of The Hundred And Fifteenth Week

Fifth day of the hundred and seventeenth week. His four hundred and eighteenth morning. Two hundred and sixty-eight days after the settlement. The fever fifty-five weeks. Two hundred and twenty-two days since the division. One hundred and sixty-eight days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was full on the Friday morning and it was full again on the Saturday morning. And the man of forty-three gave the figure and the working. And about nine people on that bank heard the figure and about four of them heard what it was a figure of.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and a bank that was empty on the Monday morning was full on the Friday morning. And that is four days both ways and it is the only figure he has got for it.

“*About a foot and four inches on the top step at about the seventh hour this morning. A foot is twelve inches, so that is sixteen inches over a step, and it was fifteen and a half on the Wednesday, and that is the working.*”

“*And on the Friday it was full and nobody went down, and that is a day gone, and I am not going to tell you it is the only one. Because a man of forty-one bailed on the Tuesday and again on the Wednesday and again on the Thursday and that is three days of a man of forty-one and about six hours of standing in water, and the Friday is a day that nobody bailed at all because the water was not there to be bailed.*”

About four people on that bank had the day gone. The rest of them had the fifteen inches.

Then the question was put, and it was put as a question and not as a want. And about nine people were on that bank and it was asked of all of them and not of anybody in particular.

The question was what turns it.

About four of the people standing on that bank said the word out loud within about nine seconds of each other. And they had said it on the Monday and on the Tuesday and in a gallery about a week ago, and nothing anybody has said in three days has changed it.

“*That is a machine and it is worked by the water under the steps and there is a hole somewhere and I have not found the hole.*”

“*It is worked by the water. It is the only thing down there that is worked by anything.*”

“*A man cut a hand in a lintel ninety steps back and there is a guild mark on that hand. And a guild marks what it makes, and I have heard the guild from a man who heard it from a man.*”

About four people on that bank were still sure it was a machine. About nine were not. And the nine were not sure of anything else. And one of the nine said out loud that not being sure of anything else is not the same as not being sure it is a machine.

Then the man who has been down twice was asked to say what turns it, and he gave the same four sentences. And about nine people in that bank had heard them before and about four of them had heard them twice.

Abel Fenner is sixty-six and digs for water and has been down those steps twice, the second time forty-one years ago when he was twenty-five. And he is the only person in four counties anybody can be sent for about a word he said in that gallery on the Monday of the hundred and fifteenth week.

“*I have a rate and a wear, and both of those are about how a thing is done and neither of those is about who does it.*”

“*I was twenty-five the second time and it was moving. I am sixty-six now and it is moving.*”

“*Those are the only two things I know about it and I have had forty-one years to add a third and I have not.*”

“*I said those on the Monday of the hundred and fifteenth week in a gallery and I am saying them on a bank, and I am saying them a second time because a question was asked of me and a man of sixty-six does not get better at answering a thing by being asked it in front of more people.*”

Nobody in that bank accepted the four sentences. Nobody in that bank rejected them. And a man of thirty-one said the whole of that out loud so that it would be in the record of the morning, whatever the morning is going to be.

“*I have watched about nine people hear the same four sentences for the second time and not one of them has said that is not enough and not one of them has said it is enough. And I do not know what a room does with a true thing it cannot check.*”

“*And I am not going to supply a fifth sentence. I have had five sentences in me since the Monday of the hundred and fifteenth week and I have not said one of them. And I am not going to say one of them in front of a room that has just heard a man of sixty-six say four for the second time.*”

Then the question of a name came up, and it came up out loud, and about nine people on that bank heard it. And it was asked by the man of thirty-one and it was the wrong question and he said so before he asked it.

“*Nine people who have agreed to do a thing have no name, and on the Sunday a man of sixty-one said that a coalition is a bigger Crown that binds nobody. And he was right. And a bigger Crown that binds nobody with no name on it is nine people who will each be asked separately in about four years.*”

“*And I am going to ask the room what to call it, and I am saying first that it is the wrong question and that I have asked it because I do not know a better one. And I would like about four of you to notice that a man who has been counting for a hundred and fourteen days has run out of questions that are about the work.*”

Then a woman of forty said a name, and she said it once. And she said it flat. And she said it was the name of something else entirely and that she was giving it to them so that nobody could say later that nobody had thought of it.

The woman of forty said no to a day for nine people on the Thursday of the hundred and seventeenth week in nine sentences, and she is on no board and there is no board and she has asked for a day against a name for about nine weeks.

“*The thing that keeps the record in four counties has a name. And a man of thirty-one said it under about ninety steps of bank on the Monday of the hundred and fifteenth week. And I have not said it out loud since and I have been carrying it about four days.*”

“*And I am saying it now because I am the one who said no. And a person who has said no to nine people is the person a room looks at when it wants a name. And I would rather hand them a name that is about somebody else than let them make one up.*”

“*The First Witness.*”

Then nothing came out of that bank for about four seconds, and the beam in the gallery was not in the room, and nothing whatever happened, and about nine people stood in the wind.

Nobody in that bank said that the two names were the same thing. A woman of twenty-eight standing about nine feet off said out loud that nobody in that bank had said it and that she had been listening for it since the Thursday.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and came up that cart road on the Monday of the hundred and thirteenth week. And she has been in that county twenty-six days. And twenty-six is six hundred and sixty-eight less six hundred and forty-two, and she cannot be sent for about in this county or in the county she came from.

“*I have been standing here listening for somebody to say that those two are the same thing and nobody has and I would like about four of you to know that I checked. And I am not going to be the one who says it either, and that is not because it is not true.*”

“*It is because I have been four hundred miles with a bag and two letters and I do not carry a thing that I have not worked out. And I have not worked this one out. And a woman of forty has just handed it to a bank of about nine people and that is her whole of it.*”

Nobody in that bank thanked the woman of forty for a name.

Then the man of thirty-one said the cost of it out loud, and it was not his. And he said so in the first sentence, and about nine people on that bank heard the first sentence and about four heard the second.

“*A woman of forty who has asked for a day against a name for about nine weeks has said a name in a room of about nine people. And a man of sixty-six said a word in a gallery on the Monday of the hundred and fifteenth week and worked out that a thing said in a room ends up in the nearest hand in this county, and I watched that happen to a man of fifty-four who cannot read.*”

“*And here is the part I am not going to be praised for. It has not gone to a man of fifty-four and it has not gone to me, and I am nobody’s. And it has gone to the one person in that bank who has spent nine weeks asking for a name to be used against her. And I would like about nine of you to notice that what has just happened is the most exact thing in this county in about nine days and it happened by accident and I am not going to call it a good thing.*”

Then a man of sixty-one said what a coalition is called this morning, and it is not that, and he said it in about five sentences and gave the reason.

“*It is called the nine. That is what it is called and it is not a name anybody can be sent for about. Because the nine changes every time somebody says no, and a woman of forty said no on the Thursday and it is eight this morning.*”

“*And I am not going to write it down. I said on the Sunday that I was not going to and a man of thirty-one is not going to be the reason a name gets put in a book in this county.*”

Then about four people on that bank said the word machine one more time, out loud, not to anybody. And about nine of them did not. And the man of sixty-one said the time out loud and it was about the eleventh hour and the water was on the top step and would be on the second by the second hour of the afternoon.

---

The fever was fifty-five weeks old. He had been in that county one hundred and fourteen days.

A question about what turns a beam about nine feet long was put to about nine people on a bank on a Saturday morning. And about four of them were still sure it was a machine and about nine of them were not sure of anything. And the man who has been down that gallery twice was asked and said the same four sentences he has said since the Monday of the hundred and fifteenth week.

Nobody in that bank accepted those four sentences and nobody in that bank rejected them, and a man of thirty-one said that he was not going to supply a fifth.

And a name was said out loud flat once on that bank by a woman of forty who has asked for a day against a name for about nine weeks. And nothing whatever happened. And nobody in that bank said the two names were the same thing, and the cost of it fell on the woman who said it.
