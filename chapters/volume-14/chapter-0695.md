# Chapter 695: Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-First Week, And A Friday, And On Which A Bank Is Full And Nobody Goes Down And Two Names Are Said Out Loud In Four Counties For The First Time, And A Chamber Is Found For One Of The Two Positions That Are Not Found, And A Place And A Distance And A Wall Are Given Out Loud And A Thumb’s Width Of Light Under A Door-Stone Is The Only New Fact About It

Fourth day of the hundred and twenty-first week. His four hundred and forty-fifth morning. Two hundred and ninety-five days after the settlement. The fever fifty-eight weeks and six days. Two hundred and forty-nine days since the division. One hundred and ninety-five days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was about sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour. The man of forty-three gave the reading and said the bank was full, and nobody asked him what a full bank is for.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept a landing nineteen years. A full bank is about sixteen inches over the top step, and he has said he has nothing to say about a night three times and has said it four times this morning.

“*About sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour. Four days since it came off those steps, and I count from one morning to the next and not from a morning to itself.*”

“*And it was about twelve inches at this hour yesterday, and that is the working.*”

“*A bank fills in about four days and empties in about three. Both of those are figures I gave this bank and neither of them is mine.*”

About four people on that bank had the four days and about nine of them had the sixteen inches.

Then a boy of thirteen said his own figure out loud, and it was a count of days and not a count of times, and nobody had asked him for it.

The boy is thirteen and is not a head in the book of heads and carts, because the penny a head is on everybody over sixteen. He carries a barrow four miles up that track.

“*Forty-two days since I went down. Forty-two is six hundred and ninety-five less six hundred and fifty-three.*”

“*And I counted it myself this morning and nobody asked me and I am not going to say anything else about it.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said two names out loud on that bank. It was the first time either of them had been said in four counties. He said where he had them, and he said he had not written them down.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s. He has been in a room four hundred miles off once in his working life, and he sat at the back of a chair and did not write down what he heard.

“*Red Basin. And Orison.*”

“*Those are the two of the nine that say not found. I have had them since a room with nine clerks in it in another county, four hundred miles off, where a clerk of thirty read nine lines off a board the size of a door in a flat voice, and six of the nine had a name or a body against them and a line saying what keeps it, and one said burned, and two said not found.*”

“*And one of the two had a place against it and the place was the city I was sitting in. And I did not write it down, and I have not written it down since, and I am not going to now.*”

About four people on that bank had the two names and about nine of them had never heard either of them.

Then a man of sixty-one said what he was going to do with the two of them. It was the same thing he had said about the six nines, and he said it flat, and it took him five sentences.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept a book of heads and carts eleven years. There are five lines on the leaf after the last head, and the last of the five is a number he wrote on the Saturday of the hundred and twentieth week.

“*I am not going to write either of those two names in this book. And I am not going to write the one a man of thirty-one has just said on a full bank in front of about nine people.*”

“*And I want it noticed that the first number I have written in this book in eleven years is a nine. The second thing anybody has handed me this week is a proper name off a board in another county.*”

“*Both of them are refused for the same reason, and the reason is the third one I gave on the Monday of the hundred and nineteenth week.*”

Then a man of forty-one gave the place, and it was in his own mouth and out of his own nineteen years, and he was not going down and nobody asked him to.

The man is forty-one and keeps two carts, has a wheel on the near side making a noise, and is the ninth of nine. He has been in and out of the ninth of nine doors twice a day for nineteen years.

“*The eighth. That is the place. It is the eighth opening along that wall from the ninth, and the ninth is the one I open, and I have known where the other eight are since I was about twenty-two and nobody ever asked me.*”

“*And I am not going down this morning to look at it. A bank is full, and that is one reason.*”

“*And the place is the eighth opening and the distance you have just had twice from two men who do not agree, and the wall is the face of the door-stone. The face of it is about as wide as a man lying down and it is cold where the hands go and warm where the feet go, and I have not put my own hand on the face of the eighth one in nineteen years, because I have not been in the eighth one in nineteen years.*”

“*The other reason is that if I go and look at it on a Tuesday when the water is off, then I will go and look at it, and then I will go in. And a man of thirty-eight told a bank nine days ago what his shoulder would do, and he was not thanked for it.*”

“*And I am not going to be the second man on that bank this week who is right and cannot say so.*”

Then a man of thirty-eight gave the distance, twice, in two of his own figures, and the two figures do not agree, and he said so before anybody found it out.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and is the only person who may carry a board about nine inches long with thirty-two notches cut in its edge.

“*About thirty-one paces of mine at about two feet a pace. Thirty-one times two is sixty-two feet.*”

“*And a man of forty-one will tell you the same place in his own unit and his unit is the nine feet of groove he pushes a stone along, and seven of those is sixty-three feet. Sixty-two and sixty-three, and the difference of one is a foot, and I am not going to take a foot off a groove to make two figures agree.*”

About four people on that bank had the sixty-two and about nine of them had the sixty-three, and a man of forty-one said he would rather have the two.

Then a man of thirty-eight said the other thing about it. He had been keeping it since the first day of the hundred and fifteenth week, and he said he had kept it for six weeks and had not been asked.

“*And I have got a second figure and it is a bad one and I have been sitting on it since the first day of the hundred and fifteenth week.*”

“*On the first day of the hundred and fifteenth week I put my lamp down on the floor of that passage about nine feet past the last of the beams, and I saw about a thumb’s width of light under the eighth door-stone.*”

“*And I have not said it. I have carried a lamp down about ninety steps four times in six weeks and I have seen that about a thumb’s width of light under that door-stone four times and I have said nothing about it, and a woman of twenty-eight said on the Tuesday of this week that a thing said out loud is a figure of about nine people.*”

Then he said why he had kept it, and it was not modesty, and he asked for it to be said that it was not modesty.

“*I kept it because I could not get a figure for what was making it. A chamber that is a day is lit by a hole in a ceiling and the stone under the hole. The eighth has two hands over it and the lintel says two people, so I was not going to be the one to say out loud that a man who cannot see well can find something a man who can see well walks past.*”

“*And it is not light. I do not know what it is. I have been putting off saying it for six weeks and I have run out of reasons and the reason I have run out of is that a man of thirty-one has been on that bank for six weeks telling us that a figure with no thing behind it does not go in a book, and I have got a thing behind it and I have not said it.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said what the finding is, and he got it wrong twice on the same morning, and both times a man who works put it back.

“*Then the eighth chamber is lit and there is a person in it and there is a keeping in it and I can go and ask for the record of the keeping—*”

“*You have put a person in it and you have not got one.*” The man of forty-one did not raise his voice. “*And a keeping in it is a job with a name on it and you have not got a name, and you have been told that four days’ running in about as many words.*”

Then a man of thirty-eight said what is actually in it, and he said it flat, and it took nine sentences, and the last two of the nine are what he was not going to say and why.

“*There is nothing in that room to find. I have thought about it for six weeks on about nine descents and there is nothing in it to find.*”

“*A map four hundred miles off used the word anchor for all nine of them and it came out in the middle of a sentence nobody asked the meaning of, and a man of seventy who has stood at a bridge in a market in another county about forty-four years has said an anchor is not a thing.*”

“*It is the plinth, and the day, and the two of them, and the nine times in ten he got it right.*”

“*So there is no stone in the eighth and there is no stone in the ninth. There is a groove nine feet long in each of them, a lintel with a line under a hand, a hole about as big as a hat, and a mark with a space in it about as wide as a thumb.*”

“*That is a function. That is what the eighth does. And I am not going to say what else could do it, and nobody on this bank is going to say it, and a woman of forty-nine gave me the word for a going that does not come back out of a shelf in her bakehouse and I gave her the credit for it in front of about nine people, and the same rule is the rule for this and I am not going to break it in a week where I have used it once.*”

About four people on that bank had the function and about nine of them had the plinth and the day and the two of them.

Then a woman of twenty-eight said one thing about being sent for, and she said it before a man of thirty-one had finished asking, and he had not been going to ask.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from. She is not one of the nine, and she has said the same sentence about a lamp and a record six times in a fortnight.

“*If a man of thirty-one is about to ask to be sent for about that room, I am going to say something about it first.*”

“*And I am not going to be a fourth thing for anybody this week.*”

“*Nobody can be sent for about that eighth opening. Not a man of thirty-one, and not a man of forty-one, and not me, and there is no form in this county that says a person may be sent for about a keeping that is four hundred years old, and there is no form that says a person may not.*”

“*And I have checked it a seventh time this morning and it has not moved. Nobody in this county has said out loud that a lamp on a hook in the ninth of nine rooms and the thing that keeps the record in four counties are the same thing.*”

“*And I am still not going to be the one who says it.*”

Then a man of fifty-four said the thing that is going to be true of all of it, and he cannot read, and he said it before anybody had thought of asking him.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel at the head of that drain, and has been the only person anybody on that bank can be sent for about three times this week.

“*I have been asked three times in nine days and I have said three times that I cannot be a keeper of it, and I am not going to be a keeper of a room either.*”

“*And I have been the only person anybody can be sent for about a wet place about as wide as one boot, and about a space in a wall, and now about a thumb’s width of light under a door-stone. That is three things in nine days and a man of fifty-four cannot read one word of any of them.*”

“*And there is no form in this county that says a man may be sent for about what he cannot read, and there is no form that says a man may not, and I said on the Saturday of the hundred and twentieth week that the first of those is a gap in the forms and not a right, and I am not going to be thanked for saying it a fourth time.*”

About four people on that bank had the three things and about nine of them had a full bank and no descent.

---

The fever was fifty-eight weeks and six days old. He had been in that county a hundred and forty-one days.

There is a chamber for one of the two positions that are not found. It is the eighth opening along a wall about a hundred and thirty feet past the last of about nine beams. It is about sixty-two feet by a man of thirty-eight’s paces and about sixty-three feet by a man of forty-one’s grooves, and the difference of one is a foot and neither figure was rounded.

And about a thumb’s width of light goes under its door-stone. A man of thirty-eight saw it on the first day of the hundred and fifteenth week and carried it about for six weeks and has still not said what it is.

Nobody went in and nobody is going to, because the way in is two people before the light, and there is no form in that county that says a person may be asked whether they will still be doing it.
