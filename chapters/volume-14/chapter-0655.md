# Chapter 655: The Sixth Day Of The Hundred And Fifteenth Week, And A Sunday, And On Which The Same Kind Of Thing Is Found At Two Sizes, And Nobody Down There Knows What Either Of Them Is For

Sixth day of the hundred and fifteenth week. His four hundred and fifth morning. Two hundred and fifty-five days after the settlement. The fever fifty-three weeks and one day. Two hundred and nine days since the division. One hundred and fifty-five days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The second chamber is twelve feet along and about nine feet across, and it is a week, and it took about nine people about four minutes to work out that as well, and the four minutes were the fifth hour of the morning.

There is a wheel in it about the height of a chest, and the wheel is of wood and about nine spokes, and there is a handle on the wheel about a foot long, and there is a board fixed to the wall with seven holes cut in it, and the holes are not the same size, and the seventh is cut deeper than the other six and has a notch in the top of it.

There is a lever on the end of a bar and a peg on the end of the lever, and the peg goes into one of the seven holes, and it has gone into one of them this morning already, and there is no second hole free.

Nobody in that chamber thanked the woman of thirty-eight for showing them the peg.

Then she said what she does, in about nine sentences, and about four of the nine had not known any of it, and about nine had known the wheel and not the doing.

Aveline Marsh is thirty-eight. She has kept this chamber about nine years. She keeps a mill four miles off the landing and about eleven people in four houses eat out of it.

“*Once a day, before the light, the same as the other one, and the wheel goes round once and the lever drops the peg and that is the day it is.*”

“*Six days the peg goes in a plain hole. The seventh it goes in the deep one with the notch, and the notch is to tell a man who has been away a week which hole is the one that matters.*”

“*And I have been doing it about nine years and I change the peg about once in that time and the reason is that the peg wears and a worn peg drops about four days late, and I could not tell you how I know that, and I know it because the mill is late too and it is the same wood.*”

About four people in that chamber had the peg. The rest of them had the wheel.

“*Nobody has asked me what this is for in about nine years, and I would like it said that I have not asked either, and that those are two different things and I have thought about the difference more than about the wheel.*”

“*And I do not want to be asked. I am not being proud. A woman who has kept a thing for about nine years and never been asked what it is for is a woman who has one thing, and if she is asked she has to have an answer, and if she has an answer then the thing is a thing that was for that.*”

“*And it is not. It is a chamber with a wheel in it. I would rather be asked about the peg than about the chamber, and nobody has asked me about the peg either, and I am going to stop at nine sentences.*”

Nobody in that chamber said a word about the nine sentences.

Then a man of sixty-one gave what a keeping costs, and he gave the working, and then he gave the part the working does not reach, and the two together are the whole of what he is for.

“*A day is about nine hours. That chamber is kept by two people for about a minute of it. Twice a day is about two minutes out of about eighteen hours of two people.*”

“*And there is no form in the county of Kell that says a person may be paid for two minutes, and nobody has ever asked to be, and I am not going to work out what two minutes is as a figure of anybody’s life, because the number would be true and it would be about nobody.*”

About four people in that chamber had the two minutes. About nine had the coming.

“*And here is the part the working does not reach, and it is the only part that is expensive, and it is one sentence.*”

“*Somebody has to come down about ninety steps every day, and about nine of those steps are under about four feet of water, and it has been about four hundred years.*”

Nobody in that chamber thanked the water for being the cheaper part.

Then the man of thirty-one said the true of one half of a thing, and he said it out loud, and it was the twentieth time he had done it in any room, and a man of sixty-six stopped him at about the fourth sentence in a chamber under a bank, and held it, and it was the first time anybody had held him in that place.

“*A chamber that is a day and a chamber that is a week are the same kind of thing at two sizes, and somebody made both of them to be looked at, and the counts are the record, and the record is the point.*”

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

Nobody in that chamber moved.

“*No. I am going to hold it, and I am going to say why, and the why is not about the sentence and it is about the room.*”

“*You have said a thing that is true of two objects and you have drawn a line between them, and a line between two objects is a shape, and a shape is what a man makes when he wants the next one to be the same.*”

“*There is no pattern down here because there is nobody down here to have one. There is a woman who turns a wheel once a day because the peg has to go in a hole, and there is a chamber with a stone in it because the stone has to go along a groove, and if I had a third one I would find a fourth, and there is no fourth and there may never be a fourth, and what you have is two jobs.*”

About four people in that chamber had the two jobs. The rest of them had the shape.

“*And I held you because you said the word shape about nine seconds after you said the word pattern, and a man of thirty-one who has been counting for one hundred and one days has started to want there to be a fourth one, and that is not the same as wanting to know what a thing is.*”

About four people in that chamber had the one hundred and one days. About nine had the two jobs.

Then the man of thirty-one was put nine feet off with a lamp, and he was put there by a man of thirty-eight and not by anybody else, and the distance was the same distance he stands at in Hask and neither of the two men said so out loud.

“*You may hold the lamp. You may not go within nine feet of that wheel, and the reason is not the wheel.*”

“*The reason is that the day’s turn has been done. That is a day. And a day does not come round again for a person, and if I let a man of thirty-one have the second turn of that wheel then the second turn means nothing and the first turn means nothing tomorrow.*”

“*I have done it in a boat. You do not get the same water twice. The second time you are doing it you are not doing the thing, you are doing a memory of the thing.*”

“*The difference is about four days of a man’s work and I am not going to explain it any better than that.*”

About four people in that chamber had the memory. The rest of them had the lamp.

Then the man of sixty-six said the thing nobody else in that chamber was going to say. He said it about the two chambers and not about the man of thirty-one. It was the second thing he had said in nine years that cost him something to say.

“*I have been in this chamber about nine times in nine years. I have never in any of them been asked what it is for either.*”

“*I am sixty-six and I am the only one of the two of us who has been in it when there was no wheel-turning to watch.*”

“*About nine years ago the wheel was turning and the peg was going in and I watched a woman of twenty-nine do it, and she is dead, and the wheel is still here, and the peg is a new one.*”

“*And I have not been able to work out in about nine years whether the woman mattered or the wheel mattered. I have not asked her.*”

“*And I am not going to tell anybody in this chamber that the wheel is the important half, because I do not know. A man of sixty-six saying the comfortable half out loud in the dark is how a chamber ends up being a story.*”

Nobody in that chamber thanked him for the dead woman of twenty-nine.

Then a man of fifty-four who cannot read asked one thing about the board on the wall, and he said he could not read before he asked, and the question was about the seventh hole.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel at the head of that drain and has not walked a quarter mile he holds in nine years.

“*I cannot read and I have said so twice this week, and I can count to seven and I have counted to seven on my fingers every Friday for nine years and that is a different thing from reading.*”

“*So I am asking why the seventh hole is notched. I have been looking at it since the fifth hour and about four of us noticed it and about nine of us did not, and nobody has said anything about it.*”

“*Because the seventh one is the one you come back for,*” said Aveline Marsh, “*and you are doing it in the dark at the sixth hour before there is any light in the hole, and a notch is a thing you can find with a thumb.*”

“*And I have a hand-marks board at my mill four miles off with the seven days cut in it, and I cut that one myself about nine years ago, and I am telling you I cut it because my hands are the same in the dark as anybody’s hands.*”

About four people in that chamber had the notch. The rest of them had the thumb.

Then the man of thirty-one noticed that a notch cut for a hand in the dark is the same shape as a notch cut for a hand in the light, and he did not say so out loud, and about four people in that chamber noticed that he did not say so, and about nine did not notice that he had noticed.

Nobody in that chamber said the two things were the same. They are not the same. One of them is in a mill four miles off a landing and one of them is in a coat on a peg in a front room in a town of about eleven hundred people, and about four hundred miles and nine days of water apart, and about nine of the people who would have said so have not.

Then they went back along the passage and stood at the foot of the ninety steps in about four feet of water. The woman of twenty-eight was first up. The man of thirty-one was fifth, and he offered to carry nothing on the way up, because he had been told three times in two days not to offer.

Nobody in that passage thanked anybody for the coming back.

---

The fever was fifty-three weeks and one day old. He had been in that county one hundred and one days.

A chamber about twelve feet long and nine feet across is a week. It is kept once a day by a woman of thirty-eight who keeps a mill four miles off. She has not been asked what it is for in about nine years and does not want to be asked.

And a chamber that is a day and a chamber that is a week are the same kind of thing at two sizes.

And a man of sixty-six stopped a man of thirty-one in about the fourth sentence and said there is no pattern down here because there is nobody down here to have one. There is a woman who turns a wheel and a chamber with a stone in it, and if he had a third he would find a fourth.

And the whole of what a keeping costs is that somebody has to come down about ninety steps every day for about four hundred years, and nobody has ever been paid for the two minutes it takes.
