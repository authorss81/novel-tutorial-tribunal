# Chapter 690: Sixth Day Of The Hundred And Twentieth Week, And A Sunday, And On Which A Bank Is Full And Nobody Goes Down And A Man Of Thirty-One Says The Whole Of The Price Out Loud Before Anybody Else Has Described It, And Then Says What The Nine Are Owed For And Does Not Take Any Of It Back

Sixth day of the hundred and twentieth week. His four hundred and fortieth morning. Two hundred and ninety days after the settlement. The fever fifty-eight weeks and one day. Two hundred and forty-four days since the division. One hundred and ninety days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was about sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour and the man of forty-three gave the reading and stopped. And then he did the two counts he does and he did not add them.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and has the block of limestone with a line cut round it nine miles up this water and two crossings of it in his own hand and no figure for the gap.

“*About sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour. Five days since it came off those steps and I count from one morning to the next and not from a morning to itself.*”

“*And that is the reading and that is all I am going to say about water in this bank until somebody asks me a question they have not asked.*”

“*And a man of thirty-four has been in an ash on the low side of that landing for sixty days. Sixty is six hundred and ninety less six hundred and thirty.*”

“*A woman of twenty-nine has been in a reed on the other side for sixty-one days. Sixty-one is six hundred and ninety less six hundred and twenty-nine. I have not added those two together in nine days and I am not starting on a Sunday.*”

Nobody on that bank walked over to the ash. Nobody on that bank walked over to the reed.

Then the things that have not moved were said out loud on that bank, one under another, and every one of them was said by somebody by name and not by the narrator.

The man of sixty-one said the first one, and he gave the figure with the subtraction in the middle of it and asked about four people to notice that.

“*A sheet of deal with a seal on it is lying face down on a barrel nine miles down this water. Nobody has taken it inside. Nobody has burned it and nobody has put a mark on it, and the stone it is on is back on the counter where it came from, and that barrel has had the sheet of deal lying face down on it for about forty days, and forty is six hundred and ninety less six hundred and fifty, and a man of forty-three said out loud on the Wednesday of the hundred and fifteenth week that a sheet of deal does not spoil in three days.*”

“*And there is no number for the local hearing. Nobody has granted it and nobody has refused it and nobody has written it down, and a man of sixty-one gave the last of that number on the Friday of the hundred and seventeenth week and stopped, and it has not moved in about nine days and it is not going to move on a Sunday.*”

“*And the hearing about every record in the world is declared by a Bench four hundred miles off and publishes no days and no room and no county. And it has still not settled one single thing that anybody on this bank has said out loud in about nine days.*”

Then the woman of twenty-eight said the other one, for the fifth time, and she said it in two sentences and did not improve on it.

“*And I have checked it a fifth time this morning. Nobody on this bank has said out loud that a lamp on a hook in the ninth of nine rooms and the thing that keeps the record in four counties are the same thing, and I am still not going to be the one who says it, and it is still not because it is not true.*”

Nobody on that bank argued with her and nobody improved on her.

Then a man of fifty-four said what he had found out about a hand on that bank on the Thursday. And he cannot read, and he asked nobody for anything and nobody asked him.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel at the head of that drain and has not walked a quarter mile he holds in nine years and has said that he cannot read a great many times.

“*I asked a woman of twenty-eight on the Saturday what she was going to do about it and she said she was not going to be a keeper of it and that she was not going to be asked about it.*”

“*And she is not a keeper of it and she has not been asked, and there is a cut across a man of thirty-one’s right hand that has been there five days and has not closed and has not opened and has not gone white and has not gone soft and has not come back into anything, and it is the only dry hand on that bank.*”

“*And I asked this morning and I am going to say the answer I got, because I asked and she gave it and she gave it in one sentence and she did not ask me for anything in return. It is that there is no figure for it and there is not going to be one.*”

About four people on that bank had the five days and about nine of them had a hand they had stopped looking at.

Then the man of thirty-one said the whole of the price out loud, in his own mouth, before anybody else had described it. And he said it as a list and he said what each item was a figure of.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and he is nobody’s, and he offered three times in about two weeks to be the thing that is sent for and was refused three times out loud by a man who cannot see well.

“*Here is what it has cost this week. I am going to say every item of it and I will not round anything, and nobody on this bank is going to hear me say it was worth it.*”

“*One cut across my right hand, from the base of the thumb to about the first joint, cut on the Tuesday of this week, and a man of thirty-eight measured it against a groove in a wall and would not round it, and there is no figure for what it cost because there is no book to check it in.*”

“*One way back. I had one from the Wednesday of the hundred and first week and I do not have it, and it was about a hundred and thirty feet of dry passage and about a foot of walked gravel in it, and it is now about three inches loose and about one boot wide, and a man of thirty-eight measured that with his own arm.*”

“*One morning of a woman of forty-four’s bread, and about nine houses four miles up a cart road a day late, and about four of the nine who cannot do a day without it, and one of the four has a Thursday and that Thursday was three days ago and I did not ask her on the Thursday and nobody on this bank asked me about the Thursday.*”

“*Seven times somebody opened a door to a man of forty-one this week. And one name out of the seven, and that name is a man who has been dead about thirty years.*”

“*One sentence in a day book that has had nothing but numbers in it for about nine days, written in a woman of twenty-eight’s own hand. And she is not going to be thanked for it and she has said so.*”

“*And one of the nine, and not one of them got back. That is the sixth item and it is the only one of the six that nobody on this bank has paid for out of pocket, and it is the one I am going to be asked about.*”

Six items went into the air on that bank, and the Thursday that had gone past went with the last of them.

Then he said the part that was owed to the next week and not to this one. And he said it out loud and did not dress it and did not apologise for it.

“*There are nine of those and there is not one of them here. Not a name, not a bit of a name, not a mark a man of sixty-six could put his hand on and say that is a cut and that is a hand.*”

“*And there is no figure for when they will be got either, and I am not going to make one on a Sunday on a bank with a full bank on it.*”

“*Nobody is to hear me say sorry for it. Nobody is to forgive me for it and nobody is to be asked to, and if anybody on this bank says that it was worth it I am going to let them say it and I am not going to have said it.*”

“*And about nine of them have to be got, and it is not the work of this week, and I would like it recorded that a man of thirty-one said that out loud on a Sunday to about nine people and did not put a single one of them on a form.*”

Nobody on that bank said the word, and nobody said the word brave. And about four people on that bank looked at the water and about nine of them looked at a man of thirty-one’s right hand.

Then a man of forty-one said what he is doing in the morning. And it was one door and one gate and about nine people had the shape of it.

“*One door. In a village I have never been inside a gate of. It is up that cart road past the bakehouse and about a mile further on, and I have walked the top of that road twice and I have not gone in the gate once.*”

“*And I am going to go in the gate. And I am not going to take anybody with me, and if anybody wants to stand behind me they can come and stand behind me, and if anybody wants to wait at the top of that flight and be told what I am saying in there they can wait there.*”

Then about nine people stood about on a bank with a full bank on it, and the wind came up the reach, and a man in an ash on the low side of a landing nine miles up that water said nothing, and a woman of twenty-nine in the reed on the other side of that landing said nothing, and about nine people on that bank walked past both of those mouths about nine times and nobody stopped at either.

---

The fever was fifty-eight weeks and one day old. He had been in that county one hundred and thirty-six days.

There is a cut across his right hand that is five days old and has not closed and has not opened and there is no figure for it and there is not going to be one. And there is about three inches of loose gravel where there was about a foot of dry walked gravel on the Monday of the hundred and eighteenth week. About one boot of walked place is left in a hundred and thirty feet of dry passage, and nobody in four counties was keeping it.

And there are nine of the terms and none of them is here, not a name and not a bit of a name, and a man of thirty-one said that out loud on a Sunday in front of about nine people and said it was not this week’s work and did not apologise for it and put not one of them on a form.

And a man of forty-one is going into a gate in the morning that he has never been inside, and the sheet of deal is still face down on a barrel nine miles down this water, and a man of thirty-four is in an ash and a woman of twenty-nine is in a reed, and both of them said nothing at all in a fortnight.
