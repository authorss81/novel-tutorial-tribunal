# Chapter 697: Sixth And Last Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-First Week, And A Sunday, And On Which A Bank Is Full For The Sixth Day Running And Nobody Goes Down, And The Second Of The Two Positions That Are Not Found Turns Out To Be The Ninth Room A Man Of Forty-One Has Been In And Out Of Twice A Day For Nineteen Years Without Knowing There Was A Position On It, And They Find Out What A Chamber Costs To Keep Standing Open, And Nobody Says What Should Go In It

Sixth and last day of the hundred and twenty-first week. His four hundred and forty-seventh morning. Two hundred and ninety-seven days after the settlement. The fever fifty-nine weeks and one day. Two hundred and fifty-one days since the division. One hundred and ninety-seven days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was about sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour. The man of forty-three gave the reading and said the bank was full for the sixth day, and said the thing he has said four times, and then said he was going to say it a fifth time on Wednesday whether anybody wanted it or not.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept a landing nineteen years. He has nothing to say about a night, and he has a block of limestone with a line cut round it nine miles up that water with two crossings in his own hand.

“*About sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour. Six days since it came off those steps, and I count from one morning to the next and not from a morning to itself.*”

“*And a full bank is about sixteen inches and it has been that since Saturday. I think it comes off in the night. I have said four times that I have no figure for a night and I am not going to say it a fifth time until Wednesday.*”

About four people on that bank had the six days and about nine of them had a Sunday with nothing to go down for.

Then a man of thirty-one said the second of the two names, and he said what it went with. A man of forty-one heard it and did not say a word for about as long as it takes a man of forty-three to give a reading.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and said two names out loud on a bank on the Friday of this week, and has not written either of them down.

“*Orison. That is the second of the two, and it is the ninth.*”

“*It is the ninth opening along that wall, and the ninth room is the one a man of forty-one has been in and out of twice a day for nineteen years. It is about a foot from a shelf that is empty and a hook that has a lamp on it.*”

Nobody on that bank said a word, and about four of them already had the ninth and about nine of them did not.

Then a man of forty-one said the part that was about himself, and he said it flat, and he did not say it loudly, and he gave the count before he gave the rest of it.

The man is forty-one and keeps two carts and has a wheel on the near side that has been making a noise for a fortnight. He has been the ninth of nine since before anybody told him there was a number on it.

“*Nineteen years, twice a day, before the light. That is a count of a pair of hands and I have not multiplied it out loud and I am not going to.*”

“*And I am the ninth of nine and I have been since I was about twenty-two. I found out on the Saturday of the hundred and nineteenth week that there was a number on it.*”

“*And on the Friday of this week I found out that the number has a name four hundred miles off on a board. Neither of those things has been asked of me, and both of them are about my own two hands.*”

“*And I am not giving it up. I want that said. I am not going to be a keeper of a position. I am the ninth of nine and I have been that for nineteen years and I am not going to be relieved of it on a Sunday because somebody read a board out loud.*”

Then he said the cost, and it had two halves, and he gave the first half and said the second half was the harder one.

“*The first half is a minute. Two people swing a door-stone about five feet high that has water under it, and then a stone the size of a door is pushed nine feet along a groove and pushed back, and then the light goes out. About a minute, once a day, before the light.*”

“*And there is no form in the county of Kell that says a man may be paid for a minute before the light. There is not one for nine of them. I have looked for one in about nine days of not looking and I have not found one, and I am not going to be the man who goes and looks for one properly.*”

About four people on that bank had the minute and about nine of them had the form that is not there.

Then a woman of forty-four said the second half, and it was the harder one, and she had been the first person in that county to be asked for something in about nineteen years.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs nineteen years for thirteen shillings and fourpence a year. She has said a list is a column seven times this fortnight, and that a thing with no person on either side of it is a thing that has not happened yet seven times.

“*The first half is a minute and a form. The second half is that about nine people on this bank now know what that room is, and that is a figure of about nine people and not of a room.*”

“*And a thing that about nine people know about is a thing that about nine people can be sent for. Not can be asked. Sent for. And there is no form in this county that says a person may be sent for about a keeping somebody has been doing for nineteen years, and there is no form that says a person may not.*”

“*And I am not going to be the one who says that a chamber is not a chamber because about nine people have found out about it. A chamber is a chamber and it was a chamber on Friday, and about nine people have not changed it by talking about it, and they have changed what it costs.*”

Nobody on that bank argued with her and about four of them had the second half and about nine of them had the first.

Then the man of forty-one said what he thought all of them were thinking, and he said he was not going to pretend it was not there, and nobody had said it.

“*You are all standing on this bank working out whether the nine of you are going to come down that flight one morning in a week to open a door-stone that takes two people.*”

“*I can see it. I am not going to pretend I cannot see it and I am not going to be angry about it either, because if I had found out on Friday that a man of thirty-four in a reed had been in it sixty-seven days I would not have been angry about that man.*”

“*Nobody thanks me for the nineteen years and nobody forgives me for them, and I am not going to be told I was right about the number.*”

“*And there is no form that says a man may be asked whether he will still be doing it. That is the whole of what I have got to say, and I have said it.*”

About four people on that bank had the nineteen years and about nine of them had a door-stone.

Then a man of thirty-one started a sentence and stopped it, and a woman of forty-four gave the count of the stopping out loud, and nobody finished the sentence for him.

“*If a man of forty-one were not—*”

“*Three times this week.*” She did not look at him. “*You have started that sentence three times this week and stopped it three times, and about four of us have counted, and you have not asked any of us to.*”

“*And I am not going to finish it for you and neither is anybody else on this bank, and if you want to know what the sentence was going to be, it is in your own mouth and it is where it has been since the Friday of this week.*”

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has a cut across his right palm that a man of thirty-eight measured against a groove in a wall and would not round.

“*Three. And I am not going to say the fourth half of it, and I am not going to say the first three quarters of it either, and a man of thirty-eight on Friday said that a thing is a function and not a stone and that nobody is going to say what else could do it.*”

“*And that is a rule and not a rule. It is a man of forty-one giving a woman of forty-nine the credit for a word, and a man of thirty-eight giving a man of seventy the credit for a plinth. I have got no better one and I am not going to look for one this week.*”

Then a woman of twenty-eight said one thing, and it was short, and she said it standing about nine feet off and she said she had not decided to be there.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from. She is not one of the nine, and she has said she is not going to be a keeper of anything about nine times in a fortnight.

“*I am not going to be a keeper of the ninth room. That is the fourth time I have said a sentence of that shape since the Saturday of the hundred and nineteenth week and I am not going to say it better.*”

“*And I have not been asked, and I am not going to be asked, and nobody on this bank can be asked about me either, and that is the reason it is mine and it is not anybody’s idea.*”

“*And I have checked it an eighth time this morning and it has not moved. Nobody in this county has said out loud that a lamp on a hook in the ninth of nine rooms and the thing that keeps the record in four counties are the same thing.*”

“*And I am still not going to be the one who says it.*”

Then about nine people stood about on a full bank on a Sunday. A man of thirty-eight sat down on the gravel at the top of that flight for about a quarter of an hour and got his shoulder back in four times, and he did not go down, and nobody asked him to.

And a man of fifty-four who cannot read said one thing at the end of it, and it was about a man of forty-one, and he was not thanked for it and did not ask to be.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel at the head of that drain. He is the only person anybody on that bank can be sent for about, and he has been asked four times in nine days and has said no four times.

“*I have been the only person anybody can be sent for about a wet place, a space in a wall, a light under a door-stone and a hand. That is four things in nine days and I cannot read one word of any of them.*”

“*And now I am going to be the only person anybody can be sent for about a man’s morning, and a man’s morning is not a thing anybody can be sent for about, and a woman of forty-four has just told us why and she did it before I could get to it.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for the fifth and I am not going to be the man who says he was right first. I have been the man who was right first about four things in nine days and it has cost this bank more than it has cost me.*”

---

The fever was fifty-nine weeks and one day old. He had been in that county a hundred and forty-three days.

The second of the two positions that are not found is the ninth room, and the ninth room has been kept once a day before the light by one man for nineteen years. A man of forty-one is the ninth of nine, and he did not know on Friday that there was a name on it and knows today.

And a chamber that about nine people know about is a chamber that about nine people can be sent for. There is no form in that county that says a person may be sent for about a keeping somebody has been doing for nineteen years, and there is no form that says a person may not.

And a man of thirty-one started a sentence about it three times this week and stopped it three times, and nobody finished it for him.
