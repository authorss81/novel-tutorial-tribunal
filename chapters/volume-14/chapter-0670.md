# Chapter 670: The Seventh And Last Day Of The Hundred And Seventeenth Week, And A Monday, And On Which The Nine Are Counted Out Loud By The Person Who Said They Would, And About Four Of The Nine Said They Would Not First, And The Man Of Sixty-One Reads All Four Of Those Out Loud

Seventh and last day of the hundred and seventeenth week. His four hundred and twentieth morning. Two hundred and seventy days after the settlement. The fever fifty-five weeks and two days. Two hundred and twenty-four days since the division. One hundred and seventy days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was full again by the seventh hour on the Monday and the man of forty-three said the working for the fourth time in about nine days. And about four people on that bank had heard all four and about nine had heard two.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and he has two crossings of a block of limestone in his own hand out of nineteen and no figure for the gap. And he has been asked about it four times in about nine days by four different people and the answer is the same three words.

“*About a foot and two inches at the seventh hour this morning. A foot is twelve inches, so that is fourteen inches over a step, and on the Saturday it was sixteen, and it is two inches lower, and that is the working.*”

“*And about four of you have been told that the next time those steps can be walked is the next time that block of limestone comes out. And I have been the one telling it for about nine days. And it is a figure of a river and I have said so on two banks and nobody has heard the other half of it.*”

“*The next time those steps can be walked is about four days after that bank is full. The block of limestone is a different thing and it is nine miles up this water. And nobody in four counties has a figure for when it comes out either. And I do not know and I am not going to invent one on a Monday morning with the door open.*”

Then the man of sixty-one counted the nine, and he read every one of them out loud with the reason that person gave, and he said before he began that he was not giving a number.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept the book of heads and carts eleven years, and there are four lines in that book on the leaf after the last head and there are not five.

“*I said on the Sunday of the hundred and sixteenth week that I was not going to say how many of us there are. And I am not. What I am doing now is reading nine mouths that said a thing, and a mouth is not a figure of anything.*”

“*And if you want a number then the number is nobody’s. And a woman of forty said on the Thursday of this week what a Crown that binds nobody does in about four years and she was right, and the number is the part of it that goes wrong.*”

Then he read the nine, and every one of them was named by the person who had said they would.

“*One. A man of sixty-six, a sinker, who said he would come down on the Tuesday and said the reason was a narrowing and not a question. And who has been down three times in three days, on the Tuesday, the Wednesday and the Thursday, and is the only one of the nine who has not said that he did not know.*”

“*Two. A woman of thirty-eight who keeps a mill four miles off, who said on the Thursday of the hundred and fifteenth week that she would not come down again this year. And who has come down four times since that Thursday, and not five, and whose mill has stopped four times.*”

“*Three. A man of thirty-eight, who is the only person who may carry a board about nine inches long with thirty-two notches cut in its edge. And who said yes in about four seconds. And who did not bail once in three days, because the bailing is a man of forty-one’s and he has said that out loud on a bank twice.*”

“*Four. A man of forty-one who keeps two carts, who said he would do the bailing before anybody asked him, and who bailed about four hours and a quarter on the Tuesday and about two hours on the Wednesday and about two hours and a quarter on the Thursday, and that is about eight hours and a half in three days. And whose Monday is not going up on the Monday.*”

“*Five. A man of fifty-four who holds the gravel at the head of that drain, who has said he cannot read six times in about nine days. And who said nobody has asked him a question and that he is not going to be asked one.*”

“*Six. A boy of thirteen who is not a head in this book because the penny a head is on everybody over sixteen, who said nobody sent him and that he is not going down there. And who went down on the Friday of the hundred and fifteenth week and has not gone down since, which is seventeen days, and who has gone up that track about four miles twice a day for the whole of them.*”

“*Seven. A woman of forty-nine who came down that road twice, who does not know what a copy is and has said so out loud twice. And who said she would be one of them and that she does not know what half of it is.*”

“*Eight. A man of sixty-one, who keeps this book, and who said he would read things out loud and not write them down, and who has said that about four times and meant it about four times.*”

“*Nine. A man of thirty-one who is nobody’s, who said he would keep asking in a room, and who was refused the eighth of nine things twice in about four days, and who is in this anyway.*”

Then he counted the four, out loud, and he said he was not smoothing it, and nobody in that bank asked him to.

“*About four of those nine said they would not first.*”

“*The woman of thirty-eight said she would not come down again this year, on a Thursday, in front of about nine people. And she came down on the Thursday of the week after it and gave the reason in four words about a board.*”

“*The man of fifty-four said he has not been asked a question and is not going to be asked one. And he has not been asked one. And he has been asked nothing for about nine days and he has been in a gallery under about ninety steps of bank four times.*”

“*The boy of thirteen said he was not going down there, and he has not. And he is one of the nine on the strength of the four miles twice a day and not on the strength of a descent. And I am putting that in because if I do not somebody will smooth it later.*”

“*And a man of thirty-one said he wanted to be the thing that is sent for, and was refused twice, and is the ninth. And I do not know what to do with him and that is not a reason to take him off the list.*”

About nine people in that bank had the four. Nobody in that bank said a word about any of the four, and nobody thanked any of the nine and nobody said that the four had been brave.

Then the two people who are not in it were read out loud too, because a man of sixty-one has never in eleven years read out one half of a thing without reading out the other half.

“*A woman of forty is not one of the nine. She said no on the Thursday of this week in nine sentences to a request that was made fairly by me. And I made it fairly on purpose, and the ninth of her sentences was not about her.*”

“*A man of about fifty-two who cannot see well is not one of the nine. And he has not said he will be near it. And I am not going to argue with a man who told a man of thirty-one the true of a thing in four sentences twice in about four days.*”

“*A woman of twenty-eight is not one of the nine, and she said so on the Sunday of the hundred and sixteenth week in nine sentences. And she has been standing about nine feet off since the Friday of the hundred and sixteenth week, and ten is six hundred and seventy less six hundred and sixty, and everything she has said in about ten days has been about herself and not about the nine.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the four things that have not moved in about nine days, and he said them flat, and he said he was not going to improve on any of them.

Ilyan Vester was thirty-one and he had been in that county one hundred and sixteen days. And one hundred and sixteen is six hundred and seventy less five hundred and fifty-four. And he was nobody’s and there is no column, no form, no rate and no hundred in that county either.

“*A sheet of deal with a seal on it is lying face down on a barrel nine miles down this water. Nobody has taken it inside, nobody has burned it and nobody has put a mark on it. And the stone is back on the counter where it came from.*”

“*A hearing about every record there is was asked for in a yard nine miles down this water on the Monday of the hundred and eleventh week. And it has gone out of the thirties. And a man of sixty-one stopped adding to that number on the Friday of this week in front of about nine people and gave a reason, and nobody granted it and nobody refused it and nobody has written it down.*”

“*Nine words are in the heads of about nine people. A man of sixty-six has them in a place nobody can be sent for about and he is four miles off a mill. And I am not going to say the nine words again in front of a bank. Because I said them twice in a row once already and a woman of twenty-eight told me that was how a saying stops being a thing a man heard.*”

“*And there is a place about nine feet past the last beam where the water stops, and it is not a chamber and it is not a gallery. And three names for it are in about nine mouths on this bank and a man of fifty-four kept a fourth one out of a passage in the dark, and there is no fourth and I have not got a fifth.*”

Nobody in that bank said a word about any of the four, and about four of them carried a whole figure out of that bank and the rest of them carried a shape.

Then the man of thirty-one said what he wanted, and it was the same want he had said on the Wednesday of the hundred and sixteenth week with a number in it. And there is no number in it now and he noticed that himself.

“*On the Wednesday of the hundred and sixteenth week I wanted a figure, and a man of sixty-one told me in front of about nine people that there is not one and is not going to be one. And that is twelve days ago, and I have said the same want since without a number in it once, and I would like about four of you to notice that.*”

“*And the difference is that on the Wednesday I had a thing to be angry at and this morning I have a man of sixty-one who has given me a hundred and forty-six thousand hours each and called it a figure of people who have not been born. And I have a woman of thirty-eight who is the reason there is a fifth line in a book that does not have one, and I have a boy of thirteen who has a number now and does not know what to do with it.*”

“*And I am going to keep asking in a room. That is the ninth of nine and it is the only one of the nine I am any good at. And a woman of twenty-eight said on the Friday of the hundred and sixteenth week that she is not going to help me with her question by being near mine, and she is not, and I am not going to ask her to.*”

Then the water came on past that landing, and the man of forty-three said the stone was back under about a foot and a half of water and that was a figure of a river and not a figure of a man. And a man of thirty-four was in an ash on the low side of that landing and a woman of twenty-nine was in the reed on the other side. And neither of them said anything, and about nine people on that bank went past the two of them about nine times and nobody stopped at either.

---

The fever was fifty-five weeks and two days old. He had been in that county one hundred and sixteen days.

The next time about ninety steps can be walked is about four days after that bank is full. And a man of forty-three has given that working four times in about nine days and has corrected the other one out loud twice. And nobody in four counties has a figure for when a block of limestone nine miles up that water comes out either.

And a sheet of deal with a seal on it is still face down on a barrel. And a hearing about every record there is has gone out of the thirties with nobody granting it and nobody refusing it and a man of sixty-one who has stopped counting it, and nine words are still in the heads of about nine people.

And the coalition is nine people, and every one of them was named by the person who said they would. And about four of the nine said they would not first and came anyway, and the man of sixty-one read all four of those out loud in a bank on a Monday morning with the doing of each one attached.

And the ninth of the nine is a man of thirty-one who asked to be the thing that is sent for twice in about four days and was refused twice and is in it anyway. And about four of the people in that bank had worked that out before the count, and he worked it out himself on the Saturday and said it out loud twice in a row and was told about it on the Thursday.
