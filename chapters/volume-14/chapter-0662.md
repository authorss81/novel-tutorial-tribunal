# Chapter 662: The Sixth Day Of The Hundred And Sixteenth Week, And A Sunday, And What A Coalition Is And What It Costs Is Said Out Loud On A Bank, And A Man Of Thirty-One Offers To Be The Instrument Again And Is Refused Again By A Man Who Cannot See Well

Sixth day of the hundred and sixteenth week. His four hundred and twelfth morning. Two hundred and sixty-two days after the settlement. The fever fifty-four weeks and one day. Two hundred and sixteen days since the division. One hundred and sixty-two days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

About eleven people were on that bank at about the ninth hour on the Sunday, and it is two more than were there on the Saturday. And the man of sixty-one said the number out loud before anybody asked him for it.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept the book of heads and carts eleven years, and there are four lines in that book on the leaf after the last head, and he has not put nine more there.

“*Eleven is a figure of a Sunday morning on a bank. It is not a figure of a county and it is not a figure of anybody’s opinion about what ought to happen. And I said the same thing on the Friday of the hundred and fifteenth week about a party, and I have said it twice since, and I am tired of hearing my own mouth do arithmetic on a room.*”

Then the man of thirty-one said the want he had come with, and then he said a second thing that nobody had asked him for.

Ilyan Vester was thirty-one and he was nobody’s, and he had been in that county one hundred and eight days, and one hundred and eight is six hundred and sixty-two less five hundred and fifty-four.

“*Yesterday I read nine jobs out loud and I said I could do none of them. Today I want the nine from yesterday to still be nine today. And that is the whole of what I want and it is about as small as a want gets.*”

“*And here is the second thing. I counted the times I said the true of one half of a thing. And the last of them was the twenty-third. And that was the Thursday of the hundred and sixteenth week, and I am not going to count them any more.*”

“*A count of times is not a count of days. A count of days is only a count of days while a person is in the place. And a count of times is a count of times whether he is in the place or not. And the two of those sat in one sentence for a long time and one of them has stopped and I have noticed it and I am not going to keep the other one up as a habit.*”

Nobody in that bank said a word about the count. About four of them wrote nothing down, and that is the whole of what the count of that was.

Then the man of sixty-one said what a coalition is, and he said it flat, and he said it was not a name and he said why.

“*A coalition is nine people who have said out loud in front of each other that they will do a thing on a day when it can be done.*”

“*That is all of it. There is no seal and there is no room and there is no list. And a list was read out loud on the Saturday and it is gone, and I am not sorry about that and I have said so.*”

“*And here is the thing I am not going to pretend is different. A man of sixty-eight said on the second day of the hundred and fourteenth week that a Crown nobody can be sent for about, borne by a person nobody can be sent for about, is a thing that binds nobody. And he was right. And a coalition of nine is a bigger one of the same and I am not going to stand here on a Sunday and tell you that nine is different from one.*”

Then he said what it costs, and it was not money, and he said the unit before he gave the figure.

“*The unit is a day. It is not money, because there is no form in the county of Kell that says a person may be paid for any part of this. And nobody in this bank has asked to be. And I have kept a book of heads and carts for eleven years and there is no column in it for a day of a man’s life given away.*”

“*And the working is this. A board about four feet by two feet takes two people to lift and two to put back. And the bottom nine steps want about four hours and a quarter of bailing before anybody puts a foot in. A man of thirty-eight takes a boat nine miles up that water and nine miles down, which is about nine hours, and reads about nine minutes of two copies, and the count of that is of Thursdays.*”

“*A woman of thirty-eight is four miles off that landing with a mill, and four miles is about two hours with a barrow each way. And I did that sum on the Thursday of the hundred and sixteenth week and it came to thirteen and a half hours of a mill and about four hours of a road, and I am not adding them together again and a woman of forty-four was there when I said why.*”

Then he refused the one thing he had been asked for since the Saturday, and it was a number, and he said why in about six sentences.

“*Somebody asked me this morning how many of us there are, and I am going to say that I am not going to say. And I want the reason written down as a reason and not as a habit.*”

“*Nine is a figure of one morning on one bank, and I said that on the Friday of the hundred and fifteenth week about nine people going down, and I have said it twice since and I am tired of it.*”

“*And a coalition is a thing that is supposed to last longer than a morning, and if you put a number on it then the number is the thing and the people are the figure. And in about four years there will be a form with a number in it and a name at the top of it. And that is how the five of you standing here stop being five and become the first line of a number, and nobody ever gets to be the first line of a number again.*”

About four people in that bank had the four years. The rest of them had a man of sixty-one refusing to count his own coalition.

Then the cost was said to the one person in that bank it was worst for, and she was told it in front of everybody and she was not asked whether she wanted to hear it.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and came up that cart road on the Monday of the hundred and thirteenth week, and she has been in that county twenty days. And twenty is six hundred and sixty-two less six hundred and forty-two. And she cannot be sent for about in this county or in the county she came from, and she has two letters in a bag that nobody has read.

“*Tell it to me and not to the room,*” she said, “*and then say it to the room. And I will not stop you. Because I have been four hundred miles with a bag and I am not going to be a person who is talked about instead of to.*”

“*Here is the cost and it is in the only unit I have. If I am one of nine people in a county where nobody can be sent for about, then a room in a county four hundred miles off can say out loud that I was one of nine. And there is no form in that county either that says they may not. And I have been careful for four hundred miles and I am not going to be careless inside about nine miles of a landing.*”

“*And I am not going to be one of the nine. I have said no to going down those steps once and I am not going to say yes to a bigger thing on a Sunday because the smaller thing was a Tuesday.*”

Then a woman of forty refused the cost, and she refused it in about six sentences, and the reason was about the coalition and not about the man of thirty-one.

Wenna Callow is forty. She has asked for a day against a name for about nine weeks. She is on no board and there is no board and she says what everybody is standing in front of before she says anything else.

“*No. I have heard the day and I am not going to give it to nine people.*”

“*I have been asking for one day of my own for about nine weeks and nobody has granted it and nobody has refused it. And a man of sixty-one said that is the same as a hearing nobody has answered, and he is right.*”

“*And this morning the answer to my nine weeks is that I am to hand a day to nine people, and in about four years there will be a form and it will have a column and my name will be in it and the column will say what I gave.*”

“*That is four things and the last one is the reason and the reason is about nine people and not about you.*”

Nobody in that bank said a word to her about it, and nobody argued with her, and about nine people in that bank did not speak for about as long as it takes to pole a quarter mile.

Then the man of thirty-one said the thing he has been wanting to say since the Wednesday of the hundred and sixteenth week. And he said it out loud and in front of everybody. And it was the second time in four days and it was the same shape both times.

“*I am going to offer to be the thing that is sent for. I am offering before anybody offers it. And I am saying that this is the fifth want I have said out loud in about nine days and that a man who says five things in nine days is a man counting.*”

“*And I am offering it because there is no form in this county that says a person may be sent for. And if nobody is sent for then the work in the first job is done by whoever happens to be standing on a bank. And I would like it to be a man who says so out loud instead of a man who happens to be there.*”

Then a man of about fifty-two who cannot see well refused him, in about eight sentences, in front of about nine people, and the reason was the shape of the whole of this and not the offer.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well, and the fifth line of one of those sheets of deal is his and not the man of forty-three’s. And he has been that person for eleven years without knowing it was a thing to be.

“*No, and I am not going to give you a new reason for it.*”

“*A thing that is sent for is a thing people are sent for about. And you are the only person in four counties with a line about you in two places. And if you are the instrument then this is not nine people it is one person with eight people.*”

“*In about four years there will be a form with a name at the top of it and every person in this county will know what the top of it is. And it will be a man of thirty-one who asked for it out loud in front of witnesses, which is the worst way of all of them to be one.*”

“*I said the same thing to you on the Wednesday of the hundred and sixteenth week in four sentences, and the reason then was that about nine of us did not know what a review is. And you asked for a job and I gave you a no, and this is the same no with a bigger coat on.*”

Then the man of thirty-one held it, and he held it in about four seconds, and he said the true of one half of a thing and not the other half, and he did not number it.

“*A man of about fifty-two has just told me the true of a thing I would have got wrong twice, and I have held it. And I am not going to say how many times I have held one. Because a man of sixty-one this morning has given up a number and I am not going to pick it up where he put it down.*”

Then about four of the people standing on that bank had said no out loud in about nine days, and the man of sixty-one said all four of them out loud with the doing of each one attached, because a number of noes is only not a column when what each one was about is said with it.

“*A woman of thirty-eight said on the Thursday of the hundred and fifteenth week that she would not come down again this year, and she came down on the Thursday after it, and I am not smoothing that.*”

“*A man of thirty-one offered to be the thing that is sent for, twice, and was refused twice, and that is twice in about four days.*”

“*A woman of forty has said no to a day for nine people, and she has said no to a day for herself for about nine weeks. And those two noes are not the same no and I am not going to put them in the same line.*”

Nobody in that bank thanked anybody for a no.

Then the man of thirty-one said what he was going to do instead, and it was smaller than the thing he had offered, and he said it out loud so that nobody would have to guess at it.

“*I am going to keep asking in a room. That is the job and it is the eighth of nine and it cannot be filled and I am going to go on saying so in front of people until somebody tells me a better shape.*”

“*And I want it said that I offered to be a thing that is sent for on a Sunday morning to about eleven people. And that about nine of them watched a man of thirty-one be told no twice about it. And that no part of that is going to be anybody’s fault but mine, including the second time.*”

---

The fever was fifty-four weeks and one day old. He had been in that county one hundred and eight days.

A coalition was said out loud on a Sunday morning on a bank: nine people who have said in front of each other that they will do a thing on a day when it can be done, with no seal, no room and no list. And a man of sixty-eight’s sentence about a Crown that binds nobody was applied to it by the man of sixty-one who has said it before.

Its cost is a day, and it is not money, and there is no form in the county of Kell that says a person may be paid for any part of it.

And a man of sixty-one refused to say how many, and said the reason out loud. And a woman of forty refused the cost in about four things for a reason about nine people and not about the man of thirty-one.

And a man of about fifty-two who cannot see well refused the man of thirty-one for the second time in about four days. And the reason was that a thing that is sent for is a thing people are sent for about.
