# Chapter 661: The Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Sixteenth Week, And A Saturday, And A List Of Nine Jobs Is Read Out Loud By A Man Of Thirty-One Who Can Do None Of Them, And A Man Of Sixty-Six Is Asked To Go Down A Third Time And Says Neither

Fifth day of the hundred and sixteenth week. His four hundred and eleventh morning. Two hundred and sixty-one days after the settlement. The fever fifty-four weeks. Two hundred and fifteen days since the division. One hundred and sixty-one days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water came up in the night and stood on the first three steps, and it did not come on any further. And the board was on its stick above the ninth where it had been since the Friday morning.

About nine people were on that bank at about the eighth hour. Four of them had been down those steps in the previous nine days and five of them had not. And the man of sixty-one said that out loud before anybody had asked him, because he had said on the Friday of the hundred and fifteenth week that a party is a figure of one morning on one bank.

Then the man of forty-three gave the two figures and gave the working, because a figure without a unit is a decoration and he is a man who keeps a landing.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and the block of limestone with a line cut round it is in that reach. And the water went over it on the Wednesday of the hundred and fifteenth week.

“*About two feet and nine inches at the eighth hour on the Friday. About three feet and three inches at the seventh hour this morning.*”

“*A foot is twelve inches, so that is thirty-three and then thirty-nine, and the difference is six, and a hand’s breadth is three, and the difference is two hand’s breadths in a day. That is the working and I have not got a figure for what it is doing.*”

“*And the water in those three steps is not the water over my stone. It comes out of that drain. And the drain is about half a mile below me, and the board is up so it goes down again.*”

The rest of that bank had the water. Nobody said what a drain was.

Then the man of thirty-one said what he wanted, and it was not a want about the water and it was not a want about the beam.

Ilyan Vester was thirty-one and he was nobody’s, and there is no column in the county of Kell with his name at the top of it and no form that renews him and no rate on him and no hundred in that county either.

“*A man of sixty-one put four lines about a gallery in a book on a leaf that is not for names, and said out loud that it is the wrong book and that there is no right one.*”

“*And I have been in this county one hundred and seven days, and one hundred and seven is six hundred and sixty-one less five hundred and fifty-four. And it is a count of days and the day I came in is not a day I was in.*”

“*And I have read those four lines nine times and I have got nothing out of them except a question. And the question is not what is down there. The question is what has to be done on the top of a bank by people who are alive.*”

Then he said the true of one half of it and then he said the other half about four seconds later. And nobody stopped him, and he noticed that nobody stopped him and did not say so out loud.

“*A man of sixty-six said on the Sunday of the hundred and fifteenth week that there is no pattern down here because there is nobody down here to have one. And he was right. And there is nobody up here to have one either, and that is the difference and I have been in this county a hundred and seven days and I can tell you it is a difference.*”

Then he read the list, and he read it out loud, and he said before he began that he could do none of it.

“*There are nine things on this and I can do none of the nine. And I am going to read them out loud because a thing nobody reads out loud does not get done in a county with no column for it. That is the whole of my qualification.*”

“*One. Two people to lift a board about four feet by two feet off its stick, and two people to put it back, and not one and not three.*”

“*Two. Somebody to stand in about four feet of water on the bottom nine steps and empty them with a pail before anybody else puts a foot in. And that is about four hours and a quarter of it.*”

“*Three. Two people to push a stone about nine feet along a groove that is worn by that exact motion, before the light, once a day. And then two people to push it back about nine feet and put the light out again.*”

Nobody in that bank said a word about the third one.

“*Four. A woman of thirty-eight to turn a wheel once a day before the light and put a peg into a hole. And the peg wears. And a worn peg drops about four days late, and I do not know how she knows and she says it is the same wood as her mill.*”

“*Five. A man of thirty-eight to take a boat and a pole nine miles up that water and nine miles down it. Which is about nine hours, and to read about nine minutes of two copies, and the count of that is of Thursdays.*”

“*Six. A man of forty-one who keeps two carts and a boy of thirteen who is not a head in a book. Because the penny a head is on everybody over sixteen, to get nine people to this bank and off it again.*”

The boy was thirteen and the man was forty-one, and those two were the only people on that bank anybody could name for the getting there and the getting back.

“*Seven. A man of sixty-one who keeps the only book in this county anybody can be sent for about, to find out what is to be put in it. Nobody has asked him that and I am asking him now and some of you are not going to answer.*”

Then he came to the eighth and he stopped, and he stopped for about as long as it takes a man of thirty-eight to pole a quarter mile. And the whole of that bank watched a man of thirty-one not read the eighth thing.

“*Eight. A man of thirty-one, who is nobody’s, to be the one that is sent for, when there is something to be sent for. There is no form in this county that says a person may be sent for, so this job cannot be filled. And I have read it out loud anyway because I wanted to hear what it sounded like.*”

Nobody on that bank said a word to him about the eighth thing.

“*Nine. A man of thirty-four in an ash on the low side of that landing, who has been in it thirty-one days. And thirty-one is six hundred and sixty-one less six hundred and thirty. And a woman of twenty-nine in a reed on the other side of that landing, who has been in it thirty-two days, and thirty-two is six hundred and sixty-one less six hundred and twenty-nine.*”

“*Nobody has asked either of them anything. I have read nine things out loud and the ninth is two people who cannot be asked and that is the only one of the nine that costs nothing to be wrong about, and I am going to leave it there.*”

About four people on that bank had the whole of the nine. The number itself was said twice, once in the middle and once at the end, and it was nine both times.

Then a woman of forty-four said the thing about the list, and she said it in about seven sentences, and the reason was about the list and not about him.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs nineteen years for thirteen shillings and fourpence a year, and she is the only person anybody in that county can be sent for about a chair.

“*A list is a column. A column is a thing people get put in. And I have kept a book of twenty-nine chairs for nineteen years and I know what happens to a name in a column inside about four years.*”

“*It does not have to be a book. It can be nine people in a room remembering, and then it is nine people, and then it is four, and then it is a thing that somebody said.*”

“*And I have said no three times to putting a hearing in that book and I am not going to make a fourth refusal out of a list of nine jobs. And I am telling you that I have said no three times and I am not going to say no a fourth time either.*”

Nobody in that bank had heard the number three before this morning, and a list that is read out loud once is gone by the time the light goes.

Then a man of sixty-one said what the list was worth, and he said the same thing he had said on the Wednesday of the hundred and fifteenth week about a leaf that is not for names. And he said it once. And the whole of it had been said on that Wednesday to a bank that was not this one.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept the book of heads and carts eleven years, and there are four lines in it on the leaf after the last head, and he is not going to write nine more.

“*I am not going to write that list down. And the reason I wrote four lines down is that it is the only book in this county anybody can be sent for about. And that is a bad reason and it is the only one I have.*”

“*A list of nine jobs that is read out loud once on a Saturday is not a thing anybody can be sent for about. And that is the whole of what it has going for it. And it is gone by the time the light goes, and I am going to be glad about that and I am not going to pretend I am not.*”

Nobody in that bank thanked him for the four lines.

Then the man of thirty-one was going to say his want again and a man of sixty-six came down the bank. And he came down it at about the ninth hour with his pick about four feet long over his shoulder, and he had not been on that bank since the Monday.

People stepped out of his way. Not all of them had known he was within a day of that landing.

And then the room asked him to go down a third time, out loud, in about nine people’s hearing. And it was put to him as a request and not as a favour. And a woman of forty-four said the two words out loud before he answered so that nobody could pretend later they had not heard which one it was.

Abel Fenner is sixty-six and digs for water and has been down those steps twice, the second time forty-one years ago when he was twenty-five. And a man of sixty-six is the only person in four counties anybody can be sent for about a word he said in a gallery in the last week.

“*No.*”

About four seconds went past. The wind came off the water.

Then he took the no back, in the same breath, and he did it in front of about nine people, and it is the only time in this county anybody has seen that done.

“*I said no and I am taking it back and I am telling you that you have all heard a man say a thing and take it back in about four seconds. And that is a thing people do in a yard when they are ashamed and not a thing people do about a bank.*”

“*I am not going to say yes and I am not going to say no and I am going to tell you why, and the why is about me and it is not about the steps.*”

“*I have been asked. That is the new part. I have been down those steps twice and I was twenty-five the second time and nobody asked me either time, and I have watched about nine of you come down that bank in about nine days and not one of you has asked me where the passage goes.*”

“*And I am the only person in four counties anybody can be sent for about a word I said in that gallery. And there is no form in this county that says I may be asked what a word means. And a man who says yes to a third descent is a man who has been asked twice and has said yes twice, and that is how a person becomes a thing that is sent for.*”

Then a woman of forty-four said the thing that has been said in that county about nine times, and she said it flat, and she said it was not new and that she was not opening it.

“*A person who cannot be asked has not refused anything. That is not a discovery and I have been saying it since the Monday of the hundred and fourteenth week and I am saying it now about a man of sixty-six and about a man of thirty-one.*”

“*And a man of sixty-six has not said no and he has not said yes. And a man of sixty-one is not going to write either of those down. And if either of you writes one of them down I will say so in a room with the door open and I will not be sorry about it.*”

Nobody in that bank said a word about the two sentences.

Then the man of thirty-one said the true of one half of a thing and said the other half after it. And this time a woman of twenty-eight was about nine feet off with a bag in her hand and she did not look at him, and two people on that bank noticed that she did not look at him.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and came up that cart road on the Monday of the hundred and thirteenth week. And she has been in that county nineteen days. And nineteen is six hundred and sixty-one less six hundred and forty-two, and she cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from.

“*You have said a whole thing twice this morning and the first time it was true and the second time it was yours.*”

“*I said that on the Thursday of this week about nine words and it took me about nine sentences and I am not going to do it twice for a different man. And the reason is the same and I am not going to improve on it.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked her for standing nine feet off.

Then a man of sixty-one said the number of a day that has been going since the Monday of the hundred and eleventh week. And he said it with the working, and then he said the other thing that has not moved.

“*A hearing about every record there is, to be read in one room, was asked for in that yard on the Monday of the hundred and eleventh week. It is the thirty-third day. Thirty-three is six hundred and sixty-one less six hundred and twenty-eight.*”

“*Nobody has granted it and nobody has refused it and nobody has written it down, and I have asked four people in this room in about nine days whether a hearing that nobody granted and nobody refused is a thing with no person on either side of it. And a woman of forty-four answered me the second time and I am not asking her again.*”

Nobody in that bank had a column for the thirty-third day, and the man of sixty-one had asked four people about it in about nine days and had been told the same thing by one of them.

Then about nine people on that bank walked past the doorway of that ash on the low side of the landing. And four of them stopped at it. And the man in it did not say anything at all, and the man of sixty-one said the thing about that out loud so that nobody would make a meaning of it.

“*He said one word on the Friday and he has said nothing since, and there are nine of us on this bank and every one of us has walked past that mouth twice today.*”

“*And I am not going to make him into a reason for a list of nine. A man who has said one word and then stopped is a man who has finished. And there is no form in this county that says a person may be sent for about whether he has finished.*”

Nobody in that bank went back to the doorway.

---

The fever was fifty-four weeks old. He had been in that county one hundred and seven days.

A list of nine jobs was read out loud once on a Saturday morning by a man of thirty-one who can do none of the nine. And it was not written down. And the eighth of the nine is a job that cannot be filled because there is no form in this county that says a person may be sent for.

And a hearing about every record there is, is the thirty-third day, and nobody granted it and nobody refused it. And a man of thirty-four in an ash had not said a word on the Saturday when about nine people walked past his mouth.

And a man of sixty-six who has been down those steps twice was asked to go down a third time, and said no, and took the no back in the same breath, and said neither yes nor no. And a woman of forty-four said out loud that a person who cannot be asked has not refused anything, and she said it as a thing she has been saying since the Monday of the hundred and fourteenth week and not as a discovery.
