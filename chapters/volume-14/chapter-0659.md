# Chapter 659: The Third Day Of The Hundred And Sixteenth Week, And A Thursday, And The Seventh Reading Of Two Copies Agrees, And On Which A Woman Who Said She Would Not Come Down Again Comes Down Again, And A Mill Stops, And A Man Of Sixty-One Does The Sum And Refuses The Division, And On Which A Saying Comes Up A Bank And There Is Nowhere To Put It

Third day of the hundred and sixteenth week. His four hundred and ninth morning. Two hundred and fifty-nine days after the settlement. The fever fifty-three weeks and five days. Two hundred and thirteen days since the division. One hundred and fifty-nine days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The seventh reading of the two copies was done on the Thursday morning and the two agreed, and the count is of Thursdays, and the sixth was the Thursday of the hundred and fifteenth week and the fifth was the Thursday of the week before that.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and he is the only person who may carry a board about nine inches long with thirty-two notches cut in its edge, and he went up the water at about the fifth hour and came down at about the second hour of the afternoon.

“*Thirty-two lines on both. The thirty-second is the thirty-second. The thirty-first is the thirty-first.*”

“*Seven times now. Three Mondays, and then four Thursdays, and the four Thursdays are the only reason the number moved twice this fortnight, because about nine of us were not there for the Mondays and about four of us noticed.*”

“*Nine hours of a boat and a pole and about nine minutes of reading, and nobody has ever once asked me to do it, and I am not going to be thanked for the seventh either.*”

Nobody in that yard thanked him for nine minutes.

Then a woman of thirty-eight came down that bank at about the fourth hour of the afternoon with her coat wet to the knee, and about nine people on that bank saw her do it, and about four of them had not known until that moment that she was going to.

On the Thursday of the hundred and fifteenth week she had said, in front of about nine people, that she was not going down those steps again this year.

Aveline Marsh is thirty-eight and keeps a mill four miles off that landing, and about eleven people in four houses eat out of it, and one of the eleven is her mother.

“*I said I would not and I have come.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked her for the coming or asked her why.

“*And I am not going to give the reason first, because a man of thirty-eight gave that one on the Friday of this fortnight and it was the best true thing anybody said in that yard, and I am not going to improve on it.*”

“*The reason is a board. The board has to go back on its stick tonight, it takes two people, and I am one of the two, and the man of sixty-six is the other, and the man of sixty-six is sixty-six, and that is the whole of the reason and it is four words long.*”

About four people on that bank had the four words. About nine had the wet coat.

Then a man of sixty-one did the sum with the working, and then he refused the division, and about four people in that bank understood the refusal and about nine understood the sum.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept the book of heads and carts eleven years, and he did it on a board because there was no paper.

“*A hand mill is about nine hours of a day for the people who eat out of it. A day and a half of a stopped mill is about thirteen hours and a half.*”

“*And the four miles to her mill and the four miles back is about two hours on that track with a barrow, and she came down it once this afternoon and she will go back up it tonight, and that is about four hours of a road.*”

“*And thirteen and a half hours of a mill and four hours of a road are not the same kind of thing, and I am not going to add them together, and that is not modesty. A day of a mill and an hour of a road are two different things and adding them is how you get a figure about nobody.*”

About four people on that bank had the refusal. About nine had thirteen and a half.

“*And here is the part I am not going to do. I could divide thirteen and a half hours between about eleven people and I could give you a figure and the figure would be true and it would be about a quarter of an hour each and nobody in this bank has ever had a quarter of an hour of anything.*”

“*A half of a loaf is not a loaf. I am not going to hand about eleven people a number instead of a morning, and I have been glad about numbers for about nine years and this is the first one I have been ashamed of.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked him for the division he did not do.

Then a man of sixty-six said a thing he has heard in that gallery, and it was about nine words, and he said it once, and he said he does not know where it came from and will not be the man who says.

Abel Fenner is sixty-six and digs for water and has been down those steps twice, and the second time was forty-one years ago, and he is the only person in four counties anybody can be sent for about the word he said in a gallery on the Monday.

“*It is this. Nine feet, and then it is somebody’s.*”

About four seconds went past and nobody filled them.

“*And I heard it in that gallery about nine years ago, standing about nine feet past the narrowing, and I have not told anybody in about nine years and I have not written it down and I am telling you now and I am telling you that I do not know who said it or when.*”

“*And I am not going to be the man who says it means something. About four of you have the whole of what nine words is and about nine of you have nine words, and both of those are what I have got and neither of them is a source.*”

About four people in that bank had the whole of the nine words. The rest of them had a number.

Then the man of thirty-one wanted the saying written down, and he said so out loud, and he said it in about nine people’s hearing, and the thing he did next was the same shape as a thing he did in the eleventh week of the hundred and fourteenth week, and about four people in that bank had seen him do it.

“*I am asking a keeper of a book to write down nine words a man heard in a gallery.*”

“*And I know what I am doing. I did the same thing about nine days ago with a Crown and I said no to it in about nine sentences and I was right, and I am now asking a different keeper about a different thing and I would like about four of you to notice that the shape is the same and that I have not noticed it until this second.*”

About four people in that bank noticed the shape. About nine had a man asking.

Then a woman of forty-four said no in about four sentences, and it was the fourth time she had said no to putting a thing in a book, and she said so herself, and about four people in that bank heard the number and about nine heard the reason.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs nineteen years for thirteen shillings and fourpence a year.

“*No.*”

About four seconds went past and the water came past the landing.

“*A saying of no does not have to be written down the way a saying of yes is, and nobody decided that, and I said that in front of about nine people on the Monday of the hundred and fourteenth week, and there was nobody in a room deciding it then and there is nobody in a room deciding it now.*”

“*This is nine words a man of sixty-six heard standing in a gallery about nine years ago, and he does not know who said them, and there is no person on either side of it, and a thing with no person on either side of it is a thing that has not happened yet.*”

“*That is three sentences. The fourth is that I have said no three times to putting a hearing in that book and I am not going to be the reason there is a form in this county that says a keeper may write down nine words a man heard standing up.*”

Nobody on that bank said a word to her about the book.

Then the man of thirty-one said the true of one half of it, and it was the twenty-third time he had done that in any room since the first day of the hundred and twelfth week, and a woman of twenty-eight stopped him at about the fourth sentence, and it was the second time she had stopped him in about nine days.

“*Nine words are in the heads of about nine people on this bank and a man of sixty-six has them in a place nobody can be sent for about, and about four of you can write and about nine of you cannot, and that is the whole of the record.*”

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

Nobody on that bank moved.

“*No. You are not going to say the rest of it, and I am not going to let you, and the reason is mine and not yours.*”

“*I said on the Wednesday of the hundred and fourteenth week that I would write down what a body says about each person in my own hand on the day it says it, and that is nine sentences, and there is no form for it, and I have not broken one of them.*”

“*There is no body in that gallery. There is a man and nine feet of floor and about nine years. And there is no person in those nine words. You are asking me to stretch a promise I made about other people so that I can keep a thing about a stone, and I am not going to do it, and I would like about four of you to hear the shape of what I have just refused and about nine of you to hear the numbers in it.*”

About four people on that bank had the shape. The rest of them had the numbers.

Then he said the second half out loud twice, in a row, and about four people in that bank noticed that he had said it twice, and the woman of twenty-eight noticed it first and said so, and neither of them was wrong.

“*It is not yours to say twice,*” said Sera Quill.

“*You have been a tester of a thing and I do not know the word for what you do here. Twice is how a saying stops being a thing a man heard and starts being a thing that is true, and you have just moved it, on a bank, in front of about nine people, in about four seconds.*”

“*And I have been four hundred miles with a bag and two letters and a day of a gallery and I have not written one of these nine words down, and I am not going to, and I have not been thanked for it and I am not going to be.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked her for four hundred miles.

Then the board went back on its stick by two people, and about nine people on that bank said the water will be on those nine steps in about four days, and about four of them said nobody knows, and about nine of the nine counted the two people and the two were the same two who had done it in the hundred and fifteenth week and the week before that.

---

The fever was fifty-three weeks and five days old. He had been in that county one hundred and five days, and the seventh reading of two copies agreed on a Thursday, and a woman who had said she would not come down again came down again because a board takes two people and one of the two is sixty-six.

And a mill four miles off stopped for a day and a half, and about eleven people in four houses eat out of it, and one of the eleven is her mother, and a man of sixty-one did the sum out loud and then refused the division and said that a half of a loaf is not a loaf.

And a man of sixty-six said nine words out loud on a bank, and they are *nine feet, and then it is somebody’s*, and he does not know who said them and will not be the man who says.

And a woman of forty-four said no in about four sentences for the fourth time, and a woman of twenty-eight said no in about nine sentences to stretching a promise she had made about other people so that a thing about a stone could be kept, and the nine words are in the heads of about nine people and nobody can be sent for about any of them.
