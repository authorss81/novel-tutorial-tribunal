# Chapter 665: The Second Day Of The Hundred And Seventeenth Week, And A Wednesday, And On Which The Second Descent Goes About Nine Feet Past The Last Beam And Finds A Place Where The Water Stops, And A Man Of Thirty-One Tries Three Names For It And Is Stopped Three Times

Second day of the hundred and seventeenth week. His four hundred and fifteenth morning. Two hundred and sixty-five days after the settlement. The fever fifty-four weeks and four days. Two hundred and nineteen days since the division. One hundred and sixty-five days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

They were on the water at about the fifth hour on the Wednesday and the man of forty-one was in about four feet of it by about the sixth. And it took him about two hours instead of about four hours and a quarter, and he said why out loud before anybody asked him.

“*A man of thirty-eight emptied nine steps yesterday in about four hours and a quarter. A day later the drain has put back about two feet of it and about two hours takes two feet back off again.*”

“*And I am going to say the other half so that nobody puts it in a book wrong. About two hours is enough for nine people to stand in. It is not a figure of those nine steps and it is not a figure of that flight and I am not going to let it be one.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked him for the two hours, and he did not wait, and the man of thirty-one noticed that he did not wait and did not say so out loud.

Then the nine went down, and the man of thirty-one went down fifth, and the lamp was the man of thirty-eight’s. And the passage was about as tall as a man and about as wide as two men for about nine feet, and then it narrowed, and then it opened out into the gallery.

The gallery is about nine feet high and about as wide as a room with a hearth in it. And there are about nine beams in the roof of it set about four feet apart. And eight of them are still, and the ninth goes along about two inches and comes back with nobody in the room.

They had all seen that on the Monday and on the Tuesday and about four of them said the word out loud again before anybody had asked them anything. And nobody in that gallery argued with them and nobody in that gallery told them they were wrong.

Then the man of thirty-one went past the last of the beams, which is the one that goes along about two inches. And about nine feet past it the roof came down and the walls came in and the floor came up. And he said the figure out loud and then a man of thirty-eight gave him a different figure and a man of fifty-four gave him the same figure by a different road.

“*About nine feet, and I have paced it, and a pace of a man of thirty-eight is about two feet and that is four and a half paces, and I am not going to round it for anybody.*”

“*And I have measured it with my own rope twice and got about nine feet both times, and the rope is mine and I did not lend it to anybody and I am not going to.*”

“*And I have laid my hand along that wall nine times going along it, and it is nine hands. And I cannot read and I can do that. And a hand of a man of fifty-four is not the same as a pace of a man of thirty-eight and I am not going to be told it is.*”

About four people in that passage had one figure and about nine had three, and all three were true and none of them could be put in a line with the others.

And then the water stopped.

It did not go down. It stopped, the way a thing stops, and there is a line about nine inches up the wall that goes back all the way to the bottom of the ninety steps, level, the whole way. And in this place the wall below the line is dry and the gravel on the floor is dry, and the gravel is about a foot deep and it is walked.

Not trodden. Walked, in about nine inches of a floor, by somebody going to a place and coming back from it, for a long time. And there is no groove in the gravel and not one in the stone under it. And a person cannot walk a floor that many times without putting a groove in it, and I am not going to give that a number in a room with a lamp in it.

About nine people in that place were looking at the gravel. About four of them were looking at the line on the wall. And nobody in that place said a word for about as long as it takes a man of thirty-eight to pole a quarter mile.

Then the man of thirty-one said the true of one half of a thing and then tried to say the whole of it. And he tried three times. And he was stopped three times, and the third stop was by a man of fifty-four who cannot read and who had not been asked anything.

“*It is not a chamber. A chamber is a job. There is no job in this and if you go at it as if there is, you will be in here about four years and you will still not know who.*”

That was the woman of thirty-eight and it was the first stop and it was three sentences.

“*It is not a gallery. A gallery has beams in the roof of it and there are none. And I have been in one nine feet back with nine beams in it and one of them going along two inches. And that is a different place and I know the difference because I have stood in both.*”

That was the man of sixty-six and it was the second stop and it was about nine seconds.

“*It is a room.*”

“*No. I cannot read and I have said so six times in about nine days, and you have said three names in three sentences. And those three names are in about nine mouths on that bank above the flight. And I am one of the nine and I am the one they will come to, because the only door into a written thing is the hand and I am the hand in this room that cannot write.*”

“*And nobody has asked me one question this morning, and I have not asked anybody one, and I am stopping a man of thirty-one from saying a fourth name in a place with no light in it. And I would like about four of you to notice that a man who cannot read has just kept a place out of a list, and that is the whole of what I am.*”

About four people in that passage had the hand. Nobody in that passage had the fourth name.

Then the man of thirty-one said that he was not going to say a fourth one, and he said it out loud. And he said where the three would be by morning. And about four people in that passage heard him do the arithmetic and about nine of them had not known there was an arithmetic.

“*Three names in three sentences in a passage with a lamp in it, and by tomorrow they are on a bank. And by the end of the week they are in a town. And I cannot get any of the three back and that is the first thing I have said out loud in about nine days that I cannot take.*”

“*And I am not going to say a fourth and I am not going to say the fifth one that I have got. And I want it said that a man of thirty-one who has been counting things for a hundred and ten days has just had a thing he cannot count.*”

Then the man of sixty-six was asked what the water stopping means, and he gave the same four sentences, and about nine people in that passage had heard them before and about four had heard them twice.

Abel Fenner is sixty-six and digs for water and is the only person in four counties anybody can be sent for about a word he said in a gallery about a week ago. And he has been asked what the water stopping means once this week and he is being asked it a second time, and he answered it the same way both times.

“*I have a rate and a wear, and both of those are about how a thing is done and neither of those is about who does it.*”

“*I was twenty-five the second time and it was moving. I am sixty-six now and it is moving.*”

“*Those are the only two things I know about it and I have had forty-one years to add a third and I have not.*”

“*And I have been asked what a line on a wall means this morning, and I have the same four sentences for that, and they are about a line and not about a person. And I am sixty-six and I am a sinker and I know what a hole is. And I will not be the man who tells you, and if you write it down in a book I will not have said it.*”

Nobody in that passage thanked him for the same four sentences twice.

Then a man of sixty-one said the number of a day, and he said the working, and then he said what a day of waiting is, and he did not give a figure for the end of it.

“*A hearing about every record there is was asked for in that yard on the Monday of the hundred and eleventh week. It is the thirty-seventh day. Thirty-seven is six hundred and sixty-five less six hundred and twenty-eight.*”

“*And I am not going to add anything to that number this morning. I have got a count of days for a hearing nobody granted and nobody refused. And a count of days is only a count of days while a person is in the place. And the place for that count is a room and there is no room and so I am going to stop adding to it and say the number as it stands and no more.*”

Then the nine came up, and about two hours and a quarter of bailing on the way back. And the board went on its stick by two people. And a boy of thirteen was at the head of the flight with a barrow and a coat and did not go down.

Nobody on that bank thanked anybody for coming up, and the man of thirty-one said out loud that the coming up is the part nobody thanks anybody for. And a man of thirty-eight said that he had said that on the Saturday of the hundred and fifteenth week in a chamber and that it was still true and that he was not going to improve on it.

---

The fever was fifty-four weeks and four days old. He had been in that county one hundred and eleven days.

The second descent went about nine feet past the last of about nine beams, and there is a place where the roof comes down and the walls come in and the floor comes up. And there is a line about nine inches up the wall that is level all the way back to the bottom of the ninety steps, and below the line the gravel is dry and about a foot deep and it is walked.

It is not a chamber and it is not a gallery and a man of thirty-one tried three names for it in three sentences and was stopped three times. And the third stop was by a man of fifty-four who cannot read and who had not been asked anything.

And a man of sixty-six was asked what the water stopping means and gave the same four sentences he has given since the Monday of the hundred and fifteenth week. And about nine people in that place had heard them before.
