# Chapter 676: Sixth Day Of The Hundred And Eighteenth Week, And A Sunday, And On Which The Water Came Off The Top Step In One Night And Nobody Has A Figure For It, And Nine Go Down Again, And A Man Of Thirty-One Is Told In About Nine Sentences At The Head Of The Flight That He Is Not The Instrument Of This Either

Sixth day of the hundred and eighteenth week. His four hundred and twenty-sixth morning. Two hundred and seventy-six days after the settlement. The fever fifty-six weeks and one day. Two hundred and thirty days since the division. One hundred and seventy-six days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank came off the top step in the night and the man of forty-three had no figure for it, and he said so before anybody asked and he said it twice.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years and has given a reading on ten mornings out of the last ten.

“*It was full yesterday at about sixteen inches over the top step and this morning at the sixth hour it is about four inches, and that is about twelve inches of a bank in one night.*”

“*And I am not going to tell you I know what that is. I have got the pair of figures and the pair of figures say nothing about a night, and a man of forty-three who invents a figure about a night in front of about nine people is a man who will be quoted.*”

“*What I will say is that those steps can be walked this morning and they could not be walked yesterday, and that is the ninth figure I have given in nine days and not one of them is written down.*”

Then the two who lift the board were found at about the seventh hour by a boy of thirteen who was not going down, and the man of sixty-one said the count out loud, and it was nine, and it was a figure of one Sunday.

The boy is thirteen and he is not a head in the book of heads and carts because the penny a head is on everybody over sixteen.

“*Twenty-three days. Six hundred and seventy-six less six hundred and fifty-three. And I went up that track at about the fifth hour for the man of forty-one and then four miles for the woman of forty-four and back, and that is about six hours with a barrow and it is not a working day and I am not going to pretend it is.*”

Then a man of thirty-eight said a count out loud on that bank, and he said it because a day is the only unit he has ever had for anything, and he reads two copies nine miles apart on a Thursday and the count of that is of Thursdays.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine and is the only person who may carry a board about nine inches long with thirty-two notches cut in its edge.

“*One hundred and twenty-two days. Six hundred and seventy-six less five hundred and fifty-four.*”

“*And I count days in Thursdays and I have told the man of sixty-one that and he has written it nowhere, and a day is a day and I do not get a better unit than anybody else for standing in cold water.*”

Then the man of thirty-one offered it a third time, in about nine words. He was standing at the head of the flight with his hands empty. A man who cannot see well was standing there, because he had come to watch, and had said on the Friday that he was going to watch.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well. The fifth line of one of those sheets of deal is his and not the man of forty-three’s. He has been that person for eleven years without knowing it was a thing to be.

“*You are not the instrument of this either.*”

“*I have refused you three times since the Wednesday of the hundred and sixteenth week, and it is eighteen days, and I am not going to give you a new reason and I am going to use the same one again, because it is the only one I have and it has not worn out.*”

“*A thing that is sent for is a thing people are sent for about. There are nine doors down there and every one of them is kept by somebody nobody can be sent for about. You are the ninth of nine and the only one of the nine who has offered. A man who offers four times in about nine days is a man who has started counting.*”

“*And I would like about four of you to notice that I cannot see very well. I have just refused a man of thirty-one at the top of about ninety steps without raising my voice once. I would like it noticed, and I am not going to say the same thing twice.*”

About nine people on that bank heard it and the man of thirty-one held it in about four seconds and said nothing.

Then the man of about fifty-two said the other half of it, which was about himself, and it was five sentences.

“*I have not been down that flight. It is wet and it is about as tall as a man and I cannot see well. I have not tried and I am not going to.*”

“*And I am standing at the top of it on a Sunday morning, because I have stood at the top of things for eleven years, and I have never once pretended to me that I was anywhere else.*”

“*And nobody on this bank is going to make a figure of me out of that, and I would like it said that I have said it first.*”

Then the nine went down. The man of forty-one bailed for about two hours and a quarter and said nothing about it. The man of thirty-eight went first with the lamp that is his, and the man of thirty-one went fifth.

They went past the last of the beams, and about nine feet past the place where the gravel is walked, and along about a hundred and thirty feet of passage. Then they went up the step, and there were nine openings in the wall with the same mark cut over every one of them.

Then they went into the eighth, and it was the same room, about as long as two men and about as wide as one, with a hook cut in the roof and a shelf cut in the wall about the height of a hand, and the shelf was empty.

“*It is the same room and it is the same mark and I have been in it about four minutes and I could not tell you which of the two I was in if somebody asked me.*”

“*And the notch is deeper than the notch in the ninth. That is a count and it is higher, and I am not going to say what it counts because I have said for eleven years that I do not know what my thirty-two is for and I am not going to pretend I know somebody else’s.*”

Then the woman of thirty-eight went into the eighth and stood in it for a while and said one thing about the wear, and it was the thing she said in a chamber a week ago and nobody improved on it.

Aveline Marsh is thirty-eight and keeps a mill four miles off that landing. About eleven people in four houses eat out of it and one of the eleven is her mother.

“*The shelf has been used and the hook has been used and both of them are worn round the front and not the back, and that is a person who takes things off and puts them on the near side and stands facing the wall.*”

“*And that is about as much as this room is going to tell anybody and I am not going to be the woman who turns a shelf into a reason.*”

Then they came up, and the board went on its stick by two people, because that is what it takes. The boy of thirteen was at the head of the flight with a barrow and did not go down. The man of about fifty-two who cannot see well was still standing at the top of it when they came up.

Nobody on that bank thanked the man of forty-one for two hours and a quarter in four feet of water. He said before anybody got near him that he was not going to be thanked and had said that four times and was going to stop saying it, which he has said is a different thing from stopping.

---

The fever was fifty-six weeks and one day old. He had been in that county one hundred and twenty-two days.

A bank came off the top step in one night, and a man of forty-three said out loud that he had no figure for it and would not invent one. Nine people went down again, and a man of forty-one bailed for about two hours and a quarter, and nobody thanked him.

A man of thirty-one offered for the third time in about nine days. A man of about fifty-two who cannot see well refused him at the top of about ninety steps in about nine sentences, out loud, without raising his voice. He said he was not going to give a new reason, because the only reason he gives has never once worn out in eleven years.

And the man of about fifty-two said out loud that he had never been down that flight and was not going to try, and asked for it to be noted that he had said it first.
