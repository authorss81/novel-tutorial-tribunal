# Chapter 651: The Second Day Of The Hundred And Fifteenth Week, And A Wednesday, And On Which The Water Goes Over A Block Of Limestone Nine Miles Up That River, And A Count Of Days In A Place Stops

Second day of the hundred and fifteenth week. His four hundred and first morning. Two hundred and fifty-one days after the settlement. The fever fifty-two weeks and four days. Two hundred and five days since the division. One hundred and fifty-one days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The man of forty-three came up that plank at about the seventh hour on the Wednesday morning with his coat wet to the knee, and he said where he had been standing before anybody asked him. That is what he does, and it is the only reason anybody in that town knows anything about the water nine miles up.

Corvin Tace is forty-three. He has kept that landing nineteen years. Nobody in that town had said his name out loud in a room until the Wednesday morning, and it was said out loud because a man of sixty-one wrote it in a book and then read it back.

Nobody on that bank thanked him for the walking.

The sheet of deal with a seal on it was where it had been since the Monday. It was lying face down on a barrel that has no board on it. Nobody had taken it inside. The barrel stood in the open through a Tuesday night, a Wednesday night and a rain that came at about the fourth hour.

“*It was on that counter on the Monday under a stone. There was nothing on the outside of the stone and nothing on the inside of it.*”

“*And about nine people were in that yard and about four of them walked past it.*”

He said that before anybody had asked him where it had been, which is also what he does.

“*It is on that barrel now, and nobody has taken it inside, and a sheet of deal does not spoil in three days.*”

“*And I am not going to say that it would spoil in a longer time, because I do not know, and neither does anybody in this room.*”

About four people on that bank had the shape of that at once.

“*A thing lying face down on a barrel is a thing a person has not decided about. The water going over a stone nine miles up does not decide it.*”

“*And I went up the night before because a year had gone and I wanted to see it go, and nobody sent for me, and there is no form in this county that says a man of forty-three may go up that water at night to look at a river.*”

“*I did it anyway. I am not asking anybody to say that was right.*”

Then the man of thirty-one said what he wanted, and he said it out loud, and it was the fourth want he had said out loud in that room in a week, and this one was larger than the other three.

Ilyan Vester was thirty-one. He had been in that county ninety-seven days. That is a count of days since he came in on the evening before the count started. The evening he came in is not a day he was in, and he says that out loud about once a week because it is the only part of the count that is his.

“*I am not going to say that I wanted to be carried out of this county, and I am not going to say that I did not.*”

Nobody on that bank moved.

“*A year in this county is the time between one crossing of a block of limestone nine miles up that river and the next one after it. A man of sixty-one said that out loud on a Saturday in front of about nine people.*”

“*And I counted from that water every morning for ninety-six days and I could not stop counting from it.*”

“*And I am not going to say out loud that I am glad. I am going to say what is true, which is that I have been waiting for a day and there is no figure for the day, and a thing with no figure is worse to wait for than a thing with one.*”

About four people on that bank had the whole of it. About nine had a man saying he was not glad, and not one of them could say afterwards what he had said instead.

Then the man of sixty-one did the two sums, and he did them out loud, and he did the second one twice.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept the book of heads and carts eleven years. It was in that book on the Wednesday morning. There is a column cut into the back of the last leaf with a knife, and the column is a number of people and not of heads.

“*First. A man of thirty-one has been in that county ninety-seven days, and ninety-seven is six hundred and fifty-one less five hundred and fifty-four.*”

“*I am not doing it out of a book. A day is a day. It is not a week and it is not a year.*”

“*Second. That same man came up that plank on the first day of the hundred and eighth week, and it is the fiftieth day since. Fifty is six hundred and fifty-one less six hundred and one.*”

“*I have done that one twice and it comes to the same both times. I would like about four of you to do it and about nine of you not to.*”

About four people on that bank did it.

“*And here is the part that is not arithmetic, and I am putting it in the same breath. If I do not, it will go about this town in a different shape by the fourth hour.*”

“*Fifty is a count of days that a person was in a place. It is a count and it is true, and it is fifty, and it is not going to be fifty-one, and it is not going to be a hundred, and it does not go on.*”

“*A count of days is only a count of days while a person is in the place. He came up that plank fifty days ago and he is not in it. That is the whole of why it stops.*”

“*There is nobody in this room who can make it go on, and there is nobody in this room who should.*”

Nobody on that bank said a word about it.

Then the man of thirty-one said the true of one half of it, and there was no day-count in it, and that was the first time in a hundred days there had not been one.

“*A man of thirty-one has just said the true of one half of a thing, and it is the sixteenth time in that room. About four of you have been counting the times and about nine have not.*”

“*And the day-count that used to go in that sentence went on the Wednesday morning with the sum. It is not in this one.*”

“*I am not going to say why a second time, because the woman of forty-four would say it is because the clock it went with has stopped, and she would be right. Saying it twice is how a figure gets to be a habit.*”

About four people on that bank had the missing number. The rest of them had a sentence.

Then the man of sixty-eight said what a lost thing to count from is, and he said it about the stone and not about the man. The rule he gave on the Thursday of the hundred and tenth week is thirty-four days old, and thirty-four is six hundred and fifty-one less six hundred and seventeen, and he did the subtraction out loud.

Pell Varn is sixty-eight. He keeps the last quarter mile. He is not in a chair, and there is no form in that county that lets a man ask to be corrected in one.

“*There is a block of limestone nine miles up that river with a line cut round it, and the water is over it now. It is the first time since he came into this county.*”

“*And the man counted from it. Ninety-six mornings.*”

“*And this morning the stone is under the water, and it will be under it for as long as it is over it, and there is no figure in four counties for how long that is. I am not going to invent one in a room with the door open.*”

“*And a man has lost the thing he counted from, and I am not going to say a word about that. I would like about four of you to notice that I have said a word about the stone instead.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked him for the stone.

Then the man of forty-three was asked what the water looked like, and he gave a figure and gave the working, because a figure without a unit is a decoration and he is a man who keeps a landing.

“*About a foot and a hand’s breadth over the cut, at about the sixth hour. A hand’s breadth is about three inches. A foot is twelve. That is fifteen inches over a line cut in a stone.*”

“*And I am not calling it a foot and a quarter, because it was not one.*”

“*And about half a mile below that landing the water is in the bank. There are about ninety steps cut in it going down. They have been cut a long time.*”

“*The bottom nine of them are under about four feet of water, and have been for as long as I have been going up that reach.*”

About four people on that bank had the ninety. About nine had a wet coat.

“*And at the top of those steps there is a board about four feet by two feet. It is propped up on a stick so the steps drain. It is up on its prop this morning, and there is nobody under it.*”

“*And there has not been anybody under it for about three years. I am not going to say nobody has been down those steps at all, because there has been a man, and he is a sinker, and he is sixty-six.*”

“*His name is not the one I have been keeping for you, and I am not going to say it on this bank. I have kept his name out of a room for about nine years and I am tired of it. I am going to write it down in my book on the Friday and read it out.*”

About four people on that bank had the man. About nine had the ninety steps and not the man.

Then the bench against the gunwale of the Gull was spoken of, and the plank, and the barrel, and the boy of thirteen who is not a head in the book of heads and carts because the penny a head is on everybody over sixteen.

“*Eleven feet of plank. Thirty feet of hurdles. Eleven people in a working week, and nine of them were on a board that was.*”

“*And the sixth reading of the two copies is on the Thursday, and this is the Wednesday before it. The fifth was the Thursday of last week. The count is of Thursdays and not of readings, and a Friday is not a Thursday however well it goes.*”

“*And a board about nine inches long with thirty-two notches in it is in a coat on a peg, and the man of thirty-one may not carry it. The rule that says so is thirty-four days old.*”

“*I have said that sum out loud already this morning and I am not going to say it again, because saying it twice is how a figure gets to be a habit.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked the man of thirty-one for holding a rule he did not make.

Then three people who were not on that bank were spoken of, and none of the three came up that cart road, and the chapter said so and did not pretend it was a thing.

“*A woman of seventy-one keeps a house that is not hers and has kept it eleven years, and nobody has come to that door in eleven years.*”

“*And there is a woman of twenty-eight in this county who came up a cart road on the Monday of the hundred and thirteenth week. She cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from. She has two letters nobody has read, and a river is not a hearing, and she will say so.*”

“*And there is a man of thirty-four in an ash on the low side of that landing who has not stood up since the second day of the hundred and twelfth week, and that is twenty-one days, and about four of us knew he was in it this morning and about nine did not. The same is true of the woman of twenty-nine in the reed on the other side of that landing, and hers began about the second hour of the day before his.*”

“*I am not going to make either of them into a reason.*”

About four people on that bank had the two ashes. The rest of them had the water.

Then the water was said to be moving, and nobody went up to look, and about nine of them heard it and about four named it.

“*One river. One stone with a line cut round it. One crossing, and the last one nobody in this room can date, and the one before that.*”

“*A year is water over stone. Nobody chose the day. Nobody in four counties can say when it comes off again.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked the river for going over.

---

The fever was fifty-two weeks and four days old. He had been in that county ninety-seven days. He had been in Hask fifty days, and fifty is six hundred and fifty-one less six hundred and one, and the count is of days he was in the place and it does not go on.

And the water went over a block of limestone with a line cut round it, nine miles up that river, at about the sixth hour. Nobody in four counties chose the day. There is no figure for when it comes off again.

And the board at the top of about ninety steps was up on its stick, about half a mile below the landing, and there was nobody under it, and there has not been anybody under it for about three years.

And a sheet of deal with a seal on it was lying face down on a barrel that has no board on it, and nobody had taken it inside, and a man of forty-three said out loud that a crossing of water does not decide a thing a person has not decided about.
