# Chapter 656: The Seventh And Last Day Of The Hundred And Fifteenth Week, And A Monday, And On Which The Passage Goes Further Than About Nine Feet, And There Is A Beam About Nine Feet Long That Is Moving With Nobody In The Room

Seventh and last day of the hundred and fifteenth week. His four hundred and sixth morning. Two hundred and fifty-six days after the settlement. The fever fifty-three weeks and two days. Two hundred and ten days since the division. One hundred and fifty-six days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The board was up on its stick again on the Monday morning and there were nine people on that bank who had been on it on the Friday, and the man of sixty-six was asked how long a day of it he could do and he gave the answer in one sentence and then said a second sentence about it.

“*Once. Once a day is once and I did it on the Friday and I am sixty-six and I am not going to be the man who does it twice in three days to make a Monday tidy.*”

“*And the woman of thirty-eight is here and the woman of twenty-eight is here and the man of thirty-eight and the man of forty-one and the man of fifty-four and the woman of forty and the man of sixty-one and the man of thirty-one, and that is nine, and about four of you are counting the nine because a man of sixty-one said on the Friday that a party is a figure of one morning on one bank.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked the nine for coming twice.

The passage goes along in the dark, and it is about as tall as a man and about as wide as two men for about nine feet, and after that it narrows, and there is water in the narrowing, and a man of sixty-six said the narrowing is as far as he had ever been and that the last time he had been as far as the narrowing it had been forty-one years ago.

Then about nine feet past the narrowing the passage opens out, and the gallery is about nine feet high and about as long as the passage and about as wide as a room with a hearth in it, and there are beams in the roof of it.

About nine of them. They are of wood, and they are the length of the gallery, and they are set about four feet apart, and they are on their side rather than their edge, and eight of them are still.

The ninth one is moving.

Nobody in that gallery said a word for about as long as it takes to pole a quarter mile.

It is about nine feet long and it is on its side and it goes along about two inches and comes back, and it goes along about two inches and comes back, and there is no person in the room and no cord on it and no water under it and no draught that nine people can feel.

About four people in that gallery said the word out loud before anybody had asked them a question.

“*That is a machine.*”

“*It is worked by the water under the steps and there is a hole somewhere and I have not found the hole.*”

“*Somebody built it. A hand cut in a lintel about ninety steps back has somebody who built this.*”

About four people in that gallery had a machine. About nine had a beam.

Then the man of thirty-one said the whole of a thing, and he said it out loud, and it was the first time in a hundred and two days he had said the whole of one instead of half of it, and he said it before he had looked properly, and he was wrong.

“*It is a machine.*”

Nobody in that gallery moved.

“*It goes at a rate and the rate does not change while we are watching, and a thing that keeps a rate has a part that makes it, and a part that makes it has a wear on it, and there is dust on that ledge under it about a foot long and the dust has a shape in it.*”

“*And I have been on a planet where the rate of a thing is the whole of what it is, and I am telling you before I have counted the rate that this is what we are looking at, and I would like about four of you to know that I said it first and about nine of you to know that I have said it before I counted.*”

About four people in that gallery had the dust. About nine had a man who had said a thing first.

Then a man of sixty-six gave him the two facts that made his reasons not reasons, and he gave them in about four sentences, and he gave them about forty-one years and about the shape in the dust, and he did not tell him what moves it.

Abel Fenner is sixty-six and digs for water and has been down those steps twice. The second time was forty-one years ago and he was twenty-five.

“*You have a rate and a wear, and both of those are about how a thing is done and neither of those is about who does it.*”

“*I was twenty-five the second time and it was moving. I am sixty-six now and it is moving. Those are the only two things I know about it and I have had forty-one years to add a third and I have not.*”

“*And the dust has a shape in it because the beam has gone along the same two inches about a hundred and forty-six thousand times, and that is not a shape made by a man. That is a shape made by a beam, and I do not know what that is, and neither do you, and you said your sentence before you counted the rate and I want it said that I noticed.*”

About four people in that gallery had the dust. The rest of them had the forty-one years.

“*And I am not going to be the man who tells you what moves it, and if you write it down in a book I will not have said it, and I said that on the Thursday and I am saying it again in a harder room.*”

Then the man of thirty-one said the true of one half of it, and it was the twenty-first time he had done that in any room, and a man of sixty-six did not stop him, because there was nothing left to stop.

“*We do not know what moves it, and there are about nine of us in a gallery and one of us has been here twice and one of us has been here once, and I would like about four of you to have that before anybody says anything else about a machine.*”

About four people in that gallery had the two. About nine had the dust.

Then the man of sixty-six said the word, and it was the first time in about four hundred years that anybody in that reach had said it out loud where another person could hear it, and he said it flat, and he said where he got it, and the cost of saying it fell on him and not on anybody else in that gallery.

“*There is a word for it and I do not know who made the word.*”

“*A man I dug with when I was about twenty-five used it. He was not from this county and I have not seen him in about forty-one years and he is not going to be found.*”

“*The word is loom.*”

“*And it is not the word for that beam. It is the word for the whole of it. The nine steps, the passage, the chambers, the beams.*”

“*A man who says that word in a gallery is saying it in about nine feet of bank and about four hundred years of other people.*”

About four people in that gallery had the word. About nine had a beam.

“*And here is what it has cost me. I said on the Thursday that I would not be the man who tells you, and I am not telling you anything, and I have still given four counties the only mouth that has the word.*”

“*There is no form in the county of Kell that says a man of sixty-six may be asked what a word means, and there is no form that says he may not. And I am the only person in four counties anybody can be sent for about this word, and about four of you can see what that is.*”

“*It is the same as a Crown that binds nobody. A man of sixty-eight said that on the second day of the hundred and fourteenth week and was right.*”

“*I have been in about nine rooms in a year and I have watched about nine people make things nobody can be sent for about. I have never made one before, and I have made one by saying a word in the dark.*”

Nobody in that gallery thanked him for the word.

Then a man of thirty-one said a name out loud, and he said it once, and he said it flat, and it was the third time in four counties and the first time below the water, and nothing whatever happened.

“*I am going to say a name out loud. It is a name and not a shout, and I am not going to say it twice and I am not going to explain it.*”

About four people in that gallery went still. About nine did not know there was anything coming.

“*The First Witness.*”

The nine of them were where they had been.

About four seconds went past. Nothing said and nothing moved. The beam went along about two inches and came back.

About nine people in that gallery were waiting for something. About four of them had not known there was anything to wait for.

“*That is the name of the thing that said its own name in a weigh-house about thirty days ago in front of about four people.*”

“*And a man of forty-three read thirty-two lines out loud in that yard on the Monday of the hundred and eleventh week, and he read all thirty-two, and the name I have just said is not one of the thirty-two and it was not on a sheet in that boat either.*”

“*I said it on the ground in this county twice and nothing happened both times.*”

“*And I have just said it under about ninety steps of bank with a name of my own in the air, and nothing has happened, and about four of you have a thing you did not have four seconds ago.*”

Then the cost of that fell, and it fell on the man in that gallery who could not read, and he said it out loud himself, and he is ordinary, and there is no person in it to be cross with.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel at the head of that drain and has not walked a quarter mile he holds in nine years. He said on the Saturday that he cannot read before anybody asked him anything, and he said it again on the Sunday, and he says it because a question about a book is a question about reading.

“*I cannot read, and I said so twice this week before anybody asked me, and a name is a thing I can hear.*”

“*There are about four of you in this gallery who can write and about nine of us who cannot.*”

“*A name that is said in a room with a lamp in it is going to be a name that four counties are going to want written down, and the only door into a written thing is the hand.*”

“*I am fifty-four. I have held that gravel for nine years, and nobody has ever wanted a thing off me.*”

“*If a name has been said under a bank by a man who cannot be sent for about, then the name is going to come to the nearest hand. The nearest hand in this county is a man of sixty-one who keeps a book, and he is the one it will go to and not me.*”

“*And I would like it said out loud that I noticed it four seconds before it happened, and that I could not stop it by saying so.*”

“*That is the whole of my nine days and my nine years.*”

About four people in that gallery had the hand. About nine had the beam.

Then a man of sixty-one said what he was going to write down in the book that night. It was a list of four lines. About four of the nine in that gallery could not read the list and did not need to.

“*A gallery about nine feet high under about ninety steps of bank, with about nine beams in the roof of it, and eight of them still.*”

“*And one of them moving about two inches and back, and nobody in the room, and no cord, and no water under it that nine people can feel, and no draught nine people can feel.*”

“*And it was moving forty-one years ago when a man of twenty-five was in it, and I did not see that. I have it from a man of sixty-six who did, and I am putting it down as a thing a man said and not as a thing I saw, because I have never been in this gallery and I am not going to write that I have.*”

Nobody in that gallery thanked him for the list.

Then the beam went along about two inches and came back, and about nine people in that gallery watched it, and about four of them were still holding their hands out from having carried a lamp.

---

The fever was fifty-three weeks and two days old. He had been in that county one hundred and two days.

About nine feet past a narrowing there is a gallery about nine feet high with about nine beams in the roof of it. Eight of them are still. One of them goes along about two inches and comes back with nobody in the room.

And a man of thirty-one said the whole of a thing instead of half of it and was wrong, and a man of sixty-six gave him two facts and would not be the man who tells anybody what moves it.

And the word was said out loud once in that reach in about four hundred years. The man who said it is the only person in four counties anybody can be sent for about it, and there is no form that says he may be asked or that he may not.

And a name was said flat once under about ninety steps of bank and nothing whatever happened, and the first cost of it fell on an ordinary man of fifty-four who cannot read and who said so twice before anybody asked him.
