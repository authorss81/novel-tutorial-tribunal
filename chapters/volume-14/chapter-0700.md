# Chapter 700: Second Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-Second Week, And A Wednesday, And On Which One Door Does Not Open And Nothing Falls Down, And Nine Boards Go Out Of A County Where There Is No Original And No Keeper, And Something That Is Not A Person Says A Question To A Man Who Is Nobody’s, And He Is Not Asked It Twice And He Does Not Answer It And Nobody Answers It For Him

Second day of the hundred and twenty-second week. His four hundred and fiftieth morning. Three hundred days after the settlement. The fever fifty-nine weeks and four days. Two hundred and fifty-four days since the division. Two hundred days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was about eight inches over the top step at the seventh hour. The man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working, and he said the thing he has been promising since Sunday, because it is Wednesday.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept a landing nineteen years. He has said he has nothing to say about a night four times, and said on Sunday that he would say it a fifth time on Wednesday whether anybody wanted it or not.

“*About eight inches over the top step at the seventh hour. Two days since it came off those steps, and I count from one morning to the next and not from a morning to itself.*”

“*And it was about four inches at this hour yesterday. That is the working.*”

“*And here is the fifth time. I have nothing to say about a night, and I have said it five times now, and I am going to stop saying it.*”

“*And the two of those figures are the only two things I have said out loud in about six weeks that are mine.*”

About four people on that bank had the five times and about nine of them had the two figures.

Then a man of sixty-one gave the three counts he gives and the one count he has been keeping since Sunday. He said them in the order he gives them, and he did not add any of them to anything.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept a book of heads and carts eleven years. There are five lines on the leaf after the last head, and a number is the last of the five, and there is not going to be a name on any of the five, and he has said that out loud this morning.

“*A man of thirty-four has been in an ash for seventy days. Seventy is seven hundred less six hundred and thirty. A woman of twenty-nine has been in a reed for seventy-one days, and seventy-one is seven hundred less six hundred and twenty-nine, and I have not added them in eighteen days.*”

“*And he has been in this county a hundred and forty-six days. A hundred and forty-six is seven hundred less five hundred and fifty-four.*”

“*And there is a second count of days on that bank that I stopped myself on the Wednesday of the hundred and fifteenth week, and it is the count of days he was in a place he is not in. I am not going to give you the figure of it and it does not go on, and nobody is going to start it on a Wednesday.*”

“*And a woman of twenty-eight came up that cart road on the Monday of the hundred and thirteenth week and has been in this county fifty-eight days. Fifty-eight is seven hundred less six hundred and forty-two.*”

About four people on that bank had the seventy and about nine of them had the fifty-eight.

Then a man of fifty-four said the thing about the door, and he cannot read. He said it at about the ninth hour with about nine people standing on a bank, and he was not asked and he did not ask.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel at the head of that drain, and is the only person anybody in that county can be sent for about four things, and has said no four times in nine days.

“*Two men put their hands on that stone again this morning and it did not move. There is about an inch of water under it and it is the same as it was on the Monday of the hundred and twentieth week, and I have put my own hand in that gap twice now and both times it was an inch.*”

“*And a chamber that is a day and is not kept is not a grave. I want that said on this bank by a man of fifty-four who cannot read.*”

“*Because somebody is going to say it in about four days, and I am not going to let the first person to say it be a man with a hand and a book.*”

Nobody on that bank said sorry and about four of them had the not a grave.

Then a woman of forty-four said the other half of that, and it was the ninth time this fortnight, and she said what she was not going to let anybody call it.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs nineteen years for thirteen shillings and fourpence a year, and there are nine of those chairs and not ten, and she is not a keeper of anything anybody can be sent for about.

“*And a shut door is not the right of refusal. I am going to say that once on this bank and I am not going to say it twice, and about four of you have been thinking it since the Tuesday of this week and one of you has been thinking it since the Sunday.*”

“*A person who cannot be asked has not refused anything. A door that will not open has not refused anybody. And the right of refusal is not in this county and it is not in that book and it is not under that bank, and nobody standing here has it, and I am not going to be thanked for the ninth.*”

About four people on that bank had the right of refusal and about nine of them had a shut door.

Then a man of thirty-one said out loud what it has come to. He said it as a list, gave the count, and said what each item was a figure of. He did not apologise for it, and he said so in advance.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s. He has a cut across his right palm that is fifteen days old and has no figure for what it cost. He has lost the way he came back on and has said so in his own mouth in a passage.

“*Eight items. I am going to say all eight and I am not going to round anything, and nobody on this bank is going to hear me say it was worth it, and if anybody says it I am going to let them say it and then it will have been said and I will not have said it.*”

“*One. A Thursday of a man of thirty-eight, and two copies nine miles apart not read for seven days, and nobody in four counties able to tell them. That is a figure of a man of thirty-eight and a figure of a gap.*”

“*Two. A name said out loud in a village a man of forty-one had never been inside a gate of. And a wall four days on with a hole in it about the height of a man. Neither of those has been walked to by anybody standing here.*”

“*Three. About a thumb’s width of light under a door-stone, carried about by a man of thirty-eight for six weeks and given out on a full bank. That is a figure of a lamp and not of a chamber.*”

“*Four. A remainder, off a man of thirty-four who has been in an ash for sixty-six days and was asked nothing in any of them.*”

“*He is not going to be thanked for carrying it, and I am not going to say he was right to carry it.*”

“*Five. One question I did not ask in a room about a hundred and thirty feet under that bank, at about the fourth second, and about four people who can tell in nine days that I did not ask it.*”

“*Six. A cut across my right hand, and the way back I had. Those two are on a list I gave out loud on the Sunday of last week and I am not going to count them twice.*”

“*They are not new today and nobody is going to take them off.*”

“*Seven. One door that will not open, and nothing fell down, and that is a figure of a door and not of a morning.*”

“*Eight. Four of the nine put back together in about four days and five of them not done, and not one name on any of the nine, and nine boards with no original and no keeper, and a man of fifty-four carrying one of them that he cannot read.*”

Eight items went into the air on that bank and nobody said the word, and about four of them counted on their fingers and about nine of them did not.

Then a woman of twenty-eight said the thing about a name, and it was a ninth time, and it was the same sentence with a day on the front of it, and she said it once.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from, and is not one of the nine, and has made nine copies and kept none of them.

“*I have checked it a ninth time this morning and it has not moved. Nobody in this county has said out loud that a lamp on a hook in the ninth of nine rooms and the thing that keeps the record in four counties are the same thing, and I am still not going to be the one who says it.*”

“*And it is still not because it is not true. And I would like it recorded that I have said the same sentence nine times and have not said it once, and that this is the last morning I am going to say it in this fortnight and I have not begun again.*”

Then a man of sixty-one said the count he has been keeping. He gave the working, and he said what he was not going to do with it, and it was the fourth name in a book that has one number in it.

“*That name has been said in this county four times. Under about ninety steps of bank on the Monday of the hundred and fifteenth week. On a bank on the Saturday of the hundred and seventeenth week, by a woman of forty. On a bank on the Monday of the hundred and eighteenth week, by a man who cannot see well, and that one was said to a stone.*”

“*And in a room under that bank on the Tuesday of this week, by a man of thirty-one, to the floor, and that one was the only one of the four that anything answered.*”

“*I am not going to write any of the four. I have written one number in this book in eleven years and it is a nine, and I am not going to write a name.*”

“*And there is not going to be a fifth of them, and I would like that said on a Wednesday by a man of sixty-one while about nine people can hear it.*”

Then a man of forty-three said the two things that have not moved in a fortnight, and he said them flat, and he gave the working with the first of them.

“*A sheet of deal with a seal on it is lying face down on a barrel nine miles down this water. Nobody has taken it inside.*”

“*That barrel has had it lying face down on it for about fifty days. Fifty is seven hundred less six hundred and fifty. I have looked at it about nine times and I am not going to be the man who brings it in.*”

“*And the local hearing has not been granted, has not been refused, and has not been written down.*”

“*A woman of twenty-eight has been on that bank every morning of this fortnight checking a thing she will not say. And a man of thirty-one said on the Sunday of last week that nine of those terms are owed and are not this week’s work.*”

About four people on that bank had the fifty days and about nine of them had a barrel.

Then something that is not a person said a question on that bank, in about nine people’s hearing, at about the ninth hour of a Wednesday. It was in plain words, and it was not a shout, and nobody shouted anything back at it.

And it said it to a man of thirty-one.

“*You can write it. One person, one set of words, and it holds. You will not have to do it twice and nobody will come and ask you whether you did it.*”

“*I am not asking you to be a good man or a clever one. I am asking you to be the one who writes it.*”

“*Will you write it.*”

Then nothing whatever happened. The wind came up the reach. The water was about eight inches over the top step and going, and the two carts did not go up that track, and a man of thirty-four in an ash said nothing, and a woman of twenty-nine in a reed said nothing.

Then a man of forty-one said he was not going to improve on it, and he gave the reason. About four people on that bank had heard him not improve on it, and about nine of them had not been waiting for it.

The man is forty-one and keeps two carts, has a wheel on the near side making a noise, and has been the ninth of nine for nineteen years, and could not open the door of it yesterday morning.

“*And I am not going to improve on that. A woman of forty-nine gave me the word for a going that does not come back, out of a shelf in a bakehouse, and I gave her the credit for it in front of about nine people, and the rule is the rule and it is not mine.*”

“*I have got nothing to say about it being fair or not fair, and I am not going to say it. I am not going to be one of about nine people on a bank who decide in about four seconds that a thing is wrong.*”

“*It might be the best offer anybody in four counties has ever been made by anything at all. I have not got a figure for how good it is, and neither has anybody here.*”

Then a man of thirty-one was asked a question and did not answer it, and he was not asked it a second time, and about nine people were standing there, and nobody answered it for him.

“*I am not going to answer that, and I am not going to say I have not understood it, and I am not going to say I need a week.*”

“*I have been in this county a hundred and forty-six days. A hundred and forty-six is seven hundred less five hundred and fifty-four. And I am nobody’s, and there is no form in this county that says a man may answer a question like that and there is no form that says he may not, and both of those have been true since I came up that plank and one of them is the reason I am not going to answer it.*”

“*And I am not going to be told in about four days that I said no. Nobody on this bank said no. Nobody on this bank said yes. A man of thirty-one did not open his mouth and about nine of you are going to call that a thing, and it is not a thing, and there is no word in this county for it and I am not going to make one.*”

About four people on that bank had the mouth that stayed shut and about nine of them had the word there is not.

Then a woman of forty-four said the last thing, and it was about a man and not about a door, and nobody improved on it, and nobody said she was right.

“*A man who does not answer is not a refusal. I said that this morning about a door and I am saying it again about a man, and it is the last time I am going to say either.*”

“*And I am not relieved and nobody is relieved. The man of forty-one has a shut door and the man of thirty-one has a question he did not answer, and those are two different sizes of the same morning.*”

“*And I am not going to put them in a line.*”

The fever was fifty-nine weeks and four days old. He had been in that county a hundred and forty-six days.

Four of the nine terms were put back together out of what was left in them, in about four days, in front of about nine people, and five of the nine are not done. The two positions that are not found were located at the eighth opening and at the ninth room. The surviving record of the burned seventh was recovered off a man in an ash, and it is a remainder and not a copy. A chamber behind a wall in that ninth room has been sealed and nothing fell down.

And the terms were copied into nine boards and carried out of the county, and there is no original and there is no keeper, and a man of fifty-four who cannot read is carrying one of them.

And something that is not a person asked a man who is nobody’s whether he would write it, in plain words, on a Wednesday morning, on a bank, in about nine people’s hearing. He was not asked it twice, he did not answer it, and nobody on that bank answered it for him.
