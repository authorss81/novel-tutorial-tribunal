# Chapter 680: Third Day Of The Hundred And Nineteenth Week, And A Thursday, And On Which The Band Says Out Loud Whether There Are Eight Of Them Or Nine And Does Not Smooth It, And A Man Of Forty-One Adds Up Eight Days Of His Own Standing In Water, And Nobody In Four Counties Has Read The Ninth Line

Third day of the hundred and nineteenth week. His four hundred and thirtieth morning. Two hundred and eighty days after the settlement. The fever fifty-six weeks and five days. Two hundred and thirty-four days since the division. One hundred and eighty days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

About four inches on the top step at the seventh hour, and the man of forty-three said it out loud and said what it is a figure of.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and he has two crossings of a block of limestone in his own hand out of nineteen years and no figure for the gap between them.

“*About four inches. Five days since it came off those steps. The pair of figures says about four days to fill, and I am not going to say the pair of figures again this week. I said on the Wednesday that I was going to stop and I am going to stop.*”

“*And I am going to say the other thing once, because it is the last time anybody has asked me about it in about nine days. The next time those steps can be walked is about four days after that bank is full. The block of limestone is a different thing nine miles up this water, and there is still no figure in four counties for when it comes out. I do not have one and I am not going to make one up on a Thursday morning with the door open.*”

About four people on that bank had no figure and about nine of them had never had one either.

Then the man of sixty-one did three counts on that bank and he said out loud before he began that these were the last three he was going to do on a bank in about nine days, and he gave his reason before he gave the numbers, which is a thing he has started doing.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept the book of heads and carts eleven years. There are four lines in it on the leaf after the last head and there are not five.

“*He has been in this county one hundred and twenty-six days. One hundred and twenty-six is six hundred and eighty less five hundred and fifty-four.*”

“*A boy of thirteen went down on the Friday of the hundred and fifteenth week and has not gone down since, and that is twenty-seven days, and twenty-seven is six hundred and eighty less six hundred and fifty-three.*”

“*A man of thirty-four has been in an ash on the low side of that landing for fifty days. Fifty is six hundred and eighty less six hundred and thirty.*”

“*A woman of twenty-nine has been in a reed on the other side for fifty-one days. Fifty-one is six hundred and eighty less six hundred and twenty-nine.*”

“*And I am not going to add them.*”

Then he said the number of things, and he said it flat, and he did not round it and he did not put it in the book.

“*And there is no number for the hearing. I gave the last of it on the Friday of the hundred and seventeenth week and stopped. I have been asked twice this week whether I have started again and I have not. I am not going to start on a Thursday, because it is the last morning of a week and a man of thirty-one would like it.*”

“*And nobody has granted it and nobody has refused it and nobody has written it down. That is three things that have not moved in about nine days. I have stopped counting one of them and not the other two.*”

Nobody on that bank said a word about that.

Then the man of forty-one said the number of his own, and he said the working, and it is the only figure in that bank that anybody can check against anything but him.

“*Two hours on the Wednesday of last week, and two hours on the Thursday. Two hours and a quarter on the Sunday, and two hours on the Monday. Two hours and a quarter on the Tuesday, and two hours on the Wednesday. That is about twelve hours and a half in eight days.*”

“*And that is a figure of a man’s legs in cold water. It is not a figure of the drain and it is not a figure of the ninth door. I am not going to put it against my name in anybody’s book, and there is nowhere to put it.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for it on this bank and I have said that five times and I am going to stop saying it, which is a different thing from stopping.*”

About four people on that bank had the twelve hours and a half. The rest of them had a man of forty-one who had stopped saying a thing.

Then the man of thirty-one said the number of them out loud, and he said it in the plainest form he could find, and he did not smooth it and nobody asked him to.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and he was nobody’s, and there is no column in the county of Kell with his name at the top of it and no form that renews him and no rate on him and no hundred in that county either.

“*It is eight. That is what it is and I am not going to say nine, because the name is the nine and the number is eight. Those are two different things, and about four people in this bank have been mixing them since the Thursday of the hundred and seventeenth week.*”

“*And it has been eight for nine days. Nine days is six hundred and eighty less six hundred and seventy-one, and a man of sixty-one said on the Thursday of that week that he was not going to go and find a ninth.*”

“*And the name is still the nine, because a man of sixty-one said so on the Saturday of last week, and nobody has changed it and nobody is going to.*”

“*And I would like about four of you to know that I have been the ninth of the nine for nine days.*”

“*And I am nobody’s, and there is no form in this county that says I am.*”

Then he said the four who said they would not first, and he said it flat, and he said that he was not smoothing it and that a man of sixty-one had read it out loud two weeks ago with the doing of each one attached.

“*About four of those eight said they would not first and came anyway. I am not going to give the reasons again, because they have been given. A man of sixty-one gave them with their names beside them, and a mouth is not a figure of anything.*”

“*And one of the four is me, and I am not going to take myself out of it. I am not going to say the eighth job cannot be filled one more time this week. I have said it twice this week already, and a woman of forty-four has told me what the correct number of times to say one thing is.*”

Then a woman of forty-nine said a thing about the number, and she does not know what a copy is, and she has come down that road three times.

“*I want it said that I do not know what eight means. I have said that I do not know what a copy is, and I have said it more times than that. Neither of those is the same as not knowing what eight means.*”

“*I know what nine loaves is and I know what about nine houses is, and that is all I have got. I am not going to pretend it is more than that. I have been in this bank for about nine days and I have liked it, and I do not want to be the reason it goes wrong.*”

About four people on that bank had the loaves. Nobody on that bank said she was a good sport and nobody said she was brave.

Then the things that had not moved were said out loud, one under another, four of them, the way they are said on a Monday.

“*A sheet of deal with a seal on it is lying face down on a barrel nine miles down this water. Nobody has taken it inside. Nobody has burned it and nobody has put a mark on it, and the stone is back on the counter where it came from.*”

“*Nine words are in the heads of about nine people. A man of sixty-six has them in a place nobody can be sent for about and he is four miles off a mill.*”

“*And I am not going to say the nine words again in front of a bank. A woman of twenty-eight told me about that. She said that saying a thing twice in a row is how a saying stops being a thing a man heard.*”

“*And there is a stone about a hundred and thirty feet past the last of about nine beams, with nine lines cut in it. The ninth was cut about nine days ago. Nobody in four counties has read one word of it.*”

“*And there is no form in this county that says a person may be asked to read it out.*”

“*And there is a lamp burning on a hook in the ninth of nine rooms, about a quarter full of oil. Nobody in four counties lit it, and nobody in four counties is going to put it out. I am not going to be the man who says it ought to be put out.*”

Nobody on that bank said a word about any of the four.

Then the man of thirty-one said what he wanted, and there was no number in it and he said so himself, and he did not say it twice.

“*I want to keep asking in a room. That is the ninth of nine and it cannot be filled and I have said that twice this week and I am not going to say it a third time.*”

“*And I am going to say one thing about this week that is mine and not the bank’s.*”

“*I came into this county one hundred and twenty-one days ago wanting a figure for something, and I have left it with nine of them. And I have not put a single one of them on a form.*”

“*Nobody is to tell me that is progress and nobody is to tell me it is not. A woman of twenty-eight has handed me a copy of two numbers in her own hand and I have put it in a coat, and I am not going to say what a coat is for.*”

Then the water came on past that landing, and the man of forty-three said the stone was back under about a foot and a half of water. He said it was a figure of a river and not a figure of a man. And a man of thirty-four was in an ash on the low side of that landing, and a woman of twenty-nine was in the reed on the other side.

And neither of them said anything. About nine people on that bank went past the two of them about nine times, and nobody stopped at either.

---

The fever was fifty-six weeks and five days old. He had been in that county one hundred and twenty-six days.

There are eight of the nine and it has been eight for nine days. The name is still the nine, and the ninth of the nine is a man of thirty-one who is nobody’s. He has said for nine days that there is no form in that county that says he is.

And nine words are still in the heads of about nine people. A stone about a hundred and thirty feet past the last of about nine beams has nine lines in it, and the ninth was cut about nine days ago. Nobody in four counties has read one word of it.
