# Chapter 658: The Second Day Of The Hundred And Sixteenth Week, And A Wednesday, And On Which An Ordinary Man Asks Whether A Notice With A Seal On It Has Settled A Hearing And Is Refused In About Four Sentences, And On Which A Man Of Thirty-One Starts To Answer A Question That A Woman Of Twenty-Eight Asked Once And Is Stopped By Her

Second day of the hundred and sixteenth week. His four hundred and eighth morning. Two hundred and fifty-eight days after the settlement. The fever fifty-three weeks and four days. Two hundred and twelve days since the division. One hundred and fifty-eight days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The yard nine miles up that water had about nine people in it at about the ninth hour on the Wednesday morning.

It is the yard where a notice came up under a stone on a counter nine days ago. It is the yard where a man of thirty-eight asked, in front of about nine people, for a hearing about every record there is.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He put the stone back on the counter where the sheet had been. Nobody had asked him to, and nobody has taken the sheet inside.

“*The stone is where the sheet was. I put it back on the Friday because it weighs about nine pounds, and a counter with a stone on it is a counter and not a mystery.*”

“*About four of you think that is clever. About nine of you do not.*”

Nobody in that yard thanked him for the stone.

Then a man of sixty-one gave the number of the day with the working. A number without a unit is a decoration, and the unit is a hearing.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept the book of heads and carts eleven years. He had it under his arm and he did not open it. The answer is not in it, and he has said that in about nine sentences in a week.

“*A man of thirty-eight asked in this yard on the Monday of the hundred and eleventh week for a hearing about every record there is to be read in one room, the ones that disagree with each other especially.*”

“*It is now the thirtieth day. Thirty is six hundred and fifty-eight less six hundred and twenty-eight, and I have done the subtraction out loud, and I have not done it in a book.*”

“*And nobody granted it and nobody refused it and nobody has written it down.*”

“*About four of you have been saying it out loud in rooms about once a day since the Monday of the eleventh week. That is how a case is kept open in a county with no column for one.*”

About four people in that yard had the thirty. About nine had the stone on the counter.

Then a man of fifty-four who cannot read asked whether the notice has settled it. He said he could not read before he asked. The question was not a stupid one and the yard did not treat it as one.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four. He holds the gravel at the head of that drain and has not walked a quarter mile he holds in nine years. He was in a gallery under about ninety steps of bank on the Monday, and he has said he cannot read three times in nine days.

“*I cannot read and I have said so three times in three days.*”

“*I have been in a room where a man of sixty-one read a line out of a stone in the dark, and about four of us understood it and about nine of us did not.*”

“*There is a sheet of deal with a seal on it lying face down on a barrel nine miles down this water, and it says a hearing is declared for every record there is in all of Veyra.*”

“*And it is the first time in about nine years that anybody has said there is a hearing at all.*”

“*And I am asking whether that notice has settled the thing a man of thirty-eight asked for in this yard. I am asking because I do not know, and I would rather ask than have decided.*”

About four seconds went past in that yard and a cart came out of the gate behind them.

Then a woman of forty refused him in about four sentences. The reason was about four years. She gave the other half as well, and about nine people in that yard had one half and about four had both.

Wenna Callow is forty. She has asked for a day against a name for about nine weeks. She is not on a board and there is no board. She says what everybody is standing in front of before she says anything else.

“*No. It has not settled it, and saying it has not settled it is not the same as saying the notice is nothing.*”

“*A notice that says every person in every county may be spoken about and no person may be sent for about it is a hearing that has said out loud that it does not need you there.*”

“*That is not a hearing you asked for. It is a hearing that has told you what it costs you, and the cost is a day and a journey, and the man of thirty-eight asked for a room.*”

“*And if you take the first for the second, then in about four years the only hearings anybody in this county has will be ones they were not sent for.*”

“*About nine of you will be glad of that and about four of you will not, and I have asked for a day against a name for about nine weeks, and I know which of those I am.*”

About four people in that yard had both halves. About nine had the cost.

“*And here is the other half. I am saying it because a woman of forty-four said no three times in about nine days to writing a hearing in a book, and would not let a thing be tidy.*”

“*The notice is real. It is the first time in about nine years that anybody has said out loud that there is a hearing at all.*”

“*About four of you know that, and about nine of you have decided not to know it. Both of those are permitted and I am not going to pretend they are the same.*”

Nobody in that yard thanked her for the two halves.

Then a man of thirty-one started to answer the question of the whole world. He got about four words out. A woman of twenty-eight stopped him in about four seconds in front of about nine people, and the reason she gave was about herself and not about the yard.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight. She is not of that county and cannot be sent for about in it, and she cannot be sent for about in the county she came from.

She has two letters in a bag with the seals unbroken. She came up that cart road on the Monday of the hundred and thirteenth week.

She had been in that yard twice since the Monday and had said about nine words in it each time.

“*No.*”

About four seconds went past and a cart came out of the gate behind them.

“*I asked a question in a yard nine miles down this water on the Tuesday of the hundred and fifteenth week, and about nine people heard it, and nobody answered it.*”

“*The question is mine. It was asked once. It has been in about four counties since the Friday.*”

“*And you are the one person in four counties that has a line about him in two places, and a public record, and a name in about nine mouths.*”

“*If you answer it then the answer is yours and not mine, and I am not going to hand my own question back to a man who is findable when I am not.*”

“*And I am saying that in a yard with about nine people in it, and about four of them can write, and that is a place where a thing can be written down.*”

“*I am asking about four of you to have the whole of that before anybody says I have stopped a man from speaking.*”

About four people in that yard had the whole of it. The rest of them had a man who was not speaking.

Then the man of thirty-one held it, and said out loud that he was not going to answer it and had not answered it.

The twenty-second time in a row he had said the true of one half of a thing in a room was a Tuesday in a town of about eleven hundred people. About four people in this yard did not know that. About nine of them had no way of finding out.

“*I am not going to answer it, and I would like it said that I have not answered it and that I am not going to.*”

“*And I would like it said that nobody in this yard has answered it either.*”

“*It is the thirtieth day of a hearing about every record there is, and about the thirtieth day of the other one, and neither of them has a person on either side of it. A woman of forty-four said that on the Monday of the hundred and fourteenth week and has said no three times since.*”

About four people in that yard had the two thirties. About nine had a yard.

Then a man of thirty-one asked for a thing to do, out loud, in about nine people’s hearing. A man of about fifty-two who cannot see well refused him in about four sentences.

It was the second refusal anybody in that yard had heard in four days, and the two men had not met before the Wednesday.

Ilyan Vester was thirty-one. He had been in that county one hundred and four days, and that is a count of days since he came in on the evening before the count started. The day he came in is not a day he was in.

“*I am going to ask for work,*” he said, before anybody spoke.

“*About the review. About a body four hundred miles off that has declared a hearing and has not said when it means to hold it. I am asking for something to be given to me to do about it, in front of about nine people, and I am asking now rather than waiting to be offered.*”

“*About four of you will hear a man asking for work. About nine of you will hear a man asking to be sent for.*”

About four people in that yard had the two readings. About nine had a man asking.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well, and everybody in that yard has known that for eleven years.

The fifth line of one of those sheets of deal is his and not the man of forty-three’s, and nobody in that county can be sent for about that fifth line. He offered the thing at a barrel to speak for a man of thirty-one on the Tuesday of the hundred and fourteenth week, which is fifteen days ago. It would have worked in about four days, and the man said no to it in about nine sentences.

“*No.*”

“*The fifth line of a sheet in this county is mine and not the man of forty-three’s, and there is no form in the county of Kell that says a person may be sent for about a fifth line.*”

“*I have been that person for eleven years without knowing it was a thing to be.*”

“*And I offered this bank one thing in about a fortnight, and it would have worked in about four days, and a man of thirty-one said no to it in nine sentences.*”

“*I have not been thanked for the offering and I am not going to be thanked for this one either.*”

“*And the reason is that there is nothing to give you, because about nine of us do not know what a review is.*”

“*If a man of thirty-one were given a thing to do about a thing nobody understands, then in about four days there is a man doing a job about a hearing four hundred miles off, and nobody can be sent for about either of them.*”

“*I know what that looks like. I have been the middle of it.*”

About four people in that yard had the middle of it. About nine had a refusal.

Then a man of sixty-one said what a review date is, and he said it with the working, and what it is a figure of was that it is not one.

“*A body four hundred miles off has declared a hearing for every record there is in all of Veyra.*”

“*It names no room and no county and no keeper. It says the records will be read in one place, and does not say which place, and does not say who keeps them.*”

“*And a body that does not publish its days cannot be late. A man of thirty-one has wanted a figure every day for one hundred and four days. There is no figure for this one and there is not going to be one.*”

“*I say that with the working, and the working is that nobody can check a thing nobody has been told the date of.*”

“*And I am not going to tell you what to do with that.*”

“*I have noticed that about four of you have stopped talking and about nine of you have not. A man of thirty-one who wanted a number has just been told there is not one, in front of about nine people, on a Wednesday.*”

Nobody in that yard thanked him for the figure that was not there.

---

The fever was fifty-three weeks and four days old. He had been in that county one hundred and four days. A hearing about every record there is was asked for in that yard on the Monday of the hundred and eleventh week, and it is the thirtieth day and nobody granted it and nobody refused it.

And an ordinary man of fifty-four who cannot read asked whether a notice with a seal on it had settled it. A woman of forty refused him in about four sentences and gave the other half as well, and said the notice is real, and that about nine of the nine in that yard have decided not to know that.

And a man of thirty-one started to answer the question a woman of twenty-eight asked once, and was stopped by her in about four seconds in front of about nine people. He held it. Nobody in that yard answered it either.

And a man of about fifty-two who cannot see well refused him work in about four sentences, and said the reason is that nobody in that yard knows what a review is.

And a body four hundred miles off does not publish its days, and a man of thirty-one who wanted a number was told in front of about nine people that there is not one.
