# Chapter 432: A Man Who Counts Halls Says No In A Market Instead Of A Room, And The Office Writes It Into A Line With His Ward In It

Week eighty-three, day seven. Shelf day three hundred and seven, the hundred and eighty-second morning, thirty-two days after the settlement, twenty-one weeks and two days of fever, the rail down twenty-two days.

The count form had come back down the hill on the same cart as the roll, and it had three lines on it where it had two, and the third was in a hand nobody in this city had seen before.

The market stands on the flat where Tallowgate widens at the bottom, and it stands there on the seventh day of the week, and about two hundred people were on the flat with it when Ilyan got there with the form in his hand and a leg he had been careful with for four miles.

“*Read it out,*” said Skell. “*Standing up. There is a man here who cannot come to a room at the foot of a hill and there is a man here who can, and I want the one who can to hear it and the one who cannot to hear it.*”

“*I will read it out,*” said Ilyan, “*and I am going to read all three lines twice, and I am not going to say anything about them until I have read them twice, because I have learned in four weeks that the first time a thing is read it is a rumour and the second time is a record.*”

He read the first two lines. The first gave nine hundred doors in the ward of this city, by street, from a man who has counted them. The second said that the number of persons was not given.

Then he read the third line twice, and the market went quiet in a way that markets do not usually go quiet.

“*Where a person of this place declines to give the number of persons, the declining is made by that person and is signed by that person, and the ward in which the person is is named at the foot of the line, and the office notes the declining and does not press for the number.*”

“*Say it again,*” said Skell.

Somebody at the back said he could not hear, and Skell said that was the right answer and told him to move up, and about two hundred people on a flat rearranged themselves and Ilyan read the third line a third time.

“*That is correct,*” said Skell.

“*Say that again for the people who moved up.*”

“*It is correct, and I have been standing here for four minutes working out why, and I have got it, and I am going to say that it is correct.*” Skell had his hands in his coat. “*A return is made by a person of this place. If the person of this place declines, then the declining is a thing of this place too, and the office is right that it can only be made by the person who made the return and not by anybody else in the ward standing behind her.*”

“*And the ward at the foot of the line,*” said Ilyan.

“*And the ward at the foot of the line,*” said Skell, “*and I will come to that and I will come to it slowly, because I have been walking towards it for four miles and I have got a good deal of it ready and I would rather it came out of me in the right order.*”

---

Ilyan had one want and it was a clean one and he did not say it, which cost him about nine minutes of the morning.

He wanted a refusal that could not be written down. He had spent four weeks learning that everything anybody in this city said in a room went up a hill in a coat inside a fortnight.

He had worked out over two days and two nights that a thing said in a room is copied, and a thing said in a market is repeated, and a thing repeated by thirty people is a thing that thirty people own. A thing thirty people own cannot be taken out of their mouths by a clerk four hundred miles off.

He also worked out, and he did not like working it out, that the only reason a refusal in a market is safe is that nobody is asking for it. The office was asking for it. It had sent a line down a hill in a fair hand saying that a declining has to be signed, and there is a version of a no that is a signature and a version of a no that is a market, and the office had built the first one and left the second one alone on purpose, and he could not say whether it was on purpose.

“*Then here is the thing I want to say,*” Skell said, at the edge of a stall where nobody was selling anything in particular. “*I have been asked for the number of persons in this ward nine times in four weeks. By a man of the seat in a room, by a woman of sixty reading a sheet out, by a man with a bad leg four times, and once by a form.*”

“*Say the number of times you have said no,*” said Ilyan.

“*Five. In this market I am going to say it a sixth time, and I am going to say it out loud, and I am going to say it where about two hundred people can hear me, and I am not going to say it quietly in a room to four people this morning because a room is a thing that gets written down and I have had four of those.*”

“*You are going to be asked for it in writing,*” Ilyan said, “*and there is a line at the foot of that form with a ward in it and your name above it, and if you say no in a market and a man of the seat writes down that you said no in a market, then there are two noes, and one of them is legible.*”

“*I have worked that out on four miles of flat road,*” said Skell. “*Say it anyway, and then say the other half, and then get out of the way of a man who is about to do the thing he came here to do.*”

---

He got out of the way.

Skell did not put his hands flat on anything, because there was nothing flat. He stood in the middle of about two hundred people and said the request out loud before he said the answer, which is a thing he does, and the market stopped buying for about forty seconds and then did not start again for about three minutes.

“*The number of persons in this ward is not given, and it is not going to be given, and the person saying so is the man who counted the nine hundred doors and is the only man here who can be asked about a hall.*”

He waited, and nobody filled the gap, which is a thing about a market and not about a room.

“*And here is the half of it that the form wants.*” He turned round so that his back was to the low row and his face was to the flat. “*I have been told to sign a line saying I decline. I am not going to sign it, and I want that said where two hundred people heard me, and I am not going to pretend that the reason I will not sign it is that I have anything clever.*”

“*Say the reason,*” said Ilyan, from about four feet behind him, quietly, and a woman at the second stall turned round and looked at both of them.

“*The reason is that the line has a ward in it.*” Skell said it to the flat. “*Nine hundred doors in the ward of this city. I have said no as a man five times in four weeks and a man can be asked what a man said. That line is going to say that the ward of this city declines to give a number of persons, and a ward is not a man, and a ward cannot be asked anything, and a ward cannot say no back.*”

“*Say the second half of that,*” Ilyan said.

“*The second half is that I have gone out of a room and into a market to say a no that I cannot now get back into a room, and I have got it out here where there is no clerk, and about thirty people will have it by the last of the light, and about nine hundred doors in this ward will have heard it by tomorrow.*”

Somebody in the crowd said *nine hundred doors*.

“*Nine hundred doors,*” said Skell, “*and I am the one who counted them, and I want everybody to write that down who has a hand that is any use.*”

Nobody wrote it down. About nine people tried.

---

The form came back into the press on the ground floor of a building at the middle of the nine bridges at a penny a page, and Ferrow set it in the window of the room so that anybody who came up that road could read all three lines for a penny, and he wrote the same three lines fair on the top sheet of a copy, and the copy is not the form and the form is not in this city at all.

It went up the hill that evening on a cart with a parcel of nothing much, and it came back down on the third day of the eighty-fourth week, and the third line had this on it under the fair hand, at the foot, in the space left for it:

*Declined. The ward of this city.*

Ilyan read it out in the market on the third day of the eighty-fourth week, standing where Skell had stood, and about a hundred and forty people were on the flat, which is fewer than the two hundred, because it is raining off the flat and does not stop.

“*Say the rest of it,*” said Skell.

“*The rest of it is that there is no name in it.*” Ilyan had his thumb on the line. “*The office has required a signature and has not got one, and instead of the name it has the ward, and the ward is correct, because the ward is nine hundred doors and the doors are the thing that was given.*”

“*Say the other half of that,*” said Skell.

“*The office has taken a no that a man said out loud on a flat with two hundred people in it and has put it in a line as a thing said by nine hundred doors.*” He put his hand down. “*And it is legible. That is what I asked it not to be, and I asked it not to be by choosing a market, and a market is the one place in this city that cannot be kept out of a book, and I chose it anyway, and I have known what I was choosing since the flat and I did it.*”

“*Then say who chose it,*” said Skell.

“*Say that again.*”

“*Say who chose the market.*” Skell did not raise his voice and about a hundred and forty people heard him. “*I have been asked all morning by a man with a bad leg whether I would say a no where a clerk could not hear it. I said yes and I said I wanted to know what he was going to do with the answer, and he told me, and I listened, and then I stood on this flat and did it.*”

The flat stayed quiet for about four seconds, and then two men at the back of it said something to each other that nobody heard, and the market started again.

“*Then take it back,*” said Ilyan.

“*No,*” said Skell. “*I am fifty-two and I have counted halls and doors for twenty years and I have said yes to a man who offered me a market and I am not going to be the man in this city who is three kinds of coward in a fortnight. I have had a legible no. It is the first one I have ever had and I would like it noticed that I got it by saying the thing out loud, which is the only method I have.*”

He looked at the line on the form, which he could not see, and which Ilyan was holding up on the flat in the rain.

“*And the trade is mine and I am not going to hand it back to him in the middle of a wet market because he did not like the price of it.*”

---

The third line of a form came back down the hill with a ward in it, and the ward is nine hundred doors, and there is no number of persons in it, and there is not going to be one.

At the last of the light a woman at the fourth stall asked Skell whether a ward could say no, and he said that he did not know and would not guess in a wet market, and then he said the one thing he knew.

“*A ward cannot say no and a ward cannot be asked anything and that is the same fact, and it is the only fact I have got about this, and I have had it since the second hour.*”

The form says the declining is made and signed by a person of this place. The form says the ward is named at the foot of the line. Both of those are true, and there is a person and there is a ward, and only one of the two of them can be written in.

The fever was twenty-one weeks and two days old, and it did not stop for a market, and about nine hundred doors had heard a man say no and none of them had been asked what they thought.
