# Chapter 483: A Wednesday On Which The Water Board Of A Town Of Four Thousand People Sits For The First Time Since The Thirtieth Day After The Division And Answers The Two Lines Of A Request In The Words Of The Request And Is Right, And Nine Men Find Out That Being Right Is Not The Same As Being Able To Do Anything, And A Man Of Thirty-One Says A Sentence In A Room Over A Saddler’s Shop For The Third Room In This Town It Has Been Said In And It Has Become A Thing The Room Uses

Third day of the ninety-first week. His two hundred and thirty-third morning. Eighty-three days after the settlement. The fever twenty-eight weeks and four days. Thirty-seven days since the division. Thirty-one days in this town.

The water board of Halloway sits in a room over a saddler’s shop at the north end of the market square, and the stair outside it is the width of one man. There were about thirty people in that room and on that stair on the Wednesday morning of the thirty-seventh day after the division, and about nine of the thirty were from the Fen.

Two things were on the table and only one of them was a request.

The first was a sheet of paper with a head on it and two lines under the head in a copyist’s hand, and the head named the water board of this town, the House of this town, the office of the county surveyor at Kerby, any person in the county of Ostrey having power in the matter, and nobody else.

The second was a sheet of paper with a head on it and nothing under the head at all, and it had come into the room in the same hand as the first and the man who had written the head was standing at the end of the table.

Nobody in that room would have said the second thing was a document and nobody in that room said it was not one.

---

Wenna Crale read the two lines out at about the ninth hour, standing at the end of the table, in the voice she uses for the minutes, and it took her about forty seconds, and about nine people in that room heard every word of it and about twenty heard it from the stair.

Then she read the answer out, and it was in the words of the request, and it was the first thing in five weeks that a body of this town had put in a minute in the words of a thing it had been asked in.

*First. That this board has no power to appoint a person to hear this matter, and cannot appoint, and has no list from which to appoint, and that the reason is given at length in the second paper on this table, and that the reason is correct.*

*Second. That this board cannot undertake that any person who comes to an arbitration in a room in this town will be asked nothing touching his character, his rate, or his household.*

*And that the reason is that this board has asked a man of fifty-four in a room what year he had been in the water, and that this board put a requirement into a minute of the thirtieth day after the division naming a woman of thirty-eight of this town, and that both of those things were done by this board and neither of them was a form.*

About thirty people in a room and on a stair above a shop did not make a sound.

“*Go on and say the rest of that, and do not make it a speech.*” said Ilyan Vester, “*because about nine of the men on that table have just heard a body refuse a thing correctly, and about nine of the people on that stair have just heard a body refuse a thing about people. The second of those is the one I would like somebody in this room to say something about.*”

---

And about four of the nine said something about it, and they said it for about eleven minutes, and none of the four had said it before, and what they said came out of them one at a time and not in the order anybody expected.

The first was a man of about fifty-three who had been on that board for six years and had a small holding on the west side.

“*I have been right five times in this room since the division. I voted against the ninth clause being read out of the book on the tenth day, and it was read out of the book on the tenth day. I voted for the gate being shut on the eleventh day and it was shut too long. I voted for the fourteen pounds and it was three and a halfpence a lot. I voted for the roll being corrected in the fourth week and it had to be corrected three times.*”

“*Go on and say the rest of it and do not make it a speech.*”

“*And I voted for nineteen houses to be put out of the water on the thirtieth day and I was right. The cause was set out in writing and a man of sixty-one was in the water himself in the ninth year before the division. I have not slept since the Wednesday and I would do it again this morning.*”

“*Say the other half of that and say it in this room.*”

“*And I have not once, in five weeks, been able to make anything happen. There is no form in the county of Ostrey that says a body of nine men may be right and not be able to do a thing. I have never in six years on this board had that put in front of me, and I am not sure that being right and being able to do anything are two different things until somebody says them out loud in a room.*”

---

And that is what happened, and it happened nine times over about eleven minutes, in a room over a saddler’s shop with a stair the width of one man outside it.

The first man said it, and then eight other men found out that each of them had been carrying a half of it for five weeks. None of them had put the two halves together, because the halves are only two halves when somebody says them in a room.

Being right is not the same as being able to do anything.

The board of Halloway has been right about the arithmetic for nine years. The board of Halloway has been right about the cause, about the nine pounds, about the form in the steward’s drawer, and about the days. It has a second book that has never been written in. It put a word into a minute of this town in the four hundredth year of the covenant and struck it out of the same minute in the room.

And in five weeks it has not stopped a bar four feet long from going unwound, or entered a household on a roll, or taken nineteen households off one, or made a single person in this town do a thing they had not already decided to do.

And the board cannot be asked about any of it, because a board is a thing a person can be sent for, and nothing in this county can be sent for.

---

And then Ilyan Vester said the sentence, and he said it in a room over a saddler’s shop for the third time in this town since he coined it in a market square in the rain, and it is the oldest thing in this band and it was six weeks old on the Wednesday.

“*A corrected roll is a corrected constituency,*” he said. “*Not a corrected count. A constituency. And a constituency is a set of doors.*”

About nine men on the fourth stool’s row had heard it before this morning. About four of them had it right.

“*Say the rest of that and go on to the end of it.*” said Ordwin Cape, from the rail, where he had come in out of the wet.

“*Say the count out loud, and say what it is a count of, and then say how many times you have said it and to how many rooms.*”

“*It is in about forty mouths in this town and about four of them have it right. One man in this town has it wrong and has had it wrong since the Sunday of the twentieth day, and has said it out loud in two public places.*”

“*Say the rest of it and do not make it a speech.*”

“*The wrong version is that the fen is nineteen more seats. I put that version in about forty mouths in a square in the rain, and I knew about four minutes after I had said it, and I have had four weeks to say so in a room and I have not.*”

Nobody in that room said anything for about a minute.

“*Say the rest of that and do not make it a speech.*”

“*And I am not going to say it in this room either. The reason is that this is the third room in this town that the sentence has been said in, and about nine of the men at that table have heard it three times, and about four of them have it right.*”

“*Go on and say the rest of that.*”

“*I have watched it happen in a square and in a stone room. A sentence a room can say is not a thing a room can hear, and there is no form in the county of Ostrey that says when a sentence has stopped working.*”

Perdy Sallow stood up from the fourth stool, which he had not done in five weeks.

“*Say the rest of that in your own mouth and do not make it a speech,*” he said, “*and then say the thing I have been carrying since the sixteenth day after the division, and we will have two of them in one room for the first time in this town.*”

“*Go on.*”

“*I wrote a day on a sheet in my own kitchen on the sixteenth day after the division and I set it against the twenty-fifth day. I wrote a day on a sheet in my own kitchen on the twenty-second day and I set it against the twenty-fourth. I have called neither of them.*”

“*Go on and say the rest of it.*”

“*I have said both of those things out loud in this town and I have never said the two of them in the same room until this morning.*”

“*Say the rest of it and do not make it a speech.*”

“*And what I have learned from a man of thirty-one saying a sentence in my room for the third time is that a day I write down is not a day I have called. A day nobody called is the same as a day that was never written, and this board has had five weeks of days that were never written, and about nine men at this table have been going about five weeks believing that they were doing something.*”

---

And then the number of days came out, and it came out because a man of fifty-four stood up on the stair and asked for it, and it is the only number anybody asked for that day and it is the number the whole of this business is made of.

“*Say the number out loud and say what it is a number of,*” said Ilyan Vester.

“*Seven days out of the water,*” said Ivo Cray. “*They were put out on the Wednesday of the thirtieth day after the division and it is the Wednesday of the thirty-seventh, and that is seven days.*”

“*Go on and say the rest of that.*”

“*And the bar has not been turned for seven days. The sixth hour of the Thursday of the thirty-first day was the first one missed, and this morning is the sixth hour of the thirty-seventh day. A bar is counted in days on which an hour came and went, and a wheel is counted in days completed. The wheel at the mill has been slow for six days and the bar is on its seventh, and those are not the same seven, and the difference between them is a Wednesday.*”

“*Say the other half of that and say it in this room.*”

“*And the Wednesday is the day this board sat. I voted for it and I voted for the frame being a season and not two years. A man of the county measured three-eighths of an inch in nine years with a straight edge and a nail at the eleventh hour of a Friday in the rain, and would not un-say a number in front of twenty people to say so.*”

Nobody in that room and on that stair said anything at all for about a minute.

“*Say the rest of that and do not make it a speech,*” said Sefa Lund, from the back of the room, where she had been standing since the eighth hour with about eight other people of the row.

“*Nine pounds is what a new heel is at the county figures. “*Eight pounds and fifteen shillings and fourpence is a quarter of the water of this town out of every household in it, and that is in the board’s own hand. The difference is fifty-six pence a quarter.*”

“*Go on and say the rest of it and do not make it a speech.*”

“*Half a penny on each of the eighty lots is forty pence, and that was the whole of the difference on the twenty-sixth day after the division, and it is not the whole of it now. There are sixteen pence of it that no lot in this county can be made to find.*”

“*Go on and say what the sixteen pence is.*”

“*It is not one thing. Fifty-six pence is thirty-eight pence and eighteen pence. The thirty-eight pence is the twelve shillings and eightpence a year that nineteen houses brought into that fund for ten days, and they were the only people in Halloway who had ever offered to pay.*”

“*Go on and say the rest of that.*”

“*It went out of the fund on the Wednesday in about four minutes, in a form with a day on it, and nobody was asked to make it. The fund is thirty-five pounds and one shilling and fourpence a year, and a quarter of it is eight pounds and fifteen shillings and fourpence, and thirty-eight pence of that quarter went out on the Wednesday in about four minutes.*”

“*Go on and say the eighteen pence.*”

“*The eighteen pence is what was short before the Wednesday. The heel is nine pounds, and the quarter in this board’s hand was eight pounds and eighteen shillings and sixpence on the twenty-ninth day after the division, and that was eighteen pence short. It is fifty-six pence short now.*”

“*Go on and say the rest of it and do not make it a speech.*”

“*A man of thirty-four in a square on that Wednesday said the difference was one woman of thirty-eight and a bar four feet long, and he was right about the woman.*”

---

The board did the only thing it had, and it took about four minutes, and about nine men in that room watched nine men do it.

It entered the two lines, and the two answers, and the reason for each answer at length, and the standing of about nine people from the Fen in that room.

Their standing was that they are not a party, because the request is a request of this town and not a request of the row. And a request of the row was entered in a minute of the nineteenth day after the division and was acted on in about four minutes on the Wednesday of the thirtieth. About nine people in that room have worked out what that means and none of them has said it.

It entered the third line, which was in Wenna Crale’s own hand and in a different ink, and it was one line, and she read it out and did not say whose it was.

*That a sentence a room can say is not a thing a room can hear, and that this board has had five such sentences in five weeks, and that the day a sentence of this town stops working nobody will be told.*

About thirty people in a room and on a stair above a shop in a town of four thousand people did not make a sound.

The fever was twenty-eight weeks and four days old. A minute of a water board of a market town carries a head and two lines and two answers, a reason for each answer, and a third line in a copyist’s hand in a different ink.

About nine people from the Fen are in that minute for the first time in their lives. They are in it as persons the board had to say were in the room, and it is the first entry that has ever been made against any of them rather than for them, and it is a thing a person can be asked about, and none of the nine of them can be.
