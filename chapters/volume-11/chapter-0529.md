# Chapter 529: The Sixth Day Of The Ninety-Seventh Week, And A Sunday On Which A Man Of Fifty-Four Of The County Rides Nine Miles Down To Make A Return About A Disagreement Between Two Towns Which Is The First Return Anybody In This County Has Ever Made That Does Not Record An Answer To A Question, And Which Records Two Answers, And The Form Has No Line For Two So It Is Written On The Back, And A Man Of Fifty-Four Signs The Back And Does Not Sign The Front, And A Man Of Thirty-One Is In The Lane When It Is Made And Cannot Be In The Room

Sixth day of the ninety-seventh week. His two hundred and seventy-ninth morning. One hundred and twenty-nine days after the settlement. The fever thirty-five weeks and one day. Eighty-three days since the division. Twenty-nine days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town two days from where he is standing.

Nine miles. A cart does it in about two hours on a good road, and Simon Verge has a horse, and there is no road to ride it on. So it is a horse at a walk and about three hours.

He came down on the Wednesday morning of last week and would not stay the night, and he left a copy of a return on the step of a porch with no name on it.

He came down on the Sunday morning of this one with a sheet of paper folded in four in the inside of his coat, and it was not the copy of a return. It was a form.

---

The county office at Aldwick keeps one book of returns and one book of orders and can make a return and nothing else.

A return is four lines and it is made out of an answer. The form says that the person is asked in respect of the matter, and that the person gave answer, and that the said answer was made use of, and that the office has no further particular in the matter.

That is the shape of it and it has been the shape of it for a hundred and forty-one years and longer. It does not say what the question was, and it does not say which answer was correct, because a return is not a thing that has a correct answer in it. It has an answer in it and a statement that the answer was used.

There is no line for two answers on that form. There is no column in the book of returns for a disagreement between two towns, and there is no column in it for a person who does not remember, and there is no form in the county of Aldwick that says a person may be sent for to be asked about any of that.

He said that in the porch at about the half hour after the tenth, in about nine sentences, standing, with the form in his hand and not opened.

“*I have brought this down because the office can make a return and nothing else, and because a return is the only thing this county makes, and because there is now a thing in this town that two towns remember differently and it is going to be asked about in Aldwick in about a year by somebody who is not any of us.*”

“*Then make it,*” said Ilyan Vester.

“*No,*” said Simon Verge, and he said it the way he said the other thing in a porch nine days ago, which is to say it without any weight on it at all.

---

That is the whole of the refusal and it took about four seconds, and then he gave the rest of it, standing on a step, in the open air, in front of about nine people.

“*A return is made by an office. It is not asked for by a person. If a man in this county asks an office for a return then the office has been asked for a return, and there is a difference between that and a thing somebody wanted.*”

“*The difference is about eleven years, and it is the difference between a return and an application, and this county has no applications and has never had any, and there is no form for one.*”

“*Then how is one made.*”

“*A return is made when there is a matter to return upon. There has been a matter in this county for a hundred and forty-one years and it was made use of and the line is struck and there is no further particular, and the return is on the shelf where it has always been.*”

“*And nobody has ever asked me to make another one and I have not made one.*” He turned the form over. “*What I have brought down is a form and not a return, and a form is a piece of paper an office uses to make a return out of a matter.*”

“*If a person in this county hands me a piece of paper and a sentence and asks me to fill a form in with it, then the office has been handed a matter by a person, and the return made out of it is a return into a matter, and the office has no power to make a return into a matter, and there is nobody in this county I could send that to and no form in it.*”

“*You have made a return before,*” said the man of thirty-one. “*You made a copy of one and you put it on a step of a porch, and it had no name on it, and that is a thing nobody in this county has ever done before, and you did it in a coat nine miles on a Sunday.*”

“*I made a copy of a return that already existed, and the office gives a copy of a return to anybody who asks in writing, and nobody has ever asked.*”

“*I have had the power to give that copy for about eleven years and I did not know it until about a fortnight ago, and neither did the office.*” He did not open the form.

“*Making a copy of a thing that exists is not the same as making the thing. And that is the whole of the difference, and I have ridden nine miles to say it out loud on a step in about nine sentences, and I would like it entered that I said it out loud.*”

“*Because in about eleven years somebody in this county is going to want a return about a Thursday, and they are going to ask me, and I am going to say this again.*”

“*And if we do not make one.*”

“*Then in about a year there will be a return in that office about this matter with four lines on it and an answer in it, and the answer will be the answer the household of Kiln Row gave a hundred and forty-one years ago.*”

“*Because that is the only answer this county has ever had in respect of the common above this town. And somebody will have copied it, and a person will be told that a man of about seventy-two was asked a question in a square in another town on the thirtieth day after the division.*”

“*And nobody will be able to say that both of those are in the same four lines, because the four lines do not say what the question was.*”

---

And that is what happened at about the second hour of the afternoon, in a room eight feet by eleven with the door open and the window down.

The man of thirty-one was not in it.

He was in the lane. That is the whole of what happened to him on that Sunday, and it took about nine minutes to establish and about four minutes for him to understand it and the rest of the day to be in a lane.

The rule is that a return is made about a person, and the person is in the return, and a person may be asked about a thing said in a room.

A man of thirty-one is on nobody’s book in two counties and can be sent for from neither. About nine people in that lane worked that out in about four seconds, and about nine of them had been saying for a fortnight that he could not be in a room where a book is read, and about four of those nine had understood why.

He said it out loud, twice, in the lane, to the recordwright, and she gave him the same answer both times, and about four people standing in that lane heard both of the answers.

“*There is a form in this county that says who may be in a room when a return is made, and I have it in my hand because I have read it about eleven years, and it says the officer and the person the return is about. You are neither.*”

“*And on the Saturday the leaf was read you offered to read it and I refused you in about two seconds, and I have understood that in four seconds every time since, and I have watched you do it eleven times.*”

“*Then I am in the lane.*”

“*You are in the lane, and about nine people in that lane know that you are in the lane because you said it out loud twice.*”

“*And in about eleven years there will be a person in this town who says the man of thirty-one was not in the room when the return was made, and that will be true, and he will not know that you were thirty feet away in a lane for about an hour with about nine people. I cannot help you with that, and I have told you so on a step before.*”

---

What he heard through the open door, and about nine people in the lane heard with him, was this.

Simon Verge made the return in about nine minutes standing at a table in a room eight feet by eleven, in the voice he uses for minutes, which is not loud and is not slow either. It is the only instrument this county has and there is no other one.

He read out the front of the form first, and it is the ordinary front, and it is four lines, and three of them are the ordinary three.

*That the household of Kiln Row in Bramblefold, being asked by this office in respect of the matter of the common above the town, gave answer that the said common was not used.*

*That the said answer was made use of.*

*That the line of the said household is struck from the roll of descent of Bramblefold in respect of the said answer.*

He stopped there, and about four people in that room did not know why he had stopped, and about nine of the people in the lane did not know either.

Then he read the fourth line, and he read it as it is on the form, and then he said out loud that the fourth line was a lie of convenience.

“*That is the fourth line of a hundred and forty-one returns in that book and it means that this office was not told anything else. I am not going to write it on a return about a disagreement between two towns.*”

“*I am going to write, in the ordinary hand, that this office has a further particular, and that what it is, is on the back of this sheet.*”

“*And there is no line on the front of this form for a further particular and there is no column in that book for one, and I am therefore writing it on the back, and the back of a return is not part of a return, and I am going to sign the back and not the front.*”

“*You cannot do that,*” said a man of about thirty-five in that room.

“*I can do it, and it is the only thing I have been able to do in about eleven years in that office, and it is not a power. It is a space.*”

“*The back of that sheet is not ruled and there is nothing on the form about the back, and if a person in about a year comes into that office and asks for the return about this matter, the clerk will hand them the front, because that is what a clerk does.*”

“*And the front will say in respect of the matter of the common above the town, answer that the said common was not used, answer made use of, and this office has a further particular which is on the back.*”

“*Then they will not see the back.*”

“*They will not see the back unless somebody asks, and there is no form in this county that says a person may be asked whether there is a further particular.*”

“*And that is the best thing I have been able to do with a thing I am not allowed to do anything with, and I have been doing it for nine minutes and I would like to go home.*”

Then he wrote the back, and it took about four minutes, and he read it out.

*That in the matter of the thirtieth day after the division, being a Wednesday, this office has received two accounts of one morning and that the office has made no return upon either and holds no return to make.*

*That the first account is of four words written in a margin of the roll of descent of Bramblefold, in a hand a hundred and forty-one years old, which read struck by order, and that no order is named on the said margin, and that the said four words name no person and no day.*

*That the second account is of four sentences written out from memory by a person of Bramblefold in about four minutes of sitting in the square of a town in another county, and that the said four sentences name a man of Bramblefold of about seventy-two years as having been asked a question in the said square and given an answer, and that the said person has said that he does not remember whether he was in the said square.*

*That this office records both of the said accounts and that neither of them is made use of by this office, and that this office does not find that either of them is correct or incorrect, and that this office has no power to find that.*

*That in the year of the said leaf this office made a return in the ordinary form, and that the said return records that an answer was given and that the answer was made use of, and that the said return does not say what the question was.*

*That this office has been asked by no person to make a return upon the present matter, and that this office has made one, and that this office records that it made one.*

About nine people in that room heard all six lines and about four of them heard the last one, which is the only one that is about this office and not about the morning.

Then he signed the back, and he did not sign the front. The front went into a leather wallet and the back went into his coat, and about nine people in that room saw him do both of those things and about four of them understood why.

“*The front is a hundred and forty-one years old in its wording and I am not going to put a man’s name on it about a morning I have not been in,*” he said.

“*And if a person in about a year asks for that return they will get the front, and the front will be a return about a Tuesday a hundred and forty-one years back, and that is what the office has got.*”

“*The back is in a coat and there is no form that says it may be asked for.*”

He said the other half of it to the man of thirty-one, in the lane, and not to the room, and he said it because he had carried it nine miles.

“*You asked for it to be made. It has been made. And a return is not a thing anybody can carry, and there is no form in this county that says a person may be sent for about one.*”

“*I have put mine in my coat and I will take it home and it will go in a drawer in a house nine miles from here, and if I am asked about it in a year I will say yes, and there will be nothing anybody can do about that.*”

The fever was thirty-five weeks and one day old. Nine miles off there is an office that keeps one book of returns and one book of orders, and a return was made in a room with the door open about a disagreement between two towns over a morning.

It is the first one anybody in that county has made that does not record an answer to a question, and it records two, and the form has no line for two, and it is on the back, and the back is signed and the front is not.

And a man of thirty-one stood in a lane thirty feet away for about an hour with about nine people and could not be in the room.
