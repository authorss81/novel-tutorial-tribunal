# Chapter 548: The Fourth Day Of The Hundredth Week, And A Friday On Which A Man Of Thirty-One Asks In A Room With The Door Open In Front Of About Nine People For The Leaf Of Forty-Three Names To Be Struck Back, And Is Refused In About Four Sentences By A Weaver Of Sixty-Two, And Then A Name Goes In At The Back Of A Book Four Hundred And Eleven Years Old On A Fresh Leaf Of The Same Paper As The Leaf The Copy Of The Four Pages Is Entered On, In The Ordinary Hand, With The Day On It, And Nothing Is Ruled And Nothing Is Headed, And The Name Is Said Aloud In That Room By The Weaver And Written Down By A Woman Who Can Be Sent For, And Nothing Is Carried

Fourth day of the hundredth week. His two hundred and ninety-eighth morning. One hundred and forty-eight days after the settlement. The fever thirty-seven weeks and six days. One hundred and two days since the division. Forty-eight days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town two days from where he is standing.

The room was the chancel, eight feet by eleven, with the door open and the window down. The book was in the vestry cupboard with the door shut on the key on a string, because what happened in that room at about the eleventh hour of this Friday was not a reading of it.

The leaf was not out. It has not been out since the Saturday of the ninety-fifth week, when it was read in that room in about eleven minutes in front of about nine people and came out of it as a sheet of paper with forty names on it and four gaps in it.

About nine people were in the room. A man of eighty-one was in the chair by the fire and had not been sent for. A woman of sixty-eight of the upper row was at the side of the window. A woman of about forty from the wool bench. A man of about thirty-four from a town four days off, at the back.

Abel Tarn was at the end. He had not been sent for and nobody had told him what the room had been opened for.

---

The keeper of the book said the terms out loud before anything else happened in it, standing at the table with her key in her hand, and she said them to the room and not to the weaver, and about nine people heard all of them.

“*Nobody is going to be asked anything today.*”

“*Then what is happening.*”

“*A name is going in at the back of that book. That is the whole of it and it has taken me four days to get to a thing I can say in nine sentences.*”

She put her hand flat on the table. There was a leaf on it and it was not the leaf.

“*A fresh leaf of the same paper as the leaf the copy of the four pages is entered on. I ruled nothing on it this morning and I have not headed it, and there is no column in this book for a name going back in and I am not going to rule one.*”

“*Say the rest of it, because you have said three sentences and about four people in this room have worked out that you are about to do a thing and about nine think it is a fifth column.*”

“*The person descended from the first name on that leaf is in this room. I am going to ask him for the name before I write it.*”

“*And if he says no.*”

“*Then I do not write it. I have said in a lane behind this town that two people in four hundred and eleven years have declined something and that it is not a form.*”

About four seconds went past in that room, and the man of thirty-one did not move and neither did anybody else in it.

“*Then say the first name on that leaf,*” said Abel Tarn.

“*That is why I asked.*”

“*I am going to say it. A leaf of descent is ruled in the same four columns as this book, and it is filled from the eldest at the top, and the first name on it is the eldest of that household. It is my grandfather’s.*”

“*Say the rest of it.*”

“*My father told me it was struck before I was born, and told me it was struck on a Tuesday, and that is the whole of what my house has ever had to say about it.*”

“*Say it out loud, then, in this room, and say it slowly, because I am going to write it down and I do not want it out of you by accident.*”

He said it. It took him about four seconds and it is the first time in this county that anybody has said that name in a room with about nine people in it.

She wrote it at the back of that book, on the fresh leaf, in the ordinary hand, with the day on it.

---

Then the man of thirty-one made the mistake, and he made it in front of about nine people in a room eight feet by eleven, and about four of them had known he was going to make it before he opened his mouth.

“*Then strike the leaf back.*”

About four seconds went past in that room. The woman of sixty-eight of the upper row took her hand off the back of the chair she was not sitting on.

“*Say the rest of that, because a keeper has to be able to write down what a person asked and I will not write four words.*”

“*Strike the leaf back. Put the forty-three back together, put the four names back that were struck out of the copy in a square, and let the leaf read as it read a hundred and forty-one years back. That is the whole of what I came to this country for and I have said so in about nine rooms in five weeks and I have been refused in every one of them.*”

The weaver refused him in about four sentences and he did not raise his voice and he had not raised it once in a hundred and forty-eight days.

“*No.*

“*That leaf is the only account anybody in this county has of the largest thing that has ever happened in it, and there is no other one, and I am not going to have it tidied.*”

“*Say the rest of it, because that is two sentences and I have been refused three times since Saturday and I know what is coming.*”

“*Four people struck their own names out of it in a square and one of them asked that it not be in a book, and she is sixty-eight and she is standing at that window, and that request is thirty days old and it has not been withdrawn and nobody has asked her about it.*”

He put his hand flat on the table, the way a man puts his hand on a loom.

“*A page with the whole of the forty-three on it and nothing else is a page about a Tuesday. A page with a household’s answer on it as well is a page about an answer, and I have read both of them and I would not know which town I was standing in.*”

“*And the fourth sentence.*”

“*I am the last of a name and I have been the last of a name for nine years, and I can stand it another nine.*”

Barnaby Rill understood all four sentences inside about four seconds from the chair and did not say anything. About nine people in that room heard a man of sixty-two refuse a man of thirty-one.

Nobody in that room thanked anybody.

---

Then she said the thing that happened instead, and it happened in about four minutes, and it was the third thing she had said in a room in five weeks that had become a thing in that book. The first had been an entry she wrote on the Sunday and a man from four days off asking whether it might be broken. The second had been a man of about seventy-two saying no in a porch on Wednesday.

“*Then it goes in at the back and not through the front.*”

“*Say the rest of that.*”

“*I am going to say what I am going to write before I write it, and about nine people in this room will hear all of it. There is no form in the county of Aldwick that says a keeper has to ask anybody whether she may enter a thing at the back of her own book.*”

She read it out standing, in the voice she uses for minutes, and she did not read it out of the book.

“*That a household of this town was asked a question a hundred and forty-one years back by an office nine miles off, and gave an answer, and the answer was made use of.*”

“*That the household gave the answer that was asked of it and there is nothing in the county of Aldwick to say that it did not.*”

“*That the leaf the answer was made use of upon is not struck back, and is not to be struck back, and stands with one stroke in a hand heavier than any other in this book and four words in the margin that name no order and no day.*”

“*That the person descended from the first name on that leaf has been told, in this room, this morning, and that he did not object and was not asked whether he objected.*”

“*That the four names struck out of a copy of that list in a square remain four gaps and are not filled, and the person who asked that the striking not be entered in this book has not been asked about any of it.*”

“*That nothing is carried.*”

Then she wrote it. Four hundred and eleven years old, a fresh leaf at the back of it, the ordinary hand, the day on it, nothing ruled, nothing headed.

“*Say the last of it,*” said the man of thirty-four from the town four days off, from the back of the room.

“*There are about nine people in here who have never heard the three words in this county connected to a name. They are about to hear *nothing carried* next to a household that answered a question, and that is the correct arrangement and it will not feel like one.*”

“*Nothing carried,*” she said, “*and the name goes in.*”

---

Then the weaver said the thing that made the afternoon, and he said it standing at the end of the room with his hands behind his back, in the way of a man of about seventy-two at the end of a bench, and he had noticed that and about four people in the room had noticed him notice it.

“*Say the rest of that, because nine words in a room is a thing you say when you have had it for four days.*”

“*I have been the last of a name since my father died, and that is nine years. There has been no page with my name in it in this county for one day in nine years.*”

“*Say the rest of it.*”

“*About thirty-eight days ago a woman of thirty-four wrote my name at the top of an entry at the back of that book, with a claim in it and nothing carried against it.*”

He looked at the leaf she had been writing on.

“*And there is my grandfather’s name on a fresh leaf this morning in a hand that can be sent for, with a day on it, and it is the first time in a hundred and forty-one years that anybody in this county has written that name down on purpose.*”

“*And what has come of it.*”

“*Nothing. It is a name at the back of a book with a day on it and a claim at the front of the same book with nothing carried against it and a sheet of paper with forty names and four gaps in it in a room with the door open. That is what has come of it.*”

“*Say the rest, because you have said that nine times in four weeks and you are tired of it.*”

“*And I said at the top of a wool bench three days ago that a page says what was done and a person in a square says what happened, and they are not the same and one of them has somebody behind it. My grandfather’s name is now on a page. It has not been in a square and it is not going to be in a square and I am sixty-two and I have not got nine years in me for a square.*”

The man of thirty-four from four days off understood the whole of that and wrote nothing down, and there is no form in the county of Aldwick that says he may.

Then Barnaby Rill said one thing from the chair, and he had not been sent for and about four of the nine had been expecting it since the seventh hour.

“*What has not been said out loud in that room this morning.*”

Nobody in that room said anything for about four seconds.

“*That there is a person in this room whose name has been in a book four hundred and eleven years old for a hundred and forty-one years with a line through it, and whose name has been on a page this morning without one, and that both of those are the same name and neither of them is the person.*”

“*Say the rest of that.*”

“*I have been that person twice in this town and I said nothing both times, and this morning I have been it a third time and I have said nothing again, and I am eighty-one and I would like it noticed that three is not a different number from two.*”

Nobody in that room thanked anybody and nobody in that room was sorry for anybody. A weaver of sixty-two said a name out loud in a room and was not thanked for it. A woman of sixty-eight stood at a window while a thing about a household she is descended from went into a book and was not thanked and was not asked. A man of thirty-one asked for a leaf to be struck back in a room with the door open and was refused in about four sentences in front of about nine people.

The fever was thirty-seven weeks and six days old. A name went in at the back of a book four hundred and eleven years old, on a fresh leaf of the same paper as the leaf the copy of the four pages is entered on, in the ordinary hand, with the day on it, and nothing was ruled and nothing was headed.

It says that a household of this town was asked a question a hundred and forty-one years back by an office nine miles off and gave an answer and the answer was made use of. It says that the leaf it was struck from is not struck back. It says that the person descended from the first name on that leaf has been told, and that nothing is carried.

And a man of thirty-one asked for the leaf to be struck back in that room in front of about nine people, and a weaver of sixty-two refused him in about four sentences. The fourth of them was that he is the last of a name and he has been the last of a name for nine years and he can stand it another nine.
