# Chapter 541: The Fourth Day Of The Ninety-Ninth Week, And A Friday On Which A Man Of Fifty-Four Of The County Rides Down Nine Miles Again With A Form Folded In Four In The Inside Of A Coat And Will Not Open It In A Lane And Then Opens It In A Porch In Front Of About Nine People, And A Man Of Thirty-One Standing At The End Of A Bench Hears That He Is Entered In The Book Of Orders At Aldwick In A Class Of His Own With A Name Over It, And The Name Is An External Witness, And It Means That A Person In That Office May Be Asked About Him

Fourth day of the ninety-ninth week. His two hundred and ninety-first morning. One hundred and forty-one days after the settlement. The fever thirty-six weeks and six days. Ninety-five days since the division. Forty-one days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town two days from where he is standing.

Simon Verge came down the lane on the Sunday of the ninety-seventh week. He made a return in a room eight feet by eleven in about nine minutes. He signed the back of it and did not sign the front, and he went back the same day.

He came down the lane again on this Friday morning, on the horse, at a walk, about three hours of it, because there is no road in this country that a horse can be ridden on toward this town.

He had a thing folded in four in the inside of his coat. About nine people in that lane worked out in about nine seconds that he had brought it nine miles for something, and not one of the nine said so.

---

The lane behind the bottom of the town is four feet wide in places because of the garden walls, and about nine of the stones in one stretch of it carry four letters and a stroke cut into the face of them, and nobody in this county can say whose they are.

The man of thirty-one walked up the lane behind him and asked him what it was, and he said it was paper, and the man of thirty-one asked him what was on the paper.

“*It is a form,*” said Simon Verge. “*And I am not opening it in a lane.*”

“*Say the rest of that, because I have asked a plain question in a lane and got half an answer.*”

“*There is a lane in this town with about nine people standing in it and a wall at the end of it, and I have learned once already on a Sunday that the part of a sheet that is not on the form does not get seen. I am not going to open a second one in a lane.*”

About nine people in that lane heard a man of fifty-four explain something about a form and about four of them understood where it had come from.

“*You learned it here.*”

“*I learned it here, on a Sunday, in a room eight feet by eleven, and about nine people heard me say it and about nine of the hundred and eighty-one in the square heard nothing at all.*”

Nobody in that lane said anything for about four seconds.

---

The recordwright came out of the meeting-house door and stood on the step and did not come down the lane, and about four of the nine in it had been waiting for her and about nine had not.

“*Mr Verge. You have ridden nine miles and you have a thing folded in four, and you will not open it where eight people are standing, and I have been the one who has not asked in this county for nineteen years.*”

“*I have not asked you anything.*”

“*You have not, and you have not been asked, and I am going to say a thing in this lane that I have not got a form for, and it is one sentence, and then you may do what you like with it.*”

She put her hand on the door frame.

“*Open it in the porch, or take it back to Aldwick in your coat and do not come down this lane again this year. Those are the two things. There is not a third one and I am not going to look for one.*”

The two women who sell eggs at the top of that lane understood the whole of it inside about four seconds, and one of them said *a form* to the other one.

“*The porch,*” he said.

“*The porch. About nine people will be in it and about four of them cannot read and about four can, and a thing that is read out in a porch is a thing anybody on the step can walk off with, and I have not said that. A man of eighty-one said that.*”

---

They went up the porch. The bench is about six feet long and there is a mark worn into the stone about two-thirds along it, where a man of about seventy-two stands on a Wednesday, and on a Friday nobody stood there at all.

The man of fifty-four sat down on the far end, which is the first time anybody has sat on that bench in this town, and he took the sheet out of the inside of his coat and unfolded it on his knees, and about nine people in that porch watched a piece of paper come out of a coat.

He read it out himself.

“*It is an entry out of the book of orders of the county of Aldwick, made on the second day of this week, which was the Wednesday of two days ago. It is written in the ordinary hand of that book.*”

“*Whose ordinary hand.*”

“*Mine. I made the entry and I have read about nine books in about eleven years and the ordinary hand of that book is the hand of three men and two of them are dead.*”

Then he read the rest of it, and it took him about nine seconds, and about four of the nine people on that step understood it before he had finished and the rest of them understood it afterwards.

“*That the entry made at the back of a book four hundred and eleven years old in a town nine miles from here, being a person whose record is not findable, having been found by the office of this county to be a person not of this county, is that the said person is entered in this book in a class by himself, and the class is headed, and the head of the class is an external witness.*”

The man who mends ploughs looked at the board and then at the floor, and about four seconds went by.

“*Say the other half of it, because that is nine seconds of a thing and there is more in it than there is on the page.*”

“*There is not more on the page. That is the whole of the page, and there are four lines under it and the fourth line is mine and it says what a person may be asked about him, and I will read that one too.*”

---

“*That a person at an external witness is not a person of the county, and that a person at an external witness may be asked about the matters on which the said person came, and that the office may make a return in respect of him in the ordinary form.*”

“*You made a return about me.*”

“*I did not. That is the half of it that I have ridden nine miles to say and I will say it before anybody asks me anything else.*”

He put his hand flat on the sheet on his knees.

“*That form has two lines on it. The first is a person of the county. The second is a person who is not of the county, and the second line has a head over it, and the head has been over it longer than I have been in that office and neither of us knows who wrote it. I filled in the second line because it is the line for you and the first one is not.*”

“*And you are the only person in this county who could have asked about the first line instead.*”

“*Nobody asked me. There is no form in this county that says a person may ask which line another person goes in, and I did not offer.*”

---

The man of thirty-one had been at the end of the bench with his leg out at the angle he stands it at, and he had not said anything for about four minutes, and then he said the thing that had been in him since the Tuesday before last.

“*I would like to know what it would take to be a person of this county.*”

“*Ask me the question again in a porch with about nine people in it, because that is where you asked it and I am not going to give you a smaller answer than the one you asked for.*”

He looked at the sheet and then at the man.

“*I do not know. I have looked for it. There is no form in the county of Aldwick that says what a person must do to become a person of it, and there is no form that says a person cannot, and I have been eleven years in an office that keeps two books and can make a return and nothing else, and I have not found one in either book or in the box they are kept in.*”

“*And that is your answer.*”

“*That is my answer and it cost me nine miles and I would have given it to you in a lane if you had asked me for it in a lane, and I have not got a better one at about the eleventh hour on a Friday.*”

---

Then the man of thirty-one said the mistake, and he made it out loud, and about four people on that step heard what it was and the rest of them heard a man being pleased with a sentence.

“*Then that is what I am. An external witness is a man who is not a person of anywhere. It is the plainest name anybody has put on me in a hundred and forty-one days.*”

Not one person on that step said a word for about four seconds.

“*No,*” said Simon Verge. “*And I am going to give you the other half of it and I am going to say it in this porch because you have said the wrong half in about nine places in three weeks and somebody ought to put it right in front of the people who heard it.*”

“*Say it.*”

“*An external witness is a person of the record and not of the place. You are in a book in Aldwick and you are on the page of it and you can be asked about by a clerk, and that is what the word says.*”

“*And the difference between those two.*”

“*The difference between those two is the whole of what has happened to you in a hundred and forty-one days. In about nine rooms you have asked to be the one who asks, and a person has told you each time that a person who cannot be sent for has nothing behind him. And this morning a form says you are not a person who cannot be asked about. You are a person who can.*”

The man who mends ploughs understood all of that inside about four seconds and did not say anything, which is the first time in five weeks that he has heard something and not asked a question about it.

“*And a clerk can ask you.*”

“*A clerk can ask you, and nobody in this county can, and I have not the power to stop it and I am not going to pretend that I have.*”

---

The recordwright said the last thing, and she said it standing in the doorway with her key in her hand, and about nine people were still in that porch and had not gone.

“*A heading is a promise about what goes in it.*”

“*Say the rest of that, because that is a thing people say and this town has been living inside one for a hundred and forty-one years.*”

“*Four words in a margin of a book in this building, and a head over them, and no order named, and no day. That is a heading and that is the promise, and the promise has been kept for a hundred and forty-one years by nobody meaning to keep it.*”

She turned the key over once in her hand.

“*And now there is a word with a line under it in a book nine miles off, and the word is over a man, and in about four years a person in that office will fill it in according to the word. That is what a heading does. That is the whole of it and you have all been here for it.*”

“*Will it hold him?*” said a man of about thirty at the end of the bench.

“*I have not seen the book and I cannot be sent for about it, because it is not in this county and there is no form in this county that reaches into another county. And what I have said is what a word with a line under it does and not what this one will do, and I am not going to say more about a book I have not read in front of about nine people.*”

Nobody in that porch thanked anybody. A man of fifty-four was not thanked for riding nine miles and opening a form, and a man of thirty-one was not thanked for hearing, and a recordwright was not thanked for standing in a doorway, and there is no form in the county of Aldwick that says a person may be thanked for any of it.

The fever was thirty-six weeks and six days old. A man of fifty-four of the county opened a form folded in four in a porch in front of about nine people, and read out an entry in a book nine miles off that puts a man of thirty-one into a class by himself with a name over the class.

The name is an external witness, and it means a person in that office may be asked about him, and there is no form in the county of Aldwick that says what a man has to do to stop being one, and about four people in that porch could say what a heading is and the rest could not.
