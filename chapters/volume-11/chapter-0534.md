# Chapter 534: The Fourth Day Of The Ninety-Eighth Week, And A Friday On Which A Woman Of About Fifty-One Who Has Had A Copy Of Four Pages In Two Rooms Over A Milliner’s For About A Year Asks In A Lane To Have Those Two Rooms Looked At By About Nine People One At A Time, And The First Person Who Looks At Them Is A Man Of About Thirty-Four From A Town Four Days Off, And What He Says Out Loud Is That He Can Tell How They Were Made And He Cannot Tell Whether They Are True, And A Man Of Thirty-One Is The Second Pair Of Eyes And Is Told In About Four Seconds That He Is The Wrong Kind

Fourth day of the ninety-eighth week. His two hundred and eighty-fourth morning. One hundred and thirty-four days after the settlement. The fever thirty-five weeks and six days. Eighty-eight days since the division. Thirty-four days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town two days from where he is standing.

The lane behind the bottom of the town is four feet wide in places because of the garden walls. About nine of the stones in one stretch of it carry four letters and a stroke cut into the face of them, and nobody in the county of Aldwick can say whose they are. A man of thirty-one has walked past them on his way up two stairs four times since the Saturday without asking.

Nobody has ever read them out loud in that lane and there is no form in this county that says a person may be asked what is cut into a stone.

Nobody had asked the woman of about fifty-one for anything in about a year. She had told nobody about the four pages, including the woman she rents the two rooms from, and that woman pays her rent and does not know.

---

She asked for it at about the fourth hour of the afternoon and she asked for it in a lane and not at a door, and about nine people were standing in that lane within about four minutes.

“*I would like the two rooms looked at.*”

About four seconds went by in that lane.

“*Say it again slower, because I have understood the words and not the shape of them and there is a difference in a lane.*”

“*I would like nine people to look at my two rooms. One at a time. About nine of them. And I would like to be standing in the lane while it is being done.*”

“*Why one at a time.*”

“*Because if they come in together then it is nine people who have seen a thing, and in about eleven years there will be a person in this town who says that nine people saw my four pages, and I have not got nine people in this county who can be sent for.*”

About four of the nine people in that lane understood the whole of that at once. About nine heard a woman ask to have two rooms looked at.

“*Why do you want it looked at at all, and I am asking because I have a right to and I have not had the practice of it.*”

“*Because there is a man from four days off who says out loud in a market square that there is a shelf, and I have four pages, and I have never let anybody see them, and I am fifty-one years old and I am not going to be sixty and wondering.*”

“*You are not going to be sixty for about nine years.*”

“*I am not going to be sixty for about nine years and in about nine years there will be a woman in those two rooms and it will not be me, and I would rather the four pages had been seen by nine people than that they were found by one.*”

The recordwright was one of the nine. She had not asked for it and she was not thanked for it, and about four of the lane understood that the first person to be asked had been the woman herself and the first person to agree had been the one who could be sent for.

---

The first person to go up the two stairs and look was the man of thirty-four from the town four days off.

He was up there about eleven minutes. Nobody went up with him and nobody stood on the landing, and about nine people in the lane below waited, and one of the nine was a man of thirty-one who could not be sent for and knew it.

He came down and stood in the lane and did not say anything for about four seconds, and then he said the thing that is the whole of what he is.

“*I can tell how they were made.*”

“*Say more than that or say less than that,*” said the recordwright, “*and I have not got the time for either.*”

“*I can tell how they were made. A person wrote them out of a thing they remembered, and the hand got faster in the second half of the third page, and there are forty-one mistakes in it and thirty-eight of them were corrected, and every one of the thirty-eight is a place where the writer went back and said no, that is not what it said.*”

“*And the other three.*”

“*The other three are places where she did not go back, and there is no way at all of telling from the paper whether she did not go back because she was right or because she was tired.*”

About four of the nine people in that lane understood all of that. About nine heard a man say the number forty-one.

“*That is the part nobody can check,*” said the recordwright.

“*That is the only part anybody can check, and it is the only part, and the reason is that a correction is a person disagreeing with themselves in writing and you cannot hide it.*”

Then he said the other half of it, and he said it standing in a lane in front of about nine people, and it was about four sentences and it was the reason he had come four days.

“*I cannot tell whether they are true.*”

Nobody in that lane said anything for about four seconds.

“*Say what you mean by that, because you have just looked at four pages and there are people in this lane who think you have told us about them.*”

“*I have told you about the hand and not about the morning. Whether the common above this town was used is a question about the morning of the thirtieth day after the division, and that is not a question about a hand.*”

“*Can you answer it?*”

“*No. About four people in that country can answer it. I have met two of them. Not one of them is in this county, and there is no form in the county of Aldwick that says a person may be sent for to a county four days off, and I have not got the authority to send anybody anywhere.*”

About nine people in that lane had heard a man of thirty-four say that he could not do the second half of a thing, and about four of them had understood what that cost him.

---

And then a man of thirty-one was the second pair of eyes, and he was the wrong kind, and he had asked to be and had not been refused because there was nothing to refuse.

He went up the two stairs and he looked at four pages for about nine minutes. He could not read the hand in the quicker copy, because that is the hand of a woman of about thirty at a stall in a town two days off, and there are about four people in this country who can read it and none of them is him.

He came down and said the true thing, because he has never once in a hundred and thirty-four days said a thing in a room that was not true.

“*There is a copy of a copy in a room eight feet by eleven with the door open, and I have read the four lines at the foot of its third page about nine times, and I do not know if they are true.*”

“*No,*” said the recordwright.

She said it in about four seconds, in a lane, in front of about nine people, and she did not raise her voice, and it was the fifth time in three weeks that a man of thirty-one had been refused a room or a thing in a room, and he understood it inside the four seconds.

“*Say the reason, because I have understood that you are saying no and not why, and the why is the part I cannot work out on my own.*”

“*A man who cannot be sent for can look at a thing and cannot be asked whether he looked.*”

“*That is two halves of one sentence and I have had the first half for about four weeks.*”

“*Then here is the second half. If you look at four pages in two rooms over a milliner’s and you go home, then in about four years there will be no way in this county of asking you what you saw. Not because you would lie. Because there is no form that says you must answer, and a man of thirty-one who cannot be sent for is a man nobody can make answer.*”

“*And if I do not look then nothing happens at all.*”

“*If you do not look then about nine people in this lane will have looked, and one of them will be a man of thirty-four from a town four days off who cannot be sent for either. That is two and not nine.*”

“*And I am one of the two, and you would be the other, and you and I are the only two of the four of us who cannot be asked whether we looked, and she is the only one of the four of us who can be sent for.*”

About four of the nine people in that lane understood the whole of that and about nine heard a woman say that a man was the wrong kind of second pair of eyes.

He said *aye*, and he said it in about four seconds, and he had been in this country a hundred and thirty-four days and he had about nine seconds and none of them was any use to him whatever.

“*Then what I can do is nothing,*” she said. “*And you have known that since the second day you were in this town, and it is the first useful thing anybody in this county has been able to tell you about yourself, and I am sorry it took a lane on a Friday to say it, and I am not going to say it again.*”

Nobody in that lane thanked anybody. A woman of about fifty-one had opened two rooms over a milliner’s and had not been thanked for it, and a man of thirty-four had looked at four pages and had not been thanked for it, and a man of thirty-one had looked at four pages and had not been thanked for it.

---

And then the man of thirty-four asked the question that about four people in that lane had been waiting three days for, and he asked it plainly and he asked it in the open air.

“*Who is it permissible to ask about the four pages they came from?*”

About nine people in that lane decided not to ask the question they had all just thought of, and it took about four seconds for all nine of them to decide it.

“*You have all just told me that the second half of that work cannot be done in this county. I have come four days to find out whether there is a way of doing it here that is not a lie, and I am asking a plain question and I would like a plain answer in about four sentences.*”

“*There is an entry in the back of that book, and it is in my hand, and it says that nobody in the county of Aldwick may be asked about the original.*”

“*You wrote it.*”

“*I wrote it fifteen days ago on a Thursday and I did not soften it and I have not softened it since, and about four people in this lane know why and about nine do not.*”

“*Why did you write it.*”

“*Because a thing in a room over a milliner’s which is the only account anybody in this country has of how four pages were made is a thing that will be asked about. Not by you. By somebody in about four years who has heard about it from a person who was not in this lane.*”

“*And you cannot stop that.*”

“*I cannot stop it and I can put an entry in a book four hundred and eleven years old with a day on it, and the day is the day I wrote it, and the entry is mine, and I am the only person anybody can be sent for about it.*”

The man of thirty-four stood in that lane and did not say anything for about four seconds.

“*Say the rest of it, because you have stopped at the part that is about you.*”

“*The rest of it is that it may be broken. Anything in a book can be broken by a person in a room in front of other people, and that is what a book is for, and I am not going to pretend that this one is different because I wrote it.*”

“*Then break it.*”

“*No. And I am not going to say no in a lane, and that is the whole of why I am saying no here instead of in a room, and you may check that in about four weeks.*”

“*Where is it to be broken, then?*”

“*In a room with the door open, with about nine people in it, and one of the nine has to ask me first, and if I say no then it does not happen, and I have said in this lane that the answer would be no and I have said it in front of nine people so that I cannot be asked privately in a fortnight and say something else.*”

About four of the nine people in that lane understood the whole of that at once, and about nine heard a woman agree that a rule in a book could be broken and refuse to break it on a Friday in a lane.

“*You have just made it a rule that is better than a rule that cannot be broken,*” said the man of thirty-four.

“*It is a rule about when and not about whether, and I have had four days to think about it and I would like somebody to tell me in a room whether it is a rule or a habit. I cannot tell the difference about my own writing.*”

---

She said one more thing at about the sixth hour and she said it standing in the lane with her key in her hand and about nine people still in it, because they had not gone and she had not told them to.

“*I have had those four pages for about a year and I have never taken them out of a drawer for anybody. Nine people have looked at them this afternoon and not one of them is going to keep them, and that is what I asked for.*”

“*Then what have you done.*”

“*I have made nine people know that there are four pages, and I have found out that one of them can tell how they were made and cannot tell whether they are true, and I have found out that the one who could tell me whether they are true is not in this county.*”

She looked at the man of thirty-four.

“*You will write to me. You said you have met two of them and neither is here. I am not asking you to bring either of them down four days. I am asking you to write, and I am asking you to say in the writing that I did not pay you. There is no form in this county that says what a woman who has four pages may pay a stranger for looking at them.

“*And I would rather it was on paper in a way that can be read.*”

“*It can be read. And I would like it written that you asked me and I did not offer.*”

“*Write that down as well.*”

The fever was thirty-five weeks and six days old. Nine people looked at four pages one at a time in two rooms over a milliner’s. About four of the nine could say how the four pages were made, and not one of them could say whether they were true.

A man of thirty-one looked at them too, and was told in about four seconds, in a lane, in front of about nine people, that he was the wrong kind of eyes.
