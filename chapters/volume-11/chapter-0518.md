# Chapter 518: The Second Day Of The Ninety-Sixth Week, And A Wednesday On Which About Nine Of About Sixty Households Come To A Meeting-House With The Door Open And The Whole Of The Arithmetic Of This Town Is Read Out In Pence Out Loud, And A Man Of Thirty-One Is Asked To Carry It And Says Yes In About Four Seconds And Is Refused In About Nine Minutes By About Nine People Who Are All Right

Second day of the ninety-sixth week. His two hundred and sixty-eighth morning. One hundred and eighteen days after the settlement. The fever thirty-three weeks and four days. Seventy-two days since the division. Eighteen days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town two days from where he is standing.

A meeting of about sixty households is a thing this county has no form for. There is no form that calls it, no form that says who may come, no form that says what it is for, and no form that says it has to produce anything.

The recordwright called it anyway, on the Sunday night, out loud, to about nine people in a porch. She said four things about it before she said a word about what it was. It is not a decision. It is not a court. It is not a meeting of the town. It is a room with the door open, and anybody who keeps a beast on the fold may come, and anybody who does not keep a beast may come and will be welcome and will not be counted.

About nine of them came.

She had said that she expected nine and had said the number out loud, because she has been the only person anybody can ask about a thing in this county for nineteen years and she is not going to be a woman who says come and then looks at the door.

The day cost a day. That is the whole of what it cost. A working day in a market town of about two thousand four hundred people is about a day of a household’s work, and about nine households lost one, and nobody in that room could put a figure on the nine of them and about four would have given a figure if anybody had asked for it in a form.

---

They came at about the tenth hour. The door was open. The window was down. There was a fire in the chancel because it was a Wednesday in a cold week, and about nine people sat down and about nine stood.

The finding of the Monday was read out first, and it is the reason the room was called at all. It is that the two objections at the foot of the entry of the fifteenth day of the ninety-third week are both against the three words nothing is carried, and that those three words are not about a half-acre, and that a person who is already in an entry does not get a second thing out of an entry and does not get a second thing put on him either.

She read it out standing, in the voice she uses for minutes, with the day on it, and about nine people heard it in a room with the door open, and about nine people in the square outside heard a woman reading out of a doorway and not one word of it, and that is the fourth time in about a fortnight that a thing has been read out of that doorway to nobody.

There is no form in the county of Aldwick that says what a finding does. It is not a decision and it is not a notice and it is not a rule, and nobody in that room can be asked what happens to it next, and about four people in that room had worked that out before she had finished reading it and about nine had not.

The first thing that happened was not about money.

“*I want it said first, and said in front of the door as well as in the room, that a meeting is not a decision,*” Marda Rill said. “*There is no form in the county of Aldwick that says a meeting of about sixty households has to produce anything at all. If you come into this room and you agree with everything I say in the next quarter of an hour, nothing will have happened by six o’clock this evening, and that is not a failure of the meeting. That is what a meeting is. It is the only instrument anybody in this county has that does not cost anybody anything to be wrong in.*”

Then she said the other half of it.

“*And nobody in this room has asked whether you wanted to come. I asked. I sent for nine households out of about sixty and I chose them, and choosing them is the whole of the problem with a meeting of sixty households and I have not solved it, and I have not pretended to.*”

Then she did the working, and she did it in pence as well as pounds, and she did it out loud, and it took about a quarter of an hour, and about nine people in that room heard the whole of it for the first time.

A hundred and forty acres, let at four shillings the acre, is five hundred and sixty shillings, which is twenty-eight pounds a year and no pence. Twenty-eight pounds is six thousand seven hundred and twenty pence. There are about sixty households in this town that keep a beast on the fold. Six thousand seven hundred and twenty pence over sixty households is a hundred and twelve pence, which is nine shillings and fourpence a household for the year.

If the leaf is put back, the descent runs again, and any ground of the four that has changed hands in a hundred and forty-one years becomes a claim, and the whole of the fold has to be let a second time. The fold is twenty-eight pounds. Fourteen pounds of it would have to be found again. Fourteen pounds spread upon about sixty households is four shillings and eightpence each, and that is half of what a household’s fold is worth in a year.

The wall is sixteen days of work by nine men, which is a hundred and forty-four days of work at eightpence a day, which is one thousand one hundred and fifty-two pence, which is four pounds and sixteen shillings and no pence.

The recordwright is two shillings a year from each household that keeps a beast, which is six pounds a year, and it has been six pounds a year for nineteen years.

“*A name is worth between four pounds sixteen shillings and six pounds this year,*” she said, “*and I have said that out loud twice in this building in about a fortnight and both times a man wrote it on a board, and I would like the nine of you to hear it from a person and not off a board.*”

Nobody in that room had heard it. That was the thing about it, and about nine people in that room found out on a Wednesday morning in the second week of the ninety-sixth week that the whole of what the last three weeks had been about is between four pounds sixteen shillings and six pounds.

And then she asked them the question, and she asked it in the plainest words anybody in that room had heard all week.

“*I want the nine of you to tell me which of you keep a beast on that fold. Not the holding, not the ground, not the claim. The fold. Because if the leaf goes back then the whole of that fold is let a second time, and the fourteen pounds has to be found again, and four shillings and eightpence a household is half of what a household’s fold is worth in a year, and there is no beast in that at any price.*”

About nine of the about sixty households in this county are the ones that would lose half of what their beasts are worth.

Four of the people in that room said so out loud, and they said it in a way that made the other five look at their boots, and one of the four then said that he had not known either and had come four miles.

Five of them did not say so. About four of the five gave a reason and about one did not, and the reasons were that a beast was not his but his son’s, and that he kept two on the low ground and not the fold, and that he had not understood which fold she meant and had not asked her in the four days there were, and the fifth said nothing at all and the recordwright let the silence stand, which is a thing she is good at.

Nobody in that room was asked twice, and about four of the nine noticed that she had asked once and would not ask again, and about nine noticed that four people in the room had said out loud the thing that nine people in that town had been saying at a pump for a week.

---

He was asked in the porch at about the eleventh hour, on the way out, by a man of thirty-one’s own age who keeps nine ewes and had walked four miles for it.

“*You have been in that porch every day for a fortnight,*” the man said. “*You have heard all of it and you have asked for none of it. I want somebody to carry the finding to the households that were not in that room. Fifty-one of them. I cannot do it and neither can she, because she is paid two shillings by every one of them and if she goes to their doors with a number they will think she is asking them for it.*”

“*Yes,*” said Ilyan Vester.

It took about four seconds and it was the fourth time in a hundred and eighteen days that he had said yes in four seconds to a thing he had not thought about.

It took about nine minutes to be refused, and about nine people refused him, and every one of them was right.

“*No,*” said the woman of forty at the wool bench, who had organised two pieces of paper in a market square on the Thursday of the week before last.

“*I have not said what I would carry it in.*”

“*You do not have to. That is the whole of the answer and I am going to give it in front of the porch and not in the room, because it is about the porch and not about the room.*” She had been in the room and had not said a word in it. “*You cannot be sent for. That is the first thing and it is the reason you have never been able to hold anything in this county, and every one of us has watched you find that out for a fortnight. The second thing is different and it is why I am not going to be softened by the first thing.*”

“*Say the rest of it.*”

“*There are about sixty households in this town and we asked nine of them to come and I was one of the nine. Nobody asked the other fifty-one whether they wanted a stranger from another county walking to their doors with a figure out of a book. If you go, they are going to have to open the door to a man they cannot send for, and they are going to have to listen to a number, and there is no form in this county that says a person may send you away again once you are on the step.*”

“*Then what is the other answer?*”

“*The other answer is that a thing a body did not ask for is not carried by anybody, and that goes for a man from another county exactly as much as it goes for a woman from a barn at Kiln Row, and I have been in that room for two hours listening to a woman of sixty-one make an argument about exactly that and I will not be the second person in three weeks to hear the same thing.*”

About nine people were in that porch by then, and about nine of them had said a version of it, and about four had said his name, and nobody had thanked anybody. The man of thirty-one understood it on the step in about four seconds, which is the difference between this and the thirty-eighth day of another division in another county, and it did him no good whatever. About four people in that porch saw the understanding happen, and about two of those four saw a man put his hands in his pockets.

**ASKED: whether a person not of this town may carry a thing for a body of about sixty households that did not ask him to carry it.**

**KNOWN: that a person who carries it is the only person anybody can be asked about it afterwards, and that nobody can be sent for to ask him, and that a body which has never been asked cannot be asked whether it would have agreed.**

**NOT KNOWN: what the body would say, because the body has never been asked, and because there is no form in the county of Aldwick that asks it, and because a form is the only thing anybody in this county has ever had for asking a thing.**

**DECISION: none is entered. The question was answered in a porch in about nine minutes by about nine people, and what a porch decides is not a thing this instrument is able to hold.**

The fever was thirty-three weeks and four days old. Four shillings and eightpence a household for the year was said out loud in a room with the door open to about nine people out of about sixty, and four of them said they kept a beast and five of them did not, and a man of thirty-one was asked to carry the rest of it and said yes in about four seconds and was refused in about nine minutes for a reason that was new, and he understood it on the step.
