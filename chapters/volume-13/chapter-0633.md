# Chapter 633: The Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Twelfth Week, And A Saturday, And On Which The Nine Names Are Read Off A Board In The Open One Under Another And The People Who Are Not In It Are Also Counted Out Loud, And On Which A Man Of Thirty-One Asks To Be Put On It And Is Refused In About Four Sentences And Holds It, And On Which A Woman Of Seventy-One Who Is Not Going To Be Asked Again Says Out Loud Why She Is Not Going To Be In It And The Reason Is About Four Years

Fifth day of the hundred and twelfth week. His three hundred and eighty-third morning. Two hundred and thirty-three days after the settlement. The fever fifty weeks old. One hundred and eighty-seven days since the division. One hundred and thirty-three days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The list was read out again on the Saturday morning at about the ninth hour at the head of the plank, in the open, one under another, the ground said before the name.

About nine people stood about while it was read. About four of them had it in their heads and the other five did not.

Nobody on that bank thanked anybody for the reading.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Wenna Callow.

“*Because a board on a barrel had nine names on it at about the fourth hour on Thursday and has nine names on it now.*”

“*And about nine people in a town of about eleven hundred heard that on Thursday, and about nine of them have not been to that room since.*”

“*And a Crown that can only be read out to the people who were in the room is not a Crown. It is a thing at a barrel.*”

The four of them on that bank took it at once.

“*So it is being read out. All nine. In the open.*”

“*And I am going to read the people who are not in it out loud afterwards, and about four of you are going to hear a number and about nine of you are going to hear a name, and both of those are in the same breath.*”

---

Then a man of sixty-one gave two counts, and he is the only man in that county who gives a number and says what it is a figure of in the same breath.

“*The first is the people in this town who cannot be sent for about a chair. That is everybody who is not sitting in one.*”

“*A person in a chair is a person anybody can ask what they said and on what day, and a person not in one is nobody.*”

“*The count of that in a town of about eleven hundred is about eleven hundred less about nine, and I am not going to write that down, because it is not a figure of anything anybody can use.*”

About four people on that bank laughed and about nine did not.

“*The second is the people nobody can be sent for about at all. That is a different figure and it is smaller and I have got it and it is one.*”

Nobody on that bank moved.

“*One, and it is a man of thirty-one, and the reason he is the one and the reason the woman of forty-four is not the one are not the same reason.*”

“*About four of you have mixed them up since Thursday and about nine have not thought about it.*”

“*A woman of forty-four is not in a chair, so there is nothing to send anybody for.*”

“*A man of thirty-one is not in this county, so there is no form that could send anybody anywhere.*”

About four people on that bank had the difference. About nine of them had not.

---

Then a man of thirty-one asked, out loud, in front of about nine people, to be put on the board.

He said the reason before he was refused, which is the only reason anybody in that bank has heard of.

“*Put my name on it. I am asking in the open on a Saturday morning in about nine people’s hearing.*”

“*I would like about four of you to stop me if I have got the shape of this wrong. I have not.*”

“*And I am going to say why I want to be on it, and then I am going to be told no, and I am going to hold it.*”

Nobody on that bank said anything, and the fire in the room at the top of that road was not lit.

“*A man of sixty-eight put a line about me on a sheet of deal in a yard nine miles up this water on the seventh and last day of the hundred and eleventh week.*”

“*I was nine feet off with a withy knife at the time, and I have not asked for it and cannot stop it and did not hear it being written.*”

“*I have been on a thing in four counties for eleven days, and there is no form anywhere that says a person may be sent for about any of it.*”

“*I have wanted for eleven days to be on a thing that a person can be sent for about, and that is the whole of the wanting, and I would like it said that it is a want and not a plan.*”

About four people on that bank had that. About nine of them did not.

Then a man of sixty-eight who is not in a chair and cannot be corrected in one refused him in about four sentences.

“*No. I told you on the Friday of the eleventh week that you may not put your hand on any of the copies, and I gave the reason then and I am giving it now and it is the same reason.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*If a Crown has a man in it that nobody can be sent for about, then in about four years the Crown and the man are the same thing.*”

“*And a Crown that cannot be asked about what is in it is a lid with a name on it, and I gave that rule sixteen days ago on a Thursday morning and I meant it about a plank.*”

“*And I am not going to let a man I like stand in the door of it.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked him for the rule.

“*That is four sentences, and I have said the true of it, and it is a reason about you and not about the Crown.*”

“*And I would like about four of you to notice that and about nine of you to notice that I have not said one word about whether the Crown is a good thing.*”

Then the man of thirty-one said nothing.

About four people on that bank noticed that he said nothing. About nine of them had not known there was anything to say.

---

Then a man of sixty-one read the second half of it out loud, which is the people who are not on the board.

About nine people in that bank heard a name they had not known. About four heard two.

“*Orla Fennimore, forty-four, keeper of the book, who said no on the Thursday in four sentences and is not in it.*”

“*The reason is that an answer said by her in the open is the position of the record, and she has been the instrument in this county for nineteen years and has never once been the person.*”

About four people on that bank had heard her say it on the Thursday. Nobody had heard it said back.

“*And a man of thirty-four in the ash on the low side of that landing, who has been in it since the second day of the hundred and twelfth week, and that is seven days, and he has not stood up once in the seven.*”

“*His name is Ivo Kellow, and he came from a town four days off, and he is not in a chair, and nobody in this town can be sent for about him.*”

“*About four people in this town knew that there was a man in that ash, and about nine of them did not, and about four of those four knew his name.*”

About four people on that bank looked at the ash.

Nobody on that bank said anything to the ash.

---

Then a woman of seventy-one came up that cart road for the first time in a fortnight, on her own legs.

There is no form in that county that says a person may be sent for about a walk up a road, and nobody sent for her.

About nine people were on that bank, and about four of them had not known she was coming.

Nobody on that bank said a word that could have moved her.

“*I am not going to be in it,*” said Eliza Lark, before anybody asked her.

About four people on that bank had expected her to say that. About nine had not.

About four seconds went past.

“*And I am going to give the reason in about nine sentences and then I am not going to say anything else about it in this town.*”

“*I have said the shape of a different thing three times in about eleven days and I will not say that a fourth, and this is not that.*”

“*About four of you will want it to be that and about nine of you will not.*”

Nobody on that bank moved, and about four of them looked at the two ashes on either side of that landing and about nine did not.

“*A man of sixty-one told this bank about an hour ago that there is one person in this county that nobody can be sent for about at all, and he counted the man of thirty-one, and he is right, and I am not going to argue with him about it in front of you.*”

“*There are two more and the two of them are a man of thirty-one standing on this bank and a man of thirty-four in an ash on the low side of that landing, and there is no form in this county that reaches either of them.*”

“*And I am neither of those, and the difference is the whole of my reason.*”

“*I am a woman of seventy-one who keeps a house that is not hers and has kept it eleven years, and in eleven years nobody has come to that door to ask me a question, and there is a difference between nobody coming and nobody being able to come.*”

“*In about four years there will be about nine people in this county that nobody can be sent for about at all, and a Crown of about nine people will want to speak for every one of them, because that is what a Crown is for and I have not got a better account of it than that.*”

“*I would rather be one of the people it does not speak for than one of the people it speaks for, and that is the whole of my reason, and it is about four years, and it is not a clever place for a woman of seventy-one to stand and I have been in this county for eleven years.*”

Then she said that she was not going to be asked anything else about it.

A man of thirty-one started to ask her something, and she said no before he had got to the end of it, out loud, in about nine people’s hearing.

About four people on that bank said nothing at all.

“*No. You have asked me four things in about nine days and I have answered about four of them, and I am not going to answer a fifth about a thing I have just said the shape of once.*”

“*I told a man of thirty-one on the fifth day of the ninth week that I would not put it in a book, and I have not, and that is a different thing and it is still true.*”

“*And a man of thirty-eight has been poling up and down this water with a brother’s name in a coat for about twelve days, and that is a different thing, and I am not asked about it either.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked her for the walk up the road.

---

Then the man of thirty-one said the true of it in about nine sentences, and it was the sixth time in thirty-two days.

A woman of twenty-nine who was in the reed caught it in about the fourth and did not stop him, and said out loud why she was not stopping him this time.

“*There are about eleven hundred people in this town. About nine of them are on a board and about eleven hundred of them are not.*”

“*A man of thirty-one asked to be one of the nine and was told no by a man of sixty-eight in four sentences.*”

“*A woman of seventy-one walked up a cart road to say she does not want to be spoken for by any of them.*”

“*And a man of thirty-four has been in an ash for seven days and has not stood up and nobody can be sent for about him.*”

About four people on that bank had the whole of it. The rest of them had a name in a book and nothing else.

“*And about four of the nine on that board have been in a chair in their lives, and about nine of the eleven hundred have not, and that is the whole of the arithmetic of it.*”

“*It took four hours to make on a Thursday and it is not a number of anybody.*”

Then a woman of twenty-nine said the true of why she was not stopping him. It was three sentences, and about four people in that town had not known any of it.

“*I am not stopping him because he has been stopped twice this week by people in this town, and both times he was right about the rule and both times the person was right and he was the one who had to put it down.*”

“*And a man who is told the true thing about a rule in a room, and is wrong in that room about a fifth of the time, is learning something.*”

“*I have been in a chair for nine years and I have watched about nine people not learn it.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked her for not stopping him.

---

The fever was fifty weeks old. He had been in that county seventy-nine days, and about nine people are on a board on a barrel and about eleven hundred are not.

And the man of sixty-one gave two figures for who cannot be sent for about, and one of them is one, and one of them is about eleven hundred less nine, and he would not write the second one down because it is a figure of nothing anybody can use.

And a man of thirty-one asked out loud to be put on it and was refused in four sentences by a man of sixty-eight who is not in a chair and cannot be corrected in one, and the reason is that in about four years a Crown and a man nobody can be sent for about are the same thing, and he held it and said nothing.

And a woman of seventy-one came up that cart road on her own legs for the first time in a fortnight, unasked and unsent for, and gave her reason in four sentences, and the reason is that in about four years a Crown of about nine people will want to speak for every person in that county that nobody can be sent for about, and she would rather be one of the people it does not speak for.

And a man of thirty-four called Ivo Kellow has been in an ash on the low side of that landing for seven days and has not stood up, and there is a name against a chair in that book with nobody in it, and no column anywhere for where he is.

And a chair in a front room on the low side of that landing has a slate nailed to the frame with no name on it, and the chair itself is not one of the twenty-nine and never was, and there is nothing against it, and there is nothing in the book for it, and there is going to be nothing in the book for it.
