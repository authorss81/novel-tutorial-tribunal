# Chapter 637: The Second Day Of The Hundred And Thirteenth Week, And A Wednesday, And On Which A Man Of Thirty-One Answers Out Loud, In A Room With The Door Open, In Front Of About Nine People, An Offer Made Nine Miles Up The Water On The Tuesday By A Man Of Fifty-Two Who Cannot See Well, And Says No To It In About Nine Sentences, And Of Those Nine About Four Are Against Him And About Four Are For Him And About One Of The Nine Has Been Waiting Nine Days To Be Asked A Question, And On Which A Name Is Said Out Loud For The First Time In That County And Nothing Whatever Happens

Second day of the hundred and thirteenth week. His three hundred and eighty-seventh morning. Two hundred and thirty-seven days after the settlement. The fever fifty weeks and four days. One hundred and ninety-one days since the division. One hundred and thirty-seven days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

They came down the water on the Wednesday morning, and a man of about fifty-two came with them because he wanted to hear the answer.

The room had a low lintel and a slate nailed to the frame with no name on it, and the door was open because it was the second hour of a Wednesday.

The Crown was on the barrel, and there were about nine people in front of it.

Nobody in that room thanked anybody for the coming down.

The offer was put again from the front, in the open, by the man who had made it, in about nine sentences.

About four people in that room had heard it and about four of them had not, and about one of them was going to have to answer it.

“*I am not going to say it better than I said it in a yard yesterday at about the fourth hour, and about four of you were in that yard and about nine of you were not.*”

“*I am going to say the whole of it again and then I am going to sit down on that bench and let him answer it and not interrupt him once.*”

About four people in that room counted that.

Nobody on that bench said anything while it was being answered.

---

Then he answered, and it was in about nine sentences, and he did not have it written, and he said so before he started.

“*I have not written this down. I would like it said that I have not.*”

“*And a man of sixty-eight in this county is a person who cannot be corrected in a chair, and I am a person who cannot be sent for about at all.*”

“*And between the two of those there is no form in the county of Kell that says a man may keep a copy of what he said.*”

The four of them who had said no to him had it again. About nine of them had not.

“*No.*”

Nothing was said, and the open door let the weather in.

“*And I am not going to give you the reason first. I am going to give you the shape of it, because about four of you in this room have been waiting three days for me to say a thing and about nine of you have been waiting for me to say the right thing.*”

“*Both of those are the same mistake and mine is bigger.*”

About four people in that room understood that.

“*The reason is a Crown of about nine people at a barrel, of whom about four cannot describe what it is for. A man of sixty-one said that out loud on the Friday at the ninth hour and has not been contradicted since.*”

“*A Crown in this county is a thing nobody can be sent for about, because the only person anybody can be sent for about in this county said no to being in it on the Thursday of last week in four sentences and meant every one of them.*”

“*So the day a Crown opens its mouth about a man it cannot be asked about, that Crown becomes a thing that people act on.*”

“*And a thing that people act on and cannot be sent for about is a lid with a name on it.*”

“*And a man of sixty-eight said that on the Friday of the eleventh week in about nine people’s hearing and said it in about nine sentences and was right, and I have been on the wrong side of it for nine days.*”

“*And I would like it said that I knew I was on the wrong side of it at about the ninth minute of the offer.*”

Nobody in that room said anything to that.

“*And the second half of it is about me and I have been putting it off for nine days and I am putting it off now.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*There is no form in this county that says a person may be spoken for. There is no form that says a person may not.*”

“*And I am the one person in four counties that nobody can be sent for about at all, and if a Crown speaks for me then it is speaking for a person it can never be asked.*”

“*And about nine days ago I stood on a bank in this town and said out loud that I wanted to be findable, and I have had nine days to find out that wanting to be findable and being findable are two different things.*”

“*And the difference is about four years, and it is the difference a man of sixty-eight put his hand on and would not give back.*”

About four people in that room had the whole of it. Nobody said anything to him about it.

“*And I have said the true of the other half as well, because a man of thirty-eight said it in a yard yesterday and about four of you were in it.*”

“*It is that it would work. It would work in about four days.*”

“*A man of fifty-two who cannot see well offered it with a woman of forty-nine standing in that yard who does not know what a copy is, and he was right.*”

“*And about four of you in that yard thought it was a good idea and about nine did not, and the four are right as well, and the nine are right as well.*”

“*And I am not going to stand in this room and be the one who decides which of those two groups of about four is the true one.*”

Nobody in that room thanked him for that either.

---

Then he said a name, and he said it once, and he said it flat, and it was the first time in that county.

About four people in that room had had it in their mouths for about eleven days. About nine had not.

“*I am going to say a name out loud. It is a name and not a shout, and I am not going to say it twice and I am not going to explain it.*”

About four people in that room went still. About nine of them had not known there was anything coming.

“*The First Witness.*”

The nine of them were where they had been.

About four seconds went past and nothing said and nothing happened, and the fire in that room was not lit, and the bench against the gunwale of the Gull was standing outside with the dew on it.

“*That is the name of the thing that said its own name in a weigh-house nine miles up this water about eleven days ago in front of about four people.*”

“*A man of forty-three read that line out loud in this county on the Friday of the eleventh week, and then a man of thirty-four said four words in a yard on the Monday.*”

“*And about four people in this room have had those four words since before I came into it.*”

Nobody in that room said anything at all.

“*And I have said it and nothing has happened.*”

“*About nine people in this room have a name they did not have an hour ago, and about four of them have had it for about eleven days and have now got it out of a second mouth.*”

“*And I would like it said that a name is a thing nine people can carry and a Crown is a thing nine people can speak, and this is a Crown of about nine people, and I have just put a name on it for nothing, in the open, on a Wednesday.*”

About four people in that room understood that. The rest of them had a name.

---

Then the cost fell, and it fell on the person in that room who had never been asked for anything in his life.

He said it out loud himself.

About nine people in that town did not know a day before Wednesday that it was going to fall on him.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*I wrote one of those sheets out on the Tuesday morning of the eleventh week, and I am going to write another one on Friday, and there are fourteen of them in four counties.*”

“*And about four people in four counties who have one of them have said out loud that they want to know how it was done.*”

About four people in that room had not thought about that. About nine of them had.

“*And the penny a head is on everybody over sixteen that comes through that landing, and it is not on anybody under sixteen.*”

“*And there is no form in this county that says a person under sixteen may be sent for about a thing he has written with his own hand.*”

“*That is how I have been safe for thirteen years and it is not because anybody thought about me. It is because I am small and I do not carry a basket.*”

About four people in that room had the whole of it. Nobody said anything to him.

“*And there is a name in about nine mouths in this room now and about nine mouths in four counties by the end of the week, and the only door into a sheet of deal is the hand that wrote it.*”

“*About four people who have one of them are going to come and find me, and my mother cannot be sent for about and I cannot be sent for about, and there is nobody in four counties who can say to them that they may not.*”

Nobody in that room thanked anybody for the name.

Then a woman of forty-one with two carts said the true of it in about nine sentences, and about four people in that room had not known any of it.

She did not say her son was a coward and she did not say he had brought it on himself.

“*He has never been asked for anything in his life and I said that out loud on the Wednesday of the eleventh week in about nine people’s hearing, and I am saying it again now because a man of thirty-one has put a name in a room.*”

“*And about four of you can see what that costs and about nine of you were listening to a new word.*”

“*It is not his fault and it is not a debt, and I would like about four of you to hear that there is nowhere in this county to put it.*”

“*Because there is no column for a thing a person owes for being thirteen.*”

“*It is in no book. It is in a room and about nine people heard it, and about four of them are going to come and find him.*”

Nobody in that room said a word to her about it.

---

And then a man of sixty-eight said the thing nobody else in that room was going to say, and he said it flat, and it was about the room and not about the name.

“*Nobody in this room stopped him.*”

About four seconds went past and nobody filled them.

“*On the seventh day of the hundred and twelfth week I would have had about four of you out of this room before a name got to the fourth hour, and about nine of you would have let it go.*”

“*And a woman of twenty-six stopped him on the Wednesday of last week about a boy’s question in about nine seconds, and there is no form in this county that says a person may stop a man of thirty-one, and she did it anyway.*”

About four people in that room had that. About nine of them did not know they had been in it.

“*About nine people in this room are the reason a name is in about nine mouths, and I am not going to say one word against any of you.*”

“*And I am going to say the arithmetical of it, because that is what I am for, and because about four of you are going to be glad of it and about nine of you are going to have it a different way by this evening.*”

“*And that is not a fault of anybody in this room.*”

Nobody in that room thanked him for the arithmetical.

---

The fever was fifty weeks and four days old. He had been in that county eighty-three days, and he said no in the open in a room with the door open to an offer that would have worked in about four days.

The reason was given in about nine sentences, and of that reason about four sentences were about a Crown of about nine people of whom about four cannot describe what they are for, and about four sentences were about himself.

He said out loud that he had known he was wrong inside about nine minutes and had wanted it anyway.

And a name was said out loud in that county for the first time, once, flat, with no explanation, and nothing whatever happened.

About nine people in a town of about eleven hundred have a name they did not have an hour before the fourth hour, and about nine people in four counties will have it by the end of the week.

And the first thing it cost fell on a boy of thirteen who had copied a sheet of deal out on the Tuesday morning of the eleventh week, and who is not a head in the book of heads and carts because the penny a head is on everybody over sixteen, and there is no form in that county that says a person under sixteen may be sent for about a thing he has written with his own hand, and about four people in four counties who have a copy of that sheet are now going to come and find the only hand that made it.
