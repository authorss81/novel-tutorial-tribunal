# Chapter 636: The First Day Of The Hundred And Thirteenth Week, And A Tuesday, And Nine Miles Up That Water In The Yard Of A Landing A Man Of About Fifty-Two Who Cannot See Well And Whose Fifth Line Is His Offers In The Open, In Front Of About Nine People, To Have The Thing At The Barrel Speak Out Loud For A Man Of Thirty-One, And He Gives His Reason And It Is A Woman Of Forty-Nine Who Does Not Know What A Copy Is, And About Four Of That Yard Think It Is A Good Idea And About Nine Do Not

First day of the hundred and thirteenth week. His three hundred and eighty-sixth morning. Two hundred and thirty-six days after the settlement. The fever fifty weeks and three days. One hundred and ninety days since the division. One hundred and thirty-six days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

They were on the water at about the fifth hour and in at about the second hour of the afternoon.

It is nine miles up and it is nine miles down, and he poled the whole of it because there is nobody in that county he would have asked to pole it.

The man of thirty-one went up with him, and was in the yard for the whole of the time, which was the first time in about four days that a room had had him in it while a thing he was on was being said.

Nobody at that landing thanked either of them for the going.

About nine people were in that yard at about the fourth hour on the Tuesday, and a man of about fifty-two was at the head of the cart road with a hand along the top of the counter because he cannot see well.

Everybody in that yard has known that for eleven years, and his name is Simon Rook.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Garrin Tolley, from the wall, with a slip of board in his coat.

“*Because I am going to say a thing out loud in about nine people’s hearing, and about four of you are going to agree with me before I have finished and about nine of you are not.*”

“*And I am saying that first so that nobody in this yard can say afterwards that they did not know what was coming.*”

About four seconds went past in that yard and a cart came out of the gate behind them.

---

Then he said it, and he said it flat, in the open, in about nine people’s hearing.

About four of them agreed with him inside about four minutes and about nine did not, and he had counted them himself and said so before he started.

“*I am asking the thing at the barrel to speak out loud for a man of thirty-one.*”

“*Not in a room nine miles down the water where it sits on a board, and not in a letter, and in four counties, out loud, in the open, on a day a person names.*”

Nobody in that yard moved.

“*And I am not asking for it as a favour and nobody sent for me, and there is no form in this county that says a man of fifty-two may ask for it.*”

“*About four of you in this yard think it is a good idea and about nine of you do not, and I would like both of those counted out loud before I say anything else.*”

About four people in that yard said something at once. Nobody else said anything.

“*Here is why, and it is not about him.*”

About four people in that yard had been waiting since the fourth hour of Monday for a reason. About nine of them had not known there was going to be one.

“*I read one of those sheets out loud in this yard on the Monday of the eleventh week and then I asked for one of my own and was given it.*”

“*The fifth line of it is mine and not the man of forty-three’s, and nobody in this county can be sent for about that fifth line.*”

“*And it is a penny a head that the man of sixty-one has been taking off me for eleven years, and he cannot stop it, because there is no form that says he may.*”

Four of them in that yard had it before the end of it. About nine of them were looking at the counter.

“*And there is a woman of forty-nine in that yard who came down the road with me four days ago, and who is not in a chair and is not a head in the book of heads and carts.*”

“*And I asked her on the Friday what a copy was, in the plainest words I had, and she said she did not know.*”

Nobody in that yard said anything at all.

“*She is forty-nine and she has been in this county four days and she is not in a book, and I cannot be sent for about her, and she cannot be sent for about.*”

“*And a thing at a barrel nine miles down this water has a name on a board and is a body that may speak for a person who cannot be asked.*”

“*And if it can do that for a woman of forty-nine who does not know what a copy is, then it can do it for a man of thirty-one who has a line about him in two places in four counties, and then he is a person somebody has spoken for.*”

About four people in that yard had the whole of it. About nine of them were still holding the earlier half.

“*And then the hearing the man of thirty-eight asked for in this yard on the Monday of the eleventh week stops being impossible, and I am going to say why, and it is the true of it.*”

“*It is the whole of my offer, and about four of you have not had it yet.*”

Nobody in that yard moved.

“*A man of thirty-eight asked that every record there is be read in one room. Nobody granted it and nobody refused it and nobody wrote it down.*”

“*It cannot be granted, because about nine people in this world are not in a room where anybody can be sent for about any of it, and about nine people in this county are in no record at all.*”

“*And a hearing that cannot hear a person who cannot be asked is not a hearing about every record. It is a hearing about about nine tenths of them, and it is honest about that.*”

“*And the man of thirty-one is the one person in four counties that is in no record at all and is not in a chair and is not in a book, and there is a line about him on a sheet in two places, and about nine people in four counties have read it out loud in about four rooms already.*”

“*If the thing at that barrel says that line out loud on a day a person names, in the open, in four counties, then he is a person a body has spoken for.*”

“*And a person a body has spoken for is a person in a hearing.*”

“*And then the man of thirty-eight has what he asked for and none of us has to decide anything, and it will have happened in about four days.*”

About four seconds went past in that yard and nobody filled them.

---

Then a man of thirty-eight said the true of it, and it was four sentences.

About nine people in that yard had known it and about four had not.

“*That would work.*”

“*I have been poling nine miles twice for about nine days waiting for a way of getting about every record into one room, and there is not one.*”

“*And that is the first thing anybody in four counties has put in front of me that is not a lid, and I am a man of thirty-eight who has said yes to about nine things in thirty-one days and about four of them were right.*”

About four people in that yard had that. Nobody thanked him for the going.

“*And about four of us think it is a good idea and about nine of us do not, and I am going to say the true of the half that does not.*”

“*Because the man of thirty-one is in this yard and I am not going to have about nine of us agree behind his back about a thing that is about him.*”

About four people in that yard had the whole of it. About nine did not.

“*A Crown is nine people at a barrel, and about four of them cannot describe what it is for, and a man of sixty-one said that on a Friday at about the ninth hour in a room nine miles down this water.*”

“*About nine people in that room had it and it has not stopped being true since.*”

“*And if that body speaks for a man of thirty-one, then a body that cannot describe what it is for is standing in the one room that about nine people in the world are going to be in, and nobody will be able to ask it what it is doing there.*”

Nobody in that yard said anything to that.

“*And I am not saying it is wrong. I am saying that a thing which is a Crown because it may speak for a person who cannot be asked, speaking for a person who is in the room, in a hearing about every record there is, is a Crown doing the one thing in the world it is not for.*”

---

Then a man of thirty-one said the true of himself, out loud, in about nine people’s hearing, in a yard at a landing nine miles up the water.

About four people in that yard had been waiting three days for it. About nine had not.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*I want it. I have wanted it for about nine days and I am going to say so out loud in a yard where about nine people can hear me.*”

“*And I am saying it before it is offered again and not after, because about four of the people in this yard have watched me do that twice in about ten days and get it wrong both times.*”

“*And I would rather this one be a thing I said first.*”

About four people in that yard had that. About nine of them had the other half of what he had said.

“*A man of sixty-eight put a line about me on a sheet of deal in this yard nine days ago, and I was nine feet off with a hedge on a Monday morning about four hours down this water.*”

“*And I asked out loud in that room on Saturday to be put on the thing at the barrel, and was refused in four sentences in about four years, and the refusal was right.*”

“*And I would like it said that the reason I want this is not that a hearing about every record there is is the right thing. I have not worked out whether it is the right thing.*”

“*The reason is that if a body speaks for me then I am in a room I cannot be sent to, and I have been nobody’s in four counties for thirty-one days, and I have been nobody’s in a way I can check.*”

“*And this is the first thing anybody has offered me in thirty-one days that I cannot check, and I want it.*”

About four seconds went past in that yard and nobody filled them.

“*Say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me.*”

“*And I am not going to say the other half in this yard. I am going to say it on Wednesday in a room where about nine people can hear it.*”

“*And I am saying now that I am going to say it, so that about nine of you know in advance that I have not forgotten it, and about four of you can come and hear it.*”

About four people in that yard understood that. Nobody said anything to it.

---

The fever was fifty weeks and three days old. He had been in that county eighty-two days, and a man of about fifty-two who cannot see well, whose fifth line is his and not the man of forty-three’s, and who is fifty-two, stood in the yard of a landing nine miles up that water and offered out loud, in the open, in about nine people’s hearing, to have a body of about nine people at a barrel nine miles down the water speak for a man of thirty-one in four counties on a day a person names.

And the reason he gave was a woman of forty-nine who came down the road with him four days ago, and who is not in a chair and is not a head in the book of heads and carts, and whom he asked on the Friday what a copy was, and she said she did not know.

And about four people in that yard thought it was a good idea and about nine did not, and a man of thirty-eight said the true of both halves, and a man of thirty-one said out loud that he wanted it before anybody offered it again, and that he was going to say the other half on the Wednesday in a room nine miles down the water where about nine people could hear it.

And at about the sixth hour of that Tuesday a lighter came up to the landing with a man of about thirty-four in it, who has kept that landing nineteen years, and who had come back down the water on the Friday of the previous week to say one sentence and go and take his work up again.

About four people in that yard had heard about the offer and about nine had not, and about four of the nine could not describe what they had said yes to.
