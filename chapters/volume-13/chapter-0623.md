# Chapter 623: The Second Day Of The Hundred And Eleventh Week, And A Wednesday, And On Which A Woman Of Forty Who Has Been Asking For A Day Against A Name Since The Friday Of Last Week Names Out Loud A Woman Of Thirty Who Has Not Said One Word Since The Seventh Hour Of Yesterday, And On Which The Naming Is Done By Her And Not By The Man Of Thirty-One And He Is Asked Why He Could Not Have Done It Himself And Has No Answer That Is Worth Anything, And On Which The Woman Of Thirty Gives The Reason In Four Sentences And The Reason Is Her Brother And A Copy Of A Thing That Can Be Sent For About

Second day of the hundred and eleventh week. His three hundred and seventy-third morning. Two hundred and twenty-three days after the settlement. The fever forty-eight weeks and four days. One hundred and seventy-seven days since the division. One hundred and twenty-three days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

A man of thirty-eight came down the water on the Tuesday night with a third copy under oilcloth and a hundred and nine miles is not the distance and the nine miles is, and he came in at about the sixth hour on the Wednesday morning and put the copy on the deck of the Gull and did not read it out and about four people asked him to and about nine did not.

It was the third copy of a sheet of deal with thirty-one lines on it. The other two were nine miles apart and did not agree, and under a rule five days old a woman of seventy-one was not true until somebody poled nine miles up the water and put a line on a sheet in a yard.

Nobody on that plank thanked the man of thirty-eight for the going.

---

The woman of thirty had been on that deck the whole of the Tuesday. She had come up the plank at the fourth hour, before the copy went, and she had been in the same room for every word of it, including a woman of seventy-one who is not in the room and is not in that county and is eleven years of a house that is not hers.

At about the seventh hour, after a man of thirty-one had said out loud that he was not going to go and tell her, she said the one thing nobody else on that deck had said, and it was four sentences, and it was about the six.

Nobody on that deck moved.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*I am not going to say it again until somebody asks me for it,*” said Perdy Lound, and she was thirty, and one of the six that have not come is her brother, and he is in a county four days off and she does not know whether he is ill.

About four people on that plank had that. About nine had the penny and not the rest.

Then she did not say another word for the rest of the day, and she did not leave, and she stayed on that deck until about the ninth hour, and about nine people on that bank did not notice a thing and about four of them did.

---

Then on the Wednesday morning, at about the second hour, a woman of forty stood up on the gravel and named her, out loud, in front of about nine people, and about four of the nine were looking at the man of thirty-one while she did it.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Wenna Callow.

“*Because a man of thirty-one has said out loud twice in two days that he would like to know who in this town is against a copy of that sheet going where it goes, and he has asked about nine people one at a time on this deck. He has not found her, and I have found her, and I would like it said out loud that I have been watching her for two days and he has not.*”

About four people on that bank said something at once. About nine did not.

“*I am standing in front of about nine people and a woman of thirty who has not said a word since about the seventh hour of yesterday. I am going to say her name out loud, and then I am going to say why I can do it and he cannot, and about four of you are not going to like the second half of it.*”

Nobody on that bank moved.

“*Perdy Lound. She is thirty. She came up that plank on the Friday of the ninth week with about eight other people for eight different reasons, and she has been up it every working day since, and she is not in a chair and there is no form in this county that says she may be sent for about anything.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*And the reason I can say it and he cannot is that he has spent nineteen days asking people who can be sent for about something. He asked me. He asked a woman of twenty-six who is in a chair and a man of sixty-one who takes the penny and a woman of twenty-nine who cuts withies and a man of fifty-four who holds the gravel. Every one of those people is a person somebody can be sent for about. Not one of them is a person somebody cannot.*”

About four people on that bank understood that. About nine did not.

“*He asked her on the sixth day of the ninth week and she gave him a true thing about her brother and he counted her. I would like it said out loud in front of about nine people that he counted her, and that I do not say that to make him smaller, and that about nine of us in this town have been counted by him in nineteen days and about nine of us noticed and about nine did not.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked her for it.

---

He was asked why, and he said the true of it, and it was not a good answer, and about four people on that bank said so and about nine did not.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*Because I have been standing in front of people who can be sent for about a thing and asking them what they think about a thing, and about nine times in nineteen days I have found that the person who is against it is a person nobody can be sent for about. I did not find her because I was not looking. I was looking where a person can be sent for, and there is no person there, and there has never been one, and the reason is a thing a woman of forty-four said out loud on the sixth day of the second week that I have written on a sheet and have not once used.*”

About four people on that bank had that. About nine did not.

“*And I would like the other half said out loud in the same breath, because I am not going to do this every morning. I did not ask her a question on the sixth day of the ninth week. She told me a thing and I took it. That is the fifth time in nineteen days that somebody has told me a true thing and I have used it as a thing I know, and I have a word for that too and it is not a good one and I am not going to say it out loud in front of nine people about her.*”

Nobody on that bank said anything to that.

---

Then she was asked, out loud, in front of about nine people, by a person in that town, and she was ready, and what she said was four sentences and about four people in that town had not known any of it.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Ibb Marshe, from the bank, where she had been for about two hours and had come up the plank for the first time in seven days and had not said why she had stopped.

“*Because a woman of forty has just named a woman of thirty out loud, and about nine of us have not spoken to her in two days, and I have come up that plank to ask her one question. And to say that I am asking it, and that I am not going to pretend it is a different question from the one you have all been having.*”

Nobody on that plank moved.

“*Then ask it,*” said Perdy Lound.

“*A copy of that sheet went nine miles up the water at about the second hour yesterday. There is going to be more of them. I am asking whether you are against that, and if you are I would like you to say it out loud in front of about nine people, and if you are not I would like you to say that out loud in front of about nine people, and I am not going to hold either of them against you after Thursday.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*I am against it,*” said Perdy Lound. “*And I am going to say why in four sentences and then I am not going to say it again, and about four of you are going to have it a different way by the seventh hour and that is not a fault of anybody.*”

Nobody on that plank thanked her for saying it out loud.

“*You have written a thing in this town that a person can be sent for about, and it is going to be read out in rooms, and I do not mean this room. I mean rooms in four days and two days and a day, in houses where a person has never been to a plank, and about four people in each of those rooms is going to say the sentence out loud and about nine is going to hear it.*”

About four people on that plank said something at once. About nine did not.

“*My brother is one of the six that have not come and he is four days off and I have not been able to say out loud in three weeks whether he is ill. A thing that gets read out in rooms is a thing people go looking with, and when they go looking for the six they are not asking me, they are asking about a list. I do not want him found. I want to be the one who tells him.*”

About four people on that plank understood every word of it. About nine had a different shape of it by the seventh hour.

Nobody on that plank said anything to that for about as long as it takes to pole nine feet of a boat.

“*And I would like the other half of it said out loud in the same breath, because I am not going to be the only one who is right in a room in this town.*”

“*A man of thirty-one has answered a question about where he came from in four things in front of about nine people on the seventh and last day of the hundred and eighth week, and I was in that room and I have not been able to unhear one word of it since.*”

About four seconds went past and nobody filled them.

“*One of the four is about a year of his own future against a stone, and about four of us here now know more about that man than about the ground his cart stands on. That is what a record does. I am not saying it should not do it. I am saying my brother is one of six and there is no version of that where I want it done.*”

---

Nobody in Hask was relieved and nobody in Hask was forgiven and nobody in Hask was thanked.

The fever was forty-eight weeks and four days old. He had been in that county sixty-nine days and there is a third copy of that sheet nine miles up the water that he cannot get back and did not know about at the second hour on the Tuesday when he said out loud that he was not going to go and tell a woman of seventy-one.

And a woman of forty named a woman of thirty out loud, and the naming was done by an ordinary person in a town of about eleven hundred people and not by him, and the reason was that he has spent nineteen days asking people somebody can be sent for about.

And a woman of thirty said the reason out loud in four sentences, and the reason is a brother in a county four days off who is one of the six that have not come. She is against the copies, and she is right, and about nine people in that town have her a different way by the seventh hour and that is not a fault of anybody.

And she is still in that town, and she came up the plank again on the Wednesday afternoon, and she has said it once, and nobody has asked her for it a second time.
