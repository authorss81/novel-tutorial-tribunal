# Chapter 643: The First Day Of The Hundred And Fourteenth Week, And A Tuesday, And On Which A Woman Of Twenty-Eight Refuses Out Loud To Let A Bench Carry A Man Of Thirty-One, And He Loses A Small Point, And On Which A Name Is Said Flat Once

First day of the hundred and fourteenth week. His three hundred and ninety-third morning. Two hundred and forty-three days after the settlement. The fever fifty-one weeks and three days. One hundred and ninety-seven days since the division. One hundred and forty-three days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

He was sitting on the bench against the gunwale of the Gull at about the seventh hour on the Tuesday morning, and the bench had been carried up to the head of the plank on the Friday of the last week but one, and nobody had sat on it until the Friday of the last week, and he had sat on it then with a knife in his hand and had not used it.

About nine people were on that bank. About four of them had seen him sit there. About nine had not, and nobody on that bank thanked anybody for the sitting.

Ilyan Vester was thirty-one, and he had been in that county eighty-nine days, and in Hask forty-two days, and the forty-two is since he came up that plank, and the eighty-nine is since he came into that county, and the day he came in is not a day he was in.

He wanted work, and he said so out loud, and it was a small want, and losing it cost him more than a large one would. About four people on that bank knew that before he spoke and about nine did not.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan, from the bench, in about nine people’s hearing.

“*Garrin Tolley poled nine miles up and nine miles down on the Thursday with a sheet in a coat, and it took him about nine hours of a boat and a pole and about nine minutes of reading.*”

“*And Thursday was the fourth time the two have agreed, and the next one is the fifth, and I am asking to take the fifth for him.*”

“*I may read them out, and I may stand nine feet off with a knife, and I may carry a sheet as far as the end of that bank, and I may not carry the board.*”

“*I am asking to carry the sheet nine miles up that water on the Thursday, with him nine feet off, and I am asking in the open.*”

Nobody on that bank moved.

Then Sera Quill said no, and she said it out loud, and she said it in front of about nine people, and she said it to the bench and not to the man, and it was the first refusal anybody had heard from her in that county.

“*No.*”

About four seconds went past and nobody filled them.

“*And I am going to say what I am refusing, because about four of you have it as a man and about nine have it as a board, and it is neither.*”

“*I am refusing to let the thing you are sitting on be the thing that carries you, and the thing you are sitting on is a bench against the gunwale of a lighter called the Gull.*”

“*A bench is a place a person sits while another person works, and you have sat on it twice, and nobody thanked you for either sitting.*”

“*If you pole nine miles with a sheet in a coat, then a record is checked by a person it cannot reach, and a record checked by a person it cannot reach is a record it cannot be wrong.*”

“*And a record that cannot be wrong is a lid with a name on it, and a man of sixty-eight said that on the Friday of the eleventh week and was right.*”

“*And I am not going to let a man I have come four hundred miles to stand beside stand in the door of it, and that is the whole of my no.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked her for four hundred miles.

Then a man of sixty-eight who is not in a chair said the consequence, and it was small, and it was about work and not about a Crown. About four people on that bank heard how small it was and about nine did not.

“*Then he may not take the sixth,*” said Pell Varn, from the wall, in about nine people’s hearing.

“*And the rule he gave on the Thursday of the tenth week is twenty-six days old, and twenty-six is six hundred and forty-three less six hundred and seventeen, and I have done the subtraction out loud.*”

“*And under it a man of thirty-one may read out, and may stand nine feet off, and may carry a sheet to the end of that bank, and may not carry the board, and may not pole nine miles with a sheet in a coat.*”

“*And he has just asked for the one thing in that list he may not have, and he has lost it, and it is a point about a pole and not about a hearing.*”

About four people on that bank had the twenty-six. About nine had the pole and not the number.

Then Ilyan got up off the bench, and he stood on the gravel, and he said out loud that he held it, and it was the second time in eleven days he had been refused for his own reason and held it.

“*I hold it.*”

“*And I would like it said that losing a pole costs me more than losing a hearing would, because a hearing is a thing nine people do, and a pole is the only thing I can do with my own hands.*”

“*And I have been nobody’s in four counties for forty-two days in a way I can check, and work I can check was the whole of what I had.*”

Nobody on that bank said a word that could have moved him.

Then he said a name, and he said it once, and he said it flat, and it was the second time in that county, and the first time in Hask, and nothing whatever happened, and the first cost fell on an ordinary person.

“*I am going to say a name out loud. It is a name and not a shout, and I am not going to say it twice and I am not going to explain it.*”

About four people on that bank went still. About nine did not know there was anything coming.

“*The First Witness.*”

The nine of them were where they had been.

About four seconds went past and nothing said and nothing happened, and the water went on moving past that landing.

“*That is the name of the thing that said its own name in a weigh-house nine miles up this water about seventeen days ago in front of about four people.*”

“*And I said it on the Wednesday of the last week in a room with the door open, and nothing happened then, and nothing has happened now.*”

Nobody on that bank said anything at all.

Then the cost fell, and it fell on a woman of forty-nine who came down that road with a man of fifty-two four days before the first day of the last week, and who is not in a chair and is not a head in the book of heads and carts, and who said on the Friday that she did not know what a copy was.

She said it out loud herself, and she is ordinary, and she is not a side, and there is no person in it to be cross with.

“*I have heard it now twice, and I did not know what a copy was on Friday, and I do not know what that name is on Tuesday.*”

“*And about nine mouths in that yard nine miles up this water have it. About nine mouths on this bank have it now, and I am one of the nine.*”

“*And I cannot be sent for about and the man I came down that road with cannot be sent for about. About four people who have a sheet will come and ask me what I heard, and there is nobody in four counties who can say to them that they may not.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked her for the hearing.

Then Sera Quill said the true of the name, and she said it flat, and she did not make it a side, because it is sincere about one thing and wrong about another, and a name carried by about nine people is not the enemy it is not.

“*It is sincere about stopping another Great Silence, and I have read enough in four hundred miles to know that silence is a real thing.*”

“*And it is wrong about what people owe a thing that kept them, and owing is a thing people decide in rooms, and a thing that decides it for them is a lid.*”

“*And I would like it said that a name carried by nine people is not a side, and a woman of forty-nine carrying it is not a fault.*”

Nobody on that bank made it anything else.

Then a man of about fifty-two who cannot see well said what carrying costs when eyes are poor, and he said it out loud, and his fifth line is his and not the man of forty-three’s, and he has been known for poor sight eleven years.

“*I came down that road four days before the first day of the last week with a woman of forty-nine, and I asked her on the Friday what a copy was, and she said she did not know.*”

“*And I offered on the Tuesday to have a Crown speak for a man of thirty-one, and it would have worked in about four days, and he said no to it on the Wednesday in about nine sentences.*”

“*And I came nine miles down this water on the Wednesday to hear the answer, and I am still here on the Tuesday, and nobody thanked me for the coming.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked him for eleven years.

Then a woman of forty-one with two carts said what a bench is a figure of, and she is on the board. About nine people on that bank heard a bench counted against a pole.

“*A bench carried up to the head of a plank for whoever is on a board to sit on is a figure of nine days of one person’s working life spent sitting.*”

“*And a pole nine miles up and nine miles down is a figure of about nine hours. About nine minutes of reading is what it takes to make two copies agree.*”

“*And sitting cannot carry a man into a hearing, and poling cannot carry him either while nobody can be sent for about him, and that is why losing a pole is small and heavy at once.*”

About four people on that bank had the nine hours. About nine had the bench and not the hours.

---

The fever was fifty-one weeks and three days old. He had been in that county eighty-nine days, and a woman of twenty-eight refused out loud in front of about nine people to let a bench carry him, and he lost a small point about a pole.

And the point is that he may not pole nine miles with a sheet in a coat, and he may read out and may stand nine feet off and may carry a sheet to the end of that bank, and losing the pole costs him more than losing a hearing would.

And a name was said flat once in that county for the second time, and nothing happened, and the first cost of the second naming fell on an ordinary woman of forty-nine who did not know what a copy was on the Friday.

And the bench against the gunwale of the Gull was empty at the tenth hour with no dew on it. About four people standing about had seen a man get up off it, and about nine had not watched him do it.
