# Chapter 642: The Seventh And Last Day Of The Hundred And Thirteenth Week, And A Monday, And On Which A Person From Outside That County Comes Up That Cart Road On Her Own Legs And Says Out Loud In The First Thing She Says What She Is

Seventh and last day of the hundred and thirteenth week. His three hundred and ninety-second morning. Two hundred and forty-two days after the settlement. The fever fifty-one weeks and two days. One hundred and ninety-six days since the division. One hundred and forty-two days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

She came up that cart road at about the third hour on the Monday morning with dust on her coat to the knees and a bag with nothing else in it but two letters with the seals unbroken, and nobody on that bank had sent for her, and nobody in four counties can be sent for about her, and she had not been asked to be in that county.

About nine people were on that bank. About four of them had heard of her. About nine had not, and nobody on that bank thanked anybody for the walking.

Ilyan Vester was thirty-one, and he had been in that county eighty-eight days, and in Hask forty-one days, and the forty-one is a count of days since he came up that plank, and the eighty-eight is a count since he came into that county, and the evening he came in is not a day he was in, and he keeps the two apart.

He wanted to go to her before anybody spoke, and he did not, and it was the first time in forty-one days he had not gone first. About four people on that bank noticed it and about nine did not.

The wind was off the water, and the Gull was lying with her head to the landing, and the pole was under her fore thwart, and a board about nine inches long was in a coat on a peg in a front room, and the Crown was on a barrel in that room with nine names under it.

About four people on that bank looked at the room and about nine looked at the road.

Then she said the first thing, and she said it out loud, and she said what she is, and she is not anybody that Crown built and she did not come because of it.

“*I am Sera Quill and I am twenty-eight and I am not of this county and I have not been asked to be, and I did not come because of a board on a barrel nine miles down this water.*”

Nobody on that bank moved.

About four seconds went past and nobody filled them.

“*I came four hundred miles with two letters nobody has read, and both of them are in that bag with the seals unbroken, and there is a third thing in the inside pocket of his coat that came back the same four hundred miles, and I am not going to ask for any of the three.*”

About four people on that bank had that. About nine had the miles and not the letters.

“*And I keep no chair in this town and no book and no landing, and there is no form in this county that says anybody may be sent for about me, and I would like about four of you to have heard that before about nine of you ask.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked her for four hundred miles.

Then a woman of forty-one with two carts said what everybody on that bank was standing in front of, because somebody has to, and she is on the board and she wrote on it.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Ibb Marshe, who is forty-one and keeps two carts and has a son of thirteen.

“*Because a person has come up that cart road on her own legs on a Monday morning. About nine people are standing about, and nobody sent for her.*”

“*And about nine people in this town have spent nine days building a thing that speaks for people who cannot be asked. About four of them cannot describe what it is for.*”

About four people on that bank counted that against each other.

Then Sera Quill said why she had come, and it was not the Crown. About four people on that bank heard it at once and about nine heard the road and not the reason.

“*I came because a boy of thirteen wrote a sheet out with his own hand on the Tuesday morning of the eleventh week. About four people in four counties who have one of them are going to come and find him.*”

“*And his mother cannot be sent for about and he cannot be sent for about, and there is nobody in four counties who can say to them that they may not.*”

“*And I have been twenty-eight years learning that a hand that writes is the only door into a thing written, and I would like it said that I did not learn it in this county.*”

Nobody on that bank said anything to that.

Then Ilyan said her name out loud, and he said it once. About four people on that bank had been waiting forty-one days to hear him say it and about nine had not known there was anything to hear.

“*Sera.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*I have been in Hask forty-one days, and I have been in that county eighty-eight days, and I have not said that name out loud in any room in either count.*”

“*And I am not going to say the rest of it here, because about nine people in this town have watched me say the wrong half first for forty-one days.*”

“*And I would like about four of you to notice that I did not go to her before she spoke.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked him for not going.

Then a man of sixty-eight who gave that town its first rule on the Thursday of the tenth week said what a person from outside is, and he said it flat, and it was about the room and not about her.

Pell Varn is sixty-eight, and he keeps the last quarter mile, and he is not in a chair, and there is no form in that county that lets a man ask to be corrected in one, and he put nine names on a board and he is one of the nine, and the rule he gave is twenty-five days old, and twenty-five is six hundred and forty-two less six hundred and seventeen, and he did the subtraction out loud.

“*A person from outside is a person no form reaches, and this town has one of those already, and he is thirty-one, and now it has two.*”

“*And a Crown of about nine people that wants to speak for every person nobody can be sent for about will want to speak for her, because that is what a Crown is for.*”

“*And she has just said out loud that she did not come because of it. About four of you have heard that and about nine have not.*”

About four people on that bank had the twenty-five. About nine had the outside and not the number.

Then a woman of seventy-one who keeps a house that is not hers came up to the top of that bank on her own legs, and she looked at the woman of twenty-eight for a long time, and nobody asked either of them to speak.

Eliza Lark is seventy-one, and she has kept that house eleven years, and she came up that cart road on the Saturday of the last week to say she does not want to be spoken for, and the reason is about four years, and she said she would not say anything else about it in that town.

“*You have come four hundred miles with nothing on you but two letters,*” said Eliza Lark, and it was not a question. About nine people on that bank heard a woman of seventy-one speak to a woman of twenty-eight for the first time.

“*And you have said out loud that nobody can be sent for about you, and I have said the same thing about myself in about nine sentences, and the difference is that nobody has come to my door in eleven years and somebody has come four hundred miles to yours.*”

Nobody on that bank answered her.

“*And I am not going to ask you anything else, and I would like it said that I have not asked you this.*”

Then Sera Quill answered her, and it was given to her and not to the room. About nine people on that bank heard a thing said to one person and not to eight others.

“*I have not come to be spoken for and I have not come to speak, and I have come to carry what a speaking leaves.*”

“*And I have not got a form for it and I am not going to ask for one.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked either of them for the carrying.

Then she set her bag down on the gravel, and dust came off the bottom of it. About nine people on that bank saw a bag with nothing else in it but two letters. About four saw dust and not letters, and nobody asked to see either.

“*Both of mine are in that bag and neither has been opened, and the page that came back four hundred miles is in that pocket, and I am not going to ask for it here.*”

“*And I have kept that pocket in mind for forty-one days without putting my hand in it, and that is a thing one person can do for another without a form.*”

About four people on that bank had the forty-one. About nine had the bag and not the days.

Then a woman of forty-one with two carts asked one thing about the road, and she asked it out loud, because a road two days long is a thing she can check with her own hands.

“*The road down is about two days, and I drove it down on the Sunday and up on the Wednesday, and I drove it myself with nobody on the shafts.*”

“*And four hundred miles is not two days, and I would like it said that nobody in this town can be sent for about four hundred miles.*”

“*And a cart with one wheel making a noise came up out of the low place with me driving it, and nobody thanked me for the driving.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked her for two days.

Then a man of thirty-one put his hand on his own coat where the inside pocket is, and he did not take anything out. About nine people on that bank saw a man touch a pocket and leave it shut. About four understood what shut means and about nine did not.

“*It is still there, and I have not read it, and I am not going to read it in a room to make a point.*”

Then a man of thirty-eight said what the day would need, and he said it out loud, because a boat and a pole and a board about nine inches long are load-bearing, and the fourth reading was on the Thursday, and the next one is the fifth.

“*Thursday was the fourth time the two of them have been in a room together, and the fourth time they have agreed, and next Thursday is the fifth, and I poled nine miles up and nine miles down to make it true.*”

“*And about nine hours of a boat and a pole and about nine minutes of reading is what it takes, and I am the one that pays it, and I am not going to be thanked for it and I am not going to stop.*”

About four people on that bank had the fifth. About nine had the hours and not the week.

---

The fever was fifty-one weeks and two days old. He had been in that county eighty-eight days, and a woman of twenty-eight came up that cart road on her own legs on a Monday morning with dust to the knees and two letters with the seals unbroken, and the first thing she said out loud was what she is.

And she is not anybody that Crown built, and she did not come because of it, and there is no form in that county that says anybody may be sent for about her, and nobody in four counties can be sent for about her either.

And a man of thirty-one said her name once and said nothing else, and a woman of seventy-one spoke to her without asking her, and a man of thirty-eight said out loud that Thursday was the fourth time two copies have agreed and next Thursday is the fifth.

And the board about nine inches long with thirty-two notches cut in its edge was in a coat on a peg in a front room. About four people in a town of about eleven hundred had held it that week. About nine had not.
