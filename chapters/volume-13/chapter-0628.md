# Chapter 628: The Seventh And Last Day Of The Hundred And Eleventh Week, And A Monday, And On Which A Woman Of Thirty Who Told The Truth About A Brother In Four Sentences On The Wednesday Says Out Loud At About The Second Hour That She Is Against Eleven Copies Of A Thing That Can Be Sent For About, And On Which A Man Of Sixty-Eight Who Gave The Rule On The Thursday Of The Tenth Week Says Out Loud That The Copies Go Over Her And That He Is Not Going To Take The Rule Back On A Monday Because A Woman Of Thirty Is Right, And On Which She Goes Four Days Up The Water At About The Third Hour And Nobody Says One Word That Could Have Moved Her, And On Which Nine Miles Up That Water In A Yard A Line About A Man Of Thirty-One Is Read Out Loud For The First Time In A Town Of About Eleven Hundred People And A Man Of Thirty-Eight Says A Thing That Was Not Said By A Person

Seventh and last day of the hundred and eleventh week. His three hundred and seventy-eighth morning. Two hundred and twenty-eight days after the settlement. The fever forty-nine weeks and two days. One hundred and eighty-two days since the division. One hundred and twenty-eight days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

They counted it out loud at about the second hour on the Monday morning at the head of the plank, and about nine people were there and ten of the twenty-nine chairs were stood up along the starboard side of the Gull with a name against them and nobody in them, and the man of thirty-eight had four sheets of deal under the after thwart and had been up since about the fifth hour.

The count was about nine for and about four against, and the man of sixty-one gave it with the working and nobody in that bank had asked him to.

“*About nine for and about four against, and about nine people who have not said, and about four of the nine for have not said either and are being counted as for because they were in the room when it was said. I am telling you that so that nobody in this town goes and says there were thirteen when there were about nine.*”

About four people on that bank counted that. About nine did not.

Then a woman of thirty who had been ready since Saturday and had not said so until the second hour said it, and it took her about nine sentences, and about nine people on that bank had known every word of it since Wednesday and about nine of them had not.

“*I am against all eleven of them, and I am not going to pretend I have not been against them since Wednesday, and I said the reason then in four sentences and I am not going to say it again today. A woman of thirty-four said on Saturday that it is not a second time, and a man of sixty-eight has spent a morning on it, and a boy of eleven is asking to go to a cart.*”

About four people on that bank understood that. About nine did not.

“*Four are going up the water under that thwart and five are going two days down this road in a cart with a man of forty-one and a woman of thirty-four, and one is staying here, and one has no arrangement, and about nine of us have known every one of those four numbers since yesterday morning. I have been against all eleven since Wednesday afternoon and I have not said so in a room until now, and that is mine and nobody else has to do anything about it.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked her for saying it out loud.

---

Then a man of sixty-eight who is not in a chair said that the copies went over her, and he said why, and about four people on that bank had the whole of it at once and about nine did not, and it was the eleventh day of a rule he had given himself.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Wenna Callow. “*Because a woman of thirty has said out loud that she is against eleven copies and a man of sixty-eight who gave the rule is about to say that they go, and about four of us have been waiting for him to say it and about nine have not, and both of those are the same thing.*”

“*I am standing in front of about nine people and a rule I gave on the Thursday of the tenth week, and I am going to say that the copies go over a woman of thirty who is right, and I am going to say why in about nine sentences, and I am not going to apologise for it afterwards and I am not going to be thanked for it and there is nothing in this for me.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*If nine people can stop a copy, then a person can be sent for about having stopped it.*”

About four people on that bank had the whole of it. About nine had a count and a road and nothing else.

“*And the reason there is a sheet of deal in this county at all, and the reason anybody can be sent for about a chair in it, is that there is no column in this county with a person’s name at the top of it and no form that renews anybody and no rate on anybody. I gave that rule on a Thursday and I meant it about a plank and a quarter mile of a cart road. I am not going to take it back on a Monday because a woman of thirty has turned out to be right about a brother in a county four days off.*”

About four people on that bank said something at once. About nine did not.

“*And I would like it said out loud that she knew. She said on Wednesday that a thing that gets read out in rooms is a thing people go looking with, and she has known every day since what this rule does, and she is not here because she has been outvoted by about nine people in the open on a Monday morning. She is here because a rule that nine people can stop is not a rule and is a lid, and I gave the first lid in this county eleven days ago by accident and I have been standing on it ever since.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked him for the rule.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*Say it out loud so that about nine people in this town have it before the fourth hour, because a stranger from a county nine miles off is going to be asked about it by somebody in about four days. I am going up the water this morning with four sheets under a thwart and about nine people in this county are against them and about four of the nine are the only people in this county anybody could be sent for about a chair, and I am not going to pretend to you that I have not thought about the four.*”

About four seconds went past.

Nobody on that bank said anything to that, and it was the first time in nineteen days that a person in that town had said a thing and had about nine people who had not been asked about it, and the man of thirty-one had done it about nine times.

---

Then she went, at about the third hour, up the cart road with a place in a cart that goes up as far as a town a day off and on from there for three more. She had arranged it on the Saturday and had told about four people and not the other five, and nobody sent for her and nobody asked her for a reason and nobody said one word to her that could have moved her.

Nobody on that bank said anything at all as she went up it.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan, to nobody in particular, on a bank, in about nine people’s hearing.

“*I am not going after her.*”

“*I want it said out loud in front of about nine people that I am not going up that road after a woman of thirty, and that a man of thirty-one said on the Friday of the tenth week that he was not going to go and ask a woman of forty-one why she had stopped coming up that plank. He has not gone after that woman either, and I have now done the same thing twice in ten days about two different people. Both times I was right and both times I said it out loud in advance.*”

About four people on that bank had that. About nine did not.

“*And I would like somebody to say out loud that the reason I am not going is not that she is right. She is right.*”

“*I have known she was right since Wednesday at about the fourth hour and I have done nothing about it for three days, and the thing I have done for three days is make a record that goes where records go. About nine people in a yard nine miles up this water are going to be two halves about it by the seventh hour, and that is a thing I built and I am not going to say it was the weather.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked anybody for anything at all.

---

Nine miles up the water, in the yard of a landing, at about the second hour of the same Monday, a man of forty-three who has kept that landing for nineteen years wrote a line on a sheet of deal with a knife out of a whittling block, in the open, in front of about nine people, and then read the whole of the sheet out loud from the first line to the last.

The two originals agreed when he had finished. Thirty-two lines, and the thirty-second one was the thirty-second one that had been in the boat at Hask since Tuesday, and the thing that was not true this week came true in a yard at about the third hour, and about four people in that yard said so out loud and about nine of them said nothing.

Then he started again from the first line, because he is the man who keeps that landing and nobody else in that yard had the right to read a sheet on a Monday, and he did not skip a line and he did not hurry the one that mattered.

“*The ground: a building with a bad coffee machine in it, in a country that is not this one, a hundred and nine miles off and across a sea, where a mark of two letters and a number is signed at the bottom of about nine hundred records. The name: one that nobody in this county makes. The day: the seventh and last day of the hundred and eighth week, a Monday, when a man of thirty-eight asked a man of thirty-one where he came from at about the fourth hour, in front of about nine people, and he answered in four things and none of the four of them was the answer.*”

About nine people in that yard heard a line about a man they had never seen.

Nobody in that yard moved.

Then a man of about fifty-two at the back of that yard said the true thing about it in four sentences, and about four people in that yard had known it before he said it and about nine of them had not.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Garrin Tolley.

“*That is the man who was in the weigh-house on the Monday morning three weeks ago with the four of us. I know it because I read that ground out of the boat on the Sunday and I read it on the Monday in this yard and I wrote it on a slip of board and the slip of board is in that boat, and I have been the only person in four counties who could carry it.*”

About four seconds went past.

Nobody in that yard thanked him for nine miles.

“*And I would like the last line read again, because about nine of you have not heard it and about four of you have not understood it.*”

“*The ground: the gravel and withy at the top of the drain above the head. The name: Wren Sayer. Gone up the water in a lighter about eleven years ago and not heard of since. He is my brother. I have not said his name on that boat in nineteen years and I have said it four times in nine days, and I am saying it a fifth time in a yard where about nine people are standing, and I would like it said that I do not know whether I am saying it for him.*”

About four seconds went past.

Then he said the four words, and he said them flat, in about nine people’s hearing, at about the fourth hour of a Monday afternoon, in a yard at a landing nine miles up the water, and he did not raise his voice and he did not say anything else afterwards.

About nine people in that yard heard it and about four of them had heard it before in about four counties and about nine of them had not.

---

Then the man of forty-three who keeps that landing asked one question out loud in the open in that yard, and he said before he asked it that he was going to ask it and that he was the only person in that yard anybody could be sent for about. About four people in that yard said nothing and about nine said something at once.

“*I have kept this landing nineteen years and I am going to ask one thing out loud in a yard where about nine people can hear me and there is nobody here I can go home with afterwards. I am asking it because a man has just said four words in my yard and about four of us in this yard have heard them before and about nine have not, and I am not going to go about for a week working out whether it is here.*”

Nobody in that yard moved.

“*Is it in this room.*”

About four seconds went past and nobody filled them and nothing said.

“*Then I have asked it in the open in a yard with about nine people in it, and I am the only person anybody could be sent for about in this yard, and there is no form in this county that will let anybody come back on the Tuesday and ask me what I meant by it. I would like about four of you to notice that I did it anyway, and about nine of you to notice that I have not asked anybody to stand next to me.*”

Nobody in that yard said anything to that.

---

Then the man of thirty-eight said the thing that is larger than that yard, and he said it because there were two halves in it, and about four people in that yard were on one of them and about nine on the other and every one of both was right.

“*About four of the people standing in this yard think those four sheets should never have been read out in this yard on a Monday, and about nine of us think they should have been, and I am one of the four and I read them out myself.*”

About four people in that yard said something at once. About nine did not.

“*And there is a sheet of deal like that one in every place there is a town. Not in every one. In every one there is a record and in about four of them it is a book and in about four it is a wall and in about one it is a set of chairs, and about nine people in the world have never been in a room where anybody could be sent for about any of it.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*So I am asking for one thing in this yard, in the open, at about the fourth hour of a Monday, and I am not asking you and I am not deciding it and nobody has asked me whether I would rather not.*”

About four seconds went past and nobody in that yard filled them.

“*I am asking that all of them be read in one room. Every one. The ones that disagree with each other especially. Because about four of us have just been two halves about a record for nine days in a town of about eleven hundred people and both of us were right, and about nine people in this world are going to be two halves about their own records and I would like to be in a room when that happens and hear it.*”

Nobody in that yard said anything to that.

Nobody in that yard thanked him for asking.

---

The fever was forty-nine weeks and two days old. He had been in that county seventy-four days and a woman of thirty went four days up the water on the Monday morning of the seventh and last day of the hundred and eleventh week, because a rule eleven days old cannot be stopped by about nine people. The woman was right, and nobody said one word to her that could have moved her.

And nine miles up that water in a yard, a line about him was read out loud for the first time in front of about nine people who had never seen him, in the plain form of a line on a sheet of deal. A man of thirty-eight said a thing that was not said by a person, and a man of forty-three who keeps that landing asked in the open whether it was in that room, and nobody answered him. He is the only person in that yard anybody can be sent for about.

And the man of thirty-eight asked for a hearing about every record there is, in a yard, out loud, at about the fourth hour of a Monday, because about nine of us were two halves about a sheet of deal. Nobody in that yard was asked whether they wanted it, and it is not that man’s decision and it is not that man’s fault. The man of thirty-one was nine miles down the water on a bank and had not asked for it and was not the reason for it.

And the count of the chairs is ten and it has not moved since the Wednesday of the tenth week. Four sheets went up the water under a thwart, and five went two days down the road in a cart, and one stayed inside the after thwart of that boat, and a woman of thirty-four who has been in a chair since she was nineteen is in that cart.
