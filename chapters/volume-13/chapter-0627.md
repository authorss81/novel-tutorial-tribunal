# Chapter 627: The Sixth Day Of The Hundred And Eleventh Week, And A Sunday, And On Which Eleven Sheets Of Deal Are Copied In About Four Hours In A Front Room On The Low Side Of A Landing By About Nine People Who Can Write And A Boy Of Eleven Who Had Never Copied A Whole One Before, And On Which A Man Of Sixty-One Gives The Count Out Loud And Says What It Is A Figure Of, And On Which It Is Found Out That All Eleven Are Copies Of A Thing That Under Its Own Rule Is Not True This Week, And On Which One Of The Eleven Has No Arrangement At All And It Is The One A Man Of Thirty-One Wrote On A Friday And Did Not Write On Sunday

Sixth day of the hundred and eleventh week. His three hundred and seventy-seventh morning. Two hundred and twenty-seven days after the settlement. The fever forty-nine weeks and one day. One hundred and eighty-one days since the division. One hundred and twenty-seven days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

About nine people can write in that town. There is no other table in it, so the copying was done on the floor of a front room on the low side of a landing with a board on a barrel against the wall, and the door was open all day because it was a Sunday. About nine people in a town of about eleven hundred people who do not live on that road came down it in the course of the day to carry something, and stood in the doorway and did not come in.

Nobody in that room thanked anybody for the eleven sheets.

The sheet is about four feet by a foot and four inches, and the man of sixty-one gave that out loud at about the ninth hour with the working, and about four people in that room had measured it themselves and about nine had not.

“*Thirty-two lines at about two inches a line is sixty-four inches, which is a foot and four inches, and that is the width the lines go down, and the ground goes across the four feet because a hand is about nine inches and a ground in this county is not shorter than a hand.*”

About four people in that room counted that. About nine of the others had cut withies at some point in their lives and could do it in their heads.

“*Now where the deal came from. Four off two cart beds, which is two a bed, and the two beds belong to a woman of forty-one who has not been up that plank in eight days and brought the lot herself at about the seventh hour and said out loud that she was not going to be thanked for it and would rather it was counted.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*Two off a thwart of the Gull, which is the same eleven feet, and the man of thirty-eight gave those on the Saturday and did not say one word about it until Monday. One off a hurdle, and there is a hurdle against a wall at the head of that quarter mile because about nine of them were pegged at the head of the plank on the Friday of the tenth week and they came out on the Monday, and the man of fifty-four let me take his and said nothing about it.*”

About four people in that room had that. About nine of the rest had a hurdle in their hands and were not thinking about a thwart.

“*Three off a barn door on the low side of the landing that a woman of seventy-one is having taken off its hinges anyway, because it is eleven feet by about three feet, and she said out loud in front of about nine people that she would rather the boards went on a thing that is being checked twice than on a fire.*”

About four people in that room had that. About nine of the rest were at the door with a hurdle in their hands.

“*And one off the lid of the press that a woman of forty-four keeps the book in, which is deal and about the right size and about four inches thick. She said out loud that she had it made out of a cart lid about nine years ago and does not know what a cart lid is about four feet across and does not want to.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*Four and two and one and three and one is eleven. That is eleven sheets of deal in a town of about eleven hundred people, and it is the first time in nineteen years that there has been more than one of anything in this county that anybody could be sent for about.*”

Nobody in that room thanked him for the count.

“*And I would like it said what it is a figure of. It is a figure of how many pieces of wood there are. It is not a figure of how many people have read one of them, and I have been in about four rooms this week and I have counted about nine people who have read one and about nine who have read two, and about nine people in this town have read none and about four of them are in this room. There is no form in this county that would let me find the second number and I have not been able to think of one.*”

---

Then the copying was done, and it was done in about four hours of daylight. About nine people wrote for about four hours, and one of the nine was a boy of eleven who had never copied a whole sheet in his life, and one of the nine was a man of sixty-one who spent about four hours reading out loud instead of writing a line.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Nevin Trask, in about the ninth hour of the day. “*Because a count has been asked for twice this morning and I have given the count of the deal and not the count of the writing, and I am going to give the second one, and about four of you are going to find out that I did not do any of it.*”

Nobody in that room moved.

“*A sheet of that thing takes a person who can write about four hours, and there are about nine hours in a day and about nine of us who can write, and that is about nine sheets if everybody writes and nobody says a thing. It is eleven.*”

About four people in that room counted that. About nine did not.

“*The difference is two, and one of them is the boy of eleven, who took about six hours on his and made two good ones wrong in the middle and cut them out with a knife, which is what the rule is for. And the other one is mine. I have not written a line in a book in eleven years that anybody could be sent for about, and I chose to read out loud for about four hours instead, and I am telling you that I chose it and that nobody made me.*”

About four people in that room said nothing at all.

“*And I would like it said that a man of thirty-one wrote one of these on a Friday evening in a hand nobody in this county can do, and that it is not the one that goes. The reason it is not the one that goes is that he is not a person anybody can be sent for about, and I have known that since about a Tuesday.*”

---

Then the eleven were read against the copy kept at the landing, which was nine miles up the water and could not be read against anything in that room, and about four people in that room said so and about nine had not thought about it until about the tenth hour.

It was the woman of thirty who had said it, and she said it in nine sentences and she said the working out loud and about four people in that room understood it at once.

“*The copy kept at the landing has thirty-one lines on it. It has had thirty-one lines on it since Monday. There are thirty-two on this one and there have been thirty-two on this one since Tuesday, when a man of thirty-one said out loud in front of about nine people what a woman of seventy-one told him and the woman who keeps the book wrote it and put the day on it.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*So the two do not agree, and the rule says the thing is not true, and I have worked it out twice this week to be sure of it and it is the same both times. A woman of seventy-one is not true this week. And every one of these eleven is a copy of a thing that is not true this week, and about nine of us have spent the whole of a Sunday copying it, and that is not anybody’s fault and I would like it said that it is not anybody’s fault.*”

Nobody in that room thanked her for the Sunday.

“*And it comes right on Monday if a man of thirty-eight takes one of the originals up the water with a line written on it, which is about nine minutes of a boat. And I have not asked him to and I am not going to, and I would like about four of you to notice that I am a woman of thirty and that I have not asked anybody for anything all day, and about four of you have not noticed that either.*”

About four people in that room understood that. About nine of the rest were writing and carried on writing.

---

Then the arrangement for the eleven was made out loud, in about nine sentences, and it covered ten of them, and the man of sixty-one said the number that was left.

“*Four go up the water under the after thwart of the Gull with the man of thirty-eight on the Monday, and he says he will take four because a thwart is a foot and four inches wide and they are a foot and four inches wide and there is no more room than that in a boat of this size.*”

About four people in that room counted that. About nine did not.

“*One goes two days down the water in the cart of a man of forty-one from a town in the county of Ashenfield, who put his own name against a chair in that town and has not taken it off, and who asked for a no on Saturday and got about four of them. About nine people did not offer one because they had not worked out what he wanted.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*And four more go in the same cart, because a woman of thirty-four who came nine miles down this water nine days ago and has been in that room twice this week has a house in that county and is going home on the Monday. There is room in a cart for four more and she said so out loud on the Friday and nobody asked her, and about four people in that room noticed and about nine did not.*”

Nobody in that room thanked her for the room in the cart.

“*And one stays. It is nailed inside the after thwart of this boat, and the rule says there are two copies and one is there, and that is the one that does not go anywhere and it will be here on the Wednesday and the Thursday and the next Monday.*”

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*And the eleventh has no arrangement at all. I have not got one and I am going to say so out loud in a room where about nine people can hear it, because about four of you are going to work out by the fourth hour that eleven minus four minus one minus four minus one is one, and I have not got a person for it, and there is no form in this county that would let me send for one.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*And I would like it said out loud that the eleventh is the one a man of thirty-one wrote on a Friday evening in a hand nobody in this county can do, and that it is the best-looking sheet of deal in four counties. He is not allowed to carry it and nobody else in this room can be sent for about it. About four of you have worked out that a woman of thirty is going four days up the water on Monday morning and has told four people and not the other five, and I am not going to say the rest of it in front of her.*”

About four people in that room understood that. About nine did not.

---

The fever was forty-nine weeks and one day old. He had been in that county seventy-three days, and eleven sheets of deal were copied in about four hours in a front room on the low side of a landing by about nine people who can write and a boy of eleven. A man of sixty-one read out loud for four hours instead of writing, and gave the count, and said what it is a figure of, which is how many pieces of wood there are and not how many people have read one of them.

And all eleven of them are copies of a thing that is not true this week under a rule six days old, because the copy kept at a landing nine miles up the water has thirty-one lines on it and this one has thirty-two. It comes right on Monday if a man of thirty-eight takes a line up the water in about nine minutes of a boat.

And ten of the eleven have an arrangement, and four go up the water on Monday with the man of thirty-eight and five go two days down the water in a cart with a man of forty-one and a woman of thirty-four who has a house in that county, and one stays nailed inside the after thwart of that boat.

And the eleventh has no arrangement, and it is the best-looking sheet of deal in four counties, and it is in a county that nobody is going to, and a woman of thirty is going four days up the water on Monday morning and has told about four people and not the other five.
