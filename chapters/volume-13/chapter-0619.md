# Chapter 619: The Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Tenth Week, And A Saturday, And On Which A Woman Of Thirty-Four Who Came Down The Water On A Friday Asks A Town Of About Eleven Hundred People For A Thing That Is Not A Chair And Is Not Out Of One, And What She Wants Is To Be Asked, And On Which A Man Of Forty-One From A Town Two Days Off Says The Thing About The Year Out Loud Again In About Nine People’s Hearing, And On Which A Man Of Thirty-One Walks Up A Plank And Asks The Woman Who Keeps The Book To Write A Line For A Woman From Another County, And On Which He Is Stopped By The Woman From Another County, And On Which Nobody In Hask Was Relieved And Nobody Was Forgiven And Nobody Was Thanked

Fifth day of the hundred and tenth week. His three hundred and sixty-ninth morning. Two hundred and nineteen days after the settlement. The fever forty-eight weeks and no days. One hundred and seventy-three days since the division. One hundred and nineteen days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

She had been asleep on a settle in a house on the low side of the landing since Friday afternoon and she had been up two days and she asked for the boat before anybody thought she wanted it.

Nobody on that plank thanked her for coming nine miles.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Garrin Tolley, from the after end, and he said it to her and not to the man of thirty-one, and about four people on that deck noticed that and about nine did not. “*Because a woman of thirty-four has come up a plank in a town she has never been in and has asked for the boat. About nine of us have been talking in front of her for two days about the one thing she has come about. I would like it said out loud that we have not done that on purpose and it has happened anyway.*”

“*I am standing in front of about nine people, and I have been in a chair since I was nineteen, and I am thirty-four. I came down the water on Friday with the man of forty-one who put his own name against a chair in my town. I have been in a room in this town for two days, and about nine of you have been saying my own business in front of me since Friday afternoon.*”

Nobody spoke for about four seconds.

“*Say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*My name is Dorcas Slee and I am thirty-four. I have wanted to be asked for about eleven years whether I wanted the chair I am in. I have come nine miles on a cart to ask this town whether a chair can be got out of, and I would like it said out loud in front of about nine people that I am not asking for it to be got out of.*”

About four people on that plank said something at once. About nine did not.

“*Then say what you are asking for,*” said Ilyan.

“*I am asking to be asked. That is the whole of it. I have been in this chair since I was nineteen, which is fifteen years, and I said yes in the open in about nine people’s hearing and I was not asked whether I wanted it and I have not been asked since, and there is no form in the county of Ashenfield that asks anybody anything. I got down off the landing at four years old and went back up at nine and I have known what a chair is for longer than most of the people standing here.*”

Nobody on that plank said anything to that.

“*And I would like the other half of it said, because I have come nine miles and I am not going to be a woman who said only the easy half. I have found out, in a kitchen on the low side, from a woman of seventy-one who was never asked either, that in this town a person can get out of a chair by saying no out loud in the open. I did not know that.*”

It was about four seconds.

“*It has taken me fifteen years and nine miles and a kitchen, and I have found out that the door has been open the whole time, and it is on the other side of a wall I did not know was a wall.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*So I am not asking for the door. I am asking because saying no is the only way out, and it is a one-way door. In about four years there will be a woman in my county who says no, and then there will be a form that says a person who says no cannot say yes again. I have watched a man of forty-one do the other thing to himself, and I am not going to be a woman who finds out that a no is the end of it.*”

Nobody on that plank thanked her for the nine miles.

---

Then a man of forty-one said the thing about the year out loud, in about nine people’s hearing, for the second time, and about four people on that deck had heard the first time and about nine had not.

Heston Brake had come up the plank at the fourth hour. He had not been in this town in his life before Friday. He was forty-one and he was of a town two days off in another county, and on the Wednesday of the ninth week he had stood under a lamp nine miles up this water and had about nine hundred words read at him by a boy of fourteen. He had gone back on purpose four weeks later and had it read at him again.

“*Then say what you came up this water for and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan, and he had not said it on Friday and had slept on it.

“*I put my own name against a chair at the head of a drain in my own town in the county of Ashenfield, and I have not taken it off. I came up here to say that to the man who wrote the thing I said it with. I am saying it to him and not to the woman, because she is the one I would have to say it to twice.*”

About four seconds went past.

Nobody on that plank said anything to that.

“*I said it on the Wednesday in about nine people’s hearing and I used his line about the year, and I said that because there is nothing in that document that stops me, and he said out loud on the Wednesday that he cannot put the sentence right without saying more, and he has not said more. I have come nine miles to find out whether he is going to, and I am not sorry about the chair and I am not going to take it off.*”

About four people on that deck had that. About nine did not.

---

Then a man of thirty-one walked up the plank and asked the woman who keeps the book to write a line for a woman from another county, and it was the fifth time in fifteen days, and he was stopped on the plank by the woman it was for.

She had come up the plank eleven minutes before and had been standing at the after end with her back to the book, because the book was not hers and it was not about her county, and she had heard the whole of it.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Dorcas Slee, and she had not said anything in this town until that second, and about four people on that plank turned round.

“*I am standing in front of a man of thirty-one who has just walked up a plank and asked a woman in a book to write my name in it, in a town of about eleven hundred people, about nine miles from the county I live in. I would like it said out loud in front of all of you that he did it to help me.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*You have done a thing in five counties and I have watched you do it twice on a deck in two days, and the thing is that somebody else carries it out of the room. I have been in a chair for fifteen years and I have never once been in a room where the person who made the thing was still in it afterwards. I did not know that was a thing anybody could tell until a woman of seventy-one said it in a kitchen on Friday and I have had two days with it.*”

Nobody on that plank moved.

“*And if the woman who keeps that book writes my name in it, then about nine people in this town can be sent for about me. I will have been got out of a chair in this county by a woman in another county who cannot be sent for about me at all, and I will not have been asked. That is the same shape as the thing I have been in for fifteen years, and I would rather have the no than have the chair. The no is nine miles down this water.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*Say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*I am not stopping you because you are wrong. I am stopping you because you are the fifth person and it is the first time in two days that anybody has said my own business back to me that was about me. Ask me the question. That is all of it. Ask me the question and I will answer it in front of about nine people and you can carry that out of the room or you can leave it, and both of those are mine.*”

Nobody on that plank said anything to that.

Then a man of thirty-eight said a name, and it was not a ground, and it is in this record because it is the first name he has said on that deck in nineteen years that is not the name of a piece of ground.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Garrin Tolley. “*Because I have been up and down this water since I was nine and I have never said a name on that deck that is not a piece of ground, and a woman has come nine miles, and I would like it said out loud that I am saying this now and not because she asked me for it.*”

Nobody on that plank moved.

“*Say it,*” said Dorcas Slee.

“*He is called Wren Sayer. He went up the water in a lighter about eleven years ago and has not been heard of since, and he is the last line on a sheet of deal that was cut in two on Thursday, and he is the only one of us who was in that weigh-house and has never come back down it. I have not said his name on this deck in nineteen years because I did not know whether saying it would be a thing I was doing for him or a thing I was doing for myself, and I have worked that out in the last four days and I have not worked it out.*”

About four seconds went past.

Nobody on that plank thanked him for it.

Then Ilyan said, in front of about nine people, that he was not going to ask her the question, and why, and that it was the fifth time he had not done the thing he had come to do in fifteen days.

Nobody on that plank thanked anybody for it.

---

The fever was forty-eight weeks old. He had been in that county sixty-five days. A woman of thirty-four came nine miles down the water to ask a town of about eleven hundred people for a thing that is not a chair and is not out of one. What she wanted was to be asked, and about nine people in that town had been talking in front of her about it for two days without knowing.

And a man of forty-one from a town two days off said the thing about the year out loud a second time and is not sorry about a chair he put his own name against and has not taken off.

And a house on the low side of that landing has a low lintel and a slate nailed to the frame with no name on it, and nine people came through that door on the Thursday of the hundred and ninth week, and a woman of seventy-one who keeps it and was never asked about it has not worked out which of the nine of them said a name out loud in her front room, and she has not guessed and she is not going to.
