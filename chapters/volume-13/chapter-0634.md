# Chapter 634: The Sixth Day Of The Hundred And Twelfth Week, And A Sunday, And The Town Does Not Sit On A Sunday, And On Which The Woman Who Keeps The Book And Who Said No To Being In The Thing Asks A Man Of Thirty-Eight In The Open, In Front Of About Nine People, Whether He Will Speak For A Man Who Went Up The Water In A Lighter About Eleven Years Ago, And He Answers Yes Out Loud In About Nine Sentences, And It Is Half Of It, And It Costs Him Two Things, And About Four People In That Room Have It As A Coward And About Nine Do Not

Sixth day of the hundred and twelfth week. His three hundred and eighty-fourth morning. Two hundred and thirty-four days after the settlement. The fever fifty weeks and one day. One hundred and eighty-eight days since the division. One hundred and thirty-four days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The town sits on a Monday and on a Thursday, and it did not sit on this Sunday. About nine people came down that cart road anyway at about the fourth hour, and the door was open because it was the fourth hour of a Sunday.

The fire was not lit.

Nobody who came down that cart road that Sunday had come to be sent for.

There was a Crown on a barrel in a room in this town. It was two days old, it had answered two things, and it had got one of them right.

About nine people in that room had worked out that a thing which has been alive two days does not have to be asked about anything it does not want to answer.

Then the woman who keeps the book asked a question, and she said out loud before she asked it that she was asking it and not the Crown, and why.

“*I am asking this and the Crown is not asking this, and I would like it said out loud in about nine people’s hearing, because about four of you have it this morning that a thing at a barrel is doing it.*”

About four people in that room had it that way. Nobody said so.

“*I keep that book and I said no on Thursday in four sentences and I meant every one of them.*”

“*A Crown of about nine people cannot answer for a man who went up this water in a lighter about eleven years ago and has not been heard of since, because there is nothing on that sheet about what he would have said.*”

“*And the sheet is what you all read out of.*”

“*So I am asking, and I am the person who asks, and I have asked two people a thing in nineteen years, and one of them said no and one of them said yes, and I have not told anybody either way.*”

“*And there is no form in this county that says a keeper of a book may ask a man a question in the open.*”

Her nineteen years went past without a word from anybody in that room.

---

Then she asked it, and the ground was said before the name, the way every line on that sheet is said.

“*The last line on that sheet. The thirty-second.*”

“*A man of about thirty-four called Wren Sayer, gone up the water in a lighter about eleven years ago and not heard of since, with a chair at the other end of the same book.*”

“*No household of his in this county, and nobody nine miles off can be sent for to ask him whether he would like to be on a Crown.*”

“*Garrin Tolley. Would you speak for him.*”

Nobody in that room moved.

About four people in that room had it as a cruel question before it was asked. About nine had not known it was going to be asked at all.

Then he said yes, and he said it out loud, and he said it in about nine sentences.

He did not take it back afterwards, and he did not soften it for anybody in the room.

“*Yes. And I am going to say the shape of it before I say the rest, and about four of you are not going to like the shape and about nine of you have not thought about it.*”

About as long as it takes to cut nine feet of hedge at about two inches at a time.

“*I will speak for the ground and the day and the boat and the eleven years and the fact that nobody in this county can be checked against him. I will not speak for what he would have said.*”

Nobody in that room said anything at all.

“*And if I am asked what he would have said I will say the true thing, which is that I do not know, and I will say it in that shape every single time.*”

“*About nine people will not like it and about four will, and I am not going to be a man who improves a thing every time he says it.*”

The four of them in that room who had been in a room with that shape had it again. About nine of them had a shape for it they liked better.

“*And here is the first of the two things it costs, and I am going to say it out loud because a woman of seventy-one said on Saturday that she is not going to be asked a fifth time about a thing she has said once.*”

“*That is the standard in this town now and I am not going to be the one who is quieter than it.*”

“*I have said my brother’s name out loud four times in nine days and I have said out loud that I do not know whether I am saying it for him or for myself.*”

“*If I am on that board then whatever that board says about Wren Sayer is the position of the record, and there is no column in any book in this county for the difference between a brother saying a name and a man at a barrel saying it for a brother.*”

About four people in that room had the true of that. About nine had not thought about it.

“*And the second of the two is that I have a child of nine, and I have told her twice that this is not our business.*”

“*I did not know on Wednesday whether that was true. It is not true.*”

“*She is going to hear about a Crown in this town by the fourth hour the same as about nine other people are, and I am going to have to tell her on Saturday that her father has put his name on a thing that answers for a man who is not here.*”

Nobody in that room said a word to him about either of those.

---

Then the Crown was asked about him, and the woman of forty-four wrote down what he had said.

It was the first thing that Crown had ever had that was not off a sheet of deal.

“*It goes on a board and not on that sheet, because that sheet is a thing anybody can be sent for about and this is a thing a man said in a room.*”

“*And about four of you can see the difference and about nine of you will find out what the difference is in about four years.*”

Nobody in that room thanked her for writing it down.

Then about four people in that room said out loud that this was half an answer, and said the word coward once between them, and about nine of them did not say it.

A woman of twenty-nine who was in the reed said the true of the four in about nine sentences, and about four of the room had not known any of it.

“*You have got it as a coward’s answer because a man who says yes to half of a thing is a man who has not said the whole of it, and about four of you have been in a room with that this week.*”

About four people in that room knew which room.

“*On the Tuesday of the eleventh week the woman who keeps that book was asked a question in three parts and answered one of them and refused two, and she said out loud that the other two are owed.*”

“*And about four of us have not stopped counting since.*”

“*A man who says yes to half of it has done exactly that, and it is a shape and not a failure.*”

“*And about nine of you have not seen it before, because in this town for nineteen years the answer was all or nothing, and a person who did half of it was a person who had not answered.*”

Nobody in that room thanked her for the shape.

“*And about four of you called it a coward once between you in about four seconds, and I would like it said that a man of thirty-eight did not hear it.*”

“*And that if he had, he would have taken his name off that board in about four seconds, and that is what it would have been, and that is the whole of why nobody in this room is going to say it again.*”

---

Then the man of thirty-one said the true of it, and it was one half of the room again, and a man of thirty-eight stopped him in about the fourth sentence.

He did it in front of about nine people.

“*A Crown that cannot answer for the eleven people a person in this town cannot be checked against is a Crown that is not worth a barrel.*”

“*And the man of thirty-eight has just put his hand on one of those eleven and agreed to half of it, and a Crown of about nine people at a barrel in a town of about eleven hundred is going to be asked about about eleven people inside a year.*”

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*And that is one true thing about one half of that room, and I would like it said out loud that it is the seventh time in thirty-one days, and that about four of you have been counting and about nine have not.*”

“*And I am not going to stop saying them because I have been caught seven times.*”

“*I am going to say the other half, and the other half is that a man of thirty-eight has a child of nine, and a woman of seventy-one who is not going to be spoken for, and a man of thirty-one who wants to be findable.*”

“*And all three of those are in that room, and a Crown of about nine people that cannot answer for any of them is a thing that about nine people made in four hours and about four people cannot describe.*”

About four people in that room understood the whole of it. Nobody else said anything.

Nobody in that room told a man of thirty-eight that he was a coward, and about four people in it had wanted to, and about nine of them never had it in the first place.

And a man of sixty-one wrote all three of those numbers in his own book in his own hand, because he has told three people in two days that he is not writing down a number that is not a head or a cart, and he wrote one.

---

The fever was fifty weeks and one day old. He had been in that county eighty days, and the first thing a Crown in that town ever had that was not off a sheet of deal is a man of thirty-eight agreeing in the open, in front of about nine people, to speak for a brother gone up the water in a lighter about eleven years ago.

And he refused the other half of it out loud, every time, in the same words, without improving it.

And the reason it cost him two things was said out loud by him and not found by anybody else: a name said by a brother is not the same thing as a name said by a man on a board, and there is no column in any book in that county for the difference, and he has a child of nine and told her twice that it is not their business, and it is not true.

And about four people in that room had it as a coward and about nine did not, and a man of sixty-one wrote all three of those numbers down in a book of heads and carts because one of them was not a head or a cart.

And on a Sunday evening about four miles off that cart road, in a house at the top of that road, a girl of nine who has come up that plank with her father every day of her life was sitting on a settle with a slate on her knees and no name on it.

She had heard about nine of the words of what happened at that barrel and had not been told any of it, and she would be told on Saturday, and about four people in that town knew she was in that house, and about nine did not.
