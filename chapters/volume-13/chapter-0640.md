# Chapter 640: The Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Thirteenth Week, And A Saturday, And The Town Does Not Sit On A Saturday, And On Which A Boy Of Thirteen Reads Out Loud A Sheet Of Deal He Wrote Out On The Friday, And On Which A Man Of Sixty-One Gives A Figure Out Loud With The Working And Says What It Is A Figure Of, And On Which A Man Of Thirty-Four Asks One Question From A Doorway And Nobody Answers It, And On Which A Man Of Thirty-Eight Is Going To Do The Same Thing Again On Thursday

Fifth day of the hundred and thirteenth week. His three hundred and ninetyth morning. Two hundred and forty days after the settlement. The fever fifty-one weeks old. One hundred and ninety-four days since the division. One hundred and forty days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The room had a low lintel and a slate nailed to the frame with no name on it, and the door was open because it was the fourth hour of a Saturday.

The board was still on the barrel where it had stood since Thursday.

About four of them had been in that room on the Friday of the hundred and eleventh week, and about four had not.

Nobody in that room had thanked the boy for the sheet and about four of them had meant not to.

The boy of thirteen read his own sheet out loud from the first line to the last, in the open, in about nine people’s hearing, on the Saturday morning.

It took him about four hours to write it out on the Friday and about nine minutes to read it, and he said both numbers out loud himself before he started, which nobody else in that room does.

“*I wrote it on the Friday and I read it on the Saturday, and I am going to say the two numbers out loud, because a man of sixty-one has been doing that in this town for a week and about four of you have got used to it and about nine of you have not.*”

Four of them in that room had it before he had finished.

“*And I am not going to read the thirty-first line in a hurry and I am not going to read it better than it is.*”

“*It is the same words as the other one, and about four of you have heard it three times, and about nine have heard it once, and that is what I have got.*”

About nine people in that room heard a line about a man of thirty-one say the same thing a fourth time in four counties.

About four of them had it in a different shape, and nobody in that room said anything while it was being read.

Then a man of thirty-eight said the tenth reading out loud, because nobody had asked him to, and he wanted it said in the open that he was not going to be asked.

“*Thursday was the ninth reading in a fortnight and the ninth one they agreed.*”

“*Next Thursday is the tenth, and I am going up in the Gull at about the fifth hour with that sheet in a coat, and I am coming down at about the ninth.*”

“*About four of you can come and watch me do it and about nine of you will not, and I am not going to stand here and ask any of you to.*”

About four people in that room said they would come. Nobody said they would not.

“*And about nine days of a boat is what a Crown of about nine people costs, and a man of sixty-one did that sum out loud on a Friday and a woman of twenty-nine did it again on a Thursday.*”

“*And neither of them was asked to, and I am the one that pays it.*”

“*And I would like it said out loud that I have not told anybody that I pay it until now, and that I am not going to be thanked for it, and that I am not going to stop.*”

Nobody in that room thanked him for the nine days.

---

Then the case was said out loud, because a case is a thing people stop saying about after a week.

About four people in that room had noticed that. About nine had not.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Wenna Callow.

“*Because on the Monday of the hundred and eleventh week a man of thirty-eight asked in a yard nine miles up this water for a hearing about every record there is to be read in one room, the ones that disagree with each other especially.*”

“*And it is now the ninth day, and nobody granted it and nobody refused it and nobody has written it down.*”

“*About nine people in four counties have heard about it and about four of them can say the whole of it.*”

About four people in that room had the whole of it. About nine of them had a part of it.

Then the woman of forty-four said why it is not in her book, and it was four sentences.

About four people in that room had heard the shape of it before. About nine had not.

“*I have been asked three times this week whether I will put it in, and I have said no three times, and the reason is the reason I gave on the Thursday of last week.*”

“*If a thing anybody can be sent for about is a thing that can be sent for, then a hearing that nobody granted and nobody refused is a thing with no person on either side of it.*”

“*And about four of you would be sent for about it in about four years, and I am not going to be the reason there is a form in this county that says a man may be asked why a hearing was not held.*”

About four people in that room had that. About nine of them did not know the shape of it.

“*So it is not written down. It is said out loud on the Monday of the eleventh week in a yard by a man of thirty-eight, and about nine people heard it, and about four of them heard the whole sentence.*”

“*That is all it is. It is still open and it is still not on anything.*”

“*And about four of us in this room are going to have to go on saying it out loud in about four days, because that is the only way it does not get lost, and I am not going to be the one who says it for him.*”

Nobody in that room thanked her for the book.

---

Then a man of sixty-one gave a figure and gave the working and said what it was a figure of, in about nine sentences.

About four people in that room had the arithmetic. About nine of them did not know it existed.

“*About eleven people come up that plank in a working week. Nine of them are on that board.*”

“*I have counted it myself on a Friday and on a Thursday, and I am not asking anybody to check me, and there is no form that lets anybody check me.*”

About four people in that room counted the two nines against each other.

“*Nine out of about eleven. That is the figure.*”

“*And I am going to say what it is a figure of, and I have said this in one form or another about four times this week and I am not going to say it differently.*”

“*It is a figure of one working week at one plank. It is not a figure of a county, it is not a figure of a hundred, there is no hundred in this county, and it is not a figure of a year.*”

“*About four of you have been about to work out what it is a year of, and I would like it said out loud that a year in this county is the time between one crossing of a block of limestone nine miles up this river and the next one after it.*”

“*And I do not know when that is, and neither does he, and neither does anybody in this room.*”

About four people in that room had that. About nine of them had only a number.

“*And a town of about eleven hundred people has about nine of them on a Crown, and about nine of them can describe what the Crown is for and about four of them cannot.*”

“*And about four of those four are in this room, and the number of people in a town of about eleven hundred who have never been asked anything is a number I have got in a column at the back of the last leaf of this book.*”

“*And I cut that column with a knife on Thursday, and nobody told me I could.*”

Nobody in that room thanked him for the nine of eleven.

---

Then a man of thirty-one started to answer a question that had not been asked, and a man of sixty-eight stopped him in about the fourth sentence.

The question was still asked after that, and it was still not answered.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan, half a beat after the voice from the doorway.

He had not yet worked out that he was the one being spoken to.

“*Hold it,*” said Pell Varn, from the barrel, in about nine people’s hearing.

“*You have not got the question yet and you are about to answer the one before it.*”

“*And I would like it said out loud in about nine people’s hearing that a woman of forty-four has told this room what happens when a keeper of a book says a thing in the open.*”

“*And that it is the position of the record, and you have been in four rooms in thirty-one days where you were the record, and about nine people carried it out of the room in a different shape.*”

Four of them in that room understood that. Nobody else said anything. Nobody filled the four seconds after it.

Then a man of thirty-four said it from the doorway of the ash, once, and he did not come out of the ash.

About four people in that room turned round. About nine of them knew where the voice was.

“*A Crown of about nine people speaks for a person who cannot be asked. It did it for me on the Monday of the last week and it could not, and it said so in the open, and about nine of you heard that and about four of you have not stopped hearing it.*”

About four seconds, and then a bench creaked outside.

“*I am going to ask one thing and I am going to ask it once and I am not going to ask it again this week.*”

“*And it is not about me, and it is not about the man of thirty-four who said four words in a yard, and it is not about the woman of thirty who went four days up the water.*”

“*And I would like about four of you to notice that I have said what it is not about before I have said what it is about.*”

About four people in that room had that. About nine of them had only the voice.

“*If a Crown speaks for a person, is that person owed a copy of what it said about them.*”

Nobody in that room answered it.

About four seconds went past and nothing said, and the fire in that room was not lit.

And the Crown was on the barrel with a word at the top of it in a hand nobody in that county makes, and about nine names under it, and a column cut with a knife at the back of the last leaf of a book of heads and carts.

---

The fever was fifty-one weeks old. He had been in that county eighty-six days, and he cannot leave the county until the water goes over a block of limestone nine miles up that river, and he does not know when, and there is no figure for it and nobody who can check it but a stone with a line cut round it.

And a Crown of about nine people at a barrel is five days old, it can speak for a person who cannot be asked, it can be asked whether a copy is right, and it stopped answering the second question on the Wednesday when a man of thirty-one said a name out loud once, and about four people in a room said the arithmetical of that and about nine people went home carrying it.

And a case is still open: a man of thirty-eight asked in a yard nine miles up that water for a hearing about every record there is to be read in one room, on the seventh and last day of the hundred and eleventh week, and nobody granted it and nobody refused it and nobody has written it down.

And the woman who keeps the book has been asked three times this week and has said no three times, and it is being kept open by four people saying it out loud in rooms about once a day, and it will be kept open that way or it will not be kept open at all.

And a figure was said out loud on a Saturday morning in about nine people’s hearing: about nine of about eleven people who come up that plank in a working week are on that board, and it is a figure of one week at one plank and not a figure of a county and not a figure of a year, and a year in that county is the time between one crossing of a block of limestone and the next, and nobody in that room knows when that is.

And a man of thirty-four asked, out loud, once, from the doorway of an ash where he has been for twenty-nine days without standing up, whether a person who is spoken for is owed a copy of what it said about them.

About nine people in that room heard the question and about four of them understood all of it, and nobody in that room answered it, and a man of thirty-eight is going up the water again on Thursday with a sheet of deal in a coat.
