# Chapter 629: The First Day Of The Hundred And Twelfth Week, And A Tuesday, And On Which A Man Of Forty-Three Who Keeps A Landing Nine Miles Up The Water Comes Down That Water In A Lighter At About The Fourth Hour Because He Asked A Question In A Yard On Monday And There Is No Form In That County That Will Let Anybody Come Back And Ask Him What He Meant, And On Which A Woman Of Twenty-Nine Who Stood Up Out Of A Chair In The Open On The Wednesday Of The Tenth Week Sits Down In It Again In The Open At About The Second Hour And Says Out Loud Why And Says That She Has Not Got A Reason That Will Be Believed In About Four Years, And On Which A Question That Was Asked On A Sunday Is Asked Again In A Kitchen And Has Still Not Been Answered

First day of the hundred and twelfth week. His three hundred and seventy-ninth morning. Two hundred and twenty-nine days after the settlement. The fever forty-nine weeks and three days. One hundred and eighty-three days since the division. One hundred and twenty-nine days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

A man of thirty-eight came down the water in the night with the Gull and had nothing with him, and at about the second hour of the morning of the first day of the hundred and twelfth week he said four things out loud on the deck of that boat in front of about nine people. He said them in the order they had happened, and about four people in that town have them all and about nine have two of them.

“*The two originals agree. Thirty-two lines, and the thirty-second one is in both of them, and the thing that was not true this week came true in a yard at about the third hour yesterday.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*About nine people in a yard at a landing read four of those sheets out loud, all of them, and about nine of them had never seen the thirty-first line before, and one man of forty-three at the back of that yard said the true thing about it in four sentences and it was me who was in the weigh-house, and about nine people there have it now who did not have it on Saturday.*”

Nobody on that plank thanked him for the going.

“*I said a thing at about the fourth hour of the afternoon in that yard. It is the fourth thing it has ever said in a mouth of anybody in four counties, and there were about nine people standing in that yard, and I said it flat and I did not say anything else and about four of them had heard it before and about nine had not.*”

About four people on that deck understood that. About nine did not.

“*And I asked for a hearing. About every record there is, read in one room, the ones that disagree with each other especially. I said it out loud on the ground that about nine people in that yard were two halves about a sheet of deal and every one of both halves was right. Nobody asked me whether I would rather not and nobody voted on it and I asked for it anyway, and it is not a decision and it is not a thing anybody in that yard agreed to.*”

Nobody on that deck said anything to that.

Nobody in Hask was relieved and nobody in Hask was forgiven and nobody in Hask was thanked, and about four people in that town had been told four things and about nine had been told two of them and not one person in that town said a word to a man of thirty-eight about any of the four.

---

Then at about the fourth hour a lighter came down the water and a man of forty-three got out of it, and he had kept that landing nine miles up for nineteen years. He had asked a question in a yard the day before, and there is no form in that county that will let anybody come back and ask a man what he meant by it, and he had come down in a hired lighter on his own account and had about nine pence and no arrangement.

About four people on that bank had been told he was coming. About nine had not.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Wenna Callow.

“*Because a man of forty-three has come nine miles down a river in a hired boat on a Tuesday morning on his own account and nobody in this town sent for him, and I would like it said out loud in front of about nine people. I do not know whether he is allowed to be in this town, and there is no form anywhere that says.*”

“*I am standing in front of about nine people and a landing nine miles up this water, and I came down in a lighter I hired with about nine pence I had left. I am not going to be thanked and I am not going to be forgiven, and I would like about four of you to notice that neither of those is anything I have come for.*”

About four people on that bank had that. About nine did not.

“*I said four words in a yard yesterday and about nine people in that yard heard them, and about four of them had heard them before and about nine of them had not. In one night about nine people in four counties have them, and that is not a thing I can be sent for about and it is not a thing I can be asked about. I came down here to say one sentence about it and then I am going to go and take my work up again on the Thursday.*”

Nobody on that bank said anything at all.

“*Say it,*” said Ilyan.

“*I asked whether it was in that room and nobody answered me and about four seconds went past, and I have been thinking about it since about the fifth hour of yesterday afternoon. I have got a worse answer than the one I was hoping for and I am still glad I asked, and I would like about four of you to notice that both of those are about me and neither of them is about anybody else.*”

About four seconds went past.

Nobody on that bank said a word to him that could have moved him.

---

And the count moved at about the second hour, before any of that, in the open, on a bank, and it moved for the fifth time in about thirty days and it moved down, and the subtraction was said out loud with it.

It was ten on the Sunday of the hundred and eleventh week, and it is nine since about the second hour of the first day of the hundred and twelfth week, when a woman of twenty-nine sat down in the reed in front of about nine people and said out loud why. There is no column in that sheet for the reason and there is not going to be one.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*Four sheets went up the water yesterday in a yard nine miles off and five went two days down this road in a cart, and about nine people in four counties are going to read those copies in about four days. About four of them are going to ask somebody what the reed is, and the somebody is going to be a woman of twenty-nine cutting withies at about two inches at a time, and about four of them are going to say her name.*”

About four people on that bank understood that. About nine did not.

“*And I stood up out of this chair in the open on the Wednesday of the tenth week and I have not been able to stop thinking about that for thirteen days, and about nine of you know that a no in the open is the only door out of a chair that anybody in this county has. There is no column for the no. There is a line with my name on it and nobody in it and that is what a chair is, and about nine people in four counties are going to read that line and there is a woman of forty-four in that cart who has been in a chair for fifteen years.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*And a woman of twenty-six said on the Wednesday of last week that a column for a reason is a column for a reason and that a person who stands up has to give one that will be believed in about four years, and about eight of the people who have been in that book could not say why. I am sitting down in it. I am going to be asked for a reason in about four days by somebody in another county and I have not got one that will be believed in about four years.*”

About four people on that bank said nothing at all.

“*I am going to say the true of it anyway because about nine of you have taught me that a thing you do not want to say out loud is not safer in a room, it is only in one room. I am sitting down in this chair because I would rather be in it than be a line with a knife in my apron read out in four counties by nine people, and that is not a reason anybody in this bank is going to believe, and I have said it in the open anyway, and I am not going to be thanked for it and I am not going to be told it was the right thing. *”

Nobody on that bank thanked her for sitting down.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*She has not got a reason that will be believed in about four years because there is not one, and I have been in a chair for nine years and I have watched it happen twice, and I am not standing up. I would like it said out loud in front of about nine people that the woman who said that on the Wednesday of last week is the reason I know it today, and that she is still in this chair and has been for eleven days.*”

About four people on that bank had that. About nine did not.

---

Then the man of thirty-one said the four words again to himself in a room, in front of about nine people, and caught it in the middle, and stopped.

“*Then say it is not anybody’s fault and go on,*” said Ilyan, and he got about four words out of it and stopped, and about four people on that bank said nothing and about nine said one thing at once.

“*No,*” said Nevin Trask, from the bank, with the landing book not under his arm.

About four seconds went past.

“*You have said that four times in fifteen days and about four of us stopped you the first time and you have gone on doing it, and I am going to say the same nine words and then I am going to do the sum, because you are about to do it again.*”

Nobody on that bank thanked the man of sixty-one for saying it before anybody else could.

“*A woman of thirty went four days up this road on Monday morning because a rule eleven days old cannot be stopped by about nine people, and she was right, and nobody said a word to her. Four sheets of that sheet went nine miles up the water and were read out in a yard and about nine people there had never seen a line about him.*”

About four people on that bank counted that. About nine of the rest had a book of heads and carts in their heads and could not get at it.

“*A man of forty-three asked in that yard whether a thing was in it and nobody answered him and he has come nine miles down a river in a hired boat to say one sentence about it and is not going to be thanked. A man of thirty-eight asked for a hearing about every record in the world in a yard over two halves, and about four of that yard think he should not have asked and about nine think he should.*”

About four people on that bank counted that. About nine did not.

“*None of it is anybody’s fault.*”

“*I am not going to say it and neither is he, and I would like it said out loud in about nine people’s hearing that that phrase has gone out of use in this town in a fortnight. I do not know what we are going to use instead, and I have a book of heads and carts and it is not going to be that.*”

Nobody on that bank said anything to that.

---

Then a question that was asked out loud on a Sunday nine days ago was asked again in a kitchen on the low side of the landing, at about the seventh hour, in front of about nine people, and it had still not been answered and it was not going to be answered in that county.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*I asked it on the Sunday on that deck, and I asked it in this kitchen on Monday morning before I went in that cart, and I am asking it now for the third time. I am going to keep asking it until somebody answers it, or until there is a woman of seventy-one in this room who says out loud that she is not going to answer it either. About four of you know which of those two things is going to happen and about nine of you do not.*”

Nobody in that kitchen moved.

Nobody in that kitchen thanked her for asking a third time.

---

The fever was forty-nine weeks and three days old. He had been in that county seventy-five days, and the count of the chairs is nine. It was eleven on the Tuesday of the hundred and eighth week and it has moved five times in about thirty days, and every one of the five has a day and a subtraction said out loud. The fifth was a woman of twenty-nine sitting down in the reed in the open, because about nine people in four counties are going to read a line with her name on it and nobody in it.

And a man of forty-three who keeps a landing nine miles up this water came down it in a hired lighter on his own account, because he asked a question in a yard and no form in that county will let anybody come back and ask him what he meant. He said that he got a worse answer than the one he was hoping for and that he was still glad he asked, and nobody on that bank said a word to him that could have moved him.

And a woman of thirty-four from another county asked for the third time, in a kitchen, out loud, whether the saying of no has to be written down the way the saying of yes is written down. Nobody in that county answered it, and the woman who could answer it best has not come up that plank since the sixth day of the hundred and ninth week and has not been asked and has not sent for anybody.
