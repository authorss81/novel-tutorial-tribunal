# Chapter 625: The Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Eleventh Week, And A Friday, And On Which A Man Of Sixty-Eight Who Gave The Rule On The Thursday Of The Tenth Week Says Out Loud That A Man Nobody Can Be Sent For About May Not Put His Hand On Any Of The Copies, And Then Asks A Woman Of Thirty A Question In Front Of About Nine People That She Is Not Ready For And Says So Out Loud And Is Not A Coward For Saying It, And On Which A Man Of Forty-One From A Town Two Days Off Offers To Carry A Copy Four Days Down The Water On His Own Account And Is Not Asked To

Fourth day of the hundred and eleventh week. His three hundred and seventy-fifth morning. Two hundred and twenty-five days after the settlement. The fever forty-eight weeks and six days. One hundred and seventy-nine days since the division. One hundred and twenty-five days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The copying was set for the Sunday, and about nine people in that town could write, and the man of thirty-eight had said on the Wednesday that he would take as many up the water as would go under a thwart, and nobody had written a figure down and nobody was going to.

On the Friday morning a man of sixty-eight who was not in a chair and could not be corrected in one said out loud that the man of thirty-one was not to write a single line of any of it, and he gave the reason and about four people on that deck had it at once and about nine did not.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Wenna Callow. “*Because a man of thirty-one has a better hand than anybody in this town and has had it for nineteen days and has not used it on that sheet, and a man of sixty-eight has just told him why in a sentence he did not expect.*”

“*I am standing in front of about nine people and a rule, and I have had a rule for six days, and I would like it said out loud that I did not think of this until about an hour ago. I have been wrong for about nine seconds of it.*”

About four people on that deck said something at once. About nine of them waited to see whether a man of thirty-one would say it again.

“*There is no form in this county that says a person can be sent for about a thing he has written with his own hand. A man of thirty-one said that on the Thursday of the tenth week, in front of about nine people, and he was right, and that is the whole of why a sheet of deal in this town is a thing anybody can be sent for about and a hundred and twenty feet of a road is not.*”

Nobody on that deck moved.

“*And if a man nobody can be sent for about writes eleven copies of it, then the record of this town has been made by a person the record cannot reach, and in about four years the record and the man are the same thing. And a woman of forty in this town has a mother of sixty-eight who went to a fair at a crossroads four miles off and came back on the Saturday and sat in the fourth chair and did not know she had gone into a number, and there is no column in that book for where she was.*”

Nobody on that deck said anything to that for about as long as it takes to pole nine feet of a boat.

“*And if your hand is on the copies, then about nine people in this town get sent for about what you said about your own mother at a fair.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*You may read them out. You may carry them. You may stand nine feet off with a knife while other people do the work, which is what you have been doing for two days and which is the best thing anybody has seen you do in nineteen days. You may not write one line of one of them, and I have said why, and I would like it said out loud that I am saying it to you and not to the woman of thirty who wrote a book of her own for nine years and is the fastest hand in this county.*”

Nobody on that plank thanked him for the rule.

---

Then he asked her the question, in the open, in front of about nine people, and it was not the one anybody expected, and it was a man of sixty-eight who was not in a chair and had just spent the morning closing a door on somebody else. He had not been in a chair for twenty-two years and there is no form in this county that lets a man ask to be corrected in one.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*No. I have asked you for a thing and I am going to ask her for a thing and I would like the two of them said out loud in the same hour, because about four of you think I am on your side and about nine think I am on hers and I am on the side of a rule I gave on a Thursday and I do not know whose that is.*”

About four people on that plank understood that. About nine of them heard a man agree with a woman and took it for the end of a thing.

“*Perdy Lound. I am sixty-eight years old and I have kept the last quarter mile of that cart road for twenty-two years and I have never once been in a chair and there is no form in this county that lets a man ask to be corrected in one. I gave that rule six days ago. There is a third copy of that sheet nine miles up the water and there are going to be more and every one of them is a thing that can be sent for about.*”

Nobody on that plank moved.

“*And one of the six that have not come is your brother and he is four days off, and you said that on the Wednesday in four sentences, and about four people here have it and about nine have it a different way.*”

About four people on that deck had that. About nine did not.

“*I am going to ask you one question and I am going to ask it out loud in front of about nine people, and I am going to ask you whether a copy of that sheet, read in a room four days off, is going to be used to go and look for the six. Yes or no. That is all of it and there is nothing in it about you and there is nothing in it about me.*”

About four people on that plank said nothing at all.

Then she was quiet for long enough that the man of thirty-eight moved a thwart about nine inches and stopped again.

“*I am not ready,*” said Perdy Lound.

About four seconds went past.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*I am not ready and I am going to say so out loud in front of about nine people, and I would like it said that a person saying she is not ready is not a person saying no and is not a person saying yes. About four of you have it as a coward and about nine of you have not thought about it.*”

Nobody on that plank thanked her for saying it out loud.

“*Here is why.*”

“*The copies are going to be made on the Sunday, all of them, in one room, and I am going to be in that room, and I am the fastest hand in this county and about nine of you know it. If I am in that room on the Sunday then the copies are made, and if I am not in that room then they are made slowly, and about four of them are made badly and one of them is made by a child.*”

About four people on that plank understood that. About nine did not.

“*And whether they are made well or made badly is not a moral question, and it is not a thing anybody can be right about, and it is the only thing about those copies that is in my hands. I cannot tell you whether I am going to hold them until Saturday, because I do not know whether I am going to be standing in this room on the Sunday. And I do not know that until I know the answer to the question you have just asked me, and I cannot get the answer to your question until Saturday.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*And I have never in my life not been ready for a question and I have never once said so out loud, and I said a thing to a man of thirty-one on the Friday of the ninth week because I was not asked and I have not been asked since. I am saying this so that about four of you can tell the other five what the shape of it is. That is all of it.*”

Nobody on that plank said anything to that, and about four people on that bank went up the road and did not say anything about it, and about nine people in a town of about eleven hundred people had her a different way by the seventh hour.

---

Then, at about the seventh hour, a man of forty-one who had come up this water on a Friday a fortnight ago and put his own name against a chair in a town two days off in another county, and had not taken it off, and is not sorry, and has not been forgiven, said a thing out loud that nobody had asked him for.

“*Then say what you came up this water for and do not be sorry for me,*” said Ilyan.

“*I am going to take one of those copies two days down the water in my own cart to my own town, and I am going to say out loud that nobody asked me and that I am not waiting to be asked. If about four of you want to say no to it, you have until Saturday, and I would rather that than a yes on Monday.*”

About four people on that plank said something at once. About nine did not.

“*I have my own name against a chair at the head of a drain in my own town and I have not taken it off and I am not going to. If a record like that can be made in a town of about eleven hundred people with twenty-nine chairs in a fortnight, then there is one in every town of about eleven hundred people and there is one in every town of about two thousand four hundred, and about nine people in this town have not thought past the end of this plank.*”

Nobody on that plank moved.

“*And I am not doing it because of him. I want that said in front of about nine people because a man of thirty-one has been in a chair he did not ask for for nine days and I am not going to be a man who carries a thing for him. I am doing it because I have my own name on a chair and I would like to know whether the copy of that sheet agrees with the book in my own town, and that is a thing about me and about two carts.*”

About four people on that plank had that. About nine of them had a hand on a sheet of deal and were not thinking about whose hand it was.

Nobody on that plank thanked him for the two days.

---

He wrote it on the deck boards at about the eighth hour on a Friday evening, in a hand nobody else in that town could do, with a cracked steel pen that he had carried into a county he cannot leave. It took him about two hours, and he did it for nothing, and he did it well. It took him about two hours and he did it for nothing and he did it well.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Wenna Callow.

“*It is the best one,*” said a man of fifty-four, who could not read and had not said so. “*I cannot read and I know that, and that is the best one and I would like it said out loud that I know it and that I have known it since about the ninth minute of it.*”

About four people on that plank understood that. About nine did not.

“*And it is not the one that goes,*” said Wenna Callow. “*And I would like that said in the same breath with the other thing, and not by me, and by the woman who wrote the one that is going to be the worst one of the lot, which is the most legible of the lot.*”

About four seconds went past.

“*Say it,*” said Ilyan.

“*I have written a worse one than that and I am going to write nine more of them on Sunday, and I would like it said out loud in front of about nine people that it is a worse one on purpose, because a record that a man of thirty-one made is a thing he can be asked about, and this is not that.*”

Nobody on that plank thanked her for the worse one.

---

The fever was forty-eight weeks and six days old. He had been in that county seventy-one days and a man of sixty-eight told him in about nine people’s hearing that a man nobody can be sent for about may not put his hand on the copies of a record, and he wrote the best sheet of deal anybody in that town has seen and it is not one of the ones that goes.

And a woman of thirty was asked a question in the open by a person in that town and said out loud that she was not ready, and gave the reason. The reason is that the only thing about those copies that is in her hands is whether the copying is done well, and she cannot know that until Saturday and she cannot know the answer to the question until she knows that.

And a man of forty-one from a town two days off in another county said out loud that he is going to take a copy there in his own cart on Monday, and nobody asked him, and he would rather a no on Saturday than a yes on Monday, and about four people on that deck had that and about nine did not.
