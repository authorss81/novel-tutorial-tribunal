# Chapter 639: The Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Thirteenth Week, And A Friday, And On Which A Man Of Sixty-Eight Who Gave The Rule Says Out Loud In Front Of About Nine People That He Is Worse Off Than He Was On The Monday Of The Hundred And Eleventh Week And Says Why And Says That Nobody Is To Do Anything About It, And On Which A Woman Of Twenty-Nine Says Out Loud That There Is Now A Thing That Will Say What She Said On A Day In Four Counties And That She Has Not Got A Reason That Will Be Believed In About Four Years, And On Which Nobody Is Relieved And Nobody Is Forgiven And Nobody Is Thanked

Fourth day of the hundred and thirteenth week. His three hundred and eighty-ninth morning. Two hundred and thirty-nine days after the settlement. The fever fifty weeks and six days. One hundred and ninety-three days since the division. One hundred and thirty-nine days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The Crown sat at about the eighth hour on the Friday morning at the head of the plank, on a board on a barrel.

There were about nine people in it and about thirteen in front of it, and it was four days old and had answered four things and got two of them right.

Nobody in front of that barrel thanked anybody for the sitting.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Wenna Callow.

“*Because a man of fifty-two who cannot see well is going to ask it whether he can see us or not, and about four of us have been waiting since Wednesday for somebody to ask it.*”

“*And about nine of us have not, and I would like it said that not asking is a thing you can do, and it is not the same as not having an opinion.*”

About four people in front of that barrel had an opinion. About nine of them did not.

The question was whether a Crown of about nine people at a barrel may be asked, out loud, in the open, to take on a hearing about every record there is.

It could not. It said so itself, in about nine sentences, and it said it correctly.

“*We are nine people and a board and a barrel, and about four of us cannot describe what this is for.*”

“*And the sheet of deal in this town is nailed inside the after thwart of that boat, and is read against another one nine miles up the water once a week, and the two of them agree because a man of thirty-eight poles nine miles twice.*”

“*There is no form in this county that says a board may hear anything, and we have not got a house, and we have not got a door, and there is about nine feet of an open boat between us and about nine of you.*”

About four people in front of that barrel had the whole of it. Nobody said anything about it.

About four of them said the request out loud anyway, in about four minutes, one after another.

About nine did not.

And a man of thirty-eight said that he was not going to be the man who asked twice, and that he had asked once in a yard on the Monday of the eleventh week and nobody wrote it down, and he was not going to be the reason it was written down now.

---

Then the man of sixty-eight said the thing, and he said it before anybody had got near it.

It took about nine sentences, and it is the truest thing anybody said in that county that week.

“*I am worse off than I was on the Monday of the hundred and eleventh week, and I am going to say it out loud in about nine people’s hearing and give the reason.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for it and I am not going to be forgiven for it, and nobody is to do anything about it.*”

About four seconds went past and nobody filled them.

“*On the Monday I said, in about nine people’s hearing, in the open, on this bank, that the copies go over a woman of thirty who is right.*”

“*And I said it because if nine people can stop a copy then a person can be sent for about having stopped it.*”

“*That was a rule of six days old and it was the best true thing I have said in about four years, and about four of you have it.*”

About four people in front of that barrel had it. About nine of them had the other half of it.

“*And a Crown of about nine people at a barrel can be asked what a thing says, and a thing at a barrel that can be asked what a thing says can be asked whether a thing is right.*”

“*And I would like it said out loud in about nine people’s hearing that a Crown can now stop a copy by being asked whether the copy is right.*”

“*And about nine of us said that on the Friday of the eleventh week was a lid with no name on it, and about four of us have not said it since, because a Crown is new and I have not had a Monday about it yet.*”

About four people in front of that barrel had the whole of that. About nine of them were still carrying the first half.

“*So here is the sentence, and it is mine, and I have been the instrument in this county for twenty-two years and I have never once been the person, and I am going to say this one as a person and I am not going to say it again.*”

“*A Crown is a lid with a name on it, and I gave the rule that made it, and a man of thirty-one asked me on the Friday of the eleventh week why a sheet of deal in this town could be sent for about and a hundred and twenty feet of a road could not.*”

“*And I told him, and he is the reason I have had to think about this for about nine days, and he is not to blame, and neither is anybody else in this bank.*”

Nobody in front of that barrel said a word to him about it.

“*And I am not going to take the rule back. A rule that goes back the second time a person turns out to be right about a brother is not a rule, it is a mood.*”

“*It is on that sheet of deal in two places and about nine people in four counties have read it, and it stays there.*”

“*And I am worse off, and I have said so out loud, and now about nine of you have it and about four of you can check it.*”

Nobody in front of that barrel thanked him for being worse off.

---

Then the woman of twenty-nine said hers, and she was in the reed.

She has not stood up since she sat down on the Tuesday of the hundred and twelfth week, and about four people in front of that barrel had not known she was going to say anything, and about nine had.

“*I sat down in that chair on the Tuesday of the hundred and twelfth week at about the second hour in the open, and I said out loud that I had not got a reason that will be believed in about four years, and that there is not one.*”

“*About nine people in that bank heard it.*”

About four people in front of that barrel had that. The rest of them had the reed and not the sentence.

“*And there is now a thing in this town that will say what I said on a day, in four counties, in the open, in rooms I have not been to, on a day a person names.*”

“*And it does not need a reason, because a reason is a thing a person is asked for and a Crown is not asked for anything.*”

About four seconds went past and nobody filled them.

“*So I have got a worse thing than not having a reason.*”

“*Before the Monday I had no reason, and the reason was mine to find or not find. Now there is a thing that will say the words I said, and the words were the words I said, and I did not choose them and I cannot get them back.*”

“*And there is nobody I can be cross with, because a barrel is not a person and a board is not a person and a Crown is not a person.*”

“*And I have been in a chair for nine years and I know exactly what it is to have no person to be cross with.*”

Nobody in front of that barrel thanked her for nine years.

“*I am not standing up. I said on the Wednesday of the tenth week that I am not going to and I am not going to.*”

“*And about four of you are on my side of that and about nine of you have not thought about it.*”

About four people in front of that barrel had that. About nine of them had a Crown.

---

Then a man of thirty-four spoke from the doorway of an ash on the low side of that landing, where he had been for twenty-nine days.

He had not stood up once in any of them, and about four people in front of that barrel knew he was there, and about nine did not.

Nobody sent for him. Nobody asked him to come out.

“*There is a Crown in this town that speaks for people who cannot be asked, and about nine people in this county cannot be asked about anything, and I am one of them.*”

“*And I have been in this ash since the second day of the hundred and twelfth week and I have not stood up in twenty-nine days.*”

About four people in front of that barrel went still. About nine of them did not know where the voice was coming from.

“*And I am not on that board and I am not in that book, and there is no name against a chair in this town for me.*”

“*About four of you have known that a man was in that ash since the second day of the hundred and twelfth week, and about nine of you have not.*”

“*And a man of sixty-one wrote my name out loud on the Saturday of the hundred and twelfth week in about nine people’s hearing, because I am a person that cannot be asked.*”

About four people in front of that barrel had that.

“*And I am going to say one thing from this doorway and then I am going to stop.*”

“*I would like about four of you to notice that I have not come out of this ash, and about nine of you to notice that a Crown of about nine people at a barrel nine feet off a landing has just been in a room with a person it cannot be sent for about.*”

“*And neither of us was asked anything.*”

About four seconds went past and nobody filled them.

Then a man of thirty-one opened his mouth to say something to that, and a woman of twenty-six in the ash on the other side of that landing said no out loud, from about nine feet of a wall away, before he had got to the end of it.

About four people in front of that barrel heard two people in two ashes say no in about four seconds, and about nine heard one.

---

Nobody in Hask was relieved and nobody in Hask was forgiven and nobody in Hask was thanked.

The fever was fifty weeks and six days old. He had been in that county eighty-five days, and a Crown of about nine people four days old was found out to be able to stop a copy of a record by being asked whether the copy is right.

And the man who gave the rule that made it said so out loud in front of about nine people, and said that he was worse off than he was on the Monday of the hundred and eleventh week, and that nobody was to do anything about it, and that the rule was not going back.

And a woman of twenty-nine said out loud that a thing in that town will now say what she said on a day, in four counties, without asking her, and that she has got a worse thing than not having a reason, and that there is no person in it to be cross with, and she is not standing up.

And a man of thirty-four spoke from the doorway of an ash where he had been for twenty-nine days without standing up, and he is on no board and in no book, and a woman of twenty-six in another ash told a man of thirty-one no out loud before he had got to the end of a sentence.

And the cost of a Crown of about nine people four days old fell in about four places at once in about thirteen people’s hearing, and about four of them said the arithmetic out loud, and about nine of them went home carrying it.

And at about the seventh hour of that Friday evening a man of thirty-one sat down on the bench against the gunwale of the Gull, which about four people in that town had noticed had never once been sat on, and about nine had not, and put a knife in his hand and did not use it.

And at the back of the last leaf of a book of heads and carts there is a column about four inches wide and about nine inches long with a number in it that is a number of people, cut with a knife by a man of sixty-one, in the open, on the Thursday of the hundred and thirteenth week, in a hand that is not the hand of the man who keeps the book.
