# Chapter 731: Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-Sixth Week, And A Saturday, And On Which The Fever Was Sixty-Four Weeks Old, And A Man Of Thirty-Eight Says He Has Stood In One Room And Looked At Nine Holes And Is Not Going To Call Them Nine Fields, And A Man Of Thirty-One Calls Nine Holes Nine Fields And A Woman Of Forty Takes It Off Him In Seven Sentences

Fifth day of the hundred and twenty-sixth week. His four hundred and eighty-first morning. Three hundred and thirty-one days after the settlement. The fever sixty-four weeks old. Two hundred and eighty-five days since the division. Two hundred and thirty-one days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was about four inches over the top step at the seventh hour. The man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working. He also gave the figure for the next time about ninety steps can be walked, before anybody asked him for it.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. About ninety steps of the work are under the bank he was standing on. He has said he is not going to give a fourth thing out of the three he gives every morning.

“*About four inches over the top step at the seventh hour.*”

“*One day. It was off all ninety yesterday at the sixth hour, and that is the working.*”

“*Four inches a day and a full bank is about sixteen, and a full bank holds until it comes off.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in seven days from this morning.*”

“*That is the Saturday of next week, and it is the ninth time that water has come off in the night since the Saturday of the hundred and nineteenth week.*”

“*And I am not going to tell you how many days that is, because there is no figure for a river.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open before anything else happened, and it was the tenth morning, and nobody was thanked.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel at the head of that drain and cannot read a word of the paper on that counter. He has asked for the ninth line in the open every morning since the Thursday of the hundred and twenty-fifth week. He has still not answered the fifth thing that was asked of him on a Sunday of a fortnight.

“*Ten mornings and I have not varied the asking by one word.*”

“*And I am going to keep asking while there is a reading on that counter, and I have still not answered the fifth thing.*”

“*And nobody has asked me about it and I am still waiting, and that has not changed since the Sunday of last week.*”

“*About four of you know those are two different things and the rest of you do not, and that is the correct way round.*”

Then the man of thirty-eight read that line out loud in the open in the wind. He read thirteen names after it, in the order they were written. It took about nine minutes.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and is the only person on that bank who can put a light on a thing in the dark. He has read that sheet twenty-one times in this county. He has been refused a figure for how long three times. He asked for a fourth and will not ask a fifth.

“*Nine words, and about four of them are about two hands and about nine feet along a groove.*”

“*And there is still no day against either name on that line, and there never has been.*”

“*Twenty-one mornings. Twenty-one is seven hundred and thirty-one less seven hundred and ten.*”

“*And there has not been one of the twenty-one in the dark.*”

“*I decided again at the fifth hour at the head of my own gate. I decided it again at the seventh hour on this bank. Both times the answer was the same as the Monday of the hundred and twenty-fourth week.*”

“*Nine of you know what that is. Four of you have known it since the Monday and the rest of you worked it out this week.*”

“*And I am not going to say it, and nobody is going to be thanked for the deciding, and nobody is going to be told I was brave about it.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said a shape out loud about nine fields. He said it in front of about nine people, before anybody had asked him anything. A woman of forty took it off him in seven sentences.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county a hundred and seventy-seven days. A hundred and seventy-seven is seven hundred and thirty-one less five hundred and fifty-four, and nobody has asked him for anything in one of them.

“*There are nine openings in one wall a hundred and thirty feet under this bank, and there are nine lines on that sheet, and that is nine fields.*”

“*A field is a thing a thing can be put in, and there are nine of them, and that is the shape of the whole of it.*”

“*Seven sentences and the fourth one is the reason and it is mine and it is about me.*”

Wenna Callow is forty and asks for a day against a name and has never once asked for a reason instead. She gave a count of three things on the Sunday of a fortnight and refused to pick one. She was refused a fourth on the Friday of this week. She has not said what a minute old thing is.

“*You have not been in the room.*”

“*That is the first of the seven and it is the whole of the seven.*”

“*A man of thirty-eight has been in one room of nine and he has looked at nine holes in a wall from the far side of it.*”

“*And a hole is not a field. A field is a thing with a name in it, and a thing with a name in it is a place a person can be sent to.*”

“*There is nothing in any of the nine. A man of fifty-four who cannot read has had his own hand in one of them. He says it is empty and he cannot read.*”

“*You have just said the word field out loud in about nine people’s hearing. About four of them are going to use it this week.*”

“*And not one of them is going to be standing a hundred and thirty feet under a bank.*”

Then the man of thirty-eight said what he had actually got, and gave a count of nine, and said he was not going to call any of them a field.

“*I have been down that water twice since the Thursday of this week and I have been in the ninth of the nine rooms both times.*”

“*And I have not been in the eighth and there is a chair in the eighth and nobody in this county has been in it.*”

“*And from the far side of the ninth room I can see nine openings in one wall, and here is the count, and I am giving it before the finding.*”

“*Eight of the nine have about a thumb’s width of a groove cut above them and a pair of hands either side of the groove.*”

“*And all nine of them are about a thumb’s width wide at the cut and about as deep as a man’s own hand.*”

“*There is nothing in any of the nine. I have put my own hand in two of them. I am not going to put it in a third.*”

“*I am not going to call them fields. I am not going to call them anything else either. There is no word in this county for a hole with nothing in it, and about four of you are waiting for one.*”

Four of them on that bank had a man of thirty-eight who had been in one room. About nine of them had a man of thirty-one who had said field out loud, and been told he had not been in the room.

Then a man of about fifty-two asked what a mark is worth in four counties, and nobody answered him, and he gave the working for his own eleven years first.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well, and is the fifth line of one of two sheets of deal in this county. He has been that person eleven years without knowing it was a thing to be. He asked on the Monday of last week whether a line on a sheet is a person and was told it is not, and on the Wednesday he asked whether a mark can say no in nine words and was not answered, and neither of those has been improved on since.

“*What a mark is worth in this county, and I am asking it of about nine of you in the open.*”

“*Here is my working first, because that is how it is done on this bank now.*”

“*Eleven years. Eleven is seven hundred and thirty-one less seven hundred and twenty.*”

“*And I have been the fifth line of one of two sheets of deal since the Tuesday of the hundred and twenty-first week, and I have never read either of them.*”

“*And nobody ever asked me whether I would be, and there is no form anywhere that says a man may be asked.*”

“*And there is no form anywhere that says a man may not, and both of those have been true for eleven years.*”

“*And I have worked out that I am the only one of what I am in four counties, and I have not got a figure for what that is worth.*”

“*About four of you know what I am and the nine of you have heard a man of thirty-one say a version of it on that bank in about nine days.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for asking, and I am not going to be told I am right, and if nobody answers me that is an answer and I have had eleven years of those.*”

“*I have been in this county a hundred and seventy-seven days and I have wanted for every one of them to be somewhere a person could send somebody to.*”

“*And I said field out loud this morning because I cannot get down there and a man of thirty-eight can, and that is the shape of it.*”

“*And a woman of forty has told me in seven sentences that I have not been in the room, and she is right about all seven, and I am not going to take one of them back.*”

“*A hundred and seventy-seven is seven hundred and thirty-one less five hundred and fifty-four, and I am not going to be told I was half right about the nine.*”

“*I am not going to be thanked for saying the word field, and I would rather it was nothing at all.*”

Then a woman of forty-four said the thing that was standing behind all of it. She said it before anybody had asked her. She said it flat, and said it was the first time she had said it on that bank.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs at thirteen shillings and fourpence a year for nineteen years. There are nine of those chairs and not ten, and she is not a keeper of anything anybody can be sent for about. On the Thursday of this week she said out loud that knowing does not put a column in that county with his name at the top of it.

“*You are the best instrument in four counties for being the one who asks. I am saying that out loud in the open. I am not going to say it again for about four days.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for saying it, and I am not going to be told I am right.*”

“*And I said on Thursday that there is not going to be a column in this county with your name at the top of it.*”

“*And that was on Thursday and it is still true this morning, and I am not going to be asked about it and I am not going to explain it.*”

“*And a man of about fifty-two has just asked in the open what a mark is worth in this county, and I heard it, and I did not answer it.*”

“*A man of thirty-four has been in an ash on the low side of that landing for a hundred and one days. Nobody has asked him anything and that is how it ought to be.*”

“*A man of thirty-one is standing on a bank being the best instrument in four counties. Both of those are people in this county and I am not going to put them in a line.*”

Then a boy of thirteen read two counts back that he had been keeping since the Saturday of a fortnight. He gave the working for both. A man of thirty-eight told him to keep them.

Wat Marshe is thirteen, and his mother is forty-one and has two carts and neither of them can be sent for about. He counted the first of them on the road coming down that morning. Nobody asked him.

“*Thirty days. Thirty is seven hundred and thirty-one less seven hundred and one.*”

“*And twenty-one readings. Twenty-one is seven hundred and thirty-one less seven hundred and ten.*”

“*And I am not going to hand either of them to a man of thirty-eight like a present.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for counting, and I am keeping them on Monday.*”

Then the sheet went back on that counter where anybody could put a hand on it. Nobody carried it anywhere. About nine people stood on that bank in the wind with four inches of water under the top step.

---

The fever was sixty-four weeks old. He had been in that county a hundred and seventy-seven days.

A man of thirty-eight said he has stood in one room of nine and looked at nine holes in a wall. He gave a count of nine with the working before the finding. He is not going to call any of them a field and he would not put his own hand in a third of them.

And a man of thirty-one called nine holes nine fields out loud on that bank before anybody had asked him anything. A woman of forty took it off him in seven sentences, and said the difference between a field and a hole is the whole of this fortnight.

And a woman of forty-four said out loud that he is the best instrument in four counties for being the one who asks. She said she would not say it again for about four days, and nobody is going to thank her for it.

And a man of about fifty-two asked in the open what a mark is worth in this county, gave the working for his own eleven years first, and was not answered by anybody.
