# Chapter 715: Third Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-Fourth Week, And A Thursday, And On Which A Woman Of Sixty-Four Who Has Been Out Of Every Book In This County For Nineteen Years Comes Up That Bank To Ask Whether Her Name Is On A Sheet Of Printed Paper And A Man Of Thirty-One Says Out Loud That It Is Not Before Anybody Has Read It And Is Wrong, And A Man Of Thirty-Eight Reads Nine Lines Out Loud For The Fifth Morning And A Woman Of Twenty-Eight Says In About Four Sentences That It Is Worse Than A Lie

Third day of the hundred and twenty-fourth week. His four hundred and sixty-fifth morning. Three hundred and fifteen days after the settlement. The fever sixty-one weeks and five days. Two hundred and sixty-nine days since the division. Two hundred and fifteen days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was about four inches over the top step at the seventh hour and the man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working. It was one day since it came off all ninety, and he gave the other half of it because he has given both halves every morning this fortnight.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and gave a figure for a night on the Saturday of last week and has not given one since. He says neither of them is his.

“*About four inches over the top step at the seventh hour. One day, and it was off all ninety yesterday at the sixth hour, and that is the working.*”

“*And I am not going to say how long the next one is. I gave you that figure three mornings running and I have given it correctly three times.*”

“*And I am going to stop while I am still ahead of the nine of you standing here.*”

Four inches on that landing, and about nine of them had a man of forty-three who had stopped giving figures while he was ahead of them.

Then a man of thirty-eight read it out loud for the fifth morning running, and it took about nine minutes. The count was still nine lines and eleven names, and the sheet went back on the counter where anybody could put a hand on it.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and has read that sheet five times in this county. Nobody has put a term on it, and he has been refused a figure for how long twice.

The count was still eleven. A woman of sixty-four was standing at the edge of that bank who had not been there on any of the four mornings before it, and about nine of them had not noticed her come up.

Then a woman of sixty-four said why she had come up that bank. She said it before anybody had asked her anything, and she said it in about four sentences, and nobody asked her a fifth question.

Ada Renk is sixty-four and got up out of the book of heads and carts in the open nineteen years ago and there has been no line for her in it since. A man of sixty-one read that out in the open at the time.

“*I have been out of every book in this county for nineteen years, and nobody has been able to find me by writing my name down in any one of them.*”

“*And I am not on that piece of paper, and nobody has told me that I am on it. Nobody has told me that I am not on it either, and I have come up this bank to be told one way or the other.*”

“*That is all I came for. I have not come up here to be asked about the asking, and I am not going to answer one question about it.*”

“*If anybody on this bank asks me what it was like to be asked on the Thursday of last week, they can go and ask the air.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for coming up four miles, and I am not going to be told it was a good thing.*”

“*And I would like the nine of you standing here to notice that I have asked to be told a thing and not to be given one.*”

About nine of them on that bank had a woman of sixty-four who had come up four miles to be told a thing and not to be given one.

Then a man of thirty-one said it out loud, flat, in front of about nine people, before anybody had read the sheet again that morning. He said it in the plainest words he had, and it was wrong.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county a hundred and sixty-one days. A hundred and sixty-one is seven hundred and fifteen less five hundred and fifty-four, and nobody has asked him for anything in one of them.

“*You are not on it. I have read that thing five times this week and your name is not on it, and there is no line with anything like it on.*”

“*I am not going to say I am sorry. I did not make a mistake this morning. I made a decision, and the decision was to say a thing I had not looked at because it is what I wanted to be true.*”

“*And about four of you heard me do it, and that is the whole of what I did.*”

Nobody on that bank said sorry and about four of them had the whole of what he did.

Then a woman of twenty-eight said what that was, in about four sentences. She did not raise her voice, and she said it was a thing about a man and not about a paper.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and is not one of the nine and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from, and she has checked a thing ten times, and has said the same sentence nine times and has not said it once.

“*That is the first time in this county that a man of thirty-one has been wrong about a fact instead of wrong about a shape. I want the difference said out loud, because about four of you have been listening to him get things wrong for ten days.*”

“*A shape is a thing he has not looked at and gone round. That is what shapes are for, and I have been telling him that since the Monday of the hundred and twenty-second week.*”

“*A fact is a thing he said out loud in front of about nine people without looking. The whole cost of it has landed on a woman of sixty-four who came up four miles to be told a thing.*”

“*And it is not a lie and it is worse than a lie, because a lie has somebody in it who decided something, and that had nobody in it at all.*”

A woman of twenty-eight stopped a thing at the fifth morning instead of the fourteenth. About nine of them had heard the difference between the two and four of them had not known there was a difference.

Then a man of thirty-one agreed with her out loud and did not improve on it, and gave the cost in his own mouth. A woman of sixty-four gave hers, and neither of them was relieved.

“*Then it is worse and I am not going to be able to argue with it and I am not going to try.*”

“*And I want one thing said about my own hand before anybody says anything else, and nobody is going to say whether it will keep and nobody is going to say it was worth it and I have said both of those out loud about four days ago.*”

“*I put my right hand flat on that counter this morning to reach the sheet and it is thirty days old. Thirty is seven hundred and fifteen less six hundred and eighty-five. That is all. It is a figure of a hand and I am not going to let any of you make it into anything else.*”

“*And a hundred and sixty-one is seven hundred and fifteen less five hundred and fifty-four and I have been in this county a hundred and sixty-one days and I said four words this morning that cost a woman of sixty-four a morning she came four miles for.*”

Then a woman of sixty-four gave her own account of it, and she gave it in three sentences, and she said it was her cost and not his and she was not going to hand it to anybody else.

“*Here is what it cost and it is mine and not his, and I am not going to hand it to the rest of you or to him.*”

“*I came up this bank to ask whether I was on a piece of paper. A man of thirty-one told me I was not, and he was wrong, and I am now standing on a bank knowing there is a place on that thing where my name could go, and yesterday morning I did not know that.*”

“*Nineteen years is a long time to be a name nobody can find by writing it down, and I would have gone back down that road this morning with the answer I came for, and I am not going to, and nobody is going to say I was right not to.*”

There was a place on that thing now, and about nine of them had a woman of sixty-four who was not going to go back down four miles with the answer she came for.

Then a man of thirty-eight said the thing that is the same on that bank every morning, and then said the other half of it, which was new. He gave it in about four sentences.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and put that sheet back on that counter on the Saturday of last week and has not carried it anywhere since, and has been asked by about four people this week whether it is safe where it is.

“*Nobody is going to know who wrote it. That is not a new thing, and a man of fifty-four who cannot read said it on that bank on the Saturday of last week. He was right, and every one of you heard him.*”

“*Here is what is new. There is a man of thirty-six who carried it, and a woman in a town a day’s walk off who gave it to him. Neither of them is in this county, and nobody who can be sent for about is going to be writing anything on it.*”

“*And it lies in the open on a counter where any person in this county can put a hand on it. That was said out loud on the Saturday by about three people including me.*”

“*And I am not going to say we were wrong, because we were not wrong. We were doing the only thing anybody in this county can do with a thing we cannot send for.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for that, and I am not going to be forgiven for it.*”

“*If any of you are going to say in about four days that somebody ought to have carried it into a room, then I would like it said to the man of thirty-eight and not to the sheet.*”

About nine of them had three people who had said out loud where a thing would live, and four of them had been on that counter on the Saturday when they said it.

Then about nine people stood on that bank in the wind, and about four of them had a man of thirty-one’s right hand on a counter at thirty days, and nobody had said a word about it.

---

The fever was sixty-one weeks and five days old. He had been in that county a hundred and sixty-one days.

A woman of sixty-four who has been out of every book in this county for nineteen years came up that bank to ask whether her name is on a sheet of printed paper. A man of thirty-one said out loud that it is not, before anybody had read it, and was wrong, and said he had not looked.

And a woman of twenty-eight said in about four sentences that it is not a lie and it is worse than a lie. A man of thirty-one said it is worse, and gave the age of a cut across his right palm out loud as thirty days, and asked that nobody say whether it will keep.

And a woman of sixty-four said what it cost and it is hers, and that she is not going back down four miles with the answer she came for, and nobody is going to say she was right not to.
