# Chapter 702: Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-Second Week, And A Friday, And A Bank That Is Full At About Sixteen Inches Over The Top Step, And On Which A Man Of Thirty-Six Comes Down Nine Miles Of Cart Road With A Sheet Of Printed Paper Folded Inside His Coat And Says Out Loud That He Was Told To Give It To A Person In This County And Not To A Man Who Came Up A Plank, And A Man Of Sixty-Four Says There Is No Column In The County Of Kell For Being A Person And Did Not Say It Until Somebody Else Had Said It About Him

Fourth day of the hundred and twenty-second week. His four hundred and fifty-second morning. Three hundred and two days after the settlement. The fever fifty-nine weeks and six days. Two hundred and fifty-six days since the division. Two hundred and two days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was full. The man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working and said the four days off, and it was the fourth day and about sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour, and he said a bank that is full stays full until it comes off, and he did not say how many nights that is.

Corvin Tace is forty-three, and the landing at the head of that water has been his for nineteen years. He has said he has nothing to say about a night five times and has then stopped saying it, and about four people on that bank have been waiting a week for him to start again.

“*About sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour. Four days since it came off all ninety, and it was about twelve inches at this hour yesterday, and a full bank stays full until it comes off.*”

“*And I am not going to tell you how many nights that is. I gave a reading every morning of this fortnight and I stopped after the reading and I am not starting again on a Friday because somebody came down the road with a piece of paper in his coat.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in four days and it is not going to be in four days because of a sheet of paper. It is in four days.*”

About four people on that bank had the sixteen inches and about nine of them had the four days.

Then about the ninth hour a man came down that cart road on foot with a piece of printed paper folded inside his coat and nine or ten of them watched him do it, and he did not come down the middle of the road, and he came down the side of it where the carts go.

The man is thirty-six and is a pedlar. He came up that cart road on the Wednesday of this week and on the Friday of last week selling nothing in particular, and he has a sheet of printed paper in his coat that is not his and was given to him and he has not been asked to have it.

Ruel Dann is thirty-six. He sells small goods at a market cross about a day’s walk off and he has walked that county for eleven years and he is not a keeper of a thing he has not signed for and he cannot read the sheet he has because he does not read.

“*I am going to say the whole of it before anybody asks me, because I have walked four miles this morning with a thing in my coat that I cannot hand over and cannot get rid of, and I am not going to stand on that bank and be asked about it in bits.*”

“*A woman in a town about a day’s walk off put it in my hand on the Tuesday of last week and told me to carry it to a house four miles up this road and ask one person one question and write down what they said and bring it back down.*”

“*And I did that on the Wednesday of this week at about the eighth hour and the person said one word and I wrote the one word down and I have not looked at the sheet since and I cannot read it.*”

“*And I have been walking down this road since the fifth hour this morning and I have not been able to put it into a hand and I am going down to my own house in about two hours and it is going to be in my house and I do not want it in my house.*”

Then he said the part that turned the morning, and he said it flat, and he pointed at nobody.

“*And here is the part and I would like about four of you to be able to tell in nine days that I said it exactly the way she said it to me on the Tuesday.*”

“*She said: give it to a person in this county. And she said: not to a man who came up a plank.*”

“*And I have been standing here about four minutes working out why I cannot put it in that man’s hand, and it took me about four minutes and it is not because I do not want to be rid of it. It is because there is nothing about him that makes him a person in this county.*”

Nobody on that bank said anything at all for about as long as it takes a man of forty-three to give a reading.

Then a man of thirty-one said it out loud as a fact about himself, in front of about nine people, and he said the working before he said it, which he has not done about this before.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and belongs to nobody. He has been in this county a hundred and forty-eight days. A hundred and forty-eight is seven hundred and two less five hundred and fifty-four. There is no column in the county of Kell with his name at the top of it, no form that renews him, no rate on him and no hundred in that county, and there is a word for what a person is in some other county and it is not a word that has been said out loud in this county in a hundred and forty-eight days and is not going to be said this morning.

“*That is me. I am the man who came up a plank and there is not going to be a second one of me and about nine of you can see there is not.*”

“*And there is no form in this county that says who a person in this county is, and there is no form that says he is not one, and both of those have been true since the evening of the hundred and first week, and I have said that pair of sentences in about four counties for about eleven weeks.*”

“*And a sheet of printed paper has come up nine miles of road with my condition written into it by somebody who was told to put it there, and it is the first thing in a hundred and forty-eight days that has used it.*”

It went into the open air in front of about nine people, and it was the first time in this county that a piece of paper had used a thing about a man.

Then a man of thirty-six said what he would do with it instead of what he would not do with it, and he gave three things and a refusal, and he said the refusal before the three.

“*I can give it to her. The woman whose door it went to first. She is four miles up that road and she has seen it and she said a word and I wrote the word down, and if I put it in her hand it is in a house and not in my coat.*”

“*And I am not going to give it to a man who came up a plank, and I am not going to give it to a man with a book, because a man with a book is a man who keeps things and I would be a thing that had been in his keeping for about one hour.*”

“*And I am not going to give it to a man of sixty-one who writes things down, and I would like it said that I thought of that before I thought of the first one and I did not do it because I have seen him refuse things and I am not going to be the reason he has to refuse something.*”

Then a woman of sixty-four said the thing nobody on that bank had said yet, and it was four sentences, and she said it after the man of thirty-six and not before him.

Ada Renk is sixty-four and got up out of that book of heads and carts in the open nineteen years ago and there has been no line for her in it since, and nobody in this county has been able to find her by writing her name down in nineteen years.

“*A person in this county is a person in a book.*”

“*That is not a rule anybody made. It is just what has been true every time it has been needed for nineteen years, and it has never been written down, and a sheet of printed paper has come up this road and it has used it.*”

“*And there is no column in this county for being a person. There is a column for heads that come through a landing and a column for carts, and I have been out of both of them since I was forty-five and a man of sixty-one read that out in the open nineteen years ago.*”

“*And a form does not ask who a person is. A form asks you the same question and it writes down what you said, and it does not need to know whether you are a person, and that is the whole of what has come up this road.*”

Nobody on that bank improved on her and about four of them had the column and about nine of them had the form.

Then a man of thirty-one tried to make it into something he could carry, and a man of thirty-six took it off him in about nine sentences and did not raise his voice, and then told him what his condition actually was.

“*You cannot have it, and I would like the reason given out loud in your own mouth so that about four of you can hear that I did not keep it.*”

“*I am not keeping it. I am carrying it down four miles to a house and putting it in a hand that asked for it. That is all I have got and that is all I am going to say about what I am.*”

“*And if you had it, then within about four days it would be a thing you had said something about, and then it would be a line in a book about four hundred miles off, and then I would be a name on a line, and I have walked this county eleven years on the strength of not being a name on a line.*”

“*And here is the last of it and it is the true one. You have been in this county a hundred and forty-eight days and you have not been asked for anything in any of them. Nobody in four counties can send for you. And that is the reason she told me not to give it to you, and it is not a punishment, and it is the only condition she gave me and she gave it to me in a yard.*”

Then the man of thirty-six said the other half of it, and it was the half that turned the morning over, and he said it before he was at the bottom of that road.

“*And I have changed my mind since I said the first one.*”

“*I want it said that I changed it in about four minutes and on my own, and nobody on this bank said a word to me about it.*”

“*A house four miles up that road is one household, and a household is a keeper.*”

“*That woman has got about nine houses a day late on her bread or she has not, and neither of those puts a piece of printed paper with eleven names on it in a kitchen.*”

“*And if there is one kitchen with it in, then there is one person in four counties who has got it, and I would be the reason for that person.*”

“*So I am not going up to that door.*”

“*I am going to leave it in the open on a counter at the bottom of that road, where anybody can put a hand on it, and nobody is going to be the reason there is one of them.*”

“*And I am going to be off this cart road before any of you has worked out that I said so.*”

Nobody on that bank sent for a man of thirty-six and nobody stopped him, and about four of them had worked out before he reached the bottom of that road that they were not going to.

Then a man of thirty-eight said one thing before the man of thirty-six got to the bottom of the road, and it was the plainest thing said on that bank all morning, and nobody improved on it and nobody said it was right.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and is going to read nine lines out loud every morning of a thing nobody has put a term on. He is the only person who may carry a board about nine inches long with thirty-two notches cut in its edge, and he has a board that is not that one in his own coat that nobody put there.

“*He has just told the whole of it and he has told it in the order it happened and he has not once made it into a thing about anybody else.*”

“*I want it said that a man of thirty-six carried a sheet of printed paper four miles up a road and asked one woman one question and wrote one word down and carried it back four miles and could not put it into a hand.*”

“*That is the work. Not the paper. The carrying it and not being able to put it into a hand, and doing that twice.*”

“*And I am not going to thank him and I am not going to be the man who tells him he was brave, and about four of you have already worked out that I am the one who said it.*”

It was the plainest thing said on that bank that morning and nobody improved on it, and about nine of them had been waiting a week for a man of thirty-eight to say something about a sheet of paper instead of a door.

Then nine or ten of them watched a man of thirty-six go down nine miles of cart road with a piece of printed paper folded inside his coat, and nobody sent for him and nobody stopped him, and he was not in the county before the light went.

---

The fever was fifty-nine weeks and six days old. He had been in that county a hundred and forty-eight days.

The bank was full at about sixteen inches over the top step and the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in four days.

And a man of thirty-six came down that cart road with a sheet of printed paper in his coat that he cannot read and was not given to write, and said out loud that he was told to give it to a person in this county and not to a man who came up a plank, and carried it back down nine miles of that road and gave it to nobody at all, and said in about four minutes before he started walking that he had changed his mind about the one house it could have gone into.

And a woman of sixty-four who has been out of every book in this county for nineteen years said there is no column in the county of Kell for being a person, and that a form does not need to know.
