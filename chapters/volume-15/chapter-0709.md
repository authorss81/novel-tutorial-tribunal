# Chapter 709: Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-Third Week, And A Friday, And On Which About Nine People Go Down To The Gravel And The Reed And A Boy Of Thirteen Says That A Woman Who Is In No Book In This County Is On The Fifth Line Of A Printed Sheet With A Day Against Her That Is The Wednesday A Thing Asked A Man Whether He Would Write It, And She Says One Word And It Is The Second One In Eighty Days

Fourth day of the hundred and twenty-third week. His four hundred and fifty-ninth morning. Three hundred and nine days after the settlement. The fever sixty weeks and six days. Two hundred and sixty-three days since the division. Two hundred and nine days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was about twelve inches over the top step at the seventh hour and the man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working, and it was three days since it came off all ninety, and then he gave a count of his own that nobody had asked him for.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept a landing nineteen years. A man of thirty-four has been in an ash on the low side of that landing for seventy-nine days and a woman of twenty-nine has been in a reed for eighty days, and he has never added the two.

“*About twelve inches over the top step at the seventh hour. Three days, and it was about eight at this hour yesterday, and that is the working.*”

“*And a man of thirty-four has been in that ash for seventy-nine days and a woman of twenty-nine has been in that reed for eighty days, and I have not added them, and I said on the Saturday of a week ago that I had not added them in fifteen days and I am not adding them today either.*”

About four people on that landing had the twelve inches and about nine of them had two counts that were not added.

About nine people went down the water and off the low side and along the edge of the drain to the reed at about the ninth hour, and nobody sent for anybody, and a man of fifty-four who cannot read went with them because he was on that bank and does not go home at the ninth hour.

Then a boy of thirteen said the thing nobody had looked for, and he said it out of a piece of printed paper he had read on a bank the day before, and he gave the working, and nine or ten of them were standing in the wet grass by that reed.

Wat Marshe is thirteen. His mother is forty-one and has two carts and neither of them can be sent for about in this county.

“*I read all eleven of them out loud on the Sunday on that bank and I have read them four times since and I am not going to pretend I have not.*”

“*The fifth line is a woman of twenty-nine and it is Nell Prince, and she has got a day against her.*”

“*And the day is the second day of the hundred and twenty-second week. That is the Wednesday, and it is nine days ago, and nine is seven hundred and nine less seven hundred, and that is the day a thing that is not a person asked a man of thirty-one on that bank whether he would write it.*”

About four people in that grass had the fifth line and about nine of them had a Wednesday that nobody had joined up before this morning.

Then a man of thirty-eight said the thing he had noticed on the Sunday and had said nothing about, and he said it flatly and asked about four of them to notice that he was the one who said it and not to help him.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine and has read that sheet out loud twice in this county, once on a bank in the open air and once at the fifth hour about a hundred and thirty feet under that bank.

“*I read that sheet out loud on the Sunday in the order it is written and on the second line of it there are four things and the second of the four is a mark cut into the paper about the size of a thumb and square.*”

“*And over the ninth opening in that wall there is a space cut between two hands about as wide as a thumb, and square, and cut to the depth of the hands, and old, and there is nothing in it, and it has been nothing in it for about four hundred years.*”

“*Those are two marks of the same size and the same shape and I have had both of them in my own hands and I have been carrying the one for about seven weeks and the other for about two days and I have not slept properly about either.*”

“*I am not going to say they are the same thing. I am going to say they are the same size and the same shape, and that one of them is cut in a wall a hundred and thirty feet under a bank and the other one is printed on a piece of paper that came up nine miles of cart road in a coat.*”

“*And I am not going to be the man who joins them up, and if about four of you are about to join them up I would like you to notice that you are doing it before you do it and not after.*”

Two marks of the same size and the same shape, and a man of thirty-eight standing in wet grass asking about nine of them not to join them up before they had done it.

Then a man of thirty-one said the true of one half of that in about four sentences and refused to say the other half and said he was going to refuse it out loud first.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s. He has been in that county a hundred and fifty-five days and has said out loud that a man who does not answer is not a refusal and has not said it since the Wednesday of a week before.

“*A name with a day against it on a printed sheet is a name somebody can be sent for about. A woman of forty said that on Sunday in the open air and she said it before anybody asked her and it is true and I am not going to stand in this grass and pretend it is not true for about four seconds because I do not like where the day falls.*”

“*I am also not going to say what that means. I have been in this county a hundred and fifty-five days, and a hundred and fifty-five is seven hundred and nine less five hundred and fifty-four, and every single thing that has gone wrong in it has been because a man stood in front of about nine people and said what a day meant.*”

“*And I am refusing the other half of it out loud, here, now, in front of about four of you, so that about four of you can tell in nine days that I said it and did not ask it.*”

He refused a question out loud in a wet field at about the ninth hour and about nine of them heard him refuse it.

Then a man of forty-three said the sentence he has been giving all week, in a different shape and about a different thing, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked him for it.

“*I have been asked four times in about six weeks what a thing means and I said I had nothing to say about a night and I said it four times and then I stopped.*”

“*And I am not going to stand in this grass and say that one word out of that reed is a refusal. I am also not going to stand in this grass and say it is not one.*”

“*And I am not going to be asked again this week and I would like about four of you to be able to tell in nine days that I said that before anybody asked me.*”

Two refusals and a man of forty-three who had given four of them over about six weeks, and about nine of them knew exactly which four they were.

Then they stood about twenty feet off that reed and did not go closer, and a man of fifty-four said why, and he cannot read, and he said it before anybody asked him.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel at the head of that drain and cannot read a word, and this is the second time in about seven days he has said out loud that nobody is going to stand in a place and read it at him.

“*I am not going to stand twenty feet off that reed and look at a woman and be the last person to hear her.*”

“*About nine of you can be sent for about about nine things and none of them is her and I have not been asked whether I would read a word to her if she said one and I am not going to find that out by standing here doing it in front of about nine people.*”

“*A man of fifty-four who cannot read is the worst person in this county to be put in front of a reed and it took about nine people in this county about six weeks to work that out and about four of them have never worked it out at all.*”

About four people in that grass had the twenty feet and about nine of them had about nine people who could be sent for about about nine things and none of them was her.

Then something that is not a person said one sentence in that reed, and it was addressed to her and not to him, and it was in about as many words as it took to say it, and nobody shouted anything back at it.

“*Nell Prince. You were in no book on the first day of the hundred and twelfth week and you have not been in any book since. Will you come at first light, on the days it is written on, and put your two hands on the stone in the ninth room and push it to the far end of the room and push it back.*”

Then nothing whatever happened, and about nine people stood in wet grass, and a man of thirty-one had a question in his mouth and did not ask it and said out loud that he was not going to.

“*I had it. I have had it since about the fourth second and I am not going to ask it and I am not going to say what I would have said if I had asked it, because a man of thirty-one who says what he would have said is a man who has said it.*”

“*And I am not going to be the man who asks it a second question this morning. A woman of twenty-eight checked a thing ten times on Monday and said it was the last of a fortnight and that she had not begun again, and I am not going to begin it either.*”

A question in a man’s mouth at about the fourth second, and about nine of them standing in grass waiting to find out what was in it.

Then she said one word, and it took her most of the time it takes a man of forty-three to give a reading, and about nine people heard it and nobody asked her anything at all.

And it was the second word she had said in eighty days.

About four people in that grass had the word and about nine of them could tell afterwards what it was about and the other half of them could not.

Nobody asked her. A man of sixty-one said out loud that he was not going to give anybody a figure for what one word had cost her, and about four of them in that grass had already worked out that they were never going to be told.

Then they went back up the water, and a man of forty-one carried a board he was never going to get back, and a boy of thirteen gave the count of the reed out loud on the bank and gave the working.

“*Eighty days. Eighty is seven hundred and nine less six hundred and twenty-nine, and she went into that reed at about the second hour on the Tuesday of the hundred and twelfth week, which is the same day, and she has not stood up since the second hour of that morning.*”

“*I counted two words in eighty days and one of them was this morning, and I am not going to be thanked for counting them and I am not going to be asked what either of them was about.*”

Eighty days and two words and not one figure for either of them, and about nine of them on that bank had walked up a road to hear the second one.

The fever was sixty weeks and six days old. He had been in that county a hundred and fifty-five days.

A boy of thirteen said that a woman who is in no book in this county is on the fifth line of a printed sheet, with a day against her, and that the day is the Wednesday a thing asked a man whether he would write it.

And something that is not a person asked her the same question in her own reed in the hearing of about nine people, and nobody answered it for her, and a man of thirty-one had a question in his mouth and did not ask it and said out loud that he was not going to.

And she said one word, and it was the second one in eighty days, and nobody asked her what it was about.
