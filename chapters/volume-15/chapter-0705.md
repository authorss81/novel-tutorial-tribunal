# Chapter 705: Seventh And Last Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-Second Week, And A Monday, And On Which A Woman Of Twenty-Eight Checks For The Tenth Time A Thing She Is Not Going To Say And Says It Is The Last Of A Fortnight And That She Has Not Begun Again, And A Man Of Thirty-One Offers For The Fifth Time To Be The Instrument Of A Different Kind Of Work And Is Refused In Nine Sentences By A Man About Fifty-Two Who Cannot See Well Who Says Out Loud That He Is The Best Instrument For It In Four Counties And That Is Why Nobody Is Going To Let Him Do It

Seventh and last day of the hundred and twenty-second week. His four hundred and fifty-fifth morning. Three hundred and five days after the settlement. The fever sixty weeks and two days. Two hundred and fifty-nine days since the division. Two hundred and five days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was still full at about the seventh hour for the seventh day and the man of forty-three gave the reading and the whole of it, and then said the one thing he had been keeping back since Saturday.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept a landing nineteen years. He has said he has nothing to say about a night five times and has then stopped saying it, and this morning he gave a figure for a night for the first time in about nine days and called it a figure of a river.

“*About sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour. Seven days, and it has been full every one of them at this hour, and I am not going to say the working out loud today because the working is the same working and you have all had it.*”

“*And it came off in the night on the Saturday of this week, four days after it was full on the Friday of this week, and that is the only night I am going to give a figure for this week and it is a figure of a river and not of a man of forty-three.*”

“*And I have been wrong about about four things in about nine days and I am going to say the true one of them, which is that I have been standing on that landing every morning of the seventh hour giving a reading of a thing that goes up and comes down and does not care what anybody is doing under it.*”

“*I would like about four of you to be able to tell in nine days that I said that about my own morning and not about anything else.*”

About four people on that bank had the seventh day and about nine of them had a man of forty-three giving a figure for a night for the first time in nine days.

Then a woman of twenty-eight checked a thing for the tenth time and said out loud that it was the last of a fortnight and that she had not begun again, and nobody improved on it and nobody asked her.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from. She came up that cart road on the Monday of the hundred and thirteenth week and has been in this county sixty-three days. Sixty-three is seven hundred and five less six hundred and forty-two. She is not one of the nine, she keeps a day book with two numbers a day in it in her own hand, and she made nine boards in four days and kept none of them.

“*I have checked it a tenth time this morning and it has not moved.*”

“*Nobody in this county has said out loud that a thing which asks a person to hold a door in the morning and a thing which keeps the record in four counties are the same thing, and I am still not going to be the one who says it, and it is still not because it is not true.*”

“*And I want it said that I have said the same sentence about nine times and have not said it once, and that this is the last morning of the fortnight and I have not begun again.*”

Then she said one more thing and she said it as a person giving a finding and not as a person giving an opinion, and she gave the working.

“*And here is the other half of this morning and it is mine and it is about a shape and not about a thing.*”

“*I have been treating a lamp on a hook and a column with a name in it as the same shape for about six weeks. They are not the same shape. A lamp on a hook is a lamp on a hook and there is nothing written on either side of it and nothing can be asked about a lamp.*”

“*A column is a place a thing goes so that it can be found again. That is all a column is. And there is no column in this county for anything except heads that come through a landing and carts and five lines on a leaf after the last head.*”

“*A shape is not a thing. I have been making them the same and I have said so out loud nine times without once saying that, and the reason I did not say it is that it would have been a sentence about a thing and I have not got a thing yet.*”

About four people on that bank had the shape and about nine of them had nine sentences and none of them said.

Then a man of thirty-one offered for the fifth time to be the instrument, and it was a different instrument, and he said so out loud before he offered it, and a man who cannot see well refused him in about nine sentences and gave the reason in the ninth.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s. He has been in this county a hundred and fifty-one days. A hundred and fifty-one is seven hundred and five less five hundred and fifty-four. He has offered four times in about three weeks to be the thing that is sent for and been refused four times out loud, and nobody ever told him to stop offering.

“*It is not the same offer and I am going to say that first.*”

“*The last four times I offered to be the thing that is sent for, which is a door or a gate or a person who is wanted. This is not that. This is: let me be the one who asks. Let me go up that road and down it and along the other four and ask about nine people one question each, in the plainest words anybody has got, one at a time, standing on their own step, and write down what they said and bring it back down here and put it on that counter for anybody to read.*”

“*And I am asking because there is no form in this county that says a person may not do it and no form that says a person may, and because I can do it better than about nine people I could name, and I have been in this county a hundred and fifty-one days and I have never once been the best instrument for anything that got sent for.*”

“*I have never once been sent for and I am not going to be, and there is about nine weeks of somebody else doing this one person at a time and it takes about nine weeks and it costs nobody anything.*”

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well. He is the fifth line of one of two sheets of deal in this county and has been that person eleven years without knowing it was a thing to be.

“*No, and here it is in front of about nine people, and I am not going to shorten it because I have said no to you four times in about three weeks and you have never once looked like a man who had been told no.*”

“*You have worked this county for a hundred and fifty-one days and I have watched you do it four times in about four weeks. A woman of forty-four. A man of sixty-one. A man of forty-three. A boy of thirteen.*”

“*Every one of them said a thing they had not planned to say. Every one of them said it to you and not to the room, and every one of them has told me since that they had not meant to.*”

“*And not one of them has said that you tricked them, and I have been waiting for that word since the first day of the hundred and nineteenth week from about nine different people.*”

“*So you are the best instrument for this in four counties and everybody on this bank knows it and I have just said it out loud.*”

“*And that is why nobody is going to let you do it. It is not a punishment and it is not distrust and there is no form anywhere that says either of those.*”

“*It is that you have never once in a hundred and fifty-one days been on the side of the person who is about to be asked, and if you were on that road in the morning you would be on the side of the asking, and there is nobody in this county who could tell the difference, and I could not tell the difference, and that is nine words and that is all I have got.*”

Nine sentences and the ninth one was the reason, and about nine of them on that bank had been waiting three weeks to hear a man say out loud that a man of thirty-one is the best instrument for something.

Then a man of thirty-one said the true thing about it out loud, and nobody improved on it and nobody said he was right and nobody said he was wrong.

“*That is the exactest thing anybody has said to me in this county and I am going to say what is true of it and then I am not going to say anything else about it this week.*”

“*I am not going to be on the side of the person who is about to be asked if I am the one asking. That is true and I have known it since a man of sixty-six put his own thumb in a worn place in a floor about five weeks ago and told me I could not get nine hundred people out of a nail.*”

“*Nobody thanks me for saying that I would have been good at it, and nobody is going to forgive me for it either, and I do not intend to be standing on this bank next week as a man who saw it coming.*”

Then a man of sixty-one refused to give a total, and gave the reason in about six sentences, and a woman of forty-four gave the other half of it before he finished.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept a book of heads and carts eleven years and wrote one number in it in eleven years.

“*I can give you every part of it and I cannot give you the total and I am not going to try.*”

“*One printed sheet. Nine lines. Eleven names. Two of them the same name twice. Two of them with no day. Two days on it that are the same day, which is a Wednesday and which is the day a thing that is not a person asked a man whether he would write it.*”

“*One man of thirty-six gone down that road and out of this county. One woman asked three times and answered twice. One woman out of every book in this county asked at about the ninth hour by something and answered. One woman in a reed who has been in no book and is not in one and I am not going to say what is in the reed this morning because I do not know and I am not going to guess on a bank.*”

“*And if you add those up you get a figure of a person. Not of a sheet, not of a week, not of a road. Of a person, and there is already more than one person on that bank who does not want to be a figure.*”

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs nineteen years for thirteen shillings and fourpence a year. She said the other half of that in about four sentences and did not raise her voice.

“*And there is a second reason he is not adding them up and he has not said it because it is mine and I have said it four times already this fortnight.*”

“*A number with a line under it is a total and a total is a thing that gets used. It gets used on a form, and a form gets printed, and a printed form comes up a road in a coat.*”

“*So the total is the last thing before the form and I am not going to have about nine people make it on a Monday because it would feel like getting to the end of something.*”

The total was not made and nobody was going to make it, and about nine of them came off that bank without a figure of a person on it.

Then a man of fifty-four said what he had been asked and what he had not been asked, and he cannot read, and nine or ten of them were on that bank and it was the last morning of the week.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel at the head of that drain and cannot read a word of the sheet that is lying in the open air about four feet from where he is standing.

“*I have been asked nothing this week and I want that said on this bank by a man of fifty-four who cannot read, on the last morning of a week, with about nine of you standing here.*”

“*I am the only person anybody in this county can be sent for about a wet place about as wide as one boot, and about a space in a wall, and about a thumb’s width of light under a door-stone, and a man’s morning. That is four things. Nobody has asked me to be a fifth and nobody has sent me.*”

“*And a woman of forty-four told you about nine people this morning that she has been asked three times. I have been asked nothing this week. And I am not putting those two side by side and I am not going to, and I would like somebody else to notice that I have not put them side by side and say it out loud in about four days so that I cannot pretend I did it.*”

He stood about four feet from a sheet of printed paper he cannot read and said it out loud anyway, and about nine of them had been there on Sunday when it was read out and he had not.

---

The fever was sixty weeks and two days old. He had been in that county a hundred and fifty-one days.

A woman of twenty-eight checked for the tenth time that a lamp on a hook and a column with a name in it are not the same shape, and said it was the last morning of a fortnight and that she had not begun again.

And a man of thirty-one offered for the fifth time to be the instrument of a different kind of work, and a man about fifty-two who cannot see well refused him in nine sentences and said out loud that he is the best instrument for it in four counties and that this is why nobody is going to let him do it.

And nobody made a total, and a man of fifty-four said he has been asked nothing this week and asked for that to be said.
