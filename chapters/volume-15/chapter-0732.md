# Chapter 732: Sixth Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-Sixth Week, And A Sunday, And About Nine People Go Down About Ninety Steps At The Fifth Hour Because A Keeping Is Done Before The Light, And A Man Of Fifty-Four Puts His Own Hand In The Ninth Opening And Says It Is Empty, And A Man Of Thirty-One Says He Wants To Be Sent For

Sixth day of the hundred and twenty-sixth week. His four hundred and eighty-second morning. Three hundred and thirty-two days after the settlement. The fever sixty-four weeks and one day. Two hundred and eighty-six days since the division. Two hundred and thirty-two days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was about eight inches over the top step at the seventh hour. The man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working. He also gave the figure for the next time about ninety steps can be walked, before anybody had asked him for it.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He has said he has nothing to say about a night five times and then stopped saying it, and has given two figures for a night since. He says neither of those two is his. He is not going to give a fourth thing out of the three he gives every morning.

“*About eight inches over the top step, at about the seventh hour.*”

“*Two days. It was about four at this hour on the Saturday, and that is the working.*”

“*And a bank that is full is about sixteen, and it holds until it comes off, and that is the whole of the working.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in six days from this morning, and that is the Saturday of next week.*”

“*A bank that is full on the fourth day comes off on the eighth.*”

“*I said that on the Wednesday of last week and I am not going to say it a fifth time.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open before anything else happened, and it was the eleventh morning, and he said the thing that had been standing behind the asking since the Friday of this week.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four, holds the gravel at the head of that drain, and cannot read a word of the paper on that counter. He has asked for the ninth line in the open every morning since the Thursday of the hundred and twenty-fifth week. The fifth thing that was asked of him on a Sunday of a fortnight is still not answered, and not one of the nine of you has asked him about it.

“*Eleven mornings and I have not varied the asking by one word.*”

“*A man of thirty-eight has been in the ninth of the nine rooms twice this week and he has told me on that bank about eight inches of water and a thumb.*”

“*I have thought about that for about four days.*”

“*I am going to ask for one thing this morning and I am not going to be thanked for asking.*”

“*I want to be in that room.*”

“*That is all of it. I cannot read, so I cannot sign anything, and I am not asking to sign anything.*”

“*And I am not asking to be one of two hands. I have heard what two hands are and I am not going to ask for that.*”

“*I am still not answering the fifth thing and nobody has asked me.*”

“*About four of you have not worked out that those are two different things.*”

Then the man of thirty-eight read that line out loud in the open, and then read thirteen names, and the sheet went back on that counter where anybody could put a hand on it.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and is the only person on that bank who can put a light on a thing in the dark. He has read that sheet twenty-two times in this county. He has been refused a figure for how long three times, asked for a fourth, and will not ask a fifth. He has decided every morning since the Monday of the hundred and twenty-fourth week not to say a name out loud. He has decided every morning since the Monday of the hundred and twenty-fourth week not to say a name out loud.

“*Nine words, and about four of them are two hands and nine feet along a groove.*”

“*And there is still no day against either name on that line, and there never was one.*”

“*Twenty-two is seven hundred and thirty-two less seven hundred and ten.*”

“*I have put a man of fifty-four’s request in my own head and I am not going to put it in a column.*”

“*I am not going to say anything about a room a hundred and thirty feet under this bank to the only person anybody in four counties can be sent for about four things.*”

“*I have not said a name out loud and I am not going to.*”

“*I have decided every morning since the Monday of the hundred and twenty-fourth week and the answer has not changed once.*”

About four of the people on that bank had a man of fifty-four who had asked to be in a room. About nine of them had a man of thirty-eight who had not put it anywhere.

Then about nine people went down about ninety steps at about the fifth hour, because a keeping is done before the light. The water was about eight inches over the top step and it was not off all ninety, and they went down it in the wet.

The man of thirty-eight went first with the lamp that is his. The man of forty-four bailed for about an hour and said nothing about it. A man of fifty-four went down at his own pace, and nobody helped him and nobody was asked to.

Then a man of fifty-four put his own hand into the ninth opening in that wall and said what was in it, and he cannot read, and he did not round it.

“*An inch and about the thickness of my own finger, and that is the water under the ninth door-stone.*”

“*That is not what I am asking about.*”

“*I have been in eight of the nine rooms in this passage in nineteen years. There is nothing in any of them.*”

“*I have had my hand in four of the nine openings.*”

“*And it is empty. It is cut about as deep as a man’s own hand and about as wide as a thumb and there is nothing in it and I cannot read so I am telling you by hand.*”

“*About four of you have been calling that a space since the Friday of last week, and so have I.*”

“*I am not going to say what a space is.*”

“*And a woman of twenty-nine in a reed is one of the people who is not in that thing on that counter four feet above this bank, and I have not been near her and I am not going to go near her this week.*”

“*And I am going to say the other half of it in this passage before the light.*”

“*A thing that is not a person asked me on a Sunday a fortnight ago whether I would come at first light on the days it is written on and put two hands on a stone.*”

“*There is a day against eight of the nine lines on that sheet.*”

“*The ninth line has never had a day, and I have been asked about the ninth line.*”

“*There is nothing on that line to come on.*”

“*About four of you have known that since the Friday of last week and the rest of you worked it out this week.*”

“*I am not going to say what that is.*”

“*I am going to say that I cannot be sent for about that line, because there is nothing to point at.*”

“*And I am going to be sent for about the other eight.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for putting my hand in a hole, and nobody is going to be told I was brave about asking to be in a room.*”

About nine of them stood in a hundred and thirty feet of dry passage with a lamp on the floor of a gallery. About four of them had a man of fifty-four who had been in eight of nine rooms and had not been asked why.

Then a man of thirty-one said out loud what he wanted, in the hearing of the nine of you standing there, before anybody had asked him anything. A woman of forty-four gave him the three places there is to put it, and then refused all three.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county a hundred and seventy-eight days. A hundred and seventy-eight is seven hundred and thirty-two less five hundred and fifty-four, and he has offered to be the one who asks six times in about three weeks and been refused six times out loud.

“*I want to be sent for about.*”

“*Not in this county. Anywhere. Any county that has a form with my name at the top of it.*”

“*I have wanted for a hundred and seventy-eight days to be a person a person can go and see.*”

“*A woman of forty-four said on the Friday of last week that knowing does not put a column in that county with my name at the top of it.*”

“*She was right and I am not going to argue with her about it.*”

“*I am going to say this anyway, because I have not said it in three weeks.*”

“*Three places. I am going to give you all three and then refuse all three in front of the nine of you standing there, and nobody is going to thank me for any of it.*”

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs at thirteen shillings and fourpence a year for nineteen years. There are nine of those chairs and not ten, and she is not a keeper of anything anybody can be sent for about. On the Saturday of this week she said he is the best instrument in four counties for being the one who asks.

“*One: a book in this county that a man of sixty-one has kept for eleven years.*”

“*He has written one number in it in eleven years, and he has refused nine spaces and two names and a first of the nine in about a fortnight.*”

“*Two: a piece of printed paper four hundred miles off, and it is to be given to one person in this county and not to a man who came up a plank, and that clause is printed at the top of it in smaller type.*”

“*That clause is a man of thirty-six being told what to do in a town two days off.*”

“*It is the one line on that thing that has ever had a person in it, and the person is not you.*”

“*Three: one of nine boards. Four of them carry a job with a space about as wide as a thumb in it where a name goes, and five of the nine jobs were never put back together.*”

“*And two of those nine are in this county and eight are in four counties and none of the eight is going to be found in about four days.*”

“*The eighth of the nine has been inside a man of fifty-four’s own coat for about five weeks.*”

“*He has not asked anybody to read it to him.*”

“*So there are three places, and I have given you all three, and I am refusing all three.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for it.*”

“*A column that gets asked once is not a column. It is a favour.*”

“*A man of forty-one said that on the last day of the hundred and twenty-fourth week, and I am using his words, because he has got it and I have not.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the true of it in his own mouth, and gave the working, and said nobody was going to be told which half of it was his.

“*She is right about all three and I am not going to take one of them back.*”

“*A hundred and seventy-eight is seven hundred and thirty-two less five hundred and fifty-four, and I have been in this county a hundred and seventy-eight days, and nobody has asked me for anything in one of them.*”

“*And I have just said out loud, in a passage a hundred and thirty feet under this bank, in the hearing of the nine of you standing there, that I want to be sent for about.*”

“*That is a want and not a finding, and a woman of forty-four told a man of thirty-one that on Saturday, and I have not improved on it.*”

“*And I am not going to be relieved of that, and I am not going to be told I was right to say it, and I would rather it was nothing at all.*”

“*And none of the three of those places is an answer to anything, and nobody in this passage is going to say that it is.*”

Then they went up. The man of thirty-eight carried the lamp up last and did not say a word about the light on the way past it. About nine of them stood at the head of that flight in the wind.

The fever was sixty-four weeks and one day old. He had been in that county a hundred and seventy-eight days.

A man of fifty-four who cannot read went down about ninety steps at the fifth hour and put his own hand in the ninth opening in that wall. He said it is empty, cut about as deep as a man’s own hand and about as wide as a thumb, and that he is telling it by hand because he cannot read.

And he said out loud in that passage that a thing that is not a person asked him a fortnight ago to come at first light on the days it is written on, and that there is a day against eight of the nine lines on that sheet and the ninth line has never had one.

And a man of thirty-one said out loud that he wants to be sent for about, in about nine people’s hearing. A woman of forty-four gave him three places there is to put that and refused all three of them, and said a column that gets asked once is a favour.
