# Chapter 724: Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-Fifth Week, And A Saturday, And On Which The Fever Was Sixty-Three Weeks Old, And A Man Of Thirty-Eight Finds A Thirteenth Name On Nine Lines And Gives The Working Before He Reads One Of Them, And It Is On A Line That Has A Day Against It, And Nobody In Four Counties Can Be Sent For About Who Wrote It

Fifth day of the hundred and twenty-fifth week. His four hundred and seventy-fourth morning. Three hundred and twenty-four days after the settlement. The fever sixty-three weeks old. Two hundred and seventy-eight days since the division. Two hundred and twenty-four days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was about eight inches over the top step at the seventh hour and the man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working.

Four of the nine people who went down that water were wet to the knee before it.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and has given two figures for a night, one on the Saturday of a fortnight ago and one on the Saturday before it, and he says neither of them is his.

“*About eight inches over the top step at the seventh hour. Two days.*”

“*It was about four at this hour yesterday, and that is the working, and I have said the working about nine times this fortnight.*”

“*And I am not going to give you a term on it. Ask a man of sixty-one and he will not give you one and I am not going to do it for him.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open before anything else happened, as he has asked for every morning since the Thursday of this week, and nobody was thanked.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel at the head of that drain. He cannot read a word of the paper on that counter, and has asked for the ninth line to be read to him in the open every morning while there is a reading on that counter.

That was the third morning and about four of the nine have noticed that he has not varied the asking by one word.

“*Same as yesterday and same as the day before, and that is three mornings and none of them is a different figure.*”

“*And I am still not going to say what I do with it.*”

“*A woman of forty has told me twice that she is not going to get one out of it, and I have not argued with her either morning.*”

Then the man of thirty-eight went down four feet and picked the sheet up off that counter and stood on that bank with it in his two hands.

He gave a number out loud and it was not the number he had given on five mornings running.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and has read that sheet thirteen times in this county, and had it in his own hands on about eleven of them.

“*Nine lines. Thirteen names. That is what is on it, and I am going to say the working before I read one of them.*”

“*There were twelve on Friday and there were twelve on Wednesday and there were twelve on Tuesday.*”

“*Fourteen is seven hundred and twenty-four less seven hundred and ten, and there are fourteen mornings of readings and not one of the fourteen has been in the dark.*”

“*And the thirteenth name is in ink and it is not the print.*”

“*It is on the seventh line, and the seventh line has got a day against it.*”

Then he read it out loud in the order it was written, because he reads everything on that sheet out loud every morning and is not going to be the man who decides which of them to leave off.

“*The seventh line, second name, and it is in ink and it is not the print, and it is under a day and not over a blank.*”

“*I am not going to say the name slower than I said the other twelve and I am not going to say it again.*”

“*And here is the other half and nobody asked me for it.*”

“*On Friday morning I walked four miles up that cart road and stood at a woman of forty-four’s own door and read her that ninth line out loud.*”

“*She did not ask me a question about it and she has not sent for me since, and I am not going up there again without being asked.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for four miles and I am not going to be told it was the right thing to do.*”

Then a woman of forty gave the whole of it in the order it went on that sheet, and gave the working, and refused to give anybody a total.

Wenna Callow is forty and asks for a day against a name. She worked out on the Monday of a fortnight that five of the eleven are in no book in this county, and has refused since then to say which three of them have a day.

She said on that Monday that she would rather somebody found out about it from a man with a hand and a book than from her, and about four of them have not understood that yet.

“*Twelve names and nine days on Friday. Thirteen names and nine days this morning.*”

“*And the new one is not on a blank. It is under a day.*”

“*And a day is a place a man sat in a chair, and that is what a woman of forty-four said through a door four miles up that cart road in about nine words.*”

“*And three of the thirteen have no day against them. Three is two and one, and the one is the twelfth, and it went on a week ago.*”

“*Four is seven hundred and twenty-four less seven hundred and twenty. That is four days.*”

“*And it is a figure of a gap and not of a person.*”

“*And I am not going to add it to the five I worked out on the Monday. The five is about people and the four is about a sheet.*”

“*And I am not going to say which three of the five have a day, and I said on the Monday that I would not, and I have not started.*”

Then a woman of sixty-four said what she had said a week early on purpose, and what it had cost her, and neither of them was about a name.

Ada Renk is sixty-four and is out of every book in this county, and has a name in ink on the ninth line of a piece of printed paper with no day against it.

She said on the Tuesday of last week that somebody was going to write another name on that thing in about four days, and nobody was going to be sent for about it.

“*I said about four days and it was four days, and I am not going to be thanked for it.*”

“*And I am not going to be told I was right, because I did not know, and I have already said that I did not know.*”

“*And I am not going to say whether it is going to be me again, and I am not going to stand here and be asked about that.*”

“*Here is the part that is mine and it is not about a name.*”

“*The twelfth went on a line with nothing against it and it cost nobody anything at all in six days.*”

“*And this one has gone on a line with a day against it, and a day is a place, and about four of you worked that out about a second after the man of thirty-eight stopped talking.*”

“*That is the first time anything on that counter has made anybody findable, and nobody in this county arranged it.*”

“*And I have been out of every book in this county for nineteen years and I know what that is worth to the person it happened to.*”

“*And I am not going to say which of the two of us it happened to, and I am not going to say it was me, and nobody on this bank is going to thank the man of thirty-eight for finding it.*”

Then the man of thirty-eight said the other half of it, which was about where he had been on Friday morning, and nobody had asked him.

Then a man of thirty-one said the shape of it out loud before anybody had finished being quiet, and a man of sixty-one took it off him in six sentences.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county a hundred and seventy days. A hundred and seventy is seven hundred and twenty-four less five hundred and fifty-four.

“*The thing that asked a man of fifty-four on Sunday at about the ninth hour on that bank is the thing that wrote a name on that counter in a night.*”

“*Nothing else in four counties can write. A pedlar can carry and a woman in a yard can give and neither of them can print.*”

“*And I am not saying that on purpose. I am saying it because it is the shape it makes and I have been making shapes out loud on this bank for about nine days.*”

“*No. Six sentences, and the sixth one is not mine, it is a fact.*”

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept a book of heads and carts in this county eleven years, and has written one number in it in eleven years, and refused a man of thirty-four’s shapes on the Tuesday of last week.

“*One: nobody in this county can be sent for about who wrote it, and that is the same sentence as the Thursday of last week.*”

“*Two: cannot be sent for about is not the same as did not, and you have put the two of them in one sentence because one of them is easier.*”

“*Three: there is no form anywhere that says a person may be sent for about a night.*”

“*Four: a thing that is not a person asked a man of fifty-four on Sunday in nine words on a bank in the open, and nobody in this county has ever seen it.*”

“*Five: and I am not going to tell you whether that is the same thing, and you are not going to be told either, and that is the whole of what I have.*”

“*Six: a man of fifty-four who cannot read has been asked every morning this week whether the nine words he has been given are the nine words that are on the thing, and he is not going to be told about your shape either.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the true of the fifth sentence in his own mouth, and gave the working, and said he would not improve on it.

“*He is right about five of those six and I am not going to argue with the fifth one.*”

“*I have wanted for a hundred and seventy days to be the man who finds things out, and there is no form in this county that says a man may not.*”

“*And what I actually want is not to find anything out.*”

“*It is to be the one somebody asks. Nine people in this county have worked that out over about nine days, and not one of them has said it.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for saying it here and I am not going to be relieved of it.*”

Then the sheet went back on that counter where anybody could put a hand on it, and about nine people stood on that bank in the wind, and nobody carried it anywhere.


---

The fever was sixty-three weeks old. He had been in that county a hundred and seventy days.

A man of thirty-eight went down four feet to a counter and found thirteen names on nine lines and not twelve, and gave the working out loud before he read one of them. There have been fourteen readings and not one of them in the dark, and the thirteenth name is in ink on the seventh line, and the seventh line has a day against it.

And a woman of forty said a day is a place a man sat in a chair, and refused to add a figure of four days to a figure of five people, and has still not said which three of the five have a day.

And a woman of sixty-four said this is the first time anything on that counter has made anybody findable, that nobody in this county arranged it, and that nobody on that bank is going to thank the man of thirty-eight for finding it.
