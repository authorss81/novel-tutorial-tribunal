# Chapter 727: First Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-Sixth Week, And A Tuesday, And A Man Of Thirty-Eight Stops In The Middle Of Reading A Line He Has Read Out Loud Seventeen Mornings Running, And A Woman Of Forty Gives Three Spaces And One Width, And A Man Of Thirty-One Says He Was Carried Over To Be A Mark

First day of the hundred and twenty-sixth week. His four hundred and seventy-seventh morning. Three hundred and twenty-seven days after the settlement. The fever sixty-three weeks and three days. Two hundred and eighty-one days since the division. Two hundred and twenty-seven days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was full at about the seventh hour for the fifth morning running and the man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working, and gave the figure for the next time about ninety steps can be walked before anybody asked him for it.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has stood on that landing every morning for nineteen years. He has not given a figure for a night since the Saturday of the hundred and twenty-second week, and he says the two he did give are neither of them his.

“*Sixteen inches over the top step at about the seventh hour.*”

“*Five days.*”

“*A bank that is full holds, and it was full on the Monday of last week, and that is the whole of the working.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is in three days from this morning, and that is the Friday of this week.*”

“*And I am not going to say that twice and I am not going to be thanked for saying it the first time on the Monday.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open before anything else happened, and it was the sixth morning and he had not varied the asking by one word.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel at the head of that drain and cannot read a word of the paper on that counter. He asked for the ninth line on the Thursday of last week and has asked every morning since while there is a reading on that counter, and he has still not answered the fifth thing that was asked of him on the Sunday of a fortnight.

“*Six mornings.*”

“*And there is a woman of forty on that bank who has been asking for a day against a name for about nineteen years, and I have not argued with her once this week.*”

“*And I have still not answered the fifth thing.*”

“*I said on the Sunday of a fortnight that I would wait to be asked.*”

“*About nine of you have not asked me and that is not a favour to anybody.*”

Then the man of thirty-eight read thirteen names out loud in the open, in the order they were written, and when he got to the top of that sheet he stopped in the middle of reading it.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and is the only person on that bank who can put a light on a thing in the dark. He has read that sheet seventeen times in this county, and has been refused a figure for how long three times, and decided again yesterday morning not to say a name out loud.

“*I read everything on that sheet out loud every morning and have not left one of them off in seventeen mornings.*”

“*And there is a line at the top of it above the nine lines, in smaller type.*”

“*It is not a name and it is not a day.*”

“*I am going to stop there.*”

“*And I am going to start it again from the top and I would like about nine of you to hear it twice before anybody says anything at all.*”

He started it again from the top and he read it a second time and his voice did not change the second time either.

“*The Shale Mirror.*”

“*Nine lines for the ninth opening, set out from a form carried over from Veyra and printed four hundred miles off.*”

“*To be read out where anybody can hear it and not to be kept.*”

“*To be given to one person in this county and not to a man who came up a plank.*”

Nobody on that bank said a word for about as long as it takes a man of forty-three to give a reading.

Then a woman of forty said what the word is, in four sentences, and then said the working for the whole of it.

Wenna Callow is forty and asks for a day against a name and has never once asked for a reason instead. She said on the Tuesday of a fortnight that a column which means one thing in a book and another thing on a sheet is one column in two places, and that there is no form in this county that says which one you are reading.

“*A mirror is a thing that shows you a thing and is not the thing.*”

“*That is what the word means in every language the nine of us have got, and nobody here needs a book.*”

“*And I said on the Tuesday of a fortnight ago that a column in two places is a thing that has been carried.*”

“*I did not know what I was talking about and I have worked it out since and I am not going to be thanked for it.*”

“*Here is the working, and I am giving it before the finding.*”

“*And I am going to read it twice, and the second time I am going to slow down, and nobody is going to thank me for either.*”

“*A name changes when it is carried.*”

“*A space where a name goes does not get narrower and it does not get wider.*”

“*There are three of those spaces in this county and all three of them are about as wide as a thumb and square.*”

“*One of them is cut into a sheet of printed paper four hundred miles off, beside the marks on that sheet.*”

“*One is cut between two hands on the ninth opening in a wall a hundred and thirty feet under a bank.*”

“*And I have had that one in my own hands.*”

“*And one of them is on a board that went out of this county on the last day of the hundred and twenty-first week.*”

“*Two of those nine are in this county and eight are in four counties, and none of the eight is going to be found in about four days.*”

“*Three spaces and one width.*”

“*Three is three and one is one and I am not going to add them.*”

“*One of the three was cut first.*”

“*The other two were cut from it.*”

“*And a copy is not an original.*”

Nine of them on that bank had a woman of forty who had worked out in nine sentences what seventeen mornings of reading had not given anybody, and four of them had three spaces of the same width.

Then a man of sixty-one said what a copy leaves out, in nine sentences, and he gave the working and he refused to give the tenth.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept a book of heads and carts in this county eleven years and has written one number in it in eleven years. There are five lines on the leaf after the last head, and a number is the last of the five, and he has not added a name to any of them.

“*One: a source does not have to be careful about what it leaves out, because it has got all of it.*”

“*Two: a copy is careful.*”

“*A copy carries what the person who made it could carry, and not the rest.*”

“*Three: and here is the figure of what is not in that sheet, and there is no tenth sentence.*”

“*Four: about nine people in this county cannot be sent for about anything.*”

“*Five: and a woman of twenty-nine in a reed is one of them.*”

“*A woman of sixty-four is one of them, and a man of thirty-one is one of them.*”

“*Six: and there is no line on that sheet for any of the three.*”

“*There is none in the book I have kept for eleven years either.*”

“*Seven: and the place nobody can be sent for about is not on the sheet.*”

“*The drain a man of fifty-four holds the gravel at is not on the sheet.*”

“*The fourth thing he can be sent for is not on the sheet.*”

“*Eight: so what that thing copies was written by somebody who could not carry the parts of this county where a person cannot be sent for about.*”

“*Nine: or somebody left them out.*”

“*I am sixty-one years old, and I have not worked out which of the two of those is true, and I am not going to.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the other half of it, and it was about himself, and he gave the working first and nobody improved on it.

Then he said one more thing before he got to himself, and a man of fifty-four took it off him in four sentences, and he cannot read.

“*No, and there is no fourth sentence in that. There are four.*”

“*One: I have been reading a line at the top of that sheet out loud in the open for seventeen mornings and I did not hear one word of it, and that is not going to be my figure.*”

“*Two: a man of fifty-four who cannot read was asked the same nine words on a bank at about the ninth hour on the Sunday of a fortnight, and there is nothing on that sheet that asks anybody anything.*”

“*Three: I am not going to be able to tell you what a thing that shows you a thing can carry.*”

“*Four: and I am not going to be thanked for asking a question on a bank in nine people’s hearing that I got wrong before anybody had answered it, and nobody is going to tell me I was half right either.*”

“*And I am not going to tell you whether the thing that asked me is on one side of that mirror or the other.*”

“*And there is nobody on this bank who can be sent for about to find out, and I am not going to be thanked for that either.*”

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county a hundred and seventy-three days. A hundred and seventy-three is seven hundred and twenty-seven less five hundred and fifty-four, and nobody has asked him for anything in one hundred and seventy-three days.

“*A dissident is somebody who was inside a thing and made another thing against it.*”

“*And you cannot check a thing from inside it, because everybody in the room agrees with the room.*”

“*So you get somebody who has never been in the room, you carry him over, and you put his mark next to the rule.*”

“*And that is what I am.*”

“*I have never been in the room.*”

“*There is no room in this county I could be sent for about, because no form in this county has my name at the top of it.*”

“*It is called an external witness where I come from. It means a mark that says this one is unsafe.*”

“*It does not supply anything at all.*”

“*That is the whole of it, and nobody in this county has been carrying a rule.*”

“*What I have been doing for a hundred and seventy-three days is the thing I was carried over to do.*”

“*I have not been relieved of it and I am not going to be.*”

Then he said the true of it, and it took him nine sentences, and he said out loud that nobody was going to be told he was right.

“*I have wanted for a hundred and seventy-three days to be somebody’s.*”

“*I said that in this county on the Wednesday of last week and a woman of forty-four told me I was the only kind of man here that is worth anything to anybody.*”

“*And I was not chosen.*”

“*I was selected.*”

“*And there is a difference and it is not a small one and I am not going to pretend it is.*”

“*Chosen is a thing you are asked about.*”

“*Selected is a thing somebody does to you and does not tell you, then asks you a question four hundred miles later, and takes what you do not say as the answer.*”

“*That is exactly what happened, and I did it, and I did not know I was doing it.*”

“*I have refused to supply things ever since with no more idea what I was for than a man of thirty-eight reading a line he had read seventeen times.*”

“*And nobody on this bank is going to tell me I was right about any part of it.*”

“*A woman of twenty-eight is going to say something about that in about nine seconds.*”

Then a woman of twenty-eight said it, in four sentences, and she did not raise her voice.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight and is not one of the nine and cannot be sent for about in this county or in the one she came from. She has said the same sentence nine times without saying it, and has not begun again, and her fortnight of not beginning is over and the seven weeks of mornings carry forward.

“*You are not going to be told you were right about any part of it, and that is not me being hard on you.*”

“*It is that you said nine sentences and eight were the same sentence in a different order.*”

“*A woman of forty has been giving the working first for three days.*”

“*And I am not going to begin again about the other thing.*”

“*Everybody on this bank now knows it is a copy, and that does not tell anybody here whether the thing that keeps the record is the same thing as the thing that says its own name.*”

“*And I checked it this morning.*”

“*Once.*”

“*And it does not carry an ordinal and I am not going to tell you what it is.*”

“*I am not going to be thanked for saying that I checked it.*”

Then a man of about fifty-two said what a mark is, and it was the thing he had asked for on the Monday and had been answered about a chair.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well, and is the fifth line of one of two sheets of deal in this county, and has been that person eleven years. He asked on the Monday of last week whether a line on a sheet is a person, and a woman of forty-four said it is not and that it is not him.

“*A mark is a thing somebody puts beside a rule to say nobody has been through it.*”

“*That is what it is, and I have been one for eleven years, and nobody ever asked me whether I would be.*”

“*There is no form anywhere that says a man may be asked.*”

“*And there is no form anywhere that says a man may not.*”

“*Both have been true for eleven years and neither is any use to me this morning.*”

“*And I am not going to be relieved and nobody on this bank is relieved.*”

“*I would like about four of you to be able to tell in four days that I said that and did not say why.*”

Then a woman of sixty-four said the part of it that was hers, and she did not hand it to anybody.

Ada Renk is sixty-four and has been out of every book in this county for nineteen years and got up out of the book of heads and carts in the open nineteen years ago, and she has a name in ink on the ninth line of that sheet with no day against it.

“*A copy leaves out what could not be carried, or what was left out on purpose, and a man of sixty-one has just said he has not worked out which.*”

“*I have been left out of every book in this county for nineteen years.*”

“*I have never once worked out whether that was somebody’s arm being full or somebody’s decision.*”

“*And this morning nine people have told me that the thing that left me out is a copy.*”

“*That does not change one thing about me, and I am not going to stand here and be told it was somebody being kind.*”

“*Being a name nobody can find by writing it down took nineteen years and it cost me one thing a day.*”

“*Being a name on a piece of printed paper took six days and it cost nobody anything at all.*”

“*I said that on the Friday of a fortnight, and a mirror does not change that either.*”

Then a woman of forty-four said the thing that was left to say, and it was four sentences, and it was not about a chair and not about a form.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and keeps a book of twenty-nine chairs at thirteen shillings and fourpence a year. Nine of those chairs and not ten, and nobody in four counties can be sent for about the book or the woman.

“*Nine of you have just worked out that a thing which prints nine lines four hundred miles off is not the thing that made the nine openings in the wall.*”

“*That is a finding and it belongs to a woman of forty.*”

“*And I am going to say the other half. Nobody on this bank has been given the right of refusing this morning, by anybody, in nine sentences or in four.*”

“*A copy is not a source, a mark is not a rule, and a man of thirty-one was not chosen.*”

“*None of the three is anybody’s right of refusing, and four of you have been waiting six weeks for somebody to say that.*”

“*And nobody is relieved, and nobody is forgiven, and nobody is going to be told this morning was a good morning.*”

“*And there is a Friday of this week that four of you have not said anything about.*”

Nine of them stood on that bank in the wind on the first morning of a week. Four of them had three spaces that are all the same width, and a copy, and nobody had said the word dissident twice.


---

The fever was sixty-three weeks and three days old. He had been in that county a hundred and seventy-three days.

A man of thirty-eight stopped in the middle of reading a line at the top of a printed sheet that he has read out loud in the open seventeen mornings running, and read it a second time, and it says that the thing that printed nine lines for the ninth opening is set out from a form carried over from Veyra.

And a woman of forty said a mirror is a thing that shows you a thing and is not the thing, and gave the working before the finding. There are three spaces in this county about as wide as a thumb and square, and all three are the same width, and one was cut first and the other two were cut from it.

And a man of thirty-one said out loud that he was carried over to be a mark and not to write, that it is called an external witness, that he was selected and not chosen, and that nobody on that bank was going to be told he was right about any part of it.
