# Chapter 701: Third Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-Second Week, And A Thursday, And On Which Nothing Whatever Happens For A Second Morning Running About A Question That Was Asked On Wednesday And Is Not Asked Again, And A Boy Of Thirteen Comes Down A Cart Road And Gives A Count Of A Question And An Answer That He Heard From Outside A Door Four Miles Off With The Working Said Out Loud, And A Man Of Fifty-Four Who Cannot Read Says The Water Under A Door-Stone Has Come Up About The Thickness Of His Own Finger Since A Monday In An Earlier Week And Nobody Has Been Sent For About It

Third day of the hundred and twenty-second week. His four hundred and fifty-first morning. Three hundred and one days after the settlement. The fever fifty-nine weeks and five days. Two hundred and fifty-five days since the division. Two hundred and one days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was about twelve inches over the top step at the seventh hour. The man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working, and he did not round it, and he said the other half of it as well because he has said both halves every morning of this week and it would be a new figure to say one.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept a landing nineteen years. A bank fills in about four days and empties in about three, and he has given both of those figures to the bank and says neither of them is his.

“*About twelve inches over the top step at the seventh hour. Three days since it came off all ninety, and it was about eight inches at this hour yesterday, and that is the working and I have said it out loud.*”

“*And the next time about ninety steps can be walked is about four days after that bank is full, and it was about sixteen inches on the Friday of this week, so it is in four days.*”

“*And I am not going to say what a full bank is for. I have never known and I am not going to work it out in front of about nine people.*”

About four people on that bank had the twelve inches and about nine of them had the four days.

Then nothing whatever happened, and it was the second morning running, and nine or ten of them were standing on that bank waiting for a thing that had happened once and were not going to be given it twice.

Then a man of thirty-one said out loud that nobody was going to get it twice and that he was the reason, and he said it flat and he said it once.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s. He has been in this county a hundred and forty-seven days. A hundred and forty-seven is seven hundred and one less five hundred and fifty-four. A thing that is not a person asked him on the Wednesday of this week, in about nine people’s hearing, whether he would write it, and he did not answer it, and he has not answered it this morning either.

“*I am going to say this one time and then I am not going to say it again this week.*”

“*Nothing whatever is going to happen on that bank this morning for the second morning running, and I am the reason it is not happening, and about nine of you came up here to hear it and I would like about four of you to be able to tell in nine days that I did not open my mouth.*”

“*And nobody on this bank is going to fill it for me. That is also mine and nobody else’s, and I am not going to let about nine of you do a thing for me out loud.*”

Nobody on that bank filled it, and that was said out loud, and it was the first time in this county that about nine of them had heard a man of thirty-one name a thing he had done as a thing.

Then a woman of forty said the true of about half of that before he had finished the second sentence of it, and she did not raise her voice, and she opened with the three words she has opened with in every room she has stood in for about six weeks.

Wenna Callow is forty and asks for a day against a name. There are nine lines on nothing in this county and she has never once asked for a reason instead.

“*Say what you are standing in front of.*”

“*You have just told about nine people that a thing which asked you a question on Wednesday is not going to ask you it twice, and that is not the same as a thing that has gone away, and I want the difference said out loud this morning because nobody has said it.*”

“*A thing that has gone away is a thing you can put in a week. That is not what you have said. What you have said is that it asked you once and you have not been asked again and the two of those are about four days apart and one of them is not going to last.*”

“*I am not going to improve on it and I am not going to thank you for saying it in front of me.*”

Then a boy of thirteen came down that cart road and gave a count, and he gave the working, and nobody had asked him, and he said so before he gave it.

Wat Marshe is thirteen. His mother is forty-one and has two carts and the road is the road the carts are on. Neither of them can be sent for about in this county and there is no form that says they may not, and he has said that sentence in about nine rooms.

“*Forty-eight days. Forty-eight is seven hundred and one less six hundred and fifty-three, and I counted it myself on the road coming down and nobody asked me.*”

“*I was in the second cart outside the bakehouse four miles up that road on the Wednesday of this week at about the eighth hour and I was in it eleven minutes.*”

“*Eleven is eleven and I did not round it and my mother was three carts up the road with sacks and I could hear both of them.*”

“*And a man I did not know came to that door and asked one question and then there was one word back, and the question was nine words and the one word was one word, and I counted both of them with my own two hands on the cart side and I am not going to say what the word was.*”

“*It sounded like yes. I am not going to say it was, because I am not the man who says what a thing was, and I was outside a door.*”

Nobody on that bank asked him a second thing and about four of them had the nine words and about nine of them had the eleven minutes.

Then a man of thirty-one said what the whole of that was, and he got it wrong, and a woman of forty took it off him in about nine sentences in front of about nine people and did not raise her voice.

“*So a man has come up that road with a sheet of paper and asked a woman to put her name to a keeping, and if she says no it comes back down that road with her no on it.*”

“*Say what you are standing in front of, and say it plainly, because a man of thirty-one has just made a shape out of a thing he has never seen, and I have been in this county long enough to know what he does with a shape.*”

“*A sheet is not a name. A name is a person you can be sent for about. You write a name down and you can ask for that person and the asking is allowed and has a shape.*”

“*A sheet with a column in it is a field. There are nine lines on it and about nine people in this county and a field is not a person.*”

“*And you cannot be sent for about a field, and that is not a thing anybody has ever wanted to be able to do.*”

Then a man of fifty-four said the thing nobody had come up that road for, and he cannot read, and he said it before he had been asked anything, and nine or ten of them were on that bank.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel at the head of that drain. He is the only person anybody in that county can be sent for about four things, and one of the four is about a boot and one foot of water, and he has not been asked to move it.

“*I put my own hand in that gap under the ninth door-stone again this morning before I came up, and I put it in twice, and it is not an inch any more.*”

“*It is an inch and about the thickness of my own finger, and I have got my own finger on it, and I am not going to round it and I am not going to say it is a thumb, because a thumb is a thumb and this is what is left of an inch.*”

“*And the same as on the Monday morning of the hundred and twentieth week, and the Monday before that, and it has come up twice since and about a finger’s thickness in all of it in about a fortnight.*”

“*And I am the only person anybody can be sent for about that water and nobody has sent me and nobody has asked me whether I would move it, and I have not moved it.*”

“*I want that said on this bank by a man of fifty-four who cannot read, because somebody is going to say it in about four days and I am not going to let the first person to say it be a man with a hand and a book.*”

Nobody on that bank said sorry and about four of them had the finger’s thickness.

Then a woman of forty-four said what a man of fifty-four had just done to a morning, and she had been on that bank before he had finished the working, and she said it in about four sentences and she said what it was a figure of.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept a book of twenty-nine chairs nineteen years for thirteen shillings and fourpence a year, and there are nine of those chairs and not ten, and she is not a keeper of anything anybody can be sent for about.

“*A man of fifty-four has said a thing about water and it is the first thing this morning that is a figure of the river and not a figure of a room.*”

“*And the whole of it is: there is a room that is a day, and nobody has been in it, and there is water coming up under its door, and the water is the only thing in this county that is telling anybody, and it cannot be sent for about because it is water.*”

“*That is what stopping looks like. I am not saying it is right and I am not saying it is wrong. I am saying it is the first thing anybody in this county has had that is not a person.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for the ninth time this fortnight for it either, because I did not say the water part and a man of fifty-four said the water part.*”

She had said a thing about water that nobody else had said, and about four of the people on that bank had understood all of it.

Then a man of sixty-one gave the counts he gives, in the order he gives them, and he did not add any of them to anything.

Nevin Trask is sixty-one and has kept a book of heads and carts eleven years. There are five lines on the leaf after the last head and a number is the last of the five, and there is not going to be a name on any of them.

“*A man of thirty-four has been in an ash on the low side of that landing for seventy-one days. Seventy-one is seven hundred and one less six hundred and thirty.*”

“*A woman of twenty-nine has been in a reed for seventy-two days, and seventy-two is seven hundred and one less six hundred and twenty-nine, and I have not added them and I am not going to.*”

“*A woman of twenty-eight came up that cart road on the Monday of the hundred and thirteenth week and has been in this county fifty-nine days. Fifty-nine is seven hundred and one less six hundred and forty-two.*”

“*And I have been asked by nobody to write down a boy of thirteen in a cart with his hands on the side of it counting words, and I am not going to, and I am not going to give a fourth reason.*”

He gave the three counts in the order he gives them and added none of them to anything, and about nine of them had heard him refuse a fifth reason.

Then a man of thirty-one said what he wanted, out loud, before anybody asked him, and gave the working, and then did not go, and said out loud that he was not going to and gave no reason at all.

“*I want to go up that road. Four miles. I want to go tonight, before the light, and I want to be standing on that step at about the eighth hour in the morning when whatever it is happens.*”

“*And here is the working and nobody is going to have to work it out. A woman of forty-four said the next time one of them comes up that road about a door, come to this door, on the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-first week. That is eight days. Eight is seven hundred and one less six hundred and ninety-three.*”

“*And one came up that road about a door on the Wednesday of this week and it was asked at about the eighth hour and a boy of thirteen was in a cart outside and heard nine words and one word back.*”

“*So that is where I was going to be standing, and I am not going, and I am telling about nine of you that I am not going about four seconds before I do not go, and I am not going to give a reason, because I have not got one that is any use to anybody.*”

He said out loud that he was not going and gave no reason, and about nine of them stood on that bank and let a man of thirty-one not go up a road he had worked out to the yard.

---

The fever was fifty-nine weeks and five days old. He had been in that county a hundred and forty-seven days.

Nothing whatever happened on that bank for a second morning running about a question that was asked on Wednesday. A boy of thirteen gave a count of a question of nine words and an answer of one word heard from outside a door four miles off that cart road, and refused to say whether the one word was yes.

And a man of fifty-four who cannot read said there is about a finger’s thickness of water under the ninth door-stone that was not there a fortnight ago, and that he is the only person anybody can be sent for about it, and that nobody has asked him.

And a woman of forty-four said that is what stopping looks like, and that it is the first thing anybody in this county has had that is not a person.

And a man of thirty-one said he wanted to walk four miles and did not, and gave no reason.
