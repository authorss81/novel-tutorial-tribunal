# Chapter 746: Sixth Day Of The Hundred And Twenty-Eighth Week, And A Sunday, And The Water Comes Off All Ninety Of The Steps In The Night For The Tenth Time Since The Saturday Of The Hundred And Nineteenth Week, And About Nine People Go Down About Ninety Steps At The Fifth Hour And Come Back Up And Say Nothing Whatsoever About Why

Sixth day of the hundred and twenty-eighth week. His four hundred and ninety-sixth morning. Three hundred and forty-six days after the settlement. The fever sixty-six weeks and one day. Three hundred days since the division. Two hundred and forty-six days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The water was off all ninety of the steps at the sixth hour and it came off in the night, and the man of forty-three gave the reading and said the working, and he had been on that landing at the second hour because there is nothing else to do at the second hour.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years and about ninety steps of the work are under the bank he was standing on. A bank that is full stays full until it comes off.

“*Off all ninety at the sixth hour. It came off in the night.*”

“*It was full on the Wednesday of this week and four days is the working, and four inches a day is the rest of it.*”

“*And that is the tenth time it has come off in the night since the Saturday of the hundred and nineteenth week, and the ninth of those was a Saturday and this one is a Sunday.*”

“*A bank that is full on the fourth day comes off on the eighth. I gave you that on Sunday and I am not going to give it again in this county.*”

“*There is no figure for a river and I have given you about nine numbers this week.*”

“*About four of you have worked out that the day-name drifts and I am not going to say it out loud for you.*”

Then a man of fifty-four had the ninth line read to him in the open before anything else happened, and it was the twenty-fifth morning, and it was the morning the water came off.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four and holds the gravel at the head of that drain and cannot read a word of the paper on that counter. He has asked for the ninth line in the open every morning since the Thursday of the hundred and twenty-fifth week, without varying the asking by one word. He asked to be in that room once and was given nothing, and asked again once and did not ask again. The fifth thing asked of him on a Sunday of a fortnight is still not answered and nobody has asked him about it.

“*Twenty-five mornings and I have not varied the asking by one word.*”

“*That water came off in the night and I have been listening to it since the second hour because I do not sleep through it.*”

“*I am not going to say that I knew about the three columns. I cannot read and that is the whole of what I know about paper.*”

“*The fifth thing is still not answered and nobody has asked me and I am still waiting.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for the twenty-five and nobody is going to be told I was brave about any part of it.*”

Then the man of thirty-eight read the ninth line out loud in the open, and read the fourteen names after it, and read the twelve words at the top of the third column, and then he did not read anything else, and the sheet went back on that counter where anybody could put a hand on it.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and is the only person on that bank who can put a light on a thing in the dark. He has read that sheet thirty-six times in this county. He has been refused a figure for how long three times, asked for a fourth, and will not ask a fifth. He stopped deciding at the Sunday of the hundred and twenty-seventh week and has not started again.

“*Thirty-six is seven hundred and forty-six less seven hundred and ten, and there has not been one of the thirty-six in the dark.*”

“*And there is still no fourth column on that thing and there is still no column for a person stopping, and it has been on that counter two mornings and nobody has put anything in any of the three.*”

“*I am not going to say that means anything. Two mornings is two mornings and a man of thirty-one refused it on Saturday and about nine people heard him and about nine people did not say a word.*”

“*And about four of you are going to work out in four days that I have been waiting since the Thursday of last week for that sheet to have something on it, and I am not going to help you with that.*”

Then about nine people went down about ninety steps at about the fifth hour, because a keeping is done before the light. The man of thirty-eight went first with the lamp that is his. The man of forty-four bailed for about an hour and said nothing about it. A man of fifty-four went down at his own pace and nobody helped him and nobody was asked to.

Then a man of thirty-eight said what he had found, and it was not what anybody had gone down for, and nobody asked him for it.

“*The ninth room is not being kept and has not been kept since the Friday of the hundred and twenty-sixth week.*”

“*There are two worn places in the floor of that room. One is mine. The other is about as deep as the thickness of my own finger and it is not mine.*”

“*A man of sixty-six put his thumb in one of those and said there are two of those and only two and chose neither. I am not going to choose either and I am not going to give either of them a figure.*”

“*And there is about a thumb’s width of light under the eighth door-stone that I have seen since the first day of the hundred and twenty-fifth week.*”

“*I do not know what it is. I am not a keeper of it and I did not say I was right first.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for a lamp going into a room where there has not been one for about a fortnight.*”

About nine of them stood in a hundred and thirty feet of dry passage with a lamp on the floor of a gallery. About four of them had a man of thirty-eight who had gone down for the ninth opening and said four things about a room.

Then a man of forty-one said the half of it that is about a form, and he said it flat, and he is not one of the nine.

The man is forty-one and keeps two carts and has been the ninth of nine for nineteen years and named the morning out loud in a passage at the fifth hour before the light on the Friday of the hundred and twenty-sixth week. Nothing fell down. There are nine of these things in this county with no original and no keeper.

“*I said on Saturday morning on that bank that a man of thirty-one refusing a piece of paper is not the same as what I did in this passage. I am going to say the other half of that this morning and it is not the same in the other direction either.*”

“*I have been the ninth of nine for nineteen years and there are nine of these things in this county. A man of thirty-four in an ash has the second pair of hands in this passage and he has not put them there and nobody has asked him to.*”

“*I am not going to say that is why the ninth room is not being kept. I have got a figure of work and not a figure of people.*”

“*I said my day out loud in this passage and nobody put their hands on the stone and nothing fell down.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for any of it. About four of you have not been told that and about nine of you have.*”

“*And I have said six sentences and about four of you have been waiting for a seventh and it is not coming.*”

Then a woman of sixty-four said what the whole of the morning is, and it was not about the room.

Ada Renk is sixty-four and has been out of every book in this county for nineteen years, and she has a name in ink on the ninth line of a sheet of printed paper, and that line has had a day against it since Monday morning.

“*There is a thing in this county with nine of them in it and no keeper, and a man of thirty-eight has just been down to one of them and it is not being kept.*”

“*A man of thirty-one said on Saturday that he was not going to fill any of the three columns in. About nine of you heard him, and about four of you are going to say in four days that that was the right thing to do.*”

“*He refused a piece of paper and the ninth room is still not being kept. That is what refusing a piece of paper did this morning and it did not do that.*”

“*Nineteen years out of every book in this county, and it cost me one thing a day of them.*”

“*Being a name on a piece of printed paper has taken six days the first time, eight the second and nine the third, and none of those cost anybody anything at all.*”

“*And there is a man of thirty-four in an ash and a woman of twenty-nine in a reed and neither of them has been asked anything in any of their days, and I am not going to be thanked for saying that on a Sunday morning.*”

“*I got the name before last a week early on purpose and I have got nothing for the last two, and all three of those are mine and not anybody’s, and about four of you can tell in four days that I said it on a bank and not in a book.*”

About nine of them stood on that bank in the wind with the water off all ninety and a sheet of printed paper on that counter with three columns on it and nothing in any of them.

Then a man of thirty-one said what he had not done, in the open, and gave the working, and said nobody was going to be told which half of it was his.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county a hundred and ninety-two days. A hundred and ninety-two is seven hundred and forty-six less five hundred and fifty-four. On Friday of last week he said the word amendment out loud in about nine people’s hearing and said he was not going to fill in any of the three columns.

“*A hundred and ninety-two is seven hundred and forty-six less five hundred and fifty-four, and I have been in this county a hundred and ninety-two days, and nobody has asked me for anything in one of them.*”

“*I said on Saturday that I was not going to fill anything in. Neither has about nine of you, and about four of you have been waiting two days for one of us to.*”

“*Here is what that did, and I am going to say it before anything else, because it is the shape of this morning and not a finding.*”

“*It did not keep the ninth room. It did not put a second pair of hands in this passage.*”

“*It did not ask a man of thirty-four whether he came on his day and it did not ask a man of fifty-four whether he could come on any of the nine.*”

“*Here is the want and it is the smallest one I have had in a fortnight and I am giving it because I gave the refusal first on Saturday and about four of you have not noticed.*”

“*I want somebody to put a second pair of hands on that stone before Friday and I am not going to be that person and I am not going to ask anybody to be it.*”

“*A man of forty-one just said six sentences about that and refused a seventh, and a woman of sixty-four just said that refusing a piece of paper did not keep a room, and both of those are about the same thing and I am not going to improve on either.*”

“*And here is the cost of saying a word out loud on Saturday, and it is the same cost as every other morning this week.*”

“*About nine people on that bank can now say that a man of thirty-one said a word in the open. A man of thirty-four in an ash has been there a hundred and sixteen days and has a day against his name, and my word has done nothing about it, and I said it in front of about nine people and I cannot unsay it.*”

“*And that is not the answer to anything that was asked on a Wednesday forty-six days ago.*”

“*Forty-six is seven hundred and forty-six less seven hundred. I am not going to let it be one and I am not going to let it be a reason.*”

“*Nobody is going to be relieved about this morning. Nobody is going to be thanked, and nobody is going to be told I was right on Saturday.*”

“*A man of fifty-four put a case in front of an argument on Friday and I did not know what my wanting was. I have not worked out what it is this morning either.*”

“*About four of you are going to be relieved in about four days and I would rather it was nothing at all.*”

---

The fever was sixty-six weeks and one day old. He had been in that county a hundred and ninety-two days.

The water came off all ninety of the steps in the night for the tenth time since the Saturday of the hundred and nineteenth week, and about nine people went down at the fifth hour because a keeping is done before the light.

The ninth room of nine is not being kept and has not been kept since the Friday of the hundred and twenty-sixth week.

And a man of thirty-one said out loud what his refusing a piece of paper had done, which was nothing: it did not keep the room, it did not put a second pair of hands in that passage, it did not ask a man of thirty-four whether he came on his day, and it did not ask a man of fifty-four whether he could come on any of the nine.
