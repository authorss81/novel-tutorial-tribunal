# Chapter 321: A Bridge With One Rail On It, And A Woman Who Knows Him By The Leg

The plank at Coldharrow said sixty-three thousand nine hundred and fifty-nine at the second light on the first morning of the sixty-eighth week, and he was standing at the foot of the ladder and read it himself, and the day it stood for was four bells and nothing whatever on top of them, which is the number most of the days in a year are.

It was his seventy-first morning in this country. He went down the lane and up a track and over a bridge with one rail on it, in the rain, with a leg, to be at it at about the seventh hour, because a person had told him the day and the hour eleven days ago and he had not written them down and there is no hand in this country he could have put them in.

He was there before her. That is the whole of what the morning was built on and he did not know it was going to be anything until it was over.

---

The bridge is a bridge and not a ford and there is no second rail on it because there has never been a second rail, and four people in that valley have asked for one in the last hundred years and the answer has been that a second rail is for a bridge that somebody is going to fall off, and nobody in that valley is going to fall off it, and that is a settled thing.

He stood at the top of it with his leg on the dry side. He did not go down. He did not go to the porch. He had come eleven miles and a track and a bridge for this and the whole of what he had to do was stand there, and there is no technique for standing there and there is not going to be one.

He thought about the sentence a man of sixty-three had given him in a field on the fourth morning of the sixty-fifth week, and it was ready, and he did not say it. *My father did it, and his father did it, and nobody ever said out loud what it was for, and if a person did not ask then nobody knew, and a thing nobody knows the reason for is a thing that stops when the last one stops.* He had carried it eleven miles and it was no use in a valley of sixty people, and he was not going to say it to a woman of seventy-four who had told him she is the last one, because saying it there would have been a man telling her what her own stopping is, and she is entitled to stop, and nothing in four hundred miles is going to put a person back on a list and call it a restoration.

He did not fix it. He did not try. He had a leg and a morning and nine words he had brought and he used none of them.

---

She came down the lane at about the seventh hour, with a stick and a hood, at the pace of a woman of seventy-four, and she stopped twice, and she did not stop a third time, exactly as she had told him she would, and he was at the bridge and she knew him by the leg.

She stopped nine feet off and put her stick down against the rail and looked at him, and neither of them said good morning, and that is correct.

“You are early,” she said.

“I did not know what early was.”

“You are early,” she said. “It is not a complaint. It is the first time anybody has been here before me and I want it said out loud that it happened, and I am not going to say it twice.”

Then she asked him what she had wanted asked, and she did not have to be asked twice, and it is the first time in eleven days that anybody in that valley has put a question to anybody in it.

---

He asked her the ordinary question again, in the ordinary words, standing the way he had stood the first time with his leg on one side of him and nothing in his hands.

“What are you for.”

Eleven days ago she had said, *There is a thing at the bottom of this valley, and there has to be a person who sees to it, and that is me, and it has no name and no wage and nobody has ever written it down, and it has been forty years, and nobody has ever asked me.* The ninth word of that had been this.

Tonight she said it in a different shape, and it was nine words, and he heard the difference and did not say he heard it.

“A person has to see to it. That person is me. Nobody has ever asked me.”

The ninth word was person.

He did not say anything about the ninth word. He has been in this country seventy-one mornings and the one thing he has learned that is worth anything is that a person who says a thing the second time in a different shape is not repeating herself, and the shape is the message, and if you point at the shape in a lane at a bridge you are a man doing a thing to a person instead of a thing to a valley.

So he asked the other two ordinary questions, in the order a person asks them.

“Who else knows.”

“Nobody,” she said, exactly as she had said it eleven days ago, in the same voice.

“Who has ever known.”

“Nobody,” she said. “And I have not told you the thing I told you last time, because you did not ask it last time and I have noticed that about you, and I am going to say it now and then I am not going to say it again in my life.”

He waited.

“There was a woman here thirty-one years ago,” she said. “She had a basket like mine and she came down that lane and she went down to the bottom and she was gone two hours and she came back up and she did not stop once, not at the bridge, not at the top, not at her own gate. And I was thirty-nine and I thought nothing about it. And in the thirty-one years since then I have thought about it perhaps four times, and every one of the four times I have stopped myself, and you may make of that what you like and I would rather you did not.”

Nobody said anything for nine seconds and the rain came off the one rail.

“I am not telling you she is the one before me,” she said. “I do not know that and I am seventy-four and I am not going to pretend to know a thing I have never asked anybody about. I am telling you that a person went down that lane in my time and did not stop, and I have never mentioned it to a living soul, and I have just mentioned it to a stranger with a bad leg, and you are welcome to make of that exactly what you are going to make of it anyway.”

---

Then she asked him one back, and it is the first time in this country that a person of one of these six places has turned one of his own questions on him, and nobody had told her to, and she said it standing up with her stick in her hand.

“What are you for.”

He had nothing.

That is not a performance and it is not a refusal and it took him nine seconds and she let all nine of them go past without helping him with any of them, which is what a person of about seventy-four does when they have asked a question and can tell that the answer is not coming in under a minute.

“I do not know,” he said.

“That is the first honest thing anybody in this valley has had off a stranger in eleven days,” she said. “The other one said nine words about a thing at the bottom of a valley and told two other people about it before he had got to the bottom of the lane. *I am not blaming you and I am not thanking you and both of those would be about me and not about you.*”

She picked her stick up.

“You are not going to be able to do anything about the thing at the bottom of this valley,” she said, “and neither am I, and that is not a wall, that is just what a person can say to a person in a lane. And I am going down now, and I am going to be about an hour down there, and you are not coming and I would rather you did not come, and the bridge is a bridge and you are welcome to stand on it.”

---

He stood on the bridge for about an hour with the rain coming off the one rail, and he did not follow her down, and he did not go to the porch, and he did not look at the board with the eleven names on it, and at the end of the hour he went back down the track.

On the way back a person of that place told him something, on his own account, in a gateway, in nine words, and it is the only piece of business that valley gave him that morning.

“The six of you are three days off,” she said. “It was four and then it was three. It has been changed twice and it is going to be changed again and one of the six is a woman who says you cannot have a day for it, and she is right, and you are not going to be in it.”

“I know I am not going to be in it.”

“You said that in a yard,” she said, “and he said it to one person after that, and a barwoman at the bottom of this county has told three people three different nothings about it, and you are the only one of the seven of you who is not talking to anybody. *That is her sentence and she did not finish it, and I am not going to finish it for her either, because it is not mine and I am seventy-four and there is a thing at the bottom of this valley.*”

She went in. He went down the track.

---

He got back to the lane at about the eleventh hour with the leg and the rain and the whole of the morning in him, and there is no instrument in this country that can take a valley out of a person, and he did not want it to, and he is not going to be thanked for any of it and he did not do it for that.

There is a thing at the bottom of that valley and there has to be a person who sees to it, and that person is seventy-four, and a thing that is kept alive by one person is a thing that stops when that person stops, and he has known that for eleven days and has not got over it and is not going to, and nobody in that valley has conceded it to him and he is not going to adjudicate it in a lane on his way up a track with a bad leg. She is entitled to stop. He cannot put her back. There is nothing in this country or in any county four hundred miles off that would let him, and if there were, it would be a form, and a form is a thing that puts a person in a record, and he has spent seventy-one mornings finding out what that costs and he was not going to pay it in a valley of sixty people for a woman who did not ask him for anything.

And then he went four days up the lane, on the same morning, because a person at the bottom of the lane had told him what a man of about thirty-eight had been saying at kitchen tables for eight days, and he had a leg and a decision and no instrument, and the hearing was three days off and had been changed twice and he was not going to be in it.

The day came to four bells and nothing whatever on top of them, which is the number most of the days in a year are, and there was no figure in that valley and there never is, and nobody in it was told anything.
