# Chapter 343: A Hearing That Cannot Be Produced, Held Anyway, In A Yard

The plank at Coldharrow said sixty-four thousand and one hundred and two at the second light on the second morning of the seventy-first week, and he read it himself off the wall at the foot of the ladder. Four bells and a market, which is eight, and the market is on the green by that tower in this county, and there is a bell in that tower that was not rung this morning either and no figure is attached to it.

It was his ninety-third morning. And it was the ninth morning of the walk, because the nine of them began on the first morning of the seventieth week and there have been eight since and this was the ninth and it was the last.

---

Nine of eleven went out at about the seventh hour and four hundred yards of hedge from the north end to the south end, and the ninth was a man with a scythe and the tenth never comes, and the twelfth name on the back of that bill was not on it and the man who wrote it does not know he wrote it.

He was not on it and has not been on any of the nine and was not going to be, and a man standing at the north end of four hundred yards of somebody's arrangement is a man who has been added to it.

Nine of eleven, in the last of the wet, with nine people stopping.

He walked down the lane afterwards and got into the yard behind the smithy at Coldharrow at about the tenth hour with a leg and the whole of the day still in front of him.

---

It is a hearing, and it is the first one that has ever been held in this county, and there is no form for it anywhere in four hundred miles and there never will be one, and it was held in a yard behind a smithy on a market morning with nine people in it and no paper of any kind.

Nobody produced it. That is the whole of it and it is worth a paragraph. A corridor was produced to a room with a hundred and forty people in it. A status was produced to a room. A hearing cannot be produced at all, because producing a thing means a person makes it and hands it to a room, and this thing is made of people saying things in front of other people, and if one of them is not in the room it is not a hearing, it is a report of a hearing, and there is no such thing in this country, and a man of about thirty-four who reads things out for a living has known that for nineteen years and could not have said it until this morning.

So it was held. That is the only verb available and it took four hours and it was in a yard because there is no room in this county with a door on it that anybody will lend.

---

Here is what actually happened in that yard, in the order it happened, and nobody had a list of it and nobody wrote one down.

The man of thirty-four said what it was, in four sentences, in the voice he reads things out in, and then stopped, and the finding is his and not the stranger's and it took him nine seconds. A thing that has to be in six places at once is a hearing. It cannot be produced because there is no paper in this county and there is no room with a door on it, and it cannot be held in one place either because a man cannot be in two places, and therefore it is held in six places on one morning and what joins them is not a document. What joins them is that six people said out loud who comes next, in six places, and then went home.

Nobody said anything. There is a market on the green outside the gate and nine people were in that yard and the tenth never comes.

“And I am going to say the other half of it before anybody asks me,” said the man of thirty-four, “and I am not going to be thanked for it. There is no form in this country that says a man may send anybody, and there is no form that says a man may be in two places, and this morning there are six people in six places saying six sentences and not one of them has been asked to be there by anybody, and every one of them is going there anyway. That is not a method. That is what is left when a method is not available.*”

---

And he stood outside the gate and stayed outside the gate, and he said so to the man of thirty-four when that man came out at about the twelfth hour, and it is worth saying that he said it out loud, in a lane, to one person, and that nobody had asked him.

“I am not in it,” he said. “*I have said I was not going to be in it in a yard and to one person and in a lane and twice at a bridge with one rail on it and none of those was an ask, and I am not in it this morning, and I am not lying about it, and the reason is not my leg any more. The reason is that a woman of about thirty-four at a bar said a thing at a cross three days ago on her own account and if I am in a yard then I am a man who put her in a thing in order to be somewhere himself and there is no form in four hundred miles that stops a man from doing that.*”

The man of thirty-four looked at him for a while.

“That is the first straight thing anybody has said about that in six weeks,” he said, “and I am not going to say anything else about it, and you are not going to be thanked, and I am going up the lane in the morning.”

---

The order was a round of turns, and the boy of seventeen had read it out in that same kitchen eight days earlier and nobody had asked him and it was the only thing anybody had ever proposed about the six in a month.

Nobody had read it out in a yard before and nobody could read it out of a book in a yard, because a book is the only thing in eleven villages that can say who is after whom and it cannot say when, and what says when is that six people are all standing up on the same morning.

So each one of them said their own turn, out loud, in their own mouth, in that yard, to whoever was standing near them.

The man of sixty-three said the woman of thirty. He had said it at a table and he said it in a yard and it came out the same, and there is no difference between a table and a yard in this county.

The woman of thirty said the man with the cart. And she said a name.

That is the beat of the morning and it is a small thing and it took her four seconds.

“I have said what I want three times,” she said, “*and I have said it in a field, to a stranger, in the rain, and nobody in this yard has heard it and nobody in this yard is going to hear it from him twice. So I am going to say a name. Say hers, and say it back, because I am the ninth of nine and I am in here and I came eleven miles on my own legs yesterday because nobody sent for me and I would not have come if somebody had.*”

And the man of thirty-four said the name of the man with the cart, in a yard, at about the eleventh hour, in the voice he reads things out in, and the man with the cart and a beast was standing at the gate with his beast's head round and he did not move and he did not say anything and nobody thanked anybody.

Nobody sent for her. She was in it anyway.

---

The man with the cart said the woman of about fifty-eight four days up the lane, and the yard went quiet for a moment, because that woman is six months gone with a child and said no to a stranger in nine words on a bridge in the middle of the last month and was right, and there is nobody in that yard who can carry her turn four days up a lane on a market morning.

And the man of thirty-four said the thing that made it a hearing rather than a complaint.

“*I am in Coldharrow one day a week and I am in the tenth village another and they have never fallen in the same week in nineteen years, and I am in that town in the spring and I am not in it now, and I will be in it in a week, and I will carry her turn up the lane on that morning and I will not put it in her mouth and I will not ask her for it. I will stand at that plinth and I will wait, and if she says who comes next she says who comes next, and if she does not then it stops at her and that is the answer.*”

Nobody had asked him to do that and he had said the price of it out loud in his own mouth before anybody could ask him, which is a thing he has been doing for nineteen years and is not going to be thanked for.

---

And the stranger stood outside a gate in a lane from the tenth hour to the last of the light and did not do one single thing, and there was nothing in that yard he could have done that would not have made it less of a hearing, and he had learned that standing at a near gate on a dry morning in a field behind eleven houses and he had paid nine minutes for it.

What the yard did, and it did it without him and without a book and without a form, was this. Six people said one sentence each. One of them said a name. One of them carried another person's sentence four days up a lane and would not put it in her mouth. One of them came eleven miles on her own legs because nobody sent for her. And at the end of it there was no document, and nothing had been produced, and nobody in that yard could have told you afterward exactly what had happened except that six people were going to say who comes next, and two of them had already said a name out loud and one of those names was a stranger's own.

And the round goes round. And it stops where a person will not say who comes next, and that is the answer, and nobody knows yet where that is, and he knows that he has been standing on the other side of the same bridge for two mornings in eighteen days.

---

The day came to four bells and a market, which is eight, and the market on the green went on and came down by the ninth hour, and nine of eleven walked four hundred yards of hedge in the last of the wet and two of them did not, and a woman of thirty said a name out loud in a yard in front of nine people because nobody had asked her to and she came eleven miles to do it, and nobody was relieved and nobody was thanked, and the hearing was held.
