# Chapter 322: It Is A Levy, And Nobody Is Going To Be Chosen

The plank at Coldharrow said sixty-three thousand nine hundred and sixty-three at the second light on the second morning of the sixty-eighth week, and he read it himself at the foot of the ladder before he went off the lane, and the day it stood for was four bells and a stranger, and the stranger was him.

It was his seventy-second morning. He came eleven miles down and off the lane to the tenth village at about the eighth hour with a leg and nine days of walking in him and one thing he had come for.

Nobody sent for anybody. There is a bar at the end of this village and a room over a smithy nine minutes from it, and a man of about thirty-eight who counts other people's money for a living is in one of them and not the other, and a woman of about thirty-four is in the other one and not the one, and both of those facts are the reason this morning can happen and neither of them has anything to do with him.

---

Nobody has been chosen.

That is the answer to the second half of what he came for, and he got it in the lane outside the smithy from a woman of about thirty-four who had it out on the end of a bar and said it before he asked, which is the way she says everything and has said for twenty-two years.

“Nobody,” she said. “The man of about thirty-four came on the market day and asked, in front of the smith's wife, and the smith's wife said she does not read a column, and he said he cannot follow a sum down a page, and a man of about forty-one who comes for the water said he would be frightened of a book, and that is three people in one morning, and there are four more in this village who could not do it either, and there is nobody else in this county who could. *He is going in about a fortnight and five days and the book is on a shelf and it is going to be on a shelf in a fortnight and five days, and it will go to the smithy nine times a year in the meantime because the woman with the bag carries it and she is not going to be chosen either and she is right, and I am not one of the six and there is no arrangement in this county that keeps me and I would not take it if there was.”

He did not ask her a third thing. He has asked that bar two questions in a month and both of them cost her something and he has not found a third one that would not.

---

The room over a smithy had four people in it and no market on. The smith's wife, because it is her room. The man of about thirty-four who reads things out, because it is the day he is here. The man of about thirty-eight at the table with the book shut in front of him, because a book that is shut is a book that is not being counted.

And the woman of about thirty-four, who came in at about the ninth hour and did not take her board out, which she has not done in twenty-two years.

He was not asked to say anything. That is the whole of what he did in that room for about an hour and ten minutes and he has been in this country seventy-two mornings and it is the first time he has been in a room with a book in it and had nothing to contribute to any of it.

---

The man of about thirty-eight said the thing, and he said it standing up, with his thumb on the shut book, in nine seconds, and he had been saying a version of it at kitchen tables for nine days and had never once said this version.

“Three pence a load,” he said. “Nine loads a day on the days the loads come. A hundred and forty days in a year in this county whatever anybody says about it. *Fifteen pounds fifteen a village a year is what that is, and she gave you that figure on the market morning, and I have said it out loud in eleven kitchens and not one of the eleven said anything back, and that is the whole of what it has ever produced.*

“*It is not a fund. It has never been a fund. It is a levy, and a levy is a figure somebody wrote down a long time ago, and everything since has been me counting what came in against the figure and finding that it came in right.*”

Nobody said anything. The smith's wife put a cup down on the sill and did not pick it up for about a minute, which is the thing she does.

“Eleven years,” he said. “Eleven years and nine thousand columns and I have never once asked what the column is a column of. And the answer, when this room worked out in four seconds that nobody has been chosen, was that nobody had, and I heard the word chosen, and the answer is the question.”

---

Then he said the second half, and it was the half that cost him, and he had worked it out in the four days and he had told nobody.

“I have checked the other ten villages four times in eleven years,” he said. “Four times. I go round them when I can and I take what each one has and I add it up against what came in, and I have never once found a hole in one of them. Not one, not a penny, not in four goes round the whole of them.

“And that is the part that frightens me, and I am going to say it out loud in a room because I have said the other part at eleven kitchen tables and I have not said this at any of them.”

He looked at the shut book.

“If I was wrong, I would have found it. I have been looking for it in eleven years with the only instrument there is and I have not found it, and the only two things that can be true are that eleven villages have never in their lives made a mistake about a number, or that the number is not a number of loads. And I know which one of those it is, because a man who cannot count does not come in right four times running.”

Nobody argued with him. The man of about thirty-four said, at about the fourth minute, that there is no form in this country that says a man may stop, and that it is true and was not asked for, and the room got worse, and the man of thirty-eight said, “I know,” and that was the second time in three days he had said those two words in that room.

---

And the man of thirty-one, who had been standing nine feet off a wall for an hour and ten minutes with nothing to say, said the only thing he said in that room, and he said it because he could not leave it alone, and he said it plainly and it was not an offer.

“Is it priced.”

The man of about thirty-eight turned round.

“No,” he said. “And that is the second thing I have not been asked in eleven years, and both of them were asked by a stranger, and the two strangers were not in the room together and were not in the village on the same day. *There is no price on a load in this county. There has never been a price on a load in this county. A thing that is a price can be asked about and a thing that is not a price is a sentence, and that is a sentence and not a figure, and you cannot enter anybody against a sentence.*”

---

Outside, in the lane, at about the eleventh hour, the man of thirty-one stood with his leg against a wall and worked out what he was about to do and what it would cost, and he did it in nine minutes and he did not tell anybody, and it is the most honest thing he has done in seventy-two mornings and it bought nothing at all.

The woman of about fifty-eight is four days up this lane in a market town in a county nobody living has stood in. She is six months gone with a child. Her father is seventy and he has kept a bridge for forty-four years and he did a column himself with a pencil at one o'clock in the morning six days ago and was right seven times in ten instead of nine, and she was asleep and was not woken, and there is no wage for a second reader at a set of beam scales in that country and there has never been one.

She said no to him in nine words on a bridge, and she was right, and he has not found one word that makes it less correct.

And he is going to go and find out, and there is nothing about going that costs him one single thing that he can measure, and that is exactly what going costs.

*The price of the technique is that a person finds out about themselves, and the reason it is the price and not a fee is that nobody asks for it, and the reason nobody asks for it is that it has never once been available.* He said that out loud to a wall in a lane in a village eleven miles off the Briar March and there was nobody in the lane and the rain took it, and he is seventy-two mornings into a country where the only instrument he has ever had is a question asked in a room, and he was about to use it again in a market he had no business in, and this time he was not offering anything, and he knew what that was, and it was not manners and it was not a technique, it was a man going to a place he had been refused in and going again for a reason that had nothing to do with the reason he was refused.

Nobody thanked him for the first time he went. Nobody is going to thank him for the second one either.

The day came to four bells and a stranger, which is seven, and the stranger was him, and the woman of about fifty at the bar at the bottom end of this lane read him off her own board in four seconds when he came back through at the last of the light and did not ask his business, and she is the only person in this county that has never asked him anything, and he has stopped being able to think of a country where that is a courtesy.
