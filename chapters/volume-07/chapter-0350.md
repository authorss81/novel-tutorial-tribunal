# Chapter 350: Nine Copies And Nobody Who Holds The Inventory

There is no figure for this morning. The plank is at the far end of the lane, nine miles up it, and a woman of forty-one was on a ladder against it at the second light, and he was in a market in the tenth village with a leg, and the day she put on that wall is not available to him in any way at all.

The day came to four bells and a market, which is eight, and the market is at the end of the road in the tenth village and it is in this county and it is on that plank, and there is one market on a day however many villages are having one, and the market four days up the lane is not this one and is not on anything.

It was his hundredth morning in this country. Nobody in that market said anything about that. There is a market in that street one day in a fortnight and thirty people in it and the ninth is the smith's wife and the tenth never comes.

---

The map is on the back of a flour mill's sack, because there is no sheet in this county and there has never been one, and a mill four miles off brings sacks, and the smith's wife had eleven in a drawer on the day six pieces of paper went out of her room in six hands.

It is nine things on a board the size of a door. That is the whole of it and it took a man of about thirty-four who reads things out for a living all morning and he did it in nine different hands because that is what copying is in a place with no scrivener in it.

Six of the nine are standing.

And standing does not mean a stone and there is no stone in this county and there is nothing in it to find and nobody in it has gone looking for anything. Standing means that there is a person in that place who says out loud who comes next, in their own mouth, in their own place, to whoever is standing near them, and nine days or a month or a year, and that is all.

One of the nine is gone. It is the porch, and the line under it says a porch in a valley of sixty people two miles off a track off the lane, eleven names, no copy, and a woman of about seventy-four who did not go down on a cold morning, and that is the whole of what is written about it and it is four words long and it is true.

And two of the nine are not found.

That is the second line and it took the longest and it is the only line anybody argued about. The basin. And Orison. And the two lines say the two words *not found* and a place, and that is all, because there is nothing else anybody in this county can honestly write, and four people in a room understood that and nine did not, and the man of thirty-four said both sentences out loud at the end of that room on purpose.

And nobody in this county has been to either and nobody is going to this month, and nobody in this county knows who laid them or when or why, and the two words on that board are the whole of the record and they are going to be the whole of the record for a long time.

---

Nobody holds the inventory.

That is the finding of the morning and it belongs to the woman of about twenty-nine and she said it at the ninth hour in a market with thirty people in it, and she had a board with a hundred and forty marks on it and no names.

“There are nine boards,” she said, “and there is going to be a ninth copy on the back of a flour mill's sack in this market by the last of the light, and I have got a list of a hundred and forty people and I do not know where the eight other boards are and neither does the man who is writing the ninth, and there is nobody in this county whose job it is to know, and there is not going to be, because there is no form in this country that says a man may appoint anybody to know.*”

She put the board down against a wall.

“*Nine people will hold one. Nobody will hold nine. That is not a defeat and it is not a victory and it is the only shape of it that this county can do, and in two years one of those nine boards is going to be in a house where nobody has read it and there is going to be a copy of a copy with a wrong thing on it, and I am not going to be the one who has to say so.*”

---

And the map is a document, and a document can be produced, in a place, on a day, to a person, and that is the whole of what it is and it is the only thing anybody in this county knows how to do with a piece of paper.

It is not a hearing. A hearing cannot be produced and has to be held and was held in a yard behind a smithy in this county in the middle of this month by nine people, and this is not that. This is a piece of paper that can be carried to a door.

And he is on it nowhere, and there is not going to be a name at the foot of it, and he said it out loud in a market street to thirty people at the tenth hour, in about a minute, and nobody asked him.

“There is no form in this country that says a man may set a name at the foot of a thing,” he said, “*and therefore every name in this country has been put there by somebody who decided to, and the man who puts a name at the foot of a thing is a man who makes a person, and a person is a thing a person can be asked about for the rest of their life, and there is nothing in four hundred miles that takes a person back out of it. So there is no name on that board and there is not going to be one, and if there is one on any of the nine in two years then somebody in a kitchen will have decided to put it there, and I am not one of the nine and I never was.*”

Nobody said anything and nine people in that market did not follow any of it and that is the ordinary number and it is not going to change.

---

And then, at about the eleventh hour, in the middle of a market, a woman of about twenty-four with a barrow of apples said it.

She had not been in that market for more than a quarter of an hour. She came in the back of it, off the road, with a barrow and a barrow-wheel that wanted oil, and she had been listening to the man of thirty-four read out what was on a board for as long as it took to get the barrow level.

She said it to the market and not to anybody in it, in the voice of a person asking about the price of something.

“Two of them are not found,” she said. “*The basin and the town. Is there somebody down there with them.*”

Nobody said anything.

She waited as long as a person waits for an answer to a thing they have asked about the price of something, and then she said: “*I did not think so. I have a brother who went at the basin in the spring and he came back and he will not talk about it and he is not a quiet man.*”

And she pushed the barrow round the end of the market and sold nine apples and went out into the road, and nobody in that market answered her, and nothing answered her, and no board said anything and nothing in that valley and nothing under that basin and nothing under that town said anything, and the woman of twenty-four is nobody's record and she will not be thanked by anybody and she is not going to be.

---

He did not answer her.

He had the instrument in his hands for four seconds and it was the one thing he has ever had and it has never once been complicated: a question, asked out loud, in front of other people, about a thing that is not his, which makes a person a person in a record.

And he has used it twice in a month. The first time it cost a woman of about thirty-four at a bar eleven miles down a lane and off it a thing she cannot put down and is never going to be thanked for and cannot say no to. The second time is in his own head nine seconds ago.

So he shut his mouth in a market street in the tenth village with thirty people in it while a woman of twenty-four pushed a barrow of apples out into the road, and he did it in four seconds and nobody noticed and nobody helped him and it cost him nothing, which is how he knows it was the right thing to do.

That is the whole of what he has. A word is not a person and a name is not a person and a board is not a person and a stranger with a leg is not a person, and there is no other hand in four hundred miles.

---

The ninth copy was finished at about the last of the light in a market street by the man of thirty-four with a strap on a bag who reads things out for a living at three tables in this county and has not been thanked for it in nineteen years and did not say so this morning.

He read the nine lines out loud to nine people, because that is what he does with a thing, and the eight lines were the arrangement and the ninth line was where it is.

And then he read the list of who is holding the nine copies, because the woman of twenty-nine with the board wanted it read, and it is nine names and she is on it, and she did not put it there.

“Ninth copy, the market at the end of this road,” he said, “*and that is a woman of about twenty-nine called — *”

And she said, out loud, in front of nine people, before he got to the end of it:

“That is not me. *I have not been asked and I have not agreed to that and I have a board with a hundred and forty marks on it and no names on it, and that is mine and it is in a different lane, and somebody in this county has decided to put me at the foot of a board of paper and I am telling you in front of these people that I did not and I would have said no if I had been asked and I am not going to be thanked for saying it.*”

Nobody said anything. There is no form in this country that says a man may remove a name from a piece of paper once it is on there, and there is not going to be one, and the man of thirty-four put the board down and said that he had not put it there either and had copied what was in front of him, and that is the answer and it is the whole of the answer.

---

Nobody was relieved. Nobody was forgiven. Nobody was thanked.

Nine copies of a partial map exist this evening and nine people hold one and nobody holds the inventory, and six of the nine are standing and one is gone and two are not found, and ninety people in nine towns cannot pay a difference that nobody in this county has written down, and a woman of about thirty-four at a bar is a person in a record, and a man of thirty-eight is somewhere on a road with a bag and nobody has asked him what he wants, and a woman of about seventy-four is in her own house with the latch stiff, and a boy of seventeen is going somewhere he has not got to yet with a book under his arm that can order things and cannot date them and a question in it with a mark on the end of it that nobody has answered and a third place he can see and has never been able to make out.

And a man of about thirty with a cart and a beast came through the market at the last of the light and went back down a mile of road that nine men keep, unpaid, ninety years, and he did not mow his length and nobody asked him why, and a man of thirty-four rolled up a board the size of a door with nine things on it and went off up the lane at a walk without saying anything to anybody, and he is not going to be thanked.
