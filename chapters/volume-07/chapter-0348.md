# Chapter 348: Six Rooms And A Thing That Goes On Its Own

The plank at Coldharrow said sixty-four thousand and one hundred and thirty-eight at the second light on the seventh and last morning of the seventy-first week, and he read it himself off the wall at the foot of the ladder. Four bells and a market and a debt called in, which is ten, and the market is at the end of the road in the tenth village and the debt is at the fourth house on a mile of road nine men keep, and both of those are in this county and on that wall, and neither of them is four days up the lane.

It was his ninety-eighth morning. Three of the seven weights on one day is the most this plank has ever carried and a day carries one of each kind, and there is a fourth weight on today that the plank has not got yet and will have by the last of the light.

---

The six were linked on one morning in six places and there is no form anywhere in four hundred miles that says a man may do that, and no document went anywhere.

He was not in five of the six places. That is not a confession and it is not modesty, it is the finding of four days ago standing up in the open again: a man cannot be in two places, and there is no form that says a man may send anybody, and what carried the six was six people saying one sentence each in their own mouths in their own places and a man of thirty-four going up the lane who was going up it anyway.

The first turn was said in a kitchen in the third village by the man of sixty-three, to a smith's wife and eight other people, and it was the woman of thirty.

The second turn was said at a near gate in a hamlet of forty people eleven miles down the lane and off it, by the woman of thirty, to a man of about fifty-one with a spade who was going to the tenth village that morning with a barrow, and it was a name, and it was the man with the cart, and she said it in nine words and the ninth of them was the name.

The third turn was said on a mile of road by the man with the cart and a beast, to nobody, because there was nobody on the length, and it was the woman of fifty-eight four days up the lane.

The fourth turn was said at the end of a main street four days up the lane by the woman of fifty-eight herself, to nine people at a market, in nine words, and she is six months gone with a child and she said it without sitting down.

The fifth turn could not be said at all. The man of thirty-eight is four days up a road with a bag and a bad back and a book he has not decided about, and nobody in any county knows which road, and there is no form anywhere that says a man may look for a man.

And the man of thirty-four said that out loud at a market at the end of that road at about the ninth hour, to a woman with a pitch who was counting on a standpipe and who asked him a question about a pitch and got it.

“That is the answer,” he said. “*It stops at him. I have been telling a stranger for six weeks that if a person will not say who comes next then it stops at them and that is the answer, and I have not believed my own sentence until a man stood in a market and told me there is no road in this country that will tell me where he is. The round stops at a man of thirty-eight with a bag and nobody is to blame and a boy of seventeen is not going to say his name and a stranger is not going to go and look for him.*”

That was on the same morning. It was not carried by anybody. It was a man of thirty-four saying a thing in a market street because he reads things out for a living and that is what he does with a thing.

---

And the sixth turn was not said, and the seventh was not said, and both of them are in a porch in a valley of sixty people two miles off a track off the lane, and nobody said either of them because nobody has asked the woman with the stick anything about anything, and he has known that for eight mornings and had it in four seconds on a bridge and has not gone.

So the six were not linked. Five were, and two are not, and one of the two is a porch.

He stood at the top of the lane at about the tenth hour with a leg and worked out in nine seconds that the six arrangements that have been open in this county for a hundred mornings are open now for one more reason than they were, and that the reason is him, and that it is not the only reason and that there is no instrument in four hundred miles that can make it not be a reason.

---

The debt was called in at about the seventh hour at the fourth house on a mile of road, and nobody in that lane had any way of stopping it and no form in this country says a man may stop being allowed to stand on a step.

There is a widow of about sixty-eight at that house with a bad hip and she has not been out of her gate before the eighth hour since the spring, and at the fifth hour every morning a barrow has been going down that lane to her gate and stopping outside it and there is a woman of about forty-one at the seventh house who puts it there at the fifth hour and has done since before the month turned and cannot now say no, and nobody in that lane knows except the man of thirty-four and the man of thirty.

The debt was called in by a man with a paper and he was right on the facts and the door was shut and it is the second time and it is not the fourth and the man with the cart went past at about the seventh hour with his beast and did not stop and said nine words to the stranger at the top gate about a sentence that gets in front of the person who put it in the lane, and nobody stopped the debt and nobody resolved it and it is not going to be resolved by a stranger with a leg and a fortnight.

---

And then, at about the second hour, a woman of about sixty-two came up the track and up the lane on foot with her sleeves over her hands, and she had come out because there was smoke coming off a porch in her own valley, and she had not gone up the track to it, and she did not know him and did not know he was on the lane.

“There is smoke off it,” she said, “*off the porch at the bottom, and it has been since before the light, and I am sixty-two and I have lived in that valley all my life and I am telling you that nobody has gone up it, and I am not going to, and I have not gone up it and I am not going to now.*”

Nobody in that lane said anything.

He got his leg over the last of the track at about the fourth hour and there were six of them in it and not one of them had been sent for and two of them were people of that valley and four of them were people of this county, and nobody had asked any of them to be there.

---

Here is what was left of it, and it took him four minutes to see and he has not got a better sentence for it in ninety-eight mornings.

The porch is a porch. It has a board in it and it has had a board in it since before the mother of a woman of seventy-four was born, and the board has eleven names on it and no date and no column and no heading and no mark against any of the eleven.

And there is a box in it. There is a hearth in it that has a door to it and a flue that goes up through the roof of a porch, and there is ash in the box from four hundred mornings, and there is paper in the porch, and there is a board in the porch, and a fire had been in that box since before the light on a cold morning because a woman of seventy-four lays one in on the cold mornings and has done it forty years and has gone down four mornings a year and has told nobody what for.

Nobody knew that. That is the whole of it. Not one person in four hundred miles knew there was a fire in that porch, because there is no form in this country that says a man may ask a woman of seventy-four what she goes down for, and nobody has ever asked her, and she is seventy-four, and she is entitled.

And she did not go down that morning. She did not go down because she is seventy-four and it was a cold morning and she has a stick and she came up the lane at the pace of a woman of seventy-four, and she has said out loud, in a lane, to a stranger with a bad leg, that if she stopped in the spring then it stopped in the spring and that would be correct.

He stood in the lane outside that porch at about the fifth hour and did not go in it and did not say one word about any of it for a long time.

---

And then the woman with the stick said the thing, and she said it in her own mouth, in a doorway, to five people, and it took her a while and she was not trying to make it land.

“You have come up that track,” she said. “*I told you on a bridge that you were going to be here in nine days and you have come up that track on the ninth day and not the tenth, and that is the only thing in this that I have any opinion about, and I have told you I was not going to say it twice.*”

Nobody said anything.

“There is a box in that porch,” she said. “*I have laid a fire in it since before I was married, on the cold mornings, and I have done it forty years, and I have been down there four mornings a year and I have not told anybody what for, and nobody has ever asked me what for, and that is the arrangement and it has been a good arrangement and it is the reason there is a board in there you could read.*”

She put her stick down against the door frame.

“I did not go down this morning,” she said. “*That is all. I did not go down this morning because I am seventy-four and it was cold and I have a stick, and I have been telling a stranger with a bad leg for nine days that a thing that is kept alive by one person is a thing that stops when that person stops, and he has not conceded it and he is not going to be able to settle it, and I said in the spring would be the spring and it is not the spring, it is a cold morning in the last week of a month and I did not go down.*”

She looked at the five of them.

“*Nobody did this. I want that said in this lane and I want it said by somebody, and I am seventy-four and I am entitled to stop and I have stopped, and there is nobody in this valley I would want to be told to go down on a cold morning, and there is not one person in this valley who knew there was a fire in there until an hour ago, including him.*”

---

Nobody blamed anybody.

That is what happened in that lane and it is what happens in a lane. The woman of sixty-two said that nobody in that valley had gone up the track and she was not going to and had not gone and was not going to now. A man of about forty-one who lives in the second house in that valley said that the porch had been a porch since before he was born and he had not been in it since he was a boy and he did not know there was a box in it. A man with a bad leg stood in a lane and said nothing at all about a bridge, or about eight mornings, or about a decision he had made in a lane at a gate, and nobody in that lane asked him about it and nobody in that lane blamed him and there is no form in four hundred miles that would let anybody blame him and there is not going to be one.

The woman with the stick said, at the sixth hour, in her own doorway: “*Do not take it out of that fire. It was mine and it was forty years and it is finished and I am seventy-four and I am not going to be asked about it by anybody, and if I am asked about it by anybody I shall say what I said.*”

And then she went in, and shut the door, and had somebody else's hand on the latch behind her because the latch is stiff and has been stiff for forty years and there is no form in this country that says a man may get a latch up for a person of seventy-four who has told him she is not going to be asked.

Nobody put it out. There is nothing in that valley that puts a porch out and there is nobody in that valley who was going to, and the valley is sixty people and a bridge with one rail on it and a track of two miles and a gate that nobody in forty years has thought was a gate, and the six arrangements in this county are five and a porch and the porch is not one of them any more.

---

Nobody was relieved. Nobody was thanked. Nobody said that it had been worth it and nobody said that it had not.

A board with eleven names on it that had been in a porch since before a woman of seventy-four's mother was born went out of that porch between the fourth hour and the last of the light, and the people of that valley looked at it in the lane, and it is gone and it cannot be got back and there is no copy of it in four hundred miles and there is not going to be one, because the arrangement was that one woman went down four mornings a year and there was never any writing about it and there is not any now.

The day came to four bells and a market and a debt called in and a fire, which is twelve, and the four of them were all in this county and all of them are on the plank at Coldharrow, and a fire is two and it is the first time in a hundred mornings that this plank has carried one, and a woman of seventy-four is entitled to stop and nobody is to blame and nobody said the sixth turn and nobody said the seventh and the round stops.
