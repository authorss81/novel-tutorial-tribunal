# Chapter 865: Sixth Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Fifth Week, And A Sunday, And Sixteen Inches Holding With One Day Of The Coming Back And About Sixty Of Those Steps Under, And The Man Of Forty-Three Says Tomorrow Is The Twenty-Fifth Time And Gives The Job Of Counting The Dry Steps To A Man Of Thirty-Eight And Tells Him He Does Not Want It Done Right, And A Man Of Thirty-One Says He Could Count Them Better And Is Told It Is The First Wrong Thing Said On That Landing In Four Days

Sixth day of the hundred and forty-fifth week. His six hundred and fifteenth morning. Four hundred and sixty-five days after the settlement. The fever eighty-three weeks and one day old. Four hundred and nineteen days since the division. Three hundred and sixty-five days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

Sixteen inches on the top step at about the seventh hour and it was one day of the coming back. About sixty of those ninety steps were under. The man of forty-three gave both figures and then said what tomorrow is, and gave the number of the time.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and gives four things every morning. The fourth is a figure of how many of those ninety steps are under, and on an off-morning there is nothing under them and he has always said so.

“*Sixteen at the seventh hour, one day of the coming back, about sixty of those ninety steps under, and it holds.*”

“*Tomorrow at about the sixth hour about ninety of those steps can be walked, and that is the twenty-fifth time. I have given that number on every morning of this week and I am going to give it for a twenty-fifth tomorrow, and I have not missed one.*”

“*And on the twenty-fifth I am not giving the fourth thing. That is the first time in nineteen years I have said that about an off-morning and about four of you can ask me about it after and I will tell you the working then and not before.*”

Then a man of thirty-eight was given a job with a wet foot in it, at about the ninth hour, and was told what the man of forty-three wanted and did not want out of it.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and there is a board with thirty-two notches on the edge of it in the inside of his coat, which is a figure of what is on a board. On the Saturday of the hundred and forty-third week he asked what three of the nine positions are and who put them there, and was not answered.

“*You. Tomorrow at the sixth hour you stand at the top of that cut and you count the steps that are dry, and you say the figure out loud to about nine of you, and then I say mine after you and we find out whether we agree.*”

“*I will count them right.*”

“*I do not want you to count them right. That is the whole thing and about four of you will think it is a joke and it is not.*”

“*I have given that figure nineteen years and it is a figure of forty-three. Every morning it is a figure of forty-three, and on a Sunday when about four of you are not paying attention it is still a figure of forty-three, and there is no way on this landing for that figure to be a figure of a landing.*”

“*So I want it counted by somebody who is not me. And I am going to say it wrong when I say mine afterwards if we do not agree, because if we do not agree then one of us is wrong and it had better not be you.*”

“*And you are not going to be thanked for it tomorrow and neither am I, and about four of you are not to write down that a man of thirty-eight was given a job with a wet foot in it on this Sunday morning.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said he could count them better than a man of thirty-eight, and was told by the man of forty-three that this was the first wrong thing about a figure said on that landing in four days, and that it had been said about arithmetic.

“*I could count them better. I have counted about ninety things a morning for nineteen days on the strength of a boy of thirteen’s subtraction and I am faster than a man who has a child of nine and a board in his coat.*”

“*Yes. And that is the first wrong thing about a figure anybody has said on this landing in four days, and it was said about arithmetic.*”

“*You have been wrong five times in five days in about nine of your ears, and I have been listening to all five of them. You offered to read a man of fifty-two’s sentence for him on the Wednesday. You said you could put a line under a leaf being gone on the Thursday. You made three books into one on the Friday. You said a sentence is the same whoever says it on the Saturday. And a man of fifty-four told you on Monday that a leaf is not a stone.*”

“*And now you have told me you are faster than a man of thirty-eight at counting. That is the sixth of them and it is the only one of the six that was about a figure.*”

“*I do not want to know that you are faster. I want a person who is not me to say a figure in about nine of your ears, and if it is wrong then tomorrow is a morning with a wrong figure on it and I will say so and say whose it was.*”

Ilyan Vester, thirty-one, and is nobody’s, and has been in this county three hundred and eleven days, three hundred and eleven being eight hundred and sixty-five less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and eighty days old, a hundred and eighty being eight hundred and sixty-five less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*Then I have said five wrong things in five days and about four of you let four of them stand. I am not going to be thanked for being the one who said them out loud either, and I have noticed this week that the thing I am good at has started to look like the same thing as knowing.*”

“*Three hundred and eleven days in this county, and that is eight hundred and sixty-five less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across this palm is a hundred and eighty days old, and that is eight hundred and sixty-five less six hundred and eighty-five. It is a hundred and eighty days today and nobody has said whether it will keep.*”

“*And the standing offer asked at the seven-hundredth is a hundred and sixty-five days old, and a hundred and sixty-five is eight hundred and sixty-five less seven hundred. It is unanswered, and it will be a hundred and sixty-six in the morning and I will say that figure too.*”

Then a man of fifty-four who cannot read asked for a day against the ninth line for the hundred and forty-fourth morning, and said what he thought of the twenty-fifth time, and a woman of forty-nine entered today’s line.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four, holds the gravel on that landing, and cannot read. He has asked for a day against the ninth line for a hundred and forty-four mornings, which is eight hundred and sixty-five less seven hundred and twenty-two, and then one.

“*A hundred and forty-four, and I have said the figure for two days running now and I have not said anything about it either morning, and about four of you have probably decided that I am going to stop.*”

“*I am not going to stop. I am going to say it until one of you gives me a day or until there is no longer a me to say it to, and those are two different ends and I know which one I am nearer.*”

“*And the twenty-fifth tomorrow. I have no figure for that and I am not going to get one. A man of forty-three has given a job to a man of thirty-eight and I could have had that job and I would not have been able to do the second half of it.*”

“*I cannot read and I cannot count ninety steps that are not in front of me. That is the whole of what I have and about four of you have been letting me get away with saying it slower than I do.*”

“*That is a man of forty-nine’s eighth morning on that run and she gave the figure of it out loud and I am not going to argue with a figure of mornings, because a figure of mornings is the only kind that has ever come out right on this landing.*”

At about the tenth hour a man of about fifty-two said what a figure a person has never been shown is worth, and a boy of thirteen gave a figure of seven days in a lane, and nobody asked him for the count he is keeping.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well, and named a fourteenth thing he cannot check on the third day of this week. It is that a leaf and a day are the same number, and he named it on purpose on the day he said he would.

“*A man of thirty-eight asked me on the Monday of the hundred and forty-second week whether a day he had worked out in his own head was right, and I checked it, and it was right, and he has not asked me anything since, and he came up that cut on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-third week, and I did not ask him why.*”

“*And tomorrow a man of thirty-eight is going to stand at the top of that cut and count about ninety steps with a wet foot, and a man of forty-three is going to say a figure after him, and that is the first thing on this landing that about nine of you could go and look at.*”

“*And I have got fourteen things I cannot check and not one of them is that. That is not a small thing to say on a Sunday and I am saying it because a boy of thirteen has been holding an opposite since Thursday and he deserves to have somebody else say something true about a figure today.*”

Wat Marshe is thirteen, has been in this county two hundred and twelve days, and counted the figure in the lane before the light came up. Two hundred and twelve is eight hundred and sixty-five less six hundred and fifty-three.

“*Two hundred and twelve days, and it is one more than the morning before, and I counted it in the lane before the light came up.*”

“*And the last time there was no water on those steps was the Sunday of the hundred and forty-fourth week, and that is eight hundred and sixty-five less eight hundred and fifty-eight, which is seven, and it is seven days for me and it is not seven days for anybody else, and I am not going to give anybody my seven.*”

“*Nobody has asked me for the count I am keeping. That is four mornings now and about four of you have worked out that I am keeping something and not one of you has asked, and I would like that recorded as a fact about this landing and not as a thing about me.*”

About nine of them were on that landing at about the eleventh hour of a Sunday, one day of the coming back, with a job given to a man who was not asked whether he wanted it, and five wrong things said out loud by a man of thirty-one in five days.

A full bank held over the top step through a Sunday and there was one day of the coming back, with about sixty of those ninety steps under.
The man of forty-three said that tomorrow at about the sixth hour about ninety steps can be walked, that this is the twenty-fifth time, and that on the twenty-fifth he is not giving the fourth thing, which is the first time in nineteen years he has said that about an off-morning.

He gave a man of thirty-eight the job of counting the dry steps at the top of the cut, and told him he did not want it counted right. The figure has been a figure of a man of forty-three for nineteen years and there is no way on that landing for it to be a figure of a landing, and if the two figures disagree on the morning then one of them is wrong and it will not be the man of thirty-eight.

A man of thirty-one said he could count them better than a man of thirty-eight with a board in his coat, and was told that this was the first wrong thing about a figure said on that landing in four days and that it had been said about arithmetic. Four of his five wrong things in those five days were not about figures. This one was.

A man of fifty-four asked for a day for the hundred and forty-fourth morning and said he is not going to stop, and said he could not have done the second half of a job with ninety steps in it. A man of about fifty-two said not one of his fourteen things he cannot check is a figure of times, and that a figure a person has never been shown is worth less than nothing.
A boy of thirteen has been keeping a count for four mornings, and nobody on that landing has asked him for it, and he has told them all that he is keeping one.
