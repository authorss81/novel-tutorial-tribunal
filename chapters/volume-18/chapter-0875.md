# Chapter 875: Second Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Seventh Week, And A Wednesday, And Four Inches On The Top Step With Seven Days Of The Coming Back And About Ninety Of Those Ninety Steps Under, And A Woman Of Thirty-Three Says There Are Two Figures Of Inches On That Landing And About Four Of You Are Going To Add Them, And A Man Of Thirty-One Says A Run With Nothing Wrong With It Is Not A Record And Is Told The Only Difference Is That Nobody Four Hundred Miles Off Will Ask To See It

Second day of the hundred and forty-seventh week. His six hundred and twenty-fifth morning. Four hundred and seventy-five days after the settlement. The fever eighty-four weeks and four days old. Four hundred and twenty-nine days since the division. Three hundred and seventy-five days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

Four inches stood on the top step at about the seventh hour and it was seven days of the coming back. About ninety of those ninety steps were under, which is all ninety of them. The man of forty-three gave that figure twice and said why he was giving it twice.

Corvin Tace is forty-three, has held that landing nineteen years, and gives four figures every morning. The fourth is a figure of steps, and this morning it came to the whole of the ninety, which has happened once before this year and not twice.

“*Four at the seventh hour, seven days of the coming back, and about ninety of those ninety steps under.*”

“*I am saying it again because it is all ninety and about four of you are going to write ninety and stop there. It is about ninety of ninety. I could not tell you where the last one stops being a step and neither could a man of thirty-eight who counted them on the seventh and last day of the hundred and forty-fifth week and got about ninety and said the same thing.*”

“*That is the first time this year it has been all ninety and it is not a good morning and it is not a bad morning. It is four inches a day and the four inches have not moved. I am not going to stand on that stone this morning and make anything of it, and about four of you were waiting for me to.*”

Then a woman of thirty-three gave her own figure and named the collision before about nine of your ears made it.

Nessa Toomy is thirty-three, keeps a run of days for a drain, and her run is a hundred and fifty-eight days long with the anchor being a step that went in flat.

“*Two inches over my step. That is my figure for this morning and it is inches of water over a step, and that is the same kind of figure a man of forty-three has just given you about his.*”

“*And I am going to say the rest of it before any of you do. There are two figures of inches on this landing this morning. One is a cut with ninety steps in it and one is a drain with a stone step at the head of it. About four of you have already put them together and got a figure of inches for the landing, and there is no figure of inches for the landing.*”

“*Mine is over a step and his is over the top step of a cut. They are the same words. They are not the same fact. I have said that once this week and I am saying it twice because a Wednesday is when about four of you decide they have understood it.*”

Then a man of thirty-eight asked whether the four of them could check each other, and was refused by a man of forty-seven, in a shape nobody had used yet.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight. A board with thirty-two notches cut in the edge of it rides in the inside of his coat, and he has a child of nine, and neither of those figures is a figure of the man.

“*Then let us use them. Four people, four runs, and each one of them looks at the other three on a morning and says the thing was done or it was not. That is a check. That is four checks instead of none, and about nine of you would call that progress in about a week and I would rather it was done in one morning.*”

Rod Veal is forty-seven and keeps a run of days for the low gate, and his run is two hundred and six days long, and the anchor is the first evening he shut that gate himself.

“*No, and I want about nine of you to hear the shape of the no, because it is not the same no I gave on the Sunday of the hundred and forty-sixth week.*”

“*A gate shut by me is not a gate shut by a man who keeps a boat. If I stood on that bank on Friday morning and shut the low gate, and then on Saturday a man who keeps a boat came and looked at it, and shut it again because it was open, and then wrote a line saying it was shut on Saturday, then the line on Saturday is his line and not mine, and my run has got a Saturday in it that I did not do.*”

“*And that is the whole of what is wrong with the idea and it is not a small objection. A line somebody entered that day is the only evidence that the day was done. If the day was done by somebody else then it is a different day’s work and it does not go on my run, and there is no form in this county that asks who.*”

“*About four of you stood and watched me shut that gate on the Monday of the hundred and forty-seventh week. About four of you watched me shut it again on the Thursday of the hundred and forty-sixth week. About four of you have been watching for nineteen years and about four of you watching is not a thing that happens to the gate.*”

“*And I will say the other half so that nobody has to work it out. About four of you who will speak is not consent. A figure of about nine of you is a figure of a bank and not a consent either. There is a man of about fifty-two forty yards from this stone who can put a line under six of the nine and not under three of them, and he has said that sentence four times this fortnight, and the four of you are about to do the arithmetic he says about four of them do in the last minute.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said that a run with nothing wrong with it at all was not a record, and told a man of forty-seven why he wanted it to be true, and was given the difference.

Ilyan Vester, thirty-one, and is nobody’s, and has been in this county three hundred and twenty-one days, three hundred and twenty-one being eight hundred and seventy-five less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and ninety days old, a hundred and ninety being eight hundred and seventy-five less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*A gate is shut whether or not anybody looks. A boat crosses whether or not anybody looks. There is no index, no stone, no charter, and nobody four hundred miles off has ever heard of any of it.*”

“*So none of those four are records. They are habits with leaves in them. And if a run with nothing wrong with it at all is not a record, then a run with something wrong with it at the front of it is not a record either, and that is the cheapest sentence anybody has said on this landing in three weeks.*”

Rod Veal said no, and said the difference, and about nine of your ears heard it.

“*It is a record.*”

“*The only difference between my two hundred and six and the ninth run is that nobody is going to come and ask to see mine. That is all. There is no stone and there is no page and there is no man four hundred miles off who wants my figures, and that does not make them not figures, it makes them figures nobody has asked for yet.*”

“*And you want it to be the other way, because if a gate run is not a record then a hand-bell run with the front of it burnt is not a record, and then a woman of twenty-nine in a reed is keeping something that is not a record, and then about four of you could put three things that are lost down and not lose anything at all. I have watched your face for a week. That is what you were doing. That is the fifteenth wrong thing and you have not had the decency to call it one.*”

Ilyan Vester said that it was, and said the working, and did not soften it.

“*It is. And here is the working and I am going to say it in about nine of your ears because if I only say it to you then in about four weeks one of you will have heard it.*”

“*I wanted a cheap sentence. I have been in this county three hundred and twenty-one days and the standing offer asked at the seven-hundredth is a hundred and seventy-five days old and unanswered, and it is eight hundred and seventy-five less seven hundred.*”

“*And the cut across this palm is a hundred and ninety days old and that is eight hundred and seventy-five less six hundred and eighty-five and nobody has said whether it will keep.*”

“*And if a run with nothing wrong with it is not a record then the three that have lost the front of them are not records, and then the whole of what I have been saying since the Wednesday of the hundred and forty-fifth week was a man talking about leaves. That is the working. It is cheap and it is mine.*”

Wat Marshe came up the lane before the light came and gave his figure, and said what he had counted on the off-morning and what he had not counted.

Wat Marshe is thirteen, has been in this county two hundred and twenty-two days, and counted the figure in the lane before the light came up. Two hundred and twenty-two is eight hundred and seventy-five less six hundred and fifty-three.

“*Two hundred and twenty-two days, and it is one more than the morning before, and I counted it in the lane before the light came up.*”

“*Nobody has asked me for the count I am keeping. That is fourteen mornings. And on the off-morning a man of forty-three had no fourth figure and about four of you felt bad for him, and a man of thirty-one had no fourth figure and not one of you felt anything for him, and neither of those is a fact about either of them and both of them are a fact about you.*”

“*That is a fact about the count. On the off-morning a man has a fourth figure or he has not got one and the count goes on either way, and that is the whole of why I have kept it, and I am still not going to say what it is.*”

Sena Dorr entered today’s line on the ninth run at about the tenth hour and read it out herself.

Sena Dorr is forty-nine and holds the ninth of the nine positions. There is no stone at it and there never has been. Twenty-three years of one line a day is what stands in the place of a stone.

“*That is the eighteenth morning in front of anybody. It is a figure of mornings. It is entered on an off-morning too, and it is entered at about the tenth hour and not at seven, because on an off-morning I get to it later and I am not going to pretend to seven.*”

“*And a man of thirty-one has just said out loud in about nine of your ears that he wanted a cheap sentence. I have been in a room four hundred miles off with nine clerks in it for twenty-three years and I have watched about nine people a day want a cheap sentence for nineteen years, and I have never once heard a man say it out loud and put the working beside it before.*”

Barnaby Crove asked for a day against the ninth line for the hundred and fifty-fourth morning, and said he still did not have the day he had promised to name.

“*A hundred and fifty-four, and I told a woman of thirty-three on the seventh and last day of the hundred and forty-sixth week that I would name a day and write a line on her run and I have not got the day.*”

“*And I am not going to tell her that I cannot get it in a way that makes her feel she has refused me, because she did not refuse me and about four of you have already told her she did.*”

“*A day is a thing that happened. I am a man who holds gravel. I have not found the day. I will ask again in the morning and I will not be thanked by her or by anybody else.*”

About nine of them were on that landing at about the eleventh hour of a Wednesday with about ninety of those ninety steps under, and a man of forty-three who had said four inches a day and refused to make anything of it.

---

Four inches on the top step, seven days of the coming back, and about ninety of those ninety steps under, all ninety of them, which is the first time this year. The man of forty-three said it twice, because about four of you were going to write ninety and stop there, and said it was not a good morning and not a bad one.

A woman of thirty-three gave her own figure that morning, two inches over a stone step at the head of a drain, and said there are two figures of inches on that landing and about four of you have already added them. They are the same words and not the same fact.

A man of thirty-eight asked whether the four ordinary runs could check each other, and a man of forty-seven refused, and gave the shape of the refusal: a line somebody entered that day is the only evidence that the day was done, and a day done by somebody else on somebody else’s run does not go on that run.

A man of thirty-one said that a run with nothing wrong with it at all is not a record, and was told it is a record, and that the only difference is that nobody four hundred miles off will ever ask to see it. He said out loud that he wanted the cheap sentence and gave the working.

And a woman of thirty-three entered this morning’s line on her drain at about the eighth hour instead of about the seventh, which is the only figure she has ever given late, and she gave no reason for it, and nobody on that bank asked her for one.
