# Chapter 854: Second Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Fourth Week, And A Wednesday, And A Full Bank At Sixteen Inches With Four Days Of The Coming Back, And A Wedge Of Leaves Is Opened On The Dry Stone And Today’s Line Is Entered On It In About Nine Ears, And A Man Of Thirty-One Says He Wants It On The Long Table And Is Given The Working Of Why A Description Outgrows A Wedge

Second day of the hundred and forty-fourth week. His six hundred and fourth morning. Four hundred and fifty-four days after the settlement. The fever eighty-one weeks and four days old. Four hundred and eight days since the division. Three hundred and fifty-four days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank was about sixteen inches over the top step at about the seventh hour, and it was four days of the coming back, and that is a full bank. The man of forty-three gave both figures. He said a full bank holds at sixteen until it comes off, and that he had said that on the second day of this week and would say it again on the eighth.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He gives four things every morning now, the fourth of them a figure of how many of those ninety steps are under, and about sixty of them are under this morning, and it is the first morning of a full bank.

“*About sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour, and it is four days of the coming back, and that is a full bank.*”

“*And about sixty of those ninety steps are under. Four inches a day is the working underneath that figure, and I have given you the working and the figure and the next time, and that is four, and I am not going to apologise for the four.*”

“*A full bank holds at sixteen until it comes off. I said that on the second day of this week and I am going to say it on the eighth, and about four of you can check whether I said it.*”

At about the ninth hour the wedge was opened on the dry stone at the top of that landing. About nine of them stood round it, and a woman of thirty-six put today’s line on it in front of them, and said the working out loud while she did it.

Hessa Vinch is thirty-six, rang a hand-bell at the top of a valley in the Briar March for nineteen years, and holds the seventh of the nine positions. The run is a bound sequence of day-leaves, a leaf for a day, one line on the leaf and nothing else. The front of it is gone and what is left is a wedge.

“*I am going to write today on it, in about nine of your ears, and I am going to write it on the last leaf there is. There is not much of that leaf, and the line is going to be a bad line because there is nowhere to put a good one.*”

“*That is the whole of the practice and there is no form in it. You do not ask anybody, and nobody asks me, and the line goes on the leaf for the day, and that is the day.*”

“*I am not going to say it out loud this morning as a thing anybody may rely on, because about four of you would take it and put it in a book by the Thursday. What I am doing is entering a day. That is a smaller thing than a thing anybody may rely on, and it is the only one I have.*”

Then a man of about fifty-two read the line on the last leaf of that wedge out loud. He said which parts of it he could put a line under and which parts he could not, gave the figure of what he cannot check, and it is thirteen.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two, cannot see well, has a palm and a forearm, and named a thirteenth thing he cannot check on the Thursday of last week, which is a figure of a woman. He said on that morning that it was the last one he was going to name before the end of the fortnight, and a fortnight from that Thursday is the Thursday of the hundred and forty-fifth week, and that is eight days off.

“*I can put a line under the fact that a leaf is gone. I can put one under the scorch on that hearthstone being the length of her forearm. I can put a line under a leaf with a line on it and a day on it.*”

“*And I cannot put a line under how many leaves are gone, and I am not going to try, and a man of thirty-one told me the shape of it this morning and the shape is not a figure.*”

“*That is the thirteenth thing I cannot check. It was named on the Thursday of last week, it is a figure of a woman, and I am not going to say which woman. I am also not going to pretend that thirteen is a better figure than twelve.*”

“*I have eight days of my own fortnight left and I am not going to name a fourteenth. If somebody here has been waiting for one to finish an argument, they can wait eight more days and then argue without it.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the want he had, in about nine ears. A woman of forty who keeps a stone said the first half of the working, and a man of about fifty-two said the rest of it, and neither of them was asked to.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county three hundred days, three hundred being eight hundred and fifty-four less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and sixty-nine days old, a hundred and sixty-nine being eight hundred and fifty-four less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*I want it on the long table in that hall. Under the charters. Where the seats are and the stones are, with a leaf from it open, and I want a man of sixty-one to write down that it was there.*”

Wenna Callow is forty, keeps the first of the six stones at the low end of that lane, and has asked for a day against a name for eleven years. On the first day of this week she said a seat is not a day. She has never published anything in her life.

“*I have kept a stone for eleven years and I have never had it on a table, and I am not asking for it to be put there. That is the whole of what I know about tables and I am not going to give you a working from a thing I have not done.*”

“*What I will say is that the first three of those four lines are what I have been doing for eleven years, with a barrow and my own back. A table is not the fourth one. The fourth one is that a term has to be published before it is a term, and nobody has told me where that goes.*”

Simon Rook, about fifty-two, gave the rest of the working out loud, and said he had been wrong about a table once and had learned it expensively and would not repeat it.

“*I put a thing on a table in about nine ears, in a year I am not going to name, and I was right about it for about nine days.*”

“*And then about four of you had described it to somebody who was not in the room, and the description was longer than the thing, and the thing was on a table the whole time.*”

“*You can lay the wedge out. I am not going to stand in front of about nine of you and stop a man of thirty-one laying a wedge out. What I am telling you is what happens in about nine days, and I have watched it happen to a thing that was not even damaged.*”

“*And a description outgrows a wedge, because a wedge is a shape and a description is a sentence, and a sentence is much easier to carry than a shape.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said that the seventh run goes back nineteen years. A woman of thirty-six told him he had taken a figure of her ringing and turned it into a figure of a book, he took it back, and the third want of the week went on the ground beside the other two.

Hessa Vinch, thirty-six, said it once and said she was not going to say it twice. A man who argues with her about a figure of her own life is arguing about the wrong thing.

“*Nineteen years is a figure of me ringing a bell, and I have been in front of about four of you saying it since the first day of this week. You have taken it and made it a figure of a book, and it is not one.*”

“*A book with a hole in the front of it is not described by a figure of years at all. You cannot add years to a hole. You cannot date a hole. The hole is where the leaves are not, and the leaves that would have told you anything are not there, and there is nobody alive who can tell you about them.*”

Ilyan Vester, thirty-one, took it back. He said he had three wants on the ground in about nine ears in about four days, and that three is a figure of wants and not of days, and he gave his own two counters with the subtraction in them.

“*Then it is gone. I have said three things I want on this landing in about four days and about four of you have heard all three, and a man of fifty-four heard two of them and has not stopped saying so.*”

“*Three is a figure of wants, and it is not a figure of days. I am not going to let about nine of you read it as a tally of my patience.*”

“*Three hundred days in this county, and that is eight hundred and fifty-four less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across this palm is a hundred and sixty-nine days old, and that is eight hundred and fifty-four less six hundred and eighty-five.*”

“*And I am nobody’s, and I am going to be nobody’s for a while yet, and a man of about fifty-two has just told me in about nine of your ears that a description outgrows a wedge. I have heard that sentence about four times in about nine days about different things, and I have never once been the man who heard it and agreed.*”

About nine of them were on that landing at about the tenth hour of a Wednesday with a full bank over the top step. A woman of thirty-six had entered today’s line on the last leaf of a wedge, and a man of about fifty-two had a figure of thirteen and four days of his own fortnight left in him.

---

The fever was eighty-one weeks and four days old. He had been in that county three hundred days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and sixty-nine days old, and a hundred and fifty-four days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

The bank was about sixteen inches over the top step with four days of the coming back, which is a full bank, and a full bank holds at sixteen until it comes off. About sixty of the ninety steps were under. The man of forty-three gave the working, the figure and the next time. He said he had given the holding rule on the second day of this week and would give it again on the eighth, and invited about four of them to check.

A woman of thirty-six opened the wedge on the dry stone and entered today’s line on the last leaf there is, in about nine ears, and said the working out loud while she did it. There is no form in it. Nobody asks her and she asks nobody, and the line goes on the leaf for the day, and that is the day.
And she said she was not going to call it a thing anybody may rely on, because about four of them would have it in a book by the Thursday. Entering a day is a smaller thing than that.

A man of about fifty-two can put a line under a leaf being gone and cannot put a line under how many leaves are gone. He named his thirteenth thing on the Thursday of last week, it is a figure of a woman, and he has eight days of his own fortnight left and will not name a fourteenth.

And a man of thirty-one said he wanted that wedge on the long table under the charters. A woman of forty who keeps a stone said she was not going to give a working from a thing she had never done. She told him instead that the fourth line of her own charter is still not published and nobody has told her where it goes. A man of about fifty-two gave the rest of the working: a description outgrows a wedge, because a wedge is a shape and a description is a sentence, and a sentence is much easier to carry.

And a man of thirty-one said the seventh run goes back nineteen years, and a woman of thirty-six told him he had taken a figure of her ringing and made it a figure of a book. You cannot add years to a hole and you cannot date a hole, and the leaves that would have told you anything are not there.
He took it back, and said that three wants had gone on the ground in about four days in about nine ears, and that three is a figure of wants and not a figure of days.
