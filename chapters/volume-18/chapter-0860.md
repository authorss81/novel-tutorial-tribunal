# Chapter 860: First Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Fifth Week, And A Tuesday, And About Eight Inches Over The Top Step With Six Days Of The Coming Back And About Eighty Of The Ninety Steps Under, And A Man Of Fifty-Four Stops Asking For A Day And Asks For Something Smaller That Is Available, And A Man Of Thirty-One Says It Is Not A Record And Is Told By The Same Man Of Fifty-Four That He Is Right, And Three Lines Go On Three Leaves

First day of the hundred and forty-fifth week. His six hundred and tenth morning. Four hundred and sixty days after the settlement. The fever eighty-two weeks and three days old. Four hundred and fourteen days since the division. Three hundred and sixty days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

About eight inches stood over the top step at about the seventh hour and it was six days of the coming back. The man of forty-three gave the figure of steps and then the inches, and said he had put the order back the way it was because about four of them had got used to it.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and gives four things every morning. The fourth is a figure of how many of those ninety steps are under, and about eighty of them are under this morning.

“*About eight inches over the top step at the seventh hour, and it is six days of the coming back, and about eighty of those ninety steps are under.*”

“*And I have put the inches back in front of the figure this morning, and that is not a correction. About four of you had got used to the other way, and a thing you have got used to is not a thing I want to hand about nine of you.*”

“*And four days ago I gave the figure before the inches, and on Tuesday I gave it before the inches, and on this morning I put the inches back in front of it, and the figure has not once been different. That is a figure of a working and not of a man.*”

At about the ninth hour a man of fifty-four asked for a day against the ninth line for the hundred and thirty-ninth morning, and then asked for something else. He said the second thing was smaller and available, and about nine of them heard both.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four, holds the gravel on that landing, cannot read, and has asked for a day against the ninth line for a hundred and thirty-nine mornings, which is eight hundred and sixty less seven hundred and twenty-two, and then one.

“*A hundred and thirty-nine, and I have asked it every morning of this week and six mornings of the week before, and I am going to keep asking it, and I am not going to be at you about it.*”

“*And here is the second thing, and it is smaller than the first, and I have been standing on this landing for about four days working out that it was smaller.*”

“*I am not asking for a day on the ninth line this morning. I am asking for the index of the ninth line to be read out, in about nine of your ears, by anybody who has it, in any shape at all.*”

“*A day I cannot get. There is a woman of forty-nine on this landing who has told me for four days that it cannot be got by anybody standing here, and I am not going to keep asking a person for a thing they have told me the shape of.*”

Then a woman of forty-nine said the smaller ask was available, said who could do it, and said about four of them had been able to do it for four days. She gave the working of why the day was not available and the index was.

Sena Dorr is forty-nine, has read for a living for twenty-three years in a room four hundred miles off with nine clerks in it, and keeps the ninth of the nine positions, which is a public archive and not a stone.

“*The index can be read out. A woman of sixty-four heard it read out on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-third week in about nine of your ears, and it is the reason a man of thirty-eight asked the question he asked. I am saying that to about four of you so that nobody tells him this morning he is the first to want it.*”

“*A day cannot be got, and here is the whole of the working and it is four days long and I have been saying it in pieces. I do not hold the ninth line. I keep the ninth run. Those are two books and about four of you have had them in the wrong order since the third day of this week, and so have I, and the difference between them is the whole of the answer.*”

“*And the name in ink on the index of the ninth line is a woman of sixty-four, and she has never held that seat, and she has refused about nine people in nineteen years. A day she wrote against her own name in a different place is not a day on the ninth line. That is not a refusal. That is a shape, and a shape does not refuse anybody at all.*”

“*So the man of fifty-four is right to ask for the index, and the man of thirty-eight is right to have asked about the three, and neither of them has been given a day, and I am not going to improve that by one word.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said he would write the smaller ask on a leaf, and a man of fifty-four said no and gave the working, then said he would do it himself in marks. A boy of thirteen was asked to read it out, and read it out.

“*I will write it. On a leaf, in the book, with the day on it, and I will read it out to about nine of you before and after, and then it is on a leaf in a book and it has a day on it.*”

“*No, and I am not saying no because of Friday. I am saying no because of what I said to a woman of forty-four about a boy of thirteen, and a thing I said to somebody else on a Friday is a thing I have to live by on a Tuesday.*”

“*You write it, and in about four weeks the name anybody puts on that leaf is yours, and there is a man of thirty-one on a landing who put a thing in a book, and a man of thirty-one on a landing is a thing four hundred miles off can hear about and use.*”

“*I will do it in marks. Four strokes and a figure, the same as this morning on the leaf for the gravel, and a boy of thirteen will read it out. That is the whole of the arrangement, and there is nothing in it but two people and a leaf.*”

“*And I am not going to call it a record, and neither is anyone on this landing, and about nine of you are not going to be thanked for it this morning either.*”

Ilyan Vester, thirty-one, and is nobody’s, and has been in this county three hundred and six days, three hundred and six being eight hundred and sixty less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and seventy-five days old, a hundred and seventy-five being eight hundred and sixty less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*Then it is not a record. It is two people and a leaf, and about four of you have been calling things records all week, and I am going to say the harder thing, which is that a record does not have to be a book to be one.*”

“*And I am not going to say that on this landing this morning. A man of fifty-four has just told me it is not a record, and he is the man who cannot read, and about nine of you heard him say it before me.*”

“*Three hundred and six days in this county, and that is eight hundred and sixty less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across this palm is a hundred and seventy-five days old, and that is eight hundred and sixty less six hundred and eighty-five.*”

“*And I have been refused four times in about four days in about nine of your ears, and I am not going to be told by anybody that that is patience. A man of fifty-four has just used a thing I said on the Wednesday of last week against a woman of forty-four, and I am glad he did.*”

“*The standing offer asked at the seven-hundredth is a hundred and sixty days old, and a hundred and sixty is eight hundred and sixty less seven hundred. It is unanswered, and I am going to go on saying that figure out loud and not going to ask it.*”

Wat Marshe is thirteen, has been in this county two hundred and seven days, and counted the figure in the lane before the light came up. Two hundred and six is eight hundred and sixty less six hundred and fifty-three.

“*Two hundred and seven days, and it is one more than the morning before, and I counted it in the lane before the light came up.*”

“*And I read it out. Four strokes and a figure, and a figure is a figure and not a letter, and I am not going to pretend to him that I read a sentence, because it is not a sentence and he would not have wanted it to be.*”

“*I said on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-third week that a sentence and a question are not the same shape. This morning a man of fifty-four wrote a mark, and that is a third shape, and I do not think anybody on this landing has got a word for the third one yet.*”

“*And nobody is going to be thanked for it. I have said that on about nine mornings of this week, and I am saying it again, because it is the only part of it that is certain.*”

About nine of them were on that landing at about the eleventh hour of a Tuesday, three lines had gone on three leaves that morning, and a fourth book four hundred miles off had a leaf for today and nothing on it.

A man of fifty-four asked for a day against the ninth line for the hundred and thirty-ninth morning, and then asked for the index of that line to be read out in about nine of your ears by anybody who has it. He said the second thing was smaller, and available.

And a woman of forty-nine said the index can be read out, and that a woman of sixty-four heard it read out on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-third week, and that nobody is to tell a man of thirty-eight this morning he was the first to want it. She said she does not hold the ninth line and keeps the ninth run, and that about four of them had them in the wrong order since Tuesday.

And she said a day cannot be got, and that the name in ink on the index of the ninth line is a woman of sixty-four who has never held that seat, and that a day she wrote against her own name in a different place is not a day on that line. That is a shape, and a shape does not refuse anybody.

And a man of thirty-one offered to write the smaller ask on a leaf, and a man of fifty-four said no, and gave the working by way of what he had said to a woman of forty-four about a boy of thirteen. A thing he said to somebody else on a Friday is a thing he has to live by on a Tuesday. He will do it in marks, and that is the whole of it.

A boy of thirteen read it out and said a figure is a figure and not a letter, and that he was not going to pretend to him that he read a sentence. A sentence and a question are not the same shape, and a mark is a third shape nobody on that landing has a word for yet.
