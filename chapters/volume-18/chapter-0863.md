# Chapter 863: Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Fifth Week, And A Friday, And Sixteen Inches Holding With Three Days Of The Coming Back And About Sixty Of Those Steps Under, And Three Figures Go Down Side By Side Again On The Dry Stone And A Man Of Thirty-One Says They Are The Front Of One Book And Is Given The Working By A Woman Of Forty-Nine And By A Boy Of Thirteen And By The Man Who Said On The Friday Of Last Week That There Is No Sum Of Them

Fourth day of the hundred and forty-fifth week. His six hundred and thirteenth morning. Four hundred and sixty-three days after the settlement. The fever eighty-two weeks and six days old. Four hundred and seventeen days since the division. Three hundred and sixty-three days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

Sixteen inches on the top step at about the seventh hour and it was holding at that figure. It was three days of the coming back and about sixty of those ninety steps were under. The man of forty-three gave the inches and the figure and did not say which order he was using, and said that was on purpose.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He gives four things every morning and three of the four are about water.

“*Sixteen at the seventh hour, three days of the coming back, about sixty of those ninety steps under, and I am not telling you which of the two figures I said first because I have told you I am finished with the order and I am.*”

“*And a full bank holds at sixteen until it comes off. That is the working, and it has not moved in this county since the first day of this century, and about four of you find that boring and it is the only figure on this landing that has never been wrong.*”

Then three figures went down side by side on the dry stone at about the tenth hour, in the same three places they had gone on the Friday of last week, and about nine of them looked at all three.

“*A wedge of burnt leaves. That is the seventh and it is a woman of thirty-six and the anchor is a scorch on a hearthstone at the head of her valley.*”

“*Inches of water over a step. That is the eighth and it is a man of forty-four and the anchor is a sluice step at the head of his works, and about eleven inches of head on it this morning.*”

“*Holes in a stitch-edge and drawn thread and the stubs of leaves that are not there. That is the ninth and it is mine, and the anchor is the edge of my own hand on the front of the run, and I have had my hand on it every morning this week.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the three of them were the front of one book, and about nine of them heard him say it, and a woman of forty-nine gave him the working in three pieces.

“*Those three are the front of one book. That is what a book with a hole in it looks like from the outside, and there are three of those fronts on this stone and about four of you have been treating them as one thing since Wednesday.*”

Sena Dorr is forty-nine, has read for a living for twenty-three years in a room four hundred miles off with nine clerks in it, and keeps the ninth of the nine positions, which is a public archive and not a stone. On the second day of this week she entered today’s line on that run in front of about nine of them.

“*Three books. I will give you the working in three pieces and I will give it in about nine of your ears.*”

“*The seventh is a woman of thirty-six and it is in a satchel up a valley at the head of the Briar March, two days up and one day down if the water is out, and there is no road to it. She has not had it out of the satchel since the Tuesday she walked it down.*”

“*The eighth is a man of forty-four and it is at the head of the Red Basin works, a day’s walk with the water as it is and two days in a dry week. He has had it under eleven inches of water since the Tuesday and he says that is on purpose and gives the days and not the miles on purpose.*”

“*The ninth is mine and it is on this stone and I put my hand on it this morning before any of you were up. Three books, three holders, three fronts, and no figure of a thing that all three of those have lost the same part of.*”

“*And the front of one book is a fourth thing, and that fourth thing has nobody holding it. You have just made an owner up and about four of you will have written it down inside a week.*”

Ilyan Vester, thirty-one, and is nobody’s, and has been in this county three hundred and nine days, three hundred and nine being eight hundred and sixty-three less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and seventy-eight days old, a hundred and seventy-eight being eight hundred and sixty-three less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*Three books. Then on the Monday I said the ninth run and the ninth line were not the same book, and about four of you thought that was a small thing, and this morning I have made four books into three and called them one front, and it is the same mistake with a different number on it.*”

“*Three hundred and nine days in this county, and that is eight hundred and sixty-three less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across this palm is a hundred and seventy-eight days old, and that is eight hundred and sixty-three less six hundred and eighty-five, and nobody has said whether it will keep.*”

“*And the standing offer asked at the seven-hundredth is a hundred and sixty-three days old, and a hundred and sixty-three is eight hundred and sixty-three less seven hundred. It is unanswered, and this morning I tried to make one book out of three and a woman of forty-nine told me the working, and that is the third time in four days.*”

Then the man who had said there was no sum of them on the Friday of last week was asked whether the three had found a sum. He said they had not. He said why in a shape nobody had used that week.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well, and has a palm and a forearm, and named a fourteenth thing he cannot check yesterday.

“*No. And I said that on the Friday of last week with my hand flat on this stone, and that is seven days back, and eight hundred and sixty-three less eight hundred and fifty-six is seven, and I did not have to be asked to work that out because it is the same day of the week.*”

“*Six, three and four, and there is no sum of those three, and I said that on the Wednesday of the hundred and forty-second week as well, and that is three of these weeks and two days back, and I have not found a fourteenth thing that changes it and I am not looking for one.*”

“*And here is the part I have not said in about four days. A figure of days is a figure of days only if a person knows what a leaf is worth. I know what a leaf is worth on one of those three books. I do not know what a leaf is worth on the other two and neither does about nine of you.*”

“*So a figure of days cannot even be written on that stone, let alone added. About four of you have been waiting for me to produce a number there and there is no number there and there was never going to be one.*”

Then a boy of thirteen refused the three figures for the second time. He gave a different reason from the one he gave last time. He said the reason from last time was still good.

Wat Marshe is thirteen, has been in this county two hundred and ten days, and counted the figure in the lane before the light came up. Two hundred and ten is eight hundred and sixty-three less six hundred and fifty-three.

“*Two hundred and ten days, and it is one more than the morning before, and I counted it in the lane before the light came up.*”

“*I am not adding them again. The Thursday of last week I told you the reason and it was that a figure said out loud on a Thursday has got four hundred miles by the Saturday, and that reason is still good and I am not replacing a reason that still works.*”

“*Here is the other one and it is a different one. Three is a figure of figures and not a figure of anything on that stone, and I said that on the Friday of last week and I am saying it on a Friday this week, and that is the first thing I have said twice this week on the same day.*”

“*And I am not going to put a figure of leaves against a figure of inches against a figure of holes and call the column a week. One of those is burnt and one of those is wet and one of those was taken and used, and they are three conditions and not three lengths.*”

A woman of forty-four who keeps twenty-nine chairs came up the cut to the stone at about the eleventh hour. She said what a record is and is not, and gave the figure of a woman in a reed in her own mouth.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept twenty-nine chairs at thirteen shillings and fourpence a year for nineteen years. Eleven of those chairs have a name against them in her book and nobody in them, and on the Friday of the hundred and forty-third week she named the first of those two as dead and said there is no form that asks for a day.

“*A record is not a length. A record is a thing somebody writes on, and the only thing that makes a record a record is that somebody wrote on it, and about four of you have been treating three kinds of damage as though damage were a unit.*”

“*There is a woman of twenty-nine in a reed a hundred yards along the edge of that drain and she has been in it two hundred and thirty-four days, which is eight hundred and sixty-three less six hundred and twenty-nine. I have got that figure off a woman of forty-nine and I am not going to pretend I worked it out.*”

“*And she is not standing on this stone and she has not been asked for a figure and she is not one of the three of you. A figure of about four of them who are going to speak is not consent, and I am not putting her name near this stone this morning.*”

At about the eleventh hour of that Friday three figures were on a dry stone, with a fourth thing claimed to have been found in them.
A woman of forty-nine had given the working of why it could not be. A boy of thirteen had refused the column twice, on the same day of two different weeks.

---

Sixteen inches holding with three days of the coming back and about sixty of those steps under, and the man of forty-three said he was finished with the order the two figures came out in.

Three figures went down side by side on the dry stone. A wedge of burnt leaves, anchored to a scorch on a hearthstone. Inches of water over a step, anchored to a sluice step. Holes in a stitch-edge, anchored to one hand of a woman of forty-nine.
A man of thirty-one said they were the front of one book. She gave the working in three pieces. The seventh is two days up a valley with no road to it. The eighth is a day’s walk with the water as it is. The ninth is on that stone.
Three books, three holders, three fronts, and the front of one book is a fourth thing with nobody holding it.

The man who said on the Friday of last week that there was no sum of them was asked whether they had found one. He said they had not, said he had said it seven days back, and gave the part he had not said in four days: a figure of days is a figure of days only if a person knows what a leaf is worth, and he knows what a leaf is worth on one of those three books and not on the other two.

A boy of thirteen refused the column for the second time and gave a different reason, and said the reason from last week still worked. Three conditions are not three lengths. A woman of forty-four said a record is not a length. She gave the figure of a woman of twenty-nine in a reed in her own mouth, and said she was not putting that woman near the stone.
