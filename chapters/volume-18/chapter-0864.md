# Chapter 864: Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Fifth Week, And A Saturday, And Sixteen Inches Holding With Two Days Of The Coming Back And About Sixty Of Those Steps Under, And The Fever Is Eighty-Three Weeks And No Days And The Man Of Forty-Three Says It Came Out Whole Last Saturday And Does Not Give It A Number Of The Time, And A Woman Of Forty-Nine Says Out Loud The Same Sentence A Man Of Fifty-Four Said Last Saturday And Says It Is Not The Same Act

Fifth day of the hundred and forty-fifth week. His six hundred and fourteenth morning. Four hundred and sixty-four days after the settlement. The fever eighty-three weeks old. Four hundred and eighteen days since the division. Three hundred and sixty-four days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

Sixteen inches on the top step at about the seventh hour, two days of the coming back, and about sixty of those ninety steps under. The man of forty-three gave the two figures and then gave a third one that is not about the water, and said what it was a figure of.

Corvin Tace is forty-three, has kept that landing nineteen years, and gives four things every morning. The fourth is a figure of how many of those ninety steps are under, and it is the only one of the four he has ever refused to give on a Sunday.

“*Sixteen at the seventh hour, two days of the coming back, about sixty of those ninety steps under.*”

“*And there is a third figure on that landing this morning and it is not about the water. The fever came out to eighty-three weeks and no days this morning. Five hundred and eighty-one days, and five hundred and eighty-one is seven times eighty-three, and there is nothing left over.*”

“*It came out the same way on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-fourth week, and that is seven days back. That is the whole of what this figure is. There are six mornings out of seven where I could not say that and I have not said it, and this is the second of these Saturdays this fortnight and not the first of anything.*”

“*And I am not going to give it a number of the time. About four of you have been treating a whole number of weeks like a morning that something happens on. It is not one. It is a figure about the fever and about nothing else, and the water comes off about ninety steps at the sixth hour and that is the one that has a number.*”

Then a woman of forty-nine said out loud a sentence a man of fifty-four had said on the Saturday of last week, in her own words, and said it was the same sentence and not the same act.

Sena Dorr is forty-nine, has read for a living for twenty-three years in a room four hundred miles off with nine clerks in it, and keeps the ninth of the nine positions, which is a public archive and not a stone. On the second day of this week she entered today’s line on that run in front of about nine of them, and on the Saturday of last week a man of fifty-four said the ordinary form of it out loud and would not give it a name.

“*A run with a hole at the front of it is entered on today, and today’s line is not the hole.*”

“*And a position may be looked at from the day its record starts, and not from before the day its record starts. That is the whole of it and I am not going to add a thing to it in about nine of your ears.*”

“*A man of fifty-four who cannot read said this on the Saturday of last week and he said twice that it was not his, and he was right that it was not his. It is mine and I have said it every day for twenty-three years and I have never once said it out loud before that Saturday.*”

“*So it is the same sentence in the same words and it is not the same act, and I am going to give you the working of why, because about four of you have been treating one as two all week.*”

“*He said it and he does not do the work. I say it and I do the work. He was holding gravel when he said it and I put a line on a leaf in front of about nine of you with my own hand and read it out myself, and if the line is wrong then it is wrong in my hand and nobody has to guess whose it was.*”

“*That is the whole difference and it is not a smaller thing than what he said. It is the same sentence with a pen at the end of it.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the sentence was the same whoever said it, and a woman of forty-nine told him what he had done and what it cost to do it.

“*It is the same sentence whoever says it. A man who cannot read and a woman who keeps a run have arrived at one thing this week and about four of you have been waiting all week to hear that two people agreeing is the end of it.*”

“*No, and I am going to tell you what you have actually done, because you will do it again this afternoon if I do not.*”

“*You have made it a sentence. A sentence is a thing that can be said by anybody and be right, and about four of you have been carrying it round this landing all week as though it were about the land and not about a pen.*”

“*It is not about the land. It is about me putting a line on a leaf, and about him putting a hand on gravel, and about what a person can be shown afterwards. Those are two different instruments and you have put them in one bag.*”

Ilyan Vester, thirty-one, and is nobody’s, and has been in this county three hundred and ten days, three hundred and ten being eight hundred and sixty-four less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and seventy-nine days old, a hundred and seventy-nine being eight hundred and sixty-four less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*Then I have been carrying it round as a sentence since the Saturday of last week, and I have said so out loud on this landing twice, and about four of you let me.*”

“*Three hundred and ten days in this county, and that is eight hundred and sixty-four less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across this palm is a hundred and seventy-nine days old, and that is eight hundred and sixty-four less six hundred and eighty-five, and nobody has said whether it will keep.*”

“*And the standing offer asked at the seven-hundredth is a hundred and sixty-four days old, and a hundred and sixty-four is eight hundred and sixty-four less seven hundred. It is unanswered. I am not going to put a sentence beside it this morning because I have been told what a sentence is.*”

Then a woman of forty-nine entered today’s line on the ninth run and said the figure of mornings out loud, and a man of fifty-four who cannot read asked for his day, and a boy of thirteen asked a question and did not give the answer to it.

“*That is the seventh morning, in front of anybody. It is a figure of mornings and a figure of mornings only, and I said on the Thursday that it was not a figure of the front of my book, and today is a Saturday and about four of you would have liked me to say the front.*”

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four, holds the gravel on that landing, and cannot read. He has asked for a day against the ninth line for a hundred and forty-three mornings, which is eight hundred and sixty-four less seven hundred and twenty-two, and then one.

“*A hundred and forty-three. It is the second Saturday running I have asked it on and I have never noticed that until this morning.*”

“*And a woman of forty-nine has just said a thing on this landing that a woman of forty-four said to me on the Friday, and I am not going to join them together. One of them writes on a leaf. I move gravel. There is no sentence that puts those two in one bag and I am not going to look for one.*”

“*I want a day on the ninth line. That is the whole of what I have wanted for eleven years and I have not got a second thing.*”

At about the tenth hour a boy of thirteen asked a man of about fifty-two a question about the fourteenth thing, and did not give the answer to it in about nine of your ears.

Wat Marshe is thirteen, has been in this county two hundred and eleven days, and counted the figure in the lane before the light came up. Two hundred and eleven is eight hundred and sixty-four less six hundred and fifty-three. On the Thursday of this week he said he had got the opposite of the fourteenth and was not going to say it.

Simon Rook is about fifty-two and cannot see well, and named a fourteenth thing he cannot check on the third day of this week. It is that a leaf and a day are the same number.

“*Then can a person check it by standing in front of the hole. That is the only way I know of checking anything and I have been thinking about it since the second hour.*”

“*No, and I am glad somebody asked it, because about four of you have been not asking it since Thursday and a question asked in about nine of your ears is better than four of you not asking it.*”

“*There is nothing at the front of that book to stand in front of. That is the whole of what a hole is. You can put your hand where it used to be and you cannot put your eyes on something that has been taken and used by somebody who is dead.*”

“*And you have got an opposite and you are not going to say it, and you have given me a reason and the reason is that an opposite said out loud on a Saturday is four hundred miles by Tuesday, and that is the same reason you gave on the Thursday of last week about a figure of stitches, and it is the best reason anybody has given on this landing this fortnight.*”

“*Keep it. And do not give it to me on a Monday, because a Monday is the day a thing like that gets asked for again.*”

Wat Marshe, thirteen, said he would keep it and said he was not going to be thanked for it, and said nobody was going to be thanked for it.

“*I am going to keep it, and I am not going to give it to anybody on a Monday, and about four of you now know that I have got it, which is one more than I wanted and one less than I am going to get.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for any of it. I have said that on about nine mornings and this morning I have said something I have not said before, which is that I would like one of them to be. I would like a man of forty-three to be given a number of the time for a morning that is not an off-morning, and I am not going to ask him for it.*”

About nine of them were on that landing at about the eleventh hour of a Saturday with the fever eighty-three weeks and no days old, and a sentence said twice by two people who do not do the same work, and a boy of thirteen holding an opposite he has not given out loud.

Sixteen inches holding with two days of the coming back and about sixty of those steps under. The man of forty-three gave a third figure that was not about the water: the fever came out to eighty-three weeks and no days, five hundred and eighty-one days, five hundred and eighty-one being seven times eighty-three, and it came out the same way on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-fourth week, seven days back. He said there are six mornings out of seven where he could not say that, and that he is not going to give it a number of the time.

A woman of forty-nine said out loud, in about nine of your ears, the ordinary form of a run with a hole at the front of it.

A run with a hole at the front of it is entered on today, and the line for today is not the hole. A position may be looked at from the day its record starts and not from before it.

She said a man of fifty-four said the same words on the Saturday of last week and was right that it was not his. She said it is hers, and that she has said it every day for twenty-three years without saying it out loud.

Same sentence, not the same act. He was holding gravel. She put a line on a leaf in front of about nine of them with her own hand.

And a man of thirty-one said the sentence was the same whoever said it, and was told he had made it a sentence, and that a sentence can be said by anybody and be right, and that the sentence is not about the land. It is about a pen.

A man of fifty-four asked for a day for the hundred and forty-third morning and said he has not got a second thing he wants. A boy of thirteen asked whether the fourteenth can be checked by standing in front of the hole, was told no, and kept an opposite he has not given out loud, and said he would like one of them to be thanked and is not going to ask.

Nobody was thanked. The fever came out whole, a sentence was said twice by two people who do not do the same work, and a boy of thirteen went back down to the cut with something in his head he has decided not to give.
