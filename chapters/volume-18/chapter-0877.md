# Chapter 877: Fourth Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Seventh Week, And A Friday, And Twelve Inches On The Top Step With Five Days Of The Coming Back And About Seventy Of Those Ninety Steps Under, And A Woman Of Twenty-Eight Walks Four Miles Up That Cart Road And Says The Rest Of Why And Is Not Saying A Yes And Is Not Saying A No And Was Not Asked For Any Of It, And A Man Of Thirty-One Says The Ordinary Form Is The Answer And Is Given The Same Sentence Twice On Purpose

Fourth day of the hundred and forty-seventh week. His six hundred and twenty-seventh morning. Four hundred and seventy-seven days after the settlement. The fever eighty-four weeks and six days old. Four hundred and thirty-one days since the division. Three hundred and seventy-seven days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

Twelve inches stood on the top step at about the seventh hour and it was five days of the coming back. About seventy of those ninety steps were under. The man of forty-three gave the three figures and said that a Friday was the day the fourth thing got asked for again.

Corvin Tace is forty-three, has held that landing nineteen years, gives four figures off one stone every morning, and has been asked whether he will give a fifth thing on a Friday and has said that a man who gives a fifth thing on a Friday gives it on a Saturday.

“*Twelve at the seventh hour, five days of the coming back, and about seventy of those ninety steps under.*”

“*It will be a full bank on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-seventh week. Sixteen, a full bank, four days of the coming back, about sixty of those steps under. I am naming that morning now, on this Friday, with the day and the week on it, because a figure said out loud on a Friday has got four hundred miles by the Sunday and a boy of thirteen told me that on the Thursday of the hundred and forty-sixth week and he was right.*”

“*And no. There is no fifth thing. A man of forty-three has four things and they are water and steps, and about four of you have been hoping for eleven days that a Friday would make a fifth out of me.*”

Then a woman of twenty-eight walked four miles up that cart road on foot and came up the cut and stopped at the top of that bank, and said her own name, and said the rest of why.

Sera Quill is twenty-eight, and cannot be sent for in this county or in the one she came from, and has been in this county two hundred and thirty-five days, two hundred and thirty-five being eight hundred and seventy-seven less six hundred and forty-two. She said her own name on that bank on the first day of the hundred and thirty-ninth week. She keeps a run of days for a school four miles down that cart road, and that run is two hundred and twelve days long, and the anchor is the first morning she opened that door and wrote the line on the same evening.

“*My name is Sera Quill and I have walked four miles up that road because nobody sent for me and nobody could have. That is not a thing about this bank. It is a thing about how I get here and I want it said first before anything else.*”

“*I am not going to be asked for the rest of why and I have not been. I have been on that road for nine days with the rest of it in my mouth and about four of you would have asked me for it on the Wednesday of the hundred and forty-seventh week if I had come up, and I know that, because a man of thirty-one asked me a thing on the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-seventh week and told a woman of forty-four about it afterwards.*”

“*So here it is, and nobody asked, and I am going to give it in five pieces and about four of you will take the first one and stop listening and I have decided I do not mind.*”

“*One. I said no twice. The second time was a rule I made for myself and not a thing you did to me. You did not make it. I made it on a Wednesday about two years ago and I have not moved it and nobody is to move it this morning.*”

“*Two. The reason I said it twice is that I was going to be made into a fourth thing out of a fourth question. Not a first, not a second, not a third. A fourth, about a woman of twenty-nine who is not in this room and has said no five times in two books and does not need me added to her.*”

“*Three. A figure of about four of you who will speak is not consent. I am saying that in my own mouth and not in a woman of forty-four’s, and I am saying it a sixth time, and it is still not consent and it does not stop being true because it has been said six times. About four of you have been using that figure since the Wednesday of the hundred and twenty-seventh week as though a number were a way of asking. It is not. And about nine of you is a figure of a bank and not a consent at all.*”

“*Four. What I keep is a run of days for a school. Two hundred and twelve days and the anchor is the first morning I opened that door and wrote it on the same evening. One leaf for one day. One line a day saying what the morning was. It is not a stone. It is not on a page four hundred miles off. Nobody is going to read it and I have never once thought about the reading of it, because it is not the reading I am doing.*”

“*Five. And this is the one I have had in my mouth for nine days, and it is not a yes and it is not a no, and I am not settled and I am not going to be settled in a morning on a bank in front of about nine of you.*”

“*I am going to go back down that road this afternoon and enter today’s line about the school. Nobody is to come up that road to look at what I do. Nobody is to ask me on a Tuesday whether I came on a Friday. And if anybody wants to know what I think about the four runs they are talking about on that bank, they can come four miles on foot and walk one, and then it will be their figure and not mine.*”

Then a woman of forty-four said she had used that woman’s name once and once only, and why, and that nobody was to be thanked.

Orla Fennimore is forty-four and has kept twenty-nine chairs at thirteen shillings and fourpence a year for nineteen years, and her question has been put down four times and is not answered.

“*That is my second time saying that name in about nine of your ears and it is going to be the last this morning.*”

“*I said it on the Monday of the hundred and forty-sixth week because she gave it on that bank herself on the first day of the hundred and thirty-ninth week and it is hers to give. I did not say it because I wanted it said. And about four of you have spent a week waiting for me to use it to make a thing, and I am not going to, and that is not restraint. That is arithmetic. Every use of her name on this bank is a use of a woman who cannot be sent for and who has said no twice.*”

“*Nobody is going to be thanked for her coming up that road. She has said so herself, twice, and I am saying it a third time because about four of you need it said in more than one mouth.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said that the ordinary form was the answer to what a man of thirty-eight had asked, and was given the same sentence twice on purpose.

Ilyan Vester, thirty-one, and is nobody’s, and has been in this county three hundred and twenty-three days, three hundred and twenty-three being eight hundred and seventy-seven less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and ninety-two days old, a hundred and ninety-two being eight hundred and seventy-seven less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*The standing offer asked at the seven-hundredth is a hundred and seventy-seven days old, and a hundred and seventy-seven is eight hundred and seventy-seven less seven hundred. It is unanswered and I have not asked it and about four of you have just worked out that I say that every morning and very few of you can tell me what it is for.*”

“*A run with a hole in the front of it is still entered on today. A position may be checked from the day its record starts and not from before it. The day its record starts is a figure of a thing and it is owed to nobody.*”

“*That is the answer to his first half. He asked what three of the nine positions are and that is the answer, and about nine of you have heard me say it and about four of you have written it down as progress. Seventeen wrong things in seventeen mornings and this is one of them and I am saying so before anybody else does.*”

Sena Dorr is forty-nine, has read for a living for twenty-three years in a room four hundred miles off with nine clerks in it, keeps the ninth of the nine positions, which is a public archive and not a stone, and said a sentence about a page in about nine of your ears on the seventh and last day of the hundred and forty-fifth week.

“*No. And I am going to say it twice on purpose, because about four of you heard the first half this morning and stopped, and you have all done that before and about four of you are not going to be told so by a woman of forty-nine.*”

“*An index is a list of what a person can be shown. A list has got one thing it can say about a position, and the one thing is whether there is a thing to go and stand in front of. That is what a page is and that is the whole of what a page is.*”

“*And a run with a hole at the front of it is entered on today. I said that on the seventh and last day of the hundred and forty-fifth week and I am saying it now and it is the same sentence and it is not progress, and a man of thirty-one has taken a thing I said about a page and put it into a man of thirty-eight’s question and it has come out as an answer.*”

“*That is not it. And I am not going to be hard with you about it, because you have now done it three times in front of me and about four of you have watched it three times and about none of you has stopped it, and that is the part that ought to worry all of you and not me.*”

Garrin Tolley said that it was not the answer to his question and that he was going to keep asking, and said what he had done this week instead.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight, and the board with thirty-two notches cut in its edge is in the inside of his coat, and he has a child of nine.

“*It is not my answer. I have said my first half three times now and been given the same thing three times and that is what a record does, and I have had that on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-fourth week and it has not moved.*”

“*And what I have done instead is go and look at four ordinary things with my own feet this week, and a woman of thirty-eight — and a woman of twenty-nine in a reed who has not been here at all and whose name I have not used this morning because about four of you would have made a fifth of her.*”

Wat Marshe is thirteen, has been in this county two hundred and twenty-four days, and counted the figure in the lane before the light came up. Two hundred and twenty-four is eight hundred and seventy-seven less six hundred and fifty-three.

“*Two hundred and twenty-four days, and it is one more than the morning before, and I counted it in the lane before the light came up.*”

“*Nobody has asked me for the count I am keeping. That is sixteen mornings and a woman of twenty-eight has just told about nine of you a figure of about four of you who will speak is not consent, and I have known that since the Wednesday of the hundred and forty-fifth week and nobody has ever asked me how I know it.*”

“*I am still not going to say the count. And I want it on the page that she was not asked for any of it. About four of you would have had it by Wednesday if a man of thirty-one had asked her once.*”

Sena Dorr entered today’s line on the ninth run at about the tenth hour and gave the figure of mornings, and a man of fifty-four asked for the hundred and fifty-sixth.

“*That is the twentieth morning in front of anybody, and it is a figure of mornings, and I entered it this morning with a Friday’s line on it and about four of you were on that bank.*”

Barnaby Crove asked for a day against the ninth line for the hundred and fifty-sixth morning and said what a woman of twenty-eight had said that he was going to think about for a week.

“*A hundred and fifty-six. And a woman of twenty-eight has walked four miles up that road to say five things and not one of them was for me, and about four of you would have said she came for me.*”

“*Here is the one I am going to think about. She said nobody is to come up that road to look at what she does. And I have been asking a woman of forty-nine to look at what she does for eleven years, and I did not know I was doing it.*”

“*I am not going to stop. I am not going to say I have learned anything. But I am going to have to sit with it, and I am going to ask for my figure in the morning as usual, because if I stop asking then the eleven years stop and I am not ready for the eleven years to stop.*”

About nine of them were on that landing at about the eleventh hour of a Friday with twelve inches on the top step, and a woman of twenty-eight who had said five pieces and gone no further, and nobody on that bank was going to be thanked.

Twelve inches on the top step, five days of the coming back, and about seventy of those ninety steps under. The man of forty-three named Saturday in advance with the day and the week on it, and said there is no fifth thing.

A woman of twenty-eight walked four miles up that cart road on foot because nobody sent for her and nobody could have, and said the rest of why without being asked for it, in five pieces. She said the second no was a rule she made for herself. She said a figure of about four who will speak is not consent and said it a sixth time in her own mouth, and about nine is a figure of a bank and not a consent at all. She said what she keeps is a run of days for a school, two hundred and twelve days of it, anchored to the first morning she opened that door.

And she said the fifth piece, which was this: she is going to go back down that road this afternoon and enter today’s line about the school, and nobody is to come up that road to look at what she does. She did not say a yes and she did not say a no and she said she is not settled and is not going to be settled in a morning on a bank in front of about nine of you.

A man of thirty-one said the ordinary form was the answer to what a man of thirty-eight had asked, and was given the same sentence twice on purpose, and was told that three people had watched him do this three times and none of them had stopped it.

A woman of forty-four said she had used that name twice and would not use it again, and that every use of it on that bank is a use of a woman who cannot be sent for and who has said no twice.
