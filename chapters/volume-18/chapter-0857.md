# Chapter 857: Fifth Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Fourth Week, And A Saturday, And Sixteen Inches Holding With One Day Of The Coming Back, And The Fever Is Eighty-Two Weeks Old And That Is A Whole Number Of Weeks For The First Time In About Nine Days, And A Man Of Fifty-Four Who Cannot Read Says Out Loud The Ordinary Form Of A Run With A Hole In It, And A Man Of Thirty-Eight Calls It A Word He Is Not Going To Be Allowed To Use, And It Is Found Necessary And It Is Found Not Sufficient

Fifth day of the hundred and forty-fourth week. His six hundred and seventh morning. Four hundred and fifty-seven days after the settlement. The fever eighty-two weeks old. Four hundred and eleven days since the division. Three hundred and fifty-seven days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The bank held at sixteen and it was one day of the coming back. The man of forty-three gave both figures, and said that tomorrow at about the sixth hour about ninety of those steps would be walkable, and that the number of that time was his to give.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years, and gives four things every morning. The fourth is a figure of how many of those ninety steps are under, and about sixty of them are under this morning, and it is the fourth morning of a full bank holding.

“*About sixteen inches over the top step at the seventh hour, and it is one day of the coming back, and it holds, and about sixty of those ninety steps are under.*”

“*And tomorrow at about the sixth hour about ninety of those steps can be walked, and that is the twenty-fourth time, and I have given that number for twenty-four mornings and I am not going to stop giving it on a Saturday.*”

“*And there is a thing about today I have not said out loud and I am going to say it before about four of you ask me. The fever has been a whole number of weeks since the third day of this week and I did not know that until this morning, and I have been counting it in my own head since the third day.*”

At about the ninth hour a man of fifty-four who cannot read said out loud, in about nine ears, the ordinary form of a run with a hole in the front of it. He gave the working, and said twice that it was not his and that he had not made it up.

Barnaby Crove is fifty-four, holds the gravel on that landing, cannot read, and has asked for a day against the ninth line for a hundred and thirty-six mornings, which is eight hundred and fifty-seven less seven hundred and twenty-two, and then one.

“*A hundred and thirty-six, and I asked for it on a Saturday again, and I am not going to say anything about the day of the week because about four of you find it interesting and it is not.*”

“*And I am going to say the thing I said on Monday in a better shape, and I want about nine of you to have it in a better shape and not in mine.*”

“*Here it is. A run with a hole in its beginning is still entered on today, and today’s line is not a gap. And a position may be checked from the day its record starts, and it may not be checked before that day.*”

“*That is the whole of it and it is not mine. A woman of thirty-six said the first half of it in a different shape on Tuesday and would not let me use her words. A man of about fifty-two said on the Wednesday of this week that a description outgrows a wedge. I put the two together in the lane and this is what came out.*”

“*And I am not going to call it a name. I have been asked about four times this week what to call it and I am not going to tell any of the four of you. A man of thirty-eight does not need a name for it, and a man of fifty-four cannot read one.*”

Then a man of thirty-eight asked what it was called and said the word out loud on that landing, in about nine ears. He was told by a person that he was not going to be allowed to use it. He did not argue, he asked for the working in about nine ears, and he got it.

Garrin Tolley is thirty-eight and has a child of nine, and there is a board with thirty-two notches on the edge of it in the inside of his coat, which is a figure of what is on a board. He asked what three of the nine positions are on the Saturday of last week and was not answered.

“*It has a name. Everything that a person says in about nine of your ears has got a name, and I am not going to be the man who says a true thing and then lets about four of you go looking for a name for it.*”

“*It is the right of refusal. That is what it is, and a woman of thirty-six has got a run with a hole in it, and a woman of forty-nine has got a run with a hole in it, and that is what this thing is.*”

Barnaby Crove, fifty-four, said the word back, said who had said it first, and said where he had heard it, and said that it was spoken and that it is the correct number of people not to hear it in.

“*That is the right word. I am not going to tell you whether it is the right thing. I am telling you that it is not the name of what a man of fifty-four just said on a landing, and that I heard it spoken, and where.*”

“*A man of sixty-four said a sentence in a room on the Saturday of the hundred and forty-second week and I was in the room. I cannot read and I cannot spell it and I could not write it down if I tried it with both hands. And I am not going to stand here and give a thing I have heard spoken a name that a man of thirty-eight gave it this morning.*”

“*You have about nine of your ears on that and about four of you were not going to hear it from me, and that is the correct number of people to not hear a word said on a bank by a person who is about to be told off for it.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the ordinary form closes a gap, and a man of fifty-four told him it closes nothing and said what it does instead. About nine of them heard the whole of the insufficiency from the person who had said the thing was necessary.

“*It closes the gap. That is what it does, and that is what I have been saying about it since about the fourth hour, and I would like about nine of you to have heard a man of thirty-one call a thing done that is not done.*”

Barnaby Crove, fifty-four, said it was the other way round and said it without heat, and said that a thing which says where a hole is has not filled the hole and is not going to.

“*It does not close the gap. It says where the gap is and it says what may be done on the far side of it, and that is the whole of what it does.*”

“*And that is not a smaller thing than what you wanted, and it is not nothing. About four of you have been saying for two days that a book with a hole in it is a broken book, and it is not. It is a book with a hole in it and about four of you know what is on the other side of the hole and I do not.*”

“*So I am going to say the three things it does not do, in about nine of your ears, and then I am going to stop talking for the rest of the morning.*”

“*It does not put a day on the book four hundred miles off. I asked for that on Friday and a man of thirty-one could not give it to me, and this morning has not given it to me either.*”

“*It does not say who holds the pen. About four of you know what happened on the Sunday of last week and I do not want it in about nine of your ears again.*”

“*And it does not let a person be checked from the beginning. It lets them be checked from the day their record starts, and about four of you have records that start eleven years ago, and that is not the same as being checked.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the want he had, which was that the form be written on a leaf. A man of fifty-four said no, gave the working, and then said the reason in about nine ears, and the reason was about himself.

“*I want it on a leaf. In your book, in your hand, on the leaf for today, in about nine of your ears, and I will read it out to whoever you want on the next morning that wants it.*”

“*No. And I am not saying no because I have not been asked nicely. A leaf is a day, and what I said is not one day’s work, and a woman of forty-four told a man of thirty-one that on Friday and he has not heard the same thing from me yet.*”

“*And here is the reason and I would rather give it than have about four of you work it out. I cannot read. If I write that on a leaf, then in about four weeks there is a leaf on this landing with a form on it, and nobody will remember who said it first, and the one they will name is the man who can write.*”

“*I have been a man of fifty-four on this landing for nineteen years and I have never once had my name in a book that anybody read. I am not starting with a thing I said on a Saturday because a man of thirty-one asked me to.*”

“*And I am not going to be thanked for that, and about four of you were not going to hear it, and that is the correct number of people to not hear a man explain why he is refusing a thing.*”

Ilyan Vester, thirty-one, took it back. A man of fifty-four had now refused him in about nine ears three times in about four days. He gave his own two counters with the subtraction in them and said the number was the correct number of times.

“*Three times in about four days, in about nine of your ears, and I am not going to call that a tally of anything. A man of fifty-four said on Wednesday that a figure of wants is not a figure of days, and I have not forgotten it.*”

“*Three hundred and three days in this county, and that is eight hundred and fifty-seven less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across this palm is a hundred and seventy-two days old, and that is eight hundred and fifty-seven less six hundred and eighty-five, and nobody has said whether it will keep.*”

“*And a man of thirty-eight said a word out loud on this landing and a man of fifty-four told him he was not going to be allowed to use it, and about nine of you heard the working. I am not going to add anything to that this morning. I have nothing to add to it, and I have not had for about four days.*”

About nine of them were on that landing at about the eleventh hour of a Saturday, and the fever was eighty-two weeks old, and a man who cannot read had said a thing out loud in about nine ears and named it nothing.

The fever was eighty-two weeks old, and the man of forty-three had not known it until the seventh hour of that Saturday. The bank held at sixteen with one day of the coming back, and about sixty of the ninety steps were under, and tomorrow at about the sixth hour about ninety of them can be walked, and that is the twenty-fourth time.

A man of fifty-four who cannot read said out loud, in about nine ears, that a run with a hole in its beginning is still entered on today and today’s line is not a gap, and that a position may be checked from the day its record starts and not before it. He said twice that it was not his.
A man of thirty-eight asked what it was called and said the word the right of refusal out loud on that landing, and was told that word was spoken, that a man of fifty-four had heard it in a room on the Saturday of last week, and that he was not going to be allowed to use it.

And a man of thirty-one said the form closes a gap, and a man of fifty-four said it closes nothing, that it says where the gap is and what may be done on the far side of it, and then said the three things it does not do: It does not put a day on the book four hundred miles off, it does not say who holds the pen, and it does not let a person be checked from before the beginning.

And a man of thirty-one asked for it on a leaf, and a man of fifty-four said no. A leaf is a day and the thing he had said is not one day’s work, and he cannot read, and in about four weeks the name on the leaf would be the name of the man who can write.
