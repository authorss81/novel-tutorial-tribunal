# Chapter 853: First Day Of The Hundred And Forty-Fourth Week, And A Tuesday, And Twelve Inches Over The Top Step With Five Days Of The Coming Back And About Seventy Of The Ninety Under, And A Man Of Forty-Four From The Eighth Of The Nine Says Eleven Inches Stand Over The First Of His Leaves, And A Man Of Thirty-One Says It Is The Same Water And Is Given The Working Of Why It Is Not, And A Woman Of Twenty-Nine In A Reed Says What It Is To Keep A Run In The Condition That Ruined It

First day of the hundred and forty-fourth week. His six hundred and third morning. Four hundred and fifty-three days after the settlement. The fever eighty-one weeks and three days old. Four hundred and seven days since the division. Three hundred and fifty-three days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

---

The man of forty-four came up the cart road at the tenth hour. The man of forty-three gave the water at the seventh hour and gave the figure with it. About nine of them noticed that he gave the figure first this morning, which is not the order he was taught.

Corvin Tace is forty-three and has kept that landing nineteen years. He gave that figure for the last time on the first day of the hundred and forty-first week, and this morning is the second time he has given it in nineteen mornings. He gave it before the inches and did not apologise for it.

“*About twelve inches over the top step at the seventh hour, and it is five days of the coming back, and about seventy of those ninety steps are under this morning.*”

“*And I gave the figure first, and about four of you noticed, and I did it on purpose and I am going to say why.*”

“*On Saturday a woman of thirty-six put a satchel on that dry stone and it was a run with the front of it gone. On Monday a man of thirty-one put a day on a book that is four hundred miles off. About nine of you have spent two days looking at the front of a thing and not at the thing.*”

“*The figure of steps is the thing. The inches are how much of it there is. I have been saying them in that order for nineteen years and I have got it wrong twice in two days and I have stopped.*”

At about the tenth hour a man of forty-four came up that cart road from the low gate end of it. He did not come up to the counter, and he said why before he was asked. Then he gave the distance to the place his run is in, in his own mouth, because nobody on that landing had ever asked him for it.

Hal Brecht is forty-four, holds the eighth of the nine positions, lost his remedy to the comparison, argued a road for eleven years, and said a compact is not a road. He declined the common archive with a duty attached. He said he would rather be wrong in a compact that is mended than right in a charter that is not, and his road is not mended.

“*I did not come up to the counter because the counter is where a man says what a day was for a thing, and I have not got a day for the thing I keep. I have got a leaf with a hole in the front of the run and a wet step, and those are two different shapes and one of them is the fault of a hand.*”

“*Here is the distance, and I am giving it before anybody asks. About four of you are already looking at the water on the low side of this landing as if it were connected to my step. It is not. The step is at the head of the works, and it is a day’s walk from here with the water as it is, and two days in a dry week.*”

“*And that is a figure of days and not a figure of miles, and I am giving the days on purpose. Miles are a figure a man reads off a paper. Days are a figure a man has walked, and I have walked it wet and I have walked it dry, and the two are not the same walk.*”

“*And about eleven inches of water are standing over the first of my leaves at this hour. Eleven is a figure of inches of water over a stone step. The step is the anchor, and I am not going to say a figure without an anchor on it in about nine of your ears.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said that the water on that step is the water that took those leaves. A man of forty-four gave him the working of why it is not, in the ordinary voice, and did not raise it once.

Ilyan Vester is thirty-one and is nobody’s, and has been in this county two hundred and ninety-nine days, two hundred and ninety-nine being eight hundred and fifty-three less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across that palm is a hundred and sixty-eight days old, a hundred and sixty-eight being eight hundred and fifty-three less six hundred and eighty-five.

“*That is the same water. The water that is on those leaves now is the water that was on them when they went, and I am not going to stand on a bank and pretend those are two things.*”

Hal Brecht, forty-four, did not interrupt him. He answered him, and said that a man of thirty-one had got one thing right and one thing very wrong, and that both were useful.

“*You have got one thing right, which is that there is water on my leaves, and that is not a small thing to get right in about nine ears.*”

“*And you have one thing very wrong, and here is the working. Your water and mine are not joined by anything you can walk. If they were joined by something I could walk, I would have used it, and I would have dried a run in a fortnight instead of nineteen years.*”

“*A run that is put in water goes into water. That is what a run does. It is not the same water. It is the same condition, and the condition is the thing, and I have never once been able to make it stop.*”

Then a woman of twenty-nine, a hundred yards along that same drain on the low side, said the thing about the condition out loud. She said she was speaking about a reed and not about a compact, and asked both of them to say it first.

Marda Vell is twenty-nine, and has been in a reed along the edge of that drain for two hundred and twenty-four days. The figure is eight hundred and fifty-three less six hundred and twenty-nine. She has refused a figure of about four of her own as consent by name for the fifth time in two books.

“*Two hundred and twenty-four days, and that is eight hundred and fifty-three less six hundred and twenty-nine, and I have been in the same reed for all of them.*”

“*A man of forty-four has said the condition is the thing, and I am going to say what that means in about nine of your ears, and I am going to say it about a reed and not about his compact, because he has not asked me and I am not speaking for him.*”

“*You put a thing in water to keep it and the water is what is killing it, and that is not a paradox. It is a habit. A person does it because the doing of it is a keeping, and there is a keeping every day, and the thing gets less every day and nobody can see it get less because it is slow.*”

“*That is a figure of a habit and not a figure of anybody. And I am not going to say a man of forty-four should get his run out of the water this morning, because he has not said he should, and I have been asked for my reason five times in two books and I am tired of being asked for my reason.*”

Then a man of thirty-one said the want he had, in about nine ears. A man of forty-four gave him the working of why it cannot be done, and counted that it was the second time.

“*I want it lifted out. Out of the water, and dried, and put somewhere dry, and I want that to happen before the next time the water comes up, and I have said that in about nine of your ears.*”

Hal Brecht, forty-four, said no and gave the working. He said about four of them had now heard a man of thirty-one want to move a run, and would hear it a third time if he kept at it.

“*No, and here is the working, and it is not mine. I can lift it out. I have got a barrow and I have got hands and I have done it before in a wetter week than this one.*”

“*And then it is a wet bound thing in a satchel, and a satchel is not a run. The first thing that happens to a leaf that has been wet is that it stops being a leaf anybody can read in about nine days.*”

“*And the other half of it. If I lift the water off that step I lift about eleven inches of head with it, and that head is what keeps the low drain on the other side of this landing clear. The low drain is where that reed is and where that ash is. About nine of them live on the low side.*”

“*That is the second time you have said a want about a run in about nine of your ears, and about four of them have heard both, and I am telling you that I have heard both.*”

“*And I am not angry. I am a man of forty-four who has been wrong in public in about nine ears more times than you have been in this county, and I know exactly what it costs to be the one who wanted something on Monday and is the reason there is water in a reed on Tuesday.*”

Ilyan Vester, thirty-one, took the want back and gave the two figures that are his own, with the subtraction in them. He said he was not going to give the third figure this morning, because he had spent it badly on Monday.

“*Then I take it back, in the shape he gave it to me in, and I am not going to say a third thing about a run this week. About four of you can write that down as the useful thing I did today.*”

“*Two hundred and ninety-nine days in this county, and that is eight hundred and fifty-three less five hundred and fifty-four. The cut across this palm is a hundred and sixty-eight days old, and that is eight hundred and fifty-three less six hundred and eighty-five.*”

“*And I am not going to give the standing offer this morning. It is a hundred and fifty-three days old, and a hundred and fifty-three is eight hundred and fifty-three less seven hundred. I said it out loud on Monday, and I am not going to say a figure every morning because I have said it on more mornings than not.*”

“*I am nobody’s, and I have been nobody’s for all of those days. A man of forty-four has just told me in about nine of your ears that he knows I want to be the one who carries things. I did not know he had heard it twice until he said it, and I would not have left that on the ground.*”

About nine of them were on that landing at about the eleventh hour of a Tuesday, and about eleven inches of water stood over the first of a run of days a day’s walk from there. A woman of twenty-nine had said in about nine ears that the condition is the thing, and a man of thirty-one had wanted it lifted out and had been given the working and taken it back.

The fever was eighty-one weeks and three days old. He had been in that county two hundred and ninety-nine days, and the cut across that palm was a hundred and sixty-eight days old, and a hundred and fifty-three days after an offer that was asked at the seven-hundredth and has not been answered.

The man of forty-three gave the figure of steps before the inches for the first time in nineteen years, and said why. About four of them had spent two days looking at the front of a thing and not at the thing. About seventy of the ninety steps were under and the bank was twelve inches over the top step with five days of the coming back.

A man of forty-four came up the cart road from the low gate end and did not come to the counter, and said why, and gave the distance to the place his run is in. The step is a day’s walk with the water as it is and two days in a dry week, and he gave days and not miles on purpose: days are a figure he has walked, miles are a figure a man reads off a paper.
About eleven inches of water stand over the first of his leaves at this hour, and eleven is a figure of inches of water over a stone step, and the step is the anchor.

And a man of thirty-one said that was the same water that took them, and a man of forty-four gave him the working of why it is not. The two are not joined by anything a man can walk, and if they were he would have dried a run in a fortnight instead of nineteen years. It is not the same water. It is the same condition.
A woman of twenty-nine in a reed a hundred yards along that drain said what that means. She said she was speaking about a reed and not about a compact, and that she had been asked for her reason five times in two books and was tired of being asked.

And a man of thirty-one said he wanted the run lifted out and dried, and a man of forty-four said no and gave the working. A wet bound thing in a satchel is not a run, and the eleven inches of head on that step keep the low drain on the other side of that landing clear. About nine of them live on the low side.
He took the want back in the shape it was given to him in, gave his own two counters with the subtraction in them, and said that a man of forty-four had told him in about nine ears that he had heard it twice.
