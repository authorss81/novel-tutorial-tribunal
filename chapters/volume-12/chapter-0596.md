# Chapter 596: The Third Day Of The Hundred And Seventh Week, And A Thursday, And On Which A Woman Who Keeps Four Beds And Has Never Given Him Her Name Prices A Year Of A Man Of Thirty-One Out Loud In About Nine People’s Hearing With The Working Said In About Nine Sentences, And On Which The Figure Is Four Pounds Eleven Shillings And Threepence And Is Hers And Not His, And On Which The Other Half Of The Price Is One, Being One Morning, One Room And One Seat In About Thirty, And On Which A Man Of Thirty-One Says Out Loud That He Is Putting Down One Year Of His Own Future From The Crossing Of The Tally Stone To The Crossing After It, And On Which He Says Out Loud That From That Morning He Counts From The Water And That There Is No Figure For It And No Form Anywhere That Stops A Man Counting From A River, And On Which It Is Said That A Person Checks It At A Block Of Limestone With A Line Cut Round It And Nowhere Else

Third day of the hundred and seventh week. His three hundred and forty-sixth morning. One hundred and ninety-six days after the settlement. The fever forty-four weeks and five days. One hundred and fifty days since the division. Ninety-six days since a page was read out in a room with the door shut, in a town in another county.

The day before was the second day of the hundred and seventh week.

---

A settlement is a thing people live under afterwards and argue about afterwards. There is no form in the county of Kell that lets one be written into anybody, and so the question on the Thursday morning was not what went on the sheet.

The question was what a man has to give up so that about nine people in a hundred can put a thumb on what he did and check it.

He had been in that county forty-two days. He had under two pounds in a purse. He had said four things at a stone step on the Saturday of the sixth week and about nine mouths in that hundred had them by the fourth hour. He had asked a woman of twenty-eight for a number of feet that fifty-eight people needed, and had been told on the ground that nobody in that hundred knows it. On the Wednesday he had stood at a gate and heard a man of fifty-four say out loud that three boards hold a level and that nobody knows what they hold off.

He wanted a settlement that could be checked.

Nobody in that room found anything to say to that.

---

Then somebody else priced it, in a mouth, with the working said out loud, and it was not him.

“*Say what you are standing in front of,*” said Aldis Crane, who had a holding, a seat in the hundred and a skep behind his own gate. “*Because there is a sheet four feet wide on that board with four things on it now and the fourth one says nothing anybody in this hundred can check, and about nine of us have been looking at it since the ninth hour.*”

“*I am standing in front of four things and about nine people, and the fourth one is a price, and I have not said it out loud, and I have had it in my head since the Monday of the sixth week.*”

Nobody in that room had anything to say to that.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not be sorry for me,*” said Aldis Crane. “*About nine of us have watched a man carry a figure out of a room four times in six weeks. Every time the figure was right and the person who gave it was worse for it.*”

Nobody in that room thanked anybody.

“*Then say that last part,*” said Hoare Ryley, from the doorway.

“*If you are about to price yourself in a room of about nine people, then about nine of us are going to sit still and hear it.*”

Nobody in that room moved.

---

“*Then price it yourself,*” said Iven Ashcombe, who had come in with no slate under his arm. “*Out loud, with the working, in about nine sentences, and name what the figure is a figure of, because about four people in this room will check it against each other and about four of them cannot add.*”

“*A bed in that house is a penny and bread in the morning is a penny, and that is what everybody in this town charges. A man of thirty-one with a leg that stopped working on a common eats three pence a day and not two.*”

Nobody in that room said a word.

“*Then say that last part again and say where it is from,*” said Aldis Crane.

“*A woman who keeps four beds said it out loud in this county in about nine people’s hearing on the Monday of the fifth week. She gave me the figure then and gave me a parcel instead of her name.*”

About four people in that room said something at once.

“*Then say the rest of that,*” said Iven Ashcombe, “*and do the sum, because a year is not a fortnight and it is not a quarter and there is nobody in this county who can tell you how long it is until that block of limestone is under.*”

“*A year is the time between one crossing of that stone and the next. Three pence a day. Three hundred and sixty-five days in it, because it is a day of a river and not a day of a number.*”

Nobody in that room said anything.

“*Then say it out loud in a figure and say whose figure it is,*” said Aldis Crane, “*because about nine people in this hundred have a figure of about four pounds eleven shillings and threepence and about nine of those nine do not know whose it is.*”

“*It is hers. She worked it out in nine sentences in front of all of us.*”

Nobody in that room had anything to say to that.

“*Then say the sum,*” said Aldis Crane.

“*A thousand and ninety-five pence. Four pounds is nine hundred and sixty of them and there is a hundred and thirty-five over, which is eleven shillings and threepence. Four pounds, eleven shillings and threepence, for a man of thirty-one keeping himself in a bed and fed for three hundred and sixty-five days in a town of about eleven hundred people.*”

Nobody in that room thanked anybody.

---

“*Then say the rest of that and do not end it at the money,*” said Iven Ashcombe. “*That is the half of the price that is about his body, and it is the half that can be worked out with a slate. About nine people in this hundred have been carrying the other half since the Monday of the fifth week.*”

Nobody in that room took it up.

“*Then say that last part,*” said Aldis Crane.

“*And nobody has said it out loud since,*” said Iven Ashcombe.

About four seconds went past.

“*Then say it,*” said Aldis Crane, “*because I am one of the thirty and you are the only person in this county anybody can be sent for. You said it in a parlour in about nine people’s hearing.*”

Nobody in that room answered him.

“*And about nine of us did not hear it and about nine did,*” said Aldis Crane.

Nobody in that room found anything to say to that.

“*One,*” said Iven Ashcombe. “*One morning, one room, one seat in about thirty. That is the whole of what a hundred has to sell. I have not got a figure for it in shillings and I am not going to invent one in my own building.*”

---

Then a man of thirty-one did the thing he had said in a yard he would not do, and he was the one who had said it, and about nine people in that room had heard him say it in a yard in the mud in another week.

“*Say what you are standing in front of, and then say it a second time because about four of us did not catch it the first,*” said Hoare Ryley, who had a barrow and had been in the doorway for about an hour. “*Because you have been in this county forty-two days and you decided in a yard in the mud that you would stay nobody’s until the year turns, and about nine of us heard you and about nine have carried it a way that was not yours. And there is a fourth thing on that sheet with nothing checkable in it.*”

“*I am standing in front of a sheet and I am going to put a price on the fourth thing and it is going to be me and not anybody else. There is no form in this county that makes a man do a thing because a condition he set himself has not been met, and there is no form in this county that makes four people answerable for a fourth thing.*”

Nobody in that room had anything to say to that.

“*Then say the price and do not be sorry for me,*” said Aldis Crane, “*because about nine of us have watched you do this four times and about four of us would rather hear it out loud from you than be asked about it in a market.*”

---

So he said it, and it is the whole of what he put down, and it is not a wound and it is not a figure of time.

A year of his future options. From the morning the water comes over that block of limestone again to the morning after it. He does not go out of this county in it.

There is no form in the county of Kell that suspends that and no form that enforces it. About nine people in that hundred believed it when he said it and about nine did not, and about nine of those nine have told it already to about nine more. The arithmetic is out of the room.

And the second half of it is not a figure at all and there is nobody he can ask about it.

“*Say the rest of that,*” said Iven Ashcombe. “*Because you have said a year, and about nine people in this room heard a number and about nine of them heard something else. I have a board outside my own door that says any person in this hundred may ask me anything and I answer it.*”

Nobody in that room had a second question.

“*Then say that last part,*” said Aldis Crane.

“*About four of us in this room believe that you have just told us what a year costs, and about four of us do not,*” said Iven Ashcombe.

Nobody in that room had anything to say to that.

“*I am going to count from the water,*” said Ilyan. “*Not this week and not while I am saying it. After. From the day the water comes over that cut to the day it comes over it again, and every morning in between I will know where that river is without being asked and without looking.*”

Nobody in that room found anything to say to that.

“*Then say the rest of that and do not end it at the knowing,*” said Iven Ashcombe.

“*There is no form anywhere in any county that stops a man counting from a river, and there is no figure for it and I would not take one if there was.*”

About four people in that room said something at once and about nine did not.

“*Then say where a person checks that,*” said Aldis Crane, “*because about nine people in this hundred have said for five weeks that a number is a thing a man can be checked against, and you have just given us one that cannot.*”

Nobody in that room said anything back.

“*At that block of limestone with a line cut round it,*” said Ilyan. “*Put your hand flat on it. A man of fifty-eight did that for the length of a count of five on the morning the water came over it and then wrote the day at the foot of a sheet of parchment two men long, and that is the whole of the authentication it has. A person checks me at that stone and nowhere else, and I will be on whichever side of it the water is on.*”

---

Then a woman of about fifty who keeps four beds and has never given him her name said the half of it out loud, and she did it in about nine people’s hearing and she did not thank anybody for it.

“*Say what you are standing in front of, and say who is doing the asking,*” said the woman of about fifty who keeps four beds. “*Because a man has just given a year out of his own future in a room of about nine people. I priced thirty days of him in this county in about nine people’s hearing nine weeks ago and gave him two acres instead of my name. About nine of us have been waiting for him to find out what the rest of it costs.*”

Nobody in that room thanked anybody.

“*It costs the other half of the price of a bed and bread for three hundred and sixty-five days, and that half is one, and it is one morning, one room and one seat in about thirty, and there is no figure in shillings for it and a water-clerk has already said so in this building twice.*”

Nobody in that room found anything to say to that.

“*Then say the rest of that,*” said Aldis Crane, “*because you have just told a man of thirty-one what he has bought with a year and about nine of us would like to know what he has bought it with.*”

Nobody in that room thanked anybody.

“*He has bought the right to be wrong in front of about nine people and to be asked about it on a Saturday morning for a year,*” said the woman of about fifty who keeps four beds. “*That is the whole of it. It is not a place. There is no column for it and there is no form that renews it and there is no rate on it. A man who pays it is not one of about thirty men and he is not anybody’s and he is not nobody’s either. He is a man who has put a year down so that about nine people can put a thumb on what he said.*”

Nobody in that room thanked anybody and nobody in that room was sorry for anybody.

**WHAT THE FOURTH THING ON THE SHEET SAYS. It is in his hand and there is no name at the top of it. A man of thirty-one has put down one year, from the crossing of the tally stone to the crossing after it. There is no form in this county that makes him and none that stops him, and anybody in that hundred may go to that stone and put a hand flat on it and ask him what he said. And from that morning he counts from the water, and there is no figure for that and nobody can check it. And this is the only line on that sheet that is about a person. There is no column for it. No person in the county of Kell is bound by a word of it.**

The fever was forty-four weeks and five days old. A man of thirty-one was priced a year out loud in a room over a stable with about nine people in it, by a woman who keeps four beds and has never given him her name. The working was said in about nine sentences. The figure is four pounds eleven shillings and threepence for three hundred and sixty-five days at three a day. The other half of the price is one, being one morning, one room and one seat in about thirty, and a water-clerk has no figure in shillings for that and will not invent one in his own building.

A year of a man of thirty-one’s future options was paid on the Thursday morning of the third day of the hundred and seventh week. It runs from the crossing of the tally stone to the crossing after it. It is not a wound and it is not a number of weeks, and there is no form in the county of Kell that suspends it or enforces it.

And from that morning he counts from the water, and it is permanent, and about nine people in that hundred have it a different way by the fourth hour, and a person checks it at a block of limestone with a line cut round it and nowhere else.
