# Chapter 91: A Question Is Not a Matter

The letter from Halloway came down the fen road on a cart on the second of the fifth month and went up four flights of stairs to a stone room off the second gallery, and it was one sheet of the district's own paper with four names and a mark on the bottom of it, and the mark is the mark of a bench of four and there are not many of those in this city.

> **TO A RECORDWRIGHT OF A PUBLIC RECORD, WHICH THE BENCH OF FOUR OF THIS DISTRICT HAS HEARD NAMES ONE AND DOES NOT KNOW THE NAME OF.**
>
> **THE SIXTH OF THIS MONTH. THE SECOND BELL. THE ROOM OVER THE PUMP. IN PUBLIC.**
>
> **ON THE TWENTY-NINTH OF LAST MONTH THE HOLDER OF OUR FIFTH TERM ASKED, ON A BANK, IN THE OPEN AIR, IN FRONT OF ABOUT TWO HUNDRED PEOPLE, WHAT A PAGE IS FOR, AND WE WERE IN THE ROOM WITH HER AND WE DID NOT ANSWER IT. THE BENCH HAS ENTERED THE QUESTION IN OUR BOOK WITH A DATE. IT IS A QUESTION AND NOT A MATTER AND WE HAVE SAID SO, AND WE HAVE NEVERTHELESS ENTERED IT, BECAUSE A BENCH OF FOUR MAY PUT WHAT IT LIKES IN ITS OWN BOOK ON ANY DAY IT SITS AND THERE IS NOBODY IN THIS CITY WHO CAN TELL US THAT IT MAY NOT.**
>
> **THE HOLDER CANNOT BE A PARTY. THE HOLDER CANNOT APPEAR IN SUPPORT OF ONE AND CANNOT BE NAMED IN ONE. WE HAVE ASKED YOU TO ATTEND AND WE HAVE NOT ASKED YOU TO COME, BECAUSE WE CANNOT ASK YOU TO COME AND THE TWO THINGS ARE NOT THE SAME THING, AND WE HAVE SAID SO IN OUR BOOK, AND A CLERK OF THIS BENCH HAS READ IT BACK TWICE.**
>
> **THE QUESTION WILL BE READ OUT ON THE SIXTH WHETHER YOU ARE THERE OR NOT. WE WILL GO ON WITH OUR OWN WORK. NO REPLY IS ASKED FOR AND NONE IS WANTED IN OUR OWN WORDS, WHICH WE WOULD RATHER YOU DID NOT IMPROVE ON.**
>
> **WE HAVE NO MONEY AND THE FOURTH BELL IS THE SIXTH BELL AT THE LOW WATER AND THE COST OF ANYBODY COMING UP THIS ROAD IS THEIRS.**

The room had a stove in it that did not draw, and had not drawn since before the ruling, and on the second of the fifth month the stove had been lit at the first bell by a man who came up with the coal and went down again, so the room was warm in the middle and cold at the edges, and the licence was on the table with all four terms face up because she had been looking at it since the second of the second month without ever deciding to.

She read the letter twice and then she put it down on top of the licence, which is a thing a person does when she is not going to answer a letter by letter.

The four terms were these and she had written none of them and had agreed to all four, and one of them had cost her a shop and two rooms and a business, and she had said that out loud in a court in front of three hundred people, and she had counted the days since, because counting is what she does.

> **A LICENCE TO CARRY A PUBLIC RECORD, ON FOUR TERMS.**
>
> **I. A LICENSED RECORDWRIGHT DOES NOT WITHDRAW FROM A MATTER WITHOUT AN ENTRY IN THE PUBLIC RECORD NAMING HER REASON.**
>
> **II. A LICENSED RECORDWRIGHT ANSWERS FOR THE RECORD AND NOT FOR THE OUTCOME, AND IF THE RECORD IS WRONG THE RECORDWRIGHT IS THE ONE WHO IS CALLED.**
>
> **III. A LICENSED RECORDWRIGHT CARRIES THE TERMS OF THE MATTER TO THE REVIEW AND TO ANY OTHER DISTRICT THAT ASKS.**
>
> **IV. A LICENSED RECORDWRIGHT TAKES NO MATTER OF HER OWN WHILE SHE HOLDS THIS ONE.**

The fourth one was a single line, and there was no date on it and there never would be, and she had watched two clerks try to put one on it in the record and had not stopped them, and the reason she had not stopped them is that a term which runs until somebody says otherwise is the only kind that means anything.

---

Ilyan Vester was in the room, because he was in most rooms, because a man with limited standing in one matter is a man who is permitted to be in rooms and is not permitted to say anything in them.

He had come up at the first bell and had not sat down. He had read the letter twice himself, slowly, with a finger on the words, which he can do with about two hundred guild words and a finger, and which is not the same as reading.

“Sixth of the month,” he said. “That is four days.”

“That is four days.”

“I will go down.”

“No,” said Sera Quill.

He stopped where he was.

“I would like to hear the reason,” he said, “because I have walked eleven miles past the point at which I was obliged to, twice in two months, and I would like to know where the point is this time.”

“The point this time is that you are named in nine sheets of paper four miles down a fen road as the carrier of the terms of a matter, and the terms say the terms go to any district that asks, at your own cost and without pay, and that if he refuses, or is unable, or is not within four miles, they are not carried and the appeal is stayed until another carrier is sworn in that district, and nobody else may carry them.” She did not put the sheet down. “That room is four miles from where I am standing. You are four miles from a bridge. Those are two different four miles and both are correct, and neither of them puts you in a room over a pump in a district that has not asked you for anything, and a bench of four cannot ask you for anything, because a bench of four in that district could not make you do it and would have to enter that it could not.”

“So I sit in Orison for four days.”

“You sit in Orison for four days and I go down on the sixth or I do not, and either way the question is read out at the second bell on a day a bench of four appointed, and the reading does not need me in it. That is the whole of what that letter is telling me, and it is telling me kindly, and Corva Silt has put the price of the road on the bottom of it in my district's own money, which is nothing.”

He put his hand flat on the table the way he did, and then took it off again.

“Can I put a question to you,” he said, “as a question.”

“You may put anything to me that is not a matter, and you may not put anything to me that is a matter, and I will not be able to tell you which of the two you have done until it is in. I am not going to do that to a man at a table in a room with a stove in it. So no.”

---

What she did instead, after he had gone down the stairs badly, was take a sheet of cheap paper off the clip and write the six heads on it, because a recordwright who cannot find a thing in her own head writes it down, and then she sat and looked at it for the better part of a bell.

A matter, in the practice she had been taught in a chamber in a city four miles from a bridge, and in the practice of the one office in this city that had ever answered a question about it in writing, was six things. A claim, which was what was wanted and by whom. A subject, which was the person it was about. A burden, which was what it cost and who carried it and when. A witness, which was the person or the thing that could test it. A remedy, which was what happened if it succeeded or if it failed. An exit, which was how it ended, or was amended, or could be refused.

She wrote the six heads in a column. Then she wrote, under each one, what this had, and what this did not have, and the answer took eleven minutes and it is in the back of her own book with a date on it and is in no record but hers.

A claim: nothing. Nobody had claimed anything. A woman of twenty-nine had asked a question in a room and the question had been put in a bench's own book with a date on it, and a date on a question is not a claim. If she answered it, the answer would be a claim against the bench, and the bench would be a party, and a party has to be served, and serving it costs a clerk's hour, and there is no clause in any instrument in this city that says how to serve a bench.

A subject: one. The subject of the question was a person, and the person was the woman who had asked it, and the person was not the person asking.

That was the whole of it, and it took her until the middle of the second bell to accept it, because it is an extraordinary thing to be told by a sheet of your own paper that the only one of the six you are standing inside of is the second, and that the second one is *the person it is about*, and that the person it is about is not you.

The System put the room in front of Ilyan at the second bell, on the stair, and it was a record of a room and not a rule about the world.

**IN THE STONE ROOM OFF THE SECOND GALLERY, THE SECOND OF THE FIFTH MONTH, AT THE SECOND BELL. A RECORD OF A ROOM AND OF A SHEET UPON A TABLE.**
**THE SIX PARTS, AS THE SHEET HAS THEM: A CLAIM. A SUBJECT. A BURDEN. A WITNESS. A REMEDY. AN EXIT.**
**FILLED: NONE. THE CLAIM IS EMPTY. THE BURDEN IS EMPTY. THE WITNESS IS EMPTY. THE REMEDY IS EMPTY. THE EXIT IS EMPTY. THE SIX ARE NOT PARTIALLY FILLED. THEY ARE BLANK.**
**THE ONE SHE IS INSIDE OF IS THE SECOND. THE SUBJECT OF THE QUESTION IS A PERSON WHO IS NOT THE PERSON ASKING.**
**THE FOURTH TERM OF HER LICENCE, IN THE ROOM'S OWN COPY, ON THE TABLE, FACE UP: SHE TAKES NO MATTER OF HER OWN WHILE SHE HOLDS THIS ONE.**
**WHAT IS NOT DECIDED IN THIS ROOM: WHETHER A QUESTION MAY BE ANSWERED WITHOUT A MATTER BEING MADE. WHETHER THE FOURTH TERM REACHES A QUESTION OR ONLY A MATTER. WHETHER A BENCH OF FOUR MAY ENTER A QUESTION IN ITS OWN BOOK AND THEREUPON BE A PARTY TO IT. WHETHER THE QUESTION ON THE SIXTH WILL BE ANSWERED BY ANYBODY.**

---

The trialwright came up the stairs at the third bell with a sheet in his hand and put it on the table face up, which is a thing he does, and it was not a notice and it was not a summons, and it had his name at the bottom of it in the hand he writes in.

Sel Harrow is the court's witness in any appeal. He has been eleven years in it. He has invented four procedures in public and named them as his own and has never once pretended that any of them is a power. He has refused the same line six times in the same four words, and he said on the twenty-seventh of the fourth month that he would refuse it on the seventh, and nobody has heard the seventh.

“Mine,” he said. “On the court's own motion, and no order has been made, and I have put it in front of you before I put it in front of anybody. That is the fourth procedure I have invented in public and it is the worst of the four.”

> **IN THE MATTER OF THE LICENCE OF S. QUILL, RECORDWRIGHT, HELD SINCE THE RULING, UPON A QUESTION ADDRESSED TO HER OUT OF A DISTRICT THAT HAS ASKED HER NOTHING.**
>
> **IT IS THE VIEW OF THE COURT, AND IT IS NOT AN ORDER, THAT THE FOURTH TERM OF A LICENCE IS A TERM UPON MATTERS AND NOT UPON QUESTIONS; THAT A QUESTION PUT TO A PERSON OUT OF A DISTRICT WHICH HAS ENTERED IT IN ITS OWN BOOK, IN THE PERSON'S OWN NAME, AT THE PERSON'S OWN COST, BY POST, IS NOT OF ITSELF A MATTER; AND THAT A LICENSED RECORDWRIGHT MAY ANSWER SUCH A QUESTION ONCE, IN WRITING, WITHOUT BEING IN A MATTER, PROVIDED SHE ENTERS THAT SHE HAS DONE SO.**
>
> **NOBODY HAS ASKED FOR THIS. THE COURT HAS PROPOSED IT. A COURT HAS NO POWER OVER THE TERMS OF A LICENCE IT GRANTED, AND THE COURT SAYS SO ON THE FACE OF THIS SHEET.**

He read it out himself, in the flat voice, and then he did not say anything for a moment, which is not a thing he does.

“The view is right,” he said. “I want that said first, and I want it said by me. It is narrow and it is correct and it costs the district nothing, and it is the fourth procedure I have invented, and it is the only one of the four I am not proud of.”

“Why not.”

“Because I put the fourth term there.” Harrow put a finger on the table beside the licence and did not touch it. “I drafted it and the court took it and you agreed to it, and I have thought about it every day since the second of the second month, and I have never once thought I was being clever. I thought I was being exact. You told me that a licensed advocate who is a man's counsel is a man with a mouth, and I wrote a term that said the thing you actually needed, which is that you would not be bought. And I put a second thing in it that I did not think about, which is that you would not be able to be *in* anything. You are the only person in this case who could have got that fixed in a week. You had it fixed in a week. It has held eleven weeks. I have watched it hold in a room in this city four miles from a bridge and it is holding now, and I have never once seen it used against you, and I have seen it used against you four times.”

“Who used it against me.”

“Two benches, a clerk, and a man who is me,” said Harrow. “I would like that in my own book and not in yours, and it is a bad confession from a man with a tray, and I am making it now rather than never, because you are going to refuse my sheet and I would rather have refused it for a reason that I have said out loud in a room.”

He put his finger on the third line of his own hand.

“Answer it once, in writing, and enter that you have.”

---

“No,” said Sera Quill.

Harrow did not move.

“May I ask why,” he said. “I have earned the asking and I know that I have not.”

“You have. Which is why I am going to answer it properly and not quickly, and you are going to stand there while I do it, because if I do it quickly you will write it down and it will be two lines long and one of the two lines will be the reason you wanted and not the reason I have.”

She put both hands flat on the table, which is a thing she has watched three clerks do and has never once done herself.

“Three reasons, and the third is the one I would go on about if you would let me give only one.

“The first is that you are asking me to give back a thing I gave. Four terms, a shop, two rooms, and a business that paid for both for six years. The fourth term is the only one of the four anybody has ever asked me whether it was worth what it cost, and you are asking me, and the answer is that a term which can be read a second way on the day it bites is not a term. It is a wall with a door in it, and the door is in it on the morning it is wanted, and I have spent six years watching doors like that and I know what they are for. They are for the person who comes at eight in the morning.

“The second is that the day a licence's fourth term can be varied for the person it binds, no fourth term binds anybody, and I do not only mean mine. Somebody in this city in about four years is going to want to file the thing they have been carrying, and the fourth term is the reason they cannot, and if you and I make a precedent today for varying a term on the day it bites, then in four years that person will be told by a clerk that a term may be varied, and the clerk will be right, and the person will be standing at a counter with a shop closed and two rooms given up and a six-year claim in a drawer, and the clerk will be quoting us.

“The third is that it does not answer the question, and that is the one I care about, and I am going to say it slowly.

“A bench of four four miles from a bridge has put a question in its own book. On the sixth that question is going to be read out in a room over a pump by a woman who cannot appeal from one word of it, to whoever comes. If I answer it in writing, and enter it, and my answer goes up the fen road into that district's book, then in about four years there will be a page in that district with a recordwright's name at the bottom of it, and a bench there will be able to say *a recordwright was asked and answered*, and the answer will be a sentence about what a page is for. And a sentence about what a page is for, with a licence number on it, is a thing that will be used against a person, because in four years somebody will want the second half of it, and there will be a woman on a bank who is the only person who can read it, and she will be the same woman, and she will be standing there with a barrow while a clerk reads out that the question has been answered.

“I would rather the question stood unanswered in two hundred people's hearing for a quarter of a year than have it answered in a room by a person with a licence and a term. That is the whole of my craft, Mr Harrow, and it is not a technique. I am a person who writes down what a thing *is* and not what a thing is *for*, and everything that has gone wrong in this case for four months has gone wrong because somebody wrote down what a thing was for.”

Nobody in the room said anything for about a minute, and the stove ticked.

“Then say it,” said Harrow. “Not to me. Say it in your own book, and I will not look at it, and I will enter that the view was proposed and refused and that no order was made, and I will enter nothing else, and the fourth procedure goes back in my pocket with its name on it and it will not be used again in this case by me.”

“I am not going to say it to a man who has offered me a way round it. I am going to say it to the person it is about, if she is ever in a room with me and not in a matter, and if she is never in a room with me then the sentence was not for her and I will find out what it was for.”

---

Then he did the thing that made him what he is, which was that he took the sheet off the table and turned it over and wrote four lines on the back of his own hand and slid it back.

> **THE COURT HAS PROPOSED A VIEW AND THE VIEW HAS BEEN REFUSED. NO ORDER WAS MADE. THE COURT HAS NO POWER OVER THE TERMS OF A LICENCE AND SAID SO ON THE FACE OF ITS OWN SHEET BEFORE IT ASKED.**
>
> **THE REFUSAL IS ENTERED WITH THE REFUSAL'S OWN REASON AS GIVEN, IN THREE PARTS, AND THE COURT IMPROVES NONE OF IT.**
>
> **MR HARROW HAS ASKED THAT IT BE ENTERED THAT HE DRAFTED THE FOURTH TERM OF THE LICENCE IN QUESTION, AND THAT HE HAS USED IT FOUR TIMES AGAINST THE PERSON WHO AGREED TO IT, AND THAT HE DID NOT KNOW HE WAS DOING IT, AND THAT THE COURT HAS NO POWER OVER HIM IN THIS AND WILL NOT PRETEND TO.**

He read it back to her twice, in the flat voice, and she made him read the last line twice as well.

“You will not get what you want out of this,” he said. “You are going to be standing in a room on the sixth with a question you cannot answer and a fourth term that stops you answering it, and the honest position is a very old one and it is called being a person who is not in the matter.”

“I have been that person for six years,” said Sera Quill. “I have never once been able to say so out loud in a room with a bench in it. Thank you for the procedure.”

Harrow went down the stairs and she heard him stop on the landing, which he does not do, and then go on down.


She wrote four lines in the back of her own book that evening, in her own hand, with a date on it, and they are in no record but hers, and the first of them is the line she wrote on the second of the second month and the other three are new.

> **A QUESTION IS NOT A MATTER. A MATTER IS A CLAIM, A SUBJECT, A BURDEN, A WITNESS, A REMEDY AND AN EXIT.**
>
> **AND THE SIX ARE BLANK, AND I AM INSIDE ONE OF THEM, AND IT IS THE ONE CALLED THE SUBJECT, WHICH IS THE PERSON IT IS ABOUT, WHICH IS A WOMAN WITH A BARROW WHO IS NOT ME AND WHO ASKED.**
>
> **A RECORD IS A THING THAT IS. A CRAFT IS A THING THAT IS FOR. I HAVE SPENT FOUR MONTHS WRITING DOWN WHAT THINGS ARE FOR IN FOUR MILES OF THIS CITY AND EVERY SINGLE THING THAT WENT WRONG IN IT WENT WRONG BECAUSE SOMEBODY WROTE DOWN WHAT A THING WAS FOR.**
>
> **ON THE SIXTH OF THIS MONTH I AM GOING TO BE FOUR MILES FROM A ROOM IN WHICH A QUESTION IS READ OUT, AND I HAVE NO ANSWER, AND I AM NOT GOING TO GIVE ONE, AND THAT IS NOT THE SAME AS HAVING A PROCEDURE.**

Then she did the other thing, and she did it because the third of the four lines had put it in her head and because a person who has just refused the easy door gets up and walks somewhere.

There is one room in this city that anybody in this case knows of that has ever had a page taken out of it. It is not a bench and it is not over a pump and it is not a court, and it is four miles from where she was standing, at the low end of a street of counting-houses, in a building with no name on the door, and the volume with the empty slot is on a shelf in it, and the slot is empty and the thread is cut and the inventory mark beside the slot is in a newer hand than the book.

And there is one other thing in that building, or in the same building, or on the same floor, and it is a desk with a stamp on it and no clerk, and an incoming index that stopped four years ago, and behind that desk there is a series running by plate, and any person may read any of it on a day, and no person has ever asked.

It cannot answer her. She knows that. It has said so twice, in the same hand, in eleven lines, and what it said was that it can prove that a rule was printed and cannot hold a person, and that an office with no person in it is not a door, it is a shelf.

But she had not asked it the question she needed to ask. She had asked it about a page, twice, and got no, and had been right to, and had gone on asking because she was looking for a page.

She did not want a page. She wanted to know what the terms of a room that hears the legality of refusing a ruling were *printed as*, in this city, in any year the desk held. That is not a question about a person. That is a question about paper. And nobody in this case had ever asked it a question about paper, because everybody who has gone up that stair in four months has gone for the other thing.

She wrote a sheet on the fourth of the fifth month, at the first bell, before she went down the road, and it was one page and it had four questions on it, and three of them were about paper.

> **TO THE STANDING AUTHORITY OF PRESERVATION. FROM S. QUILL, RECORDWRIGHT OF A PUBLIC RECORD, WHO HAS PUT TWO QUESTIONS TO THIS DESK IN THE LAST ELEVEN WEEKS AND HAS BEEN ANSWERED NO IN THE SAME HAND BOTH TIMES.**
>
> **ONE. WHAT WERE THE TERMS OF THE QUIET CHAMBER PRINTED AS, IN THIS CITY, IN ANY YEAR THAT THIS DESK HOLDS. NOT THE ORIGINAL. WHAT WAS PRINTED.**
>
> **TWO. HOW MANY TIMES WERE THEY PRINTED, AND IN WHOSE TYPEFACE, AND ON WHAT DAY, AND BY WHOSE ORDER, AND I AM ASKING FOR THE ORDER AND NOT THE NAME, BECAUSE AN ORDER IS A THING A DESK HOLDS AND A NAME OFTEN IS NOT.**
>
> **THREE. IF TWO PRINTINGS OF THE SAME TERMS DO NOT SAY THE SAME THING, WHICH OF THEM IS LATER, AND HOW DOES THIS DESK KNOW, AND I AM ASKING THE LAST PART BECAUSE I DO NOT THINK IT KNOWS AND I WOULD RATHER BE TOLD.**
>
> **FOUR. THIS DESK HAS ANSWERED ME TWICE THAT IT CANNOT HOLD A PERSON. I AM NOT ASKING IT TO. I AM ASKING WHETHER IT HAS ANYTHING TO SAY ABOUT WHAT A THING WAS FOR, AND I UNDERSTAND ALREADY THAT THE ANSWER IS NO AND THAT THE ANSWER IS NO IN A HAND THAT SAYS NO FOR A REASON, AND A REASON IS THE ONLY THING I HAVE EVER BEEN ABLE TO PAY A PERSON FOR.**

She read it over twice, and then she did a thing that she has done perhaps nine times in six years and that is the whole of what a licence is for, which is that she read a document she had written out loud to somebody else before she sent it.

She read it to Ilyan Vester on the step of a gate four miles down a fen road at the second bell on the fourth, in the cold, with the water coming up the flat and about nine people on the bank going past to the pump, and he followed it with a finger on the words and could get most of it and did not get the whole, and she did not slow down for him where he lost it, because she is not his advocate and was never going to be.

“Question four is a gift to a desk,” he said, when she had finished.

“Question four is a gift to me,” she said. “If the answer is no with a reason, then the desk has told me three times what it is for and I will stop. And if it is no without a reason, then it is a piece of paper with a stamp on it, and I will say so in the next book I write, in public, with a date.”

“You are going to write that down.”

“I am going to write it down if it happens, and not write it if it does not, and there is no way in this city to tell the difference in advance, and that is the whole of the difference between a record and a complaint.” She folded the sheet once and put it inside her coat against the paper, which is the fourth thing that is not a matter. “And I am not entering any of this in a record, and I want you to hear the reason, because you are the only person in this city who would otherwise think it an oversight.”

“The reason is that I am in it.”

“The reason is that you are in it,” she said, “the moment I put down in writing that a desk in this city has been asked a question by a recordwright, and on what day. You would become a person in a record of a thing that is not your matter, and a person in a record is a person who can be produced, and you have been produced twice this year and you wrote down yourself that a witness can be produced three times, and I am not going to be the reason.”

He looked at the water for a while.

“Then go on the fifth,” he said. “Not tomorrow. I know something about going on a day after a refusal, and I have got no standing to say it and I am saying it.”

“It is the fourth of the month.”

“It is the fourth of the month,” she said, “and I am going on the fifth, and you are not going to tell me why, and I am not going to ask you, and that is the correct arrangement between us and it is the only thing either of us has held for four months.”

---

What she found out on the fifth of the fifth month, at the first bell, in a lane at the low end of a street of counting-houses, is this, and it took four minutes and it cost her nothing, which was a surprise.

The building is not shut. There is a door and the door is not locked and there is no man at it and there has not been a man at it for four years, because a desk with a stamp on it and a series running by plate behind it is a thing a person may walk into on a day and read any of it, and the whole of the reason nobody has ever read any of it is that a person has to know to want it.

What is not open is the shelf of bound volumes, because the shelves are in a cold room at the back and the room is opened by a man with a key and the man is not a clerk and is not paid by the authority and is paid ninepence a day and sweeps the floors.

He was on his knees at the bottom of the stair with a broom when she came down the lane, and he was forty-one, and his name was Corrin Vask, and he had been sweeping the building for nine years, and he asked her, before she had said anything at all, whether she had come about the volumes or about the printed shelf, in that order, as though the two were the ordinary two kinds of thing a person came to that door for.

And when she said the volumes, he put the broom against the wall and stood up and said, “Then nobody has asked me for that one.”
