# Chapter 93: What a Rule Said

The answer came back on the eighth of the fifth month, in the same hand as the other two, which is a hand that belongs to an office and not to a person, and it is eleven lines, and it took two days and two shillings, and it is the last thing anybody in this case is going to get out of that desk.

She had left the four questions on the counter at the second bell on the sixth of the fifth month, on her way out of the building, with the leaf-work for the day already begun behind her in the cold room, and Corrin Vask wrote the time on the back of the sheet in his own small hand and put it in the drawer, because there is a drawer for things that are not answered, and a thing in a drawer is a thing that has not been thrown away, and that is the whole of the process and there is no other process.

The answer was on the counter on the eighth with a docket on it, and the docket had a number on the spine and the number is the only thing that happened to it, and there is no clerk and there is no stamp and the sheet was face up.

> **NO, TO THE FOURTH QUESTION, AND IT IS THE THIRD TIME THIS DESK HAS ANSWERED THE SAME QUESTION, IN THE SAME HAND, AND THE REASON IS NOT A REFUSAL OF THE PERSON AND IS NOT A DEFICIENCY.**
>
> **A DESK THAT KEEPS PRINTED INSTRUMENTS IS NOT A DESK THAT KEEPS THE THINGS THINGS ARE FOR. A PRINTED RULE CAN BE SHOWN TO HAVE BEEN PRINTED, AT A DATE, IN A TYPEFACE, ON AN ORDER. A PRINTED RULE CANNOT BE SHOWN TO HAVE BEEN UNDERSTOOD BY ANYBODY, AND A RULE THAT WAS UNDERSTOOD BY NOBODY WAS NOT UNDERSTOOD BY THE PERSON WHO PRINTED IT EITHER, AND THIS DESK HAS NEVER ASSUMED OTHERWISE AND WILL NOT BEGIN NOW.**
>
> **THERE IS NOTHING ON THE FACE OF ANY INSTRUMENT HELD BY THIS DESK WHICH SAYS WHAT ANYTHING IS FOR. THIS IS NOT A LOSS. IT IS THE CONDITION. WHAT IS PRINTED IN THIS CITY IS NOT LOST. WHAT THINGS ARE FOR IS NOT PRINTED ANYWHERE AND HAS NEVER BEEN PRINTED ANYWHERE, AND THE PLACE A PERSON WENT TO LOOK FOR IT IN IS A PERSON, AND NOT A DESK.**
>
> **A DESK IS A SHELF. A SHELF IS A GOOD PLACE FOR A THING THAT IS. A SHELF IS NOT A DOOR AND IT IS NOT GOING TO BECOME ONE, AND THE PERSON WHO WOULD HAVE TO WALK THROUGH IT IS THE PERSON WHO HAS TO BE CARRIED, AND CARRYING IS NOT SOMETHING A DESK DOES.**
>
> **TO THE FIRST THREE QUESTIONS: YES.**
>
> **TO THE FOURTH: NO, WITH A REASON, AND THE REASON IS THAT THE QUESTION IS ABOUT A PERSON, AND IT IS THE THIRD TIME, AND A THING THAT HAS BEEN ANSWERED THREE TIMES WITH A REASON IS A THING A PERSON OUGHT TO STOP ASKING.**

The last line is the reason this answer is different from the other two, and she read it four times standing at a counter, and then she read the docket, and the docket said the desk had added two lines of its own at the bottom, and the two lines said that the desk had held the sheet for two days and had charged a shilling a day for standing in the series room, and that the shilling a day is the same shilling that the man who opens a room has always been paid out of a drawer, and is not a fee of the authority, and is in no series, and is entered in no ledger, and that the man who takes it is a man in the building and not an officer of it.

It took two days because the first three questions were the ordinary questions and a desk that holds four years of print in a series running by plate can answer an ordinary question in about nine minutes, and the nine minutes is a clerk's reading of a shelf-mark and a hand taking three volumes down off a run, and the second day was because she asked for all three printings at once and they are on two runs and the runs are in two cases and the cases are on two shelves, and a man with one hand opening two cases and putting four volumes down and holding a lamp for somebody else is slow at it.

That is the honest account of why two days. It is not a mystery and it is not a hand that is unwilling. It is a hand that is six years gone at the wrist and it went faster on the second day than the first because it had done it once.

---

The three printings are these and they are a thing about paper and not about a person, and the middle one is the one that mattered.

A form for lodging a matter in the Quiet Chamber, printed in this city, in a district's typeface, by a shop that fills a space in a docket, un-ordered, sealed and numbered, twice, and once more by a different shop in a different typeface, in four years.

There is no original of the Quiet Chamber's terms in print anywhere in this city and there never was, and the desk says so in one line, and the line is the reason the whole of this took two days and a man with a bad hand: the terms were *entered*, by hand, in a bound volume, four hundred years ago, and what has been printed about them, three times in four years, is not the terms. It is a form, with a heading and a line for the subject and a line for the burden and a space at the foot, and a form summarising a chamber is a thing made by somebody who has read the volume and has had to leave the volume and has put down on paper what they could carry.

The first printing is the oldest of the three and it is the shortest. The second is the longest and it is the only one of the three with a line in it that the other two do not have, and the line is in the same place in all of them, which is the fourth line from the foot, and in the first and the third that fourth line is empty and there is a rule and no words, and in the second it is not empty.

Corva Silt's district's print authority would have known what to do with a form that carries an extra line and does not carry it on the other two. It would have gone back to the copy. And there is no copy in this city, because the copy is in a cold room about fifty feet from where they were standing, and a recordwright had been in it four days' work before, and had not thought to look, because nobody has ever thought to look at a form for the thing it is a form of.

> **THE SECOND PRINTING, FOURTH LINE FROM THE FOOT, WHICH THE FIRST AND THE THIRD DO NOT HAVE AND WHICH IN THE OTHER TWO IS AN EMPTY SPACE WITH A RULE UNDER IT:**
>
> **AND THE KEEPING OF IT IS A DUTY OF THE WARD.**

Eleven words and a full stop, in a district's typeface, in a print shop, on a sheet that is in a case on a run in a series a hundred yards from where she is standing, and that nobody in nine districts knows is there.

That is the whole of it. It is not the sentence. It is a form with a heading and a line for the subject and a line for the burden and a space at the foot, and somebody made it out of a copy of a bound volume, and they had to leave the volume and put down what they could carry, and what they could carry was eleven words, and the words stop in the middle of what they are about, because a form is a form and a form is a summary and a summary is allowed to stop anywhere it likes as long as nothing false is on it. And nothing false is on it. It is a true line. It is the truest line on the whole sheet and it is eleven words long and it is not enough to swear to and it is not enough to build anything out of.

And a form is a form. It was made by a shop filling a space in a docket, out of a copy somebody held, and the copy somebody held is gone, and the desk cannot say whose hands it passed through, because a copy is not a person and this desk holds paper.

What the desk can say, and did say, in answer to her third question, is this, and she wrote it out exactly as it came and then wrote her own answer under it in her own hand, and both are in her own book and one of them is on the face of the form in the series.

> **ANSWER TO THE THIRD QUESTION, AS IT CAME, IN THE DESK'S OWN HAND, ON THE FACE OF THE SAME SHEET:**
>
> **WHICH PRINTING IS LATER, AND HOW DOES THIS DESK KNOW.**
>
> **THE THIRD IS THE LATER. THE DESK KNOWS BY THE PLATE, AND THE PLATE IS SCRATCHED WITH A DATE IN A CORNER, AND THE DESK DOES NOT KNOW WHY THE SECOND HAS A LINE IN IT AND THE OTHER TWO DO NOT, AND IS NOT ASKED, AND THE DESK HOLDS THE THREE.**
>
> **AND THE DESK'S OWN NOTE, WHICH IS NOT A FEE AND NOT AN ORDER: THE THREE ARE NOT COPIES OF ONE ANOTHER. THE SECOND IS COPIED FROM A HAND AND THE FIRST AND THE THIRD ARE COPIED FROM THE FIRST, IN THAT ORDER, AND A THING COPIED FROM A THING COPIED FROM A THING LOSES WHATEVER WAS NOT IN THE PEN. THE DESK HAS NO IDEA WHAT WAS NOT IN THE PEN. THE DESK HAS THE THREE.**

And under it, in her own hand and in her own book, dated the same day, she wrote the answer she had been walking four miles for.

> **THAT IS A REASON AND IT IS THE FIRST REASON THAT DESK HAS GIVEN ME THAT IS ABOUT MY QUESTION RATHER THAN ABOUT ITS OWN JURISDICTION, AND I AM ENTERING THAT I NOTICED THE DIFFERENCE, AND THAT A REASON ABOUT JURISDICTION IS A WALL AND A REASON ABOUT A PEN IS A DOOR, AND THAT THIS DESK HAS NOW OPENED THE DOOR NINELY IN A LINE OF THREE SENTENCES AND THE DOOR IS ABOUT PAPER, AND I AM A PERSON WHO WANTS TO KNOW ABOUT A PERSON, AND I HAVE HAD FOUR MONTHS OF BOTH.**

---

The finding of the eighth of the fifth month is not a recovery and it is not a discovery about a person and it is in her own book with a date on it and in no record but hers, and she wrote it that afternoon in a room over a pump in a district four miles from a bridge, having walked down the fen road with three printings in a band and a bad hand's two days in her legs.

> **THE FINDING OF THE EIGHTH OF THIS MONTH, WHICH IS A FINDING ABOUT A SHELF AND NOT ABOUT A LEAF.**
>
> **THE DESK TOLD ME THREE TIMES WHAT IT IS, AND ON THE THIRD TIME IT TOLD ME WHY, AND THE WHY IS A REASON AND NOT A SILENCE, AND I SAID BEFORE I WENT UP THE STAIR THAT I WOULD STOP ASKING IT ONCE IT DID THAT.**
>
> **SO I AM STOPPING. A SHELF CAN TELL YOU WHAT A RULE SAID. IT CANNOT TELL YOU WHAT A RULE WAS FOR. AND THAT IS THE THIRD TIME IN FOUR MONTHS THAT THE SAME ANSWER HAS COME BACK OUT OF THE SAME KIND OF PLACE. THE FIRST WAS A BENCH OF THREE ELEVEN MILES OFF A FEN ROAD THAT SAID IN ITS OWN HAND THAT IT HOLDS NOTHING A PERSON MAY SWEAR TO. THE SECOND WAS THIS DESK, WHICH SAID TWICE THAT IT CAN PROVE A RULE WAS PRINTED AND CANNOT HOLD A PERSON. THE THIRD IS A ROOM OVER A PUMP FOUR MILES DOWN THIS ROAD WHICH HOLDS A PAGE AND CANNOT SAY WHAT A PAGE IS FOR.**
>
> **AND THE THREE OF THEM ARE NOT THE SAME THING AND THE ANSWER IS THE SAME ANSWER, AND THE ANSWER IS: A PLACE THAT KEEPT PAPER HAS NEVER ONCE BEEN ABLE TO TELL ME WHY ANYBODY WANTED IT.**
>
> **THE HONEST THING TO DO WITH A SHELF IS TO STOP ASKING IT QUESTIONS IT HAS ALREADY ANSWERED. I HAVE ASKED IT FOUR QUESTIONS AND IT HAS ANSWERED ALL FOUR AND THREE OF THEM WERE YES AND ONE OF THEM WAS NO WITH A REASON, AND I AM GOING UP THE STAIR AND DOWN THE STAIR AND I AM NOT ASKING IT AGAIN IN THIS CASE.**

And then she wrote the last part, and the last part is the part that costs, and she wrote it in the same hand and in the same hour and she did not put a line under it.

> **AND HERE IS WHAT I HAVE GIVEN UP AND I AM NOT GOING TO FIND OUT UNTIL FOUR YEARS FROM NOW WHETHER IT WAS WORTH IT: A CLERK OF A CHAMBER IN THIS CITY WROTE IN HER OWN BOOK ON THE SEVENTH OF THE FOURTH MONTH THAT ANYBODY WHO NEEDS A PAGE SWORN CAN NOW NAME THE ONE DESK THAT SAID YES, NOT TO READ FROM IT BUT TO POINT AT IT, AND THAT THE LEVERAGE IS REAL AND IT IS ON A DESK WITH A STAMP, AND THAT IT CANNOT BE USED, AND THAT IN ABOUT A YEAR SOMEBODY IS GOING TO TRY ANYWAY AND THERE IS GOING TO BE A FORM ABOUT IT WITH A WORD AT THE TOP OF IT.**
>
> **SHE WAS RIGHT ON EVERY COUNT, INCLUDING THE LAST ONE, AND I HAVE SEEN THE FORM WITH THE WORD AT THE TOP OF IT, IN NINE DISTRICTS, AND IT COST A MAN OF THIRTY-FOUR FOUR MONTHS AND A ROOF, AND IT IS NOT HER FAULT AND IT IS NOT THE DESK'S FAULT, AND IT IS NOW ON THE COUNTER FOUR MILES FROM THIS ROOM IN A SERIES RUNNING BY PLATE, IN A CASE, ON A SHELF, IN A HAND THAT BELONGS TO AN OFFICE.**
>
> **AND I HAVE JUST SPENT TWO SHILLINGS AND TWO DAYS' SWEEPING FINDING OUT THAT THE DESK WAS RIGHT, AND THE ONE THING IN THIS CITY THAT SAID YES HAS NOW SAID YES AND NO AND A REASON, IN THE SAME HAND, THREE TIMES, AND A PERSON WHO NEEDS A PAGE SWORN CAN NO LONGER EVEN POINT AT IT, BECAUSE A THING THAT HAS BEEN ASKED THREE TIMES IS A THING THAT CANNOT BE ASKED A FOURTH TIME WITHOUT BEING A COMPLAINT.**

---

What is on the counter in that building now, and was on it on the fifth, and will be on it again on any day a person walks in, which is the point of it and the whole of what an office with no person in it is for: a form for lodging a matter in the Quiet Chamber, second printing, in a case, on a run, and nobody in nine districts knows it is there, and it is the only thing anybody has that the leaf has a hand's breadth of company in.

And a man called Corrin Vask wrote two lines in a day book on the fifth and the sixth, in a small hand, in the same column as the sweeping, and the price of the two days is two shillings and two days, and the sweeping of those two days is entered in the same column and is not done and cannot be done by anybody else, and the day book has four men in it and two women and one of the women was here for a fortnight, and the last line of it is the sixth of the fifth month and it is a hand that cannot open on command.

And a form eleven words long is lying on a table in a room over a pump in a district four miles from a bridge, and a recordwright of a public record has read it four times, and she is not going to be able to read a quarter of a sentence off a leaf in a cold room for four days because of it, and it is going to make the four days very much harder, and the reason it is going to make them harder is that the eleven words are the right eleven words and they are going to be sitting in her head telling her what two illegible characters on a leaf are, and there is not one thing in this city that can tell her whether they are the ones she wants them to be.

And a person who goes looking for two particular characters on a leaf after being handed the sentence by a print shop is not reading a shadow. She has known that since the twentieth of the second month in a building with nineteen shelves in it, and she wrote it down then, and she is going to have to write it down again on the ninth with a date on it, and it is going to be the second line in her own book and it is going to be the hardest thing in this case to enter.

---

She went back up the fen road on the eighth with three printings in a band and one of them folded in four because the fourth line from the foot on that one has a crease through the word ward and a crease is not a fact and a fold is a thing she did herself.

At the second bell on the sixth of the fifth month, four miles down that road, in a room over a pump, nine people were in it, and the bench of four sat in the ordinary way, and Corva Silt read out an entry from the district's own book, and it is four lines long and it is in the district's own hand and it is the shortest entry this case has produced.

> **ENTERED IN THE BOOK OF THE BENCH OF FOUR, THE SIXTH OF THE FIFTH MONTH, AT THE SECOND BELL, IN PUBLIC, IN THE ROOM OVER THE PUMP, NINE PEOPLE PRESENT.**
>
> **THE QUESTION ENTERED BY THE HOLDER ON THE TWENTY-NINTH OF LAST MONTH, BEING WHAT A PAGE IS FOR, WHICH WAS ASKED ON A BANK IN FRONT OF ABOUT TWO HUNDRED PEOPLE AND WAS NOT ANSWED, WAS READ OUT IN THIS ROOM TODAY AS IT WAS ENTERED, WITHOUT IMPROVEMENT, AND THE BENCH HAS ENTERED THAT A QUESTION IS A QUESTION AND NOT A MATTER AND THAT IT IS NOT AN APPEAL AND THAT THE BENCH HAS NO POWER TO MAKE IT ANYTHING ELSE.**
>
> **NOBODY ANSWERED IT. THE BENCH HAS ENTERED THAT NOBODY ANSWERED IT, AND THAT IT WILL BE READ OUT AGAIN AT THE SIXTH BELL ON THE FIRST DAY OF THE NEXT QUARTER WITH THE RATE, AND THAT IF IT IS ANSWERED ON ANY DAY BEFORE THEN THE ANSWER WILL BE ENTERED WITH A DATE AND A NAME, AND THAT THE HOLDER MAY NOT APPEAR IN SUPPORT OF IT AND MAY NOT BE NAMED IN IT, AND THAT THE BENCH HAS SAID SO IN ITS BOOK TWICE.**
>
> **THE BENCH'S OWN WORK IS NOT STOPPED BY ANY OF THIS AND IS CARRIED ON AT THE SAME FIGURE, AND THE DISTRICT HAS NO MONEY, AND THE COST OF TODAY'S SITTING WAS NINE PEOPLE'S MORNING AND IS ENTERED AS THE PRICE OF THE SITTING, WHICH IS NOBODY'S, BECAUSE THE BENCH HAS NO FIGURE FOR A MORNING AND WILL NOT MAKE ONE UP.**

Nobody improved it and nobody said anything for about a minute, and then Garvin Slade, who is a salt seller and one of the four and who had not expected to be in a room that day, said, “Then it is a question with a date on it and no answer on it, which is the same as the rate, and I have been carrying a name under the rate for six weeks and I have just worked out that I have been carrying a question under it as well, and I would like the bench to notice that a man who sells salt is now in two of these and has asked for neither.”

Nobody noticed, and he was right, and he has not been thanked.
