## Central pressure

**PLANNED, from `outline/series.md`:** a water and passage charter lets a coastal guild control the only safe route through the Nettle Straits. **DELIVERED — 102, 110, 140, 141, 145, 149.** The licence is a seven-day season at thirty-one shillings that names no days and cannot be refunded on a day the water is on the bar (102), and the water is on the bar on three of the seven (108, 134, 140, 141). A breach in a defence the guild built (140); the guild is right about it and says so in its own mouth (141, 144); and it is in the charter and is not going to be defeated this season (149). **A catastrophe waiting for one bad week, and the bad week came and it was a shore going and not a catastrophe, and nobody on that coast is going to be told otherwise.**

**PLANNED, in this volume's own words:** a harbour where one body decides who may cross water and who may not, and where the people who actually know when the water is safe are not the people who hold the charter and cannot read it. The pressure is not dishonesty. **The guild is right about the strait.** The resistance is a category: a charter says what a passage is and does not say who bears it when the weather is wrong, and a person the charter cannot read is a person the charter does not see. **DELIVERED — 101, 137, 145, 149.** He cannot read and the coast knows it in the first chapter and does not keep it to itself (101). About nine hundred people on this water come to a person and not to anything with more than one door in it, said by the harbourmaster in 137 and again in 145 and again in 149, and the whole of what a body cannot stand behind is worked out in her own mouth in 145.

**PLANNED, the volume's thesis.** **This is Volume 02's shape moved to a different substance.** Volume 02's pressure was *a page is for keeping the record of a duty, and the duty goes to whoever is standing nearest.* Volume 03's pressure is *a crossing is a passage, and the passage is a thing only somebody local can give, and the body that sells it is the only body that cannot navigate.* **Same engine, different coin, different people, and it must not read as Volume 02 with the water turned up.** **DELIVERED, and the two substances are kept apart.** Volume 02's engine was a rate read out by a person who could not appeal from one word of it; this volume's is a rate on a crossing read out by a chain-man with a slate in his pocket and no rule anywhere behind any of its three marks (116, 143), and the sentence that connects them is *a person is not a body* (116, 143, 147). **The rate is a tide and not a bucket, in a different coin, at a thirteen-fold multiple, and it was found on the ground by a man of seventy-one with a pot of paint and not by a narrator.**

## Repetition — the hard constraint, carried forward from the Volume 02 audit

**CANON, and it is a record of Chapters 1–100 as they stand:** six filings that returned bounded reviews or preserved-and-declined answers; five named local arrangements with separately recorded refusals; a great many rooms that refused a clean, general, or universal answer; nine benches answering a question in writing; several public answers that turned out to be prices; a system whose empty rooms are all the same kind of empty; a document that cost more to make than to pay; and a great many benches' own minutes being the only durable thing.
