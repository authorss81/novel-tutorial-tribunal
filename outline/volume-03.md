# Volume 03 Outline — *The Saltglass Coast* (Chapters 101–150)

> **Provenance and status.** This file was created by the Volume 02 close phase, and only after the Volume 02 volume audit **passed** — `reviews/volume-02/volume-audit.md`, which records twelve hard defects inside Chapters 51–100, repaired none of them, and corrected four figures in the state record. The assignments below are copied verbatim from `outline/series.md` and are not this file's to change. **If this file and `outline/series.md` ever disagree, `outline/series.md` wins.** Items marked **CANON** are established by Chapters 1–100 and the state record and may not be contradicted. Items marked **PLANNED** are this volume's design and are not yet canon; they become canon when the chapters deliver them and are re-marked at the volume close.

## Central pressure

**PLANNED, from `outline/series.md`:** a water and passage charter lets a coastal guild control the only safe route through the Nettle Straits.

**PLANNED, in this volume's own words:** a harbour where one body decides who may cross water and who may not, and where the people who actually know when the water is safe are not the people who hold the charter and cannot read it. The pressure is not dishonesty. **The guild is right about the strait.** The resistance is a category: a charter says what a passage is and does not say who bears it when the weather is wrong, and a person the charter cannot read is a person the charter does not see.

**PLANNED, the volume's thesis.** **This is Volume 02's shape moved to a different substance.** Volume 02's pressure was *a page is for keeping the record of a duty, and the duty goes to whoever is standing nearest.* Volume 03's pressure is *a crossing is a passage, and the passage is a thing only somebody local can give, and the body that sells it is the only body that cannot navigate.* **Same engine, different coin, different people, and it must not read as Volume 02 with the water turned up.**

## Beginning state

**CANON.** Ilyan is thirty-one, Stage 2, Seam Reader. His limited standing is restored and confined to the ninth-bridge matter. He is a named carrier in a second district's charter four miles from a bridge, with a duty and a consequence, and past the fourth mile it is a favour that cannot be entered. He has been produced three times. Eight things are in the record he cannot un-enter, the newest being that his name is printed four hundred and sixty-three times in a form in a district eleven miles off, under a word that says a rule is temporary. He holds a spent blackglass token he has used four times; the fourth took his ability to read. He has an afterimage with a four-hour limit that is not a cure, a leg that does not clear of itself, a left hand that is a scar at the base of the thumb in four years, a rendering he has not explained, a vantage that has not moved, and a door in a picture at the same height and the same distance it has been at since the third of the second month. **Corva Silt and no clerk is the only other person who knows he cannot read.** He has no constituency, no party, no bench, and no body.

**PLANNED, from `outline/series.md`:** *Ilyan can argue a case but cannot navigate local maritime custom or read a saltglass record.*

**PLANNED, made concrete.** He has about two hundred guild terms of a bridge-and-weighhouse trade and a finger he can move along a line. He cannot follow a tide table, a customs formula, a song sung for four hours, or a sheet of glass that has been through a fire for a year. **The coast will find out he cannot read in the first three chapters and will not be gentle about it, and the coast's version of the discovery is different from Corva Silt's: nobody in a port town can keep that fact to themselves, because on the water a man who cannot read a glass is a man who cannot be sent.**

## Major factions and locations

**PLANNED. The coast, and no city.** Five named places, each with a person in it who wants something ordinary.

1. **The harbour of Kestrel Reach, on the Nettle Straits.** A guild house, a tide office, a stone quay, a fish market, a customs counter, and about nine hundred people. **PLANNED, the guild officer:** a woman of about fifty who has been harbourmaster's clerk for nineteen years and then harbourmaster for six, who is right about the strait, whose whole life is a thing she has verified daily, and who is going to be the volume's best speaker and not its villain.
2. **A port two Reaches down, poorer, with its own assembly and its own idea of what a passage costs.** **PLANNED:** a ferry family, four generations on the water, three brothers whose accounts of a crossing do not agree.
3. **A saltglass works.** **PLANNED:** a glassmaker, about sixty, and an apprentice in her twenties, and a kiln that takes a person, a fire, and a year.
4. **A crossing above the Reach on a road that does not go through the Straits at all, where people have always found a way round and nobody has ever written it down.** **PLANNED:** this stands in the same *position* in this volume's design as Volume 02's Bridgeward — a way through that works and that nobody has put in writing — and it is in this outline so the batch planner can see it coming. **It is not a repetition of the Bridgeward and must not be staged as one**, and per §Volume guardrails nothing on the coast may be offered as the answer to that question.
5. **The guild's own house, and a room in it with a chart nobody outside may see.** **PLANNED:** the guild is not hiding anything criminal; the chart is a working record of a moving thing and it is the only copy.

**PLANNED. The factions, none of them a villain.**

- **The water and passage guild:** not a criminal act and not a catastrophe yet. **A guild controlling the only safe route is not an abuse; it is a catastrophe waiting for one bad week.** PLANNED, the volume's structural sentence.
- **The two port communities**, one of which is asked to be a party in a matter about a strait and says no. **PLANNED: the refusal must be correct, recorded in full, and cost somebody.**
- **The ferry families**, for whom a crossing is a job and a passage is a thing a person gives away.
- **The glassworks**, for whom a record is a process and the process takes a year.
- **The Bench's reach, which is eleven miles of road and a clerk with a tray, and which has no power over any of this.**

**PLANNED, non-negotiable:** nothing on this coast may be made into Volume 02's world again. No preservation office, no counting-house, no plate, no docket board, no ninth bridge, no fen road, no cut banks, no basalt block.

## Escalation steps

**PLANNED.** Five movements of ten chapters each, ending in the volume's climax and resolution.

**101–110 — Batch 0001: the coast, and the first thing that is not a room.** Open on the ground in weather with ordinary people doing ordinary work, and **a person with a want who tells one of them something about another person that neither can act on.** The volume's first honest problem is on the page in chapter one: the coast keeps its charters in a record neither of them can read, and a man who argues cannot navigate. **The geography of the Straits is not explained in narration.** Let a working person explain the safe route by telling them which day it is not safe, and let that be the whole of the geography for three chapters. The guild is introduced as right. The batch's spine is **putting a matter in two places**, which means asking two communities to be in the same matter, and one of them refuses. **The midpoint reversal is reserved for Batch 0002 and must not appear in this batch's first third.**

**111–120 — Batch 0002: two communities, and the first pattern.** The first refusal of consent becomes a fact on the ground with a name attached. The songs and the toll marks go into the record as marks, and somebody local sees a pattern in them and is afraid of it. **The midpoint reversal lands here or in the first half of Batch 0003 and never before:** the coast's songs and toll marks contain a pattern resembling the hidden map in *The Sovereign's Wake*. **PLANNED, and this is the rule:** no System panel may confirm the pattern is a map, and nobody may name *The Sovereign's Wake* in dialogue as anything but a thing a man from a city once played. Ilyan's only contribution is asking what the marks are for; the answer is that they are for counting.

**121–130 — Batch 0003: the carrier's problem, in salt and water.** A carried account changes as it is carried. A false merge harms people who never met. The glassmaker is right about what a saltglass record is and wrong about what it is for, and a record is not an instrument and cannot be produced by a person who does not hold it. **A saltglass record is worse: it is made by a process, and the process takes a person, a fire, and a year.** The batch finds what one costs to make, and the cost is a person's work and a person's back, paid in advance by somebody who has already paid it. **This is the batch in which Ilyan is produced a fourth time, in a place where the mechanism of production is not a Bench and not a court and is right.**

**131–140 — Batch 0004: the appeal, used by somebody who cannot use it, and the breach.** On the coast there is a thing a person may appeal and a person who may be appealed to, and **the difference is a harbour custom nobody has written down.** A person tries to use the road they have and finds the road is a song, and a song is not a document. **PLANNED: this is a different failure from Volume 02's and must not be the same failure — a person may be refused a hearing by a person who is not a bench and is not a court and is right.** The tidal breach opens at the end of this batch.

**141–150 — Batch 0005: the breach, the link, and the charter.** A tidal breach forces Ilyan to **link a ferry family, a glassmaker and a guild officer rather than attack a monster.** He cannot do it alone and he cannot compel any of the three. The resolution is a **revocable** passage charter and the System recognising Nadia's old test identifier in a Veyran record. The volume closes on the new question.

## Midpoint reversal

**CANON, verbatim from `outline/series.md`:** *The coast's songs and toll marks contain a pattern that resembles the hidden map in *The Sovereign's Wake*.*

**PLANNED placement: Batch 0002's movement, and at the earliest the second half of Batch 0001's successor. It may not land in Chapters 101–110 at all, and it may not land in the first third of the batch that carries it.** This is the audit's and the phase prompt's requirement and it is a placement rule, not a content rule.

**PLANNED, how it is staged.** It is put on the page by somebody who is not Ilyan — a ferryman, a glassmaker's apprentice, a woman with a table of tides — and that person is **afraid of it before Ilyan sees it.** Ilyan's contribution is one question: *what are the marks for.* The answer is *for counting.* **Nothing is confirmed by anything. The System does not say it is a map. No one says it is a map. The recognition, when it comes, is four batches later and it is the System, not a person.**

## Climax

**CANON, verbatim from `outline/series.md`:** *A tidal breach forces Ilyan to link a ferry family, a glassmaker and a guild officer rather than attack a monster.*

**PLANNED.** There is no monster and there is nothing to attack. A tidal breach through a defence the guild built, on a stretch of water where the guild is right about the danger and the three people who can act are a family, a workshop and an office. **The climax is a linking problem and not a fight.** Ilyan's power progression for this volume is **Witness Broker**, whose stage definition is *links cases and invites consenting witnesses; becomes responsible for their safety and for separating their accounts* — so the climax is the first time in the series that his authority is exercised over other people's accounts, and it is exercised by **asking three people for permission and being refused by one of them at first.**

**PLANNED, and this is the guardrail:** he does not attack anything, does not become a hero of the water, does not save the coast, and does not get a party, a bench, a body, or a remedy he chose alone. The guild officer's life is at stake in the scene and she is the one who has to be persuaded, and she is persuaded by an argument about her own knowledge.

## Resolution

**CANON, verbatim from `outline/series.md`:** *the coast gains a revocable passage charter, but the System recognizes Nadia's old test identifier in a Veyran record.*

**PLANNED.** The charter is **revocable** and the revocation must cost somebody to exercise, or it is a licence and not a charter. The three who linked it are named in it and so is the burden, and the burden falls on a person and not on a harbour. **And then the System recognises Nadia's old test identifier in a Veyran record, and it is the first time anything in this series has recognised anything from Earth, and it is not answered in this volume.**

## The new question that motivates Volume 04

**CANON, verbatim from `outline/series.md`:** *If the game was a reflection, who made the reflection understand refusal?*

**PLANNED, how it is earned and how it is not answered.** A record on the coast, four hundred years old or made last year, carries a structure that is a translation of something, and the thing it translates has a marker in it that belongs to a tester signature from Earth that belongs to a woman named Nadia. **The System recognises it and says nothing about what it means.** The question is asked on the page by an ordinary person and it is not answered by anybody, and it is not answered by the System either, and **nobody in the volume says the word prison.**

## Emotional resolution

**PLANNED.** Ilyan's arc in this volume is that **being the man who links people is a burden and not a competence**, and that the thing he is worst at — asking people for their accounts and then being responsible for keeping them apart — is the thing the volume makes him do three times. **The cost is a relationship and not a body.** He must end the volume having been refused by somebody he was trying to help, and having not repaired it, and having written that down. **PLANNED: the volume's cost to him is that three people's accounts are now in a record with his name at the bottom of it, and the record can be got at, and he knows that, and the finding is that the broker's fee is that somebody can send for the broker's own paper.**

**And a relationship that pays.** Ilyan and Sera Quill: **CANON, professionally close and personally unaddressed, no romance beat in a hundred chapters and none planned for this one.** PLANNED for this volume: **she is not on the coast and does not come, and that is deliberate, and the reason is a licence and not a choice** — she is a recordwright of a public record and there is nothing on that coast for a public record to be sworn in, and she says so in a room before he leaves, and he goes anyway. **PLANNED: what she sends him is one sheet, and it is a question, and it is the reason he understands the refusal of consent in the first place.** Neither improves the other.

**Ansa Rell.** **CANON: out-voted, undefeated, not redeemed, a difficult ally, and Volume 03 may use her and must not redeem her.** PLANNED: she is not on the coast, she writes once, and what she writes is correct and is about money and is refused.

**Neris Ro and Tamsin Ro.** **CANON: Tamsin is not to be called and nobody is to look for her. Neris is not to be asked for anything, her right to stop everything herself stands including from Ilyan, and her reason is not to be repeated in public.**

## Power progression

**CANON, verbatim from `outline/series.md`:** *Witness Broker; the first multi-site case requires consent from rival port communities.*

**PLANNED and load-bearing.**

- **Stage 2 is not exceeded in Batch 0001.** Ilyan arrives as Stage 2 and is Stage 2 at the end of Chapter 110. **The afterimage, the leg, the hand, the rendering, the vantage, the door and the token are all exactly where Volume 02 left them and none of them is advanced in this batch.**
- **Stage 3, Witness Broker, is arrived in this volume by a practice, a failure, a relationship and a permanent cost — the same pattern as Stage 2, and it is arrived late in the volume, not in Batch 0001.** Its costs: he becomes responsible for the safety of people he has linked, and for **separating their accounts**, and the permanent cost is a permanent liability on his own record. **It is not a new power in the sense of a new thing he can do to the world; it is a new duty with a consequence, exactly as the carrier's clause was.**
- **The first multi-site case requires consent from rival port communities** and the consent is refused once, correctly, at the batch's spine.

## Relationship milestones planned for this volume

**PLANNED, all four, and none of them is canon until a chapter delivers it.** The CANON each one starts from is stated in its own line.

- **Ilyan and the guild officer:** adversarial colleagues who are both right, and who end the volume able to disagree in public and keep working. **No trust restored, no romance, no rescue.**
- **Ilyan and the ferry family's three brothers:** the first time he has to choose between a truthful account and a usable one, and the first time he carries somebody else's words and changes them by carrying them.
- **Ilyan and the glassmaker:** the first person on the coast who is *right* about his own craft and *wrong* about what it is for, and the mirror of Corva Silt, except that Corva Silt was the mechanism and this one is not.
- **Ilyan and the woman with the table of tides:** the coast's counterpart to Wenna Tace, **and this is the largest repetition risk in the volume.** See §Repetition.

## Volume guardrails

**Status of this block, stated once: the guardrails are PLANNED; the facts they cite out of Chapters 1–100 are CANON and are repeated here only so each guardrail can be checked against them.**

- **Do not restore the historical right of refusal. Do not advance the authorship question or the Shale Mirror.** The fragment is ten words with a crease in them, entered on the twelfth of the fifth month, on a facing leaf, in a building with no name on the door four miles from a court. It is a quarter of a sentence and it is not the clause. The C—2 chain, the print run, the plate, the older witness index, the fifth mark in the guild's count book, and the standing authority of preservation are exactly where Volume 02 left them. **The continuity line has been refused seven times in the same four words, nobody is named, and the holder has not read it.**
- **Do not answer the Bridgeward.** It is one step further along and no further, and its closing question is about a **person** on a form nobody has to answer to. **Nothing on the coast may be offered as the answer to it, and Chapter 100 may not be written again.**
- **Do not introduce a new final enemy.** The First Witness remains the distant institutional voice seeded in Volume 01 and is not named. **The antagonist for this volume is a category that cannot say who is in it — a rule that only one body can apply and only local people can survive — and it has no villain. The guild is right about the strait.**
- **Do not give Ilyan a party, a bench, a body, or a remedy he chose alone.** His standing is confined to the ninth-bridge matter. **The new authority is a duty with a consequence and not an office.**
- **Do not make the System omniscient on the coast.** It translates formal records, notices, and hearing-field boundaries entered into his open case. **It does not translate a song, a tide table, a harbour custom, or a saltglass record, and the volume's inability to navigate is a language limit before it is anything else.**
- **Do not name a weekday.** The guardrail that begins at Chapter 41 has held through Chapter 100 and holds here.
- **Do not name a month the coast has not earned.** The coast's own calendar is tide-based: a seven-day tide, bells, and the turn. **The narrator may date by tide and bell and by the ship's calendar the working people use, and by no other system.**
- **Preserve ordinary scale.** Every promise in this volume is to be carried by a ferryman, a glassmaker's apprentice, a woman with a table of tides, a harbourmaster's clerk of fifty, a woman who has walked a beach for eleven years, and a boy who runs a line. **Nobody on this coast may be a wizard, a chosen one, a smuggler with a title, or a person who explains the strait.**

## Repetition — the hard constraint, carried forward from the Volume 02 audit

**CANON, and it is a record of Chapters 1–100 as they stand:** six filings that returned bounded reviews or preserved-and-declined answers; five named local arrangements with separately recorded refusals; a great many rooms that refused a clean, general, or universal answer; nine benches answering a question in writing; several public answers that turned out to be prices; a system whose empty rooms are all the same kind of empty; a document that cost more to make than to pay; and a great many benches' own minutes being the only durable thing.

**PLANNED, the rule: Volume 03 may not write** a seventh local arrangement, another bounded interval, another room that declines to say anything, another round of written answers from a set of benches, another bench minute as the hero of a chapter, another public answer that turns out to be a price, or another fragment recovered from a facing leaf.

**PLANNED, the rule: Volume 03 may not re-stage:** a child asked to testify; a private account filed without consent; a marker in a working record that nobody can account for; a preservation office confronted; a plate and an index compared; a foot-gate that opens the wrong way; a barrow that is a load; a handbell attached to nothing; a bell on a rope over a wheel on a pin; a burden assigned to nobody; a keeper saying he does not read a clause; the toll bar; the facing leaf; the four lines off the ninth bridge's frame.

**PLANNED, the rule:** a marked echo of a canonical sentence by a different person is permitted. A re-staging of the same beat is not.

**PLANNED, the two largest specific risks in this volume, named so the batch planner cannot lose them:**

1. **PLANNED — a rate on a crossing.** Volume 02's spine was a rate on water and this volume is meant to make it a *tide* instead of a *bucket*, in a different coin, at a multiple on the poorest households. **That is a deliberate re-use of the shape and it is legal. It is illegal to repeat the washer, the bucket, the farthing, the barrel lid, the called reading, the eight-times figure, the man who sells salt, or a woman who walks a long way to avoid a rate.** The rate must be found on the ground by a person who has a want, and it must fall at a multiple on the poorest households, and **it must be paid by somebody whose reason for paying is not the one in Volume 02.**
2. **PLANNED — a person with a table who is the door and cannot appeal.** Wenna Tace is the reader of her district, the holder of the fifth term, the person a rate is read out by, and the person nobody may name in a support. **A woman with a table of tides would be a re-staging of her and is forbidden.** If the coast needs a person who holds a record and cannot use it, **that person must be a man, and he must be the glassmaker, and the reason he cannot use it is that his record cannot be produced at all**, which is a different failure with a different shape.

**PLANNED, and the one scene that may never be written again:** Chapter 100. A washer of sixty-one who has walked four miles for eighty-six days, asked a question by a person with no right to ask it, answering *she did not tell the district because the district does not know.* **It may not be restaged, it may not be used as a precedent, and nothing on the coast may be answered by it.**
