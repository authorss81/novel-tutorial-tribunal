**A FORM IS THE FIRST INSTRUMENT IN FIFTEEN VOLUMES WITH NOWHERE TO PUT NO.** Every other one had somewhere: a shut door, a wall you could rub out, a chair you could get up out of, a door that stops when the job stops. **A printed sheet with columns in it has two columns — a name and a day — and it has nine lines, and not one of the nine lines has anywhere to put a person stopping.** And a form is a form not because of what is on it but because of what it is asked: **it is asked the same question every time, by anybody, and it gives the same answer back, and it does not know who it asked.**
