- **Starting state.** Ilyan's public name is recognised and he has no Veyran birth record. **`series.md` says this and the handoff is silent on it, so the planner has read it off the outline and NOT off the state files, and the state files do not say it anywhere. They are separated by four hundred miles and by the fact that neither of them can be sent for, and this volume does not close that distance: Sera Quill does not appear in Chapters 501–510, and her absence in this volume's first band is a decision and not an oversight.**
- **Midpoint.** Restoring one witness's memory causes two communities to remember incompatible histories; truth becomes a civic burden rather than a simple answer. **PLACEMENT RULE, carried from the Volume 10 review: the midpoint may not land in Chapters 501–510 or 511–520 at all, and it may not land in the first third of the batch that carries it. Batches 0003 and 0004 only, and if the band count changes the midpoint moves to the middle of the second half of whichever band carries it.**
- **Power progression.** Co-Witness expands into a distributed witness network. **THE STAGE DOES NOT ADVANCE AND THE OUTLINE SAYS SO.** The line is depth and not a new panel: no single person can preserve all records, which is a finding and not a power.
- **Climax and resolution.** Ilyan restores the first removed witness and exposes a list of the original Compact's missing guarantees. The names return to the public record and Ilyan learns that he is legally classified as an external witness. **New question: *can an external person become a citizen without being absorbed into the world's preferred story?***

**No new final enemy may be introduced.** Nobody in this volume is a villain. The office is nine miles off and correct. The Returned Name Keepers are a pressure faction with an internal split and are not a unified enemy. The Archive Lords are a custody faction and not the final enemy and are not introduced until Band 4. **The First Witness is not named, not addressed and not answered, and the standing question of what it signed stays shut.** The household that was struck out of Bramblefold's book a hundred and forty-one years ago was not a villain: it gave the answer that was asked of it, and the answer was used.

## 3. The place, and why it is here

**BRAMBLEFOLD.** About two thousand four hundred people on the chalk slope at the head of a river, in the county of Aldwick, in a country district, **not upon a road of the Shelf, with no register, no counter, no appointed person and no Bench of any kind; the nearest counter is thirty-one miles off at Saltcaster on a road.** A market on a **Thursday**; a lime kiln burning about nine days in ten; a smithy; a chandler's; a saddler's shop; about eleven streets; a meeting-house with a chancel used as a strong room, **because the chancel is the only locked room in the building and the book is in it.** A fold of about **sixty households** on **a hundred and forty acres** of down. A common orchard of about **eleven acres** and about **nine hundred trees**, walled, with a frost pocket in the lower corner where the fruit is a fortnight later than in the rest of it.

**The Descent Book.** Bound in calf, four hundred years, four hands. **It is the oldest working instrument in this part of the country and it is not a covenant and it does not bind anybody: it is a roll of descent, and three things in this town go with a descent — the right to the fold, the holding called Lower Halfpenny, and a place in the burial ground behind the meeting-house.**

**The leaf.** A hundred and forty-one years back a leaf was struck through in a single stroke from top to bottom, and the head of it says **struck by order**, and **no order is named on it**, and there are **forty-three names** on that leaf. The household on it was the household of Kiln Row, which gave an answer to a question put to the whole county by an office eleven miles off at Aldwick, and the answer was used, and the line was struck. **The household was not lying. It gave the answer that was asked of it.**

**THE FINDING OF THIS VOLUME'S FIRST BAND, and it is the whole engine: THE BOOK HAS NO COLUMN FOR DEATH.** It has four hundred years of descents and no place to put a person who has died, so a man who died two years ago is still carrying his fold and his holding and has a place in the burial ground reserved; and the one removal in four hundred years is a leaf, and a leaf is the only kind of removal anybody in this town has ever seen. **Restoring the leaf therefore does not restore one name. It restores forty-three, and it restores every descendant, and it puts back a row at the top of the orchard and about forty households' half of a hundred and forty acres, and nobody in that town can separate the name from the ground.** That is the volume and it is not a bug.

**A new place gets a new distance and the distance goes into `state/continuity.md` on the day it is invented. ALL THE DISTANCES IN VOLUME 11:** Halloway is **two days from Bramblefold** — a day on foot to **Coldcombe**, a market town twenty-one miles off, and a cart the second day as far as **the Four Gates**, a toll bar four miles short of Bramblefold, and the last four miles on foot; **Aldwick is nine miles**; **Saltcaster is thirty-one miles**; **Ostrey is four days' cart east and the cart road does not come near this town.** **The Four Gates took a penny at each of four gates ninety years back and the toll was taken off and the list of the four gates is in the town's charter and nobody in that town can name which four.** Lane and wall measurements are given in the chapters and carry no reserved number.

**Non-negotiable. Nothing in this volume may be made into Halloway again.** No hearth, no beam, no board nine feet up over a fire, no water board, no sluice, no mill, no stone room with a door open, no common four hundred acres. **BRAMBLEFOLD IS A CHALK TOWN WITH A MEETING-HOUSE, A CHANCEL, A LIME KILN, A WALL, A FOLD AND AN ORCHARD, AND THE ONLY INSTRUMENT IN IT IS A BOOK THAT NOBODY CAN BE SENT FOR.** And **Volume 11 may not use the Compact of Five Towns as a remedy, may not offer anything on a bar road as the answer to anything, and may not use Chapter 500 as a precedent.**

## 5. The five bands

1. **501–510 — the page that was struck, and a town that counts from it.** Two days' road, a book with no column for death, forty-three names on a leaf with no order named against them, a fold and an orchard and a wall, and a claim entered and a page not struck back.
2. **511–520 — the keepers, and the price of a name.** Returned Name Keepers and the split inside them; property and kinship challenged in two rooms at once; the Archive Lords heard of for the first time and not met.
3. **521–530 — two towns that remember incompatibly. THE MIDPOINT.** Restoring one witness's memory causes two communities to remember incompatible histories, and truth becomes a civic burden rather than a simple answer. **The placement rule applies: not in the first third of the band that carries it.**
4. **531–540 — custody.** The Archive Lords, the public archive, and the finding that evidence can stay public without becoming a new ruling class. The distributed witness network begins, and no single person can hold it.
5. **541–550 — the first removed witness, and the list of what is missing.** The volume's climax and resolution. A name is restored and a list of the original Compact's missing guarantees is exposed. **Ilyan learns that he is legally classified as an external witness.**

**Guardrails for every band.** No month. No new final enemy. **Nobody relieved, forgiven, redeemed or thanked, and the rule holds across a chapter break, not just inside a chapter.** The First Witness is not named, not addressed and not answered. **The right of refusal stays unrestored and nobody in Bramblefold is given it.** At most one System panel in a band. **Ilyan is named in every chapter and has a want, a mistake and a cost of his own in every chapter, including the ones where he is right.** A chapter may not end on the same inventory as its neighbour. **One clock per date line and one anchor; the ordinal comes off the table.** **Titles are written LAST, against the body, and a number in a title is checked the way a date line is checked.**
