# Volume 09 — The Regent's Exception (Chapters 401–450)

**Status.** Chapters 401–430 are canon (batches 0001, 0002, 0003). Chapters 431–450 are planned here and unwritten.
**Authority order.** `outline/series.md:197` outranks `outline/ending.md`, which outranks this file, which outranks every state file and every prompt.
**The volume's five lines, fixed at the outline stage and not to be moved by a band:**

- **Central pressure.** Regent Iven Marrow offers Ilyan authority, wealth, and a return route to Earth if he backs a centralized continuity government.
- **Starting state.** Ilyan can draft remedies but cannot safely oppose a government that controls the roads.
- **Midpoint.** The return route is real, and Ilyan's test of it costs a living co-witness her anchor. **SPENT, in chapters 421–430.**
- **Power progression.** Remedy Drafter becomes Co-Witness. Ilyan shares power he cannot revoke. **SPENT, in chapter 427.**
- **Climax and resolution.** In a succession coup Ilyan refuses Marrow's authority and uses a draft to let districts choose their own representatives. Marrow is defeated, and a continuity declaration signed by the First Witness remains in force. **NOT SPENT. Chapters 441–450.**

**No new final enemy may be introduced.** Marrow is right about the roads and wrong about the remedy, and he is not a villain. The First Witness is not named in this volume and is not a person who speaks, answers, or is appealed to here.

# BATCH 0004 — CHAPTERS 431 TO 440, *WHAT THE OFFICE SENDS BACK*

**Shape of the band: consequence.** The midpoint is spent. This band is the office's replies arriving one at a time, each of them correct, and each reply landing on one person who cannot refuse. The band ends with a writ of succession in the tray, which is the volume's last turn.

### 431 — *The roll comes down the hill in oilcloth and nobody is at the cart*
Three weeks after it went up, in about twenty-one days. The cart comes into the yard at the low end of Tallowgate at the second hour with the oilcloth on the tail-board, re-tied, and the boy of sixteen is the only person at that hour. **Goal:** get eleven pages of somebody's work back into her own hands. **Resistance:** the office returned it in the ordinary way and did not have to open it, and it has been opened, and the knot is not the knot it went up with. **Change:** Fenna Rusk is not in the yard and does not come, and what Sef brings up the row is a roll that nobody can certify, including the woman who made it.

### 432 — *A man who counts halls says no in a market instead of a room, and the office writes his refusal into a line*
The count form comes back with a line against this city. Skell has the doors, nine hundred, counted twice, and no persons. **Goal:** refuse in a way that cannot be written down. **Resistance:** a refusal in a market of about two hundred people is a refusal that thirty people can remember and repeat, and the office is asking for it in writing. **Change:** his refusal is now the third line of an office document with his ward in it, and he has traded an unwritten no for a legible one.

### 433 — *A return comes back stamped against a man who is walking, and the office has named a person about a road for the first time*
Kerwin Dace's return, gone out in `422`, comes back through another city with a stamp. The fifth row has been entered against a named traveller, and the traveller is Tarin Keel. **Goal:** find out whether a man who refused conveyance can be entered anyway. **Resistance:** the entry is correct; Keel did walk out of a city on the office's road without the office's charge, and the row says a person so conveyed is not to be asked about the city afterwards. **Change:** the first time in this volume the office has put a person into a document instead of a thing, and the person is not in this city and cannot be told.

### 434 — *The office answers the co-witness: it cannot remove a name it did not enter*
A sheet comes down reading that a person not of this city cannot be entered in a register of this city and cannot be struck out of one. **Goal:** find out what the office thinks two names in one line are. **Resistance:** the answer is that the column has room for a person and a thing, and both names are already in it, and the office has declined to say which is which. **Change:** the office now knows the exact shape of what Ilyan did in the mud and cannot take it off the page, and Ilyan can no longer describe it to a room as a thing he did.

### 435 — *A keeper's box goes up on the north side of the ninth marker, and the person who cannot say no is asked to sit in it*
The office posts a box for a person to keep at the crossing, at the fifth row's rate, and the form has one line for a name of a person of this place. **Goal:** Hessa Marl refuses the box. **Resistance:** she cannot say no, and the rate is a farthing a visit, and the box is warm and has a roof. **Change:** she takes it, and what she does inside it — counting what crosses, on a slate, at the hour a cart comes in — becomes the only record of that road that is not in an office, and the office now has a person at its own bar.

### 436 — *Four pounds and four shillings, and the only way to be paid is to be a register*
An answer to the sum. **Goal:** Fenna Rusk gets the money. **Resistance:** the money is payable to a person of this place entered in a register, and being entered is the thing that took her anchor. **Change:** she refuses the money in front of nine people and the refusal is read back to her in a book four hundred miles off, and the four pounds and four shillings are still unpaid and the office has again said in writing that it notes the sum.

### 437 — *Somebody is sent for, and it is a boy of sixteen, and the summons has no head on it*
The first time in this city that anybody has been sent for. **Goal:** the boy walks in under his own power and gets out. **Resistance:** the form is a summons with no head and no line for a person who comes without an adult, and what is asked of him is a question about a road he was told about, and answering it costs the man who told him. **Change:** he answers in a way that keeps both, and for the first time in the volume he is the one who decides, and it is worse than being a doorway.

### 438 — *A man writes his own return and does not send it*
The one chapter in the band that is Ilyan alone with the cracked steel pen and four lines in a margin. **Goal:** write a return of his own, on the form, with a name at the top. **Resistance:** every line he writes is a person in this city who will be asked about him, and he has spent nine days learning what that costs. **Change:** he writes it, dates it, and does not send it, and says out loud in a room why, and the room does not understand him and he does not explain twice.

### 439 — *Eleven people put pennies in a bowl for a bell four hundred miles off, and the bowl is a line in a book*
Bess Low's thread pays its first instalment and it is not enough. **Goal:** the bell gets hung. **Resistance:** the money is eleven pence, the bell is four pounds, and the gift is now a line in a book with eleven names against it. **Change:** Bess Low refuses to be one of the eleven and the line is short by one, and she reads the shortfall out loud in a room.

### 440 — *A writ of succession comes down the hill in the tray, and it is about the roads*
The band closes. **Goal:** somebody in this city works out what a writ of succession is. **Resistance:** it is four lines and every one of them is correct, and the third line says that the roads pass with the succession. **Change:** the question of the volume stops being a bar on a road and becomes who holds the roads, and Ilyan is asked, in a room, by name, to say which of two of his own sentences he is standing behind.

# BATCH 0005 AND THE VOLUME CLOSE — CHAPTERS 441 TO 450, *THE REGENT'S EXCEPTION*

**Shape: the volume's turn and its two payoffs.** The succession coup occupies 445–447. The refusal is 446. The plural draft is 447. The close is 449–450, and it must close this volume's own promise — a bar taken down, or not taken down, in a way the reader can see — while opening the First Witness question that the series has been holding since Volume 08.

### 441 — *The writ is read out, and the Regent's exception is put in writing*
Four lines, all correct, and a fifth sheet that is not about roads at all. **Goal:** understand what is being offered. **Resistance:** the offer is generous, specific, in writing, and includes the thing Ilyan has wanted for eight volumes. **Change:** he cannot answer in the room and says so.

### 442 — *The office asks every ward for a representative, and the first ward sends the wrong kind of person*
A ward sends its hall-counter because that is the man it knows. **Goal:** Ilyan sees the shape of what he is about to propose. **Resistance:** a representative chosen because he can count is a representative chosen for his usefulness. **Change:** he has to write a form that asks a ward for a person rather than a function, and nobody in the room can say how a form asks for a person.

### 443 — *He drafts it, and the burden line names him*
The draft exists and costs. **Goal:** a form that lets a ward name its own representative. **Resistance:** the burden of checking a name the office does not recognise falls on the person next to the person named, and the only person in this city who can be next to Ilyan is Fenna Rusk. **Change:** the price he named in the mud in `427` is written into a draft in his own hand, in a room, where it can be read.

### 444 — *The price comes due in the middle of a week, and he cannot pay it back*
Every ward that answers puts a person beside his name, and each one is a person the office can now ask about him. **Goal:** keep the draft. **Resistance:** four wards answer and one is Fenna Rusk's, and she is asked in a room four hundred miles off who put him there. **Change:** she answers, and he is in the room, and it is the first time the cost he named has been paid by somebody else in front of him.

### 445 — *On the night, the hurdles are the only thing that matters*
The coup is fought over a road with a bar on one side. **Goal:** keep the crossing open. **Resistance:** four hurdles, a chain, and about eleven people, and a cart on each side. **Change:** what holds the road is not the draft and not the office; it is a barrow that goes up the south side every morning.

### 446 — *He refuses the authority, in the terms of the offer, out loud*
The volume's climax is a refusal, not a victory. **Goal:** say no to Marrow in words the offer itself uses. **Resistance:** accepting is defensible, and a frightened room will believe him to be a man who has cost them a road home. **Change:** Marrow is right that somebody has to hold the roads, and Ilyan cannot answer that, and he says so.

### 447 — *The wards choose, and the count is not what anybody expected*
The draft is used. **Goal:** a plural answer rather than a single authority. **Resistance:** some wards do not answer, some answer badly, and the office counts the answers and calls them a settlement. **Change:** the result stands and is not tidy, and the man who wrote the form cannot be removed from it and is not thanked.

### 448 — *Marrow is defeated, and the terms of the defeat are read out in a room*
The succession settles. **Goal:** get the terms in a sentence that a room can hold. **Resistance:** the terms are a compromise with a road administration that still exists. **Change:** Ilyan's standing in this city is settled and it is not what he wanted, and nobody is relieved.

### 449 — *A continuity declaration signed by the First Witness is read out in this city*
The volume's new question, on the record, in a room, with about thirty people. **Goal:** get it read. **Resistance:** the declaration is correct, and it governs people who are not in the room, and about a hundred and forty of them are dead. **Change:** the question of the volume closes as a question: what did the First Witness sign, and which people are still governed by that signature.

### 450 — *What the road looks like in the morning*
The close. **Goal:** a last image of a road, a bar, a slate and two people who cannot take each other off a line. **Resistance:** the south side still has no name in any office document. **Change:** the co-witness line is still in a book four hundred miles off, and the woman with the lamp can no longer say whether a roll is right, and the man beside her name cannot pay that back and has stopped trying to, and the fever did not stop for any of it.
