## Central pressure

**CANON, verbatim:** *Earthquakes and contradictory deeds in the Red Basin make it impossible for residents to prove which land charter governs them.*

**PLANNED, in the basin's own words, and it is the sentence every chapter in this volume has to be able to survive: there are two first days of the year in this basin and a piece of paper cannot tell you which one it was written in.**

**PLANNED, the mechanism, and it is mechanical and not mysterious. The basin has two calendars and no conversion between them.** Every deed drawn before the second shaking is dated in the **salt-pan year**, fifty saltings, kept by the people who scrape salt. Every deed drawn after the second shaking is dated in **turns since the second shaking**, and the turns were counted by whoever happened to be keeping the water-book, and the first of those was counted wrong by a day and corrected, and the correction is in a different hand. **So a boundary is not in dispute because anybody lied. A boundary is in dispute because two pieces of paper say the same words about the same strip of ground and were written eight hundred turns apart in two systems, and there is no office in Veyra that can convert one into the other, and the reason there is no such office is that nobody has ever needed one until the ground started moving.** The Bench's own slogan is *no burden without a name*; the basin's is older and plainer and it is said by salt-pan men: **a word is only true until the ground moves.**

**PLANNED, the villain, and there isn't one.** The opposition in this volume is a **price list and a distance**: a matter carried to the chamber of plenty at Orison and brought back costs **five pounds and six shillings**, and a woman who keeps a sluice is paid **nine shillings a week for sixteen weeks a year**, and a man who works at the pit is paid **ten shillings a week for fifty weeks**, and there is nobody in the Red Basin who can spare either the money or the nine days. **The people who keep this volume's disputes from being settled are not villains.
