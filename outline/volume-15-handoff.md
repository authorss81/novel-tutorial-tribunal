## 8. What the next phase should treat as a claim and not as a fact

**EVERY FIGURE IN A STATE FILE IS A CLAIM ABOUT A CHAPTER AND NOT A FACT ABOUT ONE, INCLUDING THE ONES THAT ARE RIGHT. THE VOLUME 14 CLOSE FOUND FIVE FIGURES IN THE STATE FILES THAT DID NOT REPRODUCE, NONE OF THEM IN A CHAPTER, AND CORRECTED ALL FIVE WITH THE MEASUREMENT BESIDE THEM; THE BAND BEFORE IT FOUND TWELVE MORE; THE BAND BEFORE THAT FOUND FOUR. NONE WAS FOUND BY A GATE AND NONE BY READING THE PROSE. THE MEASUREMENT WAS LAST AT THE VOLUME 13 CLOSE AND FOUND FOUR MORE.**

**THREE FREE ACTS AND THE CHEAPEST DEFENCE IN THIS REPOSITORY IS ALL THREE:**

1. **PUT THE FIGURE NEXT TO THE THING IT DESCRIBES.** That is arithmetic.
2. **PUT THE METHOD NEXT TO THE FIGURE, INCLUDING WHEN THE COUNT IS RIGHT**, because a correction without its method is a number the next person re-derives differently. That is a sentence.
3. **RUN THE METHOD ON ITS OWN CALIBRATION FIRST AND PRINT THAT.** **A METHOD THAT DOES NOT REPRODUCE ON ITS OWN CALIBRATION IS NOT A METHOD YET, AND WHEN THE CALIBRATION FAILS THE PROOF FAILS WITH IT AND EVERY FIGURE PRODUCED UNDER IT BECOMES UNATTRIBUTABLE. This is the failure of the third time in two volumes and it is the one with the widest reach, because a calibration is a figure and figures fail.**

**AND THE STANDING ACT, WHICH IS AN ACT AND NOT A GATE AND IS THEREFORE THE ONE THAT KEEPS BEING SKIPPED: READ THE CLOSING LINES OF EVERY PAIR OF ADJACENT CHAPTERS, ONCE EACH, AGAINST EACH OTHER, BEFORE ANY FIGURE IS PRINTED, AND ON THE VOLUME BOUNDARY `700`/`701` AS WELL. A REPORTED PASS IS WORSE THAN NO REPORT. A CARRIED OBJECT IS NOT A RHYME AND A RHYME IS NOT A CARRIED OBJECT, AND ONLY READING TWO CHAPTERS TOGETHER TELLS THEM APART — the barrel at `650`/`651` is a carried object and `675`/`676` and `685`/`686` are shape-collisions on neighbours, and all three were found by the reading and none by the measure.**
