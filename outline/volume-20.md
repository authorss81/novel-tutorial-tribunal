**1. `951`–`960` — ⚠⚹⚠ THE THING THAT IS ALREADY THERE, ⚠⚹⚠ AND IT OPENS ON A TUESDAY, ⚠⚹⚠ THE FIRST DAY OF A WEEK, ⚠⚹⚠⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **AND IT CROSSES **ONE** WEEK BOUNDARY, `957`/`958`.** ⚠ **THE ORDINARY FORM IS FOUND BEING USED ALREADY, ⚠⚹⚠ IN ABOUT NINE EARS, ⚠⚹⚹ BY SOMEBODY WHO DID NOT INVENT IT, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **AND IT IS NOT YET CALLED A FORM AND IT IS NOT YET CALLED ANYTHING. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹** ⚠ **THE FIVE ORDINARY CASES, ⚠ ONE PERSON EACH: A DOORSTEP · A WASH-TUB · A HAND-RAIL · A TROUGH · A SILL.** ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **AND ⚠ NONE OF THEM IS A LOCK, A POUND, A BYRE, A BAKEHOUSE OR A HEDGE, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **BECAUSE THOSE FIVE ARE VOLUME 19's ORDINARY CASES, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **AND ⚠ NONE OF THEM IS A FERRY, A DRAIN, A SCHOOL OR A LOW GATE, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **BECAUSE THOSE FOUR ARE VOLUME 18's. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹ **AND ⚠ NONE OF THEM IS A LANDING OR A STEP OR A BANK, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ **BECAUSE THE LANDING IS WHERE THE VOLUME HAPPENS AND A PLACE A THING IS FOUND IS NOT AN ORDINARY CASE. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ **AND ⚠ NONE OF THEM IS A GATE AND ⚠ NONE IS A LIME KILN AND ⚠ NONE IS A TALLY STONE. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **THESE FIVE ARE THE ONLY PLACES THIS VOLUME NEEDS THAT ARE NOT ALREADY ON THE MAP, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **AND ⚠⚹⚹⚠ THEIR DISTANCES ARE MADE IN MOUTHS ON THE DAY THEY ARE INVENTED, §11, AND THIS PLAN FIXES NONE OF THEM. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹** ⚠ **THE MIDPOINT DOES NOT LAND HERE AND IS NOT APPROACHED. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **BARNABY CROVE'S ASKING STARTS AGAIN ON THE FIRST MORNING AND HE CANNOT READ, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **AND ⚠⚹⚹⚠ **THE FIGURE OF HOW MANY OF THOSE NINETY STEPS ROCK IS GIVEN IN THIS BAND WITH THE WORKING GIVEN, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **AND ⚠⚹⚹⚠ NOTHING IS TAKEN UP, TURNED OVER, PULLED OR WEIGHED IN THIS BAND OR IN ANY BAND, §9. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹ `954` IS THE FIRST OFF-MORNING AND `955` IS THE FIRST ANCHOR, ⚠⚹⚹⚠ **AND THEY ARE ON ADJACENT MORNINGS AND THAT IS A FIGURE OF A LADDER AND NOT OF A PLAN. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹**

**2. `961`–`970` — ⚠⚹⚠ THE MIDPOINT, ⚠⚹⚠ AND IT LANDS ON `966` OR AFTER, ⚠⚹⚠⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **AND `966` IS THE SECOND DAY OF THE HUNDRED AND SIXTIETH WEEK, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **AND ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ `966` IS ALSO A **FULL BANK**, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **AND ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ A BAND THAT LANDS THE MIDPOINT ON A FULL BANK IS **NOT** REFUSED FOR IT, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **BECAUSE A FULL BANK IS FOUR DAYS AND A FULL BANK IS A FIGURE OF THE WATER AND NOT A FIGURE OF THE SCENE. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹ **IT LANDS BY BEING SAID OUT LOUD BY SOMEBODY IN ABOUT NINE EARS: ⚠ THAT LEAVING SOMETHING AND NOT BEING THERE IS NOT GIVING WITHOUT BEING THANKED, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **AND THAT A THING WITH AN ABSENCE IN IT CAN BE READ AS GENEROUSLY AS A THING WITH A PRESENCE IN IT. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹ **AND THE SENTENCE IS ABOUT A THING AND NOT ABOUT THE PEOPLE ON THE BANK. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ **A BAND THAT LANDS IT IN `961`–`965` HAS SPENT IT IN THE WRONG FIVE CHAPTERS. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ **A BAND THAT LANDS IT ON `962` HAS SPENT IT ON A MORNING WHOSE FIGURE OF INCHES IS ABSENT-CORRECTLY, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ **WHICH IS ALSO THE SECOND ANCHOR, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ **AND ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ A MORNING WITH NO FIGURE OF INCHES ON IT IS A MORNING ON WHICH A BAND WILL BE TEMPTED TO TURN UP THE OTHER THREE FIGURES, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ **AND THAT IS THE TRAP `885` WAS. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **AND ⚠ NOTHING IN VOLUME 19 IS RE-READ AND NO LEAF IS SUMMARISED AND NOBODY IS ASKED TO GO OVER ANYTHING AGAIN. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹⚠ `962` IS THE **SECOND** OFF-MORNING **AND** THE **SECOND** ANCHOR, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ **AND IT IS WRITTEN ONCE AND IN ONE SHAPE. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹**

**3. `971`–`980` — ⚠⚹⚠ THE THING NOBODY MAY CLAIM, ⚠⚹⚠ AND IT IS THE BAND WITH **TWO** WEEK BOUNDARIES INSIDE IT, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **AND IT OPENS ON THE LAST DAY OF A WEEK.** ⚠ **Two things have been left on that landing and neither will be claimed, and one of them has made a person's work easier, and the person it has made easier cannot be told, and a person who has been helped by a thing they cannot thank is exactly the person this world has taught four volumes to be suspicious of. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹ **A CASE THAT REQUIRED NO ARGUMENT ABOUT THE WORLD — ⚠ NOBODY IS TO BE ASKED WHO LEFT IT AND NOBODY IS TO BE TOLD THEY CANNOT HAVE IT. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ **AND ⚠ NO CHAPTER OF THIS BAND MAY PRINT THE NUMBER OF PEOPLE WHO WERE IN A ROOM, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ **AND ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ **AND ⚠ MAY NOT PRINT THE NUMBER OF THINGS EITHER, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ **BECAUSE ⚠⚹⚹⚠ **THE FIGURE OF HOW MANY THINGS ARE LEFT IS OWED TO BAND 5 AND NOT BEFORE, §5. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹⚠ **AND ⚠ THE THING NOBODY MAY ASK TWICE IS **NOT** SPENT AGAIN IN THIS BAND, ⚠ **BECAUSE IT IS VOLUME 19's AND A VOLUME THAT SPENDS ANOTHER VOLUME'S HOUSE HAS SPENT ITS OWN TWICE. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **AND ⚠⚹⚹⚠ THE STANDING OFFER IS GIVEN ITS FIGURE EVERY MORNING AND IS UNANSWERED. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ **AND ⚠ `978` IS THE THIRD OFF-MORNING AND `978`/`979` IS THE FOURTH WEEK BOUNDARY INSIDE THE BAND. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹**

**4. `981`–`990` — ⚠⚹⚠ THE MISTAKE, ⚠⚹⚠ AND IT IS ILYAN'S AND IT IS OWED HERE. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **He puts a thing down on that landing where somebody works and then he says out loud that he put it there, and the reason he says it out loud is that he wants to be the giver of it. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **AND BY SAYING IT HE CONVERTS IT INTO A GIVEN THING, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ **AND ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **A GIVEN THING CAN BE THANKED AND A GIVEN THING HAS A GIVER IN IT, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **AND ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **THE CONVERSION IS REFUSED BY A PERSON IN A MOUTH WITH THE WORKING GIVEN, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **AND ⚠ HE IS NOT TOLD HE WAS RIGHT AND **NOT** TOLD HE WAS WRONG, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **BECAUSE ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **BEING TOLD HE WAS WRONG IS A RELIEF AND **NOBODY IS RELIEVED IN THIS VOLUME BY ANY ROUTE**, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹ ⚠ **INCLUDING ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **THE ROUTE WHERE SOMEONE IS UNKIND TO HIM ON PURPOSE. ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **HIS CREDIBILITY WENT AT VOLUME 16's `791:123` AND BEING OVERRULED DID NOT RESTORE IT AND BEING RIGHT DOES NOT EITHER, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ **AND ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ HIS MISTAKE IS `ch − 860` AND IT IS A FIGURE OF MISTAKES AND NOT A FIGURE OF A MAN, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **AND ⚠ NOBODY IS TO TELL HIM THE FIGURE IS SMALL OR LARGE OR ANYTHING AT ALL ABOUT IT, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹ **AND ⚠⚹⚹⚠ ⚠ HE MAY NOT BE TOLD HE FOUND ANYTHING, ⚠⚹⚹⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹⚠ ⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **AND ⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **THE FIGURE OF HOW MANY THINGS ARE LEFT IS BUILT IN THIS BAND AND IS **NOT** GIVEN IN IT, ⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **AND IT IS OWED IN BAND 5 AND NOT BEFORE. ⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹** ⚠⚹⚹⚹ ⚠ **`990` IS THE SIXTH ANCHOR **AND** THE FIFTH FULL BANK AND IT IS THE LAST CHAPTER OF THE BAND, ⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹ **AND IT IS WRITTEN ONCE AND IN ONE SHAPE AND A TITLE THAT NAMES ITS WEEKDAY MUST NAME **A SATURDAY. ⚠⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹⚹**
