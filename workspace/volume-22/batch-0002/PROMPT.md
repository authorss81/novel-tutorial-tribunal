## 6. HOW TO WRITE THE MORNING

**MAKE SOMETHING HAPPEN. EVERY ONE OF YOUR TEN CHAPTERS MOVES SOMEBODY: A HAND GOES SOMEWHERE
IT DID NOT GO, A QUESTION IS PUT AND GETS A WORKING INSTEAD OF AN ANSWER, A PERSON TAKES A
TOOL AND PUTS IT DOWN AGAIN, SOMEBODY SAYS THE ONE THING THEY HAD BEEN NOT SAYING. A CHAPTER
IN WHICH NOTHING HAPPENS EXCEPT A FIGURE RISING IS THE COMPLIANCE DEMONSTRATION THAT
`workspace/volume-21/batch-0005/PROMPT.md` §4 NAMED AND THAT VOLUME 21 BAND 4 WAS. THE
FIGURES RIDE ON TOP OF A MORNING. THEY ARE NOT THE MORNING.**

**THE MIDPOINT IS NOT THE SHAPE OF THE CHAPTER IT LANDS ON.** THE MORNING IT LANDS ON IS A
MORNING LIKE THE OTHER SIX AND SOMETHING ELSE HAPPENS ON IT, AND THE SENTENCE COMES OUT OF A
MAN AT WORK AND NOT OUT OF A SCENE THAT WAS BUILT TO CARRY IT.

**A PERSON MAY BE WRONG ON THIS BAND AND THE WRONGNESS IS THEIRS.** `1050:47` has Ilyan call
his own wanting for the broom *wrong in the way wanting to smooth things is wrong*. **NOBODY
ELSE MAY TELL HIM SO, NOBODY MAY TELL BARNABY CROVE HE WAS WRONG OR RIGHT, AND NOBODY MAY TELL
GARRIN TOLLEY THAT A BROOM IN ONE PAIR OF HANDS IS A GOOD THING TO HAVE. YOU MAY NOT RESOLVE A
MOUTH AFTERWARDS.**

**VARY THE SENTENCES. THIS HOUSE'S VOICE IS A RITUAL CLAUSE — the same question at the sixth
