## HOW TO WRITE THE MORNING, AND WHAT THE LAST FIVE BANDS LEARNED THE HARD WAY

**THE FIRST PRINTING OF THIS FILE CARRIED NO CRAFT GUIDANCE AND NO GUIDANCE ON TITLES,
WHILE ITS OWN PLAN §7 SAYS *TITLES ARE WRITTEN LAST, AGAINST THE BODY* — A RULE NOBODY
CAN FAIL BECAUSE NOBODY WAS TOLD WHAT TO AIM AT. BOTH ARE HERE NOW.**

**MAKE SOMETHING HAPPEN.** A refusal standing is not a scene; it is a fact about a
morning. Every one of your ten chapters moves somebody: a hand goes somewhere it did
not go, a question is put and gets a working instead of an answer, a person takes a
tool and puts it down again, somebody says the one thing they had been not saying.
**A CHAPTER IN WHICH NOTHING HAPPENS EXCEPT A FIGURE RISING IS THE COMPLIANCE
DEMONSTRATION THAT `workspace/volume-21/batch-0005/PROMPT.md` §4 NAMED AND THAT
VOLUME 21 BAND 4 WAS.** The figures ride on top of a morning. They are not the morning.

**A PERSON MAY BE WRONG ON THIS BAND AND THE WRONGNESS IS THEIRS.** `1050:47` has
Ilyan call his own wanting for the broom *wrong in the way wanting to smooth things is
wrong*. Nobody else may tell him so. Nobody may tell Barnaby Crove he was wrong, and
nobody may tell him he was right. Nobody may tell Corvin Tace that a broom in one pair
of hands is a good thing to have. **YOU MAY NOT RESOLVE A MOUTH AFTERWARDS.**

**VARY THE SENTENCES.** This house's voice is a ritual clause — the same question at
the sixth hour, the same offer with its subtraction out loud, *and here is the reason,
because you asked me and not the landing*. That ritual stays word for word and it is
the point. **TEN CHAPTERS OF THE SAME CLAUSE IN THE SAME ORDER IS A COPY, NOT A VOICE.**
The beat you change is everything around it: who says it, what is happening while it
is said, what is in the room, where the person stands, what is done with their hands
in the paragraph after. A repeated silence must be written as a different sentence
every morning. Measure your own repetitions by reading two consecutive chapters
against each other and against themselves.

**A CLOSING IS A BEAT, NOT A STATUS REPORT.** End on something that happened in the
last paragraph — a decision, a cost, a reversal, a discovery, a question asked,
something a person did with their hands. **NOT ONE OF YOUR TEN CLOSINGS MAY BE THE
SAME DEVICE TWICE**, and a closing may not be a list of what is still unfinished, and
`1060` may not sum up the band, and none of the ten may close on the offer.

**AND THE FIGURES ARE SPOKEN, NOT SET. Every figure in this house is said out loud by
the person it belongs to, in the middle of a sentence somebody is already in, in the
middle of work.** A date line is not a paragraph header and it is not a list. `1050:31`
is the model: one man, on his feet, mud on his boots, giving his figures and then
giving the offer and then the want and the cost in the same breath, and nobody
interrupts him for it.

**AND `1058`, THE OFF-MORNING, IS THE HARDEST MORNING YOU WILL WRITE.** No figure of
inches, no figure of steps under, no figure of days, and all three absences said out
loud in words kept for a dry morning. Every other morning on that landing has been a
figure of water for nineteen years, so a morning without one will want to be about
that. **Let it be about a thing that has to be done without a figure to do it with.**
