**Goal:** to live in the town on the day after a memory has been put back and cost something. **Resistance:** about nine people in this town are repeating the second town's account of the morning in the same words they have been repeating the first one in, and a thing said in a room and repeated in a square is a thing about nine people, and the nine are not the same nine, and the year after that it will be a different nine — and that sentence was said to a man of thirty-one on the fifty-ninth day after the division in two rooms over a joiner's shop, and it is the same sentence, and the man of thirty-one recognises it and says so and is believed, and a woman of sixty-one says she has had it in a bundle of thirty-two letters for four years. **Change:** two accounts of one morning are now going round the same town in the same week, and about nine people in this town can give you both, and about nine can only give you the one they heard first, and **the number of people who can give you both is the number of people who can be asked about the other one.** **Ilyan's want:** the two to settle. **His mistake:** he says they will settle in about eleven years, and a recordwright of thirty-four says that is what a book does and not what a town does, and that she is the only person anybody can ask about either of them, and that nobody has asked her what she remembers and she has not been asked because there is no form that says a person may be asked what they remember.

### 528 — `clear` 28, a Saturday, and the first week since the wall was left with a gap
**Goal:** to put the two accounts in front of about two hundred people at a market without a fight. **Resistance:** a market square is a place with about two hundred people in it and about nine of them understand either account, and a bill of sale is a piece of paper and a page in a book is a piece of paper, and about nine people in that square cannot tell which of them was written by a person and which was written by a consequence. **Change:** the two accounts are read out one after the other, in full, by two people, and the square does not decide, and **what the square does is that about nine people in it have now heard both and are asked in the open air which one they had heard first, and about four of the nine answer that they had heard the first one, and about five answer that they cannot remember, and the recordwright says out loud that about nine out of about two hundred is the whole of the finding and that it is not a decision, and that there is no form in the county of Aldwick that says a thing anybody has heard has to be heard by anybody else.** **Ilyan's want:** the square to settle it. **His mistake:** he says the square cannot settle it, in a square, in front of about two hundred people, and about nine of them agree with him and about two hundred have not heard the question.

### 529 — `clear` 29, a Sunday
**Goal:** to find out what a person does with two true accounts when no form exists. **Resistance:** the county office at Aldwick is nine miles off and it can make a return and nothing else, and a return records that an answer was given and that the answer was made use of, and it does not record which answer was correct, and there is no column in the book of returns for a disagreement between two towns, and a man of fifty-four there has seventeen returns to make that nobody has asked him for and about a hundred and forty-one years of a book of orders with a blank half-sheet in it. **Change:** a return is made, about the disagreement, and it is the first return anybody in this county has ever made that does not record an answer to a question, and **it records two answers, and the form does not have a line for two, so it is written on the back, and a man of fifty-four signs the back and does not sign the front.** **Ilyan's want:** the return. **His mistake:** he asks for it to be made and is told that a return is made by an office and not asked for by a person, and that he can be in the lane when it is made and cannot be in the room, and about four people in that lane understand the difference and about nine do not.

### 530 — `clear` 30, a Monday, the seventh and last day of the ninety-seventh week
**Goal:** to write the week with the day on it. **Resistance:** the week's sheet is written in chalk on a board on the end wall of the smithy on the Friday night by a boy of fourteen who can count and cannot yet add, and it has a column with no heading on it, and it has a dead man of about eighty-four in the GROUND column with the day against him, and it has about nine feet of chalk in a column that has no heading, and now there is a week in which two towns remember the same morning differently, **and there is no column for that, and a boy of fourteen has to be told what to do about it and there is nobody to tell him.** **Change:** the recordwright rules **another** column, with no heading, and writes in it one line about two accounts of one morning in two towns, and the boy's name is at the foot of it because he wrote it, and **she says out loud that a book with four columns and no fifth is now a book with three columns that have no heading on them, and that a heading is a promise about what goes in it, and that she cannot make three promises she does not know the terms of.** **Ilyan's want:** one of the three headed. **His mistake:** he asks again, and she gives him the same answer, and this time she adds the half that is new, which is that if the other two were headed then a person in a hundred years would fill them in according to the heading and would fill them in wrong, and that a not-knowing in a column is better than a knowing that is not true. **Closing: a chalk line about the length of a wall and a chalk line about two towns, and a boy's name under both of them, and nothing at the top of any of the three.**
