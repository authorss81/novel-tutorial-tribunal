## 1. The band in one line

**A town has spent three weeks finding out that a book is a door, has put nineteen households through one, and then works out that the door it opened is the same door nine hundred and forty-one other people walk through every autumn — and the first person to say so out loud is a man of forty-seven who has spent nine years being the mouth of a thousand people and did not know that was the same thing as this.**

## 3. The ten cards

### 471 — *The Day A Man Wrote Down To Be A Courtesy Arrives And He Has Not Put Anything Down For It, And A Woman Of Thirty-Four Asks For The Other Eight Things To Be Read In A Square With The Door Open And He Says She May Have Them When He Has Found Them*
**Hall 25, a Friday, the day on the paper.** The board sits, or does not, and there is nothing on the agenda. **Goal:** to have a day. **Resistance:** a day a man chose himself is a wall, and everybody in the room can now tell the difference between a courtesy and a trap, and the man who wrote it cannot. **Change:** the sheet of ten things comes out of the press and the other eight are on it, and the eighth is not about the water and not about the roll, and the person who caused the eighth says so in her own mouth. **Ilyan's mistake:** he assumes the day is about the out-of-roll book and spends the morning waiting for a board to sit, and it is not about that at all.

### 472 — *A Market On A Saturday For The Fifth Time In Nine Years And A Man With A Bill On Eighty Lots Walks Into A Room Over A Taproom And Says He Has Heard The Whole Of It And Would Like To Know Whether The Water Belongs To Him*
**Hall 26.** A ratepayer of the Four Hundred, one of the eighty, and **the first person in this volume who is neither the fenrow nor the stranger nor the steward nor the board, and who wants something for himself.** **Goal:** to find out what the majority thinks it owns. **Resistance:** the water is common to all **households**, and a man who bought a lot is a household, so he is owed a hearing, and there is no form in the county of Ostrey that lets him be given one, and everybody in the room can hear him and nobody can answer him. **Change:** the majority discovers that it has a position and that the position is *we paid for it*, and it is the same three sentences the board said at 465 and it does not know it. **Ilyan's mistake:** he is the man who made the position legible, and he is standing in the room where it is legible.

### 473 — *A Sunday In Which A Woman Who Is Not A Household Finds Out That Four Men In The Row Have Been Advised Separately By The Same Man And None Of Them Was Advised With Her*
**Hall 27, and the band must breathe for one chapter before the turn.** No document, no vote, no panel. **The fenrow, alone, without the stranger, without the board, without the press.** **Goal:** to know what the row has agreed to. **Resistance:** the row did not agree to anything; four of them were each told, separately, by a man with a counter or a form or a good heart, what was best for them, and the four told versions are not the same, and the two people who were not told separately are the ones now carrying it. **Change:** **Sefa Lund is not a person with a claim and turns out to have been the only one keeping the row's accounts in her head, which is a claim nobody made and cannot be given up.** **Ilyan's mistake:** he is not in the row, is not told, and does not know, and the chapter says so plainly and does not sentimentalise it.

### 474 — *A Monday On Which The House Is Asked To Sit And The Steward Does Not Call It And Says Out Loud In A Room Over A Shop The Reason And The Reason Is That He Has Been Forty Years The Only Man In This Town Nobody Had To Wonder What He Wanted And He Is No Longer That Man*
**Hall 28, the seventh day of the ninetieth week is 475 — 474 is the last day of the eightieth-ninth.** **THE BAND'S LAST CHAPTER BEFORE THE REVERSAL, AND IT MUST NOT SPEND IT.** The question is put and it is not the question of 460. **Goal:** to have the question answered by the right body. **Resistance:** the only body that can answer it is the House, the House is the same forty-one men and one elected head in each of nine wards, **and the reason the steward does not call it is the reason he has had for forty years and it is the best reason in the volume and it is the reason the House cannot be reached.** **Change:** the steward says it, and a man of forty-seven says the other half of it in front of about nine people, and the two of them are arguing about who is the mouth of a thousand people. **Ilyan's mistake:** he is asked for his view again, as he was at 460, and he says it, and this time saying it is the worst thing he has done in the town.

### 475 — *THE MIDPOINT, IF IT IS TO LAND ANYWHERE EARLY: A Thing Goes In A Book On A Monday And Nine Hundred And Forty-One Households Find Out On A Tuesday That They Have Been On A Roll Of A Different Size Since The Fourth Day Of The Eighty-Ninth Week, And Nobody Asked Them*
**Hall 29, a Tuesday, the first day of the ninetieth week.** **If the midpoint is not to be spent in 471–474, this is the earliest chapter it can be spent in, and the outline says preferably the second half of 476 or later.** **Goal:** somebody works out what a correction does to the people who were not the subject of it. **Resistance:** the correction is *correct*, it is the steward's own form, it is in his own hand with a day on it, and it fixed the thing it was for. **Change:** the majority uses the machinery the minority was given. **DO NOT BUILD THIS ON *SEATS SCALE WITH HOUSEHOLDS*. The House does not scale. If a constitution is touched, it is a corrected roll is a corrected constituency, and the constituency is a set of doors, and the thing to change is not a number of seats.**

### 476 — *The First Of The Majority To Find Out What It Is Worth, And A Man Of Thirty-Eight Who Keeps A Sluice And Does Not Want To Be A Person With A Claim Is Named In A Minute She Did Not Agree To, For The Second Time, And Says It In A Square*
**Hall 30.** **The reversal may land here or later, and if it lands here it lands in a chair's mouth and in a document and not in a fight.** **Goal:** the majority gets what it asked for. **Resistance:** what it gets is that the row can now be required, and the requirement is a word in a minute, and the ninth clause says a hundred and thirty-eight years old that no household shall be **required to answer** against its will, and the word *required* has never been in a minute of this town until now. **Change:** the majority expels the minority, correctly, in a form, and the form is right, and the person it is done to says out loud that she did not agree, for the second time, and this time there is a minute with her name in it that she did not put there.

### 477 — *A Question That Has Been Open Since The First Day Of This Business Is Asked Of A Woman Of Twenty-Eight Four Hundred Miles Off And The Answer Comes Back In A Round Hand And Is About Whether A Voluntary Exit Should Be A Right Or A Privilege, And This Time She Is Not Writing To Him*
**Hall 31.** **Her answer of 468 arrives, and he reads it, and the first disagreement of the volume between two people who are partners on a matter and not otherwise is now a disagreement they are both arguing in, and she is still right.** **Goal:** to answer her. **Resistance:** everything she said in 468 is now demonstrable in a town of four thousand people within four days, and the demonstration is the thing he has spent a month building. **Change:** the answer to *right or privilege* is not a sentence, it is an instrument, and neither of them can build one and the only instruments in Halloway are a miller's second book, a copyist, a minute, and a woman who refuses things in lanes. **Ilyan's mistake is not the engine here** — the cost lands on her six unfiled years, which he has never once asked her about.

### 478 — *A Man Of Thirty-One Who Is On Nobody's Book Tries To Do The Only Thing He Is Able To Do In A Town That Has Not Asked Him For It And Is Refused By A Man Of Thirty-Nine With A Counter Who Has Said No To Him Before And Gives A Better Reason The Second Time*
**Hall 32.** **The Owed Instruction, second half: at least two of these ten must be chapters in which something he did is not the thing that goes wrong.** **Goal:** to be useful. **Resistance:** he is nobody, he cannot be entered, he cannot be asked about anything, and the man he wants to help has been right about refusing him once already. **Change:** the second refusal is a worse one for him and a better one for the town, and the thing he is refused is the only instrument that would have worked. **Cost:** he learns out loud that being un-askable is a hole and not a gift, from the woman of thirty-four who said the other half of it to him on the first morning of this band.

### 479 — *A Saturday In Which About Two Hundred People Stand In A Square Because A Door Has Been Opened And Closed In A Morning And Everybody In The Town Has Worked Out What A Book Done In A Morning Is*
**Hall 33.** **The market is on a Tuesday, so there is no market; there are two hundred people in a square because a town of four thousand has worked out that a roll is a list of who is in a room.** **Goal:** to know what the town now knows. **Resistance:** the finding is that a corrected roll is a corrected constituency, that a constituency is a set of doors, and that a book can be a door, and **there is no board in this county with anything to say about it, and the only office that has ever heard of it is twenty-two miles off and can make a return.** **Change:** the sheet of ten things acquires an eleventh thing on it and nobody says which.

### 480 — *The Third Of Ten Things This Band Did Not Fix Is A Sheet Of Paper In A Press, And A Roll To Be Made In The Autumn, And A Man Of Thirty-One Who Is On Nobody's Book And Is Read By Somebody In A Room In Which He Is Not Standing*
**Hall 34, a Sunday, the sixth day of the ninetieth week.** The batch closes on an instrument and not on a refusal. **Nothing is resolved.** The right of refusal is unrestored and the ninth clause has now been read out in a room twice and given to nobody. The roll for the autumn is made on a day out of a day. The frame is on a list. The heel is on the wrong side of a leaf and in a book twenty-two miles off. The out-of-roll book is shut. The four letters and a stroke are unread or they are not, and the band must not say. **And the last image is a man who is on nobody's book, and is not in the room, and is read out.** **THIS BAND DOES NOT PAY THE PLANT.** The witness line is not resolved in 471–480. It is carried, and it is four hundred miles from a woman who has not opened a range of nine hundred refusals.
