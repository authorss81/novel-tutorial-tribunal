## 1. The band in one line

**A town of four thousand people has worked out that a book is a door and that nobody in the county can be asked to stop, and a bar four feet long has not been turned for three days, and the question nobody asked on the Saturday is whether anybody can be sent for — and the answer is that a thing is sent for by a person with a form, and a town of four thousand people has four instruments and no officer, so the arbitration is asked for by a body with no power, refused by the same power, and then happens anyway, in a room, because a thing that has been asked for by everybody is not nothing.**

## 3. The ten cards

### 481 — Hall 35, a Monday, the fifth day of a bar that has not been turned
**The band opens on the water and on the arithmetic, and it must not open on a speech.** The sluice is unwound; the race is carrying what it likes; the wheel at the mill has been slow for four days and the miller has a note in his own book with one word in it. **Goal:** for somebody in that town to say what is going to happen to the water, out loud, with a day on it. **Resistance:** the water board has not sat since the thirtieth day and does not sit without nine men and a minute, and a bar cannot be turned by anybody, because turning it is not a household's business. **Change:** the first person to say it out loud is a miller of thirty-nine with a counter who has been right about everything for four years, and he says it in nine words, and it is the first sentence in this band. **Ilyan's mistake:** he goes to the mill at the eighth hour to be useful and arrives after the sentence has been said, and spends the morning being useful in the wrong place, which is what he did on the twenty-fifth day after the division and which nobody in that town has forgiven him for.

### 482 — Hall 36, a Tuesday, the first day of the ninety-first week, and a request that asks for a thing with no form
**The band crosses a week boundary here, from the last day of the ninetieth week to the first day of the ninety-first, and both date lines say so.** Somebody asks, in a room, whether anybody can be sent for, and the answer is that a thing is sent for by a person with a form. **Goal:** to get a question answered by a body. **Resistance:** there is no such body. The board has a minute and a second book and no power over the roll; the House has not been called and does not scale; the county at Kerby is twenty-two miles off and can make a return and nothing else. **Change:** a request for an arbitration is drawn up **by a copyist, in her own hand, at her own table, with no body behind it**, and the two lines of it are the first two lines of anything anybody in Halloway has ever drawn up for anybody else. **Ilyan's want:** that it be a form. **His mistake:** he tries to make it a form and it cannot be one, because there is no office to hold it, and what comes out is a sheet of paper with a head on it and nothing under the head, and Wenna Crale says so.

### 483 — A Wednesday on which the request is refused by the body that cannot answer it, and the refusal is correct
**The water board refuses the arbitration, in the words of the request, and the reason is that it has no power to appoint anybody, and it is right.** **Goal:** to have the request answered. **Resistance:** the answer is a refusal, correctly given, by nine men who are owed an answer and cannot give one. **Change:** the majority finds out that being right is not the same as being able to do anything, which is the thing that has been true in this town for four weeks and has never been said out loud by anybody. **Ilyan's mistake:** he says the sentence about the roll and the room and the doors again, in a room over a saddler's shop, and about nine men hear it for the third time and about four of them have it right, and the sentence has become a thing the room uses rather than a thing the room hears.

### 484 — A Thursday on which the thing the band is about is said to be a door, and a door can be opened by anybody who finds the handle
**The instrument problem, in a room, with about nine people in it, and it is the chapter where the answer to *right or privilege* stops being a sentence.** **Goal:** to name the instrument. **Resistance:** the four instruments in Halloway are a miller's second book that is shut, a copyist who is paid two pounds a year, a minute in a room over a saddler's shop, and a woman who refuses things in lanes; and there is a fifth thing that is not an instrument, which is a nail in a shed, and the room will not accept it. **Change:** the person who is not an instrument is named as one, and the naming is refused, and the refusal is correct. **Ilyan's cost:** the sentence he said on the Friday of the thirty-first day is what shut the book, and by this chapter about nine men in this town know it, and one of them says it to him.

### 485 — A Friday, and a room in which about two hundred people cannot get in and the door is open, and the thing that is read out is not a document
**The House or a body like it, in a room with a hearth, with the door open, and about two hundred people in the square. And the thing read out is not a document.** **Goal:** to have the thing put where everybody can hear it. **Resistance:** the only thing anybody can put in a room is a minute, and a minute belongs to a body, and there is no body. **Change:** the thing read out is **the second sheet, folded in four, with nothing on the outside of it**, read out in a town of four thousand people four hundred miles from the person who wrote it, by a man who is on nobody's book, and about two hundred people hear the six years of a woman they have never met. **Ilyan's mistake:** he reads it out because he thinks a thing read aloud in a square is a thing that can be asked about, and it is not, and he has said the opposite in nine rooms, and somebody in the square says the opposite back to him.

### 486 — A Saturday, and the arbitration, which is not a proceeding and has no procedure, and everybody attends
**THE ARBITRATION HAPPENS. It is a Saturday, in a room over a taproom, because there is nowhere else with a table in it that row can get four hundred yards up a lane to.** **Goal:** to have a question answered. **Resistance:** there is no procedure, no standing, no rules, no chair with power, and about nine people turn up and bring eleven more. **Change:** the arbitration produces **an instrument and not a decision**, and the instrument is a thing a person can be asked about, and about four people in that room understand what has happened and the rest are applauding. **Ilyan's want:** that it be a right. **His mistake:** he asks for a clause and is asked whether anybody can be given a clause, and the answer is the answer he has been giving for four weeks, and he gives it.

### 487 — A Sunday in which the fenrow is a party to a thing again, and the word *required* is in a minute a second time, and this time it is good
**The only good use the ninth clause gets in this volume, and it is the only one anybody in Halloway is ever given, and it is given to a person who is not a household and refused by a person who is, and that is the shape of the whole volume and nobody in that room knows it.** **Goal:** to have a person required to do a thing and to be able to say no. **Resistance:** the ninth clause says no household shall be required to answer against its will, and **a person who is not a household is outside it, and the only person in that room who is inside it is the man who has been the mouth of a thousand people for nine years.** **Change:** the clause is used, once, correctly, by the majority, against itself. **Ilyan's cost:** the clause is used and it is not the clause he has spent a month on, and he is the only person in that room who is glad, and he does not say so.

### 488 — A Monday, and the frame, and the season, and four weeks
**The frame.** It is about four weeks off a season and it has not been turned. **Goal:** to mend a north heel. **Resistance:** **the money.** The heel is nine pounds, the quarter in the board's hand is eight pounds and fifteen shillings and fourpence, and the roll for the autumn is a fortnight off and there is no form that lets anybody put a cost on a roll that has been made, and half a penny on each of the eighty lots is the whole of the difference. **Change:** **the levy the eighty lots have been able to pay since the twenty-sixth day and could not be asked for is now asked for, and by whom, and in what document.** **Ilyan's mistake:** he is asked for his view and he gives the true one, and the true one is that the frame is the board's and the board has no money and the only instrument in this county that could mend it is a county office twenty-two miles off that can make a return and nothing else — and about nine men in that room hear the same sentence for the fifth time.

### 489 — A Tuesday, and Sera Quill's four pages, which are four pages and not four lines, and she is not writing to him
**The band in which the letter arrives in full.** **Goal:** to know what is in the other three pages. **Resistance:** the other three pages are about instruments, and about the hundred and eighty forms she has read, and about the shelf that is eleven inches wide, and they are not addressed to him. **Change:** **the first disagreement of this band is not a disagreement, and the second is: she is right about the shelf and he is right about the lane, and neither of them can build the thing the other one needs, and the four pages say so in the round hand of the counters and are signed with no name at all.** **Ilyan's mistake:** he writes back in the small upright hand, which she told him in `468` is the hand that makes a letter usable, and uses it.

### 490 — A Wednesday, and the last of the ten, and a thing that is in a room and is not a refusal
**The band closes on an instrument and not on a resolution, as `480` closed on a man in a doorway.** **Nothing is resolved.** The right of refusal is unrestored and has been used once, in a lane, by a person it was never written for. The roll for the autumn is three days off. The frame is a season off and the season is about three weeks off. The four letters and a stroke are unread or they are not, **and the band must not say.** **And the last image is a thing with hinges on it.**
