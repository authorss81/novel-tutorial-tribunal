## 3. The ten cards

### 461 — *The Roll Is Made In The Autumn And A Man Of Thirty-Nine At The Mill Has Been Keeping A Second Book Since The Saturday*
**Twelve days after the declaration of the eighty-sixth week.** The band opens on the thing nobody is looking at, which is that **the miller stopped credit to one woman and not to the other eighteen**, and the eighteen are fine because eighteen tenements are rated as *property* on a water list even where they are not on the household roll. **Goal:** get the second book seen. **Resistance:** a miller who keeps a second book because nine hundred people buy on credit and the roll is not fast enough, and who will not hand it over to a stranger or to a clerk, and who says the useful thing himself: *a household that is not on the roll is not somebody who has gone away, it is somebody who was never counted, and I can tell you which of my nine hundred are which because I have been doing it on paper for four years.* **Change:** the second book becomes a document in this band, in Ott Vernay's hand, on a mill counter, and it is the first evidence in the volume that is not a book of a body. **Ilyan's mistake:** he asks for the book to be copied into the town's press within a day, and Vernay refuses and says *you want it in your book, not mine*, and Ilyan's copy of the refusal is in his own mouth and not in any minute.

### 462 — *A Board Of Nine Is Asked A Question It Has Never Been Asked And Its Chair Says That Answering It Will Make Him The Man Who Comes In With A Thing*
The water board is asked, in a room over a taproom, whether it will write in the out-of-roll book, and Perdy Sallow says plainly that the answer is yes and that the last time he was the man who came in with something it was the year before last and a man at Ostrey has still not paid his rate. **Goal:** find a way of using the power that does not make the chair a man who brings things. **Resistance:** every way of making it small is a way of making it somebody else's, and the two people who are available to be the somebody else are a copyist who does not want it and a stranger who cannot be entered. **Change:** Sallow writes down the shape of the question rather than answering it, and puts a day on it, and the day is nine days off, and nobody in that room can say whether a day is a courtesy or a wall.

### 463 — *The Sleeper On The Frame Goes At The North Heel And A Man Of The County Cannot Get A Word Of It Into A Minute And Says So In A Lane*
The third attack on the frame and the first night-time one. **Goal:** get the heel into writing. **Resistance:** Garrick Nye is of the county, the board does not take paper from the county, and the only person who can get a thing into a minute is a clerk who is paid two pounds and does not want it — **which was said out loud at 457 and must be used, not repeated.** **Change:** the heel gets into writing in a place nobody thought of — **on the back of the statement of 455, in the fourth paragraph that Sefa Lund struck out and signed across**, and that is a hard, unglamorous, correct place for it, and it means the document she rejected is now carrying a fact that has to be carried.

### 464 — *Four Days Of Rain And A Town Of Four Thousand People Who Begin To Say The Word Household In Front Of Him In A Way That Is About Him*
A change of pressure. No document, no vote, no panel. **The band must breathe here.** Rain, the market, the nine days of buckets becoming a habit people are annoyed by rather than a hardship, a man of twenty-two who has never been asked what he is, and a woman of thirty-four who tells him that the fenrow have begun using the word *stranger* about him and that this is not the same as the word they used on Monday. **Goal:** he wants a case to work on. **Resistance:** there is nothing to work on, and the only thing he can do is work, and the chapter is about a man with nothing to file. **Change:** he is asked to read the Book to somebody who cannot come to the Stone House, and he says no, and the reason he gives is a language reason and not a standing reason, and both of them are true.

### 465 — *The Board Sits And A Question Is Put In It That Nobody Asked For And The Answer Is Nine To Nothing Against The Nineteen Houses And The Nine Are Right*
**THE MIDPOINT MAY LAND HERE, AND NOT IN 461–464.** Nine to nothing, four of the nine saying the same three sentences, and the three sentences are: *we did not lose the water, we sold it, and we were paid; we are asked now to carry nineteen houses in a roll we did not make; and the fenrow have not asked.* **Goal:** somebody in the band tries to make the fenrow ask. **Resistance:** making them ask is the easy part and it is the whole of the damage, and **the person who tries is not Ilyan and must not be Ilyan.** It is Perdy Sallow, who does it because he has read the third line of the minute at 460 and cannot sleep on it. **Change:** the fenrow ask. They ask for the thing that has been the worst thing that could happen to them all batch, and they ask for it in writing, and Wenna Crale has to enter it.

### 466 — *A Roll Is Corrected In A Morning And Nineteen Tenements Go On It, And Nine Wards Find Out That They Have Never Elected One Seat Between Them, And Nobody In That Square Knows Yet What They Have Done*
The consequence, immediate and physical. **1,041 + 19 = 1,060 rated households, and the levy and the water change hands. THE HOUSE DOES NOT SCALE: it is nine wards, one elected head of household in each, forty-one in all, and the number of households in a ward has never been the number of seats in it and there is no form in this county that says it is. So the finding of this chapter is not that the House has sixty seats. It is that nobody in Halloway had ever been told what the House is made of, and that a corrected roll is therefore a corrected constituency, and that a constituency can be changed by a book in a morning.** **Goal:** somebody works out what the arithmetic does to a constitution. **Resistance:** the arithmetic is not the problem, and the person who first reaches for it is wrong about what it does. **Change:** a man of sixty-one watches nine men change the ground he has stood on for forty years with a right thing to say, and the right thing to say is not an answer to the thing that was asked.

### 467 — *Two Of The Nineteen Are Found To Be Two Households And One Of Those Is A Man Who Has Been Paying A Rent To A Woman Who Is Not On The Roll Either, And The Correction Turns Into An Audit Of The Whole Row*
The mistake is discovered by the fenrow themselves, not by a stranger, and it is found by a woman of thirty-four in a doorway. **Goal:** the row corrects itself. **Resistance:** correcting a row is not correcting a roll, and every household in Halloway now knows that a roll can be corrected in a morning, which means a roll is not a fact. **Change:** the audit finds that eleven other rows in the town were entered as land in some year and are not households, and the fenrow have accidentally made the roll meaningless, and nobody in that square is glad.

### 468 — *A Letter Goes Four Hundred Miles With A Witness Line In It And A Woman Of Twenty-Eight Reads It And Does Not Answer It And Writes Down That She Has Not Answered It*
**Sera Quill's letter is sent, or is not sent, and this chapter is about the sending.** The letter is the one from 460. **Goal:** the letter goes. **Resistance:** sending it means a recordwright four hundred miles off learns that a man with a defect report signature may be attached to a Veyran refusal witness, and Sera Quill is the last person in the world who should be told that by a man asking her a favour. **Change:** she writes back in a hand he did not ask her to write in, and the answer is not about the witness line, and it is the first disagreement of Volume 10 between two people who are partners on a matter and not otherwise, and it is about whether a voluntary exit should be a right or a privilege, and **she is right and he is not, and he does not get the last word in the band.**

### 469 — *The Man From The County Comes Back On A Thursday With A Bill For The Frame And Nine Pounds And Something He Did Not Tell Them In August*
Garrick Nye returns with the frame account and with a fact he had and did not use. **Goal:** find out what the county already knew. **Resistance:** **the county has had a record of that heel since the year before last and put the record in a book in a town four hundred miles off, and the county is not hiding anything criminal and the man is not hiding it either, he was waiting to be asked in the right room.** **Change:** the volume's second-off-the-shelf document arrives, and it is the first document in the volume that names an office, and the office is not the Bench and has no power over any of this.

### 470 — *The Second Of Ten Things This Band Did Not Fix, And A Corrected Roll In The Autumn, And A Man Of Thirty-One Who Is On Nobody's Book And Did It Himself*
**The band closes on a refusal of the majority's own making, stated by the man who made it, in a room, in the words of the request that caused it.** Nothing is resolved. The roll stands as corrected. The frame is on a list. The heel is written on the back of a struck paragraph. The out-of-roll book has been used once in twelve years. The right of refusal is unrestored. The witness line is unread. **And the last image is a man sitting in a room over a nail shop writing a return on his own form, and sending it, and the number is the one from 458 and it goes up a road to a clerk who can put it in a book that has a head on it.**
