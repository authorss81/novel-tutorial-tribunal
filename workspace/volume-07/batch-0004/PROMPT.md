## 6. THE TEN CARDS

**Each card is a scene, not a beat list. Every one of them has a beginning in existing pressure, a goal that can be stated in a sentence, resistance from a person or from the world or from his own limits, a change caused by the scene, a completed practical or emotional beat, and an end that opens a question or a decision or a cost. Do not manufacture suspense by cutting away mid-sentence or mid-action. Not every card needs a cliff and several of these should end on a decision.**

**AND THE SHAPE OF THE WHOLE BAND: there are no fights, no rescues, no relics, no vaults, no caves, no ruins, no temples, no battlefields, and no monsters, and the engine is a man who tries to hold six things in a country that has no word for holding anything, and the climax of the band is a man putting somebody's name on a piece of paper on purpose and saying the price out loud, and the last line is a person doing their work.**
