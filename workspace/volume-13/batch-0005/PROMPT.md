Pell Varn, sixty-eight, gave the rule on the Thursday of the hundred and tenth week, is not in a chair and cannot be corrected in one, has said out loud that a Crown is a lid with a name on it and that he gave the rule that made it, and has said out loud that he is worse off than he was and that nobody is to do anything about it. Ibb Marshe, forty-one, keeps two carts, and her son Wat Marshe is thirteen and is not in a chair and is not a head in the book of heads and carts, and copied a sheet of deal out with his own hand and said the name out loud once and paid for it. Ada Renk, sixty-four, got up out of a chair in the open nineteen years ago. Otho Pyle, forty-five, keeps a smithy and has said nothing in nine visits to that room. Marda Vell, twenty-nine, has been in an ash for nine years and has not stood up, and told a man of thirty-one on a Wednesday that he might not answer a boy's question, and he held it. Orla Fennimore, forty-four, keeps the book, has kept it nineteen years for thirteen shillings and fourpence a year, is the only person anybody in that county can be sent for about a chair, said no to the Crown in four sentences, asked a man of thirty-eight whether he would speak for a dead man, and has said no three times to writing the hearing in her book. Eliza Lark, seventy-one, keeps a house that is not hers and has kept it eleven years, came up a cart road unasked to say she does not want to be spoken for, and the reason is about four years. Ivo Kellow, thirty-four, is in an ash and has not stood up and asked at `640` whether a person who is spoken for is owed a copy of what it said about them, and nobody answered him. **A sheet of deal has thirty-two lines and there are fourteen copies of it in four counties, one of which is the best-looking sheet in four counties and is in a county nobody is going to, and it was written in a hand he is not allowed to use.** A man of about fifty-two who cannot see well offered, out loud in a yard nine miles up the water, to have the Crown speak for a man of thirty-one in four counties on a day a person names, and the reason was a woman of forty-nine who does not know what a copy is, and it would have worked in about four days, and the man of thirty-one wanted it and said so before it was offered again and then said no. **The First Witness is named by its own name once, flat, in a room with the door open, and nothing happened.** A hearing about every record there is was asked for on the seventh and last day of the hundred and eleventh week and it is the fifteenth day and nobody granted it and nobody refused it and nobody has written it down. **And a man of thirty-one is nobody's, and a thirty-first line about him is on a sheet in two places in four counties, and he is eighty-six days into a year he cannot finish, and the year is a day of a river and not a number.**
