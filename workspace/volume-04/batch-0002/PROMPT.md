## WHAT A COLD REVIEWER WILL LOOK FOR, BECAUSE IT HAS LOOKED FOR EVERY ONE OF THESE IN THIS PROJECT

- **A distance attached to a walk, a document or a person.** The towpath is six miles each way. The counting-house is four miles from the court. **A document's distance is the one that is always wrong.**
- **A career written as two periods that add up to the wrong total.** Nineteen years in an office, six of them in the chair, and **not six plus nineteen, which is twenty-five.** Nineteen years in a bench's book, and nothing before it. Nine years in a building and nothing before it. Twenty-two years a reeve and nothing before it.
- **An object in three places across two chapters.** The lease sheet is on a nail and in a drawer and in a man's coat, and **it is one nail.**
- **A count in one chapter that has two frames.** The forbearance's age is the standing example: fourteen days, and the tenth day before the page, and four weeks to the cut, and **all three are correct about three different things and may never be quoted together as one figure.**
- **A figure in a state file treated as a fact.** This project has been bitten eleven times and each time a number was written down by somebody who was tired and believed it. **Every count in your state record carries a line number or it does not go in.**
- **A bold word in running prose.** Scan for it.
- **A duplicate paragraph and a reused phrase across the batch.** Chapters 1–160 now contain a great many sentences about a person standing in a room; the words *about nine people*, *at the fourth bell* and *in the ordinary way* have all become the volume's tics and **each of them may appear often and none of them may be load-bearing in two chapters running.**
- **A scene attributed to a person who was not in it.** This class has been found in four separate batches. **If you attribute a sentence to a person, check they were there, and check the place exists.**
- **A person produced.** Osa Trin's being asked is not a production. A boy sent up a lane with a number is a production, and on this shelf the mechanism of production is a Bench form and it is four hundred years old and it is in nobody's book, **and the volume's proudest moment so far is a bench that asked instead of sending.**
