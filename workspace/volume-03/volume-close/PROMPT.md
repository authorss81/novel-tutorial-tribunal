## What this phase is

**You are closing the third volume of a male-led webnovel in which the protagonist is a man who can read the seams in records and can be made responsible for what happens to other people's accounts. The volume's spine was that being the man who links people is a burden and not a competence. The volume is over. Nothing was won. Something was written down.**

Volume 03 opened with a cart road above Kestrel Reach and a man of thirty-one who could not read. It closed with a woman of twenty-four putting a bar into a second fire in a shed two miles along a coast, and a boy of eleven going over a flat at a penny, and nobody on that flat thinking anything about any of it.

**The four things that happened, and the four that did not.**

- **A breach. Eleven yards.** On the sixth of the eighth tide the water went out through the middle of the seaward face of a wall nineteen years and eight months old and nobody was in it. The harm was not the hole and it was not the breach: **the harm was the gravel, and the gravel is two feet, and the first thing the water does on the far side of a breach is take gravel.** It is a shore going. The guild was right about that wall and is right about it now and is not a villain and was not defeated.
- **A link, and not a fight.** He asked three people whether they would like to be the one standing there. He had no standing to ask any of them. **One said yes in four seconds and said what a name at the bottom of a thing costs. One said no first, heard it come out of his own mouth, took four seconds, and then said yes. One said no before the ask and then said yes to a different question in the same hour, unasked, and would not let him believe he had persuaded her.** He attacked nothing, became no hero of the water, saved nothing, and got no party, no bench, no body, no office and no remedy he chose alone.
- **A revocable charter, and a cost to pull it.** Paper. Three hands. The three named, and the burden named, and **the burden on a person and not on a harbour.** Revocable by **any one person who goes over that flat** and by no body, and never on anybody's behalf, and it costs a day and a standing and seventy faces and no answer. **A pane is a picture of one morning and cannot be crossed out; this is paper and it can be, and that is the entire reason it can be pulled.** It is the first thing on that coast ever made out of three people's own words, and nobody in the shed said so, and it was not said as an argument in a room, because it was not an argument.
- **The System recognised something from Earth for the first time in this series, and said nothing about what it means.** Nine uneven columns with a border on two sides and a step in it, and a row of eleven marks the same size and all different, cut at the bottom of a kiln wall in a working shed by a grandmother who found them and never worked them out and told her granddaughter so. And standing on its own at the end of it, unattached: **NK-3317.** It is not a panel, it is not a translation of anything, and it stated no meaning. **A woman of twenty-four asked, in an ordinary voice with her hand flat on the wall, who was standing in front of the somewhere that this was copied from — because this coast has people in it who can say no, and there is no column anywhere on this coast for a thing that was not kept. Nobody answered her. Nothing on that coast answered her, including the System.**

**And the four that did not happen, which are the volume's real subject and must be closed as firmly as the four that did:** **the right of refusal is not restored and the historical question is not answered and nothing on that coast is offered as either; the guild did not lose and the money is not there and nobody pretended it was; the eleven yards is still going, because refusing stops nothing and the thing refused is not destroyed by the refusal; and a woman of eighty-one is still not told, and the boy of eight in her hut is still only a boy, and nobody on that coast got credit for that.**
