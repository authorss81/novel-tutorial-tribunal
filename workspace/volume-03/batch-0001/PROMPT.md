## The three hard limits on this batch

1. **Witness Broker is a practice, not a power he switches on.** He can connect compatible cases and invite a witness from one site to testify in another **if the witness and the receiving community consent.** He cannot summon a witness, cannot cross-examine anybody into the truth, and cannot force a person. **Every link must have a physical or social bridge. He becomes responsible for keeping the linked witnesses safe and for keeping their accounts separate, and a false merge harms people who never met.** The first multi-site case is this volume's consent problem and it is the batch's spine.
2. **He cannot read a saltglass record.** A saltglass record is a thing the coast keeps its charters in and it is not paper, and the shape of the thing is not the shape of an instrument. **A person who reads charters for a living and cannot read the coast's record is the volume's best problem and it is Sera's, and the batch must not solve it.** Let somebody on the coast be right about what a saltglass record is and wrong about what it is for, exactly as Corrin Vask was right about the shelves and wrong about what being asked would make him.
3. **The coast's ordinary scale must be preserved.** A guild of ferrymen and glassmakers, a toll taken at a gate on a route, a woman who keeps a table of tides, a boy who runs a line, a house with two rooms. **The Nettle Straits are a place with people in it and the batch must not make the world bigger than a district.** Every promise in Volume 03 is carried by a person with a want, on a body, in weather.

## The ten cards

Use these as chapter plans, not as prose. Each card must become a complete scene with its own physical ground, its own resistance, and its own consequence. **Chapter 101 should follow the volume's turn, not precede it: Ilyan and Sera arrive somewhere, and the first thing they are told is a thing about a person.**

**Chapter 0101 — the coast, and the first thing that is not a room.** Open on the ground. A crossing or a landing or a cart on a road above a harbour, in weather, with ordinary people doing ordinary work, and a person with a want who tells one of them something about another person that neither of them can act on. **The first honest problem of the volume is on the page in the first chapter: the coast keeps its charters in a record neither of them can read, and a man who argues cannot navigate.** Do not explain the strait in narration. Let a working person explain the safe route by telling them which day it is not safe, and let that be the whole of the geography for three chapters.

**Chapter 0102 — the guild, and what a passage is worth.** The water and passage charter is a real document with a real burden in it, and the guild that holds it is not a villain and has not been asked to be one. **Let the guild be right about the strait.** The resistance is not dishonesty; it is that the charter says what a passage is and does not say who bears it when the weather is wrong, and a person who cannot read the charter is a person the charter does not see. The finding of the chapter should be that a guild controlling the only safe route is not a criminal act and is a catastrophe waiting for one bad week, and the person who says that is a guild officer and is doing his job.

**Chapter 0103 — the second site, and the first refusal of consent.** The batch's spine: to put a matter in two places Ilyan must ask two communities to be in the same matter, and one of them says no. **The refusal must be correct, must be recorded in full, and must cost somebody.** A community that is asked to be a party in a matter about a strait has to be a party, and a party has to be served, and being served is a day of somebody's time, and the second community is poor and has a version of this. **The first multi-site case requires consent from rival port communities and this batch must show the consent being refused once, properly, and the cost of the refusal falling on a person.**

**Chapter 0104 — a rate, in a different coin.** Return, in a new place, to Volume 02's spine — the price of a document is paid by the person who makes it — and make it a *tide* instead of a *bucket*. **A rate on a crossing, collected in a coin the coast uses, that falls at a multiple on the poorest households, and a table of tides that is the coast's own version of a page.** Do not repeat the washer, the bucket, or the farthing. A different person, a different want, the same shape, and the shape must be found on the ground and not announced.

**Chapter 0105 — the saltglass, and a person who is right about it.** Somebody on the coast reads saltglass the way Corva Silt reads a bench's own hand, and explains it, and is right about what it is, and is wrong about what it is for. **The record is not an instrument and cannot be produced by a person who does not hold it, and a saltglass record is worse than that: it is a record that is made by a process, and the process takes a person and a fire and a year.** Let the batch find out what a saltglass record costs to make, and let the cost be a person's work and a person's back, and let it be paid in advance by somebody who has already paid it.

**Chapter 0106 — the songs, and the toll marks, and a pattern nobody asked for.** The coast's songs and toll marks contain a pattern that resembles the hidden map in *The Sovereign's Wake*. **The prompt reserves the midpoint for later; this chapter may put the pattern on the page and must not let Ilyan recognise it.** He can see a shape. He cannot read a saltglass record, cannot navigate, and does not have the private sequence in front of him. **Let somebody else see the pattern first and be afraid of it — a ferryman, a glassmaker's apprentice, a woman with a table of tides — and let Ilyan's only contribution be that he asks what the marks are for, and the answer is that they are for counting.** **No System panel may confirm that the pattern is a map, and no one may name *The Sovereign's Wake* in dialogue as anything but a thing a man from a city once played.**

**Chapter 0107 — the appeal, used by somebody who cannot use it.** Volume 02's resolution is that an appeal survives and is not secure, and the way it was not secure was that a rate in a bench's own book cannot be carried by anybody. **On the coast there is a thing a person may appeal and a person who may be appealed to, and the difference is a harbour custom nobody has written down.** A person tries to use the road they have and finds the road is a song, and a song is not a document, and this is a *different* failure from Volume 02's and must not be the same failure. **A person may be refused a hearing by a person who is not a bench and is not a court and is right.**

**Chapter 0108 — the cost of connecting two places.** Witness Broker's cost, paid early and in the body: to link two sites he must carry a witness's account, and a carried account changes as it is carried, and a false merge harms people who never met. **Let him carry one and get something wrong, in an ordinary way, and let the person whose words were altered be somebody who is not in the room and does not know.** A ferry family is the right instrument here: a family that has been on the water for four generations, in which the accounts of three brothers do not agree, and where agreeing them would require one of them to be wrong in public about his father. **Do not make this a lie and do not make it a mystery. Make it a thing people have always done because the water was there.**

**Chapter 0109 — what this coast has, and who is not in it.** A region with a rule and no living witness of it, one step further along from the Bridgeward, and the finding that the honest answer from every door is that the paper says something about a *person*. **Push the volume's new question — which region has accepted a rule with no living witness at all — at most twice, and do not answer it, and do not damage a route that works.** The coast is about to answer it in the wrong direction: the strait is a route that works and the guild is right about it. **The chapter's finding is that a rule with no living witness is not the same as a rule with no living person in it, and that the coast has the second and not the first, and that a toll taken eight years by a family and written into a form by a man who could not read is a rule with a person in it, and the person has not been asked.**

**Chapter 0110 — the batch close.** A partial, real, paid-for change; a burden named and visible; the thing Volume 02 was about showing up in a new shape; and an ending that is a decision, a refusal, or a discovery and not a verdict. **Tamsin Ro is not to be called and nobody is to look for her. Neris Ro is not to be asked for anything and her right to stop everything herself stands, including from Ilyan, and her reason is not to be repeated in public.** Let the case's own first promise — a burden named on both sides, a work record that is portable, a person not entered in a register is not a trespasser by that fact alone, a person can be sent for — **be tested by somebody on the coast who was not in the case and does not know what it cost, and let the test be a small physical thing and not a speech.** **No romance beat. No new enemy. Nothing that makes the world bigger.** End on a question Chapter 111 can carry, and **do not manufacture suspense by cutting away mid-sentence or mid-action.**

## Everything else that must hold

**Ilyan's limited standing is confined to the ninth-bridge matter.** He is a party in nothing else, may be cited as nothing, and may not send somebody with his name on it. **If he is sent for, the sending must cost somebody as well as him and the cost must be named.** He may not be given a party, a bench, a body, or a remedy he chooses alone on the coast. He may not be rewarded for carrying anything, and every crossing he arranges should cost somebody a day or a wage or a hand's work.

**Sera is the person this novel is for and she is not rescued and not given a procedure by a man.** Her third term — she carries the terms of the matter to the review and to any other district that asks — is the mechanism that gets the terms to a coast, and the coast is not a district. **Bryn Sallow still does not know what is in his leg and nothing in this batch may tell her.** The counting that cleared it was his own decision and is not to be undone. **The word *ward* is canon, is a duty and not a place, and may not be re-derived.** **The word *exception* may not go into any instrument in this case, and on the coast a form with a word at the top of it is the same weapon it was in Volume 02 and is not to be restaged as a mystery.** The fifth mark in the guild's count book is unexplained and must stay unexplained. **The nine anchor positions and their missing functions are not advanced here, and the Shale Mirror question is not touched.**
