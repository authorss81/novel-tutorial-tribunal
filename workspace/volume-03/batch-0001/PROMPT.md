**Volume 02's closing scene may never be written again.** A washer of sixty-one who has walked four miles for eighty-six days, asked a question by a person with no right to ask it, answering *she did not tell the district because the district does not know.* **It may not be restaged, it may not be used as a precedent, and nothing on the coast may be answered by it.**

## The repetition bar — this is the hard constraint

Chapters 1–100 already contain: six filings that returned bounded reviews or preserved-and-declined answers; five named local arrangements with separately recorded refusals; a great many rooms that refused a clean, general, or universal answer; nine benches answering a question in writing; several public answers that turned out to be prices; a system whose empty rooms are all the same kind of empty; a document that cost more to make than to pay; and a great many benches' own minutes being the only durable thing.

**Volume 03 may not write** a seventh local arrangement, another bounded interval, another room that declines to say anything, another round of written answers from a set of benches, another bench minute as the hero of a chapter, another public answer that turns out to be a price, or another fragment recovered from a facing leaf.

**Volume 03 may not re-stage:** a child asked to testify; a private account filed without consent; a marker in a working record that nobody can account for; a preservation office confronted; a plate and an index compared; a foot-gate that opens the wrong way; a barrow that is a load; a handbell attached to nothing; a bell on a rope over a wheel on a pin; a burden assigned to nobody; a keeper saying he does not read a clause; the toll bar; the facing leaf; the four lines off the ninth bridge's frame.

**A marked echo of a canonical sentence by a different person is permitted. A re-staging of the same beat is not.**

**Two specific traps in this batch:**

1. **A rate on a crossing is the same shape as Volume 02 and is legal; a rate on a bucket read aloud off a lid is not.** If you write a rate, make it a *tide*, in the coin the coast uses, falling at a multiple on the poorest households. **Do not repeat the washer, the bucket, the farthing, the barrel lid, the called reading, the eight-times figure, the man who sells salt, or a woman who walks a long way to avoid a rate.** The rate must be found on the ground by a person with a want, and the person who pays must have a different reason from Volume 02's payer.
2. **A woman with a table of tides who holds a record and cannot appeal is Wenna Tace with the water changed and is forbidden.** If the coast needs a person who holds a record and cannot use it, that person is the glassmaker, and he is a **man**, and the reason he cannot use it is that **his record cannot be produced at all** — it is made by a process that takes a person, a fire, and a year. That is a different failure with a different shape.

## The ten cards for this batch

These are the surviving cards. They were drafted before the audit and revised against `outline/series.md`, `outline/volume-03.md` and the repetition bar. Changes from the drafts are marked.

**1. The coast, and the first thing that is not a room.** Open on the ground: a crossing, a landing, or a cart on a road above a harbour, in weather, with ordinary people doing ordinary work, and **a person with a want who tells one of them something about another person that neither can act on.** The volume's first honest problem is on the page in chapter one: the coast keeps its charters in a record neither of them can read, and a man who argues cannot navigate. **Do not explain the strait in narration.** Let a working person explain the safe route by telling them which day it is not safe, and let that be the whole of the geography for three chapters.

**2. The guild, and what a passage is worth.** The water and passage charter is a real document with a real burden, and the guild is not a villain and has not been asked to be one. **Let the guild be right about the strait.** The resistance is not dishonesty: the charter says what a passage is and does not say who bears it when the weather is wrong, and a person who cannot read the charter is a person the charter does not see. The chapter's finding is that **a guild controlling the only safe route is not a criminal act and is a catastrophe waiting for one bad week**, and the person who says it is a guild officer doing her job. *Revised: the officer is a woman of about fifty, harbourmaster's clerk for nineteen years and harbourmaster for six. She is the volume's best speaker. Give her a want that is not the strait — something about her own hands, her own record, her own house — or she will be a function.*

**3. The second site, and the first refusal of consent.** The batch's spine: putting a matter in two places means asking two communities to be in the same matter, and one of them says no. **The refusal must be correct, recorded in full, and cost somebody.** A community made a party in a matter about a strait has to be served, and being served is a day of somebody's time, and the second community is poor and has a version of this. *Revised: the second site is a port two Reaches down, poorer, with its own assembly, and its own idea of what a passage costs. Do not stage the refusal as a room that declines to answer — that is barred. Stage it as a person, in the open, who has a reason and states it and is not talked out of it.*

**4. A rate, in a different coin.** Return, in a new place, to Volume 02's spine — the price of a document is paid by the person who makes it — and make it a *tide* instead of a *bucket*. A rate on a crossing, in the coin the coast uses, falling at a multiple on the poorest households, and a table of tides that is the coast's own version of a page. **Do not repeat the washer, the bucket, or the farthing.** A different person, a different want, the same shape, and **the shape found on the ground and not announced.** *Revised per the audit: the woman with the table of tides may hold the table and may not be the door. She may not be a reader of a district, may not be the one a rate is read out by, and may not be unable to appeal because she is a holder. If she cannot do anything about the rate, it must be for a reason that is about the tide and not about a document.*

**5. The saltglass, and a person who is right about it.** Somebody on the coast reads saltglass the way Corva Silt reads a bench's own hand, and is right about what it is and wrong about what it is for. **The record is not an instrument and cannot be produced by a person who does not hold it, and a saltglass record is worse: it is made by a process, and the process takes a person, a fire, and a year.** Let the batch find what one costs to make, and let the cost be a person's work and a person's back, paid in advance by somebody who has already paid it. *Revised: the glassmaker is a man of about sixty, and he is the volume's counterpart to Corva Silt with the failure inverted — she was the mechanism and could not be relieved; he is not the mechanism and cannot be produced.*

**6. The songs, and the toll marks, and a pattern nobody asked for.** The coast's songs and toll marks contain a pattern resembling the hidden map in *The Sovereign's Wake*. **THE MIDPOINT IS RESERVED FOR LATER: this chapter may put the pattern on the page and must not let Ilyan recognise it, and it may not be in the first third of this batch.** Let somebody else see it first and be afraid of it — a ferryman, a glassmaker's apprentice, a woman with a table of tides — and let Ilyan's only contribution be asking what the marks are for, the answer being that they are for counting. **No System panel may confirm the pattern is a map, and no one may name *The Sovereign's Wake* in dialogue as anything but a thing a man from a city once played.** *Revised: if you would rather hold the whole pattern for Batch 0002, hold it. Card 6 may be a chapter about songs and marks and what they are for, with no pattern in it at all, and that is a permitted and arguably better reading of the placement rule.*

**7. The appeal, used by somebody who cannot use it.** Volume 02's resolution is that an appeal survives and is not secure, and the way it was not secure is that a rate in a bench's own book cannot be carried by anybody. **On the coast there is a thing a person may appeal and a person who may be appealed to, and the difference is a harbour custom nobody has written down.** A person tries to use the road they have and finds the road is a song, and a song is not a document. **This is a different failure from Volume 02's and must not be the same failure. A person may be refused a hearing by a person who is not a bench and is not a court and is right.** *Revised: the harbour custom is a song sung at a particular stone by a particular person on a particular tide, and what is refused is not access but a hearing, and the person refusing is the harbourmaster and is right about the tide and has no legal standing whatever.*

**8. The cost of connecting two places.** Witness Broker's cost, paid early and in the body: to link two sites he must carry a witness's account, and a carried account changes as it is carried, and a false merge harms people who never met. **Let him carry one and get something wrong, in an ordinary way, and let the person whose words were altered be somebody who is not in the room and does not know.** A ferry family is the right instrument: four generations on the water, three brothers whose accounts do not agree, and agreeing them would require one of them to be wrong in public about his father. **Do not make this a lie and do not make it a mystery. Make it a thing people have always done because the water was there.** *Revised: this is Batch 0003's material in the original draft and it is kept in Batch 0001 only as the smallest possible version — he carries one account down a beach and it comes back slightly different and he notices and cannot put it back. Do not stage the three brothers here. Save them.*

**9. What this coast has, and who is not in it.** A region with a rule and no living witness of it, one step further along from the Bridgeward, and the finding that the honest answer from every door is that the paper says something about a *person*. **Push which region has accepted a rule with no living witness at most twice; do not answer it and do not damage a route that works.** The chapter's finding is that a rule with no living witness is not the same as a rule with no living person in it, that the coast has the second and not the first, and that a toll taken eight years by a family and written into a form by a man who could not read is a rule with a person in it — **and the person has not been asked.** *Revised: this card is the closest thing in the batch to re-staging the Bridgeward, and the audit flagged it. Volume 02's Bridgeward is a road with no district, a toll that has never been short, eleven dead names in a witness line, and a question about a person that nobody answered. **The coast's version must not have dead names, must not have a toll that has never been short, and must not be asked by a boy with a line book.** It is a rule with a person in it who is alive and has not been asked, and that is a different thing and the difference is the point.*

**10. The batch close.** A partial, real, paid-for change; a burden named and visible; the thing Volume 02 was about, showing up in a new shape; and an ending that is a decision, a refusal, or a discovery, not a verdict. **Tamsin Ro is not to be called and nobody is to look for her. Neris Ro is not to be asked for anything and her right to stop everything herself stands, including from Ilyan, and her reason is not to be repeated in public.** Let the case's own first promise — **a burden named on both sides, a work record that is portable, a person not entered in a register is not a trespasser by that fact alone, a person can be sent for** — be tested by somebody on the coast who was not in the case and does not know what it cost, and **let the test be a small physical thing and not a speech.** **No romance beat. No new enemy. Nothing that makes the world bigger.** End on a question Chapter 111 can carry, and **do not manufacture suspense by cutting away mid-sentence or mid-action.**
