## 3. The cards

**581 — Wednesday.** The day after a decision. What a yard decision does by the fourth hour in town: about nine mouths have it, about nine have it another way. Garr cuts; the town reads the empty square as gone. A working day with mud on it, not a room.

**582 — Thursday.** The hay. About nine men, the fortnight, wet lines, no barn. Something is hauled somewhere and it does not fit where it is hauled to. Closing: a load and who would not take it.

**583 — Friday, market.** Third market since the rising. Bray is asked again or he answers; EITHER is permitted and NEITHER may be forced — a man pressed in the open in front of three hundred people is not the same as a man asked at a field edge, and the chapter must know which it staged. Closing: how many heard it and how many cannot be asked.

**584 — Saturday, reading.** Sixth hour, slate, lamp, door open. The morning list with nine parcels is read; one of the nine is wrong and somebody says so. The six challenges are five-with-parcel and one-without; the one without finds its man or does not. Closing: a correction in about four seconds.

**585 — Sunday.** No room, no slate, no bar. People, water, walking. Somebody goes somewhere and sees something with their own eyes and not off a wall. The band breathes. Closing: what was seen.

**586 — Monday, last of the hundred and fifth.** The year of options is priced out loud BY SOMEBODY WHO IS NOT HIM, in pence or in days, in a mouth, in front of people. This is the band’s one priced figure and it belongs to another mouth. Closing: the price.

**587 — Tuesday, first of the hundred and sixth.** The thing the water does. The rising comes a day early or a day late or not at all — pick one and work it on the page with the tally stone in it. No number for the rising but the day it comes on. Closing: the stone.

**588 — Wednesday.** Ditches cut or not cut. The hundred and forty yards, the barrow, the plank, the other end nobody holds. Somebody holds it or nobody does. Closing: hands.

**589 — Thursday.** The archive question carried into a room of about thirty people and not answered: who does a hundred ask when it asks, asked again in a new mouth, four wrong or nine silent or both, nobody bound. It may travel; it may not land. Closing: an unanswered question in a new room.

**590 — Friday, market.** The band’s close, on a decision and not a resolution. The settlement band (0005) must have visible to spend: the boards answered or still silent, the hay got or gone to seed, the meadow, the camp, the wall. Close on a person asked for something who has not said no. **THE QUESTION IS FOURTEEN DAYS OLD AT THIS CHAPTER AND A MAN OF FIFTY-FOUR HAS NOT SAID NO: if the band answers it, the answer is a thing somebody does in the open and not a thing the water-clerk reads off a wall, and if the band does not answer it then the water at `587` still has to do something and the reader must be able to see that those are the same question.** Closing: the next band’s instrument, unspent.
