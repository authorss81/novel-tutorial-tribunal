## Batch goal

Move from the copied-precedent neighborhood arc into local institutions. The ninth bridge’s provisional stay remains active until the seventh morning bell after the Chapter 10 ruling. The bridgekeeper’s guild, the Ro household, the eastern and Merefen districts, and the local road arrangement now disagree about who has the authority and physical capacity to maintain the bridge and the routes connected to it. Ilyan’s first sustained public testimony is used against him: his attached name makes people treat a local compromise as his ruling, even when he did not choose it. Ilyan and Sera must work through a real professional conflict rather than a rescue partnership. The batch should end with practical recovery, a bounded institutional arrangement, and a stronger record of the pre-arrival C—2 / Sava Kest route. Do not resolve the full bridge case, the historical right of refusal, the authorship mystery, or the First Witness.

## Canon and limits to preserve

- Ilyan crossed through a separate one-use legacy null passage, not the Shale Mirror. It was non-consensual, returnless, assigned no automatic burden, consumed the ninth bridge emergency reserve, and worsened an existing western-joint drift. He carries a spent blackglass token and the signed, unmerged *Sovereign’s Wake* refusal report.
- Ilyan is early Stage 1, Case Opener. With a named advocate he can phrase one narrow challenge or provisional exception request. He can document a sequence and attached precedent, but cannot represent Cinder Ward, assign another person’s burden, force a witness, rewrite a plate, choose a remedy alone, or use a new perception.
- The System translates only formal Bench records, notices, and hearing-field boundaries entered into Ilyan’s open case. It does not translate ambient conversation, trade shorthand, tone, or local custom. The Bench preserves records and issues binding rulings. Keep panels rare, readable, and tied to the immediate record; never use real software jargon.
- Sera Quill is still a limited provisional advocate. Her scope covers the bridge charter, maintenance record, disclosure, and the attached copied-precedent review; it does not make her the Cinder Ward’s advocate or Ilyan’s general counsel. Her family withdrawal appeal remains separate and depends on the right of withdrawal. Trust is conditional and revocable.
- Tarin Keel is a cautious working ally. He protects neutral lines, witnesses, and physical evidence, but does not obey Ilyan automatically or use force against residents or guards. Oren Sedge’s guild accepts only the finite ninth-bridge maintenance burden named in the stay. Sel Harrow remains a recurring procedural obstacle, not a cartoon villain.
- Tamsin and Neris retain independent agency. Tamsin’s only offered fact remains that the warning bell rang before the bridge moved. Neris’s flood account is disputed, preserved, and not evidence unless renewed. The names inside it were not consenting witnesses. The Ro household’s bridge access, work, housing, and consent boundaries remain active.
- The ninth bridge’s one-cycle provisional stay does not amend the current charter. Appeal, enforceable exit, durable housing/work protection, and the old maintenance-review condition remain unresolved until the full hearing.
- The eastern petition and Merefen Saltwheel Road extract are public precedent witnesses, not final rulings. The Cinder Ward arrangement names work, hours, materials, route, warning line, water route, public review, and exit. Mava’s refusal is recorded; she has not signed a permanent acknowledgment. The arrangement was renewed for one additional marked interval by a new choice, not automatically.
- The original Quiet Chamber page was removed before Ilyan arrived; its empty sleeve remains in archive custody. Sera’s private duplicate was present the night before the first field and disappeared during or after that field. Keep those dates distinct. A formal continuity request has a valid office seal but no named author, a C—2 code, and a missing witness index naming Sava Kest. A second paper uses Ilyan’s private Earth sequence, carries the C—2/bridge-plate route, and has a receipt dated before the bridge notice and Ilyan’s arrival. The old bridge plate’s edge shows an S. Kest fragment. These are chain clues, not proof of a culprit or a named First Witness.
- The continuity phrase “Continuity preserves the record before remedy” is a seeded, unidentified voice/cadence. Do not identify the First Witness here.
- The bridge’s old saltglass plate contains the missing maintenance-review condition and is sealed. The current certified working plate physically replaced it. The eastern petition can be coupled to other records only when a clerk deliberately admits them as precedent witnesses.

## Required mini-arc movement

### Chapter 0021 — The Guild Counts the Work

- **POV/location:** Ilyan and Oren, the ninth-bridge workroom and the public approach, the first working morning after the local road arrangement.
- **Goal:** Determine who is responsible for keeping the bridge, the connected road, and the water route safe until the bridge review.
- **Resistance:** The guild has finite labor and a formal burden; the district wants a broader levy; Neris and Tamsin need work/housing access; Maren Kett or another eastern representative frames the copied road as proof that Ilyan’s stay has become a regional obligation. Ilyan’s attached name is treated as authority.
- **Action/consequence:** Ilyan and Oren inventory the physical work rather than argue about labels. Local workers, including the earlier crew members where useful, name the jobs and hours. Ilyan is publicly told that his documentation cannot decide the schedule. A new bridge inspection exposes that the old maintenance-review omission still governs who may pause the span.
- **Ending pull:** The guild posts a work notice naming Ilyan as the attached record but assigning no maintenance labor; the absence itself becomes politically dangerous.

### Chapter 0022 — A House at the Edge

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, Sera, Neris, and Tamsin near the Ro household’s shelter and the lower bridge road, morning.
- **Goal:** Keep the household’s housing and work route inside the live case without turning the family into a symbol or evidence bundle.
- **Resistance:** The landlord, dye-yard, district clerk, and guild each need a different record. Tamsin is invited to repeat the bell fact for a stronger public case; Neris refuses broader use. The copied Saltwheel language has made officials ask whether the Ro household can refuse a burden without losing shelter.
- **Action/consequence:** Ilyan and Sera separate access, housing, work, and testimony. Tamsin chooses whether to speak at a narrow point; Neris offers only current route facts. Their evidence exposes that the household’s exclusion is being used to justify both the bridge stay and the district levy.
- **Ending pull:** A current work record shows that the Ro household’s access has been used to justify maintenance work they did not choose, echoing the Cinder Ward sequence.

### Chapter 0023 — The Name on the Load

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, Oren, Joren Vell, and a public bridge inspection, late morning.
- **Goal:** Test whether the bridge and connected road are failing because of physical drift, copied conditions, or the identity attached to the case.
- **Resistance:** Ilyan’s name is painted on a public work slate by a district clerk; the guild fears that any failure will attach liability to him; Oren’s bridge knowledge and Joren’s road knowledge do not fully transfer. A loaded public crossing changes the load sequence.
- **Action/consequence:** Ilyan documents the sequence and lets Oren/Joren identify the actual unsafe movement. The name on the slate is not a magical control, but people behave as if it were: a cart changes its route, a witness hesitates, and the district claims Ilyan’s presence altered the bridge. Ilyan refuses to touch the span or claim the work.
- **Ending pull:** A comparison of the work slate and the C—2 route shows that the name was entered in a different hand than the copied precedent, but the hand’s authority is unknown.

### Chapter 0024 — The Assembly’s Limited Word

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, Sera, Maren Kett, and the public district assembly, afternoon.
- **Goal:** Present the bridge and local-road records without asking the assembly to treat Ilyan as the correct authority.
- **Resistance:** The assembly splits between people who need a bridge open and people who fear an unlisted household or ward becoming a precedent for every district. Maren is a credible opponent who wants a legible common rule. The Ro household’s case and Sera’s family appeal are conflated in public rhetoric.
- **Action/consequence:** Ilyan refuses a universal declaration and supports a specific household’s right to refuse a particular acknowledgment while making the effect visible. The assembly agrees only to a local-institution review, not a general rule. Sera publicly corrects Ilyan when he reaches for a broad remedy, creating a visible professional disagreement.
- **Ending pull:** The assembly orders Ilyan to give a public account of his first testimony at the bridge, making his earlier choices evidence against him.

### Chapter 0025 — Ilyan’s First Public Testimony

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, Sera, the public hearing boundary, and the bridge workroom, following morning.
- **Goal:** Answer for his actual sequence at the bridge without making the Ro household or the guild carry his defense.
- **Resistance:** The district uses his Chapter 10 stay as settled precedent, quotes his attached-name burden as a promise of general authority, and asks him to affirm a remedy he did not choose. Sera can correct the record but cannot speak for him. Tamsin and Neris may refuse to attend.
- **Action/consequence:** Ilyan states what he accepted, what he did not accept, and where his plan failed. He admits the local copied rule traveled farther than his case and that his documentation was used to legitimate enforcement. He refuses to call himself the author of the new burden. A question about the pre-arrival second paper forces him to choose whether to make his Earth sequence public again.
- **Ending pull:** His honest answer reduces the political support he had gained, but exposes that a district clerk removed a refusal line from a work register before the hearing.

### Chapter 0026 — Sera Draws a Boundary

- **POV/location:** Sera’s room, the public archive desk, and the bridge records room, evening.
- **Goal:** Separate the Ro household’s case, the Saltwheel arrangement, and Sera’s family withdrawal appeal while repairing the professional boundary with Ilyan.
- **Resistance:** Ilyan wants Sera to use the C—2 and Sava Kest clues to force the Bench to reopen the bridge record. Sera will not let him turn her family’s missing page into leverage, and she is tired of being treated as the archive that fixes his plans. The two dates and the valid seal still support several explanations.
- **Action/consequence:** Sera accepts Ilyan only as a record-checker and asks him to verify ordinary custody, not infer a villain. Their argument becomes a practical division of labor: she protects the family record; he tracks only the public bridge and copied-precedent entries. They identify that the district’s work register was altered through a routine maintenance-update process.
- **Ending pull:** The altered register carries a marginal code that points toward the same C—2 routing chain, but the code is incomplete.

### Chapter 0027 — The Witness Who Refuses the Schedule

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, Tarin, Oren, and a local bridgekeeper witness at the work boundary, late afternoon.
- **Goal:** Carry out a necessary inspection without turning a worker’s refusal into a public emergency.
- **Resistance:** Tarin’s route order limits entry; the guild wants a clean inspection; a local worker refuses to sign a schedule that would assign future labor without pay. District carts pressure the crossing, and Ilyan’s name is used to imply that the worker’s refusal threatens the bridge.
- **Action/consequence:** Ilyan accepts Tarin’s boundary and asks the worker to choose what to offer. The worker offers the physical defect and refuses the future schedule. Ilyan frames a narrow request for a paid, finite inspection, not a universal labor rule. The local witness’s refusal is recorded without being treated as consent or sabotage.
- **Ending pull:** The worker names a second missing witness from an older maintenance crew, and the old saltglass plate’s edge notation becomes relevant to the bridge’s present load.

### Chapter 0028 — Recovery and a New Ledger

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, Oren, local bridgekeepers, roadkeepers, and ward/household workers, following morning.
- **Goal:** Restore a safe visible route while separating paid work, voluntary witness work, and the Ro household’s access.
- **Resistance:** The guild cannot absorb unlimited labor; the district controls materials and schedules; the Ro household has no recognized work credit; Ilyan is exhausted and tempted to publish a broad ruling that removes local discretion.
- **Action/consequence:** Local workers name a finite repair and inspection arrangement, including hours, warning steps, payment/work credit, review, and a visible exit. Ilyan documents only. Oren’s bridge advice and Joren’s road practice are both needed. The route opens under a named local arrangement, but the district again tries to omit the household’s refusal from the common ledger.
- **Ending pull:** A new ledger preserves the paid work and the household’s refusal separately, yet its custody line points back to the pre-arrival C—2 request.

### Chapter 0029 — The Review That Cannot Convene

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, Sera, Tarin, the public assembly hall, evening.
- **Goal:** Hold the promised local-institution review before the seventh-morning-bell bridge review.
- **Resistance:** A procedural clerk says the assembly lacks a quorum because the ward has no recognized address; the district invokes the copied precedent; the Ro household’s access and housing evidence are incomplete; Sera’s family claim is being used to invalidate the bridge case. Ilyan’s prior testimony is used to challenge his standing.
- **Action/consequence:** Ilyan refuses to solve the quorum by treating the ward as a single claimant. Tarin and local witnesses propose a neutral public boundary and a limited appearance. Sera and Ilyan disagree publicly about whether to submit the pre-arrival paper. The review proceeds only as a bounded evidence session, not a final ruling.
- **Ending pull:** The clerk reveals that the missing maintenance-review condition was once routed through the same C—2 office, but the office record is incomplete.

### Chapter 0030 — The Road Beneath the Charter

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, Sera, Oren, the bridge workroom, and the public hearing boundary, following day.
- **Goal:** Preserve a practical local arrangement and make the bridge’s continuing burden visible before the seventh-morning-bell review.
- **Resistance:** The district offers a quick settlement if the Ro household signs a maintenance acknowledgment; the household refuses permanent registration; the guild wants the bridge open without unlimited liability; the copied Saltwheel petition remains dangerous; Sera’s C—2/Sava Kest evidence points toward a larger archive process but names no responsible person.
- **Action/consequence:** Ilyan supports a named, time-limited maintenance arrangement with a public review, enforceable local work terms, and a visible exit condition. The Ro household’s refusal, the guild’s finite burden, and the district’s transport effect are recorded separately. Ilyan does not claim authorship or general authority, and the current bridge charter remains unamended: appeal, enforceable exit, and durable housing/work protection are still unresolved.
- **Ending pull:** The old bridge plate’s S. Kest fragment matches the missing witness index, while the C—2 route shows that the maintenance term and Ilyan’s private sequence may have traveled through the same pre-arrival process. The next batch opens with local institutions deciding whether to reopen the bridge record under pressure.

## Relationship and power guardrails for Chapters 21–30

- Ilyan must lose some public support by admitting the limits of his stay and the consequences of his attached name. Growth is changed judgment and delegated work, not a new power.
- Sera should be competent, independent, and professionally annoyed when Ilyan reaches for the archive or broad authority. Their relationship should deepen through boundaries, not a romantic rescue beat.
- Tarin may disagree with Ilyan and still protect the neutral line. Oren’s guild accepts only named work. Tamsin, Neris, Mava, Joren, Dalla, Lio, and other local people choose their own voices and burdens.
- Keep the System rare. Formal records may show a claim, evidence, burden, or immediate choice; people supply local language, custom, implementation, and moral interpretation.
- Keep the Ninth bridge’s physical danger real. Do not solve the bridge by naming a hidden bug or magical shortcut. The missing maintenance-review condition must be supported by Oren’s plate, the current physical behavior, and living witnesses.
- Do not identify the First Witness, reveal the full original Compact, resolve the historical refusal right, or introduce a new final enemy/cosmic threat.
