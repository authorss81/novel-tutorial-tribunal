## Batch goal and range

Write Chapters 11–20 as complete finished scenes, in order, with a coherent investigation/neighborhood mini-arc. The central pressure is the copied precedent: the eastern district and a neighboring district use wording derived from the ninth-bridge stay to keep a guild road open while shifting maintenance and refusal costs onto an unregistered ward. Ilyan must learn that a remedy copied beyond its original place can become a different injustice. Sera simultaneously pursues the missing Quiet Chamber page. End the batch with a local practical arrangement and a new, earned archive or precedent discovery; do not resolve the full bridge case, the historical refusal right, or the authorship mystery.

Use roughly 2,000–3,000 words per chapter, allowing a shorter reversal or a longer public scene when the scene needs it. Do not pad. Every chapter must contain a complete action, changed situation, practical or emotional consequence, and an ending that creates a decision, reversal, discovery, or pressure. Keep Ilyan competent with sequences, physical observation, and documentation but dependent on Sera, Tarin, Oren, local workers, and witnesses for language, law, and implementation. Do not introduce a hidden ability, a new final enemy, a new cosmic threat, or a magical shortcut around consent.

## Canon and state to preserve

- Ilyan crossed by a separate one-use legacy null passage, not by the Shale Mirror. The passage was non-consensual, returnless, assigned no automatic burden, consumed the ninth bridge emergency reserve, and worsened an existing western-joint drift. He carries a spent blackglass token and the signed, unmerged *Sovereign’s Wake* refusal report.
- The System translates formal Bench records, notices, and field boundaries entered into Ilyan’s open case. It does not translate ambient conversation, trade shorthand, tone, or local custom. The Bench preserves records and issues binding rulings; the System only presents them. Use panels rarely and only when the immediate record requires one.
- The ninth bridge has a one-cycle provisional stay. The Ro household may use it until the public review at the seventh morning bell after the Chapter 10 ruling. The current charter is not amended. Appeal, enforceable exit, durable housing/work protection, and the missing maintenance condition remain unresolved.
- Ilyan is early Stage 1, Case Opener. With a named advocate he can phrase one narrow challenge or provisional exception request. He cannot assign another person’s burden, force a witness, rewrite a plate, or choose a remedy alone. Do not give him Stage 2 perception. Physical strain, bridge vibration, and ordinary observation are not new powers.
- Sera Quill is still a limited provisional advocate. Her representation resumed after the Chapter 5 privacy breach was marked disputed; trust is conditional and revocable. Her family appeal depends on the right of withdrawal. Do not make her an unconditional ally or give her access beyond her scope.
- Tarin Keel is a cautious working ally. He will protect people and neutral lines, not obey Ilyan automatically. Oren Sedge’s guild accepts only the finite maintenance burden named in the stay. Sel Harrow remains a recurring procedural obstacle, not a cartoon villain.
- Tamsin and Neris retain independent agency. Tamsin’s offered fact is that the warning bell rang before the bridge moved. Neris’s flood account is disputed, remains preserved, and is not evidence unless renewed. The names inside it were not consenting witnesses. Do not use the account as a shortcut.
- The current public Quiet Chamber wording says, “the governed may petition for correction.” A surviving public fragment says, “the governed may answer no.” The original Quiet Chamber page’s empty binding sleeve and inventory mark record its removal before Ilyan arrived. Sera’s private duplicate was present the night before the first night field and disappeared during or after that field. Keep the two dates distinct; the original chain and remover are unknown. The continuity phrase “Continuity preserves the record before remedy” is a seeded, unidentified voice, not a named First Witness reveal.
- The bridge’s old saltglass plate contains a missing maintenance-review condition. The current certified working plate replaced it physically; the original is sealed. The eastern petition is mounted on a public blackglass backing and can be coupled to other records by a clerk when the court deliberately admits them as precedent witnesses.

## Required mini-arc movement

### Chapter 0011 — The Word That Traveled

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, Sera’s room and the public bridge approach, the morning after the stay.
- **Goal:** Stop the provisional wording from being treated as a general precedent before more districts copy it.
- **Resistance:** The stay is already public; a courier brings a neighboring district’s extract showing Ilyan’s bounded language detached from its conditions. The Ro household wants safety, while the new district says the wording is the only way to keep a guild road open.
- **Action/consequence:** Ilyan and Sera trace the copy’s chain, but discover that a local clerk can lawfully reuse public wording without asking the original parties. Ilyan must choose whether to demand an immediate freeze that could close the neighboring road or ask for a bounded review. He chooses a review, accepting that his name now travels with the remedy.
- **Ending pull:** The copied extract includes a phrase not present in the original stay: “the unlisted ward may refuse at the cost of the shared road.”

### Chapter 0012 — The Road That Borrowed the Bridge

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, a neighboring district’s guild road and unregistered ward, late morning.
- **Goal:** See the physical consequences of the copied rule before proposing another exception.
- **Resistance:** Local speech, road shorthand, and work rhythms are unfamiliar. The ward’s residents are not a single group: some want the road open, some cannot afford the maintenance levy, and some have no recognized address at which to appeal. The district claims the burden is temporary.
- **Action/consequence:** Ilyan observes ordinary labor, load marks, water routes, and a failed inspection. He learns that the copied wording makes a person’s refusal the trigger for a road-wide burden, while the district’s “temporary” period has no visible end. A local roadkeeper, not Ilyan, identifies the actual unsafe condition.
- **Ending pull:** The local keeper refuses to sign a clean inspection unless the ward is represented in the hearing; the first local witness turns the case toward the Bench without accepting Ilyan’s plan.

### Chapter 0013 — A Name in the Margin

- **POV/location:** Ilyan and Sera, a record shop and the district petition desk, midday.
- **Goal:** Establish who copied the wording and what the local petition actually asks for.
- **Resistance:** The petition is a composite of several older clauses; a local clerk insists the exact language prevents panic. Sera’s family appeal makes her wary of using the Quiet Chamber dispute to win an unrelated case. Ilyan’s public identity causes people to treat him as the author of the copied remedy.
- **Action/consequence:** They discover the copy was entered as a precedent witness, not a ruling, but a district marshal has already used it to justify a maintenance levy. Ilyan separates authorship from adoption and asks for the ledger of people who accepted the burden. Sera refuses to hide the ledger from the ward.
- **Ending pull:** The ledger’s last entry is a removed witness name, and its replacement line uses the same continuity cadence heard in the first field.

### Chapter 0014 — Tarin’s Boundary

- **POV/location:** Ilyan and Tarin, the road approaches and a temporary neutral line, afternoon.
- **Goal:** Keep the local review from becoming a crowd confrontation while carrying the physical inspection.
- **Resistance:** Tarin’s superior sends a containment order. The ward residents want to enter; district carts want the road cleared; Ilyan’s own name makes him a convenient target. Tarin insists Ilyan follow a route and sequence he does not control.
- **Action/consequence:** Ilyan accepts Tarin’s boundary and helps turn the crowd-control problem into a public witness list. A loaded cart breaks a small crossing-side marker, proving the copied rule is already changing physical behavior. Tarin secures the evidence but warns that Ilyan’s next request may be used to justify a wider emergency.
- **Ending pull:** The broken marker bears a line of private wording that matches Ilyan’s Earth report more closely than any public bridge record should.

### Chapter 0015 — The Midpoint Reversal: A Good Exception, a Bad Road

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, the unregistered ward, evening.
- **Goal:** Find a way to keep the guild road open without assigning the ward’s refusal cost to everyone else.
- **Resistance:** The district invokes Ilyan’s Chapter 10 stay as settled precedent. A ward household refuses to sign a maintenance acknowledgment and is threatened with loss of access to water and shelter. Oren’s earlier success is quoted as proof that named exceptions make bridges safer.
- **Action/consequence:** Ilyan publicly states that his stay was bounded by a specific bridge, a named crew, and a review date; the copied petition has stripped away those conditions. He proposes a local correction, but the district refuses and the ward’s refusal triggers a road-wide closure. The immediate rescue succeeds, yet Ilyan’s name is now visibly attached to a consequence he did not choose.
- **Ending pull:** Sera finds a second removed page fragment in the ward’s petition box. Its surviving words are “a burden may be refused when its effect is made visible,” while the lower half has been cleanly cut away.

### Chapter 0016 — Recovery Work

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, Oren, local roadkeepers, and the ward, following morning.
- **Goal:** Restore a safe, visible route without making the ward perform gratitude or consent.
- **Resistance:** Oren’s bridge repair knowledge does not transfer automatically to the district road. The district supplies materials but controls the work schedule; the ward has no recognized work credit. Ilyan is exhausted and tempted to publish a broad ruling.
- **Action/consequence:** He asks the local keeper and ward residents to name the work, hours, and people affected. They choose a limited, renewable maintenance arrangement with an exit date and a public review. Ilyan’s role is limited to documenting the sequence. The road opens for a named route, but the district refuses to publish the ward’s refusal in the common register.
- **Ending pull:** The local arrangement works for one day, then a courier delivers an order that would renew it automatically unless the ward signs a new acknowledgment.

### Chapter 0017 — The Assembly Refuses a Clean Answer

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, Sera, and a public district assembly, afternoon.
- **Goal:** Present the copied precedent and local arrangement without asking the assembly to treat Ilyan as the correct authority.
- **Resistance:** The assembly splits between people who need the road and people who fear an unlisted ward becoming a precedent for every district. Maren Kett or a comparable eastern representative may return as a credible opponent, not a secret villain. Sera’s family appeal and the bridge case are being conflated in public rhetoric.
- **Action/consequence:** Ilyan refuses to offer a universal declaration. He supports the ward’s right to refuse a specific acknowledgment while making its effect visible. The assembly agrees to a bounded review only if Ilyan submits his public Earth sequence to a neutral comparison. He accepts the exposure, but not the authorship claim.
- **Ending pull:** The comparison clerk finds that a removed witness signature was replaced after the old Quiet Chamber page was taken from its shelf.

### Chapter 0018 — The Family Appeal

- **POV/location:** Sera and Ilyan, Sera’s room and the public archive desk, evening.
- **Goal:** Separate the family’s withdrawal claim from the bridge case and determine whether the missing page can be recovered through ordinary chain-of-custody records.
- **Resistance:** Sera will not use Ilyan’s public case to bypass her family’s limits. Ilyan wants a decisive archive answer and treats delay as another obstruction. The replacement sleeve, fresh inventory ink, and a missing clerk’s signature create several plausible explanations.
- **Action/consequence:** Sera accepts his help only as a record-checker, not an advocate for her family. They identify that the page was removed through a formal continuity request, but the request’s author is hidden behind a valid seal. Ilyan admits he cannot infer guilt from a seal alone.
- **Ending pull:** The continuity request includes the same clipped cadence as the phrase beneath the System, and a marginal code points toward a second missing witness rather than a single culprit.

### Chapter 0019 — The Copy Reaches the Road

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, Sera, Tarin, the local road, night.
- **Goal:** Stop an automatic renewal from turning a temporary local arrangement into a permanent rule.
- **Resistance:** A clerk is within the formal right to renew the copied petition; the ward is divided; the district threatens to close the route before dawn. Ilyan’s attached name is being cited as proof that the court supports the order. Tarin will not use force against residents or guards.
- **Action/consequence:** Ilyan frames a narrow challenge with Sera naming the automatic-renewal condition, the affected ward, the finite maintenance burden, and the requested public review. He asks the roadkeeper and ward witness to choose whether to testify. The Bench issues a short pause, not a final settlement. The original copied petition remains in the record, publicly dangerous but not erased.
- **Ending pull:** As the clerk seals the active plate, a second paper comes through the court slot. Its wording uses Ilyan’s private Earth sequence in a way that was never part of the ninth-bridge record.

### Chapter 0020 — The Precedent Has a Body

- **POV/location:** Ilyan, Sera, the local road and the public hearing boundary, following day.
- **Goal:** Secure a practical local review and make the copied rule’s burdens visible without pretending the bridge case is solved.
- **Resistance:** The district offers to reopen the road if the ward signs an acknowledgment; the ward refuses to make that refusal permanent; the guild wants a quick settlement. Sera’s archive clue points to a continuity request, but the responsible person remains unknown. Ilyan’s name makes every compromise look like his ruling.
- **Action/consequence:** The local road remains open under a named, time-limited maintenance arrangement with a public review and a visible exit condition; the ward’s refusal remains in the record, and the district’s attempt to hide it is exposed. Ilyan does not claim authorship or general authority. The bridge stay remains provisional, and the original charter still lacks appeal, enforceable exit, and durable housing/work protection.
- **Ending pull:** Sera finds that the second paper’s continuity request was routed through a missing witness whose name matches a fragment on the old bridge plate. The next question is not who Ilyan is, but how his private words entered a Veyran legal record before he arrived.
