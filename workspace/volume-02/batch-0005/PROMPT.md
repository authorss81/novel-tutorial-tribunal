## Where the case stands on the morning Chapter 91 opens

**The money, and it has not moved.** The ninth bridge's district road fund is four hundred and ten pounds a year. **The pump.** Half a farthing a bucket, an eighth of a penny, chalked on a barrel lid on the seventeenth of the second month and read aloud at the sixth bell on the first day of a quarter by the holder. The first full quarter's reading was the first of the fourth month. **The called reading was the nineteenth of the fourth month**, because a man withdrew his consent to the haul rate and a bench of four made a rule for itself: *where any person who has set a rate withdraws his consent in writing after the rate has been read aloud, the rate shall be read aloud again inside the quarter, at the sixth bell, at the standpipe, by the holder, to whatever people come, and the withdrawal shall be read with it. The cost of a reading is about two hundred and forty people going up a bank in the cold, and it is paid by the people who go up it, and the bench has no money and this bench knows it. This is not a right. A right is towards a person. This is towards a barrel lid.* **The next quarter's reading is the first of the seventh, which is better than two months away.** The rate has not gone up and has not gone down.

**The holder.** Wenna Tace, twenty-nine, refuse hauler, about two hundred loads a year, paid a third of a haul rate and **about a pound and eleven a quarter** for keeping nine sheets and a page. ## The ten cards

Use these as chapter plans, not as prose. Each card must become a complete scene with its own physical ground, its own resistance, and its own consequence. **Chapter 91 should follow the letter, not precede it** — the letter arrived on the second of the fifth month in the last line of Chapter 90.

**Chapter 0091 — the answer is a matter and the fourth term is in the way.** Open on the second of the fifth month in a city four miles from a bridge. A recordwright has a question in her hand that she is not allowed to answer under the fourth term of her own licence, and she has four days before somebody in this case is going to be standing in a room that is not hers. **The ground is a room, a stove that does not draw, four days of paper, and a record she is not a party to.** The resistance is her own licence, which is correct, and the woman who sent the letter, who cannot be named in a support of anything. The discovery: **a question is not a matter and a matter is a claim, a subject, a burden, a witness, a remedy and an exit, and the only one of the six she is already inside is the one about the hauler, so answering the hauler puts her inside the matter, and the fourth term says she takes no matter of her own while she holds this one, and the hauler's question is not her own and is going to become one.** **Do not resolve the fourth term. Let somebody offer to change it and let the offer be refused by the person it belongs to.**

**Chapter 0092 — the cost of looking, in a body, on the ground.** A fragment of an old chamber cannot be read in a chamber; somebody has to go where the paper is, in weather, in a building that is not a court, with a person who has no standing. **Let the person who goes be somebody with a want of their own and a reason to be near that building, and let them be right about one thing and wrong about another.** Name the cost in the body and in a day of somebody's time, and enter the price in the same book as the work, the way a clerk enters a price. **The resistance is not a locked door. It is a door that is open, and an office with no person in it, and a series that runs by plate, and a hand that is willing to turn a page because nobody has ever asked it not to.**

**Chapter 0093 — the shelf, and what a person can do with a thing that runs by plate.** The standing authority of preservation holds every instrument printed in this city for four years in a series running by plate, and any person may read any of it at a counter on a day, and **no person has ever asked.** It has already said twice, in the same hand, that it can show that a rule was printed and cannot hold a person. **This is a shelf, not a confrontation, and it is a question put in writing with an answer that has a reason in it — but a different question from the two it has already answered, and the answer must be a reason and not a silence.** What can be found there: **a record of what the terms of that chamber were printed as, which is not the original and is not a recovery, and which is a thing about paper and not about a person.** **The finding must be that the shelf can tell you what a rule said and cannot tell you what it was for, and that this is the third time in four months that the same answer has come back from the same kind of place, and that the honest thing to do with a shelf is stop asking it questions it has already answered.**
