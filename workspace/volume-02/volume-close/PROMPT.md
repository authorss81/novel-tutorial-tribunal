In summary, and in full in the state files: **Volume 02 is closed. The appeal survives and is not secure. A page is for keeping the record of a duty. Sera's fragment is ten words with a crease in them, read as a duty and not as a place, and the right of refusal is not restored. The historical question is not answered. The Bridgeward's question about a person on a form nobody has to answer to is unanswered and the road is not touched. ## Draft cards for Volume 03 Batch 0001, carried forward so the work is not lost

These ten cards were drafted before the audit and are **drafts, not decisions.** The audit must check each against `outline/series.md` and `outline/volume-03.md` and **revise or reject any that conflicts with the series spine, with a delivered Volume 02 fact, or with a repetition bar above.** Carry them into `workspace/volume-03/batch-0001/PROMPT.md` only as cards that survive that check.

1. **The coast, and the first thing that is not a room.** Open on the ground: a crossing, a landing, or a cart on a road above a harbour, in weather, with ordinary people doing ordinary work, and a person with a want who tells one of them something about another person that neither can act on. **The volume's first honest problem is on the page in chapter one: the coast keeps its charters in a record neither of them can read, and a man who argues cannot navigate.** Do not explain the strait in narration — let a working person explain the safe route by telling them which day it is not safe, and let that be the whole of the geography for three chapters.
2. **The guild, and what a passage is worth.** The water and passage charter is a real document with a real burden, and the guild is not a villain and has not been asked to be one. **Let the guild be right about the strait.** The resistance is not dishonesty: the charter says what a passage is and does not say who bears it when the weather is wrong, and a person who cannot read the charter is a person the charter does not see. The chapter's finding is that a guild controlling the only safe route is not a criminal act and is a catastrophe waiting for one bad week, and the person who says it is a guild officer doing his job.
3. **The second site, and the first refusal of consent.** The batch's spine: putting a matter in two places means asking two communities to be in the same matter, and one of them says no. **The refusal must be correct, recorded in full, and cost somebody.** A community made a party in a matter about a strait has to be served, and being served is a day of somebody's time, and the second community is poor and has a version of this.
4. **A rate, in a different coin.** Return, in a new place, to Volume 02's spine — the price of a document is paid by the person who makes it — and make it a *tide* instead of a *bucket*. A rate on a crossing, in the coin the coast uses, falling at a multiple on the poorest households, and a table of tides that is the coast's own version of a page. **Do not repeat the washer, the bucket, or the farthing.** A different person, a different want, the same shape, and the shape found on the ground and not announced.
5. **The saltglass, and a person who is right about it.** Somebody on the coast reads saltglass the way Corva Silt reads a bench's own hand, and is right about what it is and wrong about what it is for. **The record is not an instrument and cannot be produced by a person who does not hold it, and a saltglass record is worse: it is made by a process, and the process takes a person, a fire, and a year.** Let the batch find what one costs to make, and let the cost be a person's work and a person's back, paid in advance by somebody who has already paid it.
6. **The songs, and the toll marks, and a pattern nobody asked for.** The coast's songs and toll marks contain a pattern resembling the hidden map in *The Sovereign's Wake*. **The midpoint is reserved for later: this chapter may put the pattern on the page and must not let Ilyan recognise it.** Let somebody else see it first and be afraid of it — a ferryman, a glassmaker's apprentice, a woman with a table of tides — and let Ilyan's only contribution be asking what the marks are for, the answer being that they are for counting. **No System panel may confirm the pattern is a map, and no one may name *The Sovereign's Wake* in dialogue as anything but a thing a man from a city once played.**
7. **The appeal, used by somebody who cannot use it.** Volume 02's resolution is that an appeal survives and is not secure, and the way it was not secure is that a rate in a bench's own book cannot be carried by anybody. **On the coast there is a thing a person may appeal and a person who may be appealed to, and the difference is a harbour custom nobody has written down.** A person tries to use the road they have and finds the road is a song, and a song is not a document. **This is a different failure from Volume 02's and must not be the same failure. A person may be refused a hearing by a person who is not a bench and is not a court and is right.**
8. **The cost of connecting two places.** Witness Broker's cost, paid early and in the body: to link two sites he must carry a witness's account, and a carried account changes as it is carried, and a false merge harms people who never met. **Let him carry one and get something wrong, in an ordinary way, and let the person whose words were altered be somebody who is not in the room and does not know.** A ferry family is the right instrument: four generations on the water, three brothers whose accounts do not agree, and agreeing them would require one of them to be wrong in public about his father. **Do not make this a lie and do not make it a mystery. Make it a thing people have always done because the water was there.**
9. **What this coast has, and who is not in it.** A region with a rule and no living witness of it, one step further along from the Bridgeward, and the finding that the honest answer from every door is that the paper says something about a *person*. **Push which region has accepted a rule with no living witness at most twice; do not answer it and do not damage a route that works.** The chapter's finding is that a rule with no living witness is not the same as a rule with no living person in it, that the coast has the second and not the first, and that a toll taken eight years by a family and written into a form by a man who could not read is a rule with a person in it — **and the person has not been asked.**
10. **The batch close.** A partial, real, paid-for change; a burden named and visible; the thing Volume 02 was about, showing up in a new shape; and an ending that is a decision, a refusal, or a discovery, not a verdict. **Tamsin Ro is not to be called and nobody is to look for her. Neris Ro is not to be asked for anything and her right to stop everything herself stands, including from Ilyan, and her reason is not to be repeated in public.** Let the case's own first promise — a burden named on both sides, a work record that is portable, a person not entered in a register is not a trespasser by that fact alone, a person can be sent for — **be tested by somebody on the coast who was not in the case and does not know what it cost, and let the test be a small physical thing and not a speech.** **No romance beat. No new enemy. Nothing that makes the world bigger.** End on a question Chapter 111 can carry, and **do not manufacture suspense by cutting away mid-sentence or mid-action.**
