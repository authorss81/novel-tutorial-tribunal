## ⚠ WHAT THIS BAND IS

**THE THING HE PUT DOWN. THE VOLUME'S CLIMAX. AND IT IS A SUNDAY TO A TUESDAY.**

Three things happen across these ten mornings, in this order, and none of them is a gift:

1. **He puts a second thing down on that landing and does not say so, and walks down the bank.**
2. **On a later morning somebody picks it up and says out loud what they think it is, and it is not what it was, and he is on the step and cannot say so.**
3. **The figure of how many things are left on that landing and not claimed is given ONCE, in a mouth, with the working given and the thing it counts named.**

**AND A THING PUT DOWN BY A PERSON WHO CANNOT SAY HE PUT IT DOWN IS A THING THAT WILL BE MISNAMED BY SOMEBODY WHO DID NOT PUT IT DOWN, AND THAT IS THE FIRST TIME IN THIS VOLUME THAT THE HOUSE HAS COST SOMEBODY SOMETHING IT DID NOT COST THE OTHER PEOPLE.** Nobody is thanked. Nobody is forgiven. Nobody is relieved. **A thing that has made a person's work easier is not a gift**, and a thing a person cannot account for is harder to refuse than a thing that does nothing at all, and that is exactly why it cannot be refused.

**AND WHAT IS OWED TO THIS BAND FROM THE WHOLE VOLUME, BESIDES ITS OWN HOUSE:** the ordinary form of §3.4 said in a mouth · the woman of forty-four left out of it, saying so before anybody thanks her, and nobody thanking her · the standing offer spoken at **three hundred** days at `1000` and unanswered · the absence said out loud a **sixth** time, on `994`, in one shape and once · the anchor a **seventh** time, on `997` · and **NOT** the figure of how many of the ninety steps are under a given bank, which was given in band 1 and is spent.

**AND A CASE THAT REQUIRED NO ARGUMENT ABOUT THE WORLD.** Nobody is to be asked who left a thing. Nobody is to be told they cannot have it. Nobody is to be told they were helped.
